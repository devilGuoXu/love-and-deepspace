who is sylus?

sylus is the leader of onychinus, the ruler of the n109 zone, and one of the most aggressive characters in love and deepspace. his dark element abilities focus on sustained damage and an energy loop that — once mastered — feels infinite.

strengths

  • infinite energy loop — at 145% energy recharge, one perfect dodge refunds enough energy to keep his resonance skill always available
  • high sustained damage — not bursty, but consistent. his damage never stops
  • forgiving rotation — once you learn the loop, it's hard to mess up
  • self-sufficient — doesn't rely on specific teammates
  • feels amazing to play — the dodge → resonance → attack rhythm is addictive

weaknesses

  • needs practice — the energy loop requires perfect dodges. miss them, and the rotation falls apart
  • energy recharge dependent — without 145% ER, the loop breaks. you need specific substats
  • not the best burst — if you need to delete a boss in 5 seconds, xavier is better
  • gear hungry — needs good protocores to reach ER breakpoint

playstyle

aggressive. always moving. sylus wants to be in the enemy's face, dodging attacks, building energy, and spamming resonance skills. you can't play him passively — you'll lose your energy loop.

the core rotation is simple: basic attack → perfect dodge → resonance skill → repeat. once you internalize the rhythm, sylus becomes a machine gun.

best teammates

sylus doesn't need specific teammates, but he appreciates:

  • zayne — freeze gives sylus safe windows to spam attacks
  • xavier — two carries means no downtime
  • any healer — keeps him alive during aggressive plays
🎯 verdict

main sylus if: you enjoy aggressive playstyles, don't mind practicing, and want a character who rewards skill with infinite damage uptime.

don't main sylus if: you prefer slow, safe gameplay, or you don't want to farm ER substats.

who is xavier?

xavier is a knight from the dying planet philos, a deepspace hunter, and the character with the highest burst ceiling in the game. his light element abilities concentrate 80% of his damage into a 4-frame window. miss that window, and you lose 40% of your damage.

strengths

  • highest burst in the game — perfect timing = massive numbers
  • satisfying when mastered — hitting the perfect window feels incredible
  • elemental advantage — light is strong against shadow enemies
  • scales well with investment — more crit = more burst consistency
  • theoretical damage ceiling is the highest

weaknesses

  • extremely timing dependent — 24-frame startup with 80% damage in frames 18-22
  • inconsistent without practice — miss the window, and you're useless
  • low sustained damage — outside his burst window, xavier is average
  • not forgiving — one mistake costs you the run
  • needs high crit rate — 65%+ to feel consistent

playstyle

xavier is a sniper. you wait. you watch. you learn enemy patterns. then you unleash everything in a 4-frame window.

outside that window, you're building energy and setting up. inside that window, you're doing all your damage. if you miss, you wait for the next window.

this is not a character for people who like to spam buttons.

best teammates

  • zayne — freeze freezes enemies, giving xavier safe burst windows
  • sylus — sylus handles sustained damage while xavier waits for the perfect moment
  • any shielder — keeps xavier alive while he's waiting for his window
🎯 verdict

main xavier if: you're a perfectionist, you love frame-perfect gameplay, and you want the highest damage ceiling in the game.

don't main xavier if: you get frustrated easily, you don't want to practice timing, or you prefer consistent damage over burst.

who is zayne?

zayne is a world-renowned cardiac surgeon and a deepspace hunter with ice abilities. he's the most forgiving character in the game — freeze gives you time to breathe, and his consistent damage means you don't need perfect execution to clear content.

strengths

  • best crowd control — freeze stops enemies for 3-4 seconds. that's huge
  • forgiving rotation — even if you mess up, freeze gives you time to recover
  • consistent damage — no reliance on crit luck
  • new player friendly — easy to pick up, hard to fail
  • healing potential — can keep your team alive

weaknesses

  • lower damage ceiling — won't match sylus or xavier in raw numbers
  • energy recharge dependent — needs 140% ER for perma-freeze
  • can feel slow — if you like big numbers, zayne might feel boring
  • freeze doesn't work on all enemies — some bosses are immune

playstyle

zayne is a control mage. freeze enemies, then damage them while they can't move. the rotation is straightforward: build energy, freeze, damage, repeat.

you don't need perfect timing. you don't need frame-perfect dodges. just keep the freeze going and let the damage accumulate.

best teammates

  • second ice character — double ice = more freeze uptime
  • xavier — xavier bursts during freeze windows
  • any healer — makes zayne nearly unkillable
🎯 verdict

main zayne if: you're new to the game, you prefer safe playstyles, or you want a character who can clear content without perfect execution.

don't main zayne if: you're chasing speedrun records, you want the highest numbers, or you find freeze rotations boring.

who is rafayel?

rafayel is the last prince of lemuria, a painter, and the game's DOT (damage over time) specialist. his fire abilities stack burn on enemies, dealing consistent damage over time. he's not flashy — but he's effective.

strengths

  • set and forget — apply burn stacks and swap out. damage happens automatically
  • low gear requirement — doesn't need perfect protocores to work
  • f2p friendly — performs well even with budget builds
  • good for casual players — you don't need perfect execution
  • valuable in long fights — DOT shines in drawn-out battles

weaknesses

  • low burst damage — won't delete bosses quickly
  • needs time to ramp up — burn stacks take a few seconds to reach full damage
  • feels weak in short fights — by the time your DOT ramps, the fight might be over
  • not satisfying for big number chasers

playstyle

rafayel is a patient character. apply burn stacks, then dodge, survive, and let the damage tick. you don't need to constantly attack — his DOT does the work for you.

this makes him great for players who don't like button-mashing.

best teammates

  • zayne — melt reaction (freeze + fire) deals bonus damage
  • xavier — xavier bursts while burn ticks
  • any healer — keeps rafayel alive while DOT works
🎯 verdict

main rafayel if: you like DOT playstyles, you're f2p, or you want a low-effort character who still clears content.

don't main rafayel if: you want big burst numbers, you dislike waiting for damage, or you find DOT boring.

team building basics

the right team makes all the difference.

the standard formula

main carry + sub dps / support + sustain

most teams follow this formula. your main carry does the majority of damage. your sub dps provides elemental coverage or burst support. your sustain keeps everyone alive.

elemental reactions

❄️🔥 melt
zayne + rafayel = bonus damage
✨🌑 light + shadow
xavier + sylus = increased burst potential
❄️ double ice
zayne + zayne = permanent freeze
🔥✨ burning light
rafayel + xavier = DOT + burst hybrid

final verdict

🎯
if you can only build one
build zayne
he's the most forgiving. he clears all content. he'll carry you while you learn the game.
⚔️
if you can build two
build sylus + xavier
sylus for sustained damage, xavier for burst. covers everything.
🏆
if you want to speedrun
build xavier
highest ceiling. practice your timing. then delete bosses.

build rafayel
set burns, dodge, repeat. low stress. clears everything eventually.

there's no wrong choice. every character can clear all content.

but some characters will feel better to you than others.

choose the one that matches your playstyle. not the one the tier list tells you to.
