Story Summary · Plot Overview

The gray-white dream. Her back was the only color.

Released on September 30, 2024, "Moonlit Black Thorns" is Zayne's personal branch in the "Tomorrow's Prelude" series. Two months ago, Zayne returned from Mount Eternal. He hasn't been the same since. Insomnia plagues him. The same nightmare haunts him every night.

Meanwhile, a new lead emerges in the protocore smuggling case: β magnetic waves — fluctuations linked to the deep-space tunnel in the N109 Zone — have appeared in Changxia City. Coincidentally, Akso Hospital sends Zayne there to treat several patients suffering from severe protocore syndrome. As his assigned hunter companion, you travel with him.

"Other doctors are other people's doctors. Mine is right here."
—— MC, to Zayne

On the operating table in Changxia, Zayne removes a strange protocore fragment from a patient's cardiac vessel — identical in form to the black crystals found on N109 monsters. But the moment the surgery ends, alarms blare. Space tears open. They are pulled into a magnetic field created by a Wanderer.

❄️ Dream Whisper

"You shouldn't risk yourself for him. Finding your destiny is your only path to atonement."

The words barely faded before black ice pierced through Zayne's chest.

—— A curse spoken in the dream

Inside the gray-white dreamscape, Zayne finally confesses. He fears that one day, he'll be trapped in the dream and never wake up. The boundary between dream and reality is eroding. Worse — his own obsession, channeled through the crystal fragment he brought back from N109, is becoming a weapon that hurts her.

Together, they shatter the magnetic field through Resonance. But the nightmare lingers. In the final moment of the dream, he finally catches up to the girl. The moment their hands touch, black crystals spread from his palm — onto her.

This branch reveals Zayne's deepest fear: that he might become the one who hurts her.

Deep Dive Analysis

The Dream · The Healer Trapped in Ice

Black ice, spreading from his palm

The central metaphor of this branch is the dream. Gray-blue walls, blinding fluorescent lights, a dead hospital corridor — and her back, the only color in this monochrome world.

Zayne dreams of a girl. His obsession. He tries to call out to her, but no sound comes. He tries to run after her, but black crystals burst from the walls, reaching for her throat.

This isn't an ordinary nightmare. It started after Mount Eternal. It worsened after he brought back the crystal fragment from N109. The boundary between dream and reality blurs — when he's hurt in the dream, the wound remains upon waking. When she's threatened in the dream, she finds herself in real danger.

Will Zayne be trapped in his own dream, unable to wake? That is his fear. And this branch plants that seed.

✧ The Healer's Curse

Zayne is a doctor. His calling is to save. But in this branch, he becomes the one who needs saving.

In the desolate dreamscape, he confesses his guilt. He fears being trapped in the dream forever — because the boundary is eroding, and his obsession, manifested as black crystals, has become the weapon that hurts her.

This creates a powerful dramatic irony: the healer who saves others becomes the source of harm. It's the ultimate version of "the healer who cannot heal himself."

Jasmine · The Only Color

In the gray-white dream, there is a small bundle of fresh jasmine. The only color in that colorless world.

"The jasmine she gave him became the obsession of his entire existence."
—— Player comment

Jasmine recurs throughout Zayne's story. Pure. White. Fragrant. It is the only light in his world. When the dream collapses, the jasmine is the last thing to disappear.

This is the most subtle thread between Zayne and MC — she is his jasmine, the only color in his gray world, the obsession he fears waking from and fears continuing.

Black Crystals · Redemption and Self-Destruction

Crystals spread from his palm onto her

"Destroying this place is our only choice."

This line runs through the entire branch. The dream isn't somewhere Zayne wants to be. He has to shatter it himself — even if that means destroying parts of himself.

The black crystals are the physical manifestation of Zayne's obsession. They spread from his palm, threatening to consume her. But paradoxically, they are also the medium through which they connect and Resonate.

The crystals are both the weapon that hurts her and the bridge that connects them. This is the central tension in Zayne's character: he both longs to be close and fears getting too close.

❄️ Analysis · The Doctor's Pride and Fragility

Zayne admits in the branch: "If I could sleep soundly in a place like this, that's when you should really start worrying."

He hides his vulnerability behind self-deprecating humor, but inadvertently reveals his dependence on her. The crystal fragment he brought back from N109 — he knows it might be connected to his nightmares, but he doesn't discard it. Because she gave it to him.

This is his silent, unspoken devotion.

Character Development & Emotional Core

Zayne in "Moonlit Black Thorns" is not the flawless hero archetype. He is a man burdened by obsession, fear, and self-doubt.

Zayne · Silent Devotion

When provoked, Zayne's response is simple: "Thanks, but the car won't fit it." He doesn't engage in confrontation. He expresses his focus and possessiveness through actions, not words.

His deflection about his own health — "Don't worry too much" — is classic "healer who can't heal himself." He always takes care of others but refuses to admit he needs care too.

✧ Zayne · Key Quotes

  • "Got it. I'll try to stay alive." — A promise to her, and to himself
  • "If I could sleep soundly in a place like this, that's when you should really start worrying." — Hiding behind self-deprecation
  • "For my only doctor, I wouldn't dare disobey." — The one exception to his rules

MC · Persistent Presence

In this branch, MC is no passive figure. She volunteers as the accompanying hunter. She uses her Evol to buy him time during surgery. She fights beside him in the dreamscape.

"Other doctors are other people's doctors. Mine is right here." This is her confession — and her declaration of commitment.

Resonance shatters the dream — and the ice within him

Viewing Guide & Tips

📖 Before You Watch
  • Complete main story Chapter 9 — understand the protocore smuggling plot and N109 Zone lore
  • Experience Zayne's "Mount Eternal" personal story for context on the nightmare's origin
🎮 Immersion Tips
  • Choices matter: Dialogue choices affect minor conversations — save to explore branches
  • Watch his reactions: Every line Zayne speaks is worth reading twice, especially when he deflects
  • Use headphones: The dreamscape BGM is intensely atmospheric

💡 Easter Egg: The jasmine motif continues in his 5-star memory "Talking to the Moon." If you enjoy Zayne's story, don't miss it.

Personal Rating & Recommendation

★★★★☆

✧ What Works

  • Strong atmosphere: The dream-reality tension keeps you engaged
  • Zayne's vulnerability: Finally, he's not just the calm doctor — he's someone who fears, who feels guilt
  • Memorable writing: Lines like "Got it. I'll try to stay alive" stick with you
  • Rich symbolism: Jasmine, black thorns, crystals — each layer adds meaning

✧ What Could Be Better

  • Pacing: Some combat transitions feel rushed
  • Unclear mechanics: β magnetic waves and protocore science could use more explanation
8.5
Overall Score / 10
Emotional depth and character development carry this branch
"Is it the wind moving, or the banner?"
—— Zayne · Perhaps it's only the heart

"Moonlit Black Thorns" is a deep exploration of Zayne's inner world. It breaks the "calm doctor" archetype, revealing a man scarred by fear and obsession.

The crystal fragment he searched for became the thorn piercing his own heart. He wants to be close. He wants to protect. But he fears his closeness will cause harm.

If you love Zayne, this branch is essential reading. It's not sweet domestic fluff — it's a haunting meditation on redemption, obsession, and love.
