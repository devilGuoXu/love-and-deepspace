How We Work

Play First, Write Later

We do not write guides for content we have not personally cleared. Every Abyssal floor strategy, every protocore recommendation, and every character build guide on this site was tested in-game before it was published. If we recommend a team composition, it is because we used it to clear the content ourselves.

No Fluff, No Fillers

We do not pad our guides with generic advice you could find anywhere. Every paragraph serves a purpose. If you see a section header, you can expect real data beneath it. We respect your time because we are players too.

Built by Players, For Players

This site is self-funded and maintained by a small team of dedicated players. We do not take money for guide placements, we do not boost characters for compensation, and we do not write content we do not believe in. If we tell you to skip a banner, it is because the math says so.

Our Story

The Beginning

This site started the way most player resources do: with a single spreadsheet. One of us was tracking protocore drop rates across different Abyssal floors, trying to figure out whether the higher stamina cost of Floor 10 was worth the increased SSR rate. The spreadsheet grew. Friends asked for copies. The copies grew into a shared document. The document grew into a website. There was never a grand plan, just a steady accumulation of data that demanded a proper home.

Why We Kept Going

Love and Deepspace is a complex game. The difference between a good build and a great build can be subtle, and most resources available early on were either incomplete, inaccurate, or written by people who clearly did not play the characters they were writing about. We saw a gap and we filled it. The positive response from the community convinced us to keep going.

What We Are Building

We are working toward a complete knowledge base. Combat guides for every character and every game mode. Lore analysis that connects the fragments of the story into something coherent. Style guides that help you make informed decisions about where to spend your resources. Interactive tools that make planning easier. One page at a time, grounded in actual experience, written for players who care about getting better.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this site affiliated with the game developers?
No. Deepspace Hunter Station is a fan-made project. We are not affiliated with Infold Games or Papergames. All game assets and characters are trademarks of their respective owners.
How do I know your guides are accurate?
We test each build in-game before publishing. Our damage calculations are verified against actual combat results. If we make a mistake, we fix it and note the correction. Readers can flag issues through the contact page.
Do you accept guide submissions from other players?
We are open to collaboration with players who have genuine expertise and data to back up their work. Contact us through the contact page if you have something to contribute.
How can I support the site?
Share the guides with other players who might find them useful. Link to our pages in discussions. If you spot an error, let us know. That is the most valuable support you can offer.
Which operative should I build first?
It depends on your playstyle. Xavier has the highest burst potential but requires precise timing. Zayne is the most consistent and forgiving for new players. Rafayel rewards aggressive play with strong sustained damage. Sylus offers the most flexible playstyle with excellent sustain. Read our individual character guides for the full breakdown.
Do you plan to add more interactive tools?
Yes. We are working on additional tools including a protocore optimizer, a team synergy calculator, and a comprehensive memory bank with filterable search. They take time to build and test, but they are coming.
