Our Story

How It Started

Deepspace Hunter Station began as a personal spreadsheet. One player, frustrated by the lack of reliable, data-backed guides for Love and Deepspace, started documenting frame data, protocore combinations, and boss patterns. What began as a private reference document grew into a shared resource as friends and guildmates asked for copies.

Building the Database

Over the course of several months, the spreadsheet evolved into a structured wiki. Each character received a dedicated analysis page. Every Abyssal floor was mapped and documented with F2P-friendly team compositions. The focus was always the same: provide accurate, actionable information that helps real players clear content.

What's Next

We are actively expanding our coverage to include new characters, deeper lore analysis, and interactive tools like our resource calculator. Our long-term vision is to become the definitive English-language knowledge base for Love and Deepspace—built by players, for players.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official website?
No. Deepspace Hunter Station is a 100% fan-made project. We are not affiliated with Infold Games or Papergames.
How do you test your builds?
We run each build through the same floors, same rotations, multiple times. We track damage numbers, clear times, and consistency.
Are you hiring?
We're a small volunteer team. If you're passionate about Love and Deepspace and have skills in writing, data analysis, or web development, reach out!
How can I support the site?
Share our guides with friends. Link to us in discussions. And if you see an error, let us know — feedback makes us better.
Sylus or Xavier? Who should I build?
Depends on your playstyle. Sylus = infinite energy loop, aggressive, forgiving. Xavier = highest burst ceiling, timing-dependent, punishing. Read our combat guide for the full breakdown.

Maybe! Reach out via the contact page with your idea and experience. We're always looking for fresh perspectives.
