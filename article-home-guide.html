Quick Start · Unlocking the Home

Tap the "Home" icon on the main screen to enter

The Home system unlocks after completing main story chapter "Abyss Below 01-05". Once unlocked, you'll receive a free villa with a garden from Linkon City Housing Authority, plus over 1,100 free furniture items to collect.

🏠 How to Enter

Tap the Home icon on the right side of the main screen. Inside, you can freely explore every room — living room, bedroom, kitchen, garden. Every corner may hide special interactions with him.

The Home system is an independent 3D space where you can arrange furniture, grow flowers, and invite your chosen companion to share the space. All furniture and decorations can be obtained through normal gameplay — no purchases required.

📈 Fast Leveling Methods

  • Complete Settlement Guide tasks: Tap Tasks → Settlement Guide. This is your main EXP source.
  • Daily login: Home level is tied to cumulative login days — don't miss a day.
  • Gardening (spend stamina): Planting and breeding flowers consumes stamina and gives home EXP.
  • Buy and place furniture: Each new furniture item increases "Cozy Points," raising your home level.

💡 Tip: If no new tasks are available, you've reached today's progress cap. Continue tomorrow. Home is a long-term feature — no need to rush.

Flower Breeding · Complete Collection Method

The garden — your main flower breeding hub

Flower breeding is one of the most important Home features. By planting and cross-breeding flowers, you unlock the flower catalog, earn floral materials to gift to him, and increase your bond level.

🌱 Four Growth Stages

Flowers go through four stages: Seed → Seedling → Growing → Mature. Each stage may require pest control, watering, or weeding. Timely care gives growth points — only flowers with enough growth points can reach maturity.

🎻 Robustness & Performing

A flower's "Robustness" determines its final star rating (1-3 stars). You can increase robustness by "performing" (playing erhu) for the flowers. Each performance costs 20 stamina.

Prioritize flowers with "Music Lover" or "Vigorous Growth" traits — they're easier to perform to 3 stars. Cross-breeding two 3-star original-colored flowers gives a higher chance of obtaining rare or cross-colored flowers.

📋 Lazy Collector's Method

Here's the most efficient way to complete your flower catalog:

1
Buy 8 seeds of the same flower type and plant them all
2
Randomly select 4 flowers to perform to 3 stars (80+ robustness = 3 stars)
3
Cross-breed 3-star original × 3-star original → obtain cross-colored or rare flowers
4
Rare flowers don't need performing — use them directly for further cross-breeding
5
Cross-colored × original — keep cross-breeding until catalog is complete
6
Same flower type guaranteed rare on 6th cross-breed attempt — safe even with bad RNG

⚠️ Important: Floral materials have a "Bloom Period." If not used in time, they'll wilt. If you're not ready for flower arranging, harvest them as "Flowers" (inventory item) instead. Replant them when needed. Use all garden stamina daily — 720 stamina with monthly card, 480 without — all on performing to increase robustness.

Bond Progression · Getting Him to Stay

Higher bond level = more interactions

In the Home system, you can invite any of the four male leads to live with you. Increasing your "Bond Level" unlocks more interactions, story segments, and special gifts.

🏠 Getting His Access Card · Making Him Permanent

To have him stay permanently, complete the "Together Forever" quest chain. Entry: Tap his portrait in the top-left corner → "With Him" → Complete all quest stages to trigger the story where he gives you his Access Card. After that, he can visit anytime, and you can give him daily gifts.

💝 Daily Gift Guide

You can give him floral materials or gifts every day to increase bond level:

  • Gift floral materials: Tap him → "I want to give you" → Select harvested flowers
  • Gift special items: Unlock at higher bond levels
  • Interactions: Daily furniture interactions and shared activities also increase bond level

Different companions have different favorite flower species, unlocked as bond level increases.

💡 Tip: Each companion has a "first visit" gift — don't forget to claim it! Completing the "Warm Home Diary" story also gives bonus rewards.

Companion Quests · Detailed Walkthrough

"Daily Companionship" is the core quest line for interacting with him in the Home. Here's how to complete each one:

📖 Read Together
Walk to bookshelf, tap "I want to..." → "Read Carefully" and finish a book. Start with 5 books, each gives 10 diamonds
💐 Gift Flowers
Harvest flowers → convert to materials → tap him → "I want to give you" → select floral materials
🎨 Flower Arranging
Harvest flowers (5 per harvest), go to flower arranging table. Buy pots from garden shop if needed
💃 Duet Dance
Buy speakers in Home Shop, place them, then invite him to interact near the speakers
🤗 Hug
Approach him and tap the interaction prompt when it appears — unlocks with bond level
🎤 Listen to Him Sing
Tap him → "I want to see you" → Sing. After Home Level 10, duet together on piano
🔥 Fireplace Warmth
Buy fireplace at Home Level 9, approach to trigger interaction
🪑 Sofa Hug
Buy two-seater sofa at Home Level 8, sit down together to trigger

Tips & Tricks · Common Pitfalls

🪴
Use Floral Materials Quickly
They have a "Bloom Period" — after that they wilt. If not ready, harvest as "Flowers" (inventory) for later
🏠
French Door Not Working?
Move away any potted plants near the door — they block the interaction
📚
"Read Carefully" vs "Browse"
Always choose "Read Carefully" — "Browse" gives no diamonds
🎁
Gift Placement
Gifts he gives you must be placed in specific spots in furniture mode
📺
Turn Off the TV
After watching, tap TV → "I want to..." → "Turn off" — otherwise it stays on forever
🎆
Fireworks Event
Dec 31 & Jan 17-19, 7pm-5am — watch fireworks with him to trigger special chat moments
