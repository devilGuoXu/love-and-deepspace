PRO TIPS

What I Learned The Hard Way

01
Never pull on impulse
Sleep on it. If you still want it tomorrow, think about it again. Most of my regret pulls happened at 2 AM.
02
Skip the first day of banner
Wait for community reviews. I've saved thousands by not pulling on day 1 "hype."
03
Don't chase 4★ characters
You'll get them eventually. 4★ aren't worth wasting guaranteed pity on.
04
Stop at soft pity if you're unsure
70 pulls is soft pity. If you're not sure about the banner, stop at 70 and wait for the next one.
05
Use the "wait 3 days" rule
Before spending on a banner, wait 3 days. If you still want it, pull. FOMO fades fast.
06
Track your pity
Keep a note of your pity counter. Knowing you're close to guarantee changes how you spend.
FAQ

Questions You Might Have

❓ Should I buy stamina with diamonds as F2P?
No. Stamina refills are a trap for F2P. Every diamond you spend on stamina is a pull you lose. Only refill if you're whaling.
❓ What's better: saving for 150 or pulling on every banner?
Save for 150. Guarantee > luck. I've seen too many people waste 70 pulls on a banner they didn't really want and get nothing.
❓ Should I pull for weapons?
Not as F2P. Characters > weapons. A good character with a 4★ weapon > a bad character with a 5★ weapon.
❓ How many diamonds can I really save per patch?
~6-8k per month if you do everything. ~8-10k if there's an event. That's 40-60 pulls per month.
❓ Is the monthly card worth it for low spenders?
This guide is F2P. But if you're willing to spend $5, yes — it's the best value in the game. But you don't need it.
💎

The bottom line: As F2P, you can guarantee one rate-up character every 2-3 months. That's enough. You don't need every character. You need the right ones.

Be patient. Plan ahead. Skip banners that don't fit your account. And remember — the game is more fun when you're not stressed about pulls.
