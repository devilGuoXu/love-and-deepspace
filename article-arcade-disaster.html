Arcade Disaster

or: four men, one arcade, zero tickets won

it started, as most disasters do, with sylus.

"we should go to the arcade," he said.

rafayel was already planning which claw machine to conquer.

this is what happened next.

02

the arrival

the arcade. flashing lights. beeping sounds. the smell of pizza and regret.

sylus arrived first. black coat. black shoes. black sunglasses. indoors. at night.

he stood by the entrance. arms crossed. evaluating the ticket redemption counter.

(he was calculating the most efficient way to win everything.)

zayne arrived second. he brought hand sanitizer.

"you brought hand sanitizer," said sylus.

"arcade controllers are touched by hundreds of strangers."

"you're not wrong."

rafayel arrived third. wearing sunglasses. indoors. at night.

"you're wearing sunglasses again," said zayne.

"i'm mysterious."

"you look like you're hiding."

"same thing."

xavier arrived fourth. seventeen minutes late. he had fallen asleep in the car.

"the parking lot was dark," he explained.

"you weren't driving," said rafayel.

"i know. the passenger seat."

"let's go," said sylus.

and they went.

03

the ticket counter

the whale. 5,000 tickets. rafayel's destiny.

they passed the prize counter. giant plushies. cheap toys. a ridiculous amount of tickets needed.

rafayel pressed his face against the glass.

"i want the whale," he said.

"the whale is 5,000 tickets," said zayne.

"i know."

"you have zero tickets."

"i know. for now."

sylus looked at the whale. then at rafayel. then at the ticket cost.

he said nothing. but he was already counting.

xavier found a bench. sat down.

"xavier, no," said rafayel.

"i'm resting my eyes."

"we haven't started."

"the walk from the entrance was long."

04

rafayel vs. the claw machine

the enemy.

rafayel found the claw machine. the whale plushie was inside. taunting him.

"this is it," he said. "my moment."

he inserted token. moved the claw. dropped it.

the claw grabbed nothing. came up empty.

"warming up," he said.

second try. grabbed the whale. lifted it. dropped it at the top.

"SO CLOSE."

third try. fourth. fifth.

zayne watched. "you're spending a lot of money."

"it's about the journey."

"the journey is losing."

sylus inserted one token. moved the claw once. dropped it.

the claw grabbed the whale. lifted it. dropped it into the prize chute.

one try.

rafayel stared.

"how."

sylus shrugged. "luck."

"THAT WAS NOT LUCK."

sylus walked away. rafayel stared at the whale in the chute.

"he's not even going to take it," said zayne.

"i know."

"are you going to take it?"

"...no."

"why not."

"because then he wins."

zayne sighed. "you're both impossible."

05

zayne's scientific approach

he drove perfectly. he finished last.

zayne found a racing game. realistic steering wheel. pedals. bucket seat.

"this is accurate," he said. "i approve."

he sat down. adjusted the seat. adjusted the mirrors. checked the gear shift.

"it's a game," said rafayel.

"precision matters."

the race started. zayne drove perfectly. followed every traffic rule. stopped at red lights.

"THERE ARE NO RED LIGHTS IN RACING," shouted rafayel.

"safety first."

he finished last.

"i prioritized safety," he said.

"you prioritized losing," said rafayel.

zayne stood up. brushed off his coat. "i'm satisfied."

no one believed him.

06

xavier's discovery

he never fired a single shot.

xavier had wandered off. they found him at the shooting gallery.

he was holding the plastic gun. aiming at the screen. perfectly still.

"xavier?" said rafayel.

"shh. i'm focusing."

he hadn't fired a single shot.

"you have to pull the trigger," said zayne.

"i know."

"then why aren't you pulling it?"

"i'm waiting for the right moment."

"the targets are moving."

"i know. that's why i'm waiting."

he stood there for five more minutes. never fired a shot.

"i'm done," he said. put down the gun. walked away.

"you didn't shoot anything," said rafayel.

"i was conserving energy."

mephisto, perched on a nearby skee-ball machine, cawed softly.

07

sylus cleans up

thirty-seven wins. zero losses. one legend.

sylus found the fighting game. street fighter. vintage cabinet.

"oh no," said rafayel.

"oh yes," said sylus.

he inserted token. chose the most difficult character. waited.

a teenager challenged him.

sylus won in twelve seconds.

another challenger. seventeen seconds.

another. nine seconds.

a crowd formed.

"who is that guy," whispered someone.

"i don't know but i'm scared."

sylus won thirty-seven matches in a row. no one could beat him.

he stood up. walked away without looking back.

"that was terrifying," said rafayel.

"he's like that," said zayne.

"you're not scared?"

"i'm terrified. i just hide it better."

08

the ticket counting

the moment of truth.

they pooled their tickets at the end.

rafayel: 120 tickets (mostly from pity tickets).

zayne: 45 tickets (from the racing game's "participation" reward).

xavier: 0 tickets. (he never pulled the trigger.)

sylus: 3,400 tickets.

everyone stared.

"how," said rafayel.

"the fighting game gives tickets."

"THAT'S NOT FAIR."

"life isn't fair."

sylus walked to the prize counter. looked at the whale. looked at rafayel.

"do you want the whale," he asked.

rafayel crossed his arms. "no."

"liar."

"...yes."

sylus redeemed the whale. handed it to rafayel. said nothing.

rafayel held the whale. tried to look unimpressed. failed.

"this doesn't mean anything," he said.

"i know."

"i still hate you."

"i know."

he hugged the whale.

zayne sighed. xavier was already asleep on a bench.

09

the ride home

he named it "little sylus." no one will ever know.

they left at midnight. rafayel was holding the whale. xavier was asleep in the back seat.

"today wasn't terrible," said rafayel.

"you say that every time," said sylus.

"it keeps being true."

"don't ruin it."

zayne looked out the window. "we're never doing this again."

"we're definitely doing this again," said rafayel.

"unfortunately."

from the back seat, xavier mumbled: "tickets."

"go back to sleep," said rafayel.

xavier did.

the car was quiet. the city lights blurred past.

none of them would admit they'd had fun.

(they had.)
