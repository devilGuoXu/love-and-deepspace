Things I Don't Let Myself Think About

(but do anyway. at 2am. when i can't sleep. when he's not here.)

i have a list.

not written down. not anywhere he could find it. just... in my head.

things i'm not supposed to think about. things that would make him look at me differently if he knew. things that would make me look at myself differently.

these are those things.

(don't tell anyone.)

01

what if he's not even human?

two hundred years is a long time.

longer than any human should live. longer than any human can live. but he's standing right there. breathing. looking at me with those eyes that have seen centuries.

so what is he?

i've read the files. the redacted parts. the parts they don't want me to see. philos isn't a place. it was a place. a dying planet. and he... came from there?

what if he's not human at all?

what if he's something else. something older. something that just looks like us.

i don't ask. i'm afraid of the answer. what if he tells me the truth? then i have to believe it. what if he lies? then i have to pretend i don't know.

so i don't ask.

but i think about it. when i can't sleep. when he's standing too close. when i catch him looking at me with those eyes that have seen things i can't imagine.

(what are you, really?)

~ ~ ~
02

what if the person he's been waiting for isn't me?

he says things sometimes. little things. "i've been waiting for you." "i knew you'd come back."

but what if he's not talking to me?

what if he's talking to someone i used to be. someone i don't remember. someone who looked like me. sounded like me. but wasn't me.

what if i'm just... close enough?

what if one day the real one comes back. and he looks at me. and his eyes say "oh. you're not her."

i've never asked. i can't. because if he says yes — if he admits there was someone before — then everything changes. every look. every word. every "i've been waiting."

so i don't ask.

but sometimes i watch him. when he's looking at the stars. when his eyes get that distant look. and i wonder who he's really seeing.

(is it me? or is it her?)

~ ~ ~
03

what if i just... kissed him?

not during a mission. not when the adrenaline is high and everything feels like it matters more than it does.

just... a normal day.

he's standing there. looking at me. saying something ordinary. something about the weather or a mission report or what he wants for dinner.

and i just... walk over. and kiss him.

what would he do?

would he freeze? would he kiss back? would he look at me with those ancient eyes and say "finally"?

would he step away? would he pretend it didn't happen? would things get weird. would they get better.

i won't do it.

but i think about it. way too often. at the worst times. in the middle of meetings. while he's talking. while he's looking at me.

(what if he's been waiting for that too?)

~ ~ ~
04

what if he doesn't actually like me?

here's the thought that keeps me up the longest.

what if he's not here because he wants to be.

what if he's here because he made a promise. to someone else. to someone who's gone. and i'm just... the obligation.

what if he doesn't even like me?

what if he's just... stuck. stuck with me. stuck in this time. stuck in this body that's not really his.

i watch him sometimes. when he laughs. when he smiles. trying to see if it's real. trying to see if there's something behind it. or if it's just... performance.

i'll never ask.

because if he says yes — if he admits he's only here out of duty — then everything falls apart. every touch. every word. every time he looks at me like i matter.

(i'd rather not know.)

~ ~ ~
05

what if i died?

dark. i know.

but i think about it. what would he do. would he be sad. would he move on. would he find someone else. would he wait for the next one. the next me.

what if i died tomorrow?

would he cry. i've never seen him cry. does he even know how anymore. after two hundred years. after everyone he's lost.

or would he just... go quiet. the way he does. the way he does when something hurts too much to talk about.

i don't want to die. but sometimes i wonder. morbid. i know. but being around someone who's outlived everyone makes you think about things.

i don't want to leave him alone.

but everyone leaves him eventually. that's the pattern. that's the curse.

(and i'm no different.)

~ ~ ~
06

what if i asked him how old he really is?

"old enough." that's what he always says.

old enough for what. old enough to be tired. old enough to have seen everything. old enough to not care about the things normal people care about.

what if i asked for a number?

two hundred and something. but something is doing a lot of work. two hundred and thirty? two hundred and seventy? three hundred?

what was he doing in the nineteen hundreds. the eighteen hundreds. the seventeen hundreds.

who did he love. who did he lose. how many times has he done this. found someone. lost someone. started over.

i can't ask.

because i don't want to know how many came before me. i don't want to know how many will come after.

(i want to believe i'm special. even if i'm not.)

~ ~ ~
07

what if i went through his things?

he has a box. an old one. wooden. with a lock that looks like it's been there for decades.

i've seen it. on his desk. in his room. when he thinks i'm not looking.

what's inside?

letters. photographs. things from philos. things from people who are long gone. things he can't let go of.

i want to know. i want to open it. i want to see the pieces of him he keeps hidden.

but i won't. because that's his. because that's private. because some things aren't mine to know.

i won't do it.

but i think about it. what i'd find. what i'd learn. what i'd never be able to unsee.

(does he have pictures of the others? the ones before me?)

~ ~ ~
08

what if one day he just... leaves?

not in a fight. not with a goodbye. just... gone.

i come back from a mission. and his things are gone. his room is empty. like he was never there.

what if that's how it ends?

no closure. no explanation. just absence.

he's done it before. left people behind. left worlds behind. what's one more.

maybe he gets tired. maybe he finds a way home. maybe he decides two hundred years is enough waiting.

i don't want that.

but i can't stop it. no one can. he's not from here. he doesn't belong here. and one day, he might decide it's time to go back.

(and i'll be here. waiting. like everyone else he left behind.)

~ ~ ~
09

what if i asked "do you love me?"

not "do you care about me." not "do you feel responsible for me."

love.

what if i asked him that?

what would he say. would he hesitate. would he look away. would he say yes because he thinks that's what i want to hear.

would he say no. would he say "i don't know." would he say "i've loved too many times to know what it means anymore."

i've practiced the question. in my head. in the mirror. when i'm alone.

"do you love me?"

but i can't say it out loud. because once it's out, i can't take it back. and his answer — whatever it is — will change everything.

so i don't ask.

but i think about it. every day. every time he looks at me. every time he says my name.

(i'm terrified of the answer. both of them.)

~ ~ ~
10

what if none of this is real?

the big one. the one i try not to think about but always comes back.

what if this is all in my head.

what if he's not real. what if the hunters aren't real. what if the wanderers are just... shadows. and i'm lying in some hospital bed somewhere. dreaming.

what if i imagined him?

someone who's been waiting for me. someone who loves me. someone who will never leave.

what if that's why he's perfect. because i made him that way.

i touch him sometimes. just to feel if he's solid. he always is. but that doesn't mean anything. dreams feel solid too.

i can't think about this.

because if i start questioning what's real, nothing matters. every mission. every fight. every moment with him.

(but what if i'm right? what if i wake up tomorrow and he's gone?)

~ ~ ~
11

what if he's been lying this whole time?

not about big things. about little things.

about why he's really here. about what he really wants. about what he really thinks of me.

what if every kind word is a script?

he's had centuries to learn how to talk to people. how to make them trust him. how to make them love him.

what if i'm just... practice.

what if he's said the same things to a hundred other people. a hundred other mes. and they all believed him too.

i watch his mouth when he talks. looking for the lie. i never find it. that's what scares me.

i'll never know.

because i can't prove a negative. and even if i could, i wouldn't want to.

(ignorance is safer. ignorance is easier.)

~ ~ ~
12

what if he can read my mind?

he's lived for centuries. he's seen things i can't imagine. he has powers i don't understand.

what if reading minds is one of them?

what if he knows. about all of this. about the list. about the questions i never ask. about the things i imagine when i'm alone.

what if he's known from the beginning.

i look at him differently now. trying to see if he knows. trying to see if he's waiting for me to say something. to admit something.

he never gives anything away. which makes it worse. because if he can read minds, he's choosing not to react. and i don't know if that's kindness or cruelty.

i don't know.

and i can't ask. because if he can't read minds, now he knows i was wondering. and if he can, he already knows everything anyway.

(so i'll just... keep thinking. and hope he's not listening.)

~ ~ ~

that's the list. for now.

there are more. there are always more. every day i think of something new. something worse. something more embarrassing.

but these are the ones that come back. the ones that wake me up at 3am. the ones i can't escape.

i won't act on any of them. i won't ask. i won't kiss him. i won't go through his things. i won't die just to see what happens.

i'll just... keep thinking.

and hope he never finds out.

(if you're reading this, xavier — no you're not. look away.)
