The Things He Doesn't Say

(i listen anyway. to the silence. to the spaces between words. to the things he thinks i won't notice.)

he talks. just not about the things that matter.

weather. missions. food. safe things. things that don't require him to open the door he's kept locked for centuries.

but i've learned to listen differently.

to the pause before he answers. to the words he starts and doesn't finish. to the silence he leaves behind like a fingerprint.

(this is what i've heard.)

i.

the silence between words

he doesn't know i'm watching

there's a specific silence he has. not the empty kind. the full kind. the kind that means there's something he wants to say but can't.

i've learned to recognize it. the way his lips part slightly. the way his eyes drop. the way he takes a breath that doesn't go anywhere.

what are you not saying?

i never ask. because if he wanted to tell me, he would. and if he can't, asking won't help.

but i notice. every time.

— — —
ii.

the way he looks at me when he thinks i'm asleep

he thinks i don't feel it

i'm not always asleep. sometimes i pretend. just to see what he does.

he sits there. doesn't move. doesn't speak. just... looks at me.

i can feel his eyes on my face. tracing. memorizing. like he's afraid i'll disappear if he looks away.

what does he see when he looks at me?

someone he's been waiting for? someone he's already lost? someone he's going to lose again?

i never open my eyes. i'm afraid of what i'll see in his.

— — —
iii.

the things he starts to say but doesn't finish

"i just..."

"i just... never mind."

he does this all the time. starts a sentence. stops. shakes his head. changes the subject.

i've started collecting them. the unfinished sentences. the words he swallows.

"i've been thinking about..." about what?

"when i first saw you, i..." you what?

"sometimes i wonder if..." if what?

what lives in the space between "i just" and "never mind"?

i'll probably never know. but i imagine. late at night. when i can't sleep.

— — —
iv.

the box he thinks i haven't noticed

i've never asked what's inside

he has a box. wooden. dark. with a lock that looks older than me.

he keeps it on his desk. in his room. he doesn't hide it — not exactly. but he doesn't talk about it either.

sometimes i catch him looking at it. his hand resting on the lid. like he's deciding whether to open it.

what's inside?

letters. photographs. things from philos. things from people who are long gone.

things he can't let go of.

i've never asked. because whatever's in there — it's not mine to know.

— — —
v.

the way he says my name

there are two versions.

the first is when we're working. when there are other people around. when he's being "professional." my name sounds like a mission objective. efficient. distant.

the second is when we're alone. when he thinks i'm not paying attention. when his voice drops. when my name sounds like something he's been holding onto for a long time.

he doesn't know i've noticed the difference.

but i have.

— — —
vi.

the nights he doesn't sleep

we're both awake. neither of us says anything.

i hear him sometimes. walking. pacing. standing by the window.

i lie in my room. on the other side of the wall. listening.

he doesn't know i'm awake too.

what does he think about, when the world is quiet and no one is watching?

the past. the people he's lost. the ones he's still waiting for.

maybe me.

i'll never ask. because then he'd know i was listening. and some things are too fragile to be spoken out loud.

— — —
vii.

the things he remembers that i don't

he's been looking at these stars longer than i've been alive

sometimes he mentions things. little things. "you used to like this." "you said that once. a long time ago."

i don't remember. any of it.

what was i like? before?

before whatever happened. before i forgot. before he found me again.

was i different? did i laugh more? was i braver? was i...

he never tells me. maybe because it hurts too much. or maybe because he wants me to be who i am now. not who i was.

— — —
viii.

the way he touches me — carefully

he always pulls back first

he's careful. too careful. like he's afraid i'll break. or like he's afraid he'll break.

his fingers brush mine. then pull away. he rests his hand on my shoulder. but never for too long.

what is he afraid of?

hurting me? wanting me too much? losing me again?

maybe all of it.

i want to tell him it's okay. that i'm not glass. that he can hold on.

but i don't. because i'm afraid too.

— — —
ix.

the things he apologizes for (that aren't his fault)

"sorry."

he says it too much. for things that aren't his fault. for things he couldn't control. for things that happened centuries before i was born.

"sorry i couldn't protect you."

"sorry i wasn't there."

"sorry i'm like this."

what is he apologizing for, really?

for surviving. for outliving everyone. for being the one who's still here.

i never say "it's okay." because it's not okay. and i think he knows that.

— — —
x.

the things he laughs at (that aren't funny)

his smile doesn't always reach his eyes

he has a laugh that isn't a laugh. it's a deflection. a shield. a way of saying "i don't want to talk about this" without saying it.

i've learned to tell the difference. the real laugh is quiet. almost reluctant. like he's surprised it escaped.

the fake laugh is louder. faster. over too quickly.

what is he hiding behind the sound?

pain. grief. fear. the things he can't put into words.

i don't call him out on it. everyone deserves their armor.

— — —
xi.

the things he never asks me

he never asks. i never offer.

he doesn't ask me about my past. about my fears. about what i want.

not because he doesn't care. but because he's afraid of the answers. or maybe because he already knows.

what would he ask, if he let himself?

"do you think about leaving?"

"are you scared of me?"

"will you stay?"

he never asks. so i never answer. and the questions hang between us, invisible and heavy.

— — —
xii.

the one thing he said once. just once.

he never said it again. i never asked him to.

it was late. we were sitting on the roof. watching the stars.

he thought i was asleep.

he said "i'm tired." not physically. the other kind of tired. the kind that comes from carrying too much for too long.

then, quieter: "but you make it worth it."

i've never told him i heard.

because if i did, he might stop saying things when he thinks i'm not listening. and those are the only times he's honest.

so i pretend. and i wait. and i listen.

— — —

that's what i've heard.

in the pauses. in the unfinished sentences. in the things he thinks he's hiding.

he doesn't know i'm paying attention. he thinks i only hear what he says out loud.

but i've learned. silence speaks too.

(and i've been listening all along.)
