How to Live with Sylus

A guide no one asked for — 47 things I learned the hard way

Disclaimer: This guide is based on personal experience. The author is still alive. Barely. Sylus does not endorse this guide. Sylus does not know this guide exists. Please keep it that way.

I have been living with Sylus for approximately 47 days.

I have learned 47 things.

This is not a coincidence. This is a cry for help.

If you are reading this, you are probably about to live with him. Or you are trapped in a lease. Or you made a series of poor decisions, like me.

This guide will not teach you how to survive Sylus.

No one survives Sylus.

This guide will teach you how to coexist with him. There is a difference.

— — —
PART ONE

The Basics · Things That Are Black

Everything he owns. No exceptions.
01
Everything he owns is black.
His clothes. His towels. His coffee mug. His phone case. His car. His soul. (The last one is a joke. I think.) Do not buy him anything that is not black. He will not say anything. He will simply never use it. And he will leave it somewhere you can see it. As a reminder.
02
Do not touch his things.
This includes things that are not his but are black. He has claimed them. You do not know which ones. Assume everything black belongs to Sylus. This includes your black hoodie that went missing last week. It is his now. Mourn it and move on.
03
He has a favorite spot in every room.
You will not know which spot until you sit there. Then he will stand behind you. Silently. Until you move. Learn the spots. Memorize them. Never sit there.
04
He does not explain himself.
If you ask "why," he will look at you. That is the answer. The look means "I do not owe you an explanation." He is correct. You will learn to stop asking.
PART TWO

The Kitchen · Where He Stands

He's not cooking. He's not waiting. He's just there.
05
He stands in the kitchen at 3am.
Not cooking. Not eating. Not getting water. Just standing. In the dark. You will walk in. You will freeze. He will turn his head slightly. Neither of you will speak. You will back out slowly. He will still be there when you check again in an hour. Accept this.
06
Do not ask what he is doing in the kitchen.
Refer to Rule 04. The look will be worse in the dark.
07
The coffee machine is his domain.
You may use it. You may not change its settings. You may not leave it empty. You may not leave it dirty. You may not look at it wrong. He will know.
08
He does not cook. But he has opinions about your cooking.
He will not compliment you. He will stand in the doorway. Watching. When you are done, he will say "it's edible." That is a five-star review in Sylus language. Treasure it.
09
The champagne is not for sharing.
There is a bottle in the fridge. It has been there for weeks. Do not touch it. It is not a decoration. It is not an invitation. It is Sylus's champagne. It will remain in the fridge until Sylus decides otherwise. This will be never.
PART THREE

The Crow · His Name is Mephisto

He has more authority than you. Accept this.
10
Mephisto has more authority than you.
The crow lives with you now. He sits on furniture. He watches you. He reports to Sylus. You do not know what he reports. You will never know. Be nice to the crow.
11
Do not try to pet Mephisto.
He is not a pet. He is a colleague. A superior colleague. He will let you know if he wants attention. (He never wants attention.)
12
Mephisto remembers everything.
The thing you said last week. The thing you ate at 2am. The thing you broke. Mephisto knows. Mephisto remembers. Mephisto will not tell you he remembers. He will just look at you. With his tiny crow eyes. Full of judgment.
13
Talk to Mephisto. He might talk back.
Not with words. But he will tilt his head. He will caw at certain moments. He is communicating. You will not understand what. But he appreciates the effort.
14
Sylus talks to Mephisto more than he talks to you.
This is not personal. The crow has known him longer. The crow has seen things you will never see. Accept your place in the hierarchy. It goes: Sylus → Mephisto → everyone else.
PART FOUR

The Schedule · There Is No Schedule

He is awake. You should be asleep. He doesn't care.
15
He does not sleep. Or he sleeps at impossible times.
You will never see him sleep. You will never know if he sleeps. You will find him awake at 3am. You will find him awake at 6am. You will find him awake at noon. He is always awake. Or he is always asleep. You cannot tell the difference.
16
Do not comment on his sleep schedule.
He will say "I sleep when I need to." You do not know when that is. He does not know when that is. The conversation ends there.
17
He is always awake when you need something.
Middle of the night. Emergency. Panic. He will be there. You do not know how. You do not ask. You just say thank you. He will nod. He will leave. He will not mention it in the morning.
18
He is never awake when you want to show him something fun.
The meme. The video. The thing that made you laugh. He will be asleep. Or pretending to be asleep. You cannot tell. You will never show him the meme. The meme will die in your camera roll.
PART FIVE

Communication · Silent, But Loud

This look means approximately 47 different things.
19
He speaks in looks.
There is "the look" (you did something wrong), "the stare" (you are about to do something wrong), and "the glance" (he is judging you but won't say it). Learn the difference. Your survival depends on it.
20
"Okay" means approximately 47 different things.
- "Okay" (I agree)
- "Okay." (I do not agree but will not argue)
- "Okay..." (You are wrong)
- "Okay." (period, very important) (You are very wrong)
- "Okay." (with eye contact) (I am going to remember this)
- "Okay." (no eye contact) (I have already forgotten this)
- "Ok." (abbreviated) (I am annoyed)
- "K." (Run.)
21
He will never say "I'm sorry."
He will fix the thing. He will replace the thing. He will stand near you until you feel better. He will not say the words. Do not wait for them.
22
He will never say "I love you."
He will put a blanket on you when you fall asleep on the couch. He will remember how you take your coffee. He will be there when you need him. He will not say the words. Do not wait for them either.
23
Compliments sound like insults. Insults sound like nothing.
"That's not terrible" = "That's good."
"You're less annoying today" = "I enjoy your company."
"I don't hate this" = "I love this."
Silence = "You have disappointed me."
PART SIX

The Bathroom · The Forbidden Zone

No one knows what happens in there. No one asks.
24
The bathroom is his territory.
You may use the other bathroom. This one belongs to Sylus. You do not know why. You do not ask. There are products in there. Mysterious products. Black bottles. You will never touch them.
25
He takes showers at strange hours.
3am. 5am. 11am on a Tuesday. You will hear the water. You will not investigate. What happens in the bathroom stays in the bathroom.
26
Do not use his towel.
The black towel. The one that looks like all the other black towels. It is his. You will know because you will use it and then he will appear. Behind you. Silent. Regret will wash over you faster than the towel ever did.
PART SEVEN

The Things He Doesn't Say · The Silences

The silence is not empty. It's full of things he won't say.
27
He notices everything. He says nothing.
Your new haircut. The thing you're anxious about. The fact that you haven't eaten today. He knows. He will not mention it. He will just... be there. With food. Or coffee. Or silence. The silence helps.
28
He leaves things where you will find them.
Your keys. Your wallet. The thing you lost. He found them. He put them on your desk. He will not tell you. You will find them and wonder. He will pretend he doesn't know what you're talking about.
29
He stays in the room when you're sad.
Not close. Not talking. Just... present. In the corner. Reading. Or staring at the wall. He is not ignoring you. He is waiting. In case you need him. You won't say you need him. He won't ask. But he stays.
30
He leaves when you're better.
You will look up. He will be gone. You will not remember him leaving. You will not know how long he was there. You will feel better. That is all that matters.
PART EIGHT

The Possibilities · What Could Be

??
Sometimes, he smiles.
It's rare. It's quick. You might miss it if you blink. It happens when you're not looking. When you say something honest. When you laugh at something stupid. When you're just being you. He smiles. Then he stops. He will deny it if you mention it. Do not mention it.
??
Sometimes, he stays.
Not in the corner. On the couch. Next to you. Not touching. Just... close. He falls asleep. You don't move. You don't breathe too loud. You don't want him to wake up and leave. He will wake up. He will leave. But he stayed. That's the important part.
??
Sometimes, he lets you see him.
Not the Sylus everyone sees. The one underneath. The tired one. The lonely one. The one who stands in the kitchen at 3am because he doesn't know what else to do. He shows you. For a moment. Then the mask goes back on. You pretend you didn't see. He pretends he didn't show you. But you both know.
PART NINE

The Final Rules · The Ones That Matter

47
He is not as scary as he seems.
He is scarier. But also less scary. It's complicated. He has walls. High ones. Thick ones. You will not climb them. You will not break them down. You will find a door. It will be small. It will be hidden. You will knock. He might open it. He might not. That's his choice. Respect it.
48
He is worth it.
The silence. The stares. The 3am kitchen appearances. The crow that judges you. All of it. He is worth it. Not because he's easy. Because he's trying. In his own way. In the only way he knows how. He is trying. And that is everything.
49
You are worth it too.
Don't forget that. Living with someone like Sylus is hard. It's confusing. It's lonely sometimes. But you are here. You are trying too. That matters. Don't lose yourself in his darkness. Bring your own light. He needs it more than he'll ever admit.

Living with Sylus is not easy. It will never be easy.

But it is not impossible.

And sometimes, in the quiet moments, when he's not looking, when Mephisto is asleep, when the apartment is still — you will realize that you wouldn't trade it for anything.

Not even for silence.

Not even for normal.

Not even for a roommate who doesn't stand in the kitchen at 3am.

Because that roommate wouldn't be Sylus.

And somehow, inexplicably, you want Sylus.

— someone who is still learning, every day
