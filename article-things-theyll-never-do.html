Things They'll Never Do

a completely unserious list about four very serious men

let's be honest. we love these disaster men. but they have rules. invisible lines they refuse to cross.

this is a list of those lines.

(disclaimer: they'll break every single one of them for you. they just won't admit it.)

✨
xavier
the silent watcher
01
he will never say "i love you" first
he'll wait. he'll wait two hundred years. he'll wait until you say it, and then he'll smile that small smile and say it back like he's been holding it in his whole life. because he has.
02
he will never wake you up
if you're sleeping, he'll just watch. for hours. he'll count your eyelashes. he'll memorize the way your hair falls across your face. he'll let you sleep until the sun goes down. waking you would require explaining why he was staring.
03
he will never admit he's lonely
"i'm fine. i'm just tired." two hundred years of being fine. two hundred years of tired. he'll never say the word "lonely." but you can see it. in the way he follows you with his eyes. in the way he finds excuses to be in the same room.
04
he will never tell you what he's thinking
not because he doesn't want to. because he's been alone for so long he forgot how to put thoughts into words. he'll try. he'll start a sentence. "i was thinking about..." and then he'll stop. "never mind." you'll never mind. but you'll wonder.
05
he will never ask you to stay
he'll watch you leave. every time. he'll stand at the door. he'll raise his hand in a small wave. he won't say "don't go." but his eyes will. and you'll pretend not to notice. because if you did, you'd never leave. and he knows that.
✦ ✦ ✦
but for you, he will...
stay awake past midnight just to hear your voice. learn to say things without words. wait. always wait. that's the one thing he'll never stop doing.
❄️
zayne
the healer who can't heal himself
01
he will never take a day off
there's always another patient. another surgery. another life to save. he'll work through fevers. through exhaustion. through the ache in his chest that he refuses to name. the hospital would fall apart without him. that's what he tells himself.
02
he will never admit he's tired
"i'm fine." three words. he says them so often they've lost meaning. he's not fine. his coffee is cold. his eyes have shadows. but he's the doctor. doctors don't get tired. doctors don't need sleep. doctors are fine.
03
he will never let you see him cry
he's seen too much death to cry. that's what he tells himself. but the tears come anyway. in the locker room. in his car. in the dark. he wipes them away before anyone sees. especially you.
04
he will never ask for help
he's the one who helps. that's his job. his identity. his entire personality. asking for help would mean admitting he's not strong enough. and he can't afford to be weak. not when people's lives depend on him.
05
he will never stop blaming himself
the ones he couldn't save. the complications he didn't see coming. the patients who trusted him and didn't wake up. he carries them all. every single one. he'll never say their names out loud. but he remembers.
✦ ✦ ✦
but for you, he will...
pretend the coffee isn't cold. stay on the phone even when he has nothing to say. let you sit in his office while he works. he won't admit he likes the company. but he does.
🎨
rafayel
the performer who forgot the script
01
he will never show you an unfinished painting
not because he's vain. because his unfinished work is where the cracks show. where he doubts himself. where the colors don't match the vision in his head. he'll only show you the finished product. the mask. the performance.
02
he will never admit he's scared
scared of being forgotten. scared of being alone. scared that one day you'll wake up and realize he's not worth the effort. he'll laugh instead. he'll make a joke. he'll deflect. because fear is ugly, and rafayel is never ugly.
03
he will never say "i need you"
he'll say "i want you." he'll say "stay." he'll say "don't leave." but never "i need you." because needing someone means being vulnerable. and vulnerability is the one thing he's spent centuries learning to hide.
04
he will never let you see him cry
he cries at sunsets. at old movies. at songs that remind him of the ocean. but never in front of you. never where you can see. he'll turn away. he'll wipe his eyes. he'll blame the dust. you'll pretend to believe him.
05
he will never stop painting you
even if you ask him to stop. even if you never want to see another portrait. he'll keep painting. in secret. in the dark. on napkins. on receipts. your face appears everywhere. he can't help it. you're the only thing worth painting.
✦ ✦ ✦
but for you, he will...
let you see the studio. let you watch him paint. let you see him unfiltered, unpolished, unperformed. he'll still complain. that's part of the act. but he'll let you stay.
🌑
sylus
the king who hates kneeling
01
he will never say "please"
kings don't say please. kings command. kings demand. kings take. he'll ask. he'll tell you what he wants. but please? that word died a long time ago. along with other soft things.
02
he will never admit he's scared of losing you
he'll say "be careful." he'll say "don't do anything stupid." he'll send mephisto to watch over you. but he'll never say "i'm terrified." because fear is weakness. and sylus is never weak.
03
he will never apologize
not for the things he's done. not for the person he is. not for the blood on his hands. he's not sorry. he can't afford to be. regret is a luxury, and he's never had the time.
04
he will never tell you how much he thinks about you
you'd be embarrassed. he'd be embarrassed. it's easier to pretend you're just another variable. another person to protect. another investment. but mephisto knows. mephisto sees the way he says your name when you're not there.
05
he will never stop
stop protecting you. stop watching over you. stop being the monster so you don't have to be. it's not a choice anymore. it's just who he is. and he'll never apologize for that either.
✦ ✦ ✦
but for you, he will...
say your name like it's the only word that matters. send mephisto to check on you. pretend he doesn't care. badly. he's not as good at pretending as he thinks.
"they'll never say it. but they'll show you. every single day."

xavier won't say "i love you." but he'll wait. forever.

zayne won't say "i need help." but he'll let you stay.

rafayel won't say "i'm scared." but he'll hold your hand.

sylus won't say "please." but he'll ask.

in their own broken, complicated, impossible ways — they're trying.

(and maybe that's enough.)

— a hunter who's learned to listen to what they don't say
