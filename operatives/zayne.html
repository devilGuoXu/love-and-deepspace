A world-renowned cardiac surgeon and a powerful hunter. Zayne’s ice Evol is both a curse and a gift—it allows him to halt life or preserve it. His fate is eternally tied to a "prophecy of snow," and he has spent years researching a cure for the Protocore syndrome that threatens humanity.
