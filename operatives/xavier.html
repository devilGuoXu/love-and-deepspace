01. BIOGRAPHY & CHRONO ARCHIVE

Xavier is a mysterious veteran hunter known for his sleepy demeanor and unparalleled swordsmanship. Although he appears youthful, historical records from the planet Philos hint at a legacy spanning centuries. He is not just a hunter; he is a protector of a forgotten timeline. His light Evol allows him to manipulate photons, creating illusions or solidifying light into lethal blades. He often falls asleep in unusual places, but his combat instincts never fade.

02. TACTICAL SKILL BREAKDOWN

Basic: Starshade Strike

Rapid sword strikes dealing Light DMG. Final hit triggers a "Starlight Mark".

Resonance: Infinite Ray

Xavier and Hunter summon a barrage of light beams, reducing enemy mobility by 50%.

Ardent Oath: Solar Eclipse

A massive horizontal slash that clears all Starlight Marks to deal bonus explosive DMG.
