01. ARTISTIC & LORE ARCHIVE

Rafayel is a world-renowned genius painter, famous for his vibrant use of color and "living" textures. However, the art world remains oblivious to his true identity: the God of Lemuria, the sovereign of a civilization swallowed by the tides thousands of years ago. His studio, Mo Art, serves as a sanctuary and a facade for his relentless search for the truth behind the Great Flood.

His Evol manifests as "Abyssal Fire"—a paradoxical flame that burns underwater and refuses to be extinguished by conventional means. Unlike others, his energy is tied to his emotions and his bloodline. He is a man of contradictions: acting like a pampered, dramatic artist one moment, and revealing a lethal, calculating hunter's instinct the next. He claims he met the Hunter in a past life, promising that even if the ocean dries up, their contract remains eternal.

02. TACTICAL SKILL BREAKDOWN

Basic Attack: Crimson Ripple

Swings his brush-blade to deal 4 stages of Fire DMG. The final hit creates a "Flame Petal" that explodes on contact, dealing AoE damage.

Resonance: Flowing Heat

Rafayel and Hunter summon a whirlpool of fire. Enemies are pulled to the center and inflicted with 'Deep Sea Burn', losing 3% HP every second.

Ardent Oath: Abyssal Rebirth

Unleashes the full power of Lemuria. The battlefield turns into an ocean of fire, dealing 1450% DMG and resetting all skill cooldowns.
