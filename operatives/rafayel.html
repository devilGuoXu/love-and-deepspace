01. ARTISTIC & LORE ARCHIVE

Rafayel is a genius artist whose paintings are sought after by collectors worldwide. However, his vibrant colors hide a dark origin: he is the God of Lemuria, a sunken civilization. His fire Evol is unique, manifesting as "Sea Fire" that consumes everything it touches. He is unpredictable, passionate, and deeply sentimental about the lost city of the deep.
