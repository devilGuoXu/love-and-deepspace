Shop & Exchange Guide

Every currency, every exchange, ranked by value. Never waste a single coin.

Golden Rule: Never buy something from one shop if you could earn it for free elsewhere. Always check acquisition sources before spending premium currency.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Gem Shop (Paid Currency)

Best Value: Limited banner tickets (blue). Never buy standard banner tickets (free ones are plentiful). Monthly Pass: Best value in the game. 3,000 gems over 30 days + daily rewards. Battle Pass: Good value if you play daily. Bonus resources + exclusive memories. Stamina Refresh: Only refresh once per day (50 gems). Second refresh is poor value.

Resource Exchange (Free Currency)

Priority Order: Memory XP > Gold > Upgrade Materials > Boss Materials. Don't buy XP from the exchange unless you need it urgently 閳?farm it instead.

Event Shops

Always Buy: Limited banner tickets, exclusive memories, SSR shards. Buy If Needed: Memory XP, ascension materials, protocore enhancers. Skip: Gold (farm it), common materials (drop from normal gameplay).

Currency Economy and Conversion Rates

Understanding the currency conversion rates helps you evaluate whether a shop deal is actually good value. Gems are the premium currency earned through gameplay at a rate of approximately 6,000-8,000 per month for active F2P players. Gold is the basic currency earned from all activities. The conversion rate between gems and gold through standard exchange is 1 gem = 100 gold, but never convert gems to gold directly 閳?the exchange rate is intentionally poor. Event currency earned during limited events converts to standard resources at better rates than the permanent shops.

The Stardust Exchange offers the best conversion rate for free-to-play players. Accumulate stardust by salvaging unwanted memories and completing daily missions. Monthly refresh items in the Stardust Exchange should be purchased in this order: limited banner tickets (highest value), SSR shard boxes (second highest), memory enhancement materials (third), and gold (last). The monthly refresh occurs on the 1st of each month 閳?purchase the limited tickets immediately as they are the only source of guaranteed free pulls each month.

Limited Pack Value Analysis

Limited packs in the shop offer time-sensitive bundles that provide better value than standard purchases. Evaluate each pack using the "gem equivalent" method: calculate how many gems the contained items would cost if purchased individually. A pack is good value if its contents are worth 150% or more of the gem price. The weekly resource bundle (containing stamina potions and gold) is generally the best value regular pack. The outfit packs are the worst value 閳?they cost the same as a 10-pull worth of gems for purely cosmetic items.

Holiday and anniversary packs are the exceptions to normal pack evaluation. These packs often contain exclusive items that cannot be obtained elsewhere, making their value difficult to quantify. The anniversary pack typically offers three times the normal value and is the only pack that is a must-purchase for all players. Anniversary packs usually appear once per year with a 7-day purchase window. Save at least 500 paid gems in reserve for anniversary packs when you know the event window is approaching.

Limited Pack Purchase Timing

Limited packs in the shop follow a monthly release schedule. The first week of each month features the monthly resource bundle. The second week features the enhancement material bundle. The third week features the protocore bundle. The fourth week features the random bundle with varying contents. The best value limited pack is the monthly resource bundle in week one, which provides stamina potions and gold at a 40% discount compared to purchasing these resources individually through stamina refreshes.

The Stardust Exchange refreshes monthly on the 1st. Limited banner tickets are capped at 5 per month 閳?purchase immediately. SSR shard boxes are capped at 3 per month 閳?purchase second. Enhancement materials have no cap.

The Bottom Line: Monthly pass is the only purchase you need. Limited tickets > everything else. Skip the standard banner pulls. Event shops are the best resource-to-stamina value in the game 閳?always clear the limited tickets and exclusive items.
