Phone System Overview

The phone system simulates real-time communication with each operative. Operatives will call or text you based on story progression, Affinity level, time of day, and special events. Calls are fully voiced conversations that play in real-time — you can choose dialogue responses that affect the operative's reaction and the Affinity EXP reward. Text messages are written conversations with branching dialogue trees that unlock overtime.

The phone interface is accessible from the main menu. Incoming calls and messages show a notification badge. Missed calls can be returned from the call log, and unread messages are stored in the conversation history. The system tracks your conversation history for each operative, creating a persistent record of your relationship progression.

Phone Calls

Phone calls are the premium phone interaction — each call is fully voiced in your selected language (Chinese, Japanese, or English), runs 2-5 minutes, and features dynamic dialogue trees based on your choices. The voice actors deliver natural conversational performances that feel like genuine phone calls rather than scripted audio. Background noise varies by operative — Xavier calls from quiet observatory spaces, Zayne from his clinic (occasional hospital PA system in the background), Rafayel from art studios or beach locations, Sylus from the Onychinus headquarters with distant city ambience.

Call quality depends on your response choices. Choosing responses that match the operative's personality and current mood results in warmer conversations and higher Affinity EXP rewards. Choosing dismissive or inappropriate responses results in shorter, cooler conversations with reduced rewards. The system remembers your conversation history — operatives may reference previous calls in future interactions.

Text Messages

Text messages are more frequent than calls and trigger from a wider range of conditions. Messages can be single exchanges or multi-part conversations that span multiple logins. Some messages include image attachments (selfies, location photos, item photos) that add visual context. Messages have a 24-hour window to respond before they expire — unresponded messages count as missed and may affect future conversation availability.

Message responses use a multiple-choice system similar to calls. The "best" response (highest Affinity EXP) is typically the one that aligns with the operative's personality: supportive for Xavier, analytical for Zayne, playful for Rafayel, direct for Sylus. Each operative also has hidden affinity triggers — specific responses that unlock bonus dialogue or accelerate future conversation triggers.

Trigger Conditions

Phone interactions trigger from: Main Story chapter completions (guaranteed call/message after major story beats), Affinity milestones (new interactions unlock at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 75, 100), Memory collection milestones (obtaining certain SSR memories triggers related conversations), Real-time events (holiday greetings, birthday calls, seasonal messages), and Random proximity triggers (visiting the Dawnbreak Base, completing combat missions with the operative).

Some calls and messages are one-time only and will be permanently missed if not answered within their availability window. Event-triggered calls (birthdays, anniversaries) are particularly important to catch — they contain exclusive dialogue that doesn't repeat and grant larger Affinity EXP bonuses.

Call & Message Rewards

Each unique phone interaction rewards: Affinity EXP (30-200 depending on conversation quality and choices), Gems (5-30 per interaction), and in rare cases exclusive items (base decorations, profile frames, titles). The total gem payout from completing all phone interactions for a single operative is approximately 150-200 gems. For all four operatives, that's 600-800 gems from phone content alone — a significant amount for passive content.

Complete Conversation Guide

Optimal Responses
  • Xavier: Warm, supportive, patient
  • Zayne: Respectful, logical, curious
  • Rafayel: Playful, appreciative of art
  • Sylus: Direct, confident, loyal
  • Always match their current mood
Avoid These
  • Dismissing Xavier's feelings
  • Doubting Zayne's expertise
  • Ignoring Rafayel's art
  • Showing fear to Sylus
  • Being rude or impatient

Tip: Set aside 10 minutes each day to check your phone. Missed calls can be returned from the call log, but missed messages expire after 24 hours. The notification badge on the phone icon tells you if you have pending interactions. Check the phone after completing story chapters or Affinity milestones — these are guaranteed trigger moments for new content.

Summary: The phone system is Love and Deepspace's most immersive relationship feature, with fully voiced calls and branching text message conversations. Triggers come from story progression, Affinity milestones, and real-time events. Optimal responses align with each operative's personality for maximum Affinity EXP and warmer conversations. Check your phone daily, respond to messages within 24 hours, and don't miss event-triggered calls for exclusive dialogue and rewards.
