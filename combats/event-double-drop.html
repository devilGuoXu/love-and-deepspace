EVENT ARCHIVE — DOUBLE DROPS

Resource Double Drop Strategy

Double drop events double your reward per stamina spent. They are the single most stamina-efficient activity in Deepspace. If you waste a double drop event, you waste weeks of natural stamina regen.

ENTRY_#EVT_072026.07.02DOUBLE_DROP

What They Are: Weekend or event-tied periods (usually 48-72 hours) where specific domains drop double rewards. Common rotations: Memory XP Crystal Domain (Saturday), Protocore Dust Domain (Sunday), Gold Domain (Monday), or all three during major events. The double applies to ALL drops, not just base drops — this includes bonus drops from your team bonuses.

Stamina Pot Banking

The most important preparation for double drops is banking stamina pots. This single habit separates optimized accounts from casual ones.

1
Stop Using Medium & Large Pots
Starting 1 week before an expected double drop event, stop using medium and large stamina pots. Use only your natural stamina regen (240/day).
2
Collect but Don't Claim
Collect all stamina pots from mail and events but do not claim them. Let them sit in your mailbox (they expire after 7 days — mark your calendar to not lose them).
3
Target: 10+ Medium Pots
Bank 10+ medium stamina pots (600+ extra stamina) for the double drop window. 600 stamina banked = 1,200 stamina of normal farming. This is the closest thing to "free pulls" in Deepspace.

Domain Priority

Not all domains are worth farming equally during double drops. Here is the definitive priority order.

#1 PRIORITY — 70% STAMINA
Protocore Dust Domain
Protocores are the biggest long-term bottleneck. Double dust = double protocore rolls = faster gear progression. Farm this domain exclusively during double drops unless you are completely set on protocores. This is the domain that will accelerate your account the most over time.
#2 PRIORITY — 30% STAMINA
Memory XP Crystal Domain
If your protocores are in good shape, the XP domain is next best. Memory leveling costs are enormous at higher levels (200k+ XP from level 50 to 60). Every double drop run here saves you hours of normal farming.
#3 PRIORITY — 0% STAMINA
Gold Domain
Only farm gold if you are currently broke and need to level something immediately. Gold accumulates naturally over time and is never the true bottleneck for account progression. Resist the temptation.

Rule of thumb: 70% of your double drop stamina goes to protocore dust. 30% to XP crystals. 0% to gold. Stick to this split and your account will grow 2x faster than someone who farms gold.

Natural Stamina Scheduling

Double drop events usually run Friday 5:00 → Monday 5:00 server time. Proper timing maximizes your runs.

Thu
Use All Stamina Before Reset
You will not miss double drops — they start Friday. Empty your stamina bar so it begins refilling for Friday.
Fri
Wait for Event Activation
Do NOT use stamina before the event starts (check the event countdown). Wait for the double drop to activate, then spend all stamina + banked pots on your priority domain.
Sat-Sun
Full Stamina Dump
Repeat — full stamina dump on priority domains both days. Use all banked pots strategically across these two days.
Mon
Pre-Reset Sprint
If the event ends at 5:00, use your stamina before reset. Any leftover banked pots should be used here for one last run.

Total stamina available: 240 natural × 3 days = 720 + 10 banked medium pots (600) + 5 large pots (300) if you have them = 1,620 stamina. This is enough for 81 domain runs at double drops = 162 runs worth of rewards.

Pro Tip: If a double drop event overlaps with a story event, your stamina allocation changes. Story event currency is also valuable. Split: 50% stamina on double drop domain, 50% on story event farming. Do not ignore the story event entirely — its exclusive SSR shards are also time-limited. Use the 50/50 rule for overlapping events.

Team Optimization for Double Drops

Your team selection affects double drop efficiency. Small bonuses compound significantly at 2x rates.

Protocore Domain
Bring matching element character
Each domain has a recommended element — matching it gives a 10% drop rate bonus (which doubles to 20% during the event). Farm protocores for the character you are building.
XP Domain
Bring lowest-level operatives
The XP domain gives team XP proportional to damage dealt — lower-level operatives gain more from the same run. Rotate your bench through here.
Gold Domain
Bring Sylus (gold find passive)
If you must farm gold, bring Sylus for his passive gold find bonus. Every little bonus multiplies with double drops. But remember — gold should be your last priority.

DO & DON'T Summary

DO
  • Bank stamina pots for 1 week before double drops
  • Farm Protocore Dust domain as #1 priority (70%)
  • Target 1,620 total stamina (81 runs)
  • Match domain element for bonus drop rate
  • Use 50/50 split if overlapping with story events
  • Schedule around the Friday→Monday window
DON'T
  • Don't let stamina cap during the event
  • Don't use stamina before the event activates Friday
  • Don't waste double drops on the gold domain
  • Don't use medium/large pots before the bank week
  • Don't ignore story events if they overlap
  • Don't forget mail expiry on banked pots (7 days)

Summary: Bank stamina pots for 1 week before double drops. Protocore Dust > XP > Gold. 70/30 split. 1,620 stamina target (81 runs). Match domain element for bonus. Do not let stamina cap during the event. 50/50 split if overlapping with story events.
