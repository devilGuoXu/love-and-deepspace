EVENT ARCHIVE — DOUBLE DROPS

Resource Double Drop Strategy

Double drop events double your reward per stamina spent. They are the single most stamina-efficient activity in Deepspace. If you waste a double drop event, you waste weeks of natural stamina regen.

ENTRY_#EVT_072026.07.02DOUBLE_DROP

What They Are: Weekend or event-tied periods (usually 48-72 hours) where specific domains drop double rewards. Common rotations: Memory XP Crystal Domain (Saturday), Protocore Dust Domain (Sunday), Gold Domain (Monday), or all three during major events. The double applies to ALL drops, not just base drops — this includes bonus drops from your team bonuses.

Stamina Pot Banking

The most important preparation for double drops is banking stamina pots. How: Starting 1 week before an expected double drop event, stop using medium and large stamina pots. Use only your natural stamina regen (240/day). Collect all stamina pots from mail and events but do not claim them. Let them sit in your mailbox (they expire after 7 days — mark your calendar). Target: Bank 10+ medium stamina pots (600+ extra stamina) for the double drop window. Why: Double drops effectively double your stamina's value. 600 stamina banked = 1,200 stamina of normal farming. This is the closest thing to "free pulls" in Deepspace.

Domain Priority

#1 Priority — Protocore Dust Domain: Protocores are the biggest long-term bottleneck. Double dust = double protocore rolls = faster gear progression. Farm this domain exclusively during double drops unless you are completely set on protocores. #2 — Memory XP Crystal Domain: If your protocores are in good shape, the XP domain is next. Memory leveling costs are enormous at higher levels (200k+ XP from level 50 to 60). #3 — Gold Domain: Only farm gold if you are currently broke and need to level something immediately. Gold accumulates naturally over time and is never the true bottleneck. Rule of thumb: 70% of your double drop stamina goes to protocore dust. 30% to XP crystals. 0% to gold.

Natural Stamina Scheduling

Double drop events usually run Friday 5:00 → Monday 5:00 server time. Schedule: Thursday: Use all stamina before reset (you will not miss double drops — they start Friday). Friday: Do NOT use stamina before the event starts (check the event countdown). Wait for the double drop to activate, then spend all stamina + banked pots on your priority domain. Saturday & Sunday: Repeat — full stamina dump on priority domains. Monday: If the event ends at 5:00, use your stamina before reset. Total stamina available: 240 natural × 3 days = 720 + 10 banked medium pots (600) + 5 large pots (300) if you have them = 1,620 stamina. This is enough for 81 domain runs at double drops = 162 runs worth of rewards.

Pro Tip: If a double drop event overlaps with a story event, your stamina allocation changes. Story event currency is also valuable. Split: 50% stamina on double drop domain, 50% on story event farming. Do not ignore the story event entirely — its exclusive SSR shards are also time-limited. Use the 50/50 rule for overlapping events.

Team Optimization for Double Drops

Your team selection affects double drop efficiency. Protocore Domain: Bring the character whose protocores you are farming. Each domain has a recommended element — matching it gives a 10% drop rate bonus (which doubles to 20% during the event). XP Domain: Bring your lowest-level operatives to share XP. The XP domain gives team XP proportional to damage dealt — lower-level operatives gain more from the same run. Gold Domain: Bring Sylus for his passive gold find bonus (if he has one). Every little bonus multiplies with double drops.

Summary: Bank stamina pots for 1 week before double drops. Protocore Dust > XP > Gold. 70/30 split. 1,620 stamina target (81 runs). Match domain element for bonus. Do not let stamina cap during the event. 50/50 split if overlapping with story events.
