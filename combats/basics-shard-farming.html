Memory Shard Farming Guide

Where to get shards for specific memories — stages, exchange shops, events, and weekly caps.

ENTRY_#0572026.07.01FARMING

Shards vs Full Memories: Shards are fragments that combine to form memories. 30 shards = 1 SR memory. 50 shards = 1 SSR memory (or fewer for specific limited events). Shard farming is the primary way to rank up memories without relying on gacha luck.

Shard Sources by Type

Standard Banner SSRs: Main story stages (Chapter 3, 6, 9, 12 hard mode) drop specific SSR shards. Max 3 runs per day per stage. Estimated time to R1: 25-35 days of daily farming. Limited SSRs: Only available during their banner event stages. After the banner ends, no new shards can be obtained. Plan accordingly — if you want a limited SSR at R2+, farm its event stage daily during the banner period.

SR Memories: Drop from standard stages (Chapter 1-12 hard mode, various nodes). SR shards are much more common — you'll naturally accumulate enough for R6 over 2-3 months of casual play. Never intentionally farm SR shards.

Exchange Shop Sources

Three exchange shops carry shards: Battle Pass Shop (monthly refresh, 30 SSR shards per month of your choice — best value). Event Shop (varies, typically 20-50 shards of the featured memory). Stardust Exchange (convert duplicate memories into shard currency — only use for SSRs you need).

Priority: Battle Pass SSR shards always (best value per currency). Event shop shards if you're close to a rank-up threshold. Stardust exchange only for your main DPS SSR.

Weekly Caps & Efficiency

Hard mode stages have a weekly cap of 21 runs per stage (3 runs × 7 days). Plan which shards you need most each week. If you need shards for 2 different SSRs, alternate days (3 runs SSR A, 3 runs SSR B). Never spend diamonds on stamina refresh for shard farming — the cost per shard is too high (roughly 30 diamonds per shard at refresh rates).

Key Insight: Shard farming is a long-term investment. 3 weeks of daily farming = ~90 shards = 1 SSR rank. That's roughly 60 stamina per day spent on shards. Over a month, that's 1,800 stamina. Compare this to pulling a dupe from gacha (roughly 7,000 diamonds worth of pulls on average). Shard farming is 4x more efficient than pulling for rank-ups.

Summary: Farm standard SSR shards from hard stages (3/day each). Limited shards only during banners. Battle Pass shop = best value. Never refresh stamina for shards. 3 weeks = 1 rank-up. Shard farming beats pulling for rank-ups 4:1.
