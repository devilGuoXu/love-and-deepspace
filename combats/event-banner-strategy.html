Banner & Rate-Up Strategy

Limited banners are the primary way to acquire new SSR memories. Understanding pity, rate-up types, and when to skip is worth more than any gear upgrade.

ENTRY_#EVT_022026.07.02BANNER

Core Concept: Deepspace uses a standard gacha pity system: 70 pulls hard pity for SSR, 50/50 for the rate-up character. If you lose the 50/50, your next SSR is guaranteed to be the rate-up. Pity carries over between limited banners of the same type. Weapon/memory banners share pity with the character banner. Know these rules before you pull — never pull without checking your pity counter.

Banner Types

Character Event Banner: The main banner. Rate-up SSR operative memory at 0.6% base rate, rising after 62 pulls (soft pity starts). Hard pity at 70. 50/50 system. Weapon/Memory Banner: Same pity system but the weapon pool is smaller. You are more likely to get the rate-up weapon (75/25 split). Standard Banner: No rate-up. Permanent pool. Never spend limited pulls here. Beginner Banner: 20 pulls guaranteed SSR. Only spend standard tickets here. Banner Types Summary: Only pull on Character Event Banners. The other banners are traps for new players.

When to Pull

Must Pull: The rate-up character is a major upgrade to your account (fills a missing element role). You have 140+ pulls saved (guaranteed worst case). The character's R1 is significantly stronger than alternatives. Skip: You already have a strong unit of the same role. The character is a sidegrade, not an upgrade. You have fewer than 70 pulls and can't reach pity before the banner ends. You are saving for a future character you want more. F2P Rule: Pull on every other banner maximum. Skip at least 50% of banners. Focus on 1-2 main operatives and only pull their rate-ups. Spreading pulls across all banners leaves you with R0 characters and no depth.

Pity Tracking

Always track your pity manually. The in-game counter shows only your current banner pity, not your overall account pity status. My recommendation: Use a notepad or spreadsheet. Track: (1) Pulls since last SSR. (2) Whether you won or lost the last 50/50. (3) Current saved pulls. (4) Target banner pity threshold. Soft pity math: At pull 62, rate rises from 0.6% to ~5%. At pull 65, ~15%. At pull 68, ~35%. 80% of SSRs come between pulls 65-70. Only 5% come before pull 20. Never expect an early SSR — budget for hard pity.

Pro Tip: The "pity carry-over" mechanic means you can pull 30 times on one banner, stop, and those 30 pulls count toward the next limited banner. This enables a powerful strategy: pull on every banner until ~62 pity, then stop. If the next banner is a must-pull, you are at soft pity range and can snipe the rate-up with 10-20 pulls. This is called "pity banking."

R1 vs New Character

General rule: A new character at R0 gives you more account value than R1 of an existing character. New characters add elemental coverage, team composition options, and Abyss flexibility. R1 makes an existing character stronger but does not expand your roster. Exception: If the new character overlaps completely with an existing character you already have built (same element, same role), then R1 of your existing character may be more valuable. Example: If you have Xavier (Light DPS) fully built, pulling another Light DPS is less valuable than Xavier's R1.

Summary: Only pull Character Event Banners. Budget 140 pulls for guarantee. Soft pity starts at 62. Track your pity manually. Pull for new characters over R1. Skip 50% of banners. Pity bank between banners. Never pull on Standard Banner with limited tickets.
