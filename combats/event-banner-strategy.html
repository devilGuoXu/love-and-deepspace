Banner & Rate-Up Strategy

Limited banners are the primary way to acquire new SSR memories. Understanding pity, rate-up types, and when to skip is worth more than any gear upgrade.

ENTRY_#EVT_022026.07.02BANNER

Core Concept: Deepspace uses a standard gacha pity system: 70 pulls hard pity for SSR, 50/50 for the rate-up character. If you lose the 50/50, your next SSR is guaranteed to be the rate-up. Pity carries over between limited banners of the same type. Weapon/memory banners share pity with the character banner. Know these rules before you pull — never pull without checking your pity counter.

Banner Types

Deepspace has multiple banner types. Knowing which to pull on — and which to ignore — is the foundation of smart resource management.

✅ PULL HERE
Character Event Banner
The main banner. Rate-up SSR operative memory at 0.6% base rate, rising after 62 pulls (soft pity starts). Hard pity at 70. 50/50 system. This is where 90% of your limited pulls should go.
⚠️ SITUATIONAL
Weapon / Memory Banner
Same pity system but the weapon pool is smaller. You are more likely to get the rate-up weapon (75/25 split). Only pull here if you have a specific weapon need and already have your main characters built.
❌ NEVER PULL
Standard Banner
No rate-up. Permanent pool. Never spend limited pulls here. Standard tickets only — and even then, save them for the beginner banner first. The standard banner is a resource trap.
✅ DO THIS FIRST
Beginner Banner
20 pulls guaranteed SSR. Only spend standard tickets here. Complete this before touching the standard banner. This is the most efficient use of standard tickets in the game.

Bottom line: Only pull on Character Event Banners. The other banners are traps for new players. Weapon banners are for endgame optimizers only.

When to Pull

Not every banner is worth your hard-earned diamonds. Use this decision framework to evaluate each one.

Must Pull Criteria
A banner qualifies as "must pull" only when ALL of these conditions are met:
1. The rate-up character is a major upgrade for your account (fills a missing element role)
2. You have 140+ pulls saved (guaranteed worst case)
3. The character's R1 is significantly stronger than alternatives
Skip Criteria
Skip a banner if ANY of these apply:
1. You already have a strong unit of the same role
2. The character is a sidegrade, not an upgrade
3. You have fewer than 70 pulls and can't reach pity before the banner ends
4. You are saving for a future character you want more

F2P Rule: Pull on every other banner maximum. Skip at least 50% of banners. Focus on 1-2 main operatives and only pull their rate-ups. Spreading pulls across all banners leaves you with R0 characters and no depth. A focused account always outperforms a scattered one.

Pity Tracking

Always track your pity manually. The in-game counter shows only your current banner pity, not your overall account pity status.

TRACK THIS
Pulls since last SSR
This is your current pity count. Starts at 0 after each SSR. Track it across banners — pity carries over between limited banners.
TRACK THIS
50/50 Status (won or lost)
If you lost your last 50/50, your next SSR is guaranteed to be the rate-up. This is the most important piece of information for pull planning.
TRACK THIS
Current saved pulls
Know your exact pull count at all times. If you have 140 pulls, you are guaranteed to get any rate-up character you want.

My recommendation: Use a notepad or spreadsheet. I track: (1) Pulls since last SSR. (2) Whether I won or lost the last 50/50. (3) Current saved pulls. (4) Target banner pity threshold.

Soft pity math: At pull 62, rate rises from 0.6% to ~5%. At pull 65, ~15%. At pull 68, ~35%. 80% of SSRs come between pulls 65-70. Only 5% come before pull 20. Never expect an early SSR — budget for hard pity.

Pro Tip — Pity Banking: The "pity carry-over" mechanic means you can pull 30 times on one banner, stop, and those 30 pulls count toward the next limited banner. This enables a powerful strategy: pull on every banner until ~62 pity, then stop. If the next banner is a must-pull, you are at soft pity range and can snipe the rate-up with 10-20 pulls. This is called "pity banking" and it is the single most effective F2P pulling strategy.

R1 vs New Character

One of the most common dilemmas: do you pull for R1 of an existing character, or a brand new character at R0?

✅ New Character (R0)
  • Adds elemental coverage to your roster
  • Expands team composition options
  • Increases Abyss flexibility and scoring potential
  • Generally more account value than R1
⚠️ R1 of Existing Character
  • Makes an existing character stronger
  • Does NOT expand your roster
  • More valuable if the new character overlaps with an existing built character
  • Example: Having Xavier fully built + another Light DPS is wasteful

General rule: A new character at R0 gives you more account value than R1 of an existing character. New characters add elemental coverage, team composition options, and Abyss flexibility. R1 makes an existing character stronger but does not expand your roster.

Exception: If the new character overlaps completely with an existing character you already have built (same element, same role), then R1 of your existing character may be more valuable. Example: If you have Xavier (Light DPS) fully built, pulling another Light DPS is less valuable than Xavier's R1.

DO & DON'T Summary

✅ DO
  • Only pull on Character Event Banners
  • Budget 140 pulls for guarantee before pulling
  • Track your pity manually (spreadsheet recommended)
  • Prioritize new characters over R1
  • Skip at least 50% of banners
  • Use pity banking — pull to ~62 and wait
❌ DON'T
  • Don't spend limited pulls on Standard Banner
  • Don't pull without knowing your pity count
  • Don't pull on every banner — focus on 1-2 operatives
  • Don't expect early SSRs — budget for hard pity
  • Don't pull for R1 when you could get a new character
  • Don't ignore pity carry-over between banners

Summary: Only pull Character Event Banners. Budget 140 pulls for guarantee. Soft pity starts at 62. Track your pity manually. Pull for new characters over R1. Skip 50% of banners. Pity bank between banners. Never pull on Standard Banner with limited tickets.
