PROTOCORE BUILDS — TEAM SYNERGY

Protocore Team Synergies

Protocores are not just for your main DPS — support and sub-DPS builds with coordinated stats can double your team's effective power.

ENTRY_#0972026.07.02CORE_SYSTEM
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

How to Read This: This guide assumes you already have a main DPS build. If you do not, build your main DPS first (see #096). Team synergies are a secondary optimization — they matter when your main DPS is already well-geared.

The Support Framework

A team of 3 operatives usually has 1 main DPS, 1 sub-DPS, and 1 support. The support operative's protocores should not focus on personal damage. Instead, build: Memory sets with team buffs: Sets that increase team ATK, team energy regen, or enemy debuffs are worth more on a support than a personal damage set. Energy Recharge (ER) stacking: A support with 180%+ ER can generate ults for the team at 2x the normal rate. This enables more frequent Ardent Oath for your main DPS. HP% and DEF%: Support operatives need survivability to stay alive and provide buffs. A dead support provides zero value. Build HP% on slots 1/3 and DEF% on slot 2. Healing bonus: If your support has healing abilities, healing bonus main stats on protocores are efficient. A support that heals 2x more effectively reduces downtime significantly. Key insight: The best support protocore is the one that makes your main DPS deal more damage — not the one that deals damage itself. A support doing 10% of team DPS with 2,000 ATK is fine if they enable the main DPS to do 80% of team DPS at 8,000 ATK.

Sub-DPS Protocore Strategy

The sub-DPS role is a hybrid — part damage, part utility. Protocore strategy for sub-DPS: Elemental matching: If your sub-DPS shares an element with your main DPS, build the same elemental damage set. The resonance skill of the sub-DPS can trigger elemental reactions that benefit the main DPS. CRIT rate focus: Sub-DPS should prioritize CRIT rate over ATK% because their role is to proc resonance debuffs that require crits. 60%+ CRIT rate on sub-DPS is more important than raw ATK. Set synergy: Look for memory sets that have "party-wide" bonuses in their 2-pc or 4-pc effects. These are rare but extremely valuable. A sub-DPS wearing a set that gives +10% party CRIT rate is worth more than a sub-DPS wearing a personal damage set. Budget building: Sub-DPS can function with +9 protocores instead of +12. The stat loss is acceptable because the sub-DPS's primary value is utility, not raw damage. Save your +12 investment for the main DPS.

Team-Wide Stat Coordination

The most overlooked team synergy is stat coordination — ensuring no stat is redundant or wasted across the team. Energy Regen distribution: Only 1-2 team members need high ER. If all 3 have 150% ER, you are wasting protocore slots that could be damage stats. Assign ER to the support only. HP/Shield synergy: If your main DPS has a shield-based memory set (e.g., Engraved Zayne), the support should NOT run a shield set — the shields do not stack. One shield source per team is enough. Debuff coverage: Each team member should apply a different debuff if possible. One applies ATK down, another applies DEF down, the third applies slow/stun. Protocore substats can influence debuff application rates (e.g., status effect substats). Elemental coverage: A team with 2 elements covers more content than a team with 1 element. The sub-DPS should cover a different element from the main DPS for versatility. This is a team composition decision, but protocore builds should reflect the chosen coverage.

Team Protocore Budgeting: Think of protocore investment as a budget across 3 operatives × 6 protocore slots = 18 slots. Optimal allocation: Main DPS gets 10-12 of the best slots (+12 with perfect substats). Sub-DPS gets 4-6 slots (+9 with good substats). Support gets 2-4 slots (+6 with correct main stats). This allocation maximizes team damage. The most common mistake: giving each operative 6 equally mediocre protocore builds. Unequal distribution — investing heavily in one operative — always produces better results. You clear content with one strong DPS, not three average operatives.

Content-Specific Synergies

Different content types demand different team protocore synergies: Abyssal Trial (single target boss): Main DPS — max damage. Sub-DPS — debuff/battery. Support — heal/shield. Protocore focus: single-target damage for DPS, ER for sub-DPS. Deepspace Trial (multi-wave): Main DPS — AoE build. Sub-DPS — AoE or battery. Support — sustain. Protocore focus: energy regen on 2 members for constant AoE spam. Hunter Contest (timed score): Main DPS — max burst damage. Sub-DPS — CDMG support set. Support — speed/reset utility. Protocore focus: front-loaded damage (CRIT damage > sustained ATK). Co-op (shared team): Main DPS — standard build. Sub-DPS — battery/healer hybrid. Support — full battery or full healer. Protocore focus: one team member should have 200%+ ER for ult generation. If your co-op team coordinates on voice, you can run a pure battery support that generates 6+ ults per minute while the DPS does 0 ER investment.

Summary: Support = ER + survivability. Sub-DPS = CRIT rate + set synergy. Main DPS = all damage. Budget: 10-12 slots on main DPS, 4-6 on sub-DPS, 2-4 on support. Coordinate debuffs and ER — do not overlap. Match protocore strategy to content type. A well-coordinated team with a 10/4/4 split clears content 40% faster than three 6/6/6 equally-built operatives.
