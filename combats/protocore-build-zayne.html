PROTOCORE BUILDS — ZAYNE

Zayne: Frost Warden

HP-scaling hybrid DPS/support. Shield uptime and survivability are his unique contributions.

ENTRY_#0862026.06.29CORE_SYSTEM

Core Identity: Zayne scales with HP for both damage and shields. He's a hybrid — build for equal parts survivability and damage. His shields scale harder with HP than with DEF.

Key Insight: Zayne's healing and shield both scale off max HP, not ATK. HP% substats are worth more than ATK% for him. A 35k HP Zayne shields for 8k+ per skill — that's effectively +8k HP per rotation. He's the best sustain operative in the game.

Summary: HP% is Zayne's primary stat — it feeds both his damage and his shields. Frostguard 4-pc is the best general set. Don't build him like a standard DPS; embrace the HP scaling. 35k HP breakpoint is critical.
