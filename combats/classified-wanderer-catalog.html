Wanderer Catalog

Every known Wanderer type, its origin story, and how to fight it. Knowledge is the first weapon.

ENTRY_#N109_042026.06.29CLASSIFIED

What Are Wanderers? Corrupted creatures born from the Deepspace rift. They are attracted to concentrated Evol energy and prey on Hunters. Each type has a unique origin tied to the Deepspace's influence on the material world.

Wanderer Types

Void Spawn: The most common type. Formless masses of corrupted energy. They have no intelligence — only hunger. Easy to kill in groups. Weak to all elements equally.

Elemental Wanderers: Wanderers that have absorbed enough elemental energy to manifest a physical form. Fire, Ice, Lightning, and Shadow variants exist. Each is weak to its opposite element. These are the standard elite enemies.

Corrupted Operatives: Former Hunters who were overwhelmed by Deepspace corruption. They retain their combat training but lose their humanity. Phantom Xavier, Sylus, Zayne, and Rafayel fall into this category. They are the most dangerous type because they can think and adapt.

Ancient Wanderers: Massive beings that have existed since the Deepspace first opened. The Titan Sentry, Frost Tyrant, and Blood Moon Priestess are examples. They are tied to specific locations and seem to guard something.

Lore Significance

The Wanderers are not random monsters. They are a symptom of a deeper problem within the Deepspace. The N109 Zone has the highest concentration of Wanderers because the dimensional barrier is thinnest there. Some researchers believe the Wanderers are not invaders — they are refugees fleeing something worse on the other side.

Classified Note: Sylus has demonstrated the ability to communicate with certain Wanderer types. This ability is unique among known Hunters. Onychinus files suggest this is related to his Dark element Evol, but the full truth remains classified.
