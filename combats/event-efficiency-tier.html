Methodology

How tiers are calculated: Each event type is scored on: rewards per minute (stamina equivalent earned per minute of gameplay), limited item value (how valuable are the exclusive rewards), repeatability (can you earn rewards indefinitely or is there a cap), and accessibility (can all players participate regardless of account progression). Scores are normalized and ranked S through D.

Scoring system: Rewards per minute (weight: 40% of score) — calculated as stamina equivalent of all rewards divided by total time investment. Limited value (weight: 30%) — exclusive SSR memories score highest, cosmetic items score medium, farmable resources score lowest. Repeatability (weight: 15%) — uncapped rewards that let you farm beyond the shop clear are valuable. Accessibility (weight: 15%) — events that require level 50+ characters score lower than events open to all.

Tier thresholds: S-Tier (90-100), A-Tier (75-89), B-Tier (60-74), C-Tier (40-59), D-Tier (below 40). These thresholds are based on average scores across all events in the first year. Use this tier list to decide where to spend your limited playtime — an S-Tier event is worth 5x more than a D-Tier event per minute invested.

Important note: Efficiency varies by account progression. For new players, events that offer level-up materials are more valuable (they accelerate progression). For end-game players, events that offer protocore dust and pull tickets are the most efficient. Adjust the tier list based on your current account needs. This tier list assumes a mid-game account (level 40-50) as the baseline.

S-Tier Events S-TIER

S-Tier events offer exceptional rewards per minute, exclusive items that cannot be obtained elsewhere, and are accessible to most players.

⭐ ANNIVERSARY / 2.0 LAUNCH EVENTS
Score: 98/100 — Best rewards, best efficiency, exclusive items
Anniversary events are the most efficient in the game. They offer: free SSR selector, 30+ pull tickets, exclusive nameplates, double top-up reset, enhanced login rewards, and special missions that reward premium currency. The time investment is minimal (5 min/day for login, 10 min/day for missions). The rewards per hour calculation: approximately 10,000 stamina equivalent in rewards for 5 hours total playtime over the event duration. This is 5x more efficient than domain farming.
⭐ LIMITED SSR MEMORY EVENTS
Score: 95/100 — Free SSR memory with time investment
Events that offer an SSR memory as a free reward (not gacha) are always S-Tier. These events require consistent daily play (15-20 min/day for 2-3 weeks) and guarantee a powerful memory at the end. The value of one free SSR memory is approximately 14,000 premium currency (the cost to pity one SSR on a banner). Even if the memory is not meta-defining, the stat sticks alone make it worth the time. Limited SSR events run approximately 3-4 times per year.
⭐ SEASONAL SHOP EVENTS (SUMMER/WINTER)
Score: 92/100 — Generous shop — Exclusive cosmetics — High currency rates
Major seasonal events have the most generous event shops. The currency earning rate is high (approximately 50% more per minute than standard events). The shops include: exclusive outfits (value 2,400 currency each), limited furnishings, premium pull tickets, and large quantities of protocore dust. The daily currency cap is high enough that you can clear the shop of all valuable items within 15 min/day playtime. Seasonal events run twice per year.

A-Tier Events A-TIER

A-Tier events offer good rewards for reasonable time investment. They are worth prioritizing but not at the expense of S-Tier events.

BOSS CHALLENGE EVENTS
Score: 85/100. Boss challenges reward combat skill with premium currency and enhancement materials. A typical boss event: 10 min/day for 14 days = 2.3 hours total. Rewards: approximately 500 premium currency, 30 enhancement stones, 100K gold, and event shop currency worth 200 stamina of resources. The boss fights are fun and break up the farming routine. Downside: requires decent builds (level 40+ characters) — less accessible for new players.
STORY CHAPTER EVENTS
Score: 80/100. Story events add permanent narrative content while offering event rewards. The story chapters are high quality (voice acted, animated cutscenes). Rewards: 300-500 premium currency, memory enhancement materials, and a limited furniture item. Time investment: 2-3 hours total to complete all story content. The story is permanent, but the event bonuses are limited — prioritize the event missions before they expire.
MINIGAME FESTIVALS
Score: 78/100. Minigame festivals offer variety but inconsistent efficiency. Good minigames (beach volleyball, ice skating) are genuinely fun and rewarding. Bad minigames can be frustrating. Strategy: play the minigames you enjoy, skip the ones you do not. The currency rates are similar across all minigames, so you are not penalized for skipping specific ones. Do not force yourself to play minigames you hate for marginal rewards.

A-Tier strategy: Participate in A-Tier events for the premium currency and limited items. Do not feel obligated to max out the event shop — focus on the high-value items (pull tickets, dust, premium currency) and skip the low-value items (basic enhancement stones, low-tier exp crystals). A-Tier events reward selectivity, not completionism.

B-Tier Events B-TIER

B-Tier events are average — decent rewards but not worth going out of your way for. Participate if you have spare time, skip if you are busy.

1
Login Events
Score: 70/100. Login events require 1 minute per day for 7-14 days. Rewards: 5-10 pull tickets, 500K+ gold, stamina items, and sometimes a 4-star memory. The rewards are free for minimal effort. Login events always run alongside other events, so there is no opportunity cost — you are logging in anyway. Never skip login events.
2
Photo Contest Events
Score: 65/100. Photo contests ask you to take screenshots matching a theme and submit them for voting. Rewards: nameplate (participation), premium currency (top rankings). The rewards for participation are minimal (just a nameplate). The rewards for ranking high are good (500+ premium currency) but competitive. Casual photographers can skip; dedicated photographers should participate.
3
Rerun Events (Reduced Rewards)
Score: 62/100. Rerun events have reduced rewards compared to the original (typically 50-70% of original currency). They are still worth doing if you missed the original, but lower priority than new events. The rerun shop typically does not include the exclusive outfit (replaced with farmable resources). Only the limited SSR memory remains available — buy that and skip the rest.

B-Tier shortcut: For login events, just log in and claim. For photo contests, submit one entry for the participation reward. For rerun events, buy the limited SSR memory (if available) and spend the remaining currency on dust or skip it entirely. B-Tier events should not take more than 5 minutes of your daily playtime.

C-Tier Events C-TIER

C-Tier events offer poor rewards per minute or are too repetitive for the value. Only participate if you enjoy the content or have excess time.

Extended boss grindsScore: 55/100. Events that require 20+ boss kills for full rewards. Skip the last 5-10 kills — the rewards are not worth the burnout.
Low-currency minigamesScore: 50/100. Some minigames give significantly less currency per minute than others. Check community efficiency numbers before playing.
Collection events (gathering)Score: 45/100. Events that require gathering items from domains or exploration maps. Low engagement, low rewards.

Burnout warning: C-Tier events are the most common source of player burnout. Players feel obligated to complete them because "rewards are rewards." But the opportunity cost is real — time spent on C-Tier events is time not spent on S/A-Tier events, main content, or real life. Skip C-Tier events guilt-free. The rewards are not significant enough to matter in the long term.

D-Tier — Skip D-TIER

D-Tier events are actively not worth your time. Skip them unless you genuinely enjoy the content.

TIMED CHALLENGES (24-HOUR)
Score: 30/100 — Stressful, low rewards, FOMO bait
Timed challenges that require you to log in at a specific time or complete a task within a narrow window. The rewards are typically minor (a few enhancement stones). The stress of the time constraint is not worth the reward value. These events are designed to create FOMO and get you to check the game more frequently. Recognize the pattern and skip it.
COMPETITIVE RANKING EVENTS
Score: 25/100 — Whale territory, high investment, low participation reward
Ranking events where top players compete for exclusive rewards (nameplates, titles). For 99% of players, the participation reward is trivial (50 premium currency). The top 100 rewards are significant but require either whaling or extreme dedication. If you are not in the top 10% of accounts (level 60, maxed builds, all meta characters), skip ranking events. The opportunity cost of trying to compete is not worth the participation reward.
DO SKIP
  • 24-hour timed challenges
  • Competitive ranking unless you are in top 10%
  • Events requiring content you cannot access (too low level)
  • Minigames you actively dislike
  • Extended grinds past the reasonable stopping point
DON'T SKIP
  • Login events (free rewards, zero effort)
  • SSR memory events (best value in game)
  • Seasonal shops (exclusive outfits, efficient currency)
  • Anniversary events (once per year, best rewards)
  • Rerun events for limited SSR you missed

Efficiency Calculator

Use this simple formula to evaluate any event for yourself:

EFFICIENCY CALCULATOR
Step 1: Calculate total stamina value of all rewards you want from the event shop (1 pull ticket = 160 stamina worth of premium currency).
Step 2: Estimate total time to earn enough currency (check community posts for time per clear × clears needed).
Step 3: Divide stamina value by hours. If result > 500 stamina/hour, the event is efficient. If result < 200 stamina/hour, consider skipping.
Example: An event with 10 pull tickets (1,600 stamina value) + 500 premium currency (500 stamina) = 2,100 total. If it takes 3 hours to clear the shop, that is 700 stamina/hour — excellent efficiency, S-Tier.
1
Use the event shop preview
On day 1 of a new event, open the shop and note the items you want. Ignore filler items (basic stones, low-tier exp).
2
Calculate time to clear
Divide total currency needed by daily currency cap. Multiply by minutes per day. This gives total time investment.
3
Apply the efficiency formula
Total stamina value ÷ total hours. Compare to the thresholds above. Decide if the event is worth your time.
4
Factor in fun
If the event content is fun, add 50% to the efficiency score. Enjoyment has real value. Fun events are always worth your time.
5
Re-evaluate mid-event
If an event turns out to be more grindy than expected, adjust your participation. It is okay to stop at any point — sunk cost is a fallacy.

Golden Rule: The most efficient event is the one you enjoy. If you hate an event, skip it even if it is S-Tier. Your mental health and enjoyment of the game are worth more than the stamina value of event rewards. Deepspace is a game, not a second job. Play what you enjoy.

Summary: S-Tier (90-100): Anniversary events, SSR memory events, seasonal shops — clear these completely. A-Tier (75-89): Boss challenges, story events, minigame festivals — participate for premium currency only. B-Tier (60-74): Login events (never skip), photo contests, rerun events — minimal effort required. C-Tier (40-59): Extended grinds, low-currency minigames — skip unless you enjoy them. D-Tier (under 40): Timed challenges, competitive rankings — skip entirely. Use the efficiency calculator (stamina value ÷ hours) to evaluate any event. Fun is a valid factor — enjoyable events are always worthwhile. Skip guilt-free.
