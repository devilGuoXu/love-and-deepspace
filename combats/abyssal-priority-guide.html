Abyss Priority Guide

Which floors give the best rewards? Where should you stop pushing? How to maximize gems per reset.

Floor Reward Breakdown

Floors 7-8: 50 gems each (100 total). Low difficulty. These are free gems 閳?always clear them first. Floors 9-10: 80 gems each (160 total). Medium difficulty. Push these with your best team. Floors 11-12: 120 gems each (240 total). High difficulty. These separate mid-game from end-game players.

Priority Strategy

Week 1 of Reset: Push as far as you can with your best team. Don't optimize 閳?just see how far raw power takes you. Week 2: Re-optimize team compositions for each floor. Switch memories between operatives. Week 3: Last push. Consider sacrificing Stellactrum matching for counter-element advantage. Stop Point: If you can't clear a floor within 10 attempts this week, it's not happening. Save your sanity.

Team Allocation Tips

Build your strongest team first and clear with them. Then build a second team with leftover resources. Don't split your best memories across two teams 閳?you'll end up with two mediocre teams instead of one strong one. The exception is Floor 12 which requires two teams.

Weekly Reset Planning

Abyss resets every Monday at server time. Your strategy should follow a three-phase weekly plan. Monday through Wednesday: clear all floors you can comfortably manage with your best team. Record your clear times and star ratings for each floor. Thursday through Saturday: optimize team compositions for floors where you achieved 1 or 2 stars. Swap memories between operatives to maximize Stellactrum matching. Sunday: final push on the highest floor you have not yet cleared. If you are within 5% of the clear time requirement, consider using consumables for the last attempt.

Tracking your weekly progress prevents wasted attempts. The Abyss limits you to 30 total attempts per week across all floors. Use a simple note-taking system to record which team compositions worked and which failed. A failed attempt with a suboptimal team is not a total loss if you learn something about the floor's mechanics. Avoid repeating the same composition on the same floor more than three times 閳?if it has not worked by the third attempt, the composition is the problem, not your execution.

Gem and Resource Projection

A full Abyss clear (Floors 7-12 with 3 stars each) yields 500 gems per reset from clear rewards plus 180 bonus gems from star ratings, totaling 680 gems per reset. Over a 30-day month with 4 resets, this equals 2,720 gems from Abyss alone. Combined with daily mission gems (60 per day = 1,800 per month) and event rewards, Abyss represents approximately 35% of your total monthly gem income. Missing a single Abyss reset costs you roughly 600 gems on average, which is equivalent to three days of daily missions.

Invest stamina proportional to gem value. Each Abyss floor attempt costs 20 stamina. A successful floor clear with 3 stars rewards gems worth roughly 10 stamina per gem in equivalent value. This makes Abyss attempts more stamina-efficient than most content in the game. Prioritize Abyss attempts over domain farming during the first three days of each weekly reset to maximize your return on stamina investment.

A 3-star clear on Floor 9 gives more gems than a 1-star clear on Floor 10. Optimize which floors to attempt with which teams each week for maximum gem income across resets.

Key Insight: A 7-star clear on Floor 7 is worth more than a 0-star clear on Floor 9. Don't over-push 閳?clear what you can, then optimize for stars. Each star gives +10 bonus gems. Three 3-star clears on floors 7-9 = 90 bonus gems. That's almost a full floor clear.

Summary: Prioritize guaranteed gems over risky pushes. Clear floors 7-8 first (free gems). Push 9-10 with your best team. Attempt 11-12 only if you have 85k+ power. Stars matter 閳?optimize for 3-star clears over 1-star floor progression. Monthly Abyss = ~1,200 gems if you full clear, ~600 if you partial clear.
