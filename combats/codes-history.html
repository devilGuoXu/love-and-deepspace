Recurring Reward Patterns

Analysis of past campaigns reveals these recurring patterns: Monthly Login: 100 diamonds per week + random materials. Total: ~400 diamonds/month. Always claim daily. Patch Launch (Every 6 weeks): 300-500 diamonds from maintenance compensation + patch notes rewards. Always read patch notes for redemption codes. Livestream Drops: 100-300 diamonds per livestream. Requires watching the stream for a minimum time. Social Media Milestones: When official accounts hit follower milestones (1M, 2M, 5M), they release global reward codes. Typically 100-200 diamonds each. Operative Birthdays: Each operative has a birthday campaign. Rewards: 100-200 diamonds + exclusive birthday name card. Dates: Xavier (Dec 25), Zayne (Feb 14), Rafayel (Oct 3), Sylus (Apr 17). Double Drop Events: Happen approximately once per month. Alternates between protocore dust, XP, and gold domains. Survey Rewards: In-game surveys every 2-3 months. 100 diamonds per completed survey. Always fill them out.

Predicting Future Campaigns: Based on historical patterns, we can predict: August 2026 — 1.5-Year Anniversary (likely major campaign with SSR selector). October 2026 — Halloween campaign (name card + pulls). December 2026 — Christmas/Winter campaign (outfit + pulls). January 2027 — 2-Year Anniversary (biggest campaign yet, likely free limited SSR). Knowing this schedule helps you plan diamond spending. If a major campaign is 2 months away, consider saving instead of pulling on current banners. The SSR selectors from anniversary campaigns are especially valuable for new players who need to fill out their roster.

Missed Campaign Rewards

Can you claim rewards from past campaigns you missed? Generally, no. Login campaign rewards are time-limited and expire when the campaign ends. However: (1) Some event shops allow you to buy past campaign items with event currency during reruns. (2) The in-game Mailbox keeps some rewards for 30 days after claim — but unclaimed rewards from expired campaigns are lost. (3) Outfits from past campaigns may return to the premium shop for diamond purchase. (4) Name cards and profile frames from past campaigns are exclusive and have not returned in any form. The exceptions: SSR memory selectors from anniversary campaigns do not expire once claimed. You can keep the selector ticket in your inventory indefinitely and choose a memory at any time.

Summary: Past campaigns show increasing reward generosity over time. Major campaigns (anniversary, summer, winter) give 800-2,000 diamonds + exclusive items. Minor campaigns (seasonal, birthdays) give 200-500 diamonds. Recurring patterns: monthly login (400 diamonds/month), patch launch (300-500), livestreams (100-300), social milestones (100-200). Predict future campaigns using the 6-week patch cycle and seasonal calendar. Missed campaign rewards are generally lost forever except for SSR selector tickets which do not expire. Use campaign history to plan your saving and spending strategy.
