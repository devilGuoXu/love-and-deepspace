What Is the Build Repository?

The Community Build Repository is a curated collection of player-submitted builds, organized by operative, content type, and account tier. Unlike tier lists that show theoretical optimal builds, the repository shows real builds that real players use to clear real content. Each build entry includes: the player's final stats (ATK, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ER), memory set and levels, protocore main stats and roll quality, the content it was used for, and the player's account tier (F2P / dolphin / whale).

The repository serves three purposes: (1) New players can see what stats are achievable at their account stage and set realistic targets. (2) Intermediate players can find builds that match their available memories and protocores rather than chasing theoretical perfect gear. (3) Advanced players can share and compare high-end builds, pushing the boundaries of what is possible. The repository is updated weekly with new submissions and curated by a team of volunteer editors.

Current Repository Stats: 234 total builds across 4 operatives. 82 Xavier builds, 61 Sylus builds, 53 Rafayel builds, 38 Zayne builds. Average build rating: 4.2/5.0. Most submitted content: Abyss Floors 9-12 (48% of builds). Most common account tier: Dolphin (42% of builds). The repository is most useful for dolphin-tier players who want to optimize their mid-investment rosters.

Featured Community Builds

Here are five exemplary builds from the repository that demonstrate the range of viable configurations.

F2P XAVIER — ABYSS FLOOR 12
ATK 2,180 | CRIT 55% | CRIT DMG 120% | ER 115%
4pc Light Seeker (all +9), 2pc ATK% set. Protocores: Slot 4 CRIT Rate +12, Slot 5 ATK% +12, Slot 6 ATK% +9. All substats are low-to-medium rolls. This build demonstrates that F2P players can clear floor 12 with +9 protocores and no limited memories beyond the free SSR. Key: 55% CRIT rate is the floor-12 threshold — anything above is luxury. This build achieves the threshold with a single CRIT Rate main stat protocore.
DOLPHIN SYLUS — SPEEDRUN
ATK 2,650 | CRIT 68% | CRIT DMG 155% | ER 125%
4pc Execute Protocol (all +12), 2pc CRIT set. Protocores: Slot 4 CRIT Rate +15 (high roll), Slot 5 CRIT DMG +15, Slot 6 ATK% +12. R1 Execute Protocol memory. This is the budget speedrun build — achievable for monthly pass spenders. The key stat is 155% CRIT DMG, which enables execute oneshots on floor 10-11 bosses. The build hits the ER 120% DPS minimum for smooth rotation.
F2P RAFAYEL — CO-OP BOSS
ATK 2,100 | CRIT 48% | CRIT DMG 110% | ER 130%
4pc Inferno Waltz (all +9), 2pc ER set. Protocores: Slot 4 ATK% +12 (unusual — no CRIT main stat), Slot 5 ATK% +12, Slot 6 ER +12. This build prioritizes burn DoT damage over CRIT because Rafayel's burn scales with ATK%, not CRIT. The 48% CRIT is enough to trigger burn crits occasionally but not the main damage source. This is a great example of a non-meta build that works because it matches the operative's kit mechanics.

How to Browse Builds

The repository is organized by three filters: Operative, Content Type, and Account Tier. Here is how to use each filter effectively.

Filter by Operative: Select the operative you want to build for. The repository will show only builds for that operative, sorted by rating (highest first). If you are an Xavier main, start by looking at the top 10 Xavier builds to understand the stat ranges that top players achieve. The operative filter is the broadest filter — use it for general inspiration.

Filter by Content Type: Select the specific content you want to clear (e.g., Abyss Floor 10, Co-op Boss, Domain). The repository will show builds that have been verified against that content. This is the most useful filter when you are stuck on a specific challenge. Look for builds with the same account tier as yours for the most relevant stat targets.

Filter by Account Tier: Select F2P, Dolphin, or Whale to see builds from players with similar spending levels. F2P builds assume no limited memories beyond free SSRs and no protocore refills. Dolphin builds assume monthly pass + occasional BP. Whale builds assume high-investment with R3+ limited memories and unlimited protocore farming. Comparing builds across tiers helps you understand which stats improve with spending and which are pure skill/optimization.

Tip: When browsing builds, pay attention to the "roll quality" column. Two builds may show similar final stats, but the build with higher roll quality has more room to grow when you upgrade protocores. A build with ATK 2,400 from high-protocore rolls is more future-proof than a build with the same ATK from memory levels alone. Look for builds with high roll quality scores as long-term targets.

Submission Process

Submitting your build to the repository helps the community and gives you feedback from experienced builders. Here is the submission process step by step.

1
Capture Your Stats
Take screenshots of: (1) Final stat screen (ATK/CRIT/DMG/ER). (2) All 6 protocore enhancement screens showing main stats and substat roll values. (3) Memory set screen showing which memories are equipped and their levels. (4) Your account tier proof (optional — screenshot of your purchase history is accepted).
2
Note Your Content Goals
Specify which content this build is designed for. Include the specific floor/boss name and your best clear time or score. A build's quality is relative to its content goal — a build that clears Abyss Floor 12 is more impressive than the same build clearing Floor 9.
3
Fill Out the Submission Form
Use the build submission template available in the repository's submission channel. The template collects: operative name, memory sets, protocore main stats, final stats, content goal, account tier, and any notes about your rotation or playstyle that might help others replicate your results.
4
Submit for Review
Submit your build through the repository's form. A volunteer editor will review your submission within 48 hours. They may ask for clarification or additional screenshots. Once approved, your build will be added to the repository with a rating from the review team.
5
Engage with Feedback
After your build is published, community members may comment with optimization suggestions. Engage constructively — the feedback is meant to help everyone improve. Updated builds can be resubmitted for re-review. Top-rated builds are featured on the repository's main page.

Build Submission Guidelines

To ensure consistent quality, all builds must meet these minimum requirements before submission.

MINIMUM VIABLE
Memories: All 6 slots at +9+ — Main stats correct for role — CRIT Rate > 45% for DPS
This is the entry-level requirement. Builds below this threshold are not accepted because they are not optimized enough to serve as references for other players. If your build needs improvement, check the guides in the Workshop first before submitting.
RECOMMENDED FOR SUBMISSION
Memories: All 6 slots at +12+ — Protocores: 2+ perfect rolls each — Clear content 30% faster than average
Builds at this level are valuable references for the community. They demonstrate achievable optimization that intermediate players can work toward. Most submissions fall into this category.
FEATURE-WORTHY
All memories +15 — Protocores: 4+ perfect total rolls — Top 10% clear times — Exceptional roll quality
These builds are featured on the repository's main page. They represent the current meta benchmarks for each operative. Only 10-15% of submissions reach this tier.

What we do not accept: Builds with incorrect main stats (e.g., HP% on Xavier Slot 4). Builds with incomplete screenshots. Builds from account tiers labeled incorrectly (misrepresenting spending). Builds that use clearly outdated patch information. Builds submitted for content that no longer exists in the current version. Duplicate builds from the same player without significant changes. Editors reserve the right to reject any submission that does not meet quality standards.

Tip: Before submitting, compare your build to existing builds in the repository for the same operative and account tier. If your build is not clearly better or meaningfully different from what is already there, consider waiting until you have made further improvements. The repository values quality over quantity. A single excellent build from you is more valuable than three average builds. Take your time, optimize carefully, and submit when you are proud of the result.
