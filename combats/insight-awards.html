The Deepspace Awards

2026 Annual Ceremony — Fan-Voted & Editorially Selected

Welcome to the first annual Deepspace Awards. Categories were selected by the community. Winners were determined by a combination of fan voting and editorial judgment. Disputes will be settled by combat. Probably not really.

Best Operative (Combat)

● Xavier — Light Seeker set DPS
● Sylus — Execute + Dark Energy burst
● Rafayel — Burn DoT sustain
★ Sylus
Wins by a narrow margin. His execute mechanic below 30% HP is the most impactful combat ability in the game. The Dark Energy stacking system rewards skilled play. His burst damage ceiling is unmatched, and his ultimate animation is the coolest in the game by community consensus.

Best Operative (Story)

● Xavier — Mystery + quiet devotion
● Zayne — Slow-burn emotional depth
● Rafayel — Passion + artistic soul
★ Xavier
The quiet ones always win. Xavier's story arc across the main campaign and his personal memories has the most emotional range. His mystery keeps players engaged. His moments of vulnerability hit hardest because he guards himself so carefully. The library scene alone secured his win.

Worst Boss to Fight

● Crystal Monarch — Icicle spam
● Stormcaller — Teleport frustration
● Phantom Sylus — Invisibility + burst
★ Phantom Sylus
An uncontested victory. Phantom Sylus combines the worst elements of boss design: unpredictable teleport patterns, an invisibility mechanic that forces guessing, and a burst combo that can delete a support from full HP. He is the boss most likely to end a flawless run. The community hates him with a passion that borders on respect.

Most Satisfying Mechanic

● Perfect Dodge — The sound + slow-mo
● Resonance Chain — Team combo payoff
● Ardent Oath — Ultimate animation
★ Perfect Dodge
Nothing in this game feels better than hitting a perfect dodge. The sound cue, the slow-motion effect, the opening for a counter-attack. It is the most satisfying mechanic because it is purely skill-based and has an immediate, visible payoff. A well-timed perfect dodge into a full counter combo is the peak combat experience this game offers.

Most Likely to Make You Throw Your Phone

● 50-50 Loss on Limited Banner
● +15 Enhancement All Into Flat DEF
● Getting Hit by Invisible Attack in Co-op
★ +15 Enhancement All Into Flat DEF
The 50-50 loss is frustrating, but you expect it. The flat DEF enhancement is a gut punch because you had hope. You watched that protocore go to +3, +6, +9, +12, +15, and every single roll went into the one substat you were praying would not get rolled. The 50-50 loss is a known risk. The flat DEF roll is a betrayal.

Best Community Moment

● The Great Sylus Banner Countdown
● Anniversary Fan Art Flood
● The "Our Favorite Mistake" Event Collective
★ Anniversary Fan Art Flood
For a 48-hour period around the anniversary, the subreddit and Discord were flooded with original fan art. New artists, experienced artists, people who had never drawn before. The quality ranged from professional to adorable. The collective outpouring of creativity was the best representation of what this community can be when it focuses on creation rather than competition.

Best Feature Added Post-Launch

● Photo Mode 2.0 (expanded poses)
● Protocore Auto-Enhance
● Abyss Floor Practice Mode
★ Abyss Floor Practice Mode
This feature was a game-changer. Before practice mode, learning a new floor meant either sacrificing your weekly clear or watching videos. Practice mode lets you run any floor unlimited times without consuming your weekly attempt. The improvement in player skill since this feature was added is visible in clear rate statistics. A rare case of a quality-of-life feature that also makes the community better at the game.

Lifetime Achievement Award

● The Stamina Bar — Always there, never enough
● The Back Button — For letting us leave menus
★ The Stamina Bar
The single most consistent presence in every player's life. It dictates our schedules, influences our moods, and controls our resource economy. It has never asked for anything in return except our patience and our careful planning. The stamina bar is the true protagonist of Love and Deepspace, and we salute its unwavering service.
Awards are given in good fun. If your favorite did not win, consider submitting a formal complaint in the form of fan art. We accept bribes in the form of quality content.
