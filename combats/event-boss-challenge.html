Boss Challenge Events

Score attack, time attack, and multi-boss gauntlets — the hardest event type. These are the only events where your gear actually matters.

ENTRY_#EVT_052026.07.02BOSS_CHALLENGE

What They Are: Boss challenge events pit you against a series of buffed bosses with score thresholds. Unlike story events, there is no stamina cost and no shop — rewards are based on your highest score bracket. These events separate casual players from hardcore optimizers.

Score Attack Format

Structure: 4-5 boss stages. Each boss has 4 score tiers: D, C, B, A, S. Each tier has increasing score requirements. Score is based on: clear time (50%), damage dealt (30%), damage taken (10%), and dodges/parries (10%). Strategy: Clear time is the dominant factor. If you want S rank, you must clear within 60 seconds. Sacrifice survivability for damage — equip full DPS protocores. Use 2 DPS + 1 buffer (no healer). Save Ardent Oath for the first 10 seconds of the fight (pre-fill energy before starting). S-rank threshold: Typically top 10% of players. Do not feel bad about A-rank — the reward difference between A and S is usually 1-2 pulls.

Multi-Boss Gauntlet

Structure: 3 bosses in sequence. You cannot heal between bosses. Your team carries over with current HP and energy. Strategy: This is an endurance check, not a speed check. Bring Zayne on every team for sustain. Kill order matters: always kill the boss with the most dangerous AoE first. Do not use Ardent Oath on the first boss unless necessary — save it for the third boss who has the highest HP. Team recommendation: Xavier (main DPS, consistent damage), Zayne (heal + shield), Sylus (execute for final boss). This team composition works against 80% of boss combinations.

Boss Rush (Timed)

Structure: As many bosses as you can kill within 5 minutes. Bosses spawn one at a time. Harder bosses spawn as time passes. Strategy: This is pure DPS check. AoE operatives are king here because some stages have adds alongside the boss. Xavier (Light Seeker) and Rafayel (Inferno Blaze) are top performers. Wave composition: Boss 1 (0:00) → Boss 2 (0:45) → Boss 3 (1:30) → Boss + adds (2:15) → Boss 4 + elite (3:00) → Final Boss (4:00). Knowing the spawn timers lets you pre-charge Ardent Oath: use it at 0:40, 1:25, 2:10, 2:55, and 3:55. Reward threshold: Killing 5 bosses gives all non-ranked rewards. Killing 7+ gives the exclusive title.

Pro Tip: Boss challenge events are the only content where protocore optimization matters more than memory level. A well-rolled protocore set (correct main stats, 2+ good substats) can improve your score by 30% compared to random protocores. If you are stuck at A-rank, fix your protocores before your memories.

Score Optimization Checklist

Before attempting a boss challenge event, check: (1) Protocore main stats match your operative (Xavier = CRIT Rate/CRIT DMG, Zayne = HP/Healing Bonus, Sylus = ATK/Energy Recharge, Rafayel = ATK/Burn DMG). (2) Memory sets are correct (4-pc Light Seeker on Xavier, 4-pc Frostguard on Zayne, etc.). (3) Energy Recharge on supports is 150%+. (4) Your team has element advantage against the boss. (5) You have watched a S-rank clear video for positioning reference. (6) You have 60+ minutes of uninterrupted play time (attempts take 5 minutes each, you will retry 10+ times for S-rank).

Summary: Score Attack = speed (under 60s for S). Multi-Boss = endurance (Zayne sustain). Boss Rush = AoE clear (Xavier/Rafayel). Clear time is 50% of score. Protocore optimization > memory level. S-rank is for whales and grinders — A-rank rewards are fine for F2P.
