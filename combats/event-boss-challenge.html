Boss Challenge Events

Score attack, time attack, and multi-boss gauntlets — the hardest event type. These are the only events where your gear actually matters.

What They Are: Boss challenge events pit you against a series of buffed bosses with score thresholds. Unlike story events, there is no stamina cost and no shop — rewards are based on your highest score bracket. These events separate casual players from hardcore optimizers.

Score Attack Format

The standard boss challenge format with 4-5 boss stages, each ranked D through S.

Score Breakdown
Each boss has 4 score tiers: D, C, B, A, S. Each tier has increasing score requirements.
Clear Time: 50% of score — dominant factor
Damage Dealt: 30% — go full DPS
Damage Taken: 10% — don't get hit
Dodges/Parries: 10% — bonus points

Strategy: Clear time is the dominant factor. If you want S rank, you must clear within 60 seconds. Sacrifice survivability for damage — equip full DPS protocores. Use 2 DPS + 1 buffer (no healer). Save Ardent Oath for the first 10 seconds of the fight (pre-fill energy before starting).

S-rank threshold: Typically top 10% of players. Do not feel bad about A-rank — the reward difference between A and S is usually 1-2 pulls. A-rank is perfectly fine for F2P and mid-game accounts.

Multi-Boss Gauntlet

3 bosses in sequence with no healing between them. Your team carries over with current HP and energy.

Strategy
Endurance check, not speed
Bring Zayne on every team for sustain. Kill order matters: always kill the boss with the most dangerous AoE first. Do not use Ardent Oath on the first boss unless necessary — save it for the third boss who has the highest HP.
Team Recommendation
Xavier + Zayne + Sylus
Xavier (main DPS, consistent damage), Zayne (heal + shield), Sylus (execute for final boss). This team composition works against 80% of boss combinations.
Key Difference
Survival over speed
Unlike Score Attack where speed dominates, the Gauntlet rewards steady, consistent play. Don't greed for damage if it means your team dies before boss 3.

Strategy: This is pure DPS check. AoE operatives are king — some stages have adds alongside the boss. Xavier (Light Seeker) and Rafayel (Inferno Blaze) are top performers. Knowing the spawn timers lets you pre-charge Ardent Oath: use it at 0:40, 1:25, 2:10, 2:55, and 3:55 for maximum efficiency.

Reward threshold: Killing 5 bosses gives all non-ranked rewards. Killing 7+ gives the exclusive title.

Pro Tip: Boss challenge events are the only content where protocore optimization matters more than memory level. A well-rolled protocore set (correct main stats, 2+ good substats) can improve your score by 30% compared to random protocores. If you are stuck at A-rank, fix your protocores before your memories.

Score Optimization Checklist

Before attempting a boss challenge event, run through this checklist to ensure you are fully prepared.

1. Protocore Main Stats Match Your Operative
Xavier = CRIT Rate/CRIT DMG, Zayne = HP/Healing Bonus, Sylus = ATK/Energy Recharge, Rafayel = ATK/Burn DMG. Wrong main stats = 20%+ score loss.
2. Memory Sets Are Correct
4-pc Light Seeker on Xavier, 4-pc Frostguard on Zayne, etc. Set bonuses are non-negotiable for competitive scores.
3. Energy Recharge on Supports
Your support units should have 150%+ Energy Recharge. Without this, your rotation will stall and your score will suffer.
4. Element Advantage Against the Boss
Check the boss's element and bring a counter. Element advantage is a 25% damage multiplier — do not ignore it.
5. Study S-Rank Clear Videos
Watch an S-rank clear video for positioning reference. Knowing boss attack patterns and optimal positioning saves multiple attempts.
6. Set Aside 60+ Minutes
Attempts take 5 minutes each, you will retry 10+ times for S-rank. Do not start if you only have 15 minutes — rushed attempts waste your best runs.

DO & DON'T Summary

✅ DO
  • Equip full DPS protocores for Score Attack
  • Bring Zayne for sustain in Multi-Boss Gauntlet
  • Use AoE operatives (Xavier/Rafayel) for Boss Rush
  • Pre-charge Ardent Oath before starting
  • Watch S-rank clear videos for positioning
  • Fix protocores before blaming memory levels
❌ DON'T
  • Don't bring healers to Score Attack (speed is king)
  • Don't use Ardent Oath on boss 1 in Gauntlet
  • Don't ignore element advantage
  • Don't run sub-150% Energy Recharge on supports
  • Don't feel bad about A-rank — reward difference is minimal
  • Don't start a boss run session if you only have 15 minutes

Summary: Score Attack = speed (under 60s for S). Multi-Boss = endurance (Zayne sustain). Boss Rush = AoE clear (Xavier/Rafayel). Clear time is 50% of score. Protocore optimization > memory level. S-rank is for whales and grinders — A-rank rewards are fine for F2P.
