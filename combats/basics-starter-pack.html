New Player Starter Pack

A curated reading list of essential guides organized by playtime — from your first hour to your first month of Love and Deepspace.

Do not try to learn everything at once. Read one guide per day for the first week, implement what you learn, then move to the next. This structured approach prevents information overload and builds good habits from day one.

Your First Hour

Start here. These guides cover the absolute basics you need before anything else.

START HERE
Basics New Player Guide
The complete onboarding guide covering: menu navigation, first missions, gacha tutorial, team setup, and what to do in your first session. Read this before touching anything else. It sets the foundation for everything that follows.
READ AFTER
Combat Mechanics Guide
How combat works in LADS — basic attacks, dodge, Resonance, companion commands, energy system, and elemental basics. Read this before your first real combat encounter. Understanding the combat flow from the start prevents bad habits.
REFERENCE
Combat Guide Glossary
Bookmark this page. Whenever a guide uses a term you do not recognize, look it up here. The glossary covers 80+ terms from Abyss to Zone Level. Keep it open in a tab during your first week.

First hour checklist: (1) Complete the tutorial — do not skip it, even if you have played gacha games before. LADS has unique mechanics. (2) Set your name and choose your starting operative (pick whoever appeals to you most — all are viable). (3) Do your first 10-pull on the standard banner (you get enough free wishes to do this immediately). (4) Equip your best memories on your chosen operative and the Hunter. (5) Play through the first 3 story stages to unlock daily missions. (6) Complete today's daily missions (5 tasks, ~10 minutes). (7) Set a reminder to check the event screen — if there is an active event, read the event guide (below) before spending stamina.

Day 1: Foundations

Your first full day of play. Focus on understanding the core systems and avoiding the 10 mistakes that new players commonly make.

MUST READ
New Player Mistakes FAQ
The single most important guide for new players. Covers the 10 most common mistakes: wasting pulls on wrong banners, spreading resources too thin, ignoring events, neglecting support memories, and more. Read this before spending any significant resources.
SECOND
Daily & Weekly Priority Guide
What to do every day and every week for optimal progression. Daily checklist: 5 daily missions, spend all stamina, claim base rewards, check events. Weekly checklist: 9 weekly boss runs, all weekly missions, BP missions, Abyss attempts. Print this checklist and keep it by your computer.
THIRD
Stamina Efficiency Guide
How to spend your limited stamina for maximum account growth. Which domains to farm, which events to prioritize, and how to avoid wasting stamina. Stamina management is the single most important skill in LADS progression.

Day 1 priority list: (1) Complete all tutorial stages until daily missions are unlocked. (2) Do your 5 daily missions. (3) Spend all stamina on the protocore dust domain (level 30 if that is the highest you can clear, level 40+ if possible). (4) Level your main DPS memory to level 20-30 (enough to clear content, not more). (5) Do not pull on any limited banner yet — save all gems. (6) Read the New Player Mistakes FAQ before bed. Day 1 is about establishing routines, not optimizing every detail.

Day 2: Combat & Characters

Now that you have the basics, focus on building your combat skills and understanding your characters.

Advanced Combos Guide: Learn your chosen operative's optimal combo sequences. Each operative has unique skill chains and animation cancels that significantly increase DPS. Practice the basic combo in the Training Room (no stamina cost) until it becomes muscle memory. A player who knows their combo deals 20-30% more damage than one who button-mashes.

Character Guides (pick your 2 main operatives): Read the specific guide for each operative you plan to main. Xavier Guide, Zayne Guide, Rafayel Guide, or Sylus Guide. Each covers: optimal memory sets, skill priority, rotation scripts, best team compositions, and common mistakes. Focus on 2 operatives — do not spread your learning across all 4.

Team Synergy Guide: How to build teams where characters complement each other through elemental reactions, buff timing, and energy sharing. A team with good synergy deals 20-60% more damage than a team of individually strong characters who do not work together.

Day 3: Progression Systems

Understand the systems that govern your account's growth.

Memory Guide (general): How memories work — rarity, leveling, ascension, skills, sets, and protocores. This is the most complex system in LADS. Read the overview guide first, then dive into specific sub-systems as needed. Do not try to memorize everything — understand the concepts and refer back when building specific memories.

Protocore Guide: The stat enhancement system that determines 40-50% of your combat power. Learn: protocore slots (1-6), main stat pools per slot, substat enhancement, optimal main stats per character role, and when to stop enhancing (know when a protocore is "good enough" versus worth replacing).

Memory Set Collection Guide: Each operative's best memory sets, how set bonuses work (2-piece vs 4-piece), and which sets to farm first. Set bonuses provide 10-25% conditional damage increases — completing the right 4-piece set is your first major gearing goal.

Learning systems tip: Do not try to understand every detail of every system on Day 3. Focus on the 20% of knowledge that gives 80% of the results: correct main stats on protocores, activating 4-piece set bonuses, leveling memories to at least 40 before switching, and never farming the gold domain directly. The 80/20 rule applies perfectly to LADS systems — a little knowledge goes a long way.

Day 4: Resource Management

Learn how to manage your resources for long-term success.

Free Resources Guide: All the ways to earn free gems, pulls, and materials without spending money. Many new players miss significant free income sources (Crew Shop daily shards, Achievement rewards, hidden objectives, Secret exploration rewards). This guide ensures you are not leaving resources on the table.

Gacha Strategy Guide: How to approach the gacha system strategically. When to pull, when to save, how to read banner schedules, and how to plan your pulls months in advance. The most important takeaway: never pull on a limited banner unless you have 14,400 gems saved for hard pity.

Event Guide (general): How to approach events as a new player. Events are the most stamina-efficient content, but they require a strategy: which items to prioritize in the event shop (SSR memory > pull tickets > protocore dust > everything else), how much to farm, and when to stop. Events are new-player-friendly — do not skip them even if you are low level.

Resource rule of thumb: In your first month, follow the "50/30/20" rule: spend 50% of your gems on limited banners (saved until you have 14,400), 30% on stamina refills (for farming during events), and 20% on shop items (limited outfits, BP level skips if needed). After the first month, adjust to 70/20/10 (more pulls, less stamina). This ratio balances short-term progression needs with long-term pull savings.

Day 5: Events & Battle Pass

Understand the live-service elements that provide the bulk of your progression resources.

Event Calendar & Schedule: How to track upcoming events and plan your resource spending. Events run on a predictable 28-35 day cycle. Knowing what is coming lets you save the right resources (save diamonds before seasonal events, save stamina before double-drop events).

Battle Pass Guide: Whether to buy the BP, how to level it efficiently, and what rewards to prioritize. Even the free track is valuable. The paid track is the best resource-per-dollar purchase after the Monthly Card.

Event Efficiency Tier List: Which events give the best rewards per minute of playtime. S-Tier: Anniversary, seasonal events, free SSR events. D-Tier: 24-hour challenges, competitive rankings. Skip low-tier events guilt-free — your time is valuable.

Week 2: Strengthening Your Account

Now that you understand the systems, start optimizing your account for real progression. Guides for Week 2: Awakening Guide (how to unlock and upgrade memory awakenings — a significant power spike), Resonance Chain Guide (understanding memory dupes and R-levels — when to pull for dupes vs new memories), Abyss Beginner Guide (how to approach Abyss floors 1-6 — your first real endgame content), and Memory Farming Checklist (which memories to level in which order for maximum account power).

Week 2 is when you should set your first major account goal. Recommended goal: "Clear Abyss Floor 6 within 30 days." This is achievable for any F2P player who follows the starter pack guides. Floor 6 unlocks level 60 domains, which dramatically improves farming efficiency. Make this your first milestone and plan your resource allocation around it. Every resource decision should be evaluated against the question: "Does this help me clear Abyss Floor 6?"

Month 1: Endgame Prep

By the end of your first month, you should have: a solid understanding of all core systems, 2 main operatives built to level 50-60, a team capable of clearing Abyss floor 6, and a resource management plan for the next 3 months.

Guides to read in Month 1: Endgame Roadmap (what to work on after Abyss Floor 6 — domains, protocores, team optimization), F2P Replacements (how to substitute missing SSR memories with strong SR alternatives — essential for budget accounts), Abyss Floor 7-12 Guides (advanced strategies for pushing higher floors — do not rush, but understand what is coming), and the Spending Guide if you are considering purchases (the optimal spending path from F2P to dolphin).

Month 1 is also when you should evaluate your operative choice. Did Xavier or Zayne or Rafayel or Sylus click with you? If you picked based on aesthetics, check the gameplay style matches your preference. Xavier is a burst-execute DPS. Zayne is a control-freeze DPS. Rafayel is a sustained-DoT DPS. Sylus is a burst-vulnerability DPS. Each plays differently. It is okay to switch operatives in Month 1 — you have not invested enough to make switching painful. Pick the one whose gameplay you enjoy most, not the one the tier list says is strongest. You will play longer and improve faster if you enjoy the combat.

The most important advice: Love and Deepspace is a game, not a job. The starter pack guides teach you how to play efficiently, but efficiency is meaningless if you are not having fun. If a guide says "you must do X daily" and X makes you miserable, skip X. Some days you will only log in for 5 minutes to claim rewards — that is fine. Some days you will play for 3 hours exploring base interactions — that is also fine. Play at your pace, make choices that bring you joy, and use the guides as tools, not rules. A happy player who plays sub-optimally will progress further than a stressed player who min-maxes every grain of stamina.

Quick Reference Cards

Print these quick reference cards and keep them handy during your first month.

DAILY CHECKLIST
~20 min/day
(1) Log in, claim mail/BP rewards. (2) Complete 5 daily missions. (3) Spend all stamina (preferably on protocore dust domain). (4) Check event shop — buy high-value items first. (5) Interact with companion in base (feed, pet, check for visits). (6) Do 1-2 domain runs if stamina permits. (7) Check limited banner — do not pull unless you have 14,400 gems.
WEEKLY CHECKLIST
~60 min/week
(1) Run 9 weekly boss entries (3 per boss type, before Monday reset). (2) Complete all weekly missions. (3) Push Abyss floors (even 1-2 floors higher per week is good progress). (4) Complete all BP missions (especially event missions). (5) Check Crew Shop for daily SSR shards. (6) Review event progress — are you on track to clear the shop? (7) Claim all mail rewards before they expire.
DON'T EVER
Mistakes to avoid
Do not spend gems on standard banner. Do not pull on limited banner without 14,400 gems saved. Do not level every memory you own — focus on 6-8 memories for 2 teams. Do not farm the gold domain (sell bad protocores instead). Do not skip event content (most efficient rewards in the game). Do not ignore support memories (they are 30% of team power). Do not let stamina cap (one cap per day = 48 pulls lost per year).
FIRST MONTH GOALS
Set these targets:
Week 1: Complete tutorial, reach player level 20, unlock all basic systems. Week 2: Build 2 operatives to level 40, unlock level 50 domains. Week 3: Clear Abyss Floor 4, unlock level 60 domains (if possible). Week 4: Clear Abyss Floor 6, build 4 memories to level 50+, accumulate 7,000+ gems saved toward first limited banner. Month 2: First limited SSR pull, Abyss Floor 9 attempt.

Beyond the starter pack: After your first month, explore the other sections of Deepspace Hub based on your interests: Extreme Lab (math-heavy optimization), Memory Archive (memory database and art), Workshop (community builds and tools), Companion Guide (Affinity and base management), Event Archive (event strategy), Codes & Rewards (free resources), Fan Corner (community content), Boss Guides (boss mechanics), Wanderer Guides (enemy weaknesses), Style (outfits and photo mode), and Classified (lore and secrets). Each section builds on the foundation established in Basic Protocols. The hub has 200+ articles — you do not need to read them all. Read what interests you and what solves your current problem.
