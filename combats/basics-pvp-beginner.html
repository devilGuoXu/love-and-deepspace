Hunter Contest: Beginner Guide

The competitive mode explained — rankings, rewards, and how to get started without a perfect roster.

ENTRY_#0342026.06.30PVP

What Is Hunter Contest? A weekly PvP mode where you fight other players' defensive teams. You need 2 teams (6 operatives) to compete. Rewards include gems, exclusive currency, and titles. The season resets every 2 months.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Ranking System

Bronze-Silver: Beginner ranks. You can climb with 1 strong team + 1 filler team. Most opponents have incomplete rosters. Gold-Platinum: Mid ranks. You need 2 functional teams. Opponents have proper builds and synergies. Diamond-Master: End-game ranks. You need 2 optimized teams. Small mistakes lose matches. Legend: Top 500 players. Requires perfect gear and meta knowledge.

Weekly Rewards

Your rank at weekly reset determines your reward tier. Bronze: 80 gems + basic currency. Silver: 120 gems + medium currency. Gold: 180 gems + good currency. Platinum: 250 gems + premium currency. Diamond: 350 gems + premium currency + title. The gem jump from Platinum to Diamond is significant — push for Diamond if you can.

First Season Strategy

Week 1-2: Build your first team. Don't worry about the second team yet. Week 3-4: Build a functional second team using leftover memories. Even a team of level-40 SRs is better than no second team. Week 5-6: Optimize both teams. Focus on your win condition — your first team should win 80% of matches. Week 7-8: Push for your highest rank. Use all 5 daily attempts.

Team Building for Beginners

Your first PvP team should consist of one main DPS, one support, and one flex operative. Sylus is the recommended main DPS for beginners because his execute ability works at any gear level — it deals percentage-based damage that does not scale with stats. Zayne is the recommended support because his shields are effective even with minimal investment. Xavier is the recommended flex because his light attacks are easy to aim and his damage is consistent. Avoid Rafayel as your first PvP build — his burn-based damage requires high investment to be effective.

Memory requirements for PvP differ from PvE minimums. Your main DPS needs +6 memories on all six slots to be competitive in Silver. Your support operative can function with +3 memories in Silver. Your flex operative needs +6 on the Solar pair only, with +3 on the Lunar pieces. Do not invest in PvP-specific memories until you have a fully functional PvE team. PvP rewards are weekly, while PvE rewards are one-time — PvE takes priority for resource allocation.

Match Fundamentals and Rotation

Each PvP match follows a predictable flow. The first 10 seconds are the positioning phase where both teams assess each other's composition. Use this time to identify the enemy's main DPS and note their position. The next 30 seconds are the trading phase where both teams exchange damage and cooldowns. Do not use your ultimate during the trading phase unless you have a guaranteed kill. The final phase begins when one team's HP drops below 50% — this is when ultimates are used and the match is decided.

Resource tracking is the skill that separates Bronze from Gold players. Track three things during every match: your dodge cooldown (3 seconds), your enemy's ultimate energy (visible as a glow on their portrait), and your swap cooldown (3 seconds with 1-second lockout after damage). Never use your last dodge charge unless you are avoiding a confirmed hit. Always keep at least one dodge charge in reserve for unexpected attacks. If your dodge is on cooldown and the enemy Sylus has his ultimate ready, play defensively until your dodge is available again.

Hidden MMR adjusts based on win rate against similarly ranked opponents. 60% win rate increases MMR above visible rank. 40% decreases it. The system matches you by skill rather than rank.

Key Insight: Your defensive team matters less than your offensive team. The AI plays defense poorly — even a bad defense can win 20% of matches. Focus your resources on your offensive team. A strong offense with a weak defense climbs faster than a balanced team.

Summary: Build 1 strong team first. Add a 2nd team later. Offense > Defense. Push for Diamond if possible (best gem-to-effort ratio). Start early in the season — climbing in the last week is harder.
