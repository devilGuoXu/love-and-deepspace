Set Bonus Basics

Memory sets in Love and Deepspace work on a 2-piece and 4-piece system. Equipping 2 memories from the same set activates the 2-piece bonus (typically a stat boost: +15% ATK, +12% Elemental Damage, etc.). Equipping 4 memories from the same set activates the 4-piece bonus (typically a conditional effect: skills deal 25% more damage to enemies below 50% HP, Basic Attacks apply a stacking debuff, etc.). You cannot mix sets beyond 4+2 — you either run 4+2 (one main set and a secondary set bonus) or 2+2+2 (three set bonuses with no 4-piece).

The 4-piece bonus is almost always stronger than an additional 2-piece bonus from a third set. However, if you cannot complete a 4-piece set due to missing memories, 2+2+2 is a viable alternative that provides three stat bonuses without conditional triggers. The damage difference between a completed 4-piece set and a 2+2+2 configuration is typically 8-15% in favor of the 4-piece. Prioritize completing 4-piece sets for your main DPS, but do not be afraid to use 2+2+2 as a stepping stone.

Complete Set List

Light Seeker (Xavier)
2-Piece: +15% Light Damage. 4-Piece: Skills deal 25% more damage to enemies below 50% HP. Acquisition: Domain farming (Light domain), Xavier's limited banners, event shops. Priority: S-Tier for Xavier. The execute 4-piece bonus is one of the strongest in the game, synergizing perfectly with Xavier's high single-target burst. Complete this set before any other Xavier set.
Frost Weaver (Zayne)
2-Piece: +15% Frost Damage. 4-Piece: Enemies take 20% increased Frost damage for 8 seconds after being frozen. Acquisition: Domain farming (Frost domain), Zayne's limited banners. Priority: S-Tier for Zayne. The 4-piece enables freeze-detonate teams. The damage amp applies to all Frost damage from any source, making it valuable in co-op.
Inferno Waltz (Rafayel)
2-Piece: +15% Fire Damage. 4-Piece: Burning enemies take 15% increased damage from all sources. Acquisition: Domain farming (Fire domain), Rafayel's limited banners. Priority: A-Tier for Rafayel. The 4-piece is a universal damage amp that benefits the entire team, but requires consistent burn application which Rafayel provides easily.
Execute Protocol (Sylus)
2-Piece: +15% Dark Damage. 4-Piece: Dark skills ignore 30% of enemy Dark resistance. Acquisition: Domain farming (Dark domain), Sylus's limited banners. Priority: S-Tier for Sylus. Resistance ignore is multiplicative with all other damage bonuses, making this the best scaling 4-piece in the late game. The value increases as enemy resistance values increase in higher content.
Storm Chaser (Universal)
2-Piece: +12% ATK. 4-Piece: Energy Regen +20% and using Resonance gives +15% ATK for 10 seconds. Acquisition: Domain farming (all domains drop rate-up pieces). Priority: A-Tier universal set. Best for supports and secondary DPS who need energy generation. The ATK boost after Resonance is a nice bonus for burst rotations.
Crimson Oath (Universal)
2-Piece: +12% CRIT Rate. 4-Piece: After CRIT, gain 25% CRIT DMG for 5 seconds (stackable up to 2 times). Acquisition: Battle Pass, event shops, limited-time domain rotation. Priority: S-Tier universal set for DPS characters who can maintain high CRIT rate. The CRIT DMG stacking effect is one of the highest damage ceilings in the game.

Upcoming sets: Based on datamined content and developer hints, two new memory sets are expected in the 2.5 update: "Abyssal Tide" (likely a Water-element set for Rafayel with a 4-piece that applies a stacking damage-over-time effect) and "Shadow Sovereign" (likely a Dark-element set for Sylus with a 4-piece that grants damage immunity frames on Resonance use). These sets will expand build diversity significantly. Do not farm heavily for current sets if you are close to the 2.5 update — wait to see if the new sets are better for your operative.

By Operative

Xavier: Prioritize Light Seeker (4-piece). Secondary: 2-piece Crimson Oath (CRIT Rate) + 2-piece Storm Chaser (ATK). Best-in-slot: Light Seeker 4-piece + Crimson Oath 2-piece (or Storm Chaser 2-piece if you need energy). Xavier's execute bonus from Light Seeker is too strong to pass up even with superior stats on off-set pieces.

Zayne: Prioritize Frost Weaver (4-piece). Secondary: 2-piece Crimson Oath + 2-piece Storm Chaser. Best-in-slot: Frost Weaver 4-piece + Crimson Oath 2-piece. The freeze-detonate synergy is Zayne's highest damage ceiling. Without Frost Weaver, Zayne's damage drops by approximately 20%.

Rafayel: Prioritize Inferno Waltz (4-piece). Secondary: 2-piece Crimson Oath + 2-piece Storm Chaser. Best-in-slot: Inferno Waltz 4-piece + Crimson Oath 2-piece. Rafayel's burn-enabling 4-piece benefits the entire team, making it the most valuable support-set in co-op content.

Sylus: Prioritize Execute Protocol (4-piece). Secondary: 2-piece Crimson Oath + 2-piece Storm Chaser. Best-in-slot: Execute Protocol 4-piece + Crimson Oath 2-piece. Sylus's resistance ignore scales better than any other 4-piece as enemy stats increase in endgame content.

Set shortcut: If you are struggling to complete a specific operative's 4-piece, use Crimson Oath 4-piece as a universal substitute. Crimson Oath's 12% CRIT + stacking CDMG bonus works for every DPS operative and provides approximately 90% of the damage of their signature 4-piece. This lets you share protocores across operatives while you farm for their specific set. Crimson Oath is the best "bridge" set for multi-operative accounts.

Acquisition Sources

Memory sets are acquired through: Domain farming (each element domain drops its corresponding set — use stamina to farm the specific domain for your set), Limited banners (new sets debut on limited banners with rate-up — pulling on the banner gives pieces from the featured set), Event shops (events occasionally offer set selection boxes — always buy these), Battle Pass (the paid BP track offers set chests at level 30 and 50 — choose sets you need pieces for), and Protocore crafting (convert unwanted protocores into set-specific pieces at a 3:1 ratio — inefficient but targeted).

The most efficient acquisition strategy is domain farming for the 2-piece sets (they drop naturally as you farm for other reasons) and event/BP chests for the 4-piece completion. Chasing a specific 4-piece from a domain can take weeks of dedicated stamina. Instead, farm the element domain for protocore dust anyway (it is the most stamina-efficient domain), and over time you will accumulate set pieces naturally. Use event chests to fill the one or two pieces you are missing. This passive approach saves hundreds of stamina compared to active farming.

Completion Tracker System

Use this tracker system to monitor your set collection progress. For each set, track: how many unique pieces you own (out of 6 slots), the quality of each piece (SSR/SR/R), which pieces are equipped (slot 1-6 with protocore levels), which pieces you are missing (target acquisition method), and the combined set bonus activation status (2-piece active / 4-piece active / not active). A simple spreadsheet with these columns makes set management straightforward.

MINIMUM VIABLE
4 pieces of correct set + 2 pieces of any set
4-piece bonus is active. Quality does not matter yet. Even if 2 of the 4 pieces are SR, the 4-piece bonus is worth more than the stat loss from using SRs. Upgrade quality over time.
OPTIMIZED
4 SSR pieces of correct set + 2 SSR pieces of optimal secondary set
All pieces are SSR. Secondary set bonus is active. Protocores are +12+ with correct main stats. This is the target for endgame content clear.
MIN-MAXED
6 SSR pieces of correct set with perfect substats
6-piece same set gives best stat consolidation. All protocores +15 with ideal substats. This is the whale/competitive endgame target. The difference between optimized and min-maxed is approximately 5-8% damage.

Collection milestones: Track your collection against these milestones: Bronze (1 completed 2-piece set), Silver (1 completed 4-piece set for main operative), Gold (4+ completed 4-piece sets across all operatives), Platinum (6+ completed 4-piece sets, all with optimal secondary sets). Gold is the target for consistent Abyss floor 12 clears. Platinum is only needed for competitive ranking events.

Collection Priority

Set collection priority follows the same logic as operative priority. For your main operative: complete their signature 4-piece set at minimum-viable quality first (any rarity, any substats — just activate the bonus). Then upgrade the quality of those 4 pieces to SSR with correct main stats. Then add the optimal 2-piece secondary set. Finally, upgrade substats on all 6 pieces. For your secondary operative: complete a 4-piece universal set (Crimson Oath or Storm Chaser) at minimum-viable quality. Do not optimize their set until your main operative is at "optimized" quality.

Between event shops and BP, you get approximately 6-8 set selection boxes per year. Use these strategically: the first 4 boxes for your main operative's signature 4-piece, the next 2 boxes for their optimal secondary 2-piece, and the remaining boxes for your secondary operative's 4-piece. Never use a set selection box on a universal set (Crimson Oath, Storm Chaser) — those drop frequently from domains. Always use boxes on signature sets (Light Seeker, Frost Weaver, etc.) because those are domain-gated and have lower drop rates.

Account-level tracking: Keep a master list of all sets you have completed across all operatives. This helps you identify which pieces to farm next and which operatives are ready for content. A complete collection tracker (4 sets × 6 pieces = 24 unique pieces tracked) takes 15 minutes to set up and saves hours of confused farming. Update it whenever you get a new set piece or change a build. The tracker is especially useful before BP chest selection — you can see exactly which piece you are missing instead of guessing.

Set Synergy Combinations

The most powerful team compositions combine set bonuses that amplify each other. The Frost Weaver + Execute Protocol combination: Zayne's 4-piece makes enemies take 20% more Frost damage, while Sylus's 4-piece ignores 30% Dark resistance. If both operatives attack the same target, the enemy takes amplified damage from both elements, and the damage amp from Frost Weaver applies to Sylus's Dark damage too because it says "all sources." This creates a multiplicative damage stack that exceeds individual set capabilities.

Other powerful combinations: Inferno Waltz + Crimson Oath (burn damage = more CRIT DMG uptime through constant damage ticks), Light Seeker + Storm Chaser (execute threshold reached faster with energy-boosted rotations), Frost Weaver + Light Seeker (Zayne freezes for Xavier's execute window — the 8-second Frost amp perfectly overlaps with Xavier's burst rotation). The best teams are not just about individual set quality but how sets interact across team members. Use the Team DPS Simulation methods from LAB_11 to test different set combinations empirically.

Future-proofing: Do not over-invest in a single set at the expense of others. The meta shifts with each patch as new sets and memories release. A set that is S-tier today (Light Seeker) may be supplanted by a new set in 2.5. Maintain flexibility: keep all 6 protocore slots on your main DPS at +12 (not +15) until you are sure the set will remain optimal. The gold saved by not pushing to +15 can be redirected to farming a new set if the meta shifts. Adaptable accounts outperform rigid ones in live service games.
