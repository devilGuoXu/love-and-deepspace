SR Memory Review: F2P Options

Not all SRs are created equal. Some are worth the investment even if you have SSR alternatives.

ENTRY_#0942026.06.30CORE_SYSTEM
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

F2P Reality: Most players will rely on SR memories for their 2nd and 3rd Abyss teams. Investing in the right SRs can save months of farming. The wrong SRs are a dead end — you'll replace them eventually.

Best SR Memories (S-Tier)

Rapid Fire (Xavier): 2-pc: +12% ATK. 4-pc: +20% ATK for 6s after resonance. This is the best SR set in the game. The ATK buff is unconditional and works with any team comp. If you have this set with good substats, it can compete with low-investment SSR sets. Level to 50.

Iron Will (Sylus): 2-pc: +15% Energy Recharge. 4-pc: +25% DMG on next attack after dodge. Excellent transitional set. The dodge-buff playstyle teaches good habits. Level to 50.

Frostbite (Zayne): 2-pc: +12% HP. 4-pc: Shield strength +20%. Budget Frostguard. Works well for Zayne hybrid builds. Level to 40-50.

Good SR Memories (A-Tier)

Wild Spark (Rafayel): 2-pc: +10% CRIT Rate. 4-pc: Burn DMG +15%. A solid budget Inferno Blaze. The CRIT bonus is useful for any Rafayel build. Level to 40.

Guardian Protocol (Universal): 2-pc: +15% DEF. 4-pc: Shield on resonance. A niche pick for survival-heavy content (Floor 8 especially). Level to 30-40.

Energy Burst (Universal): 2-pc: +10% Energy Recharge. 4-pc: +15% DMG for 5s after ultimate. Decent filler set for any operative. Level to 30.

Do Not Invest (C-Tier)

Void Shard, Crystal Veil, Shadow Bind: These sets have weak set bonuses that don't scale. Avoid leveling them past 20. They're stat sticks at best. Salvage duplicates for enhancement materials.

SR vs SSR Upgrade Decision Framework

Deciding when to replace an SR with an SSR is a common resource management challenge. The rule of thumb is: replace an SR piece only when you have an SSR piece of the same set with equal or better substat quality. Replacing an SR with an SSR that has worse substats is a downgrade in practical performance even though the SSR has a higher level cap. The difference between an SR's base stats and an SSR's base stats at the same enhancement level is approximately 8% — two good substat rolls easily overcome this gap.

The specific breakpoint for replacement is SR level 50 versus SSR level 30. An SR at level 50 with three useful substats outperforms an SSR at level 30 with zero useful substats. An SR at level 50 with two useful substats is roughly equal to an SSR at level 30 with one useful substat. Do not replace your best SR pieces until you have an SSR piece that is enhanced to at least level 30 and has rolled at least two useful substats. Premature replacement is one of the most common mistakes that stalls mid-game account progression.

Substat Optimization for SR Pieces

SR memories have fewer total substat rolls than SSR memories — four rolls max (at +3, +6, +9, +12) compared to five rolls for SSR (at +3, +6, +9, +12, +15). This means SR pieces need higher substat quality to compete with SSR pieces. An SR with four useful substat rolls is equivalent to an SSR with three useful substat rolls. When evaluating SR pieces, use a stricter threshold: salvage any SR piece whose +3 roll is not a useful substat. The opportunity cost of leveling a suboptimal SR is higher because you will replace it eventually.

The best SR pieces for long-term use are those that roll into the same stat multiple times. An SR Rapid Fire piece with +12 CRIT Rate from two rolls into the same substat is a keeper that can serve as a permanent off-set piece even after you transition to full SSR sets. These "double roll" SR pieces are rare — approximately 10% of SR pieces achieve double rolls into a useful substat. When you find one, lock it and level it to 50. It may remain in your build for months after you start collecting SSR sets.

Key Insight: An SR at level 50 with good substat rolls outperforms an SSR at level 30 with bad substats. Don't automatically replace your SRs with SSRs — compare the actual stats. Many F2P endgame players still use 2-3 SR pieces in their final builds.

Summary: Rapid Fire (S-Tier) and Iron Will (A-Tier) are worth leveling to 50. Frostbite and Wild Spark are good to 40. Never take any SR past 50 — the resource cost isn't worth it. Salvage C-tier SRs without hesitation.
