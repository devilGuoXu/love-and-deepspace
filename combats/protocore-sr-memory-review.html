SR Memory Review: F2P Options

Not all SRs are created equal. Some are worth the investment even if you have SSR alternatives.

ENTRY_#0942026.06.30CORE_SYSTEM

F2P Reality: Most players will rely on SR memories for their 2nd and 3rd Abyss teams. Investing in the right SRs can save months of farming. The wrong SRs are a dead end — you'll replace them eventually.

Best SR Memories (S-Tier)

Rapid Fire (Xavier): 2-pc: +12% ATK. 4-pc: +20% ATK for 6s after resonance. This is the best SR set in the game. The ATK buff is unconditional and works with any team comp. If you have this set with good substats, it can compete with low-investment SSR sets. Level to 50.

Iron Will (Sylus): 2-pc: +15% Energy Recharge. 4-pc: +25% DMG on next attack after dodge. Excellent transitional set. The dodge-buff playstyle teaches good habits. Level to 50.

Frostbite (Zayne): 2-pc: +12% HP. 4-pc: Shield strength +20%. Budget Frostguard. Works well for Zayne hybrid builds. Level to 40-50.

Good SR Memories (A-Tier)

Wild Spark (Rafayel): 2-pc: +10% CRIT Rate. 4-pc: Burn DMG +15%. A solid budget Inferno Blaze. The CRIT bonus is useful for any Rafayel build. Level to 40.

Guardian Protocol (Universal): 2-pc: +15% DEF. 4-pc: Shield on resonance. A niche pick for survival-heavy content (Floor 8 especially). Level to 30-40.

Energy Burst (Universal): 2-pc: +10% Energy Recharge. 4-pc: +15% DMG for 5s after ultimate. Decent filler set for any operative. Level to 30.

Do Not Invest (C-Tier)

Void Shard, Crystal Veil, Shadow Bind: These sets have weak set bonuses that don't scale. Avoid leveling them past 20. They're stat sticks at best. Salvage duplicates for enhancement materials.

Key Insight: An SR at level 50 with good substat rolls outperforms an SSR at level 30 with bad substats. Don't automatically replace your SRs with SSRs — compare the actual stats. Many F2P endgame players still use 2-3 SR pieces in their final builds.

Summary: Rapid Fire (S-Tier) and Iron Will (A-Tier) are worth leveling to 50. Frostbite and Wild Spark are good to 40. Never take any SR past 50 — the resource cost isn't worth it. Salvage C-tier SRs without hesitation.
