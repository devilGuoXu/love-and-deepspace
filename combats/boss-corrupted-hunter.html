Boss Overview

The Corrupted Hunter is a humanoid elite boss encountered in mid-to-late Abyss floors and high-level Orbit stages. Unlike other bosses that rely on magic or massive hitboxes, the Hunter uses speed, combos, and parry mechanics to overwhelm players who button-mash.

Element Neutral (takes full damage from all)

Dodge Difficulty Very High (multi-hit combos)

Special Mechanic Parry 鈥?counters if you attack during its idle

The Parry Mechanic

The Hunter has a unique parry system. During certain phases, if you attack while it's in its "neutral" stance, it will parry your attack and counter with a fast slash. This is the #1 reason players fail against this boss.

How to Spot Parry Stance: The Hunter lowers its weapon to its side and stands still for 1-2 seconds. Its eyes glow red. If you attack during this stance, you get parried. Instead: stop attacking, bait the parry by walking close, then dodge the counter slash. The counter slash leaves a 2-second punish window.

Attack Patterns

Quick Slash Neutral 鈥?Fastest Attack

Tell: The Hunter twitches its shoulder 0.2 seconds before slashing. There is no sound cue 鈥?it's purely visual.

Behavior: A single horizontal slash. Deals 20% HP. Has a 0.15-second hitbox 鈥?the fastest in the game.

Dodge: The moment you see the shoulder twitch, dodge. Not earlier. Not later. If you wait for the weapon to move, you're already hit.

Punish: 1 second recovery. Land 2 normals or a quick RS, then back off.

Triple Strike Phase 1 鈥?Combo

Tell: The Hunter takes a wide stance and pulls its weapon back. This wind-up takes 0.6 seconds.

Behavior: Three rapid slashes in a left-right-left pattern. Each slash deals 15% HP. The third slash has a 0.1-second delay 鈥?designed to catch early dodgers.

Dodge: Left dodge 鈫?Right dodge 鈫?wait 0.1s 鈫?Left dodge. The third dodge must be delayed. Most players dodge all three at the same rhythm and get hit by the third.

Punish: After the third slash, the Hunter recovers for 2 seconds. Full N4 string or RS.

Lunging Thrust Phase 1 鈥?Gap Closer

Tell: The Hunter crouches slightly and points its weapon forward. A blue flash appears on the blade.

Behavior: A long-range lunge that covers half the arena. Deals 30% HP and applies a bleed that ticks for 5% HP per second for 3 seconds.

Dodge: Dodge sideways (left or right) at the moment the blue flash reaches maximum brightness. Do NOT dodge backward 鈥?the lunge has tracking and will catch you.

Punish: If you dodge successfully, the Hunter is staggered for 1.5 seconds. Use a charged attack or RS.

Whirlwind Slash Phase 2 鈥?Below 60% HP

Tell: The Hunter spins its weapon in a circle above its head for 1 second. A wind effect appears around it.

Behavior: A 360-degree spinning slash that hits twice. First hit deals 20% HP and knocks you back. Second hit deals 25% HP if you're in range. The second hit is delayed by 0.5 seconds.

Dodge: Dodge away from the first hit. Wait 0.5 seconds. Dodge toward the Hunter for the second hit 鈥?this puts you behind it for a punish.

Punish: After the second hit, the Hunter is exhausted for 2.5 seconds. Full N5 string + RS.

Shadow Step Phase 2 鈥?Below 60% HP

Tell: The Hunter dissolves into dark mist. No sound cue 鈥?purely visual.

Behavior: The Hunter teleports behind you and immediately attacks with a Quick Slash. The entire sequence takes 0.8 seconds from dissolve to attack.

Dodge: The moment you see the mist, start moving. Do not dodge immediately. Wait 0.4 seconds (the teleport takes time), then dodge backward. The backward dodge puts you out of range of the follow-up slash.

Punish: If you dodge the slash, the Hunter is vulnerable for 1 second. Quick RS or 2 normals.

Berserker Flurry Enrage 鈥?Below 25% HP

Tell: The Hunter roars and its weapon glows red. This is the enrage trigger.

Behavior: A 7-hit combo. Each hit deals 10% HP. The combo lasts 4 seconds. The final hit is a heavy slam dealing 35% HP. The Hunter is completely vulnerable during the entire combo but also uninterruptible.

Dodge: You cannot dodge all 7 hits individually. Strategy: perfect dodge the first hit (enters slow-mo), run away during slow-mo, then dodge the final slam. The 0.8s i-frames from the first dodge cover hits 2-4. Stay out of range for hits 5-6. Dodge the final slam.

Punish: After the final slam, the Hunter collapses for 4 seconds. This is your kill window. Everything you have.

Phase Summary

Phase 1 (100%-60%) Quick Slash, Triple Strike, Lunging Thrust, Parry Stance

Enrage (25%-0%) Adds Berserker Flurry. Attack speed +25%. Parry windows shorter.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Attacking during Parry Stance. This is the #1 killer. The Hunter's parry deals 40% HP and stuns you for 2 seconds. If you see the red-eyed stance, stop attacking immediately. Bait the parry, dodge the counter, then punish.

Mistake #2: Button-mashing through Triple Strike. The third hit has a rhythm break. If you dodge three times at the same speed, the third dodge comes out too early. Consciously pause before the third dodge.

Mistake #3: Trying to DPS through Berserker Flurry. The Hunter is uninterruptible during the flurry. Your attacks will hit, but so will all 7 of its hits. You will lose the trade. Defend first, then punish.

Mistake #4: Dodging backward against the Lunging Thrust. The thrust has forward tracking. A backward dodge keeps you in the hitbox. Sideways dodge is the only safe direction.

The Bottom Line: The Corrupted Hunter is the most technical boss in the game 鈥?it fights like a player, and it punishes players who fight on autopilot. Every attack has a distinct tell. Every combo has a rhythm that can be learned. The key is patience: wait for the openings, never attack into parry stance, and respect the enrage. Master the Hunter, and you've mastered the combat system itself.
