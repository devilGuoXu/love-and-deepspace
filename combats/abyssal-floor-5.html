Abyssal Trials: Floor 5

The Crystalline Wyrm — a DPS check with punishing enrage mechanics.

ENTRY_#1292026.06.30ABYSS

Floor 5 Threshold: Recommended power level 22,000. Solo-team floor. Enrage timer: 3 minutes. This is the first floor with a hard DPS check — you must meet the damage requirement or you cannot clear.

Boss Overview — Crystalline Wyrm

A massive serpentine boss with crystal armor. The armor reduces all damage by 50% while active. Armor shatters when you deal sufficient damage to a limb. Each shattered limb reduces the damage reduction by 10%. Four limbs total. Fully unarmored boss takes 100% damage. Target: Shatter all four limbs before enrage.

Wyrm attacks: Tail Sweep (wide AoE, back half of arena, dodge towards the front), Crystal Shard (projectile volley, dodge sideways), Roar (AoE stun, iframe through it). The Roar has a 1.2s telegraph — use resonance skill for iframes. The Tail Sweep is the most damaging attack — never stay behind the wyrm.

Limb Priority

Front Left (Frost resistance) → Front Right (Fire resistance) → Back Left (Light resistance) → Back Right (Dark resistance). The front limbs have lower HP than the back limbs. Shatter front limbs first for faster damage ramp. Focus one limb at a time — spreading damage is inefficient.

Each limb has approximately 25% of the wyrm's total HP. Total damage needed to fully remove armor: roughly 100% of the wyrm's HP in additional damage. This effectively doubles the total HP you need to deal over the fight. Efficiency matters.

Recommended Team

DPS: High single-target burst. Xavier (Light Seeker) is optimal — his resonance CRIT DMG bonus aligns well with limb focus. Sub-DPS: Sylus (Dark Champion) for energy refund and execute. Support: Zayne (Frostguard) for shields, or any healer. All three operatives should be level 60+ with +12 memories minimum. Protocore priority: CRIT DMG > ATK% > CRIT Rate.

Enrage Timer

At 3:00, the wyrm enrages. All attacks deal 200% damage. Attack speed increases by 50%. You cannot survive an enraged fight for more than 15-20 seconds. If you haven't killed the wyrm by 3:10, you've lost. If you reach 1:00 remaining with more than 2 limbs still armored, restart.

DPS check formula: The wyrm has roughly 1.5M effective HP (base 750K + 750K from armor). With a team DPS of 8,000 (combined), you need ~188 seconds. With 10,000 team DPS, ~150 seconds. Use the training dummy to measure your team's sustained DPS before attempting the floor.

Key Insight: Use your resonance + ultimate combo at the start to immediately shatter the first front limb. Opening with full burst gives you a 10% damage reduction advantage for the entire fight. The first 10 seconds are the most important in this entire floor.

Summary: Shatter Front Left → Front Right first. Save resonance for limb focus. 3-minute enrage timer is hard. Measure your DPS before attempting. Open with full burst for early advantage.
