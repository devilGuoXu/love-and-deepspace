THREAT LEVEL: EXTREME

Advanced Abyss Techniques

Beyond gear and levels — master the mechanics that separate good clears from perfect ones.

ENTRY_#1272026.06.30CHALLENGE

Goal: Turn 1-star scrappy clears into 3-star flawless victories. These techniques apply across all floors but matter most in Floors 10-12.

Positioning Fundamentals

Wall Hugging: Positioning yourself with your back to a wall prevents flanking. Use this against Rogue-type enemies. Corner Kiting: Lure enemies into corners to group them for AoE. Works on all non-boss floors. Perpendicular Movement: Move at 90-degree angles to boss attacks. Most cone attacks track slightly — perpendicular movement minimizes hitbox.

Aggro Management

The operative with the highest DPS generates the most aggro. Swap to your support to let the tankier operative take aggro. Use Zayne's shield to "taunt" — enemies slightly prefer shielded targets. In multi-elite fights, switch targets every 10 seconds to spread damage and aggro evenly.

Wave Control

Spawn Manipulation: Enemies spawn at fixed locations. Pre-position before the spawn trigger. Add Delay: Some spawns are triggered by boss HP thresholds. Stop DPS at 51% to prepare for the 50% add spawn. Wave Stacking: If you're strong enough, leave 1 low-HP mob alive while you damage the next wave. Both waves active = more enemies = more energy from kills = faster ults.

Animation Cancels

Basic attacks can be cancel into dodge or skill at any point. Don't wait for the attack to finish — cancel the recovery frames. Practice the rhythm: attack → cancel → dodge → attack. With practice, this becomes muscle memory and increases your effective DPS by roughly 15%.

Key Insight: The most common 3-star killer is not damage — it's getting hit. Each hit you take costs you time (stagger animation + recovery). A perfect dodge run with lower DPS often clears faster than a high-DPS run where you take hits. Focus on not getting hit. The damage will come.

Summary: Position against walls. Swap aggro with support. Pre-position for spawns. Cancel attack animations with dodges. The enemy can't kill you if they can't hit you. These techniques add 15-30% effective performance beyond your gear score.
