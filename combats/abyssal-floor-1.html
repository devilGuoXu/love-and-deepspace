Abyssal Trials: Floor 1

The starting point — learn the Abyss format and claim your first rewards.

ENTRY_#1302026.06.30FLOOR_1
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Floor 1 Threshold: Recommended power level 4,000. Single team, one phase. Pure introduction — no special mechanics.

Overview

Floor 1 is a tutorial-level introduction to the Abyssal Trials format. A single wave of weak Wanderers with no boss. Any team of two operatives at level 1 with basic memories can clear this floor. The purpose is to teach you the scoring system (time + damage = stars) and reward structure.

Enemies

Two waves of standard Wanderers. Wave 1: 3x Void Spawn (melee). Wave 2: 2x Void Spawn + 1x Void Archer (ranged). All enemies have approximately 2,000 HP each. No elemental shields or special mechanics. Basic attacks kill them in 2-3 hits.

Strategy

Group the melee spawns with positioning, then AoE them down. Dodge the Void Archer's projectiles (visually telegraphed). Use resonance skill on the Archer to finish. No need to save ultimates — use them liberally to speed up the clear.

Rewards

First clear: 60 diamonds, 3 Basic Resonance Crystals. 3-star clear bonus: additional 30 diamonds. Weekly reset: 20 diamonds. This floor is free diamonds — clear it every week even if you can't progress further.

Team Composition for Floor 1

Floor 1 is designed to be cleared with any team composition. There are no elemental shields, no DPS checks, and no mechanics that require specific characters. You can bring your lowest-level operatives and still clear within the time limit. This makes Floor 1 an excellent place to test new team compositions, practice rotation timing, or farm the weekly clear rewards with minimal effort. The floor does not require any Protocore investment, so even characters with unenhanced equipment will perform adequately.

Weekly Reset Value

Each weekly reset, Floor 1 provides 20 diamonds for clearing. Over the course of a month, this amounts to 80 diamonds, or approximately half a pull. While this may not seem significant, combined with all other Abyssal floors, the weekly reset diamonds from the Abyss form a meaningful portion of a F2P player\'s monthly income. Never skip your weekly Abyss clears, even if you can only complete the first few floors. The diamonds add up over time and require minimal time investment per floor.

Speed Clear Strategy for Three Stars

Three-star clear on Floor 1 requires completing the encounter within 90 seconds while maintaining all operatives above 50% HP. The threshold is generous ? any team with average gear can meet it with proper execution. Open with your highest burst operative and use resonance immediately when the wave spawns. The initial Wanderer group clusters together for approximately 2 seconds after spawning, giving a perfect AoE window. After clearing the trash wave, the boss spawns with a 1-second delay ? use this window to reposition behind the boss for bonus backstab damage.

Beyond the First Clear

After clearing Floor 1 with 3 stars, use it as a testing ground for new team compositions and rotation practice. Floor 1's low difficulty makes it ideal for experimenting with team synergies without the pressure of failing high-floor attempts. Test new memory sets here before taking them into harder content. A full test run on Floor 1 with a new composition takes 2 minutes and reveals whether the team's damage output and sustain are balanced. If a composition struggles on Floor 1, it is not viable for higher floors regardless of power rating.

Floor 1 serves as a benchmark for your account's progression. If you can clear Floor 1 with 3 stars in under 60 seconds, your account is ready for Floors 2-4. If your clear time exceeds 90 seconds, your main DPS needs more investment. Use Floor 1 as a weekly test to gauge improvement.

Pro Tip: Use this floor to practice swap-cancelling and resonance rotation. The enemies are harmless, so you can focus purely on muscle memory. Try clearing without taking any damage for practice.

Summary: Free floor, free diamonds. No mechanics to worry about. Use it for practice or speed.
