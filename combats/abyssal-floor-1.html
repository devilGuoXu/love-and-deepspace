Abyssal Trials: Floor 1

The starting point — learn the Abyss format and claim your first rewards.

ENTRY_#1302026.06.30FLOOR_1

Floor 1 Threshold: Recommended power level 4,000. Single team, one phase. Pure introduction — no special mechanics.

Overview

Floor 1 is a tutorial-level introduction to the Abyssal Trials format. A single wave of weak Wanderers with no boss. Any team of two operatives at level 1 with basic memories can clear this floor. The purpose is to teach you the scoring system (time + damage = stars) and reward structure.

Enemies

Two waves of standard Wanderers. Wave 1: 3x Void Spawn (melee). Wave 2: 2x Void Spawn + 1x Void Archer (ranged). All enemies have approximately 2,000 HP each. No elemental shields or special mechanics. Basic attacks kill them in 2-3 hits.

Strategy

Group the melee spawns with positioning, then AoE them down. Dodge the Void Archer's projectiles (visually telegraphed). Use resonance skill on the Archer to finish. No need to save ultimates — use them liberally to speed up the clear.

Rewards

First clear: 60 diamonds, 3 Basic Resonance Crystals. 3-star clear bonus: additional 30 diamonds. Weekly reset: 20 diamonds. This floor is free diamonds — clear it every week even if you can't progress further.

Pro Tip: Use this floor to practice swap-cancelling and resonance rotation. The enemies are harmless, so you can focus purely on muscle memory. Try clearing without taking any damage for practice.

Summary: Free floor, free diamonds. No mechanics to worry about. Use it for practice or speed.
