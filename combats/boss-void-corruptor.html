Void Corruptor

It splits. It spreads. It consumes. Kill the right one, or die by a thousand shadows.

ENTRY_#113 2026.06.29 ELITE_BOSS

The Core Insight: The Void Corruptor is not one enemy — it's a puzzle that attacks back. Its clone mechanic punishes players who attack blindly. Hit the wrong target and you take damage. Hit the right one and the boss staggers. This fight is as much about observation as it is about combat. Learn to read the tells, and the clones become your greatest advantage.

Boss Overview

The Void Corruptor is a dark-element elite boss found in high-level Abyss floors and the deepest Orbit stages. It specializes in area denial, clone generation, and corruption damage-over-time effects. It is widely considered one of the most mechanically complex bosses in the game.

Element Dark
Weakness Light (Xavier) — reveals real clone
Resistance High Dark resistance. Immune to DoT.
Dodge Difficulty High (unpredictable timings)
Special Mechanic Clone spawning + Corruption Zone

The Clone Mechanic — How It Works

At 80% and 50% HP, the Corruptor spawns 2 clones. All three targets look identical. Here's how to identify the real one:

How to Spot the Real Corruptor:

1. The real Corruptor casts a shadow that moves naturally. Clones have static shadows.
2. The real Corruptor's attacks deal damage. Clone attacks deal 50% damage but apply a "Void Mark" debuff that stacks.
3. Light-element attacks (Xavier RS) make the real Corruptor glow briefly — clones don't react.
4. The real Corruptor has a 0.5-second delay before attacking. Clones attack instantly.
5. When you attack a clone, you take 15% recoil damage. When you attack the real one, you don't.

Clone Kill Strategy: If you kill a clone, it explodes after 3 seconds, dealing 30% AoE damage. If you kill both clones, the real Corruptor is staggered for 4 seconds. Killing clones is often better than ignoring them — the explosion damages the real boss.

Attack Patterns

Shadow Bolt Primary — Phase 1

Tell: The Corruptor raises its hand. A dark orb forms over 1 second.

Behavior: Fires a slow-moving orb that tracks your position. Deals 20% HP. Applies a Void Mark stack (4 stacks = corruption explosion for 50% HP).

Dodge: The orb is slow. You can outrun it. Only dodge if it's about to hit you. Save your dodge for other attacks.

Punish: After firing, the Corruptor recovers for 2 seconds. Land 3-4 hits or one RS.

Corruption Zone Phase 1 — Area Denial

Tell: The Corruptor slams the ground. A dark purple circle appears on the floor.

Behavior: A 5-meter radius zone that deals 10% HP per second to anyone inside. Lasts 8 seconds. The Corruptor can place up to 2 zones simultaneously.

Strategy: Immediately leave the zone. Do not fight inside it. If the Corruptor places a zone over the entire center, fight from the edges. The zone disappears after 8 seconds.

Punish: The Corruptor has a 1-second recovery after placing the zone. Quick hit and move.

Void Slash Phase 1 — Melee Range

Tell: The Corruptor's arm glows purple. It pulls its hand back for 0.6 seconds.

Behavior: A wide dark slash that covers a 120-degree arc. Deals 25% HP and applies 2 Void Mark stacks.

Dodge: Dodge toward the Corruptor (through the slash). The hitbox is thinner near the body. Backward dodge won't clear the arc range.

Punish: 1.5-second recovery. Use a charged attack or quick RS.

Clone Spawn HP Threshold — 80% and 50%

Trigger: At 80% HP, the Corruptor teleports to the center. The screen darkens. Two clones emerge from the shadows.

Behavior: All three (real + 2 clones) attack simultaneously. Clones have 40% of the real boss's HP. If any clone survives for 15 seconds, it casts "Void Eruption" — full-arena AoE for 35% HP.

Strategy: Identify the real boss immediately (shadow test or light test). Focus the real one while AoE-damaging the clones. Kill clones before 15s timer. Prioritize clone elimination if you're taking too much damage.

Reward: Killing both clones staggers the real boss for 4 seconds. Full burst window.

Void Cascade Phase 2 — Below 50% HP

Tell: The Corruptor raises both arms. Multiple dark circles appear on the floor.

Behavior: 5 corruption zones spawn in sequence, each 1 second apart. Each zone lasts 4 seconds. The zones cover 70% of the arena in a cascade pattern.

Strategy: Move to the last zone that spawns — it's the safe spot. The cascade follows a predictable pattern (left to right). Position yourself at the right side of the arena, then move left as zones appear behind you.

Punish: After all 5 zones spawn, the Corruptor exhausts for 2 seconds. Attack from the safe zone position.

Soul Rend Enrage — Below 20% HP

Tell: The Corruptor's eyes turn white. It releases a pulse that pushes you to the arena edge.

Behavior: A corruption zone forms around the Corruptor (3-meter radius). The zone follows the boss as it moves. Staying inside the zone deals 15% HP per second. The zone lasts for the remainder of the fight.

Strategy: This is a "fight in the danger zone" mechanic. You cannot avoid the zone — it follows the boss. You have to DPS through it. Use healing abilities, if available. Keep attack uptime high to minimize time spent in the zone.

Punish: The Corruptor has no new recovery windows. Every attack punish window is shorter by 0.5 seconds. Adjust your combos accordingly.

Phase Summary

Phase 1 (100%-80%) Shadow Bolt, Corruption Zone, Void Slash
Clone Phase 1 (80%) 2 clones spawn. 15s timer. Kill or identify real boss.
Phase 2 (80%-50%) Same attacks. Clones may respawn if not all killed.
Clone Phase 2 (50%) 2 clones spawn again. Same mechanic.
Void Cascade (50%-20%) 5-zone cascade. Predictable pattern.
Enrage (20%-0%) Soul Rend — persistent zone around boss.

Recommended Team Composition

XAVIER (Main DPS — Mandatory)

Why: Xavier is the single best operative for this fight. Light element reveals the real clone, letting your entire team focus the correct target. His RS interrupts clone spawn animations. Without Xavier, the clone identification becomes significantly harder.

Build: 6-piece Solar Resonance. Prioritize Crit Rate for consistent clone identification.

RAFAYEL (Sub-DPS)

Why: Rafayel's AoE DoTs damage all clones simultaneously while you focus the real boss. His sustained damage is effective during the long clone phases. However, his DoT damage is reduced against the Dark-element Corruptor.

Build: 6-piece Tidal Wrath. ATK% focus.

ZAYNE (Support)

Why: Zayne provides survivability through HP scaling. His Frost damage is neutral against Dark. His freeze can interrupt clone attacks, reducing the pressure during clone phases.

Build: 4 Permafrost + 2 Frostbloom. HP% for survivability.

Optimal Team: Xavier (Main — mandatory) + Rafayel (AoE clone damage) + Zayne (survivability). Xavier is non-negotiable for this fight. The clone identification is too punishing without Light element.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Attacking clones without identifying them first. Every hit on a clone deals 15% recoil damage to you. If you blindly AoE, you'll kill yourself faster than the boss will. Always identify the real boss before committing damage.

Mistake #2: Ignoring the 15-second clone timer. If clones survive for 15 seconds, the Void Eruption deals 35% AoE damage. If both clones survive, you take 70% damage in one hit. Prioritize clone elimination even if it means less damage on the real boss.

Mistake #3: Standing still during Void Cascade. The zones spawn in sequence. If you stand still, you'll be inside the third or fourth zone. Keep moving right to left as the cascade progresses.

Mistake #4: Playing without Xavier. The clone mechanic is significantly harder without Light element. If you don't have Xavier built, the fight is still winnable — but you'll need to use the shadow test (observe which target has a natural-moving shadow). This is much harder to see in combat.

The Bottom Line: The Void Corruptor is the most mechanically complex boss in the game. It demands observation, target prioritization, and spatial awareness. Bring Xavier. Learn the clone tells. Respect the 15-second timer. Move through the cascade. And when the Soul Rend activates, go all in — the longer the fight lasts, the more damage you take from the persistent zone. This is a race against your own HP bar. Finish it before it finishes you.
