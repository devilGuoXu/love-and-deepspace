Short-Term (Next 2–3 Patches)

Disclaimer: These are informed predictions, not leaks or insider information. We analyze developer patterns, job postings, and industry trends. Actual content may differ. Use this as a planning guide, not a guarantee. Never spend real money based on predictions — only spend on confirmed content.

NEW OPERATIVE (HIGHLY LIKELY)
The game will introduce at least 1 new operative within the next 2 patches. Pattern: new operatives are teased in the story 1–2 patches before release. If a new character appears in the main story with combat animations, expect their banner within 2 patches. Based on story hints, the new operative may be connected to the Onychinus faction — potentially a Dark-element DPS or a support with unique mechanics.
ELEMENT EXPANSION
A new element type (beyond Light/Dark/Fire/Ice) is possible but unlikely in the short term — the devs are still balancing the existing 4. More likely: existing elements gain new interactions (e.g., Fire + Light = burn that debuffs enemy DEF). This would deepen team-building without overwhelming new players with a fifth element to build.
NEW ABYSS FLOORS (FLOOR 13–15)
Abyss will almost certainly expand to floor 15–18 within the next 3 patches. Floor 13+ will introduce new mechanics: rotating elemental shields (enemies cycle through weaknesses every 30s), multi-wave boss fights (2–3 mini-bosses before the main boss), and environmental hazards (periodic damage zones). Preparation: Save at least 2 fully leveled teams (8 operatives at 50+) for new Abyss floors. Having only 4 operatives built will be insufficient for the expanded roster requirement.

"Balance is not a destination — it is a continuous process. Each patch we learn more about how players use our characters, and we adjust accordingly. The Abyss will continue to evolve alongside our roster."

— Developer Letter, Patch 1.2

Medium-Term (3–6 Months)

PROTOCORE SUBSTAT LOCK / REROLL
The devs have teased this feature in developer notes. Expected implementation: A new item (Protocore Lock) that lets you lock 1 substat before enhancing. Cost: shop currency or event rewards. Impact: This is the single biggest QoL improvement possible for endgame players. Start saving protocore dust and lock items now. When this feature drops, being able to lock a high CRIT Rate roll before enhancing will dramatically reduce protocore farming RNG.
GUILD / CREW EXPANSION
The existing crew system is bare-bones — it has minimal interaction and no cooperative content. Expect guild boss content (weekly raid against a shared boss), guild-exclusive shops (memory shards, protocore dust), and guild cooperative events within 6 months. The devs' recent job posting for a "multiplayer content designer" strongly suggests this direction.
CHARACTER BOND SYSTEM EXPANSION
Character-specific combat bonuses unlocked through bond levels. This rewards maining a character and adds a new progression system. If bond level unlocks combat bonuses, you will want all 4 operatives at max bond. Start building relationships with all operatives equally now — do not wait until the feature is announced.

SECOND ANNIVERSARY
The biggest event of the year
Expect: free SSR selector (wider pool than first anniversary), double top-up reset, new limited operative, and a new permanent game mode. The first anniversary set the pattern — expect the second to follow but with bigger rewards. Anniversary is the single most important event to save for.
POWERCREEP ACCELERATION
Year-2 memories will have higher stat budgets
As the game matures, new memories and protocores will have higher stat budgets. The gap between launch memories and year-2 memories will grow. This is natural for gacha games — your launch memories will still be usable but not optimal. Do not feel pressured to replace everything immediately, but be aware that year-2 options will be stronger.
COLLAB EVENTS (SPECULATIVE)
Crossovers with other popular franchises
Deepspace is popular enough for crossovers. A collab with another popular game or anime would introduce limited collab memories. These would be the rarest items in the game — never returning if the collab license expires. If a collab happens for a franchise you love, it may be worth breaking your usual savings plan.

Pro Tip: The best resource for future predictions is the developer's official communication channel. Read every "Developer's Note" and "Letter from the Team" — they often hint at upcoming features. Join the beta test program if available (beta testers see content 2–4 weeks before release). Job postings are another useful signal: if the devs are hiring for a "multiplayer content designer," expect more co-op content. If they hire for "AI combat designer," expect smarter enemies.

How We Predict

Our predictions are based on three data sources:

Developer patterns: The devs follow consistent update cycles (6-week patches), consistent buff patterns (first 2 patches for new characters), and consistent reward scaling (anniversary > seasonal > normal). These patterns are reliable because they are built into the development cycle.

Community data mining: The community data mines each patch for unreleased content. While we do not share leaks, data mining often reveals file names, asset types, and code patterns that suggest upcoming features. A new "boss_mechanics" file type suggests new boss mechanics. A new "guild" folder suggests guild features.

Industry trends: Gacha games follow industry-wide patterns. After year 1, games typically add: (1) Guild/ crew systems. (2) Substat rerolling. (3) Collab events. (4) Higher-difficulty endgame modes. (5) Account-wide progression systems. Deepspace is following this pattern closely.

Risk & Uncertainty

Important: All predictions carry risk. The devs may change their plans due to player feedback, technical limitations, or business decisions. The substat lock feature may be delayed, the second anniversary may have different rewards, and collabs may never happen. Never spend real money based on predictions. Only spend on confirmed content.

To manage risk: (1) Save resources but do not hoard them to the point where your account suffers. (2) Build strong teams for current content first, then save for future content. (3) Diversify your roster — do not invest everything in one character in case the meta shifts. (4) Set a personal savings target and stick to it — do not keep increasing the target because "the next banner might be better."
