BASIC PROTOCOLS — ABYSS TEAMS

Abyss Floor Team Library

Proven team compositions for each Abyss floor — plug in your operatives and clear.

ENTRY_#0592026.07.01ABYSS_TEAMS

Different from Team Archetypes (#049): #049 covers generic team frameworks. This guide gives specific floor-by-floor compositions with actual operative names and minimum gear thresholds. Use #049 to understand the theory, use this guide to execute.

Floor 7 — Entry Gear Check

Recommended Power: 45k. Team: Xavier (DPS, Light Seeker) + Zayne (Support, Frostguard). Threshold: Xavier level 50, memories +9, 40% CRIT. Zayne level 40, memories +6. Strategy: Kill the Sentinel shield first (Zayne freeze + Xavier burst). Then focus the Commander. Save Ardent Oath for the Commander's phase shift. Clear time target: 2:30.

Alternative (no Xavier): Rafayel (DPS, Inferno Blaze) + Sylus (Sub-DPS, Dark Champion). Rafayel handles add waves, Sylus executes the Commander below 30%. Requires level 50 on both.

Floor 8 — Survival Check

Recommended Power: 55k. Team: Zayne (Sustain, Frostguard + HP build) + Xavier (DPS). Threshold: Zayne level 55, 28k+ HP, memories +9. Xavier level 50, memories +9. Strategy: Zayne carries this floor. His shields negate the DoT aura. Stay in Zayne as much as possible, swap to Xavier only for burst windows. DoT ticks for 5% HP/sec — Zayne's shield refresh every 8s keeps you alive.

Floor 9 — Double Elite

Recommended Power: 65k. Team: Rafayel (AoE DPS) + Xavier (Burst DPS) + Sylus (Execute). Threshold: All three level 55+, memories +9. Strategy: Group the two elites with positioning (stay center). Rafayel burn + Xavier AoE resonance hits both. When one elite reaches 30%, Sylus executes it while Rafayel continues burning the other. Never split damage unevenly — keep their HP bars close.

Floor 10 — Dual Elements

Recommended Power: 80k. Team: Xavier (Light) + Sylus (Dark) + Zayne (Flex). Threshold: Main DPS level 60, memories +12. Strategy: Fire + Ice shields rotate every 20s. Xavier breaks Ice shields. Sylus breaks Fire shields (via Dark damage over time). Zayne provides sustain during shield phases. Match your active operative to the current shield color.

Floor 11 — Pure DPS Check

Recommended Power: 95k. Team: Xavier (Hypercarry) + Zayne (Buff) + Sylus (Energy Battery). Threshold: Xavier level 65, memories +12, 60%+ CRIT, 140%+ CRIT DMG. 3:30 enrage. Strategy: Hypercarry team — Xavier deals 80% of damage. Zayne provides shields and HP buff. Sylus builds energy and provides execute insurance below 30%. Pre-fill Xavier's energy before entering the boss room.

Floor 12 — Final Boss

Recommended Power: 100k+. Team: Xavier (Light Seeker main DPS) + Sylus (Dark Champion execute) + Zayne (Frostguard sustain). Threshold: All three level 65+, memories +12, protocores with correct main stats. Strategy: Break the right add first (it has a dangerous AoE). Use Ardent Oath when both adds spawn. In boss phase, dodge the triple laser (sideways, not backward). Execute rotation at 30% boss HP.

Key Insight: If you're missing the recommended operatives, substitute with the same-role alternative. Xavier = any burst DPS. Zayne = any sustain support. Rafayel = any AoE specialist. Sylus = any single-target execute. The archetype matters more than the specific name.

Summary: F7: Gear check. F8: Zayne carry. F9: Dual DPS. F10: Element match. F11: Hypercarry burst. F12: Full team execution. Archetype > specific operative.
