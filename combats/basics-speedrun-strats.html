BASIC PROTOCOLS — SPEEDRUN

Boss Speedrun & Optimization

Techniques to minimize clear time — skip mechanics, align burst windows, and optimize every second.

ENTRY_#0482026.07.01SPEEDRUN

Goal: Speedrun strategies are for players who have already cleared content and want faster clear times. These techniques are high-risk, high-reward — they optimize for time, not safety. Not recommended for first-time clears.

Burst Alignment

The most important speedrun concept: aligning all your damage multipliers in a single 3-second window. The ideal burst window: Boss is staggered (+20% damage) → Resonance active (set bonus proc) → Ardent Oath ready → All offensive buffs active. A properly aligned burst window can deal 40-50% of a boss's HP in 3 seconds.

Pre-fill energy before the burst window. Swap to your support first to generate their resonance safely (their damage doesn't matter for the burst). Swap to your sub-DPS to apply debuffs. Finally swap to main DPS and unleash everything. Do NOT use resonance or Oath outside the burst window unless you're about to overcap energy.

Mechanic Skipping

Many boss mechanics are triggered by HP thresholds or time. If you can deal enough damage fast enough, you can skip mechanics entirely. Example: Boss Phase 2 triggers at 50% HP. If you deal 55% damage in a single burst window (from 100% to 45%), you skip Phase 1 entirely and the boss enters Phase 2 with your burst already done.

Common skips: Crystalline Wyrm limb shatter — if you deal 30%+ damage to a limb in one burst, it shatters instantly without the usual animation delay (-2s per limb). Twin Harbingers — if you AoE both twins to 5% simultaneously, you save the 5s kill window management entirely.

Rotation Compression

Speedrun rotations are faster than standard rotations because they skip filler. Standard: Auto x3 → Charged → Resonance → Auto x2 → Swap (4s). Speedrun: Resonance (immediately on spawn) → Swap Cancel → Auto x1 → Oath → Swap (1.5s). You sacrifice sustained DPS for burst speed. Only use compression at the start of a fight or after a boss phase transition.

Animation cancels are mandatory for speedrunning. Every 0.5s you save per cancel adds up. In a 60s fight, 10 saved seconds from animation cancels is ~16% more effective DPS.

Gear for Speed

Speedrun protocore priority: ATK% (raw damage) > Energy Recharge (faster ults) > CRIT DMG (bigger bursts) > CRIT Rate (consistency loses relevance in speedruns — you can reset for CRIT RNG). A speedrun build might have 50% CRIT rate but 200% CRIT DMG — you restart if you don't CRIT.

Key Insight: Speedrunning is a reset game. A 10-second clear attempt that fails 9/10 times is still faster on average than a 30-second clear that succeeds 10/10 times — if you can reset fast. Always restart within 5 seconds if your first burst doesn't CRIT.

Summary: Align all buffs in one 3s window. Pre-fill energy, save burst for DPS window. Skip mechanics by dealing threshold damage in one burst. Compress rotations. Build for burst, not consistency. Reset aggressively.
