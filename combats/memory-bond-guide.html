Bond Memory Guide

Everything about Bond memories: how they unlock, what rewards they provide, and how to optimize your bond progression for maximum account value.

Bond Basics

Bond memories are a special category of memory that represents the relationship between the Hunter and each operative. Each operative has a Bond tree with multiple nodes, each node unlocking through specific memory collections, affinity milestones, and story progression. Bond levels are shared across all content — leveling your bond with Xavier benefits you in combat, in the Dawnbreak Base, and in story interactions.

The Bond system has 12 levels per operative, each level requiring a specific combination of memories collected, memories ascended to certain levels, and total memory power milestones. The requirements scale progressively: early bond levels might need 3 memories at level 20, while later levels demand 6 memories at level 60 with specific elemental coverage. Planning your memory investment around bond requirements ensures you're never wasting resources on memories that don't progress your bond.

Bond Rewards

Bond milestones are organized into reward tiers. Early tiers (levels 1-4) reward Affinity EXP and basic resources. Mid tiers (levels 5-8) start giving stat bonuses — ATK, DEF, and HP — that apply account-wide. Late tiers (levels 9-12) award premium currency (gems), exclusive titles, and unique base decorations.

The stat bonuses at levels 6, 8, 10, and 12 are particularly valuable because they're additive with your existing stats and apply to all content. At bond level 12 for all four operatives, you're looking at a total of 200 bonus ATK, 120 bonus DEF, and 1,200 bonus HP — the equivalent of an extra memory substat on every loadout. These numbers matter most in endgame content where stat thresholds determine clear times.

Tracking Progress

You can view your bond progress from the main menu under Memories > Bond. The interface shows your current bond level, the requirements for the next level, and your completion percentage per requirement category. The system tracks three metrics: Memories Collected (raw count of unique memories for that operative), Memories Ascended (total ascension tiers across all memories), and Total Memory Power (sum of all memory levels).

The most common bottleneck for bond progression is Memory Power — it scales with memory levels and ascension tiers across all your memories for that operative. A single SSR at level 80 provides more power toward bond progress than two SRs at level 40. This means bond progression naturally incentivizes focused investment in your best memories rather than spreading resources thin.

Efficiency Tips

To optimize bond progression: always claim bond milestone rewards as soon as they're available (rewards do not auto-claim), prioritize memories that count toward multiple bond requirements simultaneously, and use your weekly boss attempts on the operative whose bond you're actively pushing. Bond progression is also a great reason to level SR memories to 60 — they contribute significant bond power at a fraction of the resource cost of SSRs.

Common Pitfalls

DO
  • Focus bond on one operative first
  • Check bond requirements before leveling
  • Use bond stats for account-wide planning
  • Complete bond dailies consistently
  • Level SRs for bond power efficiently
DON'T
  • Don't spread bond progression evenly
  • Don't ignore bond for "endgame only"
  • Don't waste resources on off-bond memories
  • Don't forget to claim milestone rewards
  • Don't use universal shards for bond progress

Tip: The Memory Power requirement is the hardest bond gate. Each ascension tier of an SSR (0 to 1, 1 to 2) gives a large power spike. Leveling a memory from 70 to 80 gives more power than leveling from 1 to 70 combined. If you're stuck on a bond level that requires Memory Power, focus on pushing your best SSR from 70 to 80 rather than leveling multiple memories to 40.

Summary: Bond memories provide permanent account-wide stat bonuses and premium currency rewards. Focus bond progression on one main operative to level 8, then spread to all operatives at level 4, then push your secondary operative to 8, and finally work toward all operatives at 12. Bond requirements incentivize focused memory investment — a single strong SSR at max level is worth more than scattered leveling across multiple memories.
