Bond Memory Guide

Bond memories unlock deep character stories, voice lines, and account-wide stat bonuses. This guide covers everything you need to know.

ENTRY_#MEM_072026.07.02BOND

What Are Bond Memories: Bond memories are a special memory type focused entirely on character story and relationship progression. Unlike combat memories, bond memories do not have stats, sets, or resonance skills. They exist purely for narrative content and account-wide rewards.

How Bond Memories Work

Each operative has multiple bond memory slots. Bond memories are unlocked by reaching specific affinity thresholds and completing story requirements. Once unlocked, a bond memory can be read from the Destiny Cafe or the Bond menu. Bond memories are divided into chapters — each bond contains 3-5 story chapters that progressively unlock as you raise affinity. There are no gameplay segments in bond memories. They are entirely visual novel-style sequences with character portraits, dialogue, and occasional CGs (illustrated cutscenes). Bond memories use the same voice acting as the main story — fully voiced in Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. English subtitles are available.

Unlocking Bond Memories

Bond Slot 1: Unlocked at Affinity 10. Available from the start. Bond Slot 2: Unlocked at Affinity 30. Requires clearing the first bond memory. Bond Slot 3: Unlocked at Affinity 55. Requires completing a specific main story chapter. Bond Slot 4: Unlocked at Affinity 85. Requires owning at least one limited SSR for that operative. Bond Slot 5: Unlocked at Affinity 120. Requires max ascension on any SSR for that operative. Important: Bond slot progression is per-operative. Reaching Affinity 120 with Xavier does not unlock Bond Slot 5 for Zayne. You must raise affinity individually for each operative.

Bond Level Rewards

Completing bond memory chapters gives permanent rewards: Per Chapter Clear: 30 diamonds, affinity XP, and a bond-specific reward (name card, chat bubble, or profile frame). Per Bond Completion: 100 diamonds + an exclusive voice line for your Destiny Cafe. All Bonds Completed (per operative): +2% ATK account-wide (stacks across operatives). All Bonds All Operatives: +5% CRIT DMG account-wide. This is why bond memories matter for gameplay — the account-wide bonuses are significant. +2% ATK per operative (four operatives = +8% ATK) is equivalent to an extra ATK% substat on every memory. The +5% CRIT DMG is the cherry on top.

Efficiency Strategy: Bond memories cost nothing but time to unlock. The only gate is affinity. Focus one operative at a time to Affinity 120 to unlock all bond slots and collect the +2% ATK bonus. Then move to the next operative. The account-wide bonuses make this a high-priority long-term goal. Do not ignore bond memories just because they have no combat gameplay — the stat rewards are permanent and stack.

Bond Story Highlights

Xavier Bond 3: Widely considered the best-written bond in the game. Explores his past before becoming a hunter. Contains a plot twist that re-contextualizes his main story actions. Zayne Bond 2: A quiet, introspective story about his childhood and the incident that shaped his personality. Excellent voice acting. Rafayel Bond 4: The most dramatic bond story. Major character revelation that connects to the main lore. Sylus Bond 1: Introduces his backstory and his connection to Onychinus. Essential reading for understanding his motives. Reading order recommendation: Read bonds in numerical order (Bond 1 before Bond 2). The stories reference each other and reading them sequentially enhances the narrative experience. Bond 5 for each operative is the most emotionally impactful — save it for last.

Summary: Bond memories are narrative content that gives permanent account-wide stat bonuses. They unlock at Affinity 10, 30, 55, 85, and 120. Each completed bond gives +2% ATK per operative (up to +8% total). All bonds completed give +5% CRIT DMG. Focus one operative at a time to Affinity 120. Read bonds in order. The story is excellent, the rewards are permanent — bond memories are not optional content.
