SSR Pool Overview

Zayne has 6 SSR memories available globally — 3 limited, 3 standard. His identity is defined by Frost-element control: stacking chill, freezing enemies, and detonating ice crystals for massive damage. Unlike Xavier's raw burst, Zayne rewards methodical play. His limited SSRs significantly upgrade his freeze uptime and detonation multipliers, making him a top pick for sustained DPS encounters and crowd control scenarios.

Limited Banner SSRs

Permafrost Covenant (Frost)
Zayne's flagship limited SSR. Active skill: Create a Frost zone that lasts 10 seconds — enemies inside take 30% increased Frost damage and build chill stacks twice as fast. Passive: When chill reaches 5 stacks on an enemy, detonate for 300% Frost damage and freeze for 2 seconds. R1 increases Frost zone duration to 13 seconds. R3 adds 15% Frost penetration. The cornerstone of every Zayne DPS build.
Element: Frost
Set: Permafrost
Base ATK: 878
Tier: S+
Glacial Heart (Frost)
Zayne's second limited SSR focused on survivability and sustained damage. Active skill: Summon an ice barrier that blocks 3 hits and reflects 80% damage back as Frost damage per hit. Passive: Each reflected hit generates 10 energy. R1 increases reflected damage to 120%. R3 adds a 40% slow effect to reflected projectiles. Excellent for aggressive playstyles that bait enemy attacks.
Element: Frost
Set: Permafrost
Base ATK: 865
Tier: A+
Absolute Zero (Frost/Dark)
Zayne's latest limited SSR introducing dual-element gameplay. Active skill: Fire a Frost-Dark lance that pierces through all enemies and leaves a chilling wake. Passive: Enemies in the chilling wake take 10% increased Frost and Dark damage. R1 increases debuff to 15% and adds energy regen while standing in the wake. A versatile SSR that opens hybrid team options.
Element: Frost + Dark
Set: Shadow
Base ATK: 892
Tier: A

Standard Pool SSRs

Frostbite (Frost)
Standard pool SSR. Active skill: A Frost beam that deals 5 hits of 100% damage each. Passive: Each hit has a 20% chance to freeze. Frozen enemies take 50% additional damage from Frostbite's remaining hits. Decent for sustained control but outclassed by Permafrost Covenant. A reliable option for players who lack limited Frost SSRs.
Element: Frost
Set: Permafrost
Base ATK: 840
Tier: B+
Ice Barrier (Frost)
Standard pool SSR. Active skill: Erect a frost wall that blocks enemy projectiles for 6 seconds. Passive: Allies behind the wall regenerate 2% HP per second. A purely defensive memory with niche uses in Abyssal Chaos and high-difficulty Co-op encounters. Low priority for general content but situationally invaluable against projectile-heavy bosses.
Element: Frost
Set: Tidal
Base ATK: 822
Tier: C+
Dark Resonance (Dark)
Standard pool SSR and Zayne's only non-Frost memory. Active skill: A Dark-element blast dealing 500% damage. Passive: Hitting a frozen enemy with Dark Resonance causes an elemental reaction — Shatter — dealing bonus 200% Dark damage and clearing freeze. A tech option for Frozen-Shatter combos with Absolute Zero. Niche but rewarding for theorycrafters.
Element: Dark
Set: Shadow
Base ATK: 835
Tier: C

Optimal Rotations

Zayne's frost detonation rotation requires careful chill stack management. The basic loop: apply chill with basic attacks and skills, stack to 5, then use Permafrost Covenant's detonation for burst damage. With Glacial Heart, weave in defensive baits to generate energy for the next cycle. With Absolute Zero, use Dark Resonance to trigger Shatter reactions for additional burst windows.

Tip: Zayne's freeze uptime is heavily dependent on his energy regen stat. Aim for at least 140% energy regen on your protocores to ensure your Frost zone is ready when your chill stacks hit 5. Without sufficient energy, the frost rotation feels clunky and inconsistent.

Summary: Zayne's SSR pool focuses on Frost-element control and sustained damage. Permafrost Covenant is an essential pull that defines his playstyle. Glacial Heart and Absolute Zero offer meaningful upgrades for dedicated Zayne players, while standard pool cards fill gaps for F2P builds. Prioritize energy regen in your protocore substats to maximize frost rotation uptime.
