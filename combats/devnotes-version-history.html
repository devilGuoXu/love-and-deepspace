Why Bother with Version History

Why Bother? Gacha games evolve. The meta you play today is different from last patch and will be different next patch. Understanding how the devs think (what they buff, what they nerf, what they introduce) lets you invest resources in characters and memories that will stay relevant, not ones that will be powercrept next patch.

Most players build for the current meta. Smart players build for the next meta. By studying version history, you can identify the devs' priorities and preferences. The devs consistently buff supports — so investing in Zayne is safe. The devs release new characters undertuned — so do not judge on day 1. The devs never nerf — so your investments are protected. These patterns are invisible if you only look at the current patch. Version history reveals the full picture.

"Each version is a conversation between us and the players. We see how you play, we see which characters you love, and we adjust. The game we launched in version 1.0 is not the game we are making today — and that is by design."

— Developer Interview, 1.2 Launch Event

LAUNCH STATE
Four operatives: Xavier (Light Seeker), Sylus (Dark Champion), Zayne (Frostguard), Rafayel (Inferno Blaze). Abyss had 12 floors. Basic protocore system with no roll floors or main stat guarantees. Standard banner plus the first limited banner (Xavier rate-up).
META AT LAUNCH
Xavier dominated Abyss due to Light element's wide coverage (only one enemy type resists Light). Sylus was considered weak — his execute mechanic was undertuned (20% threshold, 30% bonus, 15s cooldown). Zayne was mandatory for floor 8 survival check. Rafayel was considered a niche AoE pick, only used in multi-target floors. The community consensus: "Xavier is the only DPS you need."
KEY EVENTS
Launch event with standard rewards. First limited banner (Xavier). Community discovers the protocore farming meta (dust > everything else). First "which operative should I main?" debates begin.

Retrospective: Looking back, the launch meta was the least balanced period in the game's history. Xavier was disproportionately strong because Light element had no counters. The devs clearly planned to address this through buffs rather than nerfs — and they did, starting with 1.1.

Version 1.1 — First Major Patch

KEY CHANGES
Sylus execute rework — damage threshold increased from 20% to 30%, bonus from 30% to 40%, cooldown from 15s to 12s. First limited event with SSR memory shard system. Abyss floor 13–15 teased. Protocore system: substat roll floor added (no roll below 50% of max). XP crystal drop rates increased by 20%. Stamina cap: 100 → 120 (was this 1.0 or 1.1? Community records show this may have been launch — included for completeness).
META SHIFT
Sylus became competitive — his execute window was now large enough to make a difference in boss fights. Hypercarry Xavier teams remained king for single-target (Xavier + Zayne + any DPS). But Sylus teams became viable for the first time. The community started exploring dual-DPS compositions (Xavier + Sylus) for floors where single-target burst was not required.

The 1.1 lesson: The devs are willing to make significant balance changes based on player data. Sylus was buffed dramatically because the data showed players were not using him. If a character is underused for 1–2 patches, expect a buff. This pattern has held for every underperforming character since launch.

Version 1.2 — Summer Update

KEY CHANGES
Summer event with limited outfits. Co-op boss mode introduced. Rafayel burn DoT rework — tick interval reduced from 2.0s to 1.3s (with 4pc), burn damage increased by 15% base. Zayne Frostguard 4pc: Shield now cleanses one debuff every 8s. Sylus bug fix (hidden buff): Execute now properly benefits from CRIT DMG. Protocore system: Main stat guarantee added (first 10 domain runs per week guarantee correct main stat). Batch leveling for memories (QoL). Event shop: Guaranteed protocore added.
META SHIFT
Rafayel became the top AoE operative — his burn DPS increase of ~35% made him competitive with Xavier in multi-target content. Co-op meta established: Zayne mandatory for sustain, Sylus for execute burst, Xavier for consistent single-target, Rafayel for AoE waves. Zayne became mandatory for Abyss floors with debuff mechanics (creating the "Zayne-check" design pattern).

"The co-op mode has taught us a lot about how players interact. We see compositions we never expected — three DPS no sustain teams clearing bosses in record time, and full support teams slowly grinding through. Every playstyle has a home in Deepspace."

— Developer Note, Patch 1.2

The 1.2 lesson: 1.2 was the biggest meta shift in the game's history. Three out of four operatives received significant changes. The introduction of co-op created a new content type that required different team compositions than Abyss. Players who only built Xavier for Abyss suddenly needed Sylus (execute on co-op bosses) and Zayne (sustain). This patch taught players to diversify their roster.

Version 1.3 — Anniversary

KEY CHANGES (PROJECTED)
Free SSR selector (expanded pool). Double top-up reset. Limited SSR (new operative or variant). Protocore substat lock system teased. Anniversary event with premium rewards. New permanent game mode (speculated: endless tower or boss rush).
META SHIFT (PROJECTED)
Anniversary typically resets the meta — a new operative introduces new team compositions. The free SSR selector allows players to fill roster gaps (get Xavier if you are missing him, or Zayne for support). The substat lock system (if implemented) will change protocore farming priorities — players will save dust instead of spending it.

Anniversary strategy: Based on patterns from other gacha games and the first anniversary: (1) Save all pull resources for 2–4 weeks before anniversary — the limited operative will likely be meta-defining. (2) Do not spend the free SSR selector immediately — wait for the meta to settle and see which operative you need most. (3) If substat lock launches, spend your saved dust on your main DPS first — the CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG improvement will be the biggest gain.

Balance Change Patterns

The devs follow four consistent balance patterns visible across all versions:

PATTERN 1 — RELEASE WEAK, BUFF LATER
New characters released undertuned, buffed after 4–6 weeks
Sylus and Rafayel both followed this pattern. This makes early adopters feel rewarded while giving the devs data to calibrate. Do not judge a new character in the first 2 weeks — wait for their first balance patch.
PATTERN 2 — SYSTEM POWERCREEP, NOT DIRECT NERFS
New memories and systems powercreep old ones
No character has ever been directly nerfed. Instead, the devs introduce content that counters strong characters (resist element floors, debuff mechanics that bypass shields). Your investments are safe from direct nerfs.
PATTERN 3 — ANNIVERSARY SHAKEUP
Every anniversary introduces a major system or meta shift
SSR selector, substat lock, new memory tier — anniversary patches redefine the game. Save resources for anniversary at least 2 months in advance.
PATTERN 4 — LIMITED CHARACTER EXCLUSIVITY
Limited characters return every ~6 months on rerun
They do not enter the standard pool. If you miss a limited character, you wait 6 months minimum. Plan your pulls accordingly — if you want a returning character, save 140+ pulls for their rerun.

Zayne is the second safest (sustain is always valuable in any content). Sylus and Rafayel are more specialized but become essential in their specific content types (execute for boss fights, burn for AoE waves). A balanced roster covering all 4 elements will outperform a single maxed element in future content.

Community Meta Consensus Timeline

Community meta understanding always lags behind the actual effective meta. Here is how the consensus evolved:

LAUNCH → 1.0
"Xavier is the only DPS you need. Sylus is a trap. Zayne is mandatory for floor 8. Rafayel is niche." This consensus was based on the launch state, which was the least balanced period.
1.1 → 1.2 EARLY
"Sylus execute is viable now. Xavier + Sylus dual DPS is the new meta." The community took 2–3 weeks to fully adopt Sylus after his 1.1 buff. Early adopters benefited from the lag — they could use Sylus before everyone else realized he was good.
1.2 MID → LATE
"Rafayel burn is broken in AoE. Zayne is S-tier. Full team of all 4 at 60 is the endgame target." The community meta finally caught up to the effective meta. Rafayel's 1.2 rework took 3–4 weeks to be fully understood.

Ahead of the meta: The community meta always lags behind the actual meta by 2–4 weeks. Guide sites (including this one) capture the meta after it has stabilized. If you want to be ahead of the meta, test patch changes yourself on day 1 and form your own opinions. By the time the community agrees on the meta, the devs are already planning the next shakeup. The most valuable players in community discussions are the ones who test new characters on day 1 and share honest results — not the ones who repeat consensus from 3 weeks ago.

Summary: Game evolves through patches — 1.0 (Xavier dominant), 1.1 (Sylus buff + protocore floor), 1.2 (Rafayel buff + co-op + major meta shift), 1.3 (anniversary shakeup). New characters release undertuned then buffed. Powercreep happens through new memories/systems, not direct nerfs. Anniversary is the biggest meta shift. Read patch notes — especially bug fixes (Sylus 1.2 bug fix = 25% hidden buff). Community meta lags 2–4 weeks behind reality. Xavier is the safest long-term investment. Save resources for anniversary systems. The best way to stay ahead of the meta is to test changes yourself.
