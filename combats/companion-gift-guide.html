Gift System Basics

The gift system allows you to give each operative up to 5 gifts per day. Gifts come in four rarity tiers: Common (gray), Uncommon (green), Rare (blue), and Epic (purple). Each tier grants base Affinity EXP of 10, 25, 60, and 150 respectively. When a gift matches an operative's preference, the EXP is doubled — making an Epic preferred gift worth 300 Affinity EXP in a single click.

Each operative has three preferences: Loved gifts (2x EXP and special dialogue), Liked gifts (2x EXP with standard dialogue), and Neutral gifts (1x EXP, no bonus dialogue). There are no disliked gifts — even a non-preferred gift still grants base EXP. The preference system is fixed per operative and does not change over time (except during special events where limited-time favorites may appear).

Gift Tier Efficiency

Not all gift tiers are created equal in terms of EXP per currency spent. Rare gifts offer the best EXP-per-currency ratio at 60 base EXP for a moderate cost. Epic gifts give the most EXP per slot (150 base) but are expensive and should be reserved for Loved preferences. Common and Uncommon gifts are best used as filler when you run out of higher-tier gifts — their EXP-per-slot is low, but they're better than wasting a daily gift slot.

Best Value Gifts
  • Rare preferred: 120 EXP, moderate cost
  • Epic Loved: 300 EXP, high cost per slot
  • Rare Loved: 120 EXP per slot
  • Uncommon preferred: 50 EXP per slot
Avoid These
  • Common neutral: 10 EXP per slot
  • Epic neutral: 150 EXP, expensive
  • Rare neutral: 60 EXP, wasteful
  • Any gift on wrong operative

Gifting Strategy

The optimal daily gifting routine: use 5 Rare preferred gifts on your main operative (120 EXP each = 600 EXP/day from gifts alone). If you're pushing a specific milestone, supplement with Epic Loved gifts for the 300 EXP per slot. For secondary operatives, Uncommon preferred gifts are sufficient to maintain steady progress at a lower cost.

Tip: Save your Epic Loved gifts for double EXP events or when you're within 1-2 days of a major milestone (Affinity 30, 50, 100). The burst EXP from 5 Epic Loved gifts (1,500 EXP in one day) can push you over the threshold without waiting. During normal farming, Rare preferred gifts give the best long-term value.

Gift Sources

Gifts are obtained from multiple sources: Event shops (the most consistent source — always buy Rare gift boxes first), the Battle Pass (free and paid tracks offer gift boxes weekly), Daily Login rewards, Achievement milestones, and the Premium Shop (not recommended for gem spending). The most efficient way to build a gift inventory is to prioritize Rare gift boxes in event shops over other resource purchases.

Summary: Gift preference knowledge doubles your Affinity EXP efficiency. Xavier loves stargazing and celestial items. Zayne values medical and intellectual gifts. Rafayel treasures art supplies and ocean-themed items. Sylus prefers objects of power and luxury. Use Rare preferred gifts for daily efficiency, save Epic Loved gifts for milestone pushes, and never give neutral gifts when preferred options are available. With optimal gifting, you can earn 600+ Affinity EXP per day on your main operative.
