STYLE GUIDE — OUTFITS

Outfit Collection & Value Guide

Outfits are the true endgame for collectors. No combat stats, no gameplay advantage — but they are the most visible expression of your account. Here is how to collect smartly.

ENTRY_#STY_012026.07.02OUTFITS

Core Truth: Outfits in Deepspace are purely cosmetic. They do not give ATK bonuses, CRIT rate, or any gameplay advantage. You should only buy outfits if: (1) You enjoy the visual design. (2) You main the character. (3) You are a completionist. If none of these apply, skip all outfits and spend resources on pulls instead.

Outfit Types & Rarity

Limited Event Outfits: Available only during specific events (summer, Christmas, anniversary). Cost: ~$15-30 USD equivalent or event currency. These are the rarest and most valuable for collectors. Once the event ends, they may not return for 6-12 months. Premium Shop Outfits: Always available in the shop. Cost: $10-20 USD. No time pressure — buy whenever you want. Battle Pass Outfits: Included in the premium battle pass ($5/season). Best value outfit in the game. Free Outfits: Earned through story progression, achievements, or login events. Zero cost. Always claim these. Ranking: Battle Pass outfits are the best value ($5 for an exclusive skin + other rewards). Limited event outfits are the rarest. Premium shop outfits are the worst value — wait for a sale.

Which Outfits to Buy

S-Tier (Must Buy): Battle Pass outfit for your main operative. Anniversary limited outfit (comes with SSR selector and other rewards). Login event free outfits. A-Tier (Buy If): Seasonal limited outfit for your main operative. Holiday outfit if the design is excellent. B-Tier (Skip): Premium shop outfits for characters you do not use. Recolor variants of existing outfits. F-Tier (Never Buy): Any outfit that costs more than $30. Any outfit that is a minor recolor of a free outfit. Rule of thumb: If you would not pay $15 for the outfit as a standalone purchase, skip it. Do not justify a purchase because "it comes with other rewards" — evaluate the outfit on its own merit.

Pro Tip: Before buying any outfit, preview it in the dressing room. Check how it looks in combat (not just the preview screen). Many outfits look great in preview but have clipping issues or awkward physics in combat. Try the combat preview before spending real money.

Outfit Rotation Schedule

Limited outfits follow the same seasonal calendar as events: January (Anniversary): Premium anniversary outfit. February (Valentine's): Romance-themed outfit. June-August (Summer): Beach/swimwear outfits. December (Christmas): Winter/holiday outfits. Battle Pass: New outfit every 6 weeks (each patch cycle). Strategy: If you plan to buy only 2-3 outfits per year, buy the anniversary outfit and 1-2 battle pass outfits. These give the best value-to-visual ratio.

Summary: Outfits are cosmetic only — no gameplay value. Battle Pass ($5) is best value. Limited event outfits are rarest. Premium shop (wait for sale). Never spend more than $15 on a single outfit. Preview in combat before buying. If you skip all outfits, you lose nothing — spend on pulls instead.
