Outfit Collection & Value Guide

Outfits are the true endgame for collectors. No combat stats, no gameplay advantage — but they are the most visible expression of your account. Here is how to collect smartly.

Core Truth

Core Truth: Outfits in Deepspace are purely cosmetic. They do not give ATK bonuses, CRIT rate, or any gameplay advantage. You should only buy outfits if: (1) You enjoy the visual design. (2) You main the character. (3) You are a completionist. If none of these apply, skip all outfits and spend resources on pulls instead.

This is the most important paragraph in this guide. Outfits are a luxury, not a necessity. No content in the game requires a specific outfit. No boss is easier because you are wearing a premium skin. The only value of an outfit is emotional — does it make you happy when you look at your character? If yes, buy it. If you are on the fence, skip it. There will always be another outfit next patch.

LIMITED EVENT OUTFITS
Available during specific events only
Cost: ~$15–30 USD equivalent or event currency. These are the rarest and most valuable for collectors. Once the event ends, they may not return for 6–12 months. Anniversary outfits are the most exclusive — they may never return.
PREMIUM SHOP OUTFITS
Always available, no time pressure
Cost: $10–20 USD. Worst value for collectors because they lack exclusivity. Wait for a sale (occasional discounts of 20–30%). Only buy these if you really love the design and cannot wait for a limited alternative.
BATTLE PASS OUTFITS
Included in premium pass ($5/season)
Best value outfit in the game. $5 gets you an exclusive skin plus other rewards (pulls, resources, furniture). Every season has a new BP outfit. If you buy only one outfit type, make it Battle Pass.
FREE OUTFITS
Zero cost — always claim them
Earned through story progression, achievements, or login events. These are your "daily driver" outfits. Even if they are not the flashiest, free outfits save you money and often have great designs. Never skip a login event that gives a free outfit.

Which Outfits to Buy

S-TIER (MUST BUY)
Battle Pass outfit for your main operative
$5 for an exclusive skin + other rewards. Best value in the game. Anniversary limited outfit (comes with SSR selector and other rewards). Login event free outfits — zero cost, always claim.
A-TIER (BUY IF)
Seasonal limited outfit for your main operative
If the design is excellent and you main the character, limited outfits are worth considering. Holiday outfits near Christmas/Valentine's are often the best-designed of the year.
B-TIER (SKIP)
Premium shop outfits for characters you don't use
Recolor variants of existing outfits. These add nothing new to your collection. Only buy if you have disposable income and want every outfit for your main.
F-TIER (NEVER BUY)
Any outfit over $30. Minor recolors of free outfits.
No single outfit is worth $30+ in a game where you can get a full SSR memory for ~$30 worth of pulls. Minor recolors are a cash grab — the original free version is usually similar enough.

Rule of thumb: If you would not pay $15 for the outfit as a standalone purchase, skip it. Do not justify a purchase because "it comes with other rewards" — evaluate the outfit on its own merit. The included rewards are usually worth $2–5 in resources, which does not justify a $25 outfit if the design is mediocre.

Outfit Rotation Schedule

Limited outfits follow the same seasonal calendar as events. Knowing the schedule helps you plan your budget.

January (Anniversary)Premium anniversary outfit — the most exclusive of the year
February (Valentine's)Romance-themed outfit — high-quality design
June–August (Summer)Beach/swimwear outfits — seasonal favorites
December (Christmas)Winter/holiday outfits — return each December
Every 6 weeks (Patch)New Battle Pass outfit — best value in the game

Strategy: If you plan to buy only 2–3 outfits per year, buy the anniversary outfit and 1–2 battle pass outfits. These give the best value-to-visual ratio. The anniversary outfit is the most exclusive (may never return), and battle pass outfits are the cheapest. Skip premium shop outfits and seasonal outfits unless you have extra budget and really love the design.

Purchase Strategy by Account Type

F2P / LOW SPENDER
Zero outfit budget — free outfits only
Claim all login and event free outfits. Do not spend diamonds on outfits (diamonds are for pulls). Free outfits from story progression are your main wardrobe. You will still look good — the base designs in Deepspace are excellent.
DOLPHIN ($10–30/mo)
Battle Pass only
Buy the premium Battle Pass each season ($5) for the exclusive outfit + pull resources. Consider the anniversary outfit (once per year, ~$15–20). Skip everything else. Total annual outfit spend: ~$50–80.
WHALE ($100+/mo)
All limited outfits for your main
Buy every limited outfit for your main operative. Skip outfits for operatives you do not use. Buy premium shop outfits only on sale. Total annual outfit spend can exceed $300 — set a personal limit and stick to it.

Preview Before Buying

Pro Tip: Before buying any outfit, preview it in the dressing room. Check how it looks in combat (not just the preview screen). Many outfits look great in preview but have clipping issues or awkward physics in combat. Try the combat preview before spending real money.

Deepspace has a detailed preview system. Use it fully: (1) Check the front, back, and side views. (2) Preview combat animations — does the outfit clip during skill animations? (3) Check the photo mode appearance — if you take photos, this matters. (4) Preview with your main weapon — some outfits look different with different weapon stances. (5) Ask in community channels for real user screenshots — previews can be misleading.

F2P Outfit Options

Free outfits in Deepspace are surprisingly good. Here is what you can get without spending:

Story completion: Each operative's base outfit is unlocked through story progression. These are well-designed and thematic. Achievement rewards: Specific achievements unlock cosmetic variants. Check the achievement menu for outfit rewards. Login events: Major events (anniversary, Christmas) often give free outfits through login rewards. Never miss these. Battle Pass free track: The free Battle Pass track gives accessories and sometimes full outfits. Always reach max BP level.

With all free outfits combined, you will have 5–8 unique looks for your account. That is enough variety to rotate without spending a cent.

Summary: Outfits are cosmetic only — no gameplay value. Battle Pass ($5) is best value. Limited event outfits are rarest. Premium shop (wait for sale). Never spend more than $15 on a single outfit. Preview in combat before buying. If you skip all outfits, you lose nothing — spend on pulls instead. F2P players get 5–8 free outfits through story, achievements, and events. Set a personal budget and stick to it.
