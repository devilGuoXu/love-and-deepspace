Tender Moments & Secret Times Guide

Everything you need to know about the romantic story content in Love and Deepspace — unlock conditions, reward breakdowns, and the best order to experience each operative's story.

Overview

Tender Moments and Secret Times are the two narrative pillars of the Companion system in Love and Deepspace. While the main story follows the Hunter's journey through the world of Deepspace, Tender Moments and Secret Times focus exclusively on the romantic relationships with each operative — Xavier, Zayne, Rafayel, and Sylus. These are the moments where Affinity progression stops being a number and becomes actual story content, making them the primary motivation for building Affinity with any operative.

The two systems serve different purposes and offer different experiences. Tender Moments are visual cinematic episodes that play like short animated films, complete with full character art, CG illustrations, voice acting, and interactive dialogue choices. Secret Times are audio dramas — voice-acted stories that play in the background without requiring your full visual attention. Both are gated behind Affinity levels and both reward gems and Affinity EXP upon first completion, making them a significant source of premium currency over the lifetime of your account.

Tender Moments Explained

Tender Moments are date-style episodes that feature the Hunter spending quality one-on-one time with an operative. Each episode is a self-contained story scenario — going to a festival together, cooking a meal, stargazing on a rooftop, or navigating an awkward but charming situation. These episodes are fully produced with animated sprite work, multiple CG illustrations, and branching dialogue options that let you shape the tone of the interaction.

Each operative has multiple Tender Moment chapters unlocked progressively through Affinity level milestones. The first chapter typically unlocks around Affinity 15-20, with later chapters requiring 30, 50, 70, and beyond. Once unlocked, a Tender Moment can be replayed at any time from the Companion menu. Each chapter rewards a one-time bonus of gems (typically 50-100 per chapter) and Affinity EXP (300-800 per chapter). These rewards are substantial — completing all available Tender Moments for a single operative can yield 400+ gems and several thousand Affinity EXP.

Tip: Tender Moment chapters often include dialogue choices that affect the flow of the scene but do not change the outcome or rewards. Choose whatever feels natural — there are no wrong answers and no missable rewards. The real value is in the story and the gems.

Secret Times Explained

Secret Times are audio-only romantic stories that focus on intimate, quieter moments between the Hunter and each operative. These are designed to be listened to rather than watched — think of them as romantic audio dramas or ASMR-style experiences. Each Secret Times episode features full voice acting from the operative's voice actor, ambient sound design, and a narrative that ranges from tender confessions to playful banter to heartfelt declarations.

Like Tender Moments, Secret Times chapters are unlocked through Affinity milestones, though the unlock thresholds tend to be somewhat lower. Secret Times also reward gems (30-80 per episode) and Affinity EXP (200-500 per episode) on first completion. Because they are audio-only, Secret Times have a significant practical advantage: you can listen to them while doing other things. Commuting, exercising, doing chores, or even playing other game modes — Secret Times play in the background and don't require your eyes on the screen.

Tip: Secret Times are perfect for daily commutes or wind-down time before bed. Open the game, start a Secret Times episode, lock your phone, and listen like a podcast. You absorb the story while multitasking, and you still get the gem rewards.

How to Access

Accessing both Tender Moments and Secret Times is straightforward from the Companion menu. From the main screen, navigate to the Companion tab (the heart icon), select the operative you want to view content for, and then look for the Tender Moments tab and the Secret Times tab within their profile page. Available chapters will be highlighted; locked chapters will show their unlock requirements when tapped.

Tender Moments play as full-screen cinematic experiences. The game will transition into the episode, displaying animated character sprites against illustrated backgrounds with text dialogue and voice acting. You can tap to advance dialogue, make occasional choices, and view CG illustrations at key moments. The experience is immersive and demands your full attention — plan to set aside 10-20 minutes per episode.

Secret Times are accessed from the same Companion menu but function differently. Once you start a Secret Times episode, the game will play the audio in the background even if you switch to another app or lock your screen. A simple player interface shows the episode title and progress bar. You can pause, rewind, or stop at any time. This makes Secret Times ideal for passive consumption — during your commute, while cooking, or as a sleep aid.

Tip: Download Secret Times episodes over Wi-Fi before heading out. The audio files are preloaded and cached, so you won't need mobile data to listen during your commute. This is especially useful for daily commuters who want to make progress on multiple operative stories each day.

Priority Order

Not all Tender Moments and Secret Times are created equal in terms of gem-per-Affinity efficiency. If your goal is to maximize gem income while building Affinity strategically, here is the recommended approach.

First Priority: All Operatives to Affinity 18
The first Tender Moment chapter for Xavier, Zayne, and Rafayel unlocks at Affinity 18 (Sylus requires 20). Getting all three operatives to 18 unlocks 3 Tender Moment chapters and the corresponding gem rewards with minimal Affinity investment. This is the highest gem-per-Affinity ratio available — roughly 50 gems for 18 levels of Affinity.
Second Priority: Secret Times Chapters 1-2
Secret Times Chapter 1 unlocks at Affinity 12 for the main three and 15 for Sylus. Chapter 2 unlocks at 22 and 25 respectively. These are extremely low Affinity thresholds for the rewards offered. Prioritize unlocking Secret Times Chapters 1-2 for all operatives before pushing for higher Tender Moment chapters.
Third Priority: Tender Moments Chapter 2
Affinity 30 is a natural breakpoint since it also unlocks the 6th memory slot. Pushing your main operative to Affinity 30 unlocks Tender Moments Chapter 2, which awards additional gems and a significant story payoff. This is also the point where each operative's story starts to develop deeper emotional stakes.
Fourth Priority: Deep Progression on Main Operative
Beyond Affinity 30, focus your primary operative toward 65+ for Tender Moments Chapters 3-4 and Secret Times Chapters 3-4. These later chapters are more gem-efficient than spreading Affinity evenly across all operatives past 30 because the Affinity curve steepens — the EXP required to go from 60 to 65 on one operative is less than the combined EXP to go from 30 to 35 on two.

For pure gem efficiency, the optimal path is: all main three operatives to Affinity 18 (unlock Tender Moments 1), then all operatives to Affinity 22-25 (unlock Secret Times 1-2), then your main operative to Affinity 30 (Tender Moments 2 + memory slot), then your main operative to Affinity 65+ (chapters 3-5), then circle back for the remaining operatives.

Final Thoughts

Tender Moments and Secret Times are the emotional core of Love and Deepspace. They transform Affility from an abstract progression system into tangible, memorable story experiences that define why players invest in their favorite operatives. The gem rewards are generous enough to justify prioritizing these unlocks, but the real value lies in the stories themselves — the quiet moments of connection that make each operative feel real and the romantic tension that keeps players coming back.

Whether you are a completionist aiming to unlock everything, a gem-focused player optimizing your premium currency income, or a casual fan who just wants to experience the story for a specific operative, the path is the same: build Affinity daily through greetings, gifts, and combat pairing. The Tender Moments and Secret Times will be waiting for you at every milestone, each one a reward for your dedication.

Summary: Tender Moments (cinematic date episodes with full CG art) and Secret Times (audio-only romantic stories) are the main narrative rewards for Affinity progression. Unlock them through Affinity milestones starting at level 12-20 depending on the operative and chapter. Total rewards exceed 1,700 gems across all operatives. Prioritize getting all operatives to Affinity 18 for the first Tender Moment chapters, then unlock Secret Times for their low-investment gem payouts, then push your main operative deep. These stories are the heart of the Companion system — do not skip them.
