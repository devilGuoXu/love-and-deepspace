Abyssal Trials: Floor 2

The first elite Wanderer — your first real challenge in the Abyss.

ENTRY_#1312026.06.30FLOOR_2
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Floor 2 Threshold: Recommended power level 7,000. First floor with an elite enemy. Introduces battle rating as a factor.

Boss — Corrupted Vanguard

A corrupted humanoid operative with two phases. Phase 1 (100%-50% HP): Standard melee combos with a three-hit pattern. Dodge the third hit (overhead slam) for a perfect dodge window. Phase 2 (50%-0% HP): Enrages — attack speed +30%, gains a ranged energy wave attack. Use resonance iframes to close distance during Phase 2.

Battle Rating Mechanic

Floor 2 introduces the Battle Rating system. Your team's power level is compared to the recommended threshold. Below threshold: damage dealt reduced by up to 20%, damage taken increased by up to 20%. At or above threshold: no penalty. This is why leveling memories matters — being under the threshold is a significant handicap.

Strategy

Any team works. Save resonance skill for Phase 2 to close the gap after the energy wave attack. Perfect dodge the Phase 1 overhead slam for a free slow-motion window. If your battle rating is below 7,000, consider leveling your main DPS memories to +6 first.

Rewards

First clear: 80 diamonds, 3 Advanced Resonance Crystals. Weekly reset: 30 diamonds. 3-star clear bonus: 40 diamonds.

Memory Preparation

Before attempting Floor 2, ensure your memory setup is optimized for the battle rating system. Your main DPS should have all six memory slots filled with at least +6 enhancement. Prioritize leveling the Solar pair first — the paired set bonus provides a significant stat boost that directly affects your power rating. If you have multiple Solar sets, choose the one with the higher base stats rather than the one with better substats, because the battle rating check only considers raw power numbers.

Your support operative can lag slightly behind — +3 memories are sufficient for the off-slot since they are mainly used for resonance skills and emergency swaps. The battle rating penalty is calculated based on your primary operative, not your entire roster. This means you can field a weaker support as long as your main DPS carries the power requirement.

Execution Tips

The Corrupted Vanguard has a tell for each of its three attack patterns. The overhead slam is telegraphed by a two-second wind-up where the boss raises both arms. Use this visual cue to time your perfect dodge. The ranged energy wave in Phase 2 is preceded by the boss stepping backward — as soon as you see the back step, close distance immediately to avoid the wave entirely. Staying close to the boss in Phase 2 also forces it to use melee attacks, which are easier to dodge than the ranged wave.

Resource management matters more on this floor than on Floor 1. Your resonance skill should be used exclusively for damage during the boss's attack cooldown windows. Using resonance as an i-frame to dodge attacks is acceptable but not optimal — positioning is cheaper and leaves your burst available for damage windows.

Floor 2 introduces the battle rating mechanic, which penalizes operatives below the recommended power threshold. The penalty is multiplicative: 20% reduced damage and 20% increased damage taken at 500 power below threshold. This means a team at 6,500 power against a 7,000 threshold effectively fights at 5,200 power. The penalty magnitude makes it essential to meet the threshold before attempting this floor. Leveling your main DPS Solar pair from +3 to +6 typically provides enough power to meet the threshold.

The Corrupted Vanguard's charged attack deals 200% damage. Telegraphed by a 1.5-second weapon-lowering pause. Sidestep rather than dodge through — the hitbox is narrow. A missed charge staggers the Vanguard for 2 seconds.

Key Insight: This is the first floor where battle rating matters. If you're struggling, check your memory levels. Having +6 memories on your two main operatives is enough to hit the threshold. Don't spread resources thin — focus on 2 operatives.

Summary: First elite boss. Battle rating penalty kicks in below 7,000. Level memories to +6 on 2 operatives. Save resonance for Phase 2 gap closing.
