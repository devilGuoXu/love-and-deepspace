Endgame Progression Roadmap

You've hit level 60. Your main team is built. What now? The endgame roadmap.

ENTRY_#0452026.06.30ROADMAP

Welcome to Endgame: Level 60 is when the tutorial ends. Your goals shift from leveling up to optimizing — perfect substats, awakening key memories, and building multiple teams for Abyss and Tower depth.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Phase 1 — Solidify Core (Level 60-65)

Goal: One complete 4-pc SSR set for your main DPS at Tier 4 breakthrough. Milestones: Main DPS memories at +12 minimum. Sub-DPS at +9. Support at +6. Protocore set complete with correct main stats (don't worry about substats yet). Weekly bosses cleared. Abyss Floor 7 cleared. Total time: 2-4 weeks after hitting 60.

Spend stamina on: Weekly boss (always) → EXP books (if any operative is below 60) → Gold → Protocore farming. Do NOT farm character upgrade materials — you should have enough from natural progression.

Phase 2 — Expand Roster (Level 65-70)

Goal: Two complete teams of 3 operatives each (6 operatives total). Secondary DPS gets a 4-pc set at Tier 4. Support gets a functional 2+2 set. Protocore substats start to matter — spend gold on protocore enhancement. Abyss Floor 10 cleared. Tower Floor 50 cleared.

Key decision: Pick a second main DPS element opposite your first. If your main is Xavier (Light), build Sylus (Dark). Element coverage is the most important factor for Abyss progression.

Phase 3 — Specialization (Level 70+)

Goal: Three complete teams (8-9 operatives). All main DPS at Tier 5 breakthrough. Awaken at least one SSR memory. Protocore substats optimized (target: 60%+ CRIT, 120%+ CRIT DMG on main DPS). Abyss Floor 12 cleared. Tower Floor 100 cleared.

This phase is about optimizing, not collecting. You should be selective about what you upgrade. A memory with bad substat rolls is not worth leveling past +9. Salvage aggressively. Target specific protocore slots on farming days.

Common Endgame Mistakes

Mistake 1: Building 3 operatives of the same element. You need coverage, not depth. Mistake 2: Not logging in daily for 15 min. Daily resources compound over months. Mistake 3: Spending diamonds on standard banner. Save for limited banners. Mistake 4: Ignoring Tower. It's 8,000 free diamonds.

Endgame Content Rotation

Once you reach level 60 and complete the main story, endgame content follows a weekly rotation that maximizes resource efficiency. Monday through Wednesday focus on Abyss progression ? push your highest uncompleted floor with fresh attempts. Thursday and Friday are for boss farming and domain clears. Weekends are flexible, best used for material stockpiling or event completion. The key to endgame efficiency is aligning your stamina spending with the daily bonus rotation. Never spend stamina on content that is not on the daily bonus rotation unless you are capped on that resource. Awakening materials, protocore enhancers, and gold follow predictable bonus schedules that reward players who plan their week around them.

Endgame gearing follows a predictable path from initial SSR acquisition to optimized substat hunting. The first phase is completing a 4-piece set bonus for your main DPS with any substats. The second phase is enhancing those pieces to +12 and replacing any that rolled dead substats. The third phase is farming replacement pieces with targeted substat priorities. Most players spend 2-3 months in phase two alone, gradually replacing each piece as better substat rolls appear.

Endgame content also includes the weekly Abyss reset, which provides the most consistent gem income for advanced players. Each weekly reset on Monday refreshes all Abyss floor rewards, allowing you to earn up to 500 gems per week from Floors 7-12 alone. Combine this with daily mission gems (60 per day) and event rewards, and a focused endgame player earns approximately 8,000 gems per month.

Key Insight: Endgame is about account breadth, not depth. A well-rounded account of 8 operatives at moderate investment clears more content than 3 hyper-invested operatives. Spread your resources to hit power thresholds, then specialize.

Summary: Phase 1: One team + Abyss 7. Phase 2: Two teams + Abyss 10 + Tower 50. Phase 3: Three teams + Abyss 12 + Tower 100. Avoid single-element stacking. Log in daily. Save diamonds.
