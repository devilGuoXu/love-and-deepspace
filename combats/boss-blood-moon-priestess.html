THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Blood Moon Priestess

A dark priestess who heals herself, buffs her damage, and debuffs your team. Interruption is the only way to win.

ENTRY_#1032026.06.29ELITE_BOSS

Recommended Power: 80k+ | Element: Dark/Light | Weakness: Interrupt — any hard CC stops her channeled abilities.

Phase 1: Moonrise (100%-70%)

Moon Blessing: Priestess buffs herself with +50% damage for 10s. Interrupt or wait it out behind cover. Blood Ritual: Channels to heal 30% HP over 5s. Must be interrupted — use Sylus execute or any hard CC. Dark Orb: Slow-moving projectile that curses on hit. Easy to dodge.

Phase 2: Eclipse (70%-35%)

Eclipse: Arena darkens. Priestess gains damage reduction. Light sources appear — stand in the light. Sacrificial Dagger: Marks a target. After 3s, deals massive damage. Use Zayne's shield or perfect dodge. Moonlight Serenade: AoE charm. All operatives walk toward the Priestess. Break line of sight.

Phase 3: Blood Moon (35%-0%)

Total Eclipse: No safe zones. All damage increased. Blood Cascade: Rapid channeled heals. Interrupt every single one. Moon's Wrath: Arena-wide explosion. Only invincibility frames from Ardent Oath or perfect dodge survive.

Recommended Team: Sylus (Main DPS/Interrupt) — execute interrupts everything. Xavier (Sub-DPS) — Light advantage in Eclipse phase. Zayne (Support) — shield saves marked targets.

Advanced Tip: Save interrupt abilities for Blood Ritual. If the Priestess completes a heal, the fight length increases by 60 seconds. A single failed interrupt can cost you the run. Track her cast bar — she always heals at 70%, 50%, and 35% HP thresholds.

Summary: This is an interrupt check. Miss one Blood Ritual heal and the fight drags. Save CC for heal channels. Stand in the light during Eclipse. Phase 3 is a DPS race — blow all cooldowns.
