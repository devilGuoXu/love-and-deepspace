THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Blood Moon Priestess

A dark priestess who heals herself, buffs her damage, and debuffs your team. Interruption is the only way to win.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Recommended Power: 80k+ | Element: Dark/Light | Weakness: Interrupt — any hard CC stops her channeled abilities.

Phase 1: Moonrise (100%-70%)

Moon Blessing: Priestess buffs herself with +50% damage for 10s. Interrupt or wait it out behind cover. Blood Ritual: Channels to heal 30% HP over 5s. Must be interrupted — use Sylus execute or any hard CC. Dark Orb: Slow-moving projectile that curses on hit. Easy to dodge.

Phase 2: Eclipse (70%-35%)

Eclipse: Arena darkens. Priestess gains damage reduction. Light sources appear — stand in the light. Sacrificial Dagger: Marks a target. After 3s, deals massive damage. Use Zayne's shield or perfect dodge. Moonlight Serenade: AoE charm. All operatives walk toward the Priestess. Break line of sight.

Phase 3: Blood Moon (35%-0%)

Total Eclipse: No safe zones. All damage increased. Blood Cascade: Rapid channeled heals. Interrupt every single one. Moon's Wrath: Arena-wide explosion. Only invincibility frames from Ardent Oath or perfect dodge survive.

Recommended Team: Sylus (Main DPS/Interrupt) — execute interrupts everything. Xavier (Sub-DPS) — Light advantage in Eclipse phase. Zayne (Support) — shield saves marked targets.

Light Management in Eclipse Phase

The Eclipse phase reduces visibility and applies a stacking damage debuff to any operative outside a light source. Light sources appear in a fixed sequence: first at the center of the arena, then along the outer ring clockwise, then at random positions. Memorize the order of light spawns so you can pre-position before each light appears. Staying in darkness for more than 5 consecutive seconds applies a 10% damage vulnerability stack that persists for the rest of the fight. Three stacks means you take 30% more damage from all sources for the remainder of the encounter.

Each light source lasts 15 seconds before fading. When a light fades, move to the next spawn location immediately. Do not wait for the darkness debuff to start ticking before moving — the transition between light sources takes approximately 2 seconds, and being caught in darkness during the transition applies a stack instantly. If you are playing Zayne, time your shield to cover the transition window between light sources. His shield immunity prevents the darkness stack from applying.

Resource Allocation for Phase 3

Phase 3 is a pure DPS race with no healing allowed. The Blood Moon aura reduces all incoming healing by 80%, making sustain strategies ineffective. Before entering Phase 3, ensure all resonance skills are off cooldown and your HP is above 80%. Enter the phase with full energy on your main DPS operative. The first 10 seconds of Phase 3 are the most dangerous because the Priestess begins with Moon's Wrath, which requires a perfect dodge or Ardent Oath i-frame to survive.

Save Sylus's execute for the 35% HP threshold where the Priestess attempts her final Blood Ritual heal. Interrupting this heal is mandatory for a clean Phase 3 clear. If your execute misses, you have one additional interrupt available from your support operative's knockback skill. Do not use both interrupts before the 35% threshold — the Priestess only attempts three heals total, and missing the final one means an almost certain DPS failure in Phase 3.

The Blood Moon Priestess's Blood Ritual heal occurs at exactly 70%, 50%, and 35% HP. Pre-position your interrupt-capable operative near the Priestess 5% HP before each threshold. When the Priestess crosses the threshold, she begins a 3-second channel. Interrupt within the first second to cancel the heal entirely.

Advanced Tip: Save interrupt abilities for Blood Ritual. If the Priestess completes a heal, the fight length increases by 60 seconds. A single failed interrupt can cost you the run. Track her cast bar — she always heals at 70%, 50%, and 35% HP thresholds.

Summary: This is an interrupt check. Miss one Blood Ritual heal and the fight drags. Save CC for heal channels. Stand in the light during Eclipse. Phase 3 is a DPS race — blow all cooldowns.
