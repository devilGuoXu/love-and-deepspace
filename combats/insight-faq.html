Protocores: The Honest FAQ

No gatekeeping. No math PhD required. Just answers.

I have been collecting protocore questions from Discord, Reddit, and personal messages for six months. These are the questions that come up most often, answered in plain language with real examples from my own farming.

1 What main stat should I look for on each piece?

For your main DPS: ATK% on planetary, CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG on the crescent piece, ATK% on everything else. For supports: HP% or DEF% depending on the operative, Energy Regen on the crescent if you want faster ultimates. The exact answer depends on your operative and their signature memory, but these general rules cover 80% of cases. If you are unsure, ATK% is rarely a bad choice for a DPS and HP% is rarely a bad choice for a support.

2 How many substat lines should I aim for before enhancing?

For early game, two usable substat lines is enough. Enhance to +6 and check where the rolls went. If both rolls hit your good substats, keep going. If they missed, trash it and move on. For late game, you want three usable lines minimum before enhancing. The difference between two-line and three-line pieces is about 8% damage at +15, which matters for floor 12 pushing but does not matter for general content.

3 Should I enhance SR protocores or wait for SSR?

Enhance SR pieces to +9 as placeholders while you wait for SSR. An SR piece at +9 with good substats outperforms an unenhanced SSR with perfect substats. I kept my best SR pieces for months while slowly replacing them with SSRs. Do not leave your operatives naked waiting for perfect gear. Gear you have is better than gear you are waiting for.

4 Is Energy Regen worth building?

For specific operatives, yes. For most, no. Sylus benefits from ER at the 145% breakpoint because his ultimate is a huge portion of his damage. Zayne benefits from ER at 125% for permanent shield uptime. Xavier and Rafayel get marginal returns from ER because their damage is spread across their full kit rather than concentrated in their ultimate. Building ER on the wrong operative is a common mistake. Check the breakpoint before you invest in ER substats.

5 How long should I farm one domain before switching?

I use a simple rule: farm a domain until you have two usable pieces from that set, then switch. Two pieces give you the 2-piece set bonus, which is the most efficient stat-per-stamina return. Chasing the 4-piece bonus can take weeks longer. Get your 2-piece, switch to another domain, and come back later for the 4-piece when you have surplus stamina. This approach builds multiple sets simultaneously rather than finishing one set at a time.

6 What do I do with bad SSR protocores?

Keep a small reserve of wrong-main-stat SSRs in case a future operative needs them. I keep 10 max. Everything else gets trashed for enhancement dust. I know it hurts to trash a gold piece. But a gold piece you are not using is just taking up space. The enhancement dust from trashing it might help you roll a piece you actually use. Do not hoard garbage out of sentimentality.

7 How do I know when a protocore is good enough?

This is the most important question in the entire FAQ. A protocore is good enough when it lets you clear the content you are currently working on. If you are stuck on floor 8, you do not need floor-12-quality protocores. You need protocores that are slightly better than what you have now. Upgrade incrementally. Do not hold out for the perfect piece while your current gear holds you back. The perfect is the enemy of the good, and in protocore farming, the perfect will keep you farming for months.

8 Is there a hidden pity system for protocores?

Not for quality. The game guarantees an SSR protocore every 10 runs without one, but there is no guarantee on main stat or substat quality. You can hit SSR pity ten times in a row and get ten HP% main stat pieces. I have come close to this. The lack of quality pity is the single worst aspect of protocore farming, and it is the reason I recommend settling for good enough rather than chasing perfection.

Got a question that is not here? Ask in the community. Someone has probably asked it before. You are not the first person to be confused.
