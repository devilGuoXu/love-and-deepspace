Damage Types and Scaling: What Actually Matters

ATK% vs CRIT vs DMG Bonus. Flat stats vs percentages. Learn what actually increases your damage.

ENTRY_#005 2026.05.22 DAMAGE_SCALING

📊 The Core Insight: Not all damage stats are equal. ATK% sounds good. CRIT sounds better. But the math doesn't always agree. This guide breaks down what each stat actually does — and when to prioritize it.

The Damage Formula — Simplified

Before we talk about stats, let's look at how damage is calculated:

Final Damage = Base ATK × (1 + ATK%) × Skill Multiplier × (1 + DMG Bonus) × CRIT Multiplier (if crit)

Each multiplier is multiplicative with the others. That means 10% ATK is worth the same as 10% DMG Bonus in isolation — but they scale differently with your gear.

💡 The Golden Rule: Spread your stats evenly. 100% ATK + 0% DMG Bonus is worse than 50% ATK + 50% DMG Bonus. Diminishing returns are real.

Flat ATK vs ATK% — The Trap

Many players think flat ATK is useless. That's not entirely true — but it's also not great.

❌ Flat ATK +50
+50 damage before multipliers
After CRIT and DMG Bonus, maybe +150 total. Negligible.
✅ ATK% +10%
+100-120 damage before multipliers
After CRIT and DMG Bonus, maybe +300-400 total. Much better.

⚠️ The Math: A flat ATK substat (like +40) is worth about 3-4% ATK% on most characters. Always prioritize % stats over flat stats.

ATK% — How Much Is Too Much?

ATK% has diminishing returns. Here's why:

0% ATK → 50% ATK +50% damage (huge gain)
50% ATK → 100% ATK +33% damage (still good)
100% ATK → 150% ATK +25% damage (decreasing)
150% ATK → 200% ATK +20% damage (diminishing returns)

💡 Recommendation: Aim for 120-150% ATK from substats and main stats. Beyond that, invest in CRIT or DMG Bonus instead.

CRIT Rate vs CRIT DMG — The Balance

This is the most common question. Here's the answer:

40% CR / 150% CD
~60% effective damage
60% CR / 180% CD
~108% effective damage
70% CR / 220% CD
~154% effective damage
85% CR / 200% CD
~170% effective damage

📊 The Sweet Spot: 65-75% CRIT Rate, then stack CRIT DMG as high as possible. The 1:2 ratio (CRIT Rate : CRIT DMG) is a good guideline, but CRIT Rate has higher value until you hit 65%.

Elemental DMG% — The Underrated Stat

Elemental DMG% (Ice DMG%, Fire DMG%, etc.) is a separate multiplier. That means it's multiplicative with ATK and CRIT, not additive.

📈 Real Example
Base Damage: 10,000 100% ATK: 20,000
+ 50% Ice DMG: 30,000 (not 25,000!)
+ 200% CRIT DMG: 90,000 damage

💡 Always run Elemental DMG% on Slot 2 protocore. It's the only slot that can roll it as a main stat. This is non-negotiable for DPS characters.

Damage Per Screenshot — The Trap

It's tempting to stack everything into one number. A 300k crit looks amazing. But if you only crit 30% of the time, your average damage is lower than a 200k crit at 70% CR.

💀 Glass Cannon Build
30% CR / 280% CD
Average: 30,000 × 0.3 + 84,000 × 0.7 = 67,800
Looks good on screenshots. Inconsistent in practice.
✅ Consistent Build
70% CR / 200% CD
Average: 30,000 × 0.3 + 60,000 × 0.7 = 51,000
Lower peak, higher average. Better for clear times.

⚠️ The Truth: Consistent damage clears content faster than inconsistent peak damage. Don't chase the big number — chase the reliable number.

Summary — Stat Priority Cheat Sheet

🏆 General Priority (DPS)
  • 1. Elemental DMG% (Slot 2 main stat)
  • 2. CRIT Rate (to 65-75%)
  • 3. CRIT DMG (200%+ target)
  • 4. ATK% (to 120-150%)
  • 5. Flat ATK (avoid)
⚡ Character-Specific
  • Sylus: ER to 145% first
  • Zayne: ER to 140% first
  • Rafayel: ER to 120% first
  • Xavier: Ignore ER, full CRIT
📋 Quick Reference

🎯 The Bottom Line: Damage isn't about one big number — it's about consistent, reliable output. Spread your stats evenly. Hit your CRIT Rate breakpoint. Don't ignore Elemental DMG%. And remember: ATK% has diminishing returns.

A balanced build will always outperform a min-maxed glass cannon over 100 runs. Build for consistency. Clear faster.
