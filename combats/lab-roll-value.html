Protocore Roll Value Calculator

Not all protocore substat rolls are equal. A "CRIT Rate 3%" roll is not the same as a "CRIT Rate 10%" roll, even though both are CRIT Rate. Learn to evaluate rolls by their value range, not their stat name.

Core Concept

Core Concept: Each protocore substat has a roll range (minimum to maximum). A "low roll" is the minimum possible value. A "high roll" is the maximum. A protocore with 4 low rolls is worse than a protocore with 2 high rolls. Most players keep garbage protocores because they see "CRIT Rate" and ignore the number. Stop doing that.

The number you see on a protocore ("+6% CRIT Rate") is the SUM of all rolls into that substat. A "+6% CRIT Rate" could be one high roll (6%) or two low rolls (3% + 3%). The source does not matter — only the total matters. But understanding the roll range tells you how much potential that protocore had and whether it is worth keeping for future rolls.

Note on flat stats: Flat ATK (20–80) is inferior to ATK% (3–12%) in almost every scenario. At level 60, 80 flat ATK is roughly equivalent to 6–8% ATK% — so a high flat ATK roll is comparable to a medium ATK% roll. Flat HP and flat DEF are similarly inferior to their percentage counterparts. Only consider flat stats if the percentage version is not available.

Thresholds: Total score > 25 = god tier (keep forever). 18–24 = very good (keep). 12–17 = decent (use until you find better). 8–11 = cope (use for now, salvage later). Below 8 = salvage immediately. These thresholds assume 4 substats total (a fully leveled +15 protocore with all 4 rolls distributed). For partially leveled protocores, adjust expectations proportionally.

Stat Value Weights by Operative

Different operatives value different stats. Multiply the substat's raw score by the operative's weight to get weighted points.

XAVIER (DPS)
CRIT Rate 1.0 / CRIT DMG 0.9 / ATK% 0.7 / ER 0.3 / Flat ATK 0.2
Standard DPS weights. CRIT Rate is king because it gates CRIT DMG. Everything else = 0 weight.
SYLUS (DPS)
ATK% 1.0 / CRIT DMG 0.85 / CRIT Rate 0.7 / ER 0.5 / Flat ATK 0.3
ATK% is Sylus's highest weight because his execute deals bonus damage based on ATK. CRIT is still important but secondary.
RAFAYEL (BURN)
ATK% 1.0 / CRIT Rate 0.85 / CRIT DMG 0.7 / ER 0.6 / Flat ATK 0.2
Burn DoT scales with ATK%, making ATK% his top priority. CRIT still matters for his direct damage skills.
ZAYNE (SUPPORT)
HP% 1.0 / ER 0.9 / Healing Bonus 0.8 / DEF% 0.4
CRIT stats = 0 weight for Zayne. HP% and ER are the only stats that matter. Healing Bonus if available.

How to use weights: A 10% CRIT Rate roll on Xavier: 10 points × 1.0 weight = 10 weighted points. The same roll on Zayne: 10 × 0 = 0. A 12% HP% roll on Zayne: 10 points × 1.0 = 10 weighted points. The same roll on Xavier: 10 × 0 = 0. Use weighted scores to compare protocores across different operatives — a "Zayne god protocore" has high HP% and ER rolls, not CRIT stats.

Common mistake: Keeping a protocore with 3 perfect CRIT rolls for Zayne because "the rolls are good." No — those rolls are worthless on Zayne. Either transfer that protocore to a DPS operative or salvage it. A protocore with high CRIT rolls but low HP% is a DPS protocore, not a Zayne protocore. Evaluate protocores in the context of the operative who will use them.

How to Level Protocores

When leveling a protocore from 0 to 15, you get 4 substat rolls (at +3, +6, +9, +12). Each roll can go into any existing substat or create a new one if there are empty slots.

1
Level to +3
Get the first substat roll. If it goes into a bad substat (DEF%, flat DEF), stop and salvage immediately. Do not invest further.
2
Level to +6
If the first roll was good, level to +6. If the second roll is also good (perfect or usable substat with decent value), continue. If it is bad, salvage.
3
Level to +9
With 2 good rolls by +6, the protocore has potential. Level to +9. If the third roll is good, the protocore is worth taking to +15. If it is bad, evaluate: is 2 out of 3 good enough for a temporary piece?
4
Level to +15
Only take a protocore to +15 if you have 3+ good rolls by +9. A protocore with 2 good rolls by +6 and 3 by +9 is a safe +15. A protocore with only 2 good rolls total is not worth the dust to reach +15.

Pro Tip: A protocore with 2 good rolls by +6 is worth taking to +9. If the third roll is also good (3/3), take it to +15. If not, use it at +9 as a temporary filler and salvage when you find a better one. A +9 protocore costs far less dust than a +15 but gives ~80% of the stats. For F2P players, having four +9 protocores is better than one +15 and three +0.

Salvage vs Keep Decision Matrix

KEEP — LEVEL TO +15
All 4 rolls into perfect substats OR 3 perfect + 1 usable
Main stat must be correct for the operative. These are your long-term keepers. Example: Xavier protocore with CRIT Rate 8% / CRIT DMG 15% / ATK% 10% / ER 6%.
KEEP — LEVEL TO +9 (FILLER)
2 perfect + 2 usable rolls, main stat correct
Use these as temporary pieces while farming for replacements. Do not take them past +9. Salvage once you find a better option.
SALVAGE IMMEDIATELY
0–1 perfect rolls, wrong main stat, any DEF% on DPS, any CRIT on Zayne
Do not level these at all. Salvage at +0 for dust. Every level on a bad protocore is wasted dust you could use on a good one.

Dust is the real bottleneck. Enhancement materials (dust) are a limited resource. Every protocore you take to +15 with bad rolls is dust you cannot use on a good protocore later. Be ruthless with salvaging. A good protocore at +9 is better than a bad protocore at +15. Most players keep too many protocores "just in case" — do not be that player. If a protocore does not meet the "keep" criteria, salvage it.

Advanced Roll Evaluation Tips

Flat vs Percentage: A high flat ATK roll (60–80) is roughly equivalent to a medium ATK% roll (5–6%) for a level 60 operative. If you already have +80%+ ATK from percentage sources, flat ATK becomes slightly more valuable because percentage ATK has diminishing returns. However, flat ATK does not benefit from ATK% multipliers — so it is always worse than ATK% in builds with high ATK% bonuses.

ER for Supports: Energy Recharge is often undervalued on support builds. For Zayne, a 12% ER high roll is worth more than a 12% HP% high roll in most content, because ER enables more frequent Oath casts (which provide both healing and shield). Weight ER at 0.9 for Zayne — almost as important as HP%.

DEF% Trap: DEF% is never worth keeping on any operative. Even on Zayne, the weight is 0.4 — and that is generous. The damage reduction from DEF is so small that DEF% rolls are effectively dead stats. Salvage any protocore that rolls DEF% more than once, regardless of the other substats.

Summary: CRIT Rate roll range: 2.5–10%. CRIT DMG: 5–20%. ATK%: 3–12%. Score system: high roll on perfect substat = 10 points. Weights by operative. Stop leveling at +3 if the first roll is bad. Level to +15 only if you have 2+ good rolls by +6. Salvage ruthlessly — dust is the real bottleneck. A +9 good protocore > +15 bad protocore. Evaluate by operative context, not by raw roll quality.
