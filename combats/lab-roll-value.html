EXTREME LAB — ROLL VALUE

Protocore Roll Value Calculator

Not all protocore substat rolls are equal. A "CRIT Rate 3%" roll is not the same as a "CRIT Rate 10%" roll, even though both are CRIT Rate. Learn to evaluate rolls by their value range, not their stat name.

ENTRY_#LAB_042026.07.02PROTOCORE_MATH

Core Concept: Each protocore substat has a roll range (minimum to maximum). A "low roll" is the minimum possible value. A "high roll" is the maximum. A protocore with 4 low rolls is worse than a protocore with 2 high rolls. Most players keep garbage protocores because they see "CRIT Rate" and ignore the number. Stop doing that.

Substat Roll Ranges

CRIT Rate: 2.5% (min) — 10.0% (max) per roll. A high roll (8%+) is worth 3x more than a low roll (3%). CRIT DMG: 5.0% (min) — 20% (max) per roll. ATK%: 3.0% (min) — 12% (max) per roll. HP%: 3.0% (min) — 12% (max) per roll. DEF%: 3.0% (min) — 12% (max) per roll. Energy Recharge: 3.0% (min) — 12% (max) per roll. Flat ATK: 20 (min) — 80 (max) per roll. Flat HP: 200 (min) — 800 (max) per roll. Flat DEF: 15 (min) — 60 (max) per roll. Note: When a protocore says "+X% CRIT Rate," that number is the SUM of all rolls into that substat. A "+6% CRIT Rate" could be one high roll (6%) or two low rolls (3% + 3%). The source does not matter — only the total matters.

Roll Value Scoring System

Use this scoring system to evaluate any protocore: Perfect substat + high roll (8-10% Rate): 10 points. Perfect substat + medium roll (5-7%): 6 points. Perfect substat + low roll (2-4%): 3 points. Usable substat + high roll: 7 points. Usable substat + medium: 4 points. Usable substat + low: 2 points. Bad substat (DEF% on Xavier): 0 points regardless of roll. Thresholds: Total score > 25 = god tier (keep forever). 18-24 = very good (keep). 12-17 = decent (use until you find better). 8-11 = cope (use for now, salvage later). Below 8 = salvage immediately.

Stat Value Weights by Operative

Xavier (DPS): CRIT Rate (weight 1.0), CRIT DMG (0.9), ATK% (0.7), ER (0.3), Flat ATK (0.2). Everything else = 0. Sylus (DPS): ATK% (1.0), CRIT DMG (0.85), CRIT Rate (0.7), ER (0.5), Flat ATK (0.3). Rafayel (Burn): ATK% (1.0), CRIT Rate (0.85), CRIT DMG (0.7), ER (0.6), Flat ATK (0.2). Zayne (Support): HP% (1.0), ER (0.9), Healing Bonus (0.8), DEF% (0.4). CRIT stats = 0. How to use weights: Multiply the substat's score by the operative's weight. A 10% CRIT Rate roll on Xavier: 10 points × 1.0 weight = 10 weighted points. The same roll on Zayne: 10 × 0 = 0. A 12% HP% roll on Zayne: 10 points × 1.0 = 10 weighted points. The same roll on Xavier: 10 × 0 = 0. Use weighted scores to compare protocores across different operatives — a "Zayne god protocore" has high HP% and ER rolls, not CRIT stats.

Pro Tip: When leveling a protocore from 0 to 15, you get 4 substat rolls (at +3, +6, +9, +12). Each roll can go into any existing substat or create a new one if there are empty slots. Strategy: Level to +3 first. If the roll goes into a bad substat (DEF%, flat DEF), stop and salvage. If it goes into a good substat, continue to +6. If the second roll is also bad, salvage. A protocore with 2 good rolls by +6 is worth taking to +15. A protocore with 0 good rolls by +6 is garbage — salvage immediately and save your enhancement materials.

Salvage vs Keep Decision Matrix

Keep (level to +15): All 4 rolls into perfect substats OR 3 rolls into perfect + 1 into usable. Main stat is correct for the operative. Keep (level to +9, use as filler): 2 perfect + 2 usable rolls. Main stat is correct. Use until you find a better one. Salvage: 0-1 perfect rolls. Main stat is wrong for the operative. Any roll into DEF% or Flat DEF on a DPS. Any roll into CRIT% on Zayne. Important: Enhancement materials (dust) are a limited resource. Every protocore you take to +15 with bad rolls is dust you cannot use on a good protocore later. Be ruthless with salvaging. A good protocore at +9 is better than a bad protocore at +15.

Summary: CRIT Rate roll range: 2.5-10%. CRIT DMG: 5-20%. ATK%: 3-12%. Score system: high roll on perfect substat = 10 points. Weights by operative. Stop leveling at +3 if the first roll is bad. Level to +15 only if you have 2+ good rolls by +6. Salvage ruthlessly — dust is the real bottleneck. A +9 good protocore > +15 bad protocore.
