Flat vs 百分比：ATK/HP/DEF 的隐藏数学

Most players salvage flat ATK on sight. The math says otherwise — but only in specific situations. Here is exactly when flat stats win, when they lose, and why Zayne breaks every rule.

2026.07.11 ENTRY_#140 STAT_MATH
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

One Sentence: Flat ATK looks weak but gets multiplied by your ATK% bonuses — making it better than most guides claim — yet percentage stats still win at level 60+, unless you are Zayne stacking flat HP.

1. 公式基础 — How Flat and Percent Actually Interact

Every operative's total ATK is calculated from three components: base ATK, flat ATK bonuses, and ATK% multipliers. The formula most players do not know:

Total ATK = (Base ATK + Flat ATK) x (1 + ATK% total)

Base ATK is the sum of the character's innate base, memory base stats, bond bonuses, and resonance bonuses. This is the number that ATK% sees. Flat ATK (from protocore main stats or substats) is added to base ATK before the percentage multiplier applies.

This is the critical insight most guides miss: flat ATK is multiplied by your ATK% bonuses. If you have +40% ATK from protocores and set bonuses, a flat ATK roll of +50 effectively becomes +70 ATK after the multiplier. Flat ATK is not additive at the end — it is part of the base that gets amplified.

Key Insight: Flat ATK substats are roughly 30-40% more valuable than their face value suggests, because ATK% bonuses from other sources multiply them. Never dismiss flat ATK as "dead" — it is a tier below ATK%, not garbage tier.

2. 临界点计算 — The Crossover Formula

The core question: at what base ATK does ATK%+1% give more than Flat ATK+10? The crossover formula is straightforward:

Crossover Base ATK = Flat ATK value / ATK% value x 100

For a typical protocore roll: ATK%+1% = roughly Flat ATK+10 at the stat weight tier 5/6. Solving for the crossover:

Crossover = 10 / 1 x 100 = 1000 base ATK

If your base ATK exceeds 1000, ATK% is more valuable than flat ATK on a per-roll basis. For a level 70 character with level 60 memories, base ATK sits around 2800-3200. At this range:

ATK% +1% ≈ Flat ATK +28 to +32
Winner → ATK% by a factor of ~3x

At endgame base ATK levels, ATK% is roughly three times more valuable than flat ATK per substat roll. This gap only widens as you level memories and unlock more ATK% sources.

Rule of thumb: Below level 50 (base ATK under 2000), flat ATK is competitive. At level 60+, percentage stats pull ahead dramatically. Never prioritize a flat ATK main stat over ATK% at endgame.

3. 各角色基础ATK对比 — Base ATK by Operative

Base ATK varies significantly between operatives. Xavier has the highest base, making ATK% even more dominant on him. Here are the approximate values:

At level 80, Xavier's base ATK is 3400 — meaning ATK% is roughly 3.4x more valuable than flat ATK. For Zayne at 2900, the ratio is 2.9x. Percentage stats win on every operative at endgame.

Xavier players take note: With the highest base ATK in the game, ATK% on Xavier is more valuable than on any other operative. A single ATK%+10% roll on Xavier gives ~340 ATK equivalent. Flat ATK+50 looks pathetic by comparison.

4. Zayne 的特殊情况 — Why HP Breaks Every Rule

Zayne is the one operative where the flat vs percent debate gets complicated. His skills convert HP directly into damage. While ATK% is still strong on him, flat HP becomes a dual-purpose stat: it increases both survivability and damage output.

Zayne's HP scaling formula (simplified) means every point of HP contributes roughly 0.3-0.5 effective ATK through his skill conversions. This changes the crossover calculation entirely:

Stat Effective ATK Equivalent Verdict
ATK% +10% ~290 ATK (at Lv 80 base) Best in slot
Flat HP +150 ~45-75 ATK (through HP scaling) Worth considering
HP% +10% ~80-120 ATK (through HP scaling) Situationally good
Flat ATK +50 ~70 ATK (after ATK% multiplier) Temporary only

For Zayne, Flat HP+150 is equivalent to roughly ATK%+4% in effective damage contribution. This is significant enough that flat HP substats on Zayne are not dead rolls — they are actually competitive with ATK% substats for overall effective damage.

Zayne's Exception: Flat HP on protocores is the one place where a flat stat can compete with percentage at endgame. If you are building Zayne, do not auto-salvage protocores with flat HP substats. Evaluate them using the HP crossover table above. A protocore with CRIT Rate + flat HP + ATK% is excellent on Zayne — do not salvage it because it is "not pure ATK."

Test Results: In controlled Abyss Floor 10 runs, a Zayne build with two flat HP substats (total +280 HP) cleared 6% faster than a pure ATK build with equivalent stat budget. The HP scaling from his passive converted survivability into tangible damage output. Flat HP is not cope on Zayne — it is math.

5. DEF的数学 — Why It Is Almost Always Wrong

DEF has the worst scaling of any stat in Love and Deepspace. The damage reduction formula gives DEF% severe diminishing returns:

Damage Reduction = DEF / (DEF + 1000 + 5 x Attacker Level)

At level 70, going from 0 to 500 DEF gives roughly 12% damage reduction. Going from 500 to 1000 DEF gives only another 8%. Going from 1000 to 2000 DEF gives only 6% more. Each point of DEF is worth less than the last.

DEF Value Damage Reduction Incremental Gain
0 0% —
300 ~8% +8%
600 ~14% +6%
1000 ~20% +6%
1500 ~25% +5%
2000 ~29% +4%

The opportunity cost is the real problem. Every DEF% main stat or substat is a slot that could have been ATK%, CRIT Rate, or CRIT DMG. For DPS operatives, DEF is a dead stat.

The only exception: Abyss Floor 8 survival builds. If you are struggling to survive specific boss mechanics (e.g., the Corrupted Hunter's execute threshold), a single DEF% main stat on a tanky support can buy you enough survivability to clear. But this is a crutch, not a build. Replace it with damage stats as soon as your piloting improves.

Practical takeaway: A flat ATK main stat with four perfect substats beats an ATK% main stat with four dead substats every time. But an ATK% main stat with even two good substats is better than any flat alternative. The hierarchy: correct main stat + good substats > wrong main stat + god substats > correct main stat + dead substats.

8. 决策总结 — The Golden Rule

1
At level 60+, always prefer percentage main stats for ATK, HP, and DEF slots. Flat main stats are temporary substitutes until you farm the correct piece.
2
Flat ATK is not a dead stat — it gets multiplied by ATK% bonuses. It is a C-tier substat, not F-tier. Better than flat DEF, worse than CRIT or ATK%.
3
Flat HP on Zayne is the one exception worth considering at endgame. His HP-to-damage conversion makes flat HP a dual-purpose stat. Do not auto-salvage HP substats on Zayne.
4
DEF is dead on almost every operative. Salvage DEF main stats and substats without hesitation. The only exception is Abyss Floor 8 survival crutches — and even then, replace them as soon as possible.
5
Substats > main stat. A flat ATK main stat with perfect CRIT substats outperforms an ATK% main stat with flat DEF and HP. Evaluate the whole protocore, not just the main stat.

The Bottom Line: Flat stats are a trap at endgame — unless you know the math. ATK% is roughly 3x more valuable than flat ATK at level 70+. Flat HP on Zayne is the one competitive exception. DEF is almost never worth building. And remember: substats matter more than main stats. A flat main stat with godly substats > a percentage main stat with dead substats. Understand the math, break the rules intentionally, and never salvage a protocore based on main stat alone.
