EXTREME LAB — BREAKPOINTS

Breakpoint Calculator

Specific stat thresholds that change how your operative performs — CRIT Rate breakpoints for consistent nuking, ER breakpoints for rotation cycles, HP breakpoints for survival.

ENTRY_#LAB_072026.07.02BREAKPOINTS

What Is a Breakpoint: A specific stat value where your operative's performance qualitatively changes. Crossing a breakpoint does not give a gradual improvement — it enables a new rotation, unlocks a new clear threshold, or changes your protocore priority. Breakpoints are more important than raw stat increases.

CRIT Rate Breakpoints

30% CRIT Rate: Minimum viable. Without 30%, your CRITs are too inconsistent for any content above Abyss floor 6. Below this threshold, you should not bother with CRIT DMG — stack ATK% instead. 50% CRIT Rate: Comfortable for all content up to Abyss floor 9. At 50%, roughly every other hit crits. Most F2P players plateau here. 65% CRIT Rate: Optimal for Abyss floors 10-12 with consistent damage. At 65%, you crit 2 out of 3 hits. This is the recommended minimum for endgame content. 80% CRIT Rate: Diminishing returns territory. Only worth pursuing if you have a CRIT DMG of 170%+ to match. 95-100% CRIT Rate: Over-investment territory for most operatives. Only Sylus or operatives with "on crit" passives should push this high.

ATK Breakpoints

1,500 ATK: Minimum for Abyss floor 5. Below 1,500, your skills tickle enemies even with correct element. 2,000 ATK: Comfortable for Abyss floor 7-8. Most level 50 operatives with +9 protocores reach this. 2,400 ATK: Recommended for Abyss floor 10+. This is the breakpoint where your skills start doing meaningful damage through enemy DEF reduction. 3,000+ ATK: Whale territory. Requires level 60+ memories with perfect ATK% rolls on every protocore. The difference between 2,400 and 3,000 ATK is approximately 18% more damage before DEF calculation. Important: ATK breakpoints are affected by enemy DEF. At Abyss floor 12, enemy DEF reduces your effective ATK by ~50%. Your displayed ATK is not your real ATK — account for DEF reduction when evaluating your stats.

Energy Recharge Breakpoints

100% ER (base): One Ardent Oath per 60s of combat (assuming zero perfect dodges). Not enough for any rotation-dependent team. 120% ER: One Ardent Oath per ~45s. Minimum for DPS operatives. At 120%, you can use Oath every other rotation cycle. 140% ER: One Ardent Oath per ~35s. Ideal for most DPS operatives. Your Oath is available every 2nd or 3rd skill rotation. 160% ER: One Ardent Oath per ~28s. Support operative target (Zayne). Your Oath is up every other rotation. 180-200% ER: One Ardent Oath per ~20-22s. Over-investment for most content. Only useful in speedrun compositions where you need Oath every rotation. Breakpoint rule: Add ER until your Ardent Oath is available exactly when your main DPS's skill rotation finishes. No earlier, no later. Extra ER beyond alignment is wasted.

Pro Tip: You can check if you hit a breakpoint by testing against the Abyss floor 10 training dummy (the first enemy of floor 10). If Xavier's Light Slash crits for 18k+ with correct element, you have crossed the 2,400 ATK / 65% CRIT breakpoint. If it crits for less than 12k, you need more ATK before more CRIT. Use training dummies to validate your breakpoints — do not trust spreadsheets alone.

HP & Defense Breakpoints (Zayne)

25k HP: Minimum for Zayne support. Your shield absorbs ~3 hits from Abyss floor 8 enemies. 30k HP: Comfortable for Abyss floor 10. Shield absorbs ~5 hits. 35k HP: Recommended for Abyss floor 12. Shield absorbs ~7 hits. Your healing (15% of max HP per shield) heals for 5,250 per cast. 40k+ HP: Whale territory. The shield value increase past 35k drops by 50% due to diminishing returns. Only worth pursuing if you have BiS protocores everywhere else. DEF breakpoints: Do not worry about DEF. 1,000 DEF = ~20% damage reduction at Abyss floor 10. 2,000 DEF = ~30% damage reduction. The gain from 1,000 to 2,000 DEF is only 10% — this is 3-4 protocore rolls that could have gone into HP% instead. Never build DEF.

Practical Breakpoint Cheat Sheet

Minimum for Abyss floor 7: 1,800 ATK / 40% CRIT Rate / 100% CRIT DMG / 100% ER. Target for Abyss floor 10: 2,200 ATK / 60% CRIT Rate / 130% CRIT DMG / 120% ER. Target for Abyss floor 12: 2,600 ATK / 65% CRIT Rate / 150% CRIT DMG / 130% ER. Zayne target: 32k HP / 150% ER at minimum. Speedrun target: 2,800+ ATK / 70%+ CRIT Rate / 170%+ CRIT DMG / 140% ER — this is the point where Abyss floors 9-10 become 30-second clears.

Summary: CRIT: 50% comfortable, 65% endgame, 80% over-investment. ATK: 2,000 comfortable, 2,400 endgame, 3,000 whale. ER: 120% DPS min, 140% DPS target, 160% support target. HP: 30k Zayne min, 35k Zayne target. Never build DEF. Use Abyss floor 10 dummy to validate breakpoints.
