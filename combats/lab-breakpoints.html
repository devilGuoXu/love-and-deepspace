What Is a Breakpoint

What Is a Breakpoint: A specific stat value where your operative's performance qualitatively changes. Crossing a breakpoint does not give a gradual improvement — it enables a new rotation, unlocks a new clear threshold, or changes your protocore priority. Breakpoints are more important than raw stat increases.

Think of breakpoints as gates. Below a 50% CRIT Rate, you cannot rely on crits to kill tough enemies. At 50%, roughly half your hits crit, making damage predictable. At 65%, you can plan rotations around crits. Each gate changes how you play, not just how much damage you deal.

There are five major stat categories with distinct breakpoints: CRIT Rate, ATK, Energy Recharge, HP, and (rarely) DEF. Every operative type prioritizes these differently. DPS operatives live or die by their CRIT and ATK breakpoints. Supports chase ER and HP thresholds. Understanding which breakpoints matter for your operative is the first step to efficient gearing.

Edge Case — Sylus: Sylus's execute mechanic benefits from high CRIT Rate because the execute window is short (boss below 30% HP). Every non-crit during execute is a lost opportunity. Sylus players should target 70%+ CRIT Rate even if it means sacrificing some CRIT DMG. For other DPS operatives, 65% is the ceiling.

ATK Breakpoints

ATK is your base damage stat, but it is deceptive — enemy DEF at higher floors reduces your effective ATK significantly. The displayed ATK number on your stat screen is not the real damage you deal.

Important: ATK breakpoints are affected by enemy DEF. At Abyss floor 12, enemy DEF reduces your effective ATK by ~50%. Your displayed ATK is not your real ATK — account for DEF reduction when evaluating your stats. A character with 2,400 displayed ATK has roughly 1,200 effective ATK against floor 12 enemies.

The 2,400 Rule: Once you reach 2,400 ATK with correct element matching, your skills start doing meaningful damage through enemy DEF at Abyss floors 10+. Below 2,400, even perfect CRITs will feel weak because the DEF reduction eats too much of your damage. Prioritize ATK% substats on all protocores until you cross 2,400.

Breakpoint Rule: Add ER until your Ardent Oath is available exactly when your main DPS's skill rotation finishes. No earlier, no later. Extra ER beyond alignment is wasted. To find your target: time your rotation (skills 1→2→3→auto-fill). If Oath is not ready when rotation ends, add ER. If Oath is ready 5+ seconds before rotation ends, remove ER in favor of other stats.

Speedrun breakpoint: 180–200% ER enables Oath-per-rotation, which is only useful in speedrun compositions where you need Oath available every skill rotation cycle. For normal play, this is wasted investment — those substat rolls should go into ATK% or CRIT instead.

Never Build DEF. The gain from 1,000 to 2,000 DEF is only 10% damage reduction. That is 3–4 protocore rolls that could have gone into HP% instead. On Zayne, HP% gives both survivability AND healing output. On DPS, any DEF roll is a completely wasted stat. If a protocore rolls DEF% more than once on a DPS-oriented piece, salvage it.

HP healing math for Zayne: At 35k HP, each shield cast heals the active operative for 15% of max HP = 5,250 HP over 4 seconds. At 40k HP, that becomes 6,000 HP — a 750 HP increase for 5k more HP investment. The extra 5k HP costs you 3–4 protocore rolls that could instead go into Energy Recharge for faster Oath generation. For most content, 35k HP + 150% ER is better than 40k HP + 130% ER.

Speedrun target2,800+ ATK / 70%+ CRIT / 170%+ CRIT DMG / 140% ER

Quick Decision Tree: (1) Is CRIT Rate ≥ 50%? If no → farm CRIT Rate protocores before anything else. (2) Is ATK ≥ 2,000? If no → level memories to 50+ before upgrading protocores further. (3) Is ER ≥ 120% for DPS / 150% for Zayne? If no → replace ER-less protocores. (4) Is CRIT DMG ≥ 130%? If yes → you are ready for Abyss floor 10. Follow this order and you will never waste resources on the wrong stat.

Validating Your Breakpoints

Spreadsheets are useful, but real testing beats theory. Use the Abyss floor 10 training dummy (the first enemy of floor 10) to validate your breakpoints.

Xavier Light Slash crits for 18k+2,400 ATK / 65% CRIT breakpoint crossed ✓
Xavier Light Slash crits for 12k–18kNeed more ATK before more CRIT
Xavier Light Slash crits below 12kATK severely lacking — prioritize ATK% first

How to test: Enter Abyss floor 10. Use your main skill on the first enemy (the training dummy). Record the crit damage number. Repeat 5 times. Take the average. Compare against known breakpoint benchmarks for your operative. Do not trust spreadsheets alone — real damage numbers account for all the hidden variables (DEF, elemental resistance, skill-specific multipliers) that calculators may miss.

Common validation trap: Testing in open-world content instead of Abyss. Open-world enemies have lower DEF than Abyss enemies. A build that feels strong in the overworld may be weak in Abyss. Always validate against Abyss floor 10+ enemies for endgame accuracy.

Summary: CRIT: 50% comfortable, 65% endgame, 80% over-investment. ATK: 2,000 comfortable, 2,400 endgame, 3,000 whale. ER: 120% DPS min, 140% DPS target, 160% support target. HP: 30k Zayne min, 35k Zayne target. Never build DEF. Use Abyss floor 10 dummy to validate breakpoints. Follow the decision tree: CRIT Rate → ATK → ER → CRIT DMG in that order.
