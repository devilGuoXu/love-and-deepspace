COMPANION GUIDE — GIFTS

Gift Preference Guide

Gift giving is the most consistent way to raise affinity. This guide covers every operative's preferences and the best ways to acquire gifts.

ENTRY_#COM_022026.07.02GIFTS

Core Rule: Always give preferred gifts. A preferred gift gives 50 XP. A neutral gift gives only 25 XP. A disliked gift gives 5 XP and reduces the operative's temporary mood. The difference between a preferred and neutral gift over 100 days is 2,500 XP — a full affinity level lost.

Xavier — Gift Preferences

Loves: Stargazing maps, Celestial globes, Rare astronomy books. These give 50 XP and trigger special dialogue. Likes: Light crystals, Star-shaped jewelry, Poetry collections. These give 50 XP but without special dialogue. Neutral: Generic souvenirs, Flowers, Common books. These give 25 XP. Dislikes: Crowded place souvenirs, Loud novelty items, Extremely bright objects. These give 5 XP and negative mood. Xavier gift strategy: Stargazing maps are the best gift for Xavier. They are available from the Crew Shop rotation. Buy them whenever they appear. Astronomy books are a close second and are available from the Event Shop during certain events.

Zayne — Gift Preferences

Loves: Ice crystal sculptures, Vintage medical texts, Rare herbal teas. Likes: Calming music boxes, Frost-themed jewelry, Calligraphy sets. Neutral: Generic art pieces, Common teas, Normal books. Dislikes: Impractical gag gifts, Overly expensive items, Loud or flashy objects. Zayne gift strategy: Ice crystal sculptures are Zayne's BiS gift. They appear in the Crew Shop and as event rewards. Medical texts are also excellent and have the advantage of being available from the permanent shop. Zayne dislikes expensive gifts more than other operatives — giving him a high-cost item he dislikes is doubly wasteful. Stick to low-cost preferred gifts.

Rafayel — Gift Preferences

Loves: Art supplies (high-quality brushes, rare pigments), Flame-themed jewelry, Exotic ingredients. Likes: Handmade crafts, Music boxes, Decorative weapons (miniature). Neutral: Generic art prints, Ordinary ingredients, Common accessories. Dislikes: Mass-produced items, Boring everyday objects, Anything related to water (ironically). Rafayel gift strategy: Art supplies are Rafayel's best gift. The high-quality brush set from the Crew Shop is the single best gift item in the game — it gives 50 XP and a unique voice line. Rafayel appreciates uniqueness. Gifts that seem hand-picked or rare score higher than expensive generic items. A cheap but thoughtful gift beats an expensive but generic one.

Sylus — Gift Preferences

Loves: Dark artifacts, Rare gemstones, Strategy game sets. Likes: Fine wine, Antique books, Shadow-themed accessories. Neutral: Generic collectibles, Decent wine, Normal games. Dislikes: Bright or colorful items, Repetitive gifts (giving the same gift twice in a row), Childish items. Sylus gift strategy: Dark artifacts are Sylus's BiS gift. They are rare and expensive but give maximum XP. Rare gemstones are more commonly available and are a solid second choice. Never give Sylus the same gift twice in a row — he has a hidden "repetition penalty" that reduces XP by 50% for duplicate consecutive gifts. Rotate between two preferred gifts to avoid this penalty.

Gift Acquisition: Crew Shop: Gift boxes refresh daily. Buy them every day. Cost: 300 crew coins per box. Event Shop: Limited gifts during events often give 2x affinity. Save event currency for these. Battle Pass: The paid pass includes exclusive gift items. Weekly Missions: Some weekly missions reward gift tokens. Domain Drops: Rarely, domains drop gift materials that can be crafted into gifts. Crafting: The Destiny Cafe has a gift crafting station. Combine materials to create preferred gifts. Check the crafting recipe list weekly — it rotates. The most efficient gift acquisition strategy is: (1) Buy Crew Shop gift boxes daily. (2) Complete event shop gift purchases. (3) Craft gifts weekly using domain materials. (4) Never buy gifts with diamonds.

Gift Economy Management

Gift management is a resource game within the game. Each operative needs approximately 500 preferred gifts to reach affinity 150 (50 XP per gift, ~25,000 XP). With one gift per day per operative, that is 500 days — far longer than the game has been out. This means supplementing with event gifts and crafting is essential. Priority order for gift spending: (1) Always give the daily gift to your focus operative. (2) Use event-limited gifts during double XP events. (3) Craft gifts when you have excess domain materials. (4) Buy gift boxes from the Crew Shop even if you do not need them immediately — stockpile for future operatives. The 80/20 rule: 80% of your gifts should go to one operative until they reach affinity 100. 20% can be distributed to other operatives for daily interaction unlocks (affinity 10 for Bond Slot 1).

Summary: Xavier loves astronomy items. Zayne loves ice sculptures and medical texts. Rafayel loves art supplies. Sylus loves dark artifacts and gems. Always give preferred gifts. Buy Crew Shop gift boxes daily. Never give disliked gifts. Rotate gifts for Sylus. Stockpile event gifts. Focus 80% of gifts on one operative at a time. Gift management is the most consistent affinity source — do it right and your account grows steadily.
