EVENT ARCHIVE — STORY EVENTS

Limited Story Events

Limited-time story events are the backbone of Love and Deepspace's live service. They offer the best reward-to-stamina ratio in the game. Missing them means missing exclusive memories, protocores, and lore.

ENTRY_#EVT_012026.07.02STORY_EVENT

What They Are: Story events run for 14-21 days, featuring a limited narrative, event-exclusive shop, and usually 1-2 free SSR memories. They follow a predictable formula: story stages (one-time) → farm stages (repeatable for currency) → event shop. Understanding this formula lets you clear any new event in under 30 minutes of planning.

Event Structure

Every story event follows the same skeleton: Phase 1 (Days 1-7): Story stages unlock daily. Read for lore, skip for efficiency. Each stage gives first-clear diamonds and event currency. Phase 2 (Days 7-14): Challenge stages unlock. Harder fights with better currency drops. Farm these. Phase 3 (Days 14-21): Bonus stages and final shop rotation. No new content — pure farming phase. Stamina budget: Clear all story stages first (one-time cost ~120 stamina). Then farm the highest-tier challenge stage only. Never farm lower-tier stages — the currency-per-stamina ratio is always better at higher tiers.

Hidden Objectives

Most events have 3 hidden achievement objectives per stage: (1) Clear without any operative falling below 50% HP. (2) Clear within 90 seconds. (3) Clear using only 2 operatives. Each hidden objective gives 20 diamonds. Total hidden diamonds per event: ~400-600. Always check the objective list before spending stamina — missing these is leaving free diamonds on the table.

Free Memory Strategy

Events almost always offer 1 SSR memory shard farm and 1 SR memory. Priority: Get the SSR memory to Rank 1 (R1) first. This costs ~3,000 event currency. Then buy the SR memory. Then go back to farm more SSR shards for Rank 2 (R2). Never buy SR memories before getting the SSR to R1 — SR memories are easier to get elsewhere. R2 threshold: ~8,000 total currency. Budget 14 days of natural stamina regen (240/day) plus the 5 free stamina packs the event gives.

Pro Tip: The first 3 days of any event are the most stamina-efficient because story stages give bonus first-clear currency. Save your stamina pots for day 4 onwards, when only the repeatable farm stage remains.

Event-Exclusive Protocores

Events often sell a "pre-rolled" SSR Protocore with guaranteed CRIT Rate main stat and 2 locked substats (usually CRIT DMG + ATK%). These are the best value purchases in the event shop after the SSR memory. They cost ~1,500 currency and are guaranteed to roll well. Compared to farming protocore domains (average 50 stamina per garbage protocore), these are a 10x efficiency gain. Buy them every event.

Summary: Story → Challenge → Bonus phase structure. Hidden objectives = free diamonds. SSR R1 > SR > SSR R2 > Protocore > materials. Buy the guaranteed protocore every event. Save stamina pots for after day 3.
