Limited Story Events

Limited-time story events are the backbone of Love and Deepspace's live service. They offer the best reward-to-stamina ratio in the game. Missing them means missing exclusive memories, protocores, and lore.

ENTRY_#EVT_012026.07.02STORY_EVENT

What They Are: Story events run for 14-21 days, featuring a limited narrative, event-exclusive shop, and usually 1-2 free SSR memories. They follow a predictable formula: story stages (one-time) → farm stages (repeatable for currency) → event shop. Understanding this formula lets you clear any new event in under 30 minutes of planning.

Event Structure (3 Phases)

Every story event follows the same skeleton. Understanding the phase timing lets you plan stamina spending ahead of time.

1
Phase 1 — Story Stages (Days 1-7)
Story stages unlock daily. Read for lore, skip for efficiency. Each stage gives first-clear diamonds and event currency. Stamina cost: ~120 total for all story stages (one-time).
2
Phase 2 — Challenge Stages (Days 7-14)
Harder fights with better currency drops. Farm these exclusively. Never farm lower-tier stages — the currency-per-stamina ratio is always better at higher tiers.
3
Phase 3 — Bonus & Farming (Days 14-21)
Bonus stages and final shop rotation. No new content — pure farming phase. Burn remaining stamina pots here to reach your currency targets.

Stamina Budget Rule: Clear all story stages first (one-time cost ~120 stamina). Then farm the highest-tier challenge stage only. Higher tiers always give better currency-per-stamina ratios.

Hidden Objectives

Most events have 3 hidden achievement objectives per stage. Each gives 20 diamonds. Total hidden diamonds per event: ~400-600. Always check the objective list before spending stamina — missing these is leaving free diamonds on the table.

Free Memory Strategy

Events almost always offer 1 SSR memory shard farm and 1 SR memory. Follow this priority order to maximize value.

1
SSR Memory → R1 (Rank 1)
Your #1 priority. Costs ~3,000 event currency. The event SSR is exclusive for ~6 months before entering the standard pool. Getting it to R1 now saves you half a year of waiting.
2
Buy the SR Memory
After securing SSR R1, buy the SR memory. SR memories are easier to get elsewhere, so they come second — but they are still good value at event prices.
3
SSR Memory → R2 (Rank 2)
Go back to farm more SSR shards for R2. R2 threshold: ~8,000 total currency. Budget 14 days of natural stamina regen (240/day) plus the 5 free stamina packs the event gives.

Never buy SR memories before getting the SSR to R1 — SR memories are much easier to obtain elsewhere and will come naturally over time.

Pro Tip: The first 3 days of any event are the most stamina-efficient because story stages give bonus first-clear currency. Save your stamina pots for day 4 onwards, when only the repeatable farm stage remains.

Event-Exclusive Protocores

SSR Protocore — Event Shop
Guaranteed CRIT Rate main stat
2 locked substats: CRIT DMG + ATK%
These are the best value purchases in the event shop after the SSR memory. They cost ~1,500 currency and are guaranteed to roll well.
Efficiency Comparison
Event Protocore: ~1,500 currency
Farming domains: ~50 stamina per garbage roll
Compared to farming protocore domains, event protocores are a 10x efficiency gain. Buy them every event without exception.
Purchase Priority
SSR R1 → Protocore → SR → Materials
If you have to choose between the protocore and the SR memory, buy the protocore. It is a permanent power upgrade that never becomes obsolete.

DO & DON'T Summary

DO
  • Follow the Story → Challenge → Bonus phase structure
  • Farm the highest-tier challenge stage only
  • Check and complete hidden objectives every stage (free diamonds)
  • Prioritize SSR R1 before buying anything else
  • Buy the guaranteed protocore every event
  • Save stamina pots for after day 3
DON'T
  • Don't farm lower-tier stages — worse currency-per-stamina ratio
  • Don't buy SR memories before SSR R1
  • Don't skip hidden objectives (they are free diamonds)
  • Don't waste stamina pots in the first 3 days
  • Don't skip the protocore even if you are short on currency
  • Don't ignore the event calendar — phase timings matter

Summary: Story → Challenge → Bonus phase structure. Hidden objectives = free diamonds. SSR R1 > SR > SSR R2 > Protocore > materials. Buy the guaranteed protocore every event. Save stamina pots for after day 3.
