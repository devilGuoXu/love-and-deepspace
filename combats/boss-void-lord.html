Boss Overview — The Void Lord

Void Lord is the gatekeeper of Chaos Abyss Floor 14. It's not the hardest boss in terms of raw damage, but its phase transition mechanic has ended more runs than any attack. The fight is divided into two distinct phases, and Phase 2 is where most players fail.

Phase 1 — Burn Phase (100% - 41% HP)

Phase 1 is straightforward. Void Lord uses a limited attack set and has no invincibility mechanics. Your goal here is simple: burn him down to 40% as fast as possible, but with one crucial caveat — you need to save your cooldowns.

⚠️ Critical Tip: Do NOT use your Ardent Oath in Phase 1. Save it for Phase 2. The damage window after breaking the invincibility shield is where you need burst. Using ultimate early is the #1 mistake I see.

🌑 Phase 1 Attacks
Simple, telegraphed attacks. Use this time to learn his rhythm.
Slash Combo: Three quick swipes. Dodge sideways.
Void Blast: Ranged orb. Easy to sidestep.
Dash: Teleports behind you. Turn and dodge.

Phase 2 — The Invincibility Phase (40% HP)

When Void Lord reaches 40% HP, he teleports to the center of the arena and activates an invincibility shield. During this time, he takes ZERO damage. This is where 90% of players fail.

🛡️
What Happens at 40%?
  • Boss becomes invincible (all damage reduced to 0)
  • 5 Light Orbs spawn around the arena
  • Boss continues attacking while invincible
  • You must collect all 5 orbs to disable the shield
  • After shield drops, boss has 50% reduced defense for 10 seconds

Light Orb Mechanics — Everything You Need to Know

1
Orbs spawn in fixed positions around the arena edge
2
Touch an orb to collect it (no special action needed)
3
Orbs disappear after 15 seconds
4
Boss continues attacking while you collect
5
After 5th orb, shield drops immediately

💡 Pro Tip: Learn the orb spawn locations before the fight. There are 7 possible spawn points, but only 5 orbs spawn each time. Plan your collection route in advance. Wasting time searching = taking unnecessary damage.

Orb Collection Strategy — The Right Way

❌ Bad Strategy
Running around randomly, collecting orbs as you see them, getting hit by boss attacks.
Result: You die or lose too much time.
✅ Good Strategy
Know the spawn points. Plan a circular route. Collect 2-3 orbs, dodge boss attack, collect remaining orbs.
Result: Clean transition with full HP.
🏆 Best Strategy
Split up. One player collects orbs while others distract boss. In solo play, use characters with movement skills (dashes, teleports).
Result: 5-8 second orb collection, full HP, ready to burst.

Phase 2 — The Burst Window (Shield Down)

After collecting all 5 orbs, Void Lord's shield drops and he becomes vulnerable with 50% reduced defense for 10 seconds. THIS is where you unload everything.

10 seconds
50% defense reduction — your biggest damage window
1
Save all cooldowns for Phase 2
Do NOT use Ardent Oath or Resonance Skills in Phase 1 if possible. You need them for the burst window.
2
Collect orbs efficiently (5-8 seconds)
Practice orb routes. The faster you collect, the more time you have to burst.
3
Shield drops → IMMEDIATE BURST
Resonance Skill → Ardent Oath → Full combo. Do not hesitate.
4
Shield returns after 10 seconds
If boss isn't dead, repeat the orb mechanic. But with good burst, he should be close to dead.
💥 Why Saving Cooldowns Matters
Normal rotation outside burst window: 100,000 damage
Same rotation during 50% defense reduction: 200,000 damage
+ Ardent Oath during burst window: 300,000+ damage
💀 Total burst potential: 3x normal damage

Attack Patterns — Phase 2 (Invincible Phase)

During the orb collection phase, Void Lord is still attacking. Here's what to expect:

🌀 Void Storm
Creates a swirling vortex that pulls you toward center.
Counter: Run outward. Don't fight the pull — just keep moving away.
💫 Homing Orbs
Fires 3 orbs that track your position.
Counter: Dodge perpendicular to orb path. They track, so dodge at the last moment.
🌊 Void Wave
Ground wave that expands outward.
Counter: Jump over or dodge through. Timing is strict.
🔄 Teleport Slam
Boss teleports to a random player and slams.
Counter: Watch for the teleport animation, dodge immediately.

Required DPS: 45k — What This Actually Means

The 45k DPS requirement is often misunderstood. It doesn't mean your character sheet needs 45k ATK. It means your effective damage per second needs to average 45k over the 3:30 enrage timer.

Total boss HP: ~1,500,000
Enrage timer: 210 seconds
Required average DPS: ~7,150
Wait — 7k, not 45k? Yes. The "45k" refers to burst DPS during the vulnerability window, not sustained DPS. Don't be intimidated by the number.

💡 Math Check: 1.5M HP ÷ 210 seconds = 7,143 DPS sustained. But because the boss has invincibility phases, your actual damage window is shorter. Aim for 15-20k sustained DPS to clear comfortably.

Common Mistakes (I Made All of These)

❌ Using Ardent Oath in Phase 1
This is the #1 run killer. You need that burst for Phase 2. Be patient.
❌ Running around randomly for orbs
Have a plan. Know the spawn points. Collect in a circle.
❌ Ignoring boss attacks during orb phase
He's still attacking. Dodge while collecting. Don't tunnel vision on orbs.
❌ Not splitting orbs in co-op
In multiplayer, assign each player to different orb spawns. Don't all run to the same orb.
❌ Hesitating during the burst window
10 seconds goes fast. Press your buttons. Don't overthink it.
❌ Forcing a second orb phase
If you don't kill him in the first burst window, you'll need to do orbs again. Try to avoid this — save enough resources for the kill.
