Sylus: Combat Rotation Guide

Sustained pressure, execute chaining, and stealth — Sylus is the most complex operative to master.

ENTRY_#0402026.06.30OPERATIVE_GUIDE

Playstyle: Sylus is a sustained-pressure DPS with unique execute mechanics. Unlike Xavier (burst) or Zayne (hybrid), Sylus wants to stay on the field as long as possible, stacking Dark Energy and chaining executes. His damage ramps up over time — the longer the fight, the more he outpaces other operatives.

Core Rotation

Sylus's rotation is flexible — it changes based on energy level and boss HP. The standard opener: Stealth → Backstab (400% DMG) → Auto x4 → Resonance → Auto x2 → Resonance. Use Stealth to initiate every engagement. The Backstab bonus is huge and guaranteed if you attack from behind within 2s of entering stealth.

Execute threshold: When an enemy is below 30% HP, Sylus's Resonance deals bonus execute damage (scales with missing HP, up to +100% at 5% HP). Against bosses, prioritize bringing the boss to execute range before using your ultimate. If the boss is above 30%, auto-attack to build energy. If below 30%, use Resonance immediately.

Dark Energy Management

Sylus generates Dark Energy stacks from: Backstab (+2), Resonance hit (+3), Perfect Dodge (+1). At 5 stacks, your next Resonance consumes all stacks for +25% bonus damage. At 10 stacks (cap), +50%. The 10-stack Resonance is your biggest damage spike — plan around it. Against bosses, you can hit 10 stacks in roughly 15 seconds with optimal play.

Positioning

Sylus is a melee-range fighter. Stay within 2m of the target at all times. His auto-attacks have the shortest range of any operative. Use Stealth to close gaps and reposition behind the boss. Stealth has a 12s cooldown — use it on cooldown for the Dark Energy stacks even if you don't need to reposition.

Pro Tip: Sylus's Perfect Dodge refunds 15% Resonance Energy (with Dark Champion set). If you can consistently perfect dodge, you can maintain near-100% Resonance uptime. Practice the timing on the training dummy — dodge the dummy's attack just before it hits.

Summary: Sustained pressure fighter. Open with Stealth + Backstab. Stack Dark Energy to 10 for +50% Resonance. Execute below 30% HP. Perfect dodge for energy refund. Melee range always.
