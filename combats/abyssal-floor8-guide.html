THREAT LEVEL: EXTREME

Abyssal Floor 8

The first survival check. Floor 8 tests your sustain and CC just as much as your damage.

ENTRY_#1242026.06.30FLOOR_8
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Required Power: 55k+ | Clear Time: 5:00 | Key Mechanic: Constant DoT aura prevents stalling. Stay aggressive or die.

Wave 1: Vanguard + Rogue

Vanguard: A tanky elite with frontal cleave. Do not stand in front. Kite in circles. Rogue: A fast melee that flanks. Dodge the triple stab combo. Strategy: Kill Rogue first 閳?the Vanguard is slow and easy to avoid. Use Zayne to freeze the Vanguard while you burst the Rogue.

Wave 2: The Survival Gauntlet

DoT Aura: A persistent poison effect ticks for 2% max HP per second. Heal or die. Zayne's shields excel here. Summoner Elite: Spawns small adds that explode on death. Kill them at range to avoid the explosion. Timer Mechanic: Every 30 seconds, the DoT damage increases by 1%. Speed is survival.

Wave 3: Commander Elite

Phase 1 (100%-50%): Two-handed weapon mode. Slow, heavy hits. Punish after each swing. Phase 2 (50%-0%): Dual-wield mode. Fast combos, less damage per hit. Stay behind. Enrage: Below 20%, the Commander gains attack speed and lifesteal. Save Ardent Oath for this phase.

Recommended Team: Zayne (Support/Heal) 閳?mandatory for DoT sustain. Xavier (Main DPS) 閳?consistent damage, good dodge capabilities. Sylus (Sub-DPS) 閳?execute on summons.

Alternative Team Compositions Without Zayne

If you do not have Zayne built, you can substitute a high-HP Rafayel with healing memories as your sustain option. Rafayel's passive HP regeneration, combined with HP-focused protocores, provides approximately 70% of Zayne's sustain capability. The tradeoff is reduced damage output from the support slot. A second option is running double DPS with Xavier and Sylus while relying on perfect dodge uptime to minimize DoT damage. This composition requires near-perfect play but clears faster than the sustain composition.

When running without Zayne, consumable preparation becomes essential. Use the highest tier HP regeneration food available before starting the run. Time your food consumption so the regeneration overlaps with the DoT aura activation in Wave 2. The regeneration effect stacks additively with passive healing but does not stack with multiple food uses 閳?use exactly one food item before Wave 2 and save additional charges for the Commander fight.

Execution Framework for Three-Star Clear

Three-star clear on Floor 8 requires completing all waves within 4 minutes while keeping all operatives alive. The time constraint is the stricter condition. To meet it, you must minimize downtime between waves and maintain near-constant damage output during each wave. Wave 1 should be cleared within 60 seconds. Wave 2 within 90 seconds. Wave 3 within 90 seconds. Use a stopwatch on your phone to track your pace during practice runs and restart if you fall behind schedule.

The execute mechanic on Sylus is your best tool for speeding up the Commander kill in Wave 3. Save Sylus's execute for the Commander's Phase 2 transition at 50% HP. Executing during the weapon-switch animation deals damage while the Commander is scripted to stand still, giving you a 3-second window of free damage. This combined burst can skip the Commander's dual-wield phase entirely if your damage output is high enough.

The DoT aura on Floor 8 deals damage equal to 2% of max HP per tick, with ticks every 2 seconds. Without healing, you die in exactly 50 seconds from full HP. Zayne's shield blocks 3 ticks (6 seconds) of DoT damage. The shield has a 12-second cooldown, so you cannot maintain 100% shield coverage. Use your shield to cover every other DoT tick cycle.

Key Mechanic: The DoT aura cannot be cleansed. You must out-heal or out-shield it. Zayne's skill 2 shield blocks 3 ticks of DoT damage. Time your shields to the pulse rhythm 閳?the DoT ticks every 2 seconds. Shield right after a tick for maximum coverage.

Summary: Floor 8 is a sustain check. If you can keep your HP above 50% while dealing damage, you pass. Zayne is almost mandatory for the DoT phases. Kill the Rogue first. Abuse the Vanguard's slowness. Save ults for Commander's enrage.
