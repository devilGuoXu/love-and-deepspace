THREAT LEVEL: EXTREME

Abyssal Floor 8

The first survival check. Floor 8 tests your sustain and CC just as much as your damage.

ENTRY_#1242026.06.30FLOOR_8

Required Power: 55k+ | Clear Time: 5:00 | Key Mechanic: Constant DoT aura prevents stalling. Stay aggressive or die.

Wave 1: Vanguard + Rogue

Vanguard: A tanky elite with frontal cleave. Do not stand in front. Kite in circles. Rogue: A fast melee that flanks. Dodge the triple stab combo. Strategy: Kill Rogue first — the Vanguard is slow and easy to avoid. Use Zayne to freeze the Vanguard while you burst the Rogue.

Wave 2: The Survival Gauntlet

DoT Aura: A persistent poison effect ticks for 2% max HP per second. Heal or die. Zayne's shields excel here. Summoner Elite: Spawns small adds that explode on death. Kill them at range to avoid the explosion. Timer Mechanic: Every 30 seconds, the DoT damage increases by 1%. Speed is survival.

Wave 3: Commander Elite

Phase 1 (100%-50%): Two-handed weapon mode. Slow, heavy hits. Punish after each swing. Phase 2 (50%-0%): Dual-wield mode. Fast combos, less damage per hit. Stay behind. Enrage: Below 20%, the Commander gains attack speed and lifesteal. Save Ardent Oath for this phase.

Recommended Team: Zayne (Support/Heal) — mandatory for DoT sustain. Xavier (Main DPS) — consistent damage, good dodge capabilities. Sylus (Sub-DPS) — execute on summons.

Key Mechanic: The DoT aura cannot be cleansed. You must out-heal or out-shield it. Zayne's skill 2 shield blocks 3 ticks of DoT damage. Time your shields to the pulse rhythm — the DoT ticks every 2 seconds. Shield right after a tick for maximum coverage.

Summary: Floor 8 is a sustain check. If you can keep your HP above 50% while dealing damage, you pass. Zayne is almost mandatory for the DoT phases. Kill the Rogue first. Abuse the Vanguard's slowness. Save ults for Commander's enrage.
