DEV NOTES — ANNIVERSARY

Anniversary Retrospective

Looking back at Deepspace's journey — launch, major updates, community growth, and what the next anniversary might bring.

ENTRY_#DEV_062026.07.02VERSION

How to Read This: Long-time players can relive the memories. New players should read the "what you missed" section to understand the game's history and what limited content they cannot access anymore.

Launch to First Anniversary

The game launched with 3 operatives (Xavier, Zayne, Rafayel) and approximately 20 hours of story content. The first 3 months focused on establishing the core gameplay loop. Key milestones: Month 1: Server stability issues resolved. First limited banner set records. Daily active users peak at 2M+. Month 2: First major event (Valentine's). First outfit banner introduced. Community photo mode trend goes viral. Month 3: Sylus released as the 4th operative. N109 Zone story arc begins. Player base grows 40%. Month 4-6: Abyssal Trial introduced. Protocore system refined. First balance patch. Month 7-9: Co-op mode added. Senior Hunter Contest introduced. First anniversary preparation events. Month 10-12: Anniversary celebration. Free SSR selector. Major story update. The first year established Deepspace as a top-tier mobile game with a dedicated global community.

What Changed: Year-over-Year Comparison

Comparing the game at launch vs. now: Content volume: Launch had 8 story chapters + 3 character stories. Now has 20+ chapters + 12 character stories + 4 bond stories per operative. Content has tripled. Combat depth: Launch had 2 protocore sets per operative. Now has 5+ viable sets per operative with set-specific strategies. Economy: Launch gave ~3,000 diamonds/month as F2P. Now gives ~5,000/month. Income has increased 67% through events and permanent content. Community: Launch subreddit: 50k members. Now: 500k+. Content creation ecosystem has grown from ~20 dedicated creators to 200+. Revenue model: No significant changes. Battle pass + limited shop + monthly pass remain the core. No predatory monetization added — the team has stayed true to their "fair pricing" promise.

What You Missed (New Player Section)

If you started late, these are the limited items you cannot obtain anymore: Limited outfits: Approximately 15 limited outfits have been released. Some may rerun, but most are gone for 6-12 months minimum. Limited SSR memories: 10+ limited SSRs. Some are meta-defining (e.g., Sylus's debut memory). These will rerun eventually. Event-exclusive furnishings: 8 furniture sets from seasonal events. Cosmetic only — no gameplay impact. Namecards and titles: 20+ namecards from events. Cosmetic only. Login bonuses: If you missed the first anniversary login, you missed a free SSR selector and 2,000 diamonds. The second anniversary will likely have similar rewards. Story context: Limited-time story events are archived in summary form on this site. The main story is always accessible — you have not missed any critical plot permanently.

Second Anniversary Predictions: Based on the first anniversary and developer interviews, the second anniversary will likely include: (1) Free SSR selector (as is tradition). (2) Premium outfit for an anniversary theme. (3) Major story update — possibly the Deepspace origin reveal. (4) New permanent game mode (speculated: guild/co-op expansion). (5) 2x drop rates on all domains for 7 days. (6) Login calendar with 2,000+ free diamonds. (7) Limited memorial namecard and title. Preparation tips: Save 20,000+ diamonds starting 2 months before anniversary. Do not spend event currency 3 weeks before anniversary — anniversary shops have the best value items of the year. Save stamina potions for anniversary double-drop weeks.

Community Growth Milestones

The Deepspace community has grown significantly: Official Discord: 300k+ members. Active channels for every operative, theorycrafting, photo mode, and fan content. Subreddit: 500k+ members. Daily discussion threads, build questions, and fan art. Bilibili: The largest Chinese-language community. High-quality guide creators and story analysis. Twitter/X: Official account has 1M+ followers. Community tags like #DeepspacePhoto and #LoveAndDeepspaceArt are consistently trending. Fan events: Community-run screenshot contests, cosplay showcases, and fan fiction events happen monthly. The community is the healthiest it has ever been — welcoming to new players, active in discussion, and passionate about the game.

Summary: First year saw 3x content growth, 67% more F2P income, 10x community growth. Limited items from first year include ~15 outfits and ~10 SSRs — most will rerun second year. Second anniversary predicted: free SSR selector, major story update, new game mode, double drops. Save resources 2 months before anniversary for maximum value. The community of 500k+ is welcoming — join the Discord or subreddit today.
