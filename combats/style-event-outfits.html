Event-Limited Outfit Catalog

A comprehensive catalog of every limited-time outfit released in Year 1. Includes how to obtain each skin, estimated cost, exclusivity rating, and whether it is worth chasing as a collector.

ENTRY_#STY_072026.07.03OUTFITS

Key Insight: Of the 18 event-limited outfits released in Year 1, only 5 are considered "true exclusives" — outfits that have not returned and likely never will. The remaining 13 have either returned in a rerun event or are expected to return on a 6-12 month cycle. The distinction matters for collectors: true exclusives are worth paying a premium for; rotational exclusives are not — you can wait for the rerun.

The event-limited outfit ecosystem in Year 1 consisted of 18 unique outfits spread across 12 events. The average cost per outfit was $15-30 USD, with some outfits available for free through event progression and others requiring direct purchase. The total cost to collect all 18 outfits was approximately $380 USD — equivalent to roughly 3-4 complete pity cycles on limited banners. This comparison underscores the most important decision framework: outfits versus pulls. Every dollar spent on an outfit is a dollar not spent on a limited memory.

Outfit Categories

Event-limited outfits fall into four categories based on how they are distributed. Each category has different value-for-money characteristics.

Battle Pass Exclusive
$5 — Best value; 1 per season
Event Shop Purchase
Free with event currency; grind required
Premium Direct Purchase
$15-30; highest revenue category
Bundle Exclusive
$25-50; includes other rewards

Battle Pass Exclusives (8 outfits): The most common category. Each season's Battle Pass includes a limited outfit available at level 50 of the premium track. At $5 per pass, these are the best value limited outfits in the game. The trade-off is that you cannot buy them retroactively — if you miss a season, the outfit is permanently missable (so far). Battle Pass outfits have the highest collection completion rate because of their low cost.

Event Shop Purchases (4 outfits): Available for event currency during major events. These are technically "free" but require dedicated farming to earn enough currency. The opportunity cost is the event currency you could have spent on other shop items (tickets, dust, etc.). Evaluate whether the outfit is worth the currency before grinding — for F2P players, tickets and dust are usually higher priority.

Premium Direct Purchases (4 outfits): The most expensive category. These outfits are sold directly in the premium shop for $15-30 each. They are tied to specific events (anniversary, Valentine's, summer) and may not return. Premium outfits have the highest quality designs and the most elaborate effects, but also the highest regret rate if purchased impulsively.

Bundle Exclusives (2 outfits): Only available as part of a paid bundle ($25-50) that includes other items (pulls, resources, furniture). These are the hardest to evaluate because you are paying for the bundle's total value, not just the outfit. If the bundle's non-outfit items are worth ~$15-20 in pull value, then the effective outfit cost is $10-30. Evaluate bundles by subtracting the value of the non-outfit items.

True Exclusives (May Never Return)

These 5 outfits are considered "true exclusives" — released for special occasions with no confirmed rerun plan based on developer statements and historical patterns.

ANNIVERSARY GOWN
Xavier — Celestial Oath (Premium, $25)
Released for the 1st Anniversary celebration. Features unique particle effects, custom combat animations, and a dedicated photo mode pose. The highest-quality outfit in Year 1. Value rating: 5/5 for Xavier mains, 3/5 for others. If you skipped this in the anniversary event, expect to never see it again — anniversary exclusives are historically the least likely to rerun.
VALENTINE'S EXCLUSIVE
Sylus — Crimson Vow (Premium, $20)
Valentine's 2026 limited outfit. Romance-themed with heart particle effects and a custom "embrace" photo pose. Value rating: 5/5 for Sylus mains, 4/5 for collectors. Valentine's outfits have a higher chance of rerunning next Valentine's compared to anniversary outfits, but the first release is always the safest bet for exclusivity.
SUMMER BEACH
Rafayel — Tidal Sonata (Premium, $20)
Summer 2026 seasonal outfit. Beach theme with water effects and a unique "sunset" photo mode filter. Value rating: 4/5 for Rafayel mains, 3/5 for collectors. Summer outfits traditionally rerun each summer, but the specific design may not return.
BATTLE PASS SEASON 1
Zayne — Frost Commander (BP, $5)
The very first Battle Pass exclusive outfit. Frozen armor theme with ice particle effects. The rarest outfit in the game by ownership percentage — only players who purchased BP Season 1 own it. Value rating: 5/5 for collectors (rarest outfit), 4/5 for Zayne mains. If you missed Season 1 BP, this outfit is almost certainly never returning.
COLLABORATION EVENT
Xavier — Nexus Guardian (Bundle, $35)
Released during the limited collaboration event (Summer 2026). Only available as part of the $35 collaboration bundle. The bundle included 10 limited pulls, 200 protocore dust, and a namecard — estimated non-outfit value: ~$12. Effective outfit cost: ~$23. Value rating: 4/5 for Xavier mains, 2/5 for others. Collaboration outfits are true exclusives — the collab license may not be renewable.

Rotational Limiteds (Will Return)

The remaining 13 limited outfits are expected to return on a 6-12 month cycle based on rerun patterns observed in Year 1.

BATTLE PASS SEASONS 2-8
7 outfits across all operatives ($5 each)
Battle Pass outfits from Seasons 2-8. These have not rerun yet, but the developers have indicated that older BP outfits may return as limited shop items or in a "rerun pass" system. The value proposition is simple: $5 for an exclusive outfit is the best deal in the game regardless of rerun timing. If you play regularly, buying each season's BP is the single most efficient collection strategy.
MAJOR EVENT SHOPS
4 outfits from event shops (free with grind)
These outfits were available during major events (Christmas, Lunar New Year, Halloween, Summer Kickoff). Each cost event currency that required 5-7 days of dedicated farming. All four have been confirmed as "returning" in their respective seasonal events. If you missed them, wait for the seasonal rerun rather than spending real money on alternative skins.
HOLIDAY PREMIUM
2 premium outfits (Christmas, Lunar New Year)
Christmas and Lunar New Year premium outfits ($20 each). Both are expected to return during their respective holidays each year. The Christmas outfit (Rafayel — Winter Sonata) returned in Year 1's second Christmas event, confirming the rotation pattern. Lunar New Year outfits are also expected to be annual releases with the previous year's design available for purchase.

Value Ranking

Based on exclusivity, design quality, and cost, here is the definitive value ranking of all 18 event-limited outfits.

S-TIER
BP Season 1 (Zayne), Anniversary Gown (Xavier), Valentine's (Sylus)
Highest exclusivity + best design quality. These three outfits are the most valuable in any collection. BP Season 1 is the rarest. Anniversary Gown has the best effects. Valentine's Sylus has the best photo mode integration. If you own only 3 limited outfits, these should be them.
A-TIER
All other BP outfits, Summer Beach (Rafayel), Collab Bundle (Xavier)
High value for their respective mains. BP outfits are cheap and exclusive. Summer and Collab outfits are high quality but situational. These are worth buying if you main the operative and have the budget.
B-TIER
Event shop outfits, holiday premium outfits
Rotational limiteds that will return. Event shop outfits are "free" with grind — worth the time investment. Holiday premiums are good quality but do not pay a premium for FOMO because they will return next year. Wait for the rerun if you are unsure.
C-TIER
Premium shop outfits for non-main operatives
If you do not use the operative, the outfit has no value to you. Even if the design is beautiful, it sits in your inventory unused. Only buy outfits for operatives you actively use and enjoy looking at in combat and photo mode.

Tip: Before buying any limited outfit, ask yourself: "Would I pay $20 for this outfit if I knew it would come back next year for $15?" If the answer is no, wait for the rerun. True exclusives are the only outfits that justify FOMO pricing. For rotational limiteds, patience is always rewarded with either a discount or a clearer decision when the rerun comes. The only limited outfit I regret not buying is the BP Season 1 — everything else I skipped eventually returned or I stopped caring about.

Summary: 18 event-limited outfits released in Year 1. 5 true exclusives (may never return) — BP Season 1 Zayne, Anniversary Xavier, Valentine's Sylus, Summer Rafayel, Collab Xavier bundle. 13 rotational limiteds expected to return on 6-12 month cycles. Battle Pass ($5) is the best value. Event shop outfits are free with grind. Premium direct purchases ($15-30) are for dedicated mains only. Bundle outfits ($25-50) vary by non-outfit item value. True exclusives are worth FOMO pricing; rotational limiteds are not — wait for the rerun.
