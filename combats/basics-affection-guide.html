Affection & Date Guide

Gifts, dates, affinity — the relationship system that unlocks the best character content.

ENTRY_#022 2026.06.29 AFFECTION

Why Affection Matters: Affinity unlocks date scenes, voice lines, name cards, and stat bonuses. High affinity with all operatives gives permanent account-wide benefits. It's not just flavor — it's progression.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Gift Preferences

Each operative has preferred gifts that give bonus affinity.

Xavier
Likes: Stargazing maps, Light crystals, Astronomy books. Dislikes: Crowded places, Noisy items.
Sylus
Likes: Dark artifacts, Rare gems, Strategy games. Dislikes: Bright flashy items, Repetitive gifts.
Zayne
Likes: Ice sculptures, Medical texts, Calm music. Dislikes: Impractical items, Overly expensive gifts.
Rafayel
Likes: Art supplies, Flame motifs, Rare ingredients. Dislikes: Boring items, Anything mass-produced.

Date Mechanics

Dates unlock at specific affinity thresholds. Each date has a story chapter and a gameplay segment.

Affinity 10: First date unlock. Casual outing. No special requirements.
Affinity 25: Second date. Requires one preferred gift given previously.
Affinity 50: Third date (romantic). Requires 3+ preferred gifts and a specific memory equipped.
Affinity 80: Fourth date. Longest date with branching dialogue options.
Affinity 100: Final date. Unlocks exclusive name card and voice line.

Affinity Farming

Daily Gift: You can give one gift per operative per day. Always give a preferred gift for maximum affinity.

Date Reply: After each date, the operative sends a message. Replying gives bonus affinity (one-time per date).

Event Gifts: Limited-time events often include exclusive gift items that give 2x affinity. Save these for high-affinity thresholds.

Priority: Focus one operative at a time to 100 affinity. Spreading gifts across all 4 operatives slows down everyone's unlock pace.

Gift Economy and Resource Planning

Gift items are available from multiple sources, and knowing which to buy saves significant currency over time. The Crew Shop offers a daily gift box for 5,000 gold that contains a random preferred gift for one of the four operatives. This is the most cost-effective gift source — always purchase it. The Event Shop occasionally offers limited gift boxes for event currency that give 2x affinity. Stockpile event currency specifically for these gift boxes rather than spending it on other items. The Battle Pass offers selectable gift boxes at tier 30 and tier 50 that allow you to choose which operative receives the gift.

Gift crafting is a weekly activity that converts common materials into rare gift items. The crafting recipe requires 5 common materials (easily farmed from domain runs) to produce 1 rare preferred gift worth 60 affinity EXP. Complete the weekly gift crafting missions every week without exception — the material cost is trivial and the affinity return is significant. Over a month, weekly crafting produces 8 rare gifts worth 480 total affinity EXP per operative. Combined with daily Crew Shop purchases, this generates enough gift value to raise one operative from affinity 1 to affinity 30 in approximately three weeks.

Date Content Optimization

Date content unlocks at specific affinity thresholds, but the order in which you experience dates across operatives affects your total gem income. Each operative's first date (Affinity 10) rewards 50 gems. Your strategy should be to raise all four operatives to Affinity 10 before pushing any single operative higher. This nets 200 total gems from first dates while the cost to reach Affinity 10 is minimal — approximately one week of consistent gift giving per operative. After all first dates are unlocked, focus on a single operative to Affinity 50 for the third date and its larger gem reward.

Date dialogue choices sometimes affect minor rewards but never lock you out of content. The branching dialogue options at Affinity 50 and Affinity 80 dates affect which voice lines and base decorations you receive but all routes lead to the same final unlock. Choose the dialogue option that feels most natural to you rather than optimizing for rewards — the difference is cosmetic, not mechanical. If you want to experience all dialogue branches, the "Story Archive" feature allows replaying completed dates with different choices after you have unlocked them the first time.

Efficiency Tip: Gift items from the Crew Shop are the most consistent source of preferred gifts. Buy gift boxes daily. Complete the weekly gift crafting missions for rare gifts.

The Bottom Line: Affinity is a long-term system. Give one preferred gift per operative daily. Focus on one operative at a time. Save event gifts for milestones. And enjoy the dates — they're some of the best-written content in the game.
