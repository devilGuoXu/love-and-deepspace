Deepspace Origin

The dimensional rift that changed everything. Where did it come from — and what is on the other side?

ENTRY_#N109_052026.06.29CLASSIFIED

The Event: The Deepspace rift appeared without warning 17 years ago. A tear in reality over the Pacific Ocean. The initial energy release caused worldwide blackouts and the first Wanderer sightings. The Hunter Corps was formed within a month.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Scientific Theories

Dimensional Collision: The leading theory. Our universe collided with a parallel dimension, creating a permanent scar between realities. The Wanderers come from this other dimension. This explains why they have no known origin within our universe.

Evolutionary Awakening: A minority theory. The Deepspace is not a wound but a birth — humanity's next evolutionary step. The rift is a conduit for Evol energy, and Wanderers are the immune response of reality rejecting this change. This theory has gained traction within Onychinus.

Mythological Parallels

The Deepspace shares striking similarities with the concept of the "Abyss" in ancient mythology — a boundary realm between worlds. The N109 Zone was named after the 109th recorded Wanderer incursion, but numerology fans note that 109 is also a prime number associated with "threshold" in several esoteric traditions.

Deepspace Energy Readings

Monitoring stations around the Deepspace rift have recorded unusual energy patterns that do not correspond to any known physical phenomena. The energy signature is composed of three distinct frequencies that cycle in a repeating 72-hour pattern. This pattern has remained consistent since the rift first appeared, suggesting either a natural resonance or a deliberate signal. The Hunter Corps has classified the raw frequency data, but independent researchers who have analyzed leaked readings describe the pattern as "structured" and "deliberate" rather than random.

The energy levels near the rift fluctuate with an amplitude that correlates to solar activity, but the correlation is imperfect. Some fluctuations occur without any corresponding solar event, and some solar events produce no detectable change in the rift's output. This imperfect correlation suggests that while the rift is influenced by external factors, it has an internal energy source that operates independently of our dimension's physical laws.

What Lies Beyond

No probe sent into the Deepspace has ever returned. Operatives who have touched the rift report visions of a world burning in eternal twilight. Sylus, who has spent more time near the Deepspace than any other Hunter, has never spoken about what he has seen. When asked, he changes the subject.

Classified Note: Recent readings from the N109 monitoring station indicate the Deepspace is expanding at an accelerating rate. At current projections, the rift will engulf the Hunter Corps HQ within 50 years. This information has not been made public.

Dimensional Impact on Earth

The Deepspace rift's presence has fundamentally altered Earth's environment beyond the immediate combat zone. Seismic activity near the rift has increased by 300% since the rift first appeared. Ocean currents in the Pacific have shifted, creating new weather patterns that affect global climate. The energy emissions from the rift have accelerated plant growth in a 500-kilometer radius, creating an artificial jungle zone around the quarantine perimeter. Researchers have documented over 200 new plant species that appear to be mutations caused by dimensional energy exposure, none of which exist in any pre-rift botanical record.

Global Response to the Rift

The international response to the Deepspace rift was unprecedented in human history. Within 72 hours of the rift's appearance, the United Nations convened an emergency session that established the Hunter Corps as a global task force. The initial budget allocation for Hunter Corps operations exceeded the combined military spending of all nations for that year. Seventeen years later, the Hunter Corps operates with independent authority across all member nations, with a permanent headquarters established in neutral territory near the N109 Zone.

The Deepspace rift emits energy at a consistent frequency of 109 MHz. This prime number appears throughout the game's dimensional mechanics. The N109 Zone was named for this frequency, not for the 109th incursion as officially stated. The frequency corresponds to a resonant frequency of Earth's magnetic field.
