Weekly Events

Weekly Art Prompt (#LADSArtPrompt)
Every Monday, the community Discord posts a weekly art prompt — a theme, a quote, or a scenario related to Love and Deepspace. Artists have until Sunday to create and post their interpretation. No skill requirements, all styles welcome. Previous prompts: "Operative at Rest," "Favorite Memory Card," "Alternate Outfit Design," "Hunter and Operative Swap." Held on the official Discord and cross-posted to Twitter.
Fan Fiction Fridays (#LADS_FicRec)
Every Friday, community members recommend their favorite fan fictions on Twitter and Reddit. The weekly thread is pinned in the #fan-fiction channel on Discord. Authors can self-promote their works, and readers can discover new stories. All relationships and genres are welcome. A great way for new writers to get initial readership.
Screenshot Saturday (#LADS_ScreenCap)
Share your best Photo Studio screenshots every Saturday. The community votes on favorites, and top screenshots are featured in the following week's community newsletter. Themes rotate monthly — July 2026 theme is "Golden Hour," focusing on warm lighting compositions.

Monthly Events

Zine Submissions (Monthly Theme)
The Love and Deepspace Community Zine project accepts submissions monthly. Each month has a theme (e.g., "Seasons," "Elements," "Journeys"). Artists and writers can submit work for inclusion in the free digital zine published at the end of each quarter. Submissions are curated but all skill levels are encouraged to apply. The zine reaches 5,000+ readers per issue.
Community Co-Op Night (Last Saturday)
Voice chat co-op sessions on the community Discord. Players team up to tackle Abyss floors, boss rushes, and co-op content together. New players welcomed — veteran players provide guidance. No pressure, casual atmosphere. Headset recommended but not required.
Writing Exchange (Mid-Month)
Fan fiction writers exchange beta reading and feedback. Sign up through the Discord, get paired with a partner, exchange works-in-progress, and provide constructive feedback. Focus on character voice accuracy and narrative flow. A great way to improve your writing through peer review.

Special Events

Annual special events include: Anniversary Celebration (January — the community's biggest event with multi-week activities including art contests, writing challenges, and community awards), Valentine's Exchange (February — paired gift exchange where creators make art or write stories for their partners), Summer Festival (June-August — themed around beach and vacation concepts, with a community beach party on the Discord), Halloween Spooktacular (October — dark/horror-themed prompts), and Winter Wonderland (December — holiday-themed events and a charity fundraiser).

Host Your Own Event

The Love and Deepspace community encourages members to host their own events. To host: choose an event type (art challenge, writing prompt, co-op session, discussion panel), set a date and time (announce 2+ weeks in advance), promote through the community channels (Discord #events, Twitter using #LADSEvent), and moderate constructively. The community mod team can help promote your event to the wider community. Past member-hosted events include: "Zayne Reading Club" (weekly chapter discussions), "Rafayel Art Streams" (live drawing sessions), and "Sylus Theory Hour" (lore analysis voice chat sessions).

Community Resources

Key community hubs: Official Love and Deepspace Discord (the largest and most active community space), r/LoveAndDeepspace Reddit (structured discussions and weekly threads), @LaDS_Community Twitter (cross-platform event promotion), and the Community Wiki (guide for new members, event archives, and creator directory). All events listed in this calendar are fan-organized and not officially affiliated with Papergames or the game developers.

Tip: The most rewarding community experiences come from participating, not just lurking. Join a weekly event — even if you don't create, comment on others' work. The Love and Deepspace community is notably positive and supportive. A simple "I love your style!" comment can make a creator's day and build connections that last across multiple events. Community is built through participation, not observation.

Summary: The Love and Deepspace community events calendar runs parallel to official in-game events, offering weekly art prompts, writing exchanges, screenshot contests, and co-op game nights. Monthly events include zine submissions and writing exchanges. Annual special events like the Anniversary Celebration and Valentine's Exchange are the community's biggest moments. All skill levels are welcome at every event. Join the Discord, follow the event tags on Twitter, and participate — the community rewards engagement with connection and creative growth. The next major event is the Summer Festival running through August.
