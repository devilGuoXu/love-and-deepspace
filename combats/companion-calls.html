COMPANION GUIDE — CALLS

Voice Call & Message Guide

Operatives can call and message you outside of the Destiny Cafe. This guide covers how to unlock calls, trigger messages, and manage your communication log.

ENTRY_#COM_062026.07.02CALLS

Communication System Overview: The communication system simulates messaging and calling with operatives. Messages are text conversations with branching replies. Calls are fully voiced audio conversations. Both trigger based on in-game events, affinity milestones, and random intervals.

Message System

Messages appear in the in-game phone app. Operatives send messages based on: Affinity Milestones: Every 5 affinity levels (5, 10, 15, etc.) triggers a new message chain. These are one-time messages that advance the relationship narrative. Event Triggers: Completing a bounty, leveling a memory, or finishing a story chapter can trigger a message from the operative commenting on your achievement. Random Messages: Operatives send random messages approximately once every 3 days. These are short check-ins ("How are you?", "Thinking of you"). Replying maintains the communication streak. Message Replies: Each message has 2-3 reply options. Replies do not affect affinity directly but do affect the operative's next message tone. Positive replies lead to warmer future messages. Neutral replies maintain status quo. Negative or ignoring messages leads to cooler messages. Message threads are saved permanently and can be re-read from the phone app. Some messages include photo attachments — these are saved to your gallery automatically.

Voice Call System

Voice calls are the most immersive communication feature. Calls are triggered by: Initial Call: First call unlocks at Affinity 10. The operative introduces themselves and thanks you for your partnership. Milestone Calls: Affinity 25, 50, 75, 100, 125, and 150 each trigger a unique call. These calls are longer (2-5 minutes) and follow a narrative arc. Event Calls: Some limited events include exclusive voice calls. These are often tied to completing event story chapters. Random Calls: Operatives can call you at random intervals (approximately once per week). These are shorter check-ins. Random calls have a cooldown of 3 days between calls from the same operative. Call Features: Calls play in real-time with full voice acting. You can put the call on speaker or use headphones. Calls can be replayed from the call log at any time. During calls, you do not speak — the operative speaks to you, and you choose from text responses that the operative reacts to. Call Etiquette: If you miss a call, the operative leaves a voicemail. Voicemails are shorter than live calls but contain the same core content.

Communication Streak System

There is a hidden communication streak system. If you reply to messages and answer calls consistently, the operative's communication frequency increases. A 7-day streak of replying to all messages triggers a special message. A 30-day streak triggers a special voice call. Breaking the streak (ignoring 3 messages or missing 2 calls in a row) resets the streak and lowers communication frequency. Streaks are per-operative. Maintaining streaks with all four operatives requires daily phone checks. The streak system is not shown in-game — it is a hidden mechanic discovered by the community. The rewards are minor (exclusive messages and calls) but add to the immersion.

Communication Log Management

The phone app stores all message threads and call logs. You can: (1) Re-read any message thread. (2) Replay any voice call. (3) Delete messages (not recommended — they are permanent and have no storage limit). (4) Set a favorite message or call that appears on your profile. (5) Export calls as audio files (Android only). The communication log is sorted by operative and date. There is no limit to how many messages or calls can be stored. The log is an important resource for lore enthusiasts — many story details and character insights are hidden in message threads and calls. The writers use the communication system to deliver content that would not fit in the main story.

Maximizing Communication Content: To see all communication content: (1) Raise affinity actively — most messages and calls are affinity-gated. (2) Reply to every message within 24 hours to maintain the streak. (3) Answer calls whenever they come — missed calls give voicemails instead of full calls. (4) Complete events — event calls are limited-time and cannot be obtained after the event ends. (5) Level memories — memory leveling triggers congratulatory messages. (6) Read messages in order — the narrative flows chronologically. Missing a message chain can make later messages confusing. Communication content is some of the most personal and well-written in the game. Treat it as a core part of the experience, not a side feature.

Known Message & Call Counts

As of the current version: Xavier: 32 unique message threads, 14 voice calls. Zayne: 28 unique message threads, 12 voice calls. Rafayel: 30 unique message threads, 13 voice calls. Sylus: 26 unique message threads, 11 voice calls. Total: 116 messages and 50 calls across all operatives. This count increases with each update. New operatives may be added in the future with their own communication threads.

Summary: The communication system includes messages (text with branching replies) and voice calls (fully voiced audio conversations). Messages unlock every 5 affinity levels and from event triggers. Calls unlock at Affinity 10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 125, and 150. Random messages arrive every ~3 days, random calls every ~7 days. Replying to messages and answering calls maintains a hidden streak that increases communication frequency. All logs are permanently saved. Communication content is narrative-rich and essential for the full character experience.
