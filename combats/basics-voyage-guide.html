Deepspace Voyage Guide

Exploration mode — node types, reward priorities, stamina-free progression, and route optimization.

ENTRY_#0562026.07.01EXPLORATION

No Stamina Cost: Deepspace Voyage is one of the few systems that does not cost stamina. This means it's pure value — you should always complete your available voyages every day. The opportunity cost of skipping is zero, the reward value is significant.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Voyage Node Types

Three node types: Story Nodes (advance the exploration narrative, one-time clear), Resource Nodes (repeatable, give EXP books/gold/crystals), Event Nodes (random encounters with bonus rewards). Resource nodes cost Voyage Energy (separate from stamina, regenerates over time). Story nodes cost no energy. Event nodes cost energy but have higher reward value.

Priority: Clear all new Story Nodes first (permanent progression). Then spend Voyage Energy on Event Nodes (best reward-to-energy ratio). Then Resource Nodes. Never let Voyage Energy cap — it regenerates slowly and wasted regen is permanent loss.

Reward Analysis

Voyage rewards are roughly equivalent to 60 stamina worth of materials per day for an active player. Monthly total: ~1,800 stamina equivalent in free resources. The breakdown: 40% EXP books, 30% gold, 20% breakthrough crystals, 10% miscellaneous (furniture, gifts). This makes Voyage the second-largest source of EXP books after event shops.

Efficiency Tips

Use the Auto-Voyage feature for resource nodes you've already cleared (saves 1-2 minutes daily). Prioritize crystal nodes when you're close to a breakthrough tier. Save event node completions for daily missions — completing 3 nodes counts toward your daily activity. The voyage pass (if available) doubles rewards for 30 days — it's the best value purchase in the game after monthly pass.

Voyage Energy Management

Voyage Energy regenerates at a rate of 1 unit per 6 minutes, giving you approximately 240 units per day. The maximum capacity is 200 units, meaning you should log in at least once every 20 hours to avoid wasted regeneration. The most efficient use of Voyage Energy is Event Nodes, which cost 30 energy and give approximately 50% more rewards per energy than Resource Nodes at 20 energy. If no Event Nodes are available, run Resource Nodes that drop the material type you are currently bottlenecked on.

Voyage Energy regeneration pauses while you are in combat or in menus. This is a minor effect but can add up over a long session. To maximize daily energy gain, complete your Voyage runs first thing when logging in, before entering any combat content. If you have the voyage pass active, the regeneration rate increases to 1 unit per 4 minutes, giving you approximately 360 units per day. The voyage pass is the only way to exceed the 200-unit cap — with the pass, the hard cap becomes irrelevant because you will always have energy to spend.

Event Node Rotations and Bonuses

Event Nodes follow a daily rotation pattern. Monday and Thursday feature crystal nodes with +50% drop rate. Tuesday and Friday feature gold nodes with +50% drop rate. Wednesday and Saturday feature EXP book nodes with +50% drop rate. Sunday features a random bonus on all nodes. Align your Voyage energy spending with these bonus days to maximize your material gain. If you need gold specifically, save your Voyage Energy reserves for Tuesday and Friday.

The bonus events are not always available — they appear randomly alongside standard Event Nodes. If a bonus day matches a material type you need, spend all your Voyage Energy on that bonus event before it rotates. The bonus events persist for 24 hours from the start of the daily reset. Set a reminder if you play at inconsistent times to ensure you do not miss the bonus window. Missing two consecutive bonus events costs approximately 60 stamina worth of materials in opportunity cost.

Exploring all story nodes in a voyage region rewards 100 gems. Clearing all resource nodes rewards 50 gems. Completing the entire map rewards a unique name card. Total one-time rewards: approximately 500 gems.

Key Insight: Voyage is the most overlooked free resource system. Most players ignore it after the initial story clear. The daily resource income from Voyage adds up to roughly 25% of your total weekly material gain. Don't skip it.

Summary: Free stamina-less system. Story nodes first, event nodes second, resource nodes third. Don't cap Voyage Energy. 1,800 stamina equivalent per month. Auto-Voyage for speed.
