Elemental Wanderers

Fire, ice, and lightning — each elemental variant demands a different approach. Know your elements, or get wiped.

ENTRY_#WDR_022026.07.02ELEMENTAL

Core Identity: Elemental Wanderers are upgraded Void Spawn with one major difference — they deal elemental damage and have elemental shields. Match the counter-element to break them, or face 50% reduced damage. Unlike Void Spawn, these enemies require actual gear checks.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Fire Variant — Ember Weaver

Appearance: Humanoid with glowing orange cracks across its body. Heat distortion around its hands. HP: Medium. Attack Pattern: Three attacks: (1) Fireball — throws a slow orb that explodes in a 2-unit radius. Telegraph: gathers flame in palm for 1s. (2) Flame Dash — becomes a fireball and charges forward. Telegraph: crouches, flames engulf body. (3) AoE Eruption — slams ground, creating a 3-unit fire ring. Only at below 40% HP.

Elemental Shield: Fire (red). Use Ice to break. Weakness: Ice (1.5x), Water if applicable. Resists: Fire (0.5x). Parry Window: Fireball — parry the projectile back at it (timing: when orb is 1 unit away). Flame Dash — parry at the start of the charge (0.2s window, tight). Strategy: Zayne is the hard counter here. His Ice attacks break the shield in 2 hits. After shield break, Ember Weaver is stunned for 3s — use Ardent Oath for maximum damage. Dodge the AoE ring by moving inside the gap (the ring has a small opening on one side).

Ice Variant — Frost Weaver

Appearance: Pale blue-white body with frost armor. Mist trails from its limbs. HP: Medium-High. Attack Pattern: Three attacks: (1) Ice Shard — fires a fast, linear projectile. Telegraph: arm extends, frost gathers at fingertips (0.4s). (2) Freeze Ground — creates a 2.5-unit ice circle on the ground that slows and damages. Telegraph: stomps foot, ice spreads outward. (3) Icicle Rain — summons icicles from above in a 3x3 grid. Only at below 50% HP.

Elemental Shield: Ice (blue). Use Fire to break. Weakness: Fire (1.5x). Resists: Ice (0.5x). Parry Window: Ice Shard — parry is not recommended (projectile too fast). Dodge sideways. Freeze Ground — jump or dodge at the moment of the stomp. Strategy: Rafayel (Inferno Blaze) is your best choice. Burn DoT deals double damage against Frost Weaver. The Freeze Ground attack has a 0.5s cast time — watch the foot stomp animation. Once the ice circle appears, you cannot stand in it. Fight on the move. When Icicle Rain starts, run to the edge of the arena — the grid pattern leaves the corners safe.

Lightning Variant — Storm Weaver

Appearance: Crackling yellow energy, floating slightly above ground. HP: Medium. Attack Pattern: Two fast attacks: (1) Lightning Bolt — instant cast, hits the player's current position with a 1.5-unit radius. No telegraph (this is the dangerous one). (2) Chain Lightning — fires a bolt that chains to nearby targets (including other wanderers). Telegraph: arms spread wide, 0.8s charge. (3) Teleport Strike — disappears and reappears behind the player with a melee hit. Only at below 30% HP.

Elemental Shield: Lightning (yellow). Use any element but no multiplier (shield has no specific weakness). Weakness: None (neutral to all). Resists: None. Parry Window: Chain Lightning — you can parry the initial bolt to negate the chain. Timing: when the bolt leaves its hands. Teleport Strike — perfect dodge the moment it appears (0.2s reaction window). Strategy: Storm Weaver is the most dangerous elemental variant because Lightning Bolt has no telegraph. You must keep moving at all times — never stop. Stay close (melee range reduces its Lightning Bolt accuracy by making it overshoot). Use Sylus for his execute passive — Storm Weaver's low HP phase (Teleport Strike spam) is chaotic, so finishing it below 30% is safer.

Pro Tip: When fighting mixed elemental groups (e.g., Ember + Frost together), always kill the Storm Weaver first — its chain lightning buffs the other wanderers. Then Frost Weaver (it has the highest HP). Ember Weaver last. Never leave a Storm Weaver alive while focusing another target.

Elemental Shield Break Mechanics

Each elemental shield has 3 "break points." Each matching-element hit removes 1 point. After all 3 points are removed, the shield breaks and the wanderer is stunned for 3s. Non-matching elements remove only 0.5 points per hit. Resonance skills remove 2 points. Ardent Oath removes all 3 instantly regardless of element. Practical takeaway: If you don't have the matching element, use Ardent Oath to bypass the shield mechanic entirely.

Recommended Operatives by Variant

Ember Weaver → Zayne (Frostguard): Ice shield break in 2 hits, freeze CC, sustained damage. Frost Weaver → Rafayel (Inferno Blaze): Fire shield break, burn DoT (double damage). Storm Weaver → Sylus (Dark Champion): No elemental advantage needed, execute passive makes the chaotic low-HP phase safe. Mixed groups → Xavier (Light Seeker): Light element has no weakness to any element — Xavier works against all three, though with slightly lower shield break efficiency.

Summary: Ember → Ice (Zayne). Frost → Fire (Rafayel). Storm → any (Sylus execute). Shield = 3 hits with matching element, or 1 Ardent Oath. Storm Weaver is the priority target in mixed groups. Keep moving against Lightning Bolt — never stand still.
