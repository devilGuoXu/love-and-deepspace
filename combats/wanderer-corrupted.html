WANDERER ENCYCLOPEDIA — CORRUPTED

Corrupted Operatives

Phantom mirrors of Xavier, Sylus, Zayne, and Rafayel — these wanderers use your own skills against you. Know their copied moves to turn the tables.

ENTRY_#WDR_032026.07.02CORRUPTED

Core Identity: Corrupted Operatives are created from the Deepspace's memory of hunters. Each one copies a specific operative's skill set with a dark twist. They are mini-boss enemies — expect higher HP, unique mechanics, and dangerous phase transitions. Unlike standard wanderers, each Corrupted Operative is a check of your ability to counter your own character's moves.

Phantom Xavier — Light Seeker Corrupted

Appearance: Xavier's silhouette, but wreathed in purple-black energy. Eyes glow white. HP: High. Difficulty: 3/5. Attack Pattern: (1) Light Slash — quick horizontal slash, 0.3s telegraph (arm pulls back). Hits in a 120-degree arc. (2) Teleport Strike — vanishes and reappears behind you, same as the player's skill. Telegraph: shimmer in the air 0.5s before appearance. (3) Light Burst — charges for 1.5s, then fires a wide beam. Dodge perpendicular. (4) Phase 2 (below 40% HP): Summons 2 Phantom Slashers. Gains Attack Speed buff for 10s.

Weakness: Dark (Sylus). Resists: Light (0.5x, ironic). Parry Window: Light Slash — parry at the start of the swing (0.2s). Teleport Strike — parry the moment the shimmer appears behind you. Strategy: Sylus is the best counter because Phantom Xavier resists Light. Stay mobile — his Teleport Strike is designed to punish players who stand still. When he enters Phase 2, use your Ardent Oath immediately to clear the summoned Slashers. After the Slashers are dead, focus Xavier. His enrage (Attack Speed buff) lasts exactly 10s — you can kite him during this time.

Phantom Sylus — Dark Champion Corrupted

Appearance: Sylus's form, shadowy with red energy veins. HP: Very High. Difficulty: 4/5. Attack Pattern: (1) Dark Slash — heavy overhead strike, 0.8s telegraph (raises arm high). Deals 2x damage if it hits. (2) Execute — lunges forward when you are below 50% HP. Unavoidable unless you perfect dodge. Telegraph: red flash in his eyes (0.2s). (3) Shadow Pool — creates a 4-unit dark circle on the ground that deals DoT and slows by 30%. Lasts 8s. (4) Phase 2 (below 30% HP): Gains life steal on all attacks (15% of damage dealt heals him).

Weakness: Light (Xavier). Resists: Dark (0.5x). Parry Window: Dark Slash — parry just before the strike lands (0.3s window, generous). Execute — perfect dodge only, cannot be parried. Time the dodge when his eyes flash red. Strategy: Xavier's Light attacks deal 1.5x damage. The biggest threat is Execute below 50% HP — keep your HP above 60% using Zayne's shields or healing items. Stay out of Shadow Pool at all costs (the slow is lethal when combined with his Phase 2 life steal). In Phase 2, burst him down fast — the longer the fight goes, the more he heals. Save Ardent Oath for the Phase 2 transition.

Phantom Zayne — Frostguard Corrupted

Appearance: Zayne's shape, encased in jagged ice armor. Frost aura. HP: High. Difficulty: 3/5. Attack Pattern: (1) Ice Shard Volley — fires 5 ice shards in a spread pattern. Telegraph: raises both hands, 0.8s charge. (2) Freeze Armor — gains a temporary Ice shield (3 hits) that reflects 20% damage back. Lasts 10s. (3) Frost Nova — AoE freeze in a 3-unit radius. Only if you stay in melee range for 5+ seconds. (4) Phase 2 (below 35% HP): Continuous Frost Nova pulses every 4s.

Weakness: Fire (Rafayel). Resists: Ice (0.5x). Parry Window: Ice Shard Volley — you can parry one shard back at him. Best to dodge sideways instead. Frost Nova — cannot be parried, MUST be dodged (0.5s telegraph: ground frost). Strategy: Rafayel's burn DoT is critical here — it deals damage through Freeze Armor without triggering the reflect. When Freeze Armor is up, STOP attacking and reposition. Frost Nova punishes melee players who over-commit. In Phase 2, maintain distance — the continuous Frost Nova makes melee nearly impossible. Use ranged attacks and let your burn DoT work.

Phantom Rafayel — Inferno Blaze Corrupted

Appearance: Rafayel's outline, wreathed in blue-white flames. HP: Medium-High. Difficulty: 4/5. Attack Pattern: (1) Flame Wave — sends a 5-unit-wide wall of fire forward. Telegraph: sweeps arm horizontally, 0.6s charge. (2) Burning Ground — sets the floor on fire for 6s in a 3-unit circle. Can have 2 active simultaneously. (3) Explosive Teleport — disappears and reappears at your location with an explosion (3-unit radius). Telegraph: laughter sound cue + vanishing animation (0.4s). (4) Phase 2 (below 50% HP): Gains 2 extra teleport charges — teleports 3 times in quick succession.

Weakness: Ice (Zayne). Resists: Fire (0.5x). Parry Window: Flame Wave — parry the fire wall at the moment it reaches you (counts as a projectile parry). Explosive Teleport — cannot be parried, perfect dodge required. Audio cue is your best warning. Strategy: Zayne's Frostguard set freezes Phantom Rafayel, interrupting his Flame Wave spam. The arena will fill with Burning Ground quickly — you must fight on the move. When he Explosive Teleports, dodge IMMEDIATELY after the laughter sound. Phase 2 is extremely dangerous — triple teleport means triple explosions. Save your dodge energy and never dodge twice in a row unless absolutely necessary. Prioritize positioning over damage in Phase 2.

Pro Tip: Corrupted Operatives share a universal mechanic: they all have a "mirror weakness" — each is weak to the element that counters their original operative's main element. Xavier (Light) → weak to Dark (Sylus). Sylus (Dark) → weak to Light (Xavier). Zayne (Ice) → weak to Fire (Rafayel). Rafayel (Fire) → weak to Ice (Zayne). It's a perfect element cycle — bring the right counter and you've already won half the fight.

Difficulty Ranking & Rewards

Easiest → Hardest: Phantom Zayne (predictable, avoidable mechanics) → Phantom Xavier (Phase 2 adds are dangerous) → Phantom Rafayel (Burning Ground restricts movement) → Phantom Sylus (Execute + life steal is a brutal combo). Reward Table: All drop Memory XP Crystals (random), Protocore Fragments (guaranteed 2), and Memory Shards (low chance, 15%). Phantom Sylus has a 5% chance to drop a SSR Protocore. Phantom Xavier drops 1 extra Memory Shard. Farm Xavier for shards, farm Sylus for protocores.

Summary: Phantoms are mirror matches — bring the counter-element. Xavier → Sylus (Dark). Sylus → Xavier (Light). Zayne → Rafayel (Fire). Rafayel → Zayne (Ice). Each has a dangerous Phase 2 gimmick. Respect their mechanics and they are manageable. Ignore them and they will kill you faster than any boss.
