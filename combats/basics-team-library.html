Team Composition Library

Every team archetype — how to build, when to use, and which operatives to slot in.

ENTRY_#0492026.07.01TEAM_BUILDING

Archetypes, Not Cookie-Cutters: These are team frameworks, not rigid compositions. Learn the archetype, then adapt it to your roster. The best team is the one that fits the operative you have built, not the one the tier list says is optimal.

Hypercarry Team

Core: 1 main DPS + 2 supports. When to use: Single-boss fights, DPS checks, Abyss floors with single elite/boss. How it works: The main DPS deals 80% of team damage. Both supports exist to buff, shield, and enable the main DPS. Supports generate energy and swap out immediately — they should not stay on field longer than 2 seconds.

Example: Xavier (Light Seeker, main DPS) + Zayne (Frostguard, shield + HP buff) + third operative (any, pure energy battery). Rotation: Support → Resonance (generate energy) → Swap to Zayne → Shield → Swap to Xavier → Burst window. Supports never use auto-attacks — only resonance and swap.

Pros: Highest single-target DPS potential. Easy to gear (only one operative needs top-tier gear). Cons: Fragile — if main DPS dies, run is over. Weak against multi-target floors.

Dual-DPS Team

Core: 2 DPS + 1 flex. When to use: Multi-target floors, double elite encounters, Abyss floors 9-10. How it works: Both DPS deal roughly equal damage. The flex slot can be support, sustain, or a third DPS depending on the floor. The key is element coverage — two DPS of different elements handle most shield rotations.

Example: Sylus (Dark, single-target) + Rafayel (Fire, AoE) + Zayne (hybrid sustain). Sylus handles the boss, Rafayel clears adds, Zayne keeps everyone alive. Sylus and Rafayel alternate field time based on the current threat.

Pros: Flexible, covers multiple elements, good for add-heavy floors. Cons: Gear is split between two DPS. Lower peak DPS than hypercarry.

Sustain Team

Core: 1 tank/healer + 2 DPS. When to use: DoT floors (Abyss 8), survival checks, content where you can't avoid damage. How it works: Zayne (Frostguard) is the anchor — his shields and healing out-sustain any DoT. The two DPS focus on dealing damage while Zayne rotates in to refresh shields.

Pros: Unkillable. Best for learning new content. Cons: Lower DPS. Slow clear times. Only useful when survivability is the bottleneck.

Budget Team (F2P)

Core: 1 SSR DPS + 2 SR supports. When to use: Early-mid game, Abyss floors 1-7. How it works: Your single SSR carries the damage. SR supports provide utility (shields, energy, debuffs) but deal negligible damage. Don't invest resources in SR supports past level 40 — they're temporary.

Example SSR DPS + SR Rapid Fire (Xavier sub-DPS) + SR Energy Burst (universal battery). The SSR does 70%+ of damage. Replace SR supports with your second SSR as soon as possible.

Pro Tip: Most players should default to Hypercarry for single-target content and Dual-DPS for multi-target. Sustain is a tool for specific walls, not a daily driver. Budget is for early game only — transition out as soon as you have a second SSR built.

Summary: Hypercarry (single target) > Dual-DPS (multi-target) > Sustain (survival) > Budget (early game). Know which archetype fits the content, then slot in your best operatives of the required elements.
