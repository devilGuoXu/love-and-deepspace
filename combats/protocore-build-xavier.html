Xavier: Light Hunter

CRIT-stacking burst DPS. Max damage windows, delete enemies.

ENTRY_#0842026.06.29CORE_SYSTEM

Core Identity: Xavier is a burst-window DPS. He excels at short, devastating engagements. Build for max CRIT and CRIT DMG. Energy Recharge is secondary — he generates energy naturally through his combos.

Best Sets

Lightning Assault (4-pc)
+30% CRIT DMG on Resonance skill. Xavier's best set — aligns with his burst playstyle.
Use for: Abyss, Boss fights, N109
Solar Flare (2-pc + 2-pc ATK)
+18% ATK + +15% Light DMG. More consistent damage than Lightning Assault.
Use for: Long fights, Sustain comps

Stat Targets: CRIT Rate 70%+ | CRIT DMG 160%+ | ATK 2,800+ | Energy Recharge 110%

Key Insight: Xavier's Resonance skill has a built-in CRIT rate boost. At max stacks, you gain 15% CRIT rate for 4 seconds. This means you can build slightly less CRIT rate (target 65%) and invest more into CRIT DMG. The breakpoint is 65% self-buffed to 80%.

Summary: CRIT > CRIT DMG > ATK%. Xavier lives and dies by his burst windows. Lightning Assault 4-pc is optimal. Target 65% CRIT rate (80% with self-buff) and as much CRIT DMG as possible.
