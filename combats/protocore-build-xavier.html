Burst Rotation and Energy Management

Xavier's optimal damage rotation centers around his Resonance skill, which gains stacks from landing basic attacks. Each basic attack hit generates one stack, up to a maximum of five stacks. At five stacks, the next Resonance skill deals 40% additional damage and gains the 15% CRIT rate bonus. The optimal opener is: enter combat with three basic attacks, use Resonance at five stacks, use one basic attack chain, then use Ardent Oath. This sequence delivers maximum burst damage within a 6-second window that aligns with most boss vulnerability phases.

Energy regeneration for Xavier is naturally higher than other operatives because his light attacks are multi-hit. Each hit generates a small amount of energy, and Xavier's basic attack chain has the highest hit count per second of any operative. This means Xavier builds Ardent Oath charge faster in sustained combat than any other operative — approximately 20% faster than Sylus and 30% faster than Zayne. Your rotation should prioritize staying in combat rather than kiting, because downtime directly translates to slower energy generation. Use perfect dodges to maintain uptime rather than disengaging.

Set Choice by Content Type

The choice between Lightning Assault 4-piece and Solar Flare 2+2 depends on the content you are running. Lightning Assault is superior for Abyss floors and boss fights where the fight duration is under 90 seconds. The burst damage from the CRIT DMG bonus on Resonance allows you to skip mechanics by pushing HP thresholds faster. For longer encounters like the N109 Zone expeditions or survival modes, Solar Flare 2+2 provides more consistent damage because the ATK and Light DMG bonuses apply to all attacks rather than just Resonance.

If you have both sets fully built, switch between them based on the specific floor you are attempting. Lightning Assault for Floors 7-10 where clear time matters most for star ratings. Solar Flare for Floors 11-12 and boss rushes where sustained damage is more valuable. A third option — the 4-piece standard ATK set — is a budget alternative that performs adequately until you complete either of the two recommended sets. Never run a 2-piece set without a complementary 2-piece bonus; the 4-piece bonuses are too valuable to sacrifice.

Xavier needs 5 basic attacks within 4 seconds for maximum Resonance stacks. If losing stacks to boss untargetability, use 3-stack rotation instead. A 3-stack Resonance deals 80% of 5-stack damage but is more consistent.

Key Insight: Xavier's Resonance skill has a built-in CRIT rate boost. At max stacks, you gain 15% CRIT rate for 4 seconds. This means you can build slightly less CRIT rate (target 65%) and invest more into CRIT DMG. The breakpoint is 65% self-buffed to 80%.

Summary: CRIT > CRIT DMG > ATK%. Xavier lives and dies by his burst windows. Lightning Assault 4-pc is optimal. Target 65% CRIT rate (80% with self-buff) and as much CRIT DMG as possible.
