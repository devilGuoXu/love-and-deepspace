Follower Milestones

Love and Deepspace's official social media accounts (primarily Twitter/X) release redemption codes when they hit follower milestones. These codes typically award 150-300 gems plus upgrade materials. The pattern has been consistent: every 100K followers on the Global Twitter account triggers a milestone code. The account crossed 600K followers in mid-2026 and continues to grow. Each milestone code is valid for 30-60 days, giving players ample time to redeem.

Notable past milestones: 100K (Mar 2024), 200K (Jul 2024), 300K (Dec 2024), 400K (Apr 2025), 500K (Aug 2025), 600K (Mar 2026). The next milestone (700K) is expected within 2-3 months based on current growth rates. Each milestone code has increased in value — the 600K code (DEEPSPACE630K) awarded 200 gems plus 10 SSR shards, the highest non-anniversary code value to date.

Share Rewards

The in-game share system rewards players for sharing content to social media. Daily share: Share a memory card or photo to any platform — reward is 20 gems per day (one-time reward, resets daily). Event shares: During limited events, sharing event pages awards additional event currency or stamina pots. Memory collection share: When you obtain a new SSR, sharing it to social media awards 50 gems (one-time per SSR).

The daily share reward alone adds up to 600 gems per month — a significant income source that requires only 10 seconds per day. Simply share a random memory card to a platform (you can share to a private account or even cancel the share after the reward is claimed — the game awards the gems on share initiation, not completion).

Community Contests

The official social channels host regular community contests with in-game rewards. Contest types include: Fan Art Contests (submit memory card art, winners receive gems and exclusive titles — typically quarterly), Screenshot/Photo Contests (best Photo Studio compositions, winners get gems and premium currency), Lore Writing Contests (fan fiction and character analysis pieces), and Trivia/Quiz Events (live on Discord, winners get instant code rewards).

Participation rewards are common even for non-winners — many contests grant a participation code to everyone who submits an entry. Follow the official accounts and join the Discord server for contest announcements. The art contests tend to have the largest prize pools (500-1,000 gems for first place).

Each platform has a different reward focus. Twitter/X is the primary code distribution channel — all milestone codes and most livestream codes are posted here first. Discord hosts community events, trivia contests, and developer Q&A sessions with code drops. YouTube is used for developer livestreams where codes are announced verbally and in the chat. Instagram focuses on visual content (art, photos) and contest announcements.

Tracking Sheet

To maximize social rewards, maintain a simple tracking sheet: follow all four official platforms, enable push notifications for the Twitter/X account (critical for 24-48 hour codes), join the Discord and set notifications for the #announcements channel only (to avoid noise), check YouTube for livestream schedules weekly, and set a daily reminder for the in-game share reward.

Summary: Social media rewards provide a steady stream of free gems and resources. Follow the official Twitter/X for milestone codes (every 100K followers, 150-300 gems), do your daily in-game share for 20 gems/day (600 gems/month), participate in community contests for prize opportunities, and watch developer livestreams for time-sensitive codes.
