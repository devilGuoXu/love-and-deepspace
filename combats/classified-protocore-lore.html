Aether Core & Protocore Lore

The Nature of the Aether Core

The Aether Core defies conventional classification. It is neither fully physical nor fully energy-based, existing in a state that our researchers describe as "suspended between dimensions." The Core emits a constant low-frequency energy signature that fluctuates in response to nearby Evol activity, suggesting a direct connection to the fundamental forces that govern reality in the Love and Deepspace world.

Physical Properties. When observed directly, the Aether Core appears as a crystalline structure approximately 30cm in diameter, emitting a soft luminescence that cycles through colors corresponding to the known Evol types. Its surface temperature remains constant at 22.3°C regardless of environmental conditions. Attempts to sample its material have failed — instruments that make contact with the Core register as reading themselves, suggesting the Core reflects or duplicates whatever interacts with it.

Energy Signature. The Core's energy output fluctuates in a pattern that some researchers have compared to a heartbeat. This has led to the controversial theory that the Core is not merely a power source but a life form — or at least something that mimics life. The "heartbeat" pattern accelerated during documented Wanderer incursions and slowed during periods of relative peace, as though the Core responds to the state of the world.

Dimensional Anchoring. Our most significant finding: the Aether Core appears to anchor the dimensional boundary between our reality and the Wanderer dimension. When the Core is stable, the boundary is stable. When the Core is disturbed — through excessive Protocore extraction or deliberate dimensional manipulation — the boundary weakens, and Wanderer incursions increase. This correlation has been documented across 17 major incursion events.

Origin Theories

The origin of the Aether Core remains the subject of intense debate among researchers. Three primary theories have emerged, each with supporting evidence and significant gaps.

The Natural Formation Theory. The Aether Core is a natural phenomenon — a crystalized point where dimensional energy concentrated over millennia. This theory is supported by the Core's geological-like structure and its apparent age (carbon-dating of surrounding materials suggests the Core is at least 10,000 years old). However, natural formation does not explain the Core's apparent awareness or its responsive behavior.

The Artifact Theory. The Aether Core was created by a lost civilization — possibly the civilization of Philos — as a tool for dimensional manipulation. This theory is supported by the Core's structured internal geometry (it has a crystalline lattice that resembles advanced circuitry) and by recovered texts from Philos that describe a "heart of the world" that matches the Core's description. If this theory is correct, the Core is technology, not geology.

The Entity Theory. The Aether Core is a trapped entity — a being of pure energy that was imprisoned in physical form. This theory is the most controversial but explains several anomalies that other theories cannot. The Core's "heartbeat," its apparent responsiveness to Evol users, and its preference for certain individuals (particularly the Hunter) all suggest an entity with awareness and will. Who trapped it, and why, remains unknown.

Our Assessment. Based on available evidence, the Artifact Theory is most consistent with observed data. The Entity Theory explains the Core's behavior but lacks direct evidence. The Natural Formation Theory fails to explain too many anomalies. We classify the Aether Core as "likely artificial in origin, possesses properties consistent with awareness, and should be treated with extreme caution."

Protocores: Fragments of Power

Protocores are the most tangible evidence of the Aether Core's influence on the world. These crystalline fragments are found throughout the Deepspace and in the remains of defeated Wanderers, and they are the primary resource used to enhance Evol abilities.

Formation and Distribution. Protocores form spontaneously in areas of high dimensional energy concentration. The Deepspace rift is the largest known source, producing Protocores at a rate that correlates directly with the Core's energy fluctuations. Wanderers also produce Protocores — when a Wanderer is defeated, its body dissolves, often leaving a Protocore behind. This has led to the theory that Protocores are the "seeds" of Wanderer existence — that a Wanderer forms around a Protocore rather than a Protocore forming within a Wanderer.

Protocore Types and Ranks. Protocores are categorized by type (ATK, HP, DEF, CRIT, etc.) and rank (from common to SSR). Higher-rank Protocores have more complex internal structures and stronger energy signatures. The classification system used by the Hunter Corps is functional but incomplete — there are known Protocore variants that do not fit the standard categories and are classified separately as "anomalous."

Anomalous Protocores. Certain Protocores exhibit properties that cannot be explained by current theories. These anomalous cores may change their type classification over time, emit energy signatures that do not match any known Evol, or — in rare documented cases — appear to communicate with specific individuals. The most famous anomalous Protocore is associated with the events leading to the formation of the N109 Zone.

Protocore Degradation. Protocores are not permanent. They degrade over time when removed from high-energy environments, losing their potency and eventually crumbling into inert dust. This degradation process is accelerated by use — a Protocore equipped in a combat setting will degrade faster than one stored in a stabilized environment. The half-life of a Protocore depends on its rank and type, ranging from weeks (common) to years (SSR).

The Aether Core-Evol Connection

The relationship between the Aether Core and Evol abilities is the most studied and least understood aspect of the Core's influence. Every Evol user is connected to the Core whether they know it or not.

Evol Awakening. The appearance of the Deepspace rift did not create Evol abilities — historical records from Philos describe Evol-like powers existing long before the rift. However, the rift dramatically accelerated the frequency of Evol awakenings. Before the rift, Evol users were rare and their abilities weak. After the rift, awakenings became common, and powers became significantly stronger. The Aether Core appears to amplify the conditions necessary for Evol awakening.

Evol Type Distribution. Analysis of Evol types across the population reveals a correlation with geographical proximity to the Aether Core. Individuals who awakened near the Core (within the N109 Zone) developed different Evol types on average than those who awakened distant from it. This suggests the Core influences not only whether awakening occurs but what form the Evol takes.

Evol Amplification via Protocores. The system of equipping Protocores to enhance Evol abilities works because Protocores are fragments of Aether Core energy. When a user equips a Protocore, they are essentially connecting themselves to a tiny piece of the Core, drawing additional power from the same source. This explains why Protocore enhancement is universal — any Evol user can benefit from any Protocore type, because all energy ultimately comes from the same source.

The Limitation. There appears to be a ceiling on how much Aether Core energy a human can channel. Beyond a certain threshold, the body cannot contain the energy and begins to break down. This is theorized to be the cause of "Evol burnout" — a condition where Evol users lose their abilities after pushing themselves too far. The Hunter Corps has lost several promising operatives to Evol burnout, often in cases where they were using high-rank Protocores beyond their natural capacity.

Protocores and Wanderer Existence

The connection between Protocores and Wanderers may be more fundamental than previously understood. Emerging research suggests that Wanderers are not creatures that happen to contain Protocores — they are Protocores that have manifested physical forms.

The Manifestation Theory. According to this theory, a Protocore is the "seed" of a Wanderer. When a Protocore is exposed to sufficient dimensional energy — such as within the Deepspace rift — it begins to accumulate matter around itself, forming a body. The type of Wanderer that emerges depends on the Protocore's type and rank, as well as environmental factors. This theory explains why defeating a Wanderer leaves a Protocore behind: it is the core of the creature's existence, and destroying the body merely returns the Protocore to its unmanifested state.

Implications. If the Manifestation Theory is correct, then every time a Protocore is used for enhancement, it carries the potential to become a Wanderer under the right conditions. This raises disturbing questions about the widespread use of Protocores in combat enhancement and equipment manufacturing. Is the Hunter Corps inadvertently seeding the next generation of Wanderers?

Counter-Evidence. Critics of the Manifestation Theory point out that Protocores used in equipment have never been observed to spontaneously manifest as Wanderers. The conditions required for manifestation appear to be specific — high dimensional energy concentration, proximity to the Deepspace rift, and possibly an unknown catalyst. However, the possibility remains that the catalyst is simply time and that sustained Protocore use will eventually lead to manifestation incidents.

The Hunter's Unique Connection

The current Hunter — the protagonist of the story — has a demonstrated connection to the Aether Core that exceeds normal parameters. This connection is the subject of intense study and classified speculation.

Elevated Resonance. The Hunter's energy signature resonates with the Aether Core at significantly higher amplitude than any other recorded individual. Standard resonance among Evol users rates between 1 and 30 on the resonance scale. The Hunter rates consistently above 90, with spikes recorded at 150+ during critical moments. This level of resonance has only been observed in two other historical records, both associated with major dimensional events.

The Core's Response. The Aether Core responds to the Hunter's presence in documented ways not observed with other individuals. The Core's energy output increases by an average of 15% when the Hunter is within 50 meters. Its "heartbeat" pattern synchronizes with the Hunter's pulse. In one documented event, the Core emitted a specific frequency pattern that matched the Hunter's Evol signature precisely — an occurrence that researchers describe as "impossible under current theoretical models."

Theories. Current theories for the Hunter's unique connection include: the Hunter is a descendant of the civilization that created the Aether Core (Philos lineage theory), the Hunter is the reincarnation of an entity that was imprisoned within the Core (reincarnation theory), the Hunter and the Core are fragments of the same original being (fragmentation theory), or the Hunter's connection is not unique but simply the first to be properly measured (measurement bias theory). The Hunter Corps has not authorized direct research on the Hunter to determine which theory is correct.

Onychinus and the Core

Onychinus, the criminal organization that controls the N109 Zone, has a relationship with the Aether Core that is both more direct and more secretive than the Hunter Corps. Sylus, the leader of Onychinus, has made the Core the foundation of his power.

Onychinus Research. Onychinus maintains classified research facilities within the N109 Zone dedicated to Aether Core and Protocore study. Their research is believed to be more advanced than Hunter Corps research, operating without the ethical restrictions that constrain official research. Unconfirmed reports suggest Onychinus has successfully created artificial Protocores and has attempted — with unknown success — to communicate directly with the Aether Core.

The Core as Territory. Sylus's control of the N109 Zone is not merely political. The N109 Zone sits atop the largest known concentration of Protocore deposits outside the Deepspace rift itself. Control of the zone means control of Protocore extraction, refinement, and distribution. This economic foundation gives Onychinus power that rivals national governments.

Sylus's Personal Connection. Sylus himself has a documented energy resonance with the Aether Core that is second only to the Hunter's. His resonance reading of 78 is significantly higher than any other known individual. This may explain his interest in the Hunter — not romantic or political interest, but scientific interest in the only person whose connection to the Core exceeds his own.

Dangers and Ethical Concerns

The widespread use of Protocores and the ongoing research into the Aether Core raise significant dangers and ethical concerns that have not been adequately addressed by the Hunter Corps or any governing body.

Protocore Dependence. The Hunter Corps and the global combat infrastructure have become dependent on Protocore technology. If the Aether Core were to be damaged, destroyed, or removed, the entire Protocore supply chain would collapse, rendering most combat equipment useless and leaving humanity defenseless against Wanderer incursions. This single point of failure represents an unacceptable strategic vulnerability.

Ethical Extraction. If the Manifestation Theory is correct, then Protocore extraction is not mining but harvesting — the collection of potential life forms for use as tools. This raises ethical questions that the Hunter Corps has shown no interest in addressing. Is it moral to use Protocores if they are potential Wanderers? Is it moral to destroy Wanderers if they are merely manifested Protocores seeking existence?

Core Exploitation. The Aether Core itself may be subject to exploitation if its nature is better understood. If the Core is an entity, then attempts to control or extract more power from it constitute a form of enslavement. If the Core is artificial, then it may have limits that, when exceeded, could cause catastrophic failure. Either scenario requires careful consideration that current research prioritizes speed over caution.

Recommendation. Further research into the Aether Core and Protocore system is necessary but must be conducted with ethical guidelines. Classification of Core research should not be used to hide uncomfortable findings. The connection between Protocore use and future Wanderer manifestation should be a priority research area. And the Hunter should be informed of their unique connection to the Core — they deserve to know what they are.

Classified Observation: The Aether Core's "heartbeat" pattern has been decelerating steadily over the past three months. If this trend continues, the Core will reach zero activity — complete energy cessation — within eighteen months. The implications of a silent Core are unknown. No precedent exists.

Summary: The Aether Core is the foundation of the power system in Love and Deepspace — likely an artificial construct from the lost civilization of Philos, with properties that suggest awareness. Protocores are its fragments, scattered across dimensions, that form the basis of Protocore enhancement technology. The Core's connection to Evol abilities is fundamental: it amplifies awakening and powers enhancement. The relationship between Protocores and Wanderers may be more direct than understood — Wanderers may be manifested Protocores rather than creatures that contain Protocores. The Hunter has a unique connection to the Core that exceeds all known parameters. Onychinus controls Protocore production through its territory. The entire system faces existential risks from Protocore dependence, potential ethical violations, and the Core's mysterious deceleration.
