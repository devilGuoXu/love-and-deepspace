Memory Breakthrough & Awakening

Breakthrough levels, awakening skills, and optimal material investment across your roster.

ENTRY_#0432026.06.30CORE_SYSTEM

System Overview: Breakthrough increases a memory's level cap and unlocks passive stat bonuses. Awakening unlocks a memory's unique passive skill. Both systems consume significant resources — understanding priority is essential.

Breakthrough Tiers

Each memory has 6 breakthrough tiers. Tier 1 (Level 20 cap): 3 common crystals. Tier 2 (Level 30): 5 common crystals + 3 mid crystals. Tier 3 (Level 40): 8 mid crystals + 3 rare crystals. Tier 4 (Level 50): 5 rare crystals + 3 core crystals. Tier 5 (Level 60): 8 core crystals + 3 primal crystals. Tier 6 (Level 70): 12 primal crystals + 3 memory duplicates.

The stat bonuses from breakthrough are: Tier 1-3 give ATK% or HP% (flat). Tier 4-5 give CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG (% scaling). Tier 6 gives a unique set bonus enhancement. The jump from Tier 3 to Tier 4 is the biggest power spike — the CRIT stats from Tier 4-5 double your damage potential.

Awakening Skills

Awakening requires the specific memory duplicate (same memory card) plus awakening crystals. Each SSR memory has a unique awakening skill. Examples: Light Seeker — Resonance CRIT DMG bonus extends to 6s. Dark Champion — Execute threshold increases from 30% to 40%. Inferno Blaze — Burn spread range +50%. Frostguard — Shield gain +25%.

Awakening priority: Only awaken SSRs you use in your main Abyss teams. An awakened SSR is roughly 15-20% stronger than a non-awakened version. Do NOT awaken SRs — the resource cost is too high relative to the benefit. Do NOT awaken off-element SSRs.

Resource Planning

Weekly crystal income: Weekly bosses drop 1-2 breakthrough crystals each (3 runs = 3-6 crystals). Events are the best source of rare and core crystals. Never spend diamonds on breakthrough materials in the shop — they appear naturally from farming. Awakening crystals are event-limited — only spent on your top-priority memory.

Priority order: Take your main DPS memory to Tier 4 first (CRIT stats). Then your sub-DPS. Then your support. Then main DPS to Tier 5. Awaken only after the memory is Tier 5.

Key Insight: A Tier 4 SSR outperforms a Tier 6 SR. Don't invest breakthrough materials in SRs past Tier 3. Save all rare+ crystals for SSRs. Many players waste months of crystals on SRs that get replaced — don't be that player.

Summary: Breakthrough Tier 4 is the big power spike (CRIT stats). Awaken only main team SSRs. Never breakthrough SRs past Tier 3. Save crystals for SSRs. Weekly bosses + events are your main crystal sources.
