THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Crystal Monarch

A regal boss encased in eternal ice. It creates defensive crystals and punishes blind aggression.

ENTRY_#1002026.06.29ELITE_BOSS

Recommended Power: 78k+ | Element: Ice | Weakness: Fire — Rafayel is mandatory. Bring AoE to clear crystal adds.

Phase 1: Frozen Court (100%-65%)

Crystal Wall: Monarch erects a wall of ice crystals. Destroy them quickly or they explode. Use AoE abilities. Shard Volley: Fires homing ice shards. Dodge three times in succession. Frost Pulse: AoE freeze wave. Jump to avoid. Getting frozen is a death sentence.

Phase 2: Shattered Throne (65%-30%)

Reflect Shield: Monarch becomes immune and reflects damage. Stop attacking. Use this time to heal and reposition. Crystal Rain: Shards fall from the sky in a pattern. Watch the ground shadows. Royal Decree: Marks one operative for an unavoidable ice spike. Switch to that operative and use defensive abilities.

Phase 3: Frozen Heart (30%-0%)

Permafrost: The entire arena begins freezing. Stay in unfrozen zones. Blizzard's Eye: Massive AoE centered on the Monarch. Run to the outer edge. Heart of Ice: Monarch reveals its core. All damage is doubled. Burst phase.

Recommended Team: Rafayel (Main DPS) — Fire melts everything. Zayne (Support) — his Frost resistance helps in Phase 3. Xavier (Flex) — Light damage for crystal adds.

Advanced Tip: Save Rafayel's Ardent Oath for the Reflect Shield phase. The shield has a hidden HP bar — enough Fire AoE damage can break it early, skipping the reflect mechanic entirely.

Summary: Rafayel is your win condition. Destroy crystals quickly, stop attacking during Reflect Shield, and stay mobile in Phase 3. This fight rewards AoE damage and patience.
