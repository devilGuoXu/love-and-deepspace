Combat Stat Thresholds

Every endgame activity has hidden stat requirements. Here is exactly how much ATK, HP, DEF, and CRIT you need.

ENTRY_#0802026.07.02THRESHOLDS

How to Read This: Find your current level of content (Early, Mid, Late, Endgame) and check the stat thresholds listed. If you are below the threshold, your priority is gear farming. If you meet them, your issue is gameplay rather than stats.

Early Game Thresholds (Level 1-50)

At this stage, thresholds are flexible — content is designed to be clearable with basic gear. Minimum ATK: 2,000 (any operative). Below this, your damage is too low for weekly bosses. Recommended HP: 8,000+. Below this, you risk being one-shot by boss mechanics. DEF: Not important at this stage. CRIT Rate: 20%+ is comfortable. Below 20%, resonance damage feels inconsistent. CRIT Damage: Whatever you get naturally — do not prioritize CDMG until late game. Check: If you are above these thresholds and still stuck, the issue is gear quality (protocore main stats are wrong) rather than stat values. Check your protocore loadout.

Mid Game Thresholds (Level 50-65)

Mid game is where stat thresholds start to matter. Content expects specific stat values. ATK: 4,000-5,000 for main DPS. Below 4,000, Abyss floors 40-50 will time out. Support operatives can be at 2,500-3,500. HP: 12,000+ for any operative that sees enemy hits. Squishier operatives like Rafayel need 14,000+. DEF: 1,500-2,500. Below 1,500, boss attacks chunk too hard. DEF is a hidden efficiency stat — it is worth more than most players think because it reduces healing downtime. CRIT Rate: 35-45% for main DPS. This is the sweet spot where resonance crits feel reliable. CRIT Damage: 80%+ starts to be noticeable. Aim for 100% if possible. Energy Regen: 120-130% for consistent Ardent Oath cycling. Check: If you meet all thresholds and still cannot clear Abyss 50, the problem is team composition or play pattern, not stats.

Late Game Thresholds (Level 65-75)

Abyss floors 60-80 and SHC rank 5% require these stats. ATK: 6,000-7,500 on main DPS. Support operatives should be at 3,500-4,500. HP: 16,000+ for all operatives. Endgame boss aoe hits for 8,000-10,000 damage — you need the HP buffer. DEF: 2,500-3,500. DEF reduction on endgame content is significant. CRIT Rate: 50%+. Aim for 55% as a comfortable target. CRIT rate is the most important offensive stat at this stage. CRIT Damage: 120%+. Every point of CRIT rate above 55% should be balanced with CRIT damage. Energy Regen: 130-140%. Faster Oath cycling = significantly more damage. Check: At this stage, protocore substat quality is the difference between hitting thresholds and missing them. +12 protocores with 3+ relevant substats are mandatory.

Endgame Thresholds (Level 75+): Abyss floors 80-100 and top 1% SHC require optimized gear. ATK: 8,000-9,000 for main DPS. This is the practical cap for most operatives without signature memories. HP: 18,000-20,000. Content at this level hits for 12,000+. DEF: 3,500+. The difference between getting two-shot and three-shot is DEF. CRIT Rate: 60-70%. Past 70%, investment in CDMG is more valuable. CRIT Damage: 150%+. This is the min-max zone. Energy Regen: 140%+. Some builds sacrifice CRIT for 150%+ regen for constant Oath uptime. Important: These are practical caps, not hard caps. You can exceed these with great gear, but the marginal gain per stamina spent beyond these thresholds is very low.

How to Fix Missing Thresholds

If you are below a threshold, here is the fastest fix: Missing ATK: Farm protocores with ATK% main stat on Solar and Lunar slots. ATK% is the most common main stat and the easiest to fix. Missing HP: HP% protocores on slots 1-2. HP is easier to stack than most players realize — swap one protocore and gain 2,000-4,000 HP. Missing CRIT Rate: CRIT rate main stat protocore on slot 4 or 6 (depending on set). A single CRIT rate piece can add 15-20% rate. Missing CRIT Damage: CDMG main stat on slot 5 or 6. Balance is key — do not stack CDMG above 200% without 50%+ CRIT rate. Adjusting priorities: If you are below on ATK and CRIT, fix CRIT first. CRIT rate is harder to come by than ATK (ATK has more sources: memories, bonds, level-ups, resonance). CRIT is almost entirely gated by protocores and memory substats.

Summary: Early: 2,000 ATK / 20% CRIT. Mid: 4,000-5,000 ATK / 35-45% CRIT. Late: 6,000-7,500 ATK / 50%+ CRIT. Endgame: 8,000-9,000 ATK / 60-70% CRIT / 150%+ CDMG. CRIT rate is the hardest stat to acquire — prioritize it. Missing a threshold usually means farming better protocores, not leveling more memories. Use these thresholds as a checklist when you hit a wall.
