Weekly Boss & Domain Guide

Efficient farming routes for materials, weekly boss rotation, and domain priority.

Weekly Reset: Every Monday at server reset. Weekly bosses, domain rewards, and Abyss all reset. Plan your week around this 閳?never do your weekly bosses on Sunday (do them Monday-Tuesday for the whole week ahead).

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Weekly Boss Priority

You have 3 weekly boss attempts. Each gives ascension materials and gold. Priority 1: The boss that drops materials for your main DPS operative. Check what materials your core memories need. Priority 2: The boss that drops gold. If no one needs materials, farm the highest-level boss for maximum gold. Priority 3: Any remaining attempts on the boss that drops the most useful material type (even if you don't need it now 閳?you will later).

Domain Rotation

Domains rotate daily. Each day has a different material type. Monday-Thursday: Character-specific materials. Plan around the operative you're building. Friday: Gold domain. Always run this 閳?gold is the universal bottleneck. Saturday-Sunday: All domains open. Use these days to fill gaps in your material stockpile.

Stamina Efficiency

Your best stamina-to-value ratio is: Weekly bosses (first 3) > Domain Friday (gold) > Character domains > Memory XP domains. The last 10% of stamina each day is best spent on gold 閳?it's the resource you always need more of.

Boss Selection Strategy

Each weekly boss drops materials for specific memory ascension tiers. Check which tier your primary operative needs before choosing your boss. Tier 3 ascension materials drop from level 70 bosses. Tier 4 drops from level 80 bosses. Tier 5 drops from level 90 bosses. You cannot use lower-tier materials to ascend past tier 3, so farming the wrong boss level wastes your weekly attempts. Always verify your current ascension tier in the memory screen before spending attempts.

Material drop rates vary by boss difficulty. The highest-level boss available to you drops roughly 20% more gold per attempt than the lowest-level option. If you are only farming for gold, always run the highest unlocked level. If you are farming for materials, run the level that matches your current ascension tier exactly 閳?running a higher level gives ascension materials you cannot use yet, and running a lower level gives materials that are no longer useful.

Long-Term Planning

Weekly boss materials follow a predictable cadence across updates. Each new operative release introduces a new boss with their associated materials. If you are saving for a future operative, stockpile their materials in advance by farming the weekly boss that shares their element type. Elemental affinity determines which boss drops which material type 閳?Fire materials come from fire-aligned bosses, Ice from ice-aligned, and so on. This pattern has held across all major updates since launch.

Anticipate upcoming ascension needs by planning two weeks ahead. If you know you will ascend a main DPS from tier 3 to tier 4 next week, farm their tier 4 materials this week during the double-reward window. The double rewards apply to the first three clears per week regardless of boss choice, so you can stockpile future materials without sacrificing current progress. This forward planning eliminates the material bottleneck that stalls most players at tier 4 ascension for two to three weeks.

Key Insight: The 3 weekly boss attempts give double rewards on the first clear. Never skip them. Even if you don't need the materials, the gold alone is worth more per stamina than any domain. Weekly bosses are the best stamina value in the game.

Weekly boss materials are shared across all operatives, meaning you can funnel all three weekly kills toward a single operative's ascension needs. This is most efficient when focusing on building one main DPS. Once your main DPS has reached the maximum ascension tier, switch to farming materials for your secondary DPS.

Summary: Weekly bosses first (best value). Gold domain on Friday. Character materials on matching days. Fill gaps on weekends. Never let your stamina cap 閳?even inefficient stamina usage is better than capped stamina.
