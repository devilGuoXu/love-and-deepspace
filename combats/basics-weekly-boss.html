Weekly Boss & Domain Guide

Efficient farming routes for materials, weekly boss rotation, and domain priority.

ENTRY_#0332026.06.30FARMING

Weekly Reset: Every Monday at server reset. Weekly bosses, domain rewards, and Abyss all reset. Plan your week around this — never do your weekly bosses on Sunday (do them Monday-Tuesday for the whole week ahead).

Weekly Boss Priority

You have 3 weekly boss attempts. Each gives ascension materials and gold. Priority 1: The boss that drops materials for your main DPS operative. Check what materials your core memories need. Priority 2: The boss that drops gold. If no one needs materials, farm the highest-level boss for maximum gold. Priority 3: Any remaining attempts on the boss that drops the most useful material type (even if you don't need it now — you will later).

Domain Rotation

Domains rotate daily. Each day has a different material type. Monday-Thursday: Character-specific materials. Plan around the operative you're building. Friday: Gold domain. Always run this — gold is the universal bottleneck. Saturday-Sunday: All domains open. Use these days to fill gaps in your material stockpile.

Stamina Efficiency

Your best stamina-to-value ratio is: Weekly bosses (first 3) > Domain Friday (gold) > Character domains > Memory XP domains. The last 10% of stamina each day is best spent on gold — it's the resource you always need more of.

Key Insight: The 3 weekly boss attempts give double rewards on the first clear. Never skip them. Even if you don't need the materials, the gold alone is worth more per stamina than any domain. Weekly bosses are the best stamina value in the game.

Summary: Weekly bosses first (best value). Gold domain on Friday. Character materials on matching days. Fill gaps on weekends. Never let your stamina cap — even inefficient stamina usage is better than capped stamina.
