Event Companion Content & Limited-Time Relationship Guide

Every major event in Love and Deepspace introduces exclusive companion content — limited Tender Moments, phone calls, gifts, outfits, and base decorations. This guide breaks down what each event type offers, how to prioritize spending, and what you can never get back.

Unlike permanent affinity sources that can always be farmed, event Tender Moments and phone calls are gone forever if you miss the window. Understanding the content categories and their return patterns is essential for account planning.

Event Content Overview

Love and Deepspace runs limited-time events approximately every 3-4 weeks, each bringing a fresh wave of companion-specific content. The scope of companion content varies significantly between event types. Major story events (such as the main storyline chapters and seasonal celebrations) offer the most companion content, while smaller rerun or minigame events may offer little to none. Understanding what each event contains before it starts lets you budget your gems and time effectively.

Companion event content falls into five main categories: Tender Moments, phone calls, gift items, outfit variants, and base decorations. Each category has different acquisition methods, gem yields, and permanence rules. Some content is free through event progression, some costs event currency or gems, and some is locked behind real-money purchases. The event shop, event story stages, and limited-time gacha banners are the primary distribution channels for these items.

Event Content Types

TMEvent Tender Moments150-300 Gems
Cinematic dates tied to event storylines. These are fully voiced, animated cutscenes similar to permanent Tender Moments but gated behind event progress. You must reach specific event stages or accumulate enough event currency to unlock each episode. Each completed episode rewards 50-100 gems, with a full 3-episode Tender Moment set giving 150-300 gems total. These are the highest-value event companion rewards — but they are permanently missable if the event ends before you unlock them. Unlike permanent Tender Moments, there is no catch-up mechanic for event-exclusive episodes.
PCEvent Phone Calls50-100 Gems
Limited-time phone calls that operatives make to the Hunter during events. These calls are triggered by reaching event milestones or completing specific event stages. If answered during the event window, the call is permanently saved to your call log and can be replayed anytime. If missed, the call is lost forever — there is no call-back feature for event-exclusive calls. Each unique call rewards 25-50 gems upon first playback. Events typically include 1-2 unique calls per operative featured in the event storyline.
GIEvent Gift ItemsAffinity EXP
Exclusive gift types available only during the event period. These come in two varieties: limited-event gifts that disappear from your inventory when the event ends (use before expiry), and permanent-addition gifts that join the standard gift pool after the event concludes. Check the item description carefully — if it says "Available until [date]" the gift expires; if it says "Can also be obtained from [event]", it becomes permanent. Event-exclusive gifts typically give 60-150 Affinity EXP with matching operative preferences, making them highly efficient for affinity leveling. Save permanent-addition event gifts for daily cap use, and use expiring gifts immediately.
OVEvent Outfit VariantsCosmetic Exclusive
Alternate outfits for operatives released alongside specific events. These are purely cosmetic but often include unique animations, special greeting lines, and exclusive photo mode poses. Event outfits are typically obtained through: event shop purchase (event currency), limited-time outfit gacha (gems), or real-money bundles. Some outfits return during rerun events, but collaboration outfits and anniversary-exclusive variants have never returned. Outfits are the lowest priority for free-to-play players but can be the highest-value cosmetic investment for operative mains.
BDEvent Base DecorationsDecoration
Limited-time furniture and decorations available from event shops or as milestone rewards. These items customize the Dawnbreak Base and provide ambient interactions with your operative. Event decorations range from small table ornaments to large room layouts. Most event decorations return during rerun events, though collaboration-exclusive decorations do not. Base decorations are primarily for personal enjoyment and do not provide gameplay benefits — spend event currency on these only after securing Tender Moments and critical upgrade materials.

Content Value Assessment

Not all event companion content is worth equal investment. Use the following framework to evaluate what an event offers against your personal account priorities. The assessment weighs gem cost against rewards, permanence, and personal operative preference.

Free Content
No gem or real-money cost. Earned through event progression or event shops using free event currency.
Priority: Highest
Always claim all free companion content before spending currency on anything else. Event Tender Moments and phone calls that are free milestone rewards should be your first target.
Gem-Cost Content
Requires spending gems directly or pulling on limited banners to unlock.
Priority: Medium
Evaluate the gem-to-reward ratio. A 10-pull (1,500 gems) for an event memory that unlocks a Tender Moment gives ~300 gems back plus story content. Compare against your monthly gem income before committing.
Paid-Only Content
Locked behind real-money purchases. Outfit bundles, premium pass tiers, and exclusive gift packs.
Priority: Lowest
Only recommended for operative mains or completionists with disposable income. Free-to-play players should treat paid content as nonexistent and focus entirely on free and gem-cost offerings.

Tip: Always check the event shop before spending any currency. Some Tender Moments that appear to be gem-cost are actually earnable through event progression once you reach higher stage clears. Wait until day 3-4 of an event before making spending decisions — by then, community guides will confirm exact unlock requirements and costs.

Rerun Prediction

Predicting which event companion content will return is essential for new players deciding whether to panic-spend or wait. Based on historical patterns and developer communications, event content falls into three rerun categories.

Annual Return
Content that returns on a predictable yearly schedule. Birthday events for each operative rerun annually with the same companion content available for purchase. Major holiday events (Lantern Festival, Summer Festival) have historically rerun the following year with the same shop and milestone rewards. If you miss a birthday Tender Moment, you can get it exactly one year later. These are the safest events to skip if you are low on resources — your content will come back.
Possible Rerun
Standard seasonal events and story-chapter events that may or may not return. The developer has rerun some but not all standard events after 6-12 months. Events tied to game anniversaries or major version updates have the highest rerun probability. Reruns typically retain event shops but may increase gem costs or change reward structures. Do not rely on a rerun happening — treat content as missable and only skip if the opportunity cost is too high.
Never Returns
Collaboration events with external IPs and the game's launch anniversary event content have been confirmed as never returning. Collaboration licenses expire, and anniversary-exclusive items are marketed as once-in-a-lifetime. If you see "Limited Collaboration" or "Anniversary Exclusive" on any companion content, treat it as your only chance. No exceptions have been made for these categories in any server region to date.

Tip for New Players: Do not panic over missing event content from before you started playing. Running events typically offer more companion content than reruns. Focus on current and upcoming events rather than grieving past missables. The game adds new Tender Moments and phone calls faster than any single player can fully experience.

Preparation Guide

Strategic preparation before an event launches can mean the difference between clearing all companion content and leaving gems on the table. The preparation window is typically 3-7 days before a major event begins, based on social media teasers and patch notes.

1
Maintain 500+ Stamina ReserveStop spending stamina refreshes 3 days before a major event. Keep at least 500 stamina in reserve for event stage clears. Event stages cost 20-30 stamina per clear and you need 20+ clears to unlock all Tender Moment episodes. Stockpile your stamina refill items from daily rewards and do not claim them until the event starts.
2
Save 3,000+ Gems for Event MemoriesEvent memories on limited banners often gate companion content. The 5-star event memory guarantees a Tender Moment unlock. Budget at least 3,000 gems (20 pulls) for events featuring your main operative. For events where no main operative is featured, save your gems entirely and rely on free event content only.
3
Do Not Claim Event Gifts EarlyEvent gift items from the shop and milestones have two variants: permanent (joins gift pool after event) and limited (expires). Do not claim any gift item from the event shop until you have verified its type via community sources. Limited gifts should be saved for the final day of the event in case you hit a progress wall that requires spending — they can serve as emergency currency exchange.
4
Review Datamine and Community GuidesWithin 24 hours of an event launch, community dataminers publish full content lists including all companion content unlock requirements. Read at least two sources before spending anything. Pay special attention to whether Tender Moments are milestone-gated (free) or banner-gated (require specific memories). Adjust your resource spending accordingly.

Tip: Create a dedicated in-game loadout preset for each operative before the event starts. When an event drops, you want to be ready to clear stages with your best team immediately — not spending the first two days reorganizing your protocores and memory sets. Pre-farm the materials you will need for any event memory you plan to pull.

Event Priority Checklist

Use this decision checklist when a new event launches to determine how much resource investment each piece of companion content deserves. Answer yes or no to each question; more yes answers means higher priority.

Is this operative my main or secondary main?
Yes = High priority. Content for your main operative has long-term value — you will replay Tender Moments, re-listen to calls, and wear outfits frequently.
No = Low priority. Skip gem-cost content for non-main operatives unless you are a completionist.
Does this event offer a permanent outfit variant?
Yes = Consider gem investment. Permanent outfits stay in your collection forever and may never return.
No = Focus on Tender Moments and phone calls only. Outfits that require real money are skippable.
Is the companion content free through event progression?
Yes = Always claim everything. Free content is the highest value for every account type.
No = Evaluate the gem cost against your current savings. Do not spend below 1,000 gem emergency reserve.
Does this content return annually or in reruns?
Yes = Safe to skip if resources are tight. You will have another chance in 6-12 months.
No = Prioritize acquisition if it is collaboration or anniversary exclusive. This is your only window.
Do I have enough stamina and gems to clear all content without spending real money?
Yes = Proceed with standard resource allocation. Complete all milestones before the final week.
No = Prioritize free content only. Skip gem-cost and paid content. Save resources for the next event that may feature your main operative.

Summary: Event companion content is the most time-sensitive system in Love and Deepspace, but not all of it is worth chasing. Prioritize free content above all else, invest gems in content for your main operative, and never spend real money on limited items unless you have fully exhausted free options. Maintain a 500+ stamina reserve and 3,000+ gem buffer before major events. Use the priority checklist to make calm, informed decisions instead of panic spending on the last day. Remember: the game releases new companion content faster than any player can fully experience — missing one event is not the end of your journey.
