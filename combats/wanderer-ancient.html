WANDERER ENCYCLOPEDIA — ANCIENT

Ancient Wanderers

The colossal boss-type wanderers — multi-phase, multi-weakness, multi-death. These are not enemies; they are disasters with legs. Every Ancient Wanderer is a skill check across gear, positioning, and endurance.

ENTRY_#WDR_042026.07.02ANCIENT

Core Identity: Ancient Wanderers are the rarest and most powerful wanderer type. Unlike standard wanderers that appear in groups, Ancients are solitary boss encounters. Each has unique weak points that must be destroyed to trigger phase transitions. They appear in Abyss floors 9-12, endgame events, and boss rush mode. Ancients are immune to all crowd control effects — you cannot stun, freeze, or knock back an Ancient.

Titan Sentry — The Colossus

Appearance: 15-meter humanoid golem with crystalline armor plates. Carries a massive stone club. HP: Extreme. Phases: 3. Attack Pattern: Phase 1: Slow club swings (3-hit combo), ground slam (AoE 4-unit radius). Telegraph is generous (1-1.5s). Phase 2 (armor break): Crystalline plates on arms shatter. Gains attack speed. Adds a charge attack. Phase 3 (critical): Chest crystal exposed. Gains a laser beam attack from the crystal.

Weak Points: Arm joints (Phase 1) → Knee joints (Phase 2) → Chest crystal (Phase 3). Each weak point has its own HP bar. Destroying a weak point staggers the Sentry for 4s. Elemental Weakness: Ice (Zayne) — slows its attack speed by 20%. Strategy: Phase 1: Focus the left arm joint. Use Zayne's ice to slow its swing combo. Phase 2: Dodge the charge (sideways, always sideways). Hit the knee joints — destroying both knees locks it in place for 6s. Phase 3: The laser beam has a 1s charge time (crystal glows). Run perpendicular. Destroy the chest crystal to end the fight instantly.

Crystal Monarch — The Frozen Throne

Appearance: Giant ice crystal construct, vaguely humanoid. Surrounded by a permanent blizzard. HP: Extreme. Phases: 3. Attack Pattern: Phase 1: Ice shard rain (continuous), ice spike eruptions from ground (targets player position). Phase 2 (shield break): Gains an Ice shield (6 hits). Uses Frost Breath — a cone AoE that freezes. Phase 3 (enrage): Continuous AoE frost damage. Ice spikes spawn faster.

Weak Points: Core crystal (all phases — only vulnerable for 3s after destroying ice armor segments). Elemental Weakness: Fire (Rafayel) — burn DoT prevents its Ice shield from regenerating. Resists: Ice (0.25x). Strategy: Phase 1: Stay at medium range. Spikes spawn at your feet — move after every attack. Phase 2: Break the Ice shield with Rafayel's fire. During Frost Breath, run behind the Monarch (the cone is frontal only). Phase 3: Pop your Ardent Oath at the start of enrage to skip the most dangerous part. Keep moving non-stop — standing still for 2 seconds means death.

Void Corruptor — The Shadow Puzzle

Appearance: Amorphous shadow mass with multiple tentacles. A glowing purple core pulses in its center. HP: Very High. Phases: 2 (but phase 1 has sub-stages). Attack Pattern: Phase 1 cycles between 3 stances: (A) Tentacle Sweep — wide horizontal swipe. (B) Void Orbs — fires 3 tracking purple orbs. (C) Shadow Clones — splits into 3 copies, only 1 is real. Phase 2 (core exposed): Gains teleport + slam attack. All attacks deal double damage.

Weak Points: The core is exposed during specific attacks (Tentacle Sweep recovery, after destroying all Void Orbs). Elemental Weakness: Light (Xavier) — reveals the real body during Shadow Clones. Resists: Dark (0.5x). Strategy: This fight has three sub-stages in Phase 1. (A) Tentacle Sweep: jump over the sweep, hit the core. (B) Void Orbs: destroy all 3 orbs with ranged attacks — this staggers the Corruptor for 5s. (C) Shadow Clones: Xavier's Light element reveals the real one (it has a faint Light glow). Hit the real one to progress. Repeat the cycle 3 times to enter Phase 2. Phase 2: Save Ardent Oath for this phase — it's a DPS race. The teleport slam has a 0.5s ground shadow telegraph.

Pro Tip: Ancient Wanderers share one weakness: their weak points are ALWAYS vulnerable for exactly 3 seconds after a specific attack. Learn the timing — 1 second to position, 2 seconds to deal damage. If you miss the window, you wait for the next rotation. Patience beats greed every time against Ancients.

Farming Ancients for Rewards

Ancient Wanderers are the only reliable source of SSR Protocores outside events. Titan Sentry: Drops DEF and HP main stat protocores (Slot 3, 5). Best for tank builds. Crystal Monarch: Drops ATK and CRIT protocores (Slot 1, 4). Best for DPS builds. Void Corruptor: Drops Energy Recharge and Healing Bonus (Slot 2, 5). Best for support builds. Recommended farm: Crystal Monarch for DPS units → Titan Sentry for tanks → Void Corruptor last (support builds are lower priority).

Summary: Ancients are multi-phase boss fights with weak point mechanics. Titan Sentry → destroy joints, use Ice. Crystal Monarch → core crystal, use Fire. Void Corruptor → cycle stances, use Light. Weak points have 3s vulnerability windows. Patience is the real DPS check. Each drops specific SSR Protocore slots — farm accordingly.
