Zayne: Combat Rotation Guide

Hybrid DPS/Support — shield, freeze, and HP-scaling sustain loops.

ENTRY_#0412026.06.30OPERATIVE_GUIDE

Playstyle: Zayne is the only hybrid operative — he deals damage while keeping the team alive. His unique mechanic converts HP into both shields and damage. Unlike Xavier (burst) or Sylus (execute), Zayne wins through attrition — he outlasts the enemy while maintaining steady DPS.

Core Rotation

Zayne's rotation prioritizes shield maintenance: Auto x3 → Frost Strike (hold attack) → Resonance → Shield Refresh → Auto x3. The Shield Refresh is a manual activation (tap shield button) when your shield drops below 50%. Never let your shield break — losing it resets your HP-scaling damage bonus for 3 seconds.

Frost Strike after 3 autos applies a 2s slow to the target. Use this to control boss positioning. Resonance applies a 1s freeze to non-boss enemies and deals bonus damage to frozen targets. Against bosses, Resonance still applies a slow effect (30% movement speed reduction for 3s).

HP Management

Zayne's damage scales with max HP. Each point of HP above 20,000 gives +0.05% damage bonus (capped at 50% bonus at 30,000 HP). His shield also scales with HP — 35% of max HP as shield strength. Stack HP on memories (slot 1 and 3 main stats) but don't neglect CRIT — without CRIT, your HP-scaling is wasted.

Healing priority: Zayne's Resonance heals the active operative for 15% of Zayne's max HP. Use Resonance when your team is below 70% HP — the heal is significant. His Ardent Oath also provides a party-wide shield for 20% of Zayne's max HP for 8 seconds. Time Oath before heavy boss attacks.

Team Synergy

Zayne pairs best with: Xavier (Light Seeker) — your shields reduce Xavier's need to dodge, letting him maintain DPS uptime. Rafayel — your freeze sets up his burn spread (burned frozen targets take +25% damage). Sylus — your sustain lets Sylus stay in melee range longer.

Key Insight: Zayne is the only operative who can solo-carry sustain-heavy floors (Floor 8). His self-healing, shields, and HP-scaling damage make him uniquely suited for content where other operatives would die to DoT. If you're stuck on a survival floor, bring Zayne.

Summary: Hybrid sustain fighter. Maintain shield above 50%. Stack HP to 30k for 50% damage bonus. Use Resonance for healing. Freeze to control and set up combos. Pairs with everyone.
