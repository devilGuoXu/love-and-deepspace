CLASSIFIED N109 — HISTORY

Deepspace World Timeline

The complete chronology — from the Protofield Era to the present day. Events, discoveries, and the hidden threads connecting them.

ENTRY_#N109_072026.07.01TIMELINE

Scope: This timeline consolidates information from main story chapters, memory anecdotes, worldbuilding entries (#024), and lore analysis articles. Some dates are inferred from contextual clues where exact dates are not provided. Spoilers for all current story content.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Protofield Era (Unknown — 500 Years Ago)

The Protofield — an ancient civilization with advanced energy technology. They discovered the Deepspace and attempted to harness it. The Protofield's capital was located in what is now the N109 Zone. Their technology was based on "Resonance Cores" — precursors to modern memories. The civilization collapsed when the Deepspace energy source destabilized, creating the Wanderers.

Key event: The First Resonance Catastrophe. A chain reaction of Deepspace energy release wiped out 90% of the Protofield population and created the first Wanderer spawn points (now called "rifts"). The survivors scattered and lost most of their technology. This is why modern humanity has advanced tech but no memory of the Protofield — it was deliberately erased as a trauma response.

The Dark Centuries (500 — 100 Years Ago)

The "Lost Age" — fragmented records describe a world rebuilding from the Catastrophe. Wandering tribes, rogue Wanderer attacks, and isolated settlements. The first Hunters emerged during this period — individuals with natural Resonance affinity who could fight Wanderers. They formed the first Hunter organizations, which eventually became the modern Hunter Corps.

During this period, something else was happening in the shadows — the first Onychinus-like organizations formed. Criminal networks that exploited Wanderer remains for power. This is when the "N109 Zone" designation first appeared (a geographic marker for the most contaminated area, where Protofield ruins were most accessible).

Modern Era — Pre-Story (100 — 10 Years Ago)

Technological renaissance. The Hunter Corps was formally established. Memory technology was reverse-engineered from Protofield artifacts found in N109 excavations. The four main characters were born during this period. Sylus's rise in Onychinus happened 15-20 years before the main story. Xavier arrived on Earth approximately 10 years before the story (his "crash landing").

Zayne trained as a Hunter and joined the Corps. Rafayel established his identity as an artist while secretly investigating his heritage. The MC (the player character) began her journey as a new Hunter recruit — this is where the main story begins.

Present Story — Key Events

Main Story Chapters 1-4: MC joins Hunter Corps, meets Xavier (colleague), Zayne (childhood friend), Rafayel (mysterious artist). First major Wanderer outbreak. Chapters 5-8: Introduction of Sylus and Onychinus. N109 Zone exploration. Discovery of the Protofield connection. Chapters 9-12: The truth about Wanderer origins. The corrupted Hunter incidents. Phantom battles begin.

Chapter 13+: Escalation — the Wanderer threat is revealed to be only the first stage of a larger problem. The Deepspace itself is destabilizing. The 50-year warning from Protofield records: if the Deepspace is not stabilized, a second Catastrophe will occur.

Connection: The Wanderer catalog (#N109_04) lists 4 types of Wanderers — but the timeline suggests there may be a 5th type that hasn't been publicly revealed yet. The Protofield records reference "Ancients" — Wanderers that predate the Catastrophe. If this is true, the Wanderers aren't a side effect of the Catastrophe — they were the cause.

Summary: Protofield era (500+ years ago) → First Catastrophe creates Wanderers → Dark Centuries of rebuilding → Modern era with Hunter Corps + Onychinus → Present story. The 50-year warning looms. The Wanderers may have been the cause, not the symptom. The timeline is still unfolding.

The timeline of major events in Love and Deepspace reveals a pattern that researchers have only recently identified. Major dimensional events occur in 17-year cycles. The Deepspace rift appeared 17 years ago. The fall of Philos, based on recovered archaeological evidence, occurred approximately 17 Philos-years before the rift's appearance. The Aether Core's energy output follows a 17-month cycle of peaks and troughs. This 17-based pattern suggests a dimensional resonance frequency that connects all major events in the timeline.

Dimensional events are accelerating: 12 in years 1-5, 47 in years 5-10, 200+ in years 10-15. The 17-year cycle theory predicts a major event approaching that could fundamentally alter the dimensional landscape.
