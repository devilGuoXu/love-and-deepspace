Login & Check-in Events

The easiest rewards in the game: log in, click, collect. No stamina, no combat, no gear check. But even these have optimization tricks most players miss.

ENTRY_#EVT_102026.07.02LOGIN

What They Are: Login events reward you simply for opening the game each day. They run continuously — there is almost never a day without an active login event. Types: monthly calendar (30 days), 7-day new player (first week), special event login (7-14 days during major events), and cumulative login (login X total days for milestone rewards).

Monthly Login Calendar

Resets: 1st of every month. Reward structure: Day 1-25: Small materials (5k gold, 1 medium stamina pot, enhancement ore). Day 26-28: 1 limited pull ticket each. Day 29: 5 limited pull tickets. Day 30: 10 limited pull tickets. Total monthly: 23 pulls worth of resources. Strategy: You cannot miss days and catch up — login calendars are day-locked. If you miss day 15, you cannot claim day 15's reward after day 16 passes. Set a daily alarm for 5 minutes before server reset. The server reset time is fixed (check your server's daily reset in settings). Pro tip: The 10-pull reward on day 30 is the single largest free pull injection in the game. Never let your monthly login streak break in the last 3 days.

New Player 7-Day Login

Duration: First 7 days of your account. Rewards: Day 1: 1 SSR memory selector (choose from 4 starters). Day 3: 5 limited pulls. Day 5: 1 SSR protocore (guaranteed ATK% main stat). Day 7: 1 SR memory (your choice of starter). Strategy: The SSR selector on day 1 is critical. Choose Xavier (Light Seeker) — he is the best generalist for early content. Do NOT choose based on personal preference — Xavier clears content faster. The guaranteed protocore on day 5 is also for Xavier (ATK% slot 3). Milestone unlocks: The 7-day login does not require consecutive logins — you have 14 days total to claim all 7 days. If you miss day 2, you can still claim it on day 8. But the timer still counts down — after 14 days, unclaimed rewards are lost.

Event Login Calendars

Major events (anniversary, summer, Christmas) always include a 7-14 day login bonus. Typical rewards: Day 1-3: Materials. Day 4: 1 limited pull. Day 5: Exclusive name card or portrait frame. Day 7: 3-5 limited pulls. Day 10 (if 14-day): 1 costume piece or 5 pulls. Strategy: Event login calendars do NOT overlap with the monthly calendar — they are separate tabs. You can claim both daily. This means during major events, you get double login rewards for 7-14 days. Plan your pull spending around these overlaps. Cumulative login: Some events have a separate "login X times during the event" tracker (not consecutive). Log in 5 times total during a 14-day event for bonus pulls. This is easy to miss because it is in a different menu tab. Check the event details screen every day during major events.

Mailbox Rewards

Many login rewards are delivered via in-game mail, not the login calendar. Mail types: Developer apology mail (200-500 diamonds, common after maintenance issues). Maintenance compensation (100 diamonds per hour of downtime). Event milestone mail (sent after the event ends to all participants). Community goal mail (sent when the community reaches a collective goal like "1M total boss kills"). Mail expiry: Mails expire 7 days after receipt. The "received" time is when the devs sent it, not when you read it. If you don't log in for 6 days, you have 1 day to claim before it expires. Log in at least once every 6 days to avoid missing mail rewards. Hidden mail trick: Some mails have "limited time" rewards attached. If the mail says "claim within 3 days," the item inside may also expire. Claim the item immediately after opening the mail — do not leave items sitting in your mailbox.

Pro Tip: Set a "deepspace alarm" on your phone for 5 minutes before daily reset. Login, claim all login calendars (monthly + event), check mail, claim all items, and logout. This takes 2 minutes. If you do this every day, you will never miss a login reward. The difference between a player who logs in daily and a player who logs in every 3 days is 50+ pulls per month. Consistency is the real P2W.

Annual Login Rewards

Deepspace has a cumulative login tracker separate from the monthly calendar. Milestones at: 30 days (title), 100 days (SSR selector shards), 200 days (exclusive outfit), 365 days (premium name card + 10 pulls). Strategy: This is the only login tracker that rewards total logins, not consecutive logins. Even if you take a 2-week break, your cumulative progress does not reset. The 365-day reward is a badge of honor — plan to reach it if you intend to play long-term. Fun fact: Most players quit between day 30-60. If you reach day 100, you are in the top 30% of active accounts by retention. The game rewards loyalty disproportionately — the day 200 and 365 rewards are designed to keep you hooked.

Summary: Monthly login = 23 pulls. Never miss day 28-30. New player 7-day = pick Xavier. Event login = double rewards during major events. Mail expires in 7 days — log in every 6 days minimum. Set a daily alarm for server reset. 2 minutes per day = 50+ extra pulls per month. Consistency beats everything.
