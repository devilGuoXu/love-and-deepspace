Login & Check-in Events

The easiest rewards in the game: log in, click, collect. No stamina, no combat, no gear check. But even these have optimization tricks most players miss.

ENTRY_#EVT_102026.07.02LOGIN

What They Are: Login events reward you simply for opening the game each day. They run continuously — there is almost never a day without an active login event. Types: monthly calendar (30 days), 7-day new player (first week), special event login (7-14 days during major events), and cumulative login (login X total days for milestone rewards).

Monthly Login Calendar

Resets: 1st of every month. You cannot miss days and catch up — login calendars are day-locked.

Days 1-25
Small materials
5k gold, stamina pot, enhancement ore
These are consolation rewards. Nice to have but not the main attraction. Claim them daily.
Days 26-28
1 limited pull ticket each
3 tickets total
The value starts ramping up. Missing these days hurts. Set a reminder.
Days 29-30
Day 29: 5 tickets
Day 30: 10 tickets
The 10-pull reward on day 30 is the single largest free pull injection in the game. Never let your monthly login streak break in the last 3 days.

Strategy: Set a daily alarm for 5 minutes before server reset. The server reset time is fixed (check your server's daily reset in settings). Total monthly: 23 pulls worth of resources.

New Player 7-Day Login

Duration: First 7 days of your account (you have 14 total days to claim all rewards — missed days can be claimed later within the window).

1
Day 1 — SSR Memory Selector
Choose Xavier (Light Seeker) — he is the best generalist for early content. Do NOT choose based on personal preference — Xavier clears content faster. This choice determines your early game pace.
3
Day 3 — 5 Limited Pulls
Free pulls for the limited banner. Save them for a banner that matches your chosen operative.
5
Day 5 — SSR Protocore (ATK%)
Guaranteed ATK% main stat. Slot this on Xavier. This single protocore carries your early game damage.
7
Day 7 — SR Memory (Your Choice)
Pick the starter that complements Xavier. This rounds out your early team composition.

Milestone note: The 7-day login does not require consecutive logins — you have 14 days total to claim all 7 days. If you miss day 2, you can still claim it on day 8. But the timer still counts down — after 14 days, unclaimed rewards are lost.

Event Login Calendars

Major events (anniversary, summer, Christmas) always include a 7-14 day login bonus. These are separate tabs from the monthly calendar — you can claim both daily.

DAYS 1-3
Materials
Standard filler rewards. Small amounts of gold, XP, and enhancement ores.
DAY 4
1 Limited Pull
First meaningful reward. This is when the calendar starts paying off.
DAY 5
Exclusive Name Card or Portrait Frame
Cosmetic reward that never returns. Worth logging in for if you care about collection.
DAY 7
3-5 Limited Pulls
The main prize. This alone makes the event login calendar worth tracking.
DAY 10 (14-day)
1 Costume Piece or 5 Pulls
Extra bonus for longer calendars. Typically the best single-day reward after day 7.

Cumulative login: Some events have a separate "login X times during the event" tracker (not consecutive). Log in 5 times total during a 14-day event for bonus pulls. This is easy to miss because it is in a different menu tab. Check the event details screen every day during major events.

Mailbox Rewards

Many login rewards are delivered via in-game mail, not the login calendar. This is one of the most commonly overlooked reward sources.

100 diamonds/hour

Event Milestone Mail

🌍
Community Goal Mail
Collective rewards

⚠ Mail expiry: Mails expire 7 days after receipt. The "received" time is when the devs sent it, not when you read it. If you don't log in for 6 days, you have 1 day to claim before it expires. Log in at least once every 6 days to avoid missing mail rewards.

Hidden mail trick: Some mails have "limited time" rewards attached. If the mail says "claim within 3 days," the item inside may also expire. Claim the item immediately after opening the mail — do not leave items sitting in your mailbox.

Pro Tip: Set a "deepspace alarm" on your phone for 5 minutes before daily reset. Login, claim all login calendars (monthly + event), check mail, claim all items, and logout. This takes 2 minutes. If you do this every day, you will never miss a login reward. The difference between a player who logs in daily and a player who logs in every 3 days is 50+ pulls per month. Consistency is the real P2W.

Annual Login Rewards

Deepspace has a cumulative login tracker separate from the monthly calendar. This rewards total logins, not consecutive logins.

30 Days — TitleMilestone 1
100 Days — SSR Selector ShardsMilestone 2
200 Days — Exclusive OutfitMilestone 3
365 Days — Premium Name Card + 10 PullsMilestone 4

Strategy: This is the only login tracker that rewards total logins, not consecutive. Even if you take a 2-week break, your cumulative progress does not reset. The 365-day reward is a badge of honor — plan to reach it if you intend to play long-term.

Fun fact: Most players quit between day 30-60. If you reach day 100, you are in the top 30% of active accounts by retention. The game rewards loyalty disproportionately — the day 200 and 365 rewards are designed to keep you hooked.

DO & DON'T Summary

✅ DO
  • Set a daily alarm for 5 minutes before server reset
  • Claim both monthly AND event login calendars
  • Log in at least once every 6 days for mail rewards
  • Choose Xavier from the new player SSR selector
  • Claim mail items immediately after opening
  • Track your cumulative login milestones
❌ DON'T
  • Don't break your monthly login streak in the last 3 days
  • Don't leave items sitting in your mailbox
  • Don't skip event login calendars — they are free pulls
  • Don't choose SR memory over SSR from the selector
  • Don't underestimate the 50+ pull/month value of consistency
  • Don't forget about cumulative login trackers

Summary: Monthly login = 23 pulls. Never miss day 28-30. New player 7-day = pick Xavier. Event login = double rewards during major events. Mail expires in 7 days — log in every 6 days minimum. Set a daily alarm for server reset. 2 minutes per day = 50+ extra pulls per month. Consistency beats everything.
