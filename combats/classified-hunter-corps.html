Hunter Corps: Secrets & Shadows

Internal documents, leaked intelligence, and an uncomfortable truth about the organization that claims to protect humanity.

Classified Assessment: The Hunter Corps presents a clean public image as a humanitarian organization dedicated to protecting humanity from Wanderers. Beneath the surface, internal documents reveal a far more complex reality — classified research divisions, memory manipulation programs, and a deeply complicated relationship with the truth about Deepspace.

Public Face vs Hidden Reality

On paper, the Hunter Corps exists for a single purpose: protect humanity from the Wanderer threat. Their recruitment materials emphasize valor, sacrifice, and the noble calling of standing between civilization and the abyss. Recruits are shown carefully curated footage of hunters defending cities, rescuing civilians, and recovering protocores for peaceful research. None of this is false. It is simply incomplete.

The Corps' public-facing narrative paints the organization as a unified force. In reality, the Corps operates as a collection of semi-autonomous divisions, each with its own budget, leadership hierarchy, and — crucially — its own information clearance protocols. What the Research Division knows, Field Operations may not. What Intelligence Bureau uncovers, Memory Archive may never see. This compartmentalization is not accidental. It is a deliberate architecture of control designed to ensure that no single operative — and certainly no field hunter — possesses the full picture of what the Corps truly does.

Onychinus intelligence has confirmed through multiple independent sources that the Corps maintains at least three programs that contradict its public charter: a classified research initiative into the origin of Deepspace rifts, a protocore development program with direct ties to Philos artifacts, and a memory suppression protocol applied to high-value hunters without their knowledge or consent. These programs are funded through off-book accounts routed through shell humanitarian organizations, making them invisible to standard auditing procedures.

Internal Divisions

The Hunter Corps is not a monolith. It is a federation of four major branches, each operating with significant autonomy and, in some cases, competing agendas. Understanding these divisions is essential to understanding where the Corps' true power — and its true vulnerabilities — lie.

BRANCH ALPHA / FIELD OPERATIONS
Front-Line Hunters
The public face of the Corps. Field operatives are the ones who appear in news broadcasts, receive medals, and die on camera. They are given just enough information to do their jobs effectively — and no more. Field hunters do not know the true origin of the Wanderers they fight. They do not know what happens to the protocores they recover. They are told what they need to hear, and that is all. This division is the most heavily monitored, with embedded handlers in every major hunter team.
BRANCH BETA / RESEARCH DIVISION
Wanderer & Protocore Studies
The Research Division operates out of a secured facility whose location is known only to division heads and the Corps Council. Its mandate: study Wanderer biology, protocore energy signatures, and — most controversially — the nature of the rifts themselves. Onychinus has obtained partial research logs indicating the division has known for at least three years that Deepspace rifts are not naturally occurring phenomena. This information has never been released to the public or to field hunters.
BRANCH GAMMA / INTELLIGENCE BUREAU
N109 Surveillance & Counter-Intelligence
The branch most directly engaged with Onychinus. Intelligence Bureau handles surveillance of N109 Zone activities, monitors known criminal organizations, and conducts counter-intelligence operations against corporate and foreign entities. They are the Corps' spies, and they know Onychinus exists. What they do not know — despite their confidence — is the depth of Onychinus's penetration into their own ranks. The Bureau maintains detailed files on every known N109 operative, including Sylus. None of these files are complete or accurate.
BRANCH DELTA / MEMORY ARCHIVE
History, Records & Memory Management
The most mysterious division. Memory Archive is nominally responsible for maintaining historical records of Corps operations. In practice, it manages the Corps' memory suppression program — a series of protocols that selectively modify or remove hunters' memories of classified operations. Officially, these protocols exist to protect hunters from psychological trauma. Unconfirmed reports suggest they are used to protect the Corps from exposure. Memory Archive personnel operate under higher clearance than any other non-Council division.

What the Corps Hides

Three specific secrets have been confirmed by Onychinus intelligence. Each represents a fundamental betrayal of the Corps' public mission. Each is supported by documentary evidence obtained from compromised Corps data relays.

Secret I: The Rifts Are Not Natural
Deepspace rifts are not geological or cosmic phenomena. They are manufactured. Research Division internal memos reference "rift seeding" and "activation sequences" — language that implies deliberate creation and control. The Corps has known this since at least 2023 and has actively suppressed all public investigation into rift origins. The evidence: a partially recovered Research Division report (codenamed PROJECT CHRYSALIS) describing the energy signature patterns of artificial vs. natural rifts, concluding that all major rifts in the last decade share artificial markers.
Classification: COSMIC / EYES ONLY
Evidence Grade: CONFIRMED — Partial document recovery
Secret II: Protocores Are Connected to Philos
Protocores are not byproducts of Wanderer crystallization. They are remnants of a civilization called Philos — a world that existed before the current one. The Corps' Research Division has been quietly excavating Philos artifacts and reverse-engineering their energy technology under the guise of "protocore development." The energy that powers hunter weapons, the resonance that enables Evols — all of it traces back to Philos technology. The Corps' cover story (protocores are Wanderer crystallizations) is a convenient fiction that hides a much older and more dangerous truth.
Classification: COSMIC / EYES ONLY
Evidence Grade: CONFIRMED — Artifact correlation & linguistic analysis
Secret III: The Memory Suppression Program
Memory Archive operates a systematic program of memory modification applied to hunters who encounter classified information during field operations. The official justification is "psychological protection." The actual purpose appears to be operational security. Hunters who witness events that contradict the Corps' official narrative are quietly reassigned to "extended psychological evaluation" — a euphemism for memory suppression treatment. Upon release, these hunters retain no memory of the classified event. They continue their duties unaware that anything was taken from them. Onychinus has identified at least seven confirmed cases of memory-suppressed hunters currently serving in active field roles.
Classification: TOP SECRET / MEMORY ARCHIVE
Evidence Grade: CONFIRMED — Seven verified cases with medical records

Sylus's Intelligence Network

The Hunter Corps believes it is the hunter. It is wrong. Onychinus has maintained a persistent intelligence presence within the Corps for years, and that presence runs deeper than the Intelligence Bureau suspects.

Sylus's network within the Corps operates on three levels. The first level is information brokers — freelance operatives who sell Corps data on the N109 black market without knowing they are working for Onychinus. These cutouts provide deniability while feeding a steady stream of low-clearance intelligence: personnel transfers, supply chain movements, mission schedules. The second level is double agents — Corps employees who have been turned through leverage, coercion, or ideological alignment. These assets occupy positions in Field Operations and the Intelligence Bureau. At least one sits on the Bureau's N109 surveillance desk, ensuring that anything the Bureau "discovers" about Onychinus is carefully curated. The third level is technical interception. Onychinus maintains a dedicated signals intelligence unit that monitors encrypted Corps communications. The Corps knows its communications are monitored. They rotate encryption protocols every 72 hours. Onychinus breaks each new protocol within 12 hours.

The Corps knows it has moles. The Intelligence Bureau has conducted three internal leak investigations in the past eighteen months. None has identified a single Onychinus asset. This is not because the investigations are incompetent. It is because the Bureau's own security protocols were designed with input from Onychinus double agents, who ensured that every investigation methodology contained a blind spot large enough to protect the network. The mutual surveillance between the Corps and Onychinus has created a cold war fought below the threshold of open conflict. Both sides know the other is compromised. Neither can afford to act on that knowledge without exposing their own capabilities.

The MC's Role

Of all the mysteries surrounding the Hunter Corps, none is more personal than their interest in the MC. The Corps' recruitment and monitoring of the MC is not standard procedure. It is exceptional, and the reasons for it are not benevolent.

The MC possesses a Resonance Evol of unusual power and stability. Onychinus's own analysis indicates that the MC's resonance capacity is approximately 300% higher than the average hunter with a compatible Evol. This makes the MC uniquely valuable — and uniquely dangerous. The Corps cannot control the MC's resonance. They can monitor it, direct it, and exploit it, but they cannot replicate or suppress it. The MC represents a variable the Corps cannot manage through their standard protocols. Their response has been to keep the MC close, assign missions that test the limits of the MC's abilities, and document every resonance fluctuation for Research Division analysis.

Evidence of manipulation appears in the MC's early mission assignments. Onychinus has obtained internal Corps communications showing that the MC's initial deployment pattern was deliberately designed to expose the MC to specific types of Wanderer encounters — encounters that produced resonance data the Research Division needed for an ongoing project. The missions were not chosen for the MC's development. They were chosen for the data they would generate. The Corps' interest in the MC is not entirely benevolent. The MC is a resource. A powerful, unpredictable, and closely monitored resource. The question that remains unanswered is what the Corps plans to do when the MC becomes too powerful to monitor — or too informed to control.

Onychinus Assessment: The Hunter Corps is neither hero nor villain in the traditional sense. It is an institution that has convinced itself that its ends justify its means. The secrets it keeps are not kept out of malice, but out of a belief that humanity cannot handle the truth. That belief may be the most dangerous secret of all.

Summary: The Hunter Corps operates behind a carefully constructed public image. Its internal divisions maintain competing agendas. Its classified programs directly contradict its humanitarian charter. Onychinus has successfully infiltrated all four major branches. The MC's recruitment was motivated by the Corps' need to study and control an unusually powerful Resonance Evol. The cold war between the Corps and Onychinus continues, with neither side willing to escalate — yet.
