Co-op Memory Builds

Specialized builds for co-op content — your solo DPS build may not be optimal in a group.

ENTRY_#0602026.07.01COOP_BUILDS

Co-op vs Solo Building: In solo content, your DPS is everything. In co-op, you have teammates covering roles. A "good" co-op build sacrifices personal DPS to enable team damage. Building for co-op is about synergizing, not optimizing individual performance.

Tank Build — Sylus

Sylus is the best tank in co-op due to his high base DEF and self-sustain. Build: Void Walker 2-pc (ER) + Guardian Protocol 2-pc (DEF). Main stats: HP% on Slot 1, DEF% on Slot 3, Healing Bonus or HP% on Slot 5. Substats: DEF% > HP% > Energy Recharge. Target: 15k+ HP, 8k+ DEF, 140%+ ER.

Playstyle: Stay in front of the boss. Use resonance for aggro generation. Perfect dodge for damage reduction. Your job is not to deal damage — it's to hold aggro and survive. A tank that dies in 3 hits is worthless. A tank that survives 30 seconds gives the DPS team 30 seconds of free damage.

Support Build — Zayne

Zayne's co-op role is pure support. Build: Frostguard 4-pc (HP + Shield). Main stats: HP% all slots. Substats: HP% > Energy Recharge > DEF%. Target: 35k+ HP, 160%+ ER.

Playstyle: Your resonance heals the active operative for 15% of Zayne's max HP. At 35k HP, that's 5,250 HP per heal. Your Ardent Oath gives the entire party a shield for 20% of Zayne's max HP (7,000 shield). Keep shields up on the tank. Heal the DPS when they take AoE damage. Never use Oath for damage — always for the party shield.

DPS Build — Xavier

Co-op DPS Xavier is similar to solo but with one change: swap CRIT Rate Slot 5 for ATK% Slot 5. In co-op, you have Zayne's shields keeping you safe, so you can afford to build full damage. With ATK% main stat and CRIT DMG substats, your burst windows hit harder even if your average damage is slightly less consistent.

Flex Build — Rafayel

Rafayel in co-op is an add-clear specialist. His AoE burn is invaluable for keeping the arena clean of spawns. Build: Inferno Blaze 4-pc (burn). Prioritize Energy Recharge over CRIT — your job is to have resonance up for every add wave, not to maximize burn damage. 150%+ ER minimum.

Key Insight: Co-op builds are NOT for general use. If you equip a tank Sylus build in solo Abyss, you will fail DPS checks. Similarly, a co-op DPS Xavier build in solo content has inconsistent CRIT. Only equip these builds when specifically queuing for co-op. Use the memory loadout slots (if available) to swap quickly.

Summary: Tank Sylus (HP/DEF, hold aggro). Support Zayne (35k HP, shield party). DPS Xavier (ATK% slot 5, burst). Flex Rafayel (ER focus, add clear). Only use in co-op — swap back for solo.
