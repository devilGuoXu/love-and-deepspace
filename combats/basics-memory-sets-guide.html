Memory Set Bonuses

Every memory carries a piece of a larger power. Collect the set, unlock the potential.

ENTRY_#010 2026.06.29 SET_BONUS_GUIDE

The Core Insight: Memory set bonuses are not optional extras — they are the foundation of your build. A complete 6-piece set bonus is worth roughly 15-20 levels worth of stats. Running mismatched memories means you're fighting with one hand tied behind your back. Understanding which sets to use, when to break them, and how to combine them is the key to endgame power.

How Memory Sets Work

Memories belong to sets. When you equip multiple memories from the same set, you unlock tiered bonuses:

2-Piece Bonus Small stat boost (e.g., +8% ATK)
4-Piece Bonus Medium effect (e.g., +15% Crit Rate)
6-Piece Bonus Large effect + unique mechanic

The 6-piece bonus is the real prize. It often unlocks a unique mechanic that defines the operative's playstyle — like Xavier's bonus light blade hit, or Rafayel's DoT extension. Without the 6-piece, you're playing a gimped version of your operative.

Important: Set bonuses only activate if the memories are equipped on the same operative. You cannot split a set across two operatives and get partial bonuses. Each operative's set bonus is calculated independently from their equipped memories.

Optimal Set Combinations

In endgame, you usually want a full 6-piece set for the unique mechanic. But there are situations where mixing sets is optimal:

When to Mix: Mixed sets are viable when: (a) you don't have a complete 6-piece yet, (b) the 6-piece bonus is weak for your playstyle, or (c) you need specific stat thresholds (e.g., 65% Crit Rate) that 4+2 provides better than 6.

4 Solar + 2 Shadow Xavier crit build. +12% CR + dodge refund.
4 Tidal + 2 Embers Rafayel hybrid. DoT +18% + 15% RS damage.
4 Permafrost + 2 Frostbloom Zayne freeze build. Max freeze uptime.
4 Shadow + 2 Solar Sylus hybrid. Energy refund + ATK%.
6 of any 5-star set The endgame goal. Unique mechanic unlocked.

Farming Priority

Memory set farming is a long-term investment. Here's the recommended order:

Phase 1 — Any 4-piece
Equip any matching 4-piece set on your main operative. Doesn't matter which — just get the bonus active.
Phase 2 — Correct 4-piece
Farm the right set for your main operative. Solar for Xavier, Permafrost for Zayne, etc.
Phase 3 — 6-piece main
Push your main operative to 6-piece for the unique mechanic. This is your biggest power spike.
Phase 4 — 4-piece for Team 2
Get your second Abyss team to 4-piece. Don't chase 6-piece here yet.
Phase 5 — 6-piece all teams
Endgame. All three teams at 6-piece with correct sets.

Most players get stuck in Phase 2-3 because they try to 6-piece their main before their second team has any set bonus. This is backwards. Two teams at 4-piece will clear more content than one team at 6-piece and the other at 0.

Set + Stellactrum Interaction

Memory set bonuses and Stellactrum matching are independent but complementary systems. Here's how they stack:

6-piece set bonus ~25-35% effective damage increase
6/6 Stellactrum match +60% ATK/HP (separate multiplier)
Combined 2.0-2.4x total multiplier

Priority Rule: Stellactrum matching comes first — it's a bigger raw stat bonus. Once you have 6/6 color match, then optimize your set bonus within those color constraints. A 4-piece set with 6/6 color match beats a 6-piece set with 2/6 match every time.

Memory Leveling for Set Optimization

Not all memories in a set are worth leveling equally. Here's how to prioritize:

PRIORITY 1 — MAIN STAT MEMORIES
Memories whose main stat is ATK%, Crit Rate, or Crit DMG are your highest value. Level these to max first, regardless of set. A correct main stat memory from the wrong set is better than a wrong main stat from the right set.
PRIORITY 2 — SET SLOT FILLERS
Once your main stat memories are leveled, fill the remaining slots with memories from the correct set. These "filler" memories don't need good substats — they just need to complete the set bonus. Level them to 40 to unlock the set effect.
PRIORITY 3 — SUBSTAT OPTIMIZATION
After your 6-piece is active, replace fillers with memories that have good substats (Crit Rate, Crit DMG, ATK%). This is the long tail of optimization — each replacement gives 2-5% more damage.

Character-Specific Set Recommendations

XAVIER

Best Set: Solar Resonance (6-piece)

Substats Priority: Crit Rate > Crit DMG > ATK% > Energy Regen

Budget Option: 4 Solar + 2 Embers (if missing 2 Solar pieces)

Why: Xavier's entire kit scales with Crit. The 6-piece light blade extra hit is 15-20% of his total damage. Without it, he's significantly weaker.

ZAYNE

Best Set: Permafrost Sovereign (6-piece)

Substats Priority: HP% > Energy Regen > Crit DMG > ATK%

Budget Option: 4 Permafrost + 2 Frostbloom

Why: Zayne scales with HP. The energy refund from the 6-piece is critical for his rotation — without it, he runs out of energy mid-combo and his DPS plummets.

RAFAYEL

Best Set: Tidal Wrath (6-piece)

Substats Priority: ATK% > Crit Rate > Crit DMG > Energy Regen

Budget Option: 4 Tidal + 2 Solar

Why: The DoT extension and Oath refund are Rafayel's core mechanics. The 6-piece turns him from a mediocre DPS into a sustained damage monster.

SYLUS

Best Set: Shadow Bind (6-piece)

Substats Priority: ATK% > Crit Rate > Energy Regen > Crit DMG

Budget Option: 4 Shadow + 2 Embers

Why: The extended Dark Energy state is transformative for Sylus. Without it, you spend half your time outside your damage state. With it, the uptime goes from 55% to 75%.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Leveling wrong-set memories with good substats. That perfect Crit Rate memory is tempting, but if it's from the wrong set, it's a trap. A correct-set memory with average substats will outperform it once the set bonus is active.

Mistake #2: Breaking 6-piece for a small stat gain. Replacing one piece of a 6-set with a "better" off-set piece is almost never worth it. The 6-piece bonus is worth roughly 30-40 substat rolls. One piece with slightly better substats (3-5 rolls) doesn't compensate.

Mistake #3: Farming 6-piece for every operative before building depth. Three operatives with 4-piece correct sets clear Abyss 12. One operative with 6-piece and two with mismatched sets often can't. Build breadth first, then depth.

Mistake #4: Ignoring 4-star sets. A 6-piece Frostbloom set on Zayne is better than a 2-piece Permafrost set. Don't bench 4-star sets just because they're lower rarity. They're placeholders that let you play the game while you farm 5-star pieces.

The Bottom Line: Memory sets are the scaffolding of your build. Start with any 4-piece. Upgrade to the correct 4-piece. Push to 6-piece for the unique mechanic. Then and only then worry about substats. Follow this order, and you'll never waste a single stamina point. The set bonus is not a luxury. It's the minimum requirement for endgame content.
