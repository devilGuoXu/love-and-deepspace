Common Tier List Mistakes

Mistake #1: Taking tier lists as absolute truth. A tier list reflects community consensus, not your account's specific needs. If your only SSR is Rafayel, you build around Rafayel 閳?don't bench him because he's A-tier. A-invested operative beats a S-tier operative with no memories.

Mistake #2: Ignoring rotation changes. Tier lists shift every Abyss rotation. The current meta favors Xavier (Light) because the boss lineup is weak to Light. Next rotation might favor Flame or Frost. Build depth, not flavor-of-the-month.

Mistake #3: Confusing "tier" with "usability." A C-tier operative on the correct element (matching boss weakness) outperforms an S-tier operative on a resisted element. Element advantage is worth more than any tier ranking. Always check the boss before choosing your team.

The Bottom Line: Tier lists are a starting point, not a destination. Xavier and Sylus share the top spot for different reasons. Zayne enables teams. Rafayel clears fast. Pull for your account's needs, not for the tier list. And remember: a well-built team with proper Stellactrum and element matching beats a misaligned "S-tier" team every time.
