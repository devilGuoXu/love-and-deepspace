COMPANION GUIDE — AFFINITY

Affinity Level-Up Guide

Affinity is the relationship system that unlocks dates, bond memories, voice lines, and account-wide stat bonuses. Here is how to raise it efficiently.

ENTRY_#COM_012026.07.02AFFINITY

Why Affinity Matters: Affinity is not just flavor — it directly gates bond memories (stat bonuses), Secret Times tracks, and date scenes. Every operative has the same affinity system, but the rewards are per-operative. Higher affinity = more permanent account stats.

Affinity System Overview

Affinity is increased by interacting with an operative through various activities. Each operative has an independent affinity level starting at 1 and currently capped at 150. The XP required per level increases as you go higher — early levels take 100-200 XP, while levels past 100 take 1,000+ XP each. Total XP to reach level 100 is approximately 25,000 XP per operative. Total XP to reach level 150 is approximately 60,000 XP per operative. At the current maximum rate of ~600 XP per day, reaching level 150 with one operative takes about 100 days of consistent interaction. This is a long-term system — there are no shortcuts.

Affinity XP Sources

Daily Gift (1 per operative per day): Preferred gift = 50 XP. Neutral gift = 25 XP. Disliked gift = 5 XP. Always give preferred gifts. Total: 50 XP/day per operative. Daily Interaction (Destiny Cafe): Tap the operative around the cafe to trigger a random interaction. 10 XP per interaction, up to 3 interactions per day per operative. Total: 30 XP/day. Daily Bounties: Completing bounties with an operative in the party grants 20 XP for that operative. Total: 60 XP/day (3 bounties x 20 XP). Memory Leveling: Leveling an operative's memory to certain breakpoints gives affinity XP. +10 XP per memory at levels 20, 40, 60. Secret Times: First listen of each track gives 100 XP. Subsequent listens give 5 XP. Tender Moments: Each ending completion gives 50-100 XP. Date Scenes: First completion of a date scene gives 150 XP. Total daily XP potential is 140 XP from daily activities plus bonus XP from memory and date activities.

Key Affinity Milestones

Level 10: Bond Memory Slot 1 unlocked. Date 1 unlocked. Level 25: Date 2 unlocked. New Destiny Cafe interactions. Level 30: Bond Memory Slot 2 unlocked. Level 35: Secret Times Track 2 unlocked. Level 50: Date 3 (romantic) unlocked. New voice lines. Level 55: Bond Memory Slot 3 unlocked. Level 60: Secret Times Track 3 unlocked. Level 80: Date 4 unlocked. New cafe outfit variant. Level 85: Bond Memory Slot 4 unlocked. Level 90: Secret Times Track 4 unlocked. Level 100: Date 5 (final) unlocked. Exclusive name card. Level 120: Bond Memory Slot 5 unlocked. Level 130: Secret Times Track 5 unlocked. Level 150: Exclusive voice line + profile frame. The most important milestones for gameplay are level 30 (Bond Slot 2 = more stat bonuses) and level 85 (Bond Slot 4 = requires limited SSR). Plan your gift spending around these breakpoints.

Efficiency Strategy: Focus one operative to level 100 before starting another. Spreading gifts across all four operatives delays everyone's milestone unlocks. The optimal path: Xavier to 100 for the +2% ATK bond bonus, then Zayne to 100, then Rafayel, then Sylus. By the time you finish all four, you will have +8% ATK and +5% CRIT DMG account-wide from bond completions. Use preferred gifts only. Buy gift boxes from the Crew Shop daily. Save event-limited gifts for high-affinity thresholds (80+) where the XP requirements spike.

Common Mistakes

Mistake 1 — Spreading gifts evenly: Giving one gift to each operative daily means no one reaches milestones quickly. Focus fire one operative. Mistake 2 — Ignoring Destiny Cafe interactions: The 3 daily taps are free XP that many players forget. Set a reminder if needed. Mistake 3 — Using non-preferred gifts: A neutral gift gives half the XP of a preferred gift. A disliked gift gives almost nothing. Always save up for preferred gifts from the shop. Mistake 4 — Skipping bounties: 60 XP per day from bounties adds up to 1,800 XP per month per operative. Do not skip them. Mistake 5 — Not reading new content: Each new bond memory, Secret Times track, and Tender Moment gives a big one-time XP bonus. Unlock and consume all new content as it becomes available.

Summary: Affinity is a long-term system that gates bond memories, stat bonuses, and date content. Each operative needs ~25,000 XP to reach 100 and ~60,000 XP to reach 150. Daily sources give ~140 XP per operative. Focus one operative at a time. Use preferred gifts only. Complete daily interactions and bounties. Unlock and consume new content for bonus XP. The +8% ATK and +5% CRIT DMG from bond completions make affinity farming a high-priority long-term goal.
