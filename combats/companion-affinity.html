Affinity Overview

The Affinity system tracks the relationship between the Hunter and each of the four operatives: Xavier, Zayne, Rafayel, and Sylus. Affinity levels are independent per operative, starting at level 1 and currently capping at level 120. Each level requires a specific amount of Affinity EXP, with the curve steepening significantly after level 50. Affinity is earned through daily interactions, gifts, combat, and story progression, with most sources having daily or weekly caps that limit how fast you can progress.

Affinity is not just flavor content — it gates meaningful gameplay features. Memory slots unlock at specific Affinity thresholds (6 memory slots require Affinity 30+), certain equipment types are locked behind Affinity levels, and the ability to unlock Tender Moments and Secret Times requires minimum Affinity levels. Additionally, each Affinity milestone awards gems, gold, and exclusive base decorations that cannot be obtained elsewhere.

Affinity Sources

Daily Greeting (60 EXP/day)
Each operative can be greeted once per day for 60 Affinity EXP. The greeting is a simple interaction that takes 10 seconds. This is the highest EXP-per-minute activity in the game. Never miss your daily greetings — 60 EXP/day = 1,800 EXP/month per operative.
Gift Giving (Variable EXP)
Each operative can receive 5 gifts per day. Gift EXP varies by quality: Common (10 EXP), Uncommon (25 EXP), Rare (60 EXP), and Epic (150 EXP). Matching gift preferences doubles the EXP. Epic preferred gifts are the most efficient EXP source but are expensive. Budget players should use Rare preferred gifts.
Combat Affinity
Clearing stages with an operative in your party earns Affinity EXP. The amount scales with stamina cost: 5 EXP per 10 stamina spent. This means your daily stamina (240 natural regen) can earn up to 120 Affinity EXP per operative if you use them exclusively. Not the fastest source but passive — you're getting combat done anyway.
Story & Memory Rewards
Reading Tender Moments, completing Secret Times episodes, and progressing main story chapters award one-time Affinity EXP bonuses. These are significant (500-2,000 EXP each) but are non-repeatable. Save these for when you need a push to reach a specific milestone.

Milestone Rewards

Key Affinity milestones and their rewards: Level 10 (50 gems, title unlock), Level 20 (100 gems, exclusive base item), Level 30 (150 gems, 6th memory slot), Level 40 (200 gems, 4th gift slot), Level 50 (250 gems, exclusive outfit piece), Level 60 (300 gems, 5th gift slot), Level 80 (400 gems, exclusive portrait frame), Level 100 (500 gems, ultimate title), Level 120 (600 gems, exclusive base decoration).

Operative Priority

For most players, prioritizing one main operative to Affinity 50+ provides the best returns. The 6th memory slot at level 30 is the critical early milestone — every player should get at least one operative to 30 as fast as possible. Once your main operative is past 30, raise all operatives to at least 20 for the gem rewards and base items, then focus on your main again toward 50+.

Tip: Don't hoard gift boxes. The daily gift cap (5 per operative) means you can never "catch up" on missed gift days. Use your gift boxes daily even if you're saving for a specific operative's birthday or event. Sitting on unopened gift boxes is the same as throwing away Affinity EXP.

Summary: Affinity progression is a daily commitment that rewards consistency over bursts. Greet your operative daily (60 free EXP), give 5 preferred gifts (use Rare tier for efficiency), and spend your stamina with your target operative. Get one operative to Affinity 30 for the 6th memory slot, then build evenly across all operatives to 20 before pushing your main to 50+. The gem rewards alone make Affinity one of the best value activities in Love and Deepspace.
