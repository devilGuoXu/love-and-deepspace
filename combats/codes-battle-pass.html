Battle Pass Value Analysis

Every tier of the Love and Deepspace Battle Pass analyzed — F2P vs paid track rewards, value per dollar, and when it is worth buying.

Battle Pass Structure

The Love and Deepspace Battle Pass runs in 6-week cycles (aligned with version updates). It has three tiers: Free (no purchase — 20 reward levels), Premium Pass ($9.99 — 50 reward levels with enhanced rewards), and Premium Pass + ($14.99 — all Premium Pass rewards + 10 bonus levels + exclusive items). Each tier has the same progression mechanism — earn BP EXP through daily missions (100 EXP/day), weekly missions (500 EXP/week), and event missions (variable, 200-500 EXP per event). Each BP level requires 1,000 EXP to unlock.

The BP cycle is designed so that consistent daily play (20-30 minutes/day) reaches level 50 by week 4-5, leaving 1-2 weeks of buffer. Inconsistent players who miss days will struggle to reach 50. The BP also has a "catch-up" mechanic: you can spend 100 gems per level to buy levels directly (up to 10 levels per cycle). This costs 1,000 gems to catch up 10 levels — which is still less than the value of the BP rewards, making it worthwhile if you are close to a major reward threshold. Never buy more than 10 levels — the cost exceeds the value after that point.

Free Track Rewards

The free track offers 20 levels of rewards to all players. While significantly less valuable than the paid track, the free track still provides meaningful resources: 5 Limited Pull Tickets (levels 5, 10, 15, 19, 20 — total value 750 gems), 200,000 Gold (levels 2, 7, 12, 17), 50 Protocore Dust (levels 3, 8, 13, 18), 100 Skill Materials (levels 4, 9, 14), 30 Ascension Crystals (levels 6, 11, 16), and 1 SR Memory Selection Box (level 20). Total free track value: approximately 1,500 gems equivalent — all completely free with gameplay.

The free track alone makes the BP worth completing even for F2P players. The 5 Limited Pull Tickets are the highlight — they represent 5 pulls toward any banner without spending a single gem. The gold, dust, and materials cover approximately 1 week of domain farming. The SR selection box at level 20 can fill a gap in your collection or provide materials for ascending an existing SR. Never skip BP missions as an F2P player — the free track rewards are significant and require no gem or dollar investment. Treat BP leveling as a weekly priority alongside weekly boss clears.

Paid Track Rewards

Purchasing the Premium Pass ($9.99) unlocks 30 additional levels and enhances all existing rewards. Key Paid Track rewards: 10 additional Limited Pull Tickets (total 15 across both tracks), 500,000 Gold (total 700K), 150 Protocore Dust (total 200), 300 Skill Materials (total 400), 100 Ascension Crystals (total 130), 1 SSR Protocore Selection Box (level 50 — choose any main stat), 1 Exclusive Nameplate (level 30 — cosmetic), 1 Exclusive Furniture Item (level 45 — cosmetic), and 1 Memory Set Chest (level 40 — choose any 2-piece set).

The SSR Protocore Selection Box at level 50 is the single most valuable item in the BP. An SSR protocore with a guaranteed correct main stat (e.g., CRIT Rate on Slot 4, Elemental Damage on Slot 5) is worth approximately 500 gems in domain farming value. But more importantly, it removes the RNG from protocore acquisition — you get exactly the piece you need. This box alone justifies the BP purchase for players who are stuck on a specific slot. The Memory Set Chest at level 40 is the second most valuable item — it lets you complete a 4-piece set without farming the domain for weeks.

BP reward math: Total paid track rewards have a gem-equivalent value of approximately 8,000-9,000, depending on how you value the exclusive items. At a cost of $9.99 (approximately 660 gems if bought directly), the BP returns 12-14x the gem value. However, this assumes you value all resources. If you only care about pulls and protocores, the value is approximately 5,000 gems = 7.5x returns. Still excellent value. The BP is the second-best purchase after the Monthly Card (which returns 15x value). If you spend any money on LADS, the Monthly Card + Battle Pass are the optimal combination.

Premium Diamond Track

The Premium Diamond Track ($14.99 — also called BP+) includes everything from the Premium Pass plus: 10 bonus BP levels (skip straight to level 10), and 1,200 premium currency (gems) awarded at levels 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 (240 gems each). The $5 price increase over the standard Premium Pass returns 1,200 gems (which would cost approximately $12 if bought directly). This makes the Diamond Track's bonus content worth approximately 2.4x its $5 cost — making it an excellent upgrade if you are already buying the Premium Pass.

However, the Diamond Track has a catch: the 1,200 gems are tied to BP level progression. You only get the gems if you reach level 50. If you reach level 50 anyway (which most premium buyers do), the Diamond Track is a no-brainer upgrade. If you are uncertain about reaching level 50, buy the standard Premium Pass first and upgrade to Diamond Track later (the upgrade is available until the last week of the BP cycle). Never buy the Diamond Track upfront if you are unsure about your playtime — buy it at level 50 when all gems are confirmed.

Payment strategy: Optimal BP purchasing: Start the cycle as F2P. Play normally for 2 weeks. If you have reached level 25-30 by week 2 (indicating you will reach level 50 comfortably), purchase the Premium Pass ($9.99). When you reach level 50, evaluate: do you want the extra 1,200 gems? If yes, upgrade to Diamond Track for another $5. This phased approach minimizes risk — you only spend money after confirming you can unlock all the rewards. Never buy the BP on day 1 unless you are certain about your 6-week play schedule.

Value Comparison

Comparing Battle Pass value to other purchases in the game: Monthly Card ($4.99) = 15x value (3,000 gems total over 30 days). Premium BP ($9.99) = 8-12x value (8,000-9,000 gem equivalent). Diamond BP upgrade ($5.00) = 4x value (1,200 gems directly). Top-up packs (first purchase bonuses) = 3x value. Direct gem purchases (no bonus) = 1x value (baseline). Skin/outfit purchases = value varies by personal preference (0x if you do not care about cosmetics, infinite if you love the outfit).

Leveling Guide

To reach BP level 50 in 6 weeks, you need 50,000 total BP EXP (50 levels × 1,000 EXP each). Daily missions provide 100 EXP × 42 days = 4,200 EXP. Weekly missions provide 500 EXP × 6 weeks = 3,000 EXP. This leaves 42,800 EXP to earn from event missions (approximately 8,000-10,000 per cycle) and the remainder from repeatable missions (20 EXP per domain run, unlimited). The repeatable missions are where consistent players pull ahead — 10 domain runs per day (450 stamina) = 200 EXP per day from repeats. Over 42 days: 8,400 EXP. With events + repeats, a daily player earns approximately 1,200-1,500 EXP/day, reaching level 50 in approximately 33-40 days.

Event missions give 200-500 EXP each. They overlap with event participation — you get BP EXP for playing events. Do not go out of your way for event missions; they are auto-completed by normal event play.

Repeatable domain runs
If you are behind schedule (below level 30 by week 4), run more domains. 20 EXP per run. 10 extra runs per day = 200 EXP. 3 days of extra runs = 1 BP level. Use stamina pots if needed.

Leveling shortcut: If you are at level 45+ with 1 week remaining, you will naturally reach level 50 through daily missions alone (700 EXP from dailies + 500 from weeklies + event missions = enough for the last 5 levels). Do not buy level skips in this situation. Only buy levels if you are below level 35 with 1 week remaining — at that point, natural EXP cannot catch up, and buying 5-10 levels (500-1,000 gems) is still cheaper than the value you would lose by not reaching level 50 (which includes the SSR protocore box worth 500+ gems and 10+ pull tickets).

When to Buy & Skip

Buy the Premium BP if: You play daily (20+ minutes), you can reach level 50 (assess your first 2 weeks), you need the resources (mid-game player, level 40-60), and you already have the Monthly Card. The BP + Monthly Card combination is the optimal spending strategy for dolphin players ($15/month total).

Skip the BP if: You are a strict F2P player (the free track is still worth completing — do not skip missions even if you do not buy), you play irregularly (cannot commit to daily play for 6 weeks), you are an endgame player with maxed resources (the BP resources become less valuable when you have stockpiled months of materials), or you only care about pulls (buy the Monthly Card instead — it provides more pulls per dollar for pure gem income).

Consider the Diamond Track if: You always reach BP level 50, you want the extra 1,200 gems (8 pulls), and you already buy the Premium BP. The Diamond Track upgrade is the most efficient way to convert $5 into gems at a 4x value ratio. However, if $5 matters to you, skip the Diamond Track — the 1,200 gems are not essential for progression. They accelerate banner pulling by approximately 1 week of savings.

Long-term value perspective: Over one year (8-9 BP cycles), purchasing the Premium BP each cycle costs approximately $80-90. Total returns: approximately 120 Limited Pull Tickets, 5.6M gold, 1,600 protocore dust, 8 SSR protocore boxes, 8 memory set chests, and 8 exclusive cosmetics. This is the most resource-efficient way to build an account alongside the Monthly Card. A player who buys Monthly Card + BP for one year will have a significantly stronger account than a player who spends the same $100-150 on direct gem packs. The compound effect of consistent resource income over time is substantial.
