Material Drop Types

Wanderer drops fall into three categories. Understanding the tier determines what you can expect per kill and whether resin is worth spending on that specific route.

Common Drops — Guaranteed Per Kill

Wanderer Core Shards100% — 1–3 per kill (type-dependent)
Elemental Residue80% — 1 per kill, 20% chance of 2
Dimensional Essence60% — 1 per kill (Void Spawn only)
Corruption Crystals70% — 1–2 (Corrupted / Phantom types)

Rare Drops — Probability Based

Upgrade Gem (Green)25% — Elite+ wanderers
Upgrade Gem (Blue)10% — Commander / Ancient types
Memory Shard Fragment5% — Any wanderer, any rank
Protocore Dust15% — Elemental / Elite wanderers

Boss Drops — Guaranteed From Field Bosses

Boss Core100% — 1 per kill (Abyssal Siren, Frost Tyrant, etc.)
Memory Shard (Full)100% — 1 per weekly boss kill
Rare Upgrade Gem (Purple)100% — 1 per boss kill
Stellactrum Fragment50% — 1 bonus drop chance

Common drops are the backbone of material crafting. Rare drops are the bottleneck for late-game upgrades. Boss drops are time-gated — you get 3 per week per boss. Never skip your weekly boss kills.

Drop Rate Boost Events

Event windows dramatically improve loot efficiency. Plan your resin spending around these calendars.

Double Drop Events

Runs approximately once per month. Doubles all common drops (Core Shards, Elemental Residue) and adds a +50% bonus to rare drops. The event lasts 72 hours and applies to all overworld wanderer kills. Stack your resin refreshes for this window. A typical 72-hour double-drop window yields the same loot as 144 hours of normal farming.

Event-Specific Materials

Limited-time events often introduce cursed or corrupted wanderer variants that drop exclusive materials. These materials cannot be farmed outside the event window and are required for event-exclusive upgrades. Always prioritise event wanderers over standard farming — event materials rarely return.

Maximising Boosted Windows

During a boost event, run your lowest-efficiency routes first (Ruins of Aethel, Abyss Depths) since the multiplier applies proportionally more to rare drops. Save high-efficiency routes (Twilight Corridor) for outside the event window. Use condensed resin if available — double-drop events double the value of condensed resin kills. Never run boss fights during double-drop events; boss drops are unaffected by the multiplier.

Pro Tip: Most double-drop events overlap with stamina-reset login bonuses. Log in at reset time, collect the bonus stamina, spend it immediately, then refresh at the 36-hour mark before the event ends. This gives you 3 full stamina bars inside the boosted window.

Material Conversion Guide

Not all drops are equally useful at every stage of progression. Conversion lets you trade surplus materials for the ones you actually need.

Conversion Ratios

Core Shards → Upgrade Gem (Green)10 : 1
Green Gem → Blue Gem5 : 1
Blue Gem → Purple Gem3 : 1
Elemental Residue → Any Element3 : 1 (same tier)
Memory Shard Fragments → Full Shard5 : 1
Protocore Dust → Protocore Essence10 : 1

When to Convert vs Farm Directly

Convert when: You have >200 surplus Core Shards and need Green Gems for ascension. The conversion tax (10:1) is painful but faster than farming Elites for a specific gem colour. You have off-element Residue that you'll never use — convert it to the element you need.

Farm directly when: You need Purple Gems. The 3:1 conversion from Blue to Purple is costly (15 Green → 3 Blue → 1 Purple = 150 Core Shards for one Purple gem). You're better off farming Ancients or Abyssal Guardians who drop Purple Gems at 5% base rate. You need Memory Shards for a specific operative — conversion is random. Farm the specific wanderer family that drops that operative's shards.

Never convert: Boss Cores. They are time-gated and have no conversion path. Every boss core is precious.

Summary: Common drops fuel early-mid progression. Rare drops are the endgame bottleneck. Convert surpluses only when you have a material surplus ratio >5:1. Farm route efficiency: Twilight Corridor > Shadowed Copse > Ember Basin > Conjurer's Tower > Ruins of Aethel > Abyss Depths. Save your resin boosters for double-drop events and run the rare-drop routes during boosted windows.
