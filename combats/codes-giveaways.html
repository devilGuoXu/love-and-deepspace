Livestream Codes

Developer livestreams are the most consistent source of giveaway codes. The official Love and Deepspace YouTube channel hosts streams for major version updates, anniversary celebrations, and seasonal events. Codes are typically announced 15-30 minutes into the stream and displayed on screen for 30-60 seconds. The code is then posted in the stream chat and later on Twitter/X.

Livestream codes have the shortest expiration windows — typically 24-48 hours from the end of the stream. The codes are single-use per account and usually worth 100-200 gems plus materials. To maximize your chance of catching livestream codes: subscribe to the YouTube channel with notifications enabled, check the community tab for stream schedules, and join the stream within the first 15 minutes to catch the first code drop.

Tournament & Event Codes

Special tournament events and community competitions sometimes produce exclusive codes. The Love and Deepspace Abyss Speedrun Championship (held quarterly) awards participation codes to all competitors and viewer codes during the live broadcast. The Community Creator Showcase (monthly) features fan content and offers viewer codes during the presentation stream. Regional fan meetups and conventions also distribute exclusive physical cards with redemption codes.

These codes are the rarest and most valuable. Tournament participation codes might be worth 300+ gems plus exclusive titles or base decorations. Viewer codes during broadcasts are smaller but more accessible. If you're skilled at Abyss content, consider competing in the speedrun events for the participation rewards alone.

Content Creator Codes

Love and Deepspace partners with content creators (streamers, YouTubers, artists) who occasionally receive codes to distribute to their audiences. These codes are typically worth 50-100 gems and are valid for 1-2 weeks. Creator codes are usually shared during streams or as community post rewards.

Finding creator codes requires following the creator's channels. Major Love and Deepspace content creators announce codes on their Discord servers or during their streams. Codes are typically limited in quantity (first-come, first-served) or have a per-account cap. These are lower value than developer codes but are more accessible due to longer validity periods.

In-Game Event Rewards

Not all event rewards come through codes — many are distributed directly through in-game events. Limited-time web events often award gems and materials for completing social media tasks. In-game mail campaigns distribute anniversary rewards and compensation for maintenance periods. Pre-registration campaigns for major updates reward codes that are sent via email before the update drops.

Pre-registration rewards are particularly valuable. Before each major version update, the official site hosts a pre-registration page where players can register their UID in advance. Upon reaching milestone registration numbers, all registered players receive code rewards via email. These codes typically award 100-200 gems plus limited-time items and have a 2-4 week validity window.

Event Calendar

Predictable giveaway events: Developer Livestreams (every major update, approximately every 6-8 weeks), Anniversary (January, biggest code drops of the year), Summer Festival (June-August, multiple code waves), Winter Festival (December, seasonal code + holiday event codes), Halloween (October, small code), and Valentine's Day (February, romantic-themed rewards). Tournament events occur quarterly but on variable schedules.

Tip: Create a secondary Twitter account that follows ONLY the Love and Deepspace official account and the top 5 content creators. Use this account as a dedicated codes feed — mute all other accounts and keywords. This creates a notification stream that contains nothing but code announcements. You'll never miss a code drop due to notification clutter.

Summary: Giveaway and event codes are the highest-value but most time-sensitive rewards in Love and Deepspace. Livestream codes drop during developer streams and expire in 24-48 hours. Tournament and creator codes are rarer but offer premium rewards. Pre-registration for major updates yields reliable code rewards. The key to catching all codes is notification management: follow official Twitter/X with push notifications enabled, watch livestreams live, and join the community Discord codes channel.
