Community Giveaways Guide

Giveaways are a fun way to earn extra rewards and connect with the community. This guide covers official and fan-run giveaways, entry methods, and winning strategies.

ENTRY_#CDE_052026.07.02GIVEAWAYS

Types of Giveaways: Giveaways in Love and Deepspace fall into three categories: official Papergames giveaways, content creator giveaways, and community-run giveaways. Each type has different entry requirements and prize pools.

Official Papergames Giveaways

Official giveaways are hosted by the development team through social media channels. Entry Methods: Retweet + follow (Twitter), join + react (Discord), subscribe + comment (YouTube), follow + tag friends (Instagram). Typical Prizes: Grand prize: 10,000 diamonds + SSR selector + exclusive merch. Runner-up: 5,000 diamonds + limited pulls. Participation: 100-300 diamonds for all eligible entries. Frequency: Major events (anniversary, summer, winter) and follower milestones. How to find: Follow @LoveDeepspace_EN on Twitter. Join the official Discord and check #giveaways channel. Subscribe to the official YouTube channel. Tips for official giveaways: Read the terms carefully. Some giveaways are region-restricted. Complete all entry requirements — missing one step disqualifies you. If the giveaway requires tagging friends, tag friends who actually play the game for better engagement metrics. Official giveaways often have large prize pools but also large entry numbers (10,000+ entrants). Focus on giveaways with multiple winner tiers rather than single-grand-prize giveaways for better odds.

Content Creator Giveaways

Popular content creators often host giveaways for their communities. Where to find: YouTube (guide creators, lore channels), Twitch (streamers who specialize in Deepspace), TikTok (short-form content creators), Twitter fan accounts. Typical Prizes: Game currency (500-2,000 diamonds), battle pass gifts, merchandise, art commissions. Entry Methods: Subscribe to the channel, follow the stream, comment on a video, join the creator's Discord server. Tips for creator giveaways: These have smaller entry pools (100-1,000 entrants) so your odds are significantly better. Support creators you genuinely enjoy — the community aspect is part of the experience. Join creator Discord servers — they often announce giveaways there before posting publicly. Some creators do monthly giveaways for active community members. Being an engaged community member increases your chances of being selected for "community choice" giveaways. Creator highlight: Several dedicated Deepspace creators run weekly giveaway events on Twitch during their streams. Tune in during giveaway times for a chance to win.

Community-Run Giveaways

Fan-run giveaways organized by community groups, fan sites, and Discord servers. Where to find: Fan site Discords, Reddit community events, dedicated giveaway Twitter accounts, fan art group challenges. Typical Prizes: Smaller than official giveaways: 100-500 diamonds, name cards, profile frames, art commissions. Entry Methods: Participate in community events, create fan content, refer new players, engage in community discussions. Legitimacy Check: Only participate in community giveaways from trusted sources. Red flags: Asking for your account password (never share this). Asking for payment to enter. Prizes that seem too good to be true (10,000 diamonds from an unverified account). Links to suspicious websites. Stick to giveaways hosted by known community figures, verified fan sites, or moderators of official Discord servers.

Winning Strategy: (1) Enter every giveaway you qualify for — volume increases odds. (2) Focus on giveaways with multiple winners rather than single-prize pools. (3) Participate in content creator giveaways — smaller entry pools = better odds. (4) Be active in community Discord servers — some giveaways require a minimum activity level. (5) Create fan content (art, guides, videos) — many giveaways have creator categories with separate prize pools. (6) Enter during off-peak times. Giveaways announced on weekends get more entries than weekday announcements. (7) Set up a dedicated email and social media account for giveaway entries. (8) Use a spreadsheet to track: giveaway name, entry date, prize, winner announcement date. Following up on winners who do not claim prizes within 24 hours — some giveaways redraw unclaimed prizes.

Prize Claiming Guide

If you win a giveaway: (1) The host will contact you through the entry platform (usually DM or @mention). (2) Provide your in-game UID (Settings > Account > UID). Never provide your password. (3) Prizes are typically sent within 3-7 business days. (4) In-game currency prizes are sent through the in-game mail system. (5) Merchandise prizes require providing a shipping address. Only provide this to official Papergames accounts. (6) If you do not hear back within 7 days of the winner announcement, follow up with the host. (7) Some giveaways have a claim window (e.g., 48 hours to respond). Check your messages regularly after entering. Tax Implications: For large merchandise prizes (value over $100), you may need to pay import duties depending on your country. Official giveaways usually cover shipping costs. Community giveaways may not. Check the terms before entering.

Summary: Three giveaway types: official (large pools, many entrants), content creator (smaller pools, better odds), community-run (smallest pools, best odds but verify legitimacy). Entry methods: retweets, follows, comments, subscriptions, community participation. Strategy: enter all giveaways, focus on multi-winner pools, engage with creator communities, track your entries. Never share your account password. Claim prizes within 48 hours of winning. Giveaways are a fun bonus — treat them as a community activity rather than a primary reward source. The diamonds you win are a nice extra, but consistent daily play is the real path to account growth.
