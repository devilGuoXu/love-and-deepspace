Why Event Planning Matters

Stamina is currency. Every event converts your stamina into rewards — but the exchange rate varies wildly. A well-planned event run can yield 3x the account value per stamina compared to an unplanned one. The difference between a player who plans and one who wings it is thousands of stamina saved per month. This guide gives you the framework to make those calculations yourself.

Events are the primary source of free resources in Love and Deepspace. Between limited-time shops, event-exclusive memory boxes, and discounted stamina packs, an average event cycle delivers 2,000-5,000 free gem equivalents in raw value. The catch: you have limited stamina, limited time, and limited currency. Spending on the wrong items — or farming the wrong stages — can cost you weeks of progress.

The planner below treats event participation as an optimization problem. You have constraints (stamina, time, team strength) and you want to maximize value (limited items, account progression resources). By converting every reward to a common stamina-equivalent value, you can compare apples to oranges and make data-driven spending decisions.

Event Type Taxonomy

Not all events are created equal. Understanding the four major event types helps you prepare the right resources and set realistic expectations for what you can earn.

⚔
Combat Events
Stamina gated — need built teams. High reward density but require leveled memories and protocores. Examples: Bounty Blitz, Abyss cycles. Typically last 14 days, cost 120-200 stamina daily for max rewards. Best source of limited protocore boxes and SSR memory shards.
📖
Story Events
Time gated — minimal stamina, high narrative rewards. These unlock lore, companion bond levels, and limited-time story memories. Examples: Main story updates, character milestone events. Low farming pressure — focus on reading and one-time clear rewards.
🎮
Mini-Game Events
Skill gated — no stamina cost, limited daily attempts. Examples: Photography contests, rhythm games, racing minigames. Pure profit — zero stamina investment for free rewards. The catch: limited attempts per day means you must log in consistently. Missed days are lost value.
⚙
Grind Events
Stamina sink — unlimited farm, diminishing returns. Examples: Double drop campaigns, material blitz. These events let you farm unlimited stages with bonus rewards, but the marginal value per run drops as you deplete the bonus pool. Stop when the bonus pool runs dry.

Preparation varies by type: Combat events demand pre-built teams and saved stamina reserves. Story events require nothing but time. Mini-game events punish missed logins. Grind events are the most stamina-efficient for account resources but offer no exclusive items. Plan your preparation proportional to the event type's value density.

Reward Value Calculator

To evaluate whether an event reward is worth farming, convert everything to a common currency: stamina equivalence. This tells you how much stamina you would normally spend to obtain that item outside of the event. If the event offers it at a discount, the event is efficient.

Stamina Equivalent Reference Table
1 Stamina (baseline)= 1.2k Credits
SSR Memory Shard (1)= 60 Stamina
Protocore Box (1)= 40 Stamina
Purple Enhancement Ore (1)= 8 Stamina
Blue Enhancement Ore (1)= 4 Stamina
Green Enhancement Ore (1)= 2 Stamina
EXP Bottle (large)= 10 Stamina
Memory Ascension Material (blue)= 12 Stamina
Wish Crate (1)= 150 Stamina

How to use this table: When an event shop offers a Purple Enhancement Ore for 10 event currency, and farming that currency costs 1 stamina per 2 currency, the effective cost is 5 stamina per ore. Since the ore is worth 8 stamina, you save 3 stamina per ore — a 37.5% discount. Apply this logic to every item in the event shop. Buy items with the highest discount percentage first.

The trap: some event shops offer items that appear discounted but require farming inefficient stages. Always cross-reference the stamina cost of the event currency against your standard farming routes. If the event stage has worse drop rates than your best domain, the "discount" is an illusion. Use domain drop rate data from the community spreadsheets to validate event stage efficiency before committing stamina.

Quick rule of thumb: If an event item costs less than 75% of its stamina equivalent, it is worth buying. If the price is 75-100%, consider buying only if you need the item now. Above 100% of stamina equivalent — skip it unless you have excess event currency with nothing better to spend on.

Limited Shop Priority

Event shops are filled with items of wildly different value. This tier list tells you what to buy first and what to skip. Always check the current event's specific item list against this framework.

STIER S — MUST BUY
SSR Memory Boxes • Limited Memories • Unique Cosmetics
These items are time-limited and cannot be obtained after the event ends. SSR memory boxes alone are worth 60 stamina per shard plus the gacha value of a guaranteed SSR. Unique cosmetics (outfits, accessories, namecards) never return in most cases. Budget your entire event currency around clearing S-tier items first. If you can only buy one category, this is it. Example: the Pulse Hunter SSR crate from limited events — worth thousands of gems in gacha value.
ATIER A — HIGH VALUE
Enhancement Ores • Protocore Boxes • Stamina Refills
These are the items you will need in bulk every patch. Enhancement ores and protocore boxes have fixed stamina equivalent values and are almost always sold at a 20-40% discount in event shops. Stamina refills are particularly strong — they let you convert event currency into more stamina, which you then use to farm other items. This creates a positive feedback loop. Buy A-tier items after clearing S-tier, until the discount drops below 20%.
BTIER B — CONDITIONAL
Credits • EXP Bottles • Memory Shards (Standard)
Buy these only if you have remaining event currency after S and A tiers. Credits and EXP bottles are plentiful from daily farming and domain runs. The event discount on these is typically small (10-15%). They are not worth dedicating stamina to farm specifically. However, if you have leftover currency from S/A purchases, these items are better than nothing — they still represent a small stamina saving over domain farming.
CTIER C — SKIP
Character Fragments (R/SR) • Low-Grade Materials
R and SR character fragments are available from permanent content and have very low stamina value. Low-grade enhancement materials (green ores, basic ascension mats) drop in abundance from normal play. These items are bait — they look cheap but offer minimal account value. The only exception is if you are a new player who desperately needs any materials to progress. In that case, treat C-tier as a desperation buy, not a strategic purchase.

Event Timeline Planner

Follow this 5-step process for every major event. It takes 15 minutes at the start of the event and saves you hours of inefficient farming.

1
Read the event preview
Before the event launches, read the patch notes or community preview. Identify the S-tier rewards. Note the limited shop items, any new memory banners, and the event type. This tells you whether to save stamina or spend it before the event starts.
2
Calculate total currency needed
Sum the cost of all S-tier and A-tier items you want. Add a 10% buffer. This is your target currency number. Divide by the event duration to get your daily currency target. Example: need 12,000 currency over 14 days = ~857 currency per day.
3
Determine daily stamina budget
Calculate the stamina required per day to hit your currency target. Factor in natural stamina regen (240/day at max rank), daily log-in stamina, and any free stamina from event missions. If the required stamina exceeds your natural + free stamina, you need to use reserves or adjust expectations.
4
Identify free currency sources
Events always provide free currency through daily missions, achievement milestones, and first-time clear bonuses. These sources often cover 30-50% of your total currency needs. Do not start farming the event stage until you have exhausted all free currency sources — you might need less stamina than you think.
5
Monitor progress mid-event
At the 50% mark, check your currency progress. Are you ahead of or behind the daily target? If behind, you may need to increase your stamina budget. If ahead, you can redirect stamina to domain farming. Mid-event adjustments prevent last-minute panic farming on the final day.

If an event preview does not mention exclusive memories, you can safely spend wish crates on other banners. Coordinate with the banner schedule to maximize limited-time value.

Preparation Checklist

Before any major event, run through this checklist. Being prepared can mean the difference between clearing the shop and leaving value on the table.

1Save 3+ days of natural stamina reserve
Stop using stamina refills 3 days before a known event start. Let your stamina cap accumulate. At max rank, this gives you ~720 stamina in reserve (natural regen + mailbox stamina). Some events release with no warning, so keeping 1-2 days of reserve stamina at all times is a good habit.
2Pre-farm event materials
If the event preview says "requires Memory Ascension materials" or "needs specific element protocores," farm those before the event starts. During the event, material prices in the domain increase due to player demand in co-op modes. Pre-farming saves you stamina and frustration.
3Ensure teams can clear combat stages
Combat events have stage level requirements. If the event preview says "recommended level 50+", make sure your team is at least level 45 with +6 protocores. Under-leveled teams waste stamina on failed attempts or low-score clears. Use the DPS Calculator (WRK_03) to check if your team meets the DPS threshold for the event's hardest stage.
4Save wish crates for limited memories
If the event coincides with a limited memory banner, you want your wish crates ready. Do not open crates for standard memories 2 weeks before a major event — the new limited memory might be a meta-defining SSR. Check the banner schedule on the official social media channels 1 week before patch day.
5Plan gem spending for exclusive shop items
Some event shops offer items that cost gems (not event currency). These are typically cosmetic items, namecards, or exclusive titles. Decide your gem budget in advance — 500 gems for a limited title you love is fine; 500 gems for a title you will never equip is wasted. Treat gem-based event purchases as luxury items and budget them separately from the stamina-to-currency optimization.

Summary: Event planning converts stamina into consistent account growth. Convert all rewards to stamina equivalent. Prioritize S-tier (limited exclusives) > A-tier (ores, protocores, stamina refills) > B-tier (credits, EXP) > C-tier (skip). Follow the 5-step timeline for every major event. Historical patterns show 6,000-10,000 free gems per month from events. Preparation checklist: save 720 stamina reserve, pre-farm needed materials, ensure team strength, save wish crates, and budget gem spend. A player who plans their event participation gains 2-3x the account value of a player who does not — the effort-to-reward ratio is the best in the game.
