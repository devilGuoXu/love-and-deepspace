EXPAND WANDERER — CHEAT SHEET

Quick Weakness Cheat Sheet

Every wanderer has an elemental weakness. Exploiting it doubles your damage and triggers weakness break. This cheat sheet covers every wanderer type.

ENTRY_#WDR_102026.07.02CHEAT SHEET

Weakness System Basics: Each wanderer has one elemental weakness. Attacking with the matching element deals 2x damage and fills the weakness break meter. When the meter is full, the wanderer is stunned for 4 seconds and takes 1.5x damage from all sources for 8 seconds.

Common Wanderer Weaknesses

These wanderers appear in most regions and domains: Shadow Hound: Weak to Light (Xavier). No secondary weakness. Break threshold: 5 light attacks. Rift Shade: Weak to Fire (Rafayel). No secondary weakness. Break threshold: 4 fire attacks. Flame Imp: Weak to Ice (Zayne). Break threshold: 3 ice attacks. Frost Wraith: Weak to Fire (Rafayel). Break threshold: 4 fire attacks. Treant Sapling: Weak to Fire (Rafayel). Secondary weakness: Dark (Sylus). Break threshold: 5 fire or 7 dark attacks. Verdant Sprite: Weak to Dark (Sylus). No secondary. Break threshold: 3 dark attacks. Void Shade: Weak to Light (Xavier). Break threshold: 4 light attacks. Phantom Scout: Weak to Dark (Sylus). Break threshold: 3 dark attacks. Thorn Viper: Weak to Ice (Zayne). Secondary: Fire (Rafayel). Break threshold: 5 ice or 6 fire attacks. Dark Stalker: Weak to Light (Xavier). Break threshold: 5 light attacks.

Elite Wanderer Weaknesses

Elites have higher break thresholds and sometimes two-stage weaknesses: Glacial Stalker (Elite): Weak to Fire. Shield phase: Ice barrier (breaks with 3 fire attacks). Break threshold: 8 fire attacks total. Resists: Ice damage (-50%). Magma Beast (Elite): Weak to Ice. Shield phase: Lava armor (breaks with 4 ice attacks). Break threshold: 10 ice attacks total. Resists: Fire damage (-50%). Permafrost Guardian (Elite): Weak to Fire. Shield phase: Frozen shell (breaks with 5 fire attacks). Break threshold: 12 fire attacks. Resists: Ice damage (-75%). Inferno Lord (Elite): Weak to Ice. Shield phase: Flame cloak (breaks with 4 ice attacks). Break threshold: 12 ice attacks. Resists: Fire damage (-75%). Ancient Treant (Elite): Weak to Fire. Secondary: Dark. Shield phase: Bark armor (breaks with 6 fire or 9 dark attacks). Break threshold: 15 attacks of the triggering element. Resists: Physical damage (-50%). Abyssal Lord (Elite): Weak to Light. Shield phase: Void barrier (breaks with 5 light attacks). Break threshold: 15 light attacks. Resists: Dark damage (-50%). Has a dangerous enrage mechanic at 30% HP — save your stun for this phase.

Boss Weaknesses

Bosses have complex weakness systems with multiple phases: Inferno Colossus: Phase 1: Weak to Ice. Phase 2 (50% HP): Gains fire resist (-90%) and weakness shifts to Dark. Phase 3 (25% HP): All elemental resistances activate, but physical damage +50%. Strategy: Ice team Phase 1, Dark team Phase 2, physical damage Phase 3. Frost Tyrant: Phase 1: Weak to Fire. Phase 2 (60% HP): Ice armor reduces all damage by 75%. Break armor with 8 fire attacks. Phase 3 (30% HP): Weak to both Fire and Dark. Strategy: Pure fire team with Dark secondary. Break armor immediately at Phase 2 transition. Abyssal Siren: Always weak to Light. No phase shifts. But has a "Song" mechanic: every 20 seconds, puts all operatives to sleep unless you hit her with a Light attack during the 2-second cast. Strategy: Light team mandatory. Time your attacks to interrupt the Song. Ancient Treant Lord: Weak to Fire. Secondary: Dark. Spawns adds at 75%, 50%, 25% HP. Adds are weak to Dark. Strategy: Fire main DPS, Dark support to clear adds. Ignoring adds makes the boss heal 5% HP per add per second. Phantom Stalker: Weak to Dark. No phase shifts. But has 70% dodge chance against non-Dark attacks. Strategy: Pure Dark team. Bring Sylus for guaranteed hits. Void Lord: Phase 1: Weak to Light. Phase 2 (40% HP): Gains "Void Shift" — alternates weakness between Light and Dark every 10 seconds. Strategy: Bring both Light and Dark operatives. Switch DPS every 10 seconds.

Team Building by Weakness: Build at least two operatives per element to handle any wanderer composition: Light team: Xavier (main), any Light SSR support. Fire team: Rafayel (main), any Fire SSR support. Ice team: Zayne (main), any Ice SSR support. Dark team: Sylus (main), any Dark SSR support. For domains and general content: bring your strongest element and a secondary that covers common resistances. For boss fights: build specifically around the boss's phase weaknesses. A well-prepared team with correct element coverage clears content 30-50% faster than a mismatched team. The weakness break stun is the single most powerful damage window in the game — exploit it every time.

Quick Reference Table

Light (Xavier): Good against: Shadow Hound, Void Shade, Dark Stalker, Abyssal Siren, Void Lord. Weak against: Nothing (Light has no natural resistances). Standard domain choice. Fire (Rafayel): Good against: Rift Shade, Frost Wraith, Treant Sapling, Glacial Stalker, Permafrost Guardian, Ancient Treant, Frost Tyrant. Weak against: Magma Beast, Inferno Lord, Inferno Colossus (Phase 1). Best element for general farming. Ice (Zayne): Good against: Flame Imp, Thorn Viper, Magma Beast, Inferno Lord, Inferno Colossus (Phase 1). Weak against: Frost Wraith, Glacial Stalker, Permafrost Guardian, Frost Tyrant. Best for fire-heavy zones. Dark (Sylus): Good against: Verdant Sprite, Phantom Scout, Phantom Stalker, Inferno Colossus (Phase 2). Weak against: Void Shade, Dark Stalker, Abyssal Lord, Void Lord (Phase 1). Best for dark-weak wanderers and invisibility mechanics. Build at least one strong team per element. Swap operatives based on the wanderers you are facing. The weakness system rewards preparation and punishes brute force.

Summary: Every wanderer has an elemental weakness. Exploiting it doubles damage and triggers weakness break (stun + 1.5x damage). Common wanderers: Light beats dark types, Fire beats ice types, Ice beats fire types, Dark beats nature types. Elites have shield phases requiring element-specific breaks. Bosses have multi-phase weakness shifts. Build one team per element. Match element to content for 30-50% faster clears. The weakness break stun is the most powerful damage window in the game. Never fight a wanderer without exploiting its weakness.
