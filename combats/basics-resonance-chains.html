BASIC PROTOCOLS — SYNERGY

Cross-Operative Resonance Chains

Hidden synergies between specific operative pairings — elemental reactions that multiply your damage.

ENTRY_#0532026.07.01SYNERGY

Beyond Team Synergy: Guide #009 covered general resonance combos and rotation alignment. This guide focuses on specific pairings with unique mechanical interactions — not all operatives work equally well together.

Xavier + Zayne — Shatter Reaction

The most powerful pairing in the game. Xavier's Light (resonance) followed by Zayne's Frost (resonance) triggers Shatter — a hidden reaction that deals 35% bonus damage and applies a 2s stun. The combo: Xavier Resonance → Swap → Zayne Resonance (within 1.5s). The Shatter damage scales with both operatives' ATK, making this the highest burst potential in the game.

Optimal setup: Xavier applies Light mark with his resonance. Zayne's frost explosion detonates the mark. The Shatter CD is 8s per target — you can trigger it every other rotation. Time your Ardent Oath to land during the stun window for guaranteed full damage.

Sylus + Rafayel — Combustion Chain

Sylus's Dark (DoT + execute) paired with Rafayel's Fire (burn spread) creates a Combustion Chain. When a target with Sylus's Dark DoT (from auto-attack finishing strike) is hit by Rafayel's burn, the DoT combusts for instant 50% of its remaining damage. This turns Sylus's slow DoT into burst damage.

Rotation: Sylus applies Dark DoT (auto x4 finisher) → Swap to Rafayel → Apply burn (Flame Whip) → DoT combusts for burst. This combo is most effective against high-HP elites where Sylus's DoT has time to stack before combustion.

Zayne + Xavier — Freeze Extension

Zayne's freeze normally lasts 1s on non-boss enemies. With Xavier's Light mark applied to a frozen target, the freeze duration extends to 2.5s. During extended freeze, all damage dealt is treated as if from behind (+15% bonus). This is the best mob-control chain for multi-wave abyss floors.

Rafayel + Sylus — Shadowflare

When Rafayel's burn is active on a target below 50% HP, Sylus's execute threshold effectively becomes 40% instead of 30%. This is a passive chain — no specific rotation needed, just have both burn and execute active. The Shadowflare effect makes Sylus the best partner for Rafayel in boss fights.

Universal Chain — Element Cascade

Any 3-element team can trigger Element Cascade by rotating through all 3 elements in sequence within 4 seconds. Cascade deals 20% bonus damage as the fourth element (Light + Dark + Frost → bonus Fire damage). This is the most flexible chain and works with any triple-element team composition.

Pro Tip: Resonance chains have hidden cooldowns (8-12s depending on the chain). You cannot trigger the same chain on the same target repeatedly — track the cooldown mentally. Chains are most impactful at the start of a fight (pre-loaded burst) and during boss stagger windows.

Summary: Xavier+Zayne = Shatter (highest burst). Sylus+Rafayel = Combustion (DoT detonation). Zayne+Xavier = Freeze Extension (mob control). Rafayel+Sylus = Shadowflare (execute amp). 3-element = Cascade. Track chain CDs.
