Memory Farming Checklist

Optimized daily and weekly checklist for memory farming — shards, materials, gold, and resource efficiency maximization.

Key Insight: Consistent daily farming is the difference between a 6-month SSR and a 2-month SSR. Memory shard farming, material gathering, and resource management follow a predictable weekly cadence. Optimizing this routine saves hundreds of hours and thousands of gems over your account's lifetime.

Weekly Checklist

Bounty Missions
3 weekly bounty missions that reward memory upgrade materials. Always pick the Crystal stage (purple crystal) — it has the best stamina-to-value ratio. The other stages drop gold and EXP materials which are easier to farm elsewhere.
Abyss Shop Reset
Check the Abyss Shop every Monday for the monthly reset. Buy SSR memory shards first (5 available), then buy SR shards if you have remaining currency. Abyss tokens are earned from Abyss floor clears.
Event Shop Clear
During active events, check the event shop daily for memory-related items. The SSR memory shard box (10 shards) is always the highest priority purchase. Skip material purchases if you have to choose between shards and materials — shards are time-gated, materials are only stamina-gated.
Weekly Boss
3 weekly boss attempts. These drop memory ascension materials and gold. Use on the boss that drops crystals for the operative you're currently building. Save your attempts for the weekend when you have the highest stamina regen.

Shard Farming Route

Memory shards are the primary bottleneck for SSR completion. Here's the optimized weekly route: Crew Shop (14 shards/week = 2/day at 200 coins each), Event Shop (10 shards per event, variable timing), Abyss Shop (5 shards/month = ~1/week average), and limited banner pulls (variable, but average 1 SSR per 80-120 pulls).

The golden rule: never split shard purchases across multiple SSRs. Focus every crew coin, event currency, and Abyss token on ONE SSR until it's complete. A completed SSR at level 80 is infinitely more valuable than 5 partial SSRs at level 40. The psychological temptation to "diversify" is the number one mistake in memory farming.

Material Efficiency

Memory upgrade materials come from specific stages. The most efficient farming route: Gold stages (best value on Gold-efficient days), Crystal stages (spend 60 stamina daily — crystals are the most common bottleneck from levels 40-70), and EXP material stages (use leftover stamina — EXP is rarely a bottleneck if you do events). The material cost to take a memory from 1 to 80 is approximately: 1.2 million gold, 120 purple crystals, 60 blue crystals, and 480 EXP bottles.

Gold Management

Gold is the most consistently scarce resource in memory progression. The primary gold sources are bounty missions, event shops, weekly bosses, and the Battle Pass. Avoid spending gold on non-essential upgrades (leveling unused memories, buying shop items that aren't time-gated). A good rule: maintain a 500k gold reserve at all times for unexpected memory ascensions.

Tip: Use the in-game sticky note or external tracking to monitor your shard count for your target SSR. Write down "45/60" and update daily. The psychological boost of watching the number climb keeps you consistent. Missing one day of Crew Shop shards doesn't seem like much, but 30 missed days = 1 lost SSR. Consistency is everything in memory farming.

Summary: Memory farming is a marathon, not a sprint. The daily checklist takes 10 minutes and generates ~2 SSR shards per day from Crew Shop alone — that's one free SSR per month. Add event shops, Abyss shop, and banner pulls, and you're looking at 1-2 SSR completions per month as F2P. Focus shard purchases, never split resources, and maintain a 500k gold reserve. Consistency beats intensity every time.
