Abyssal Chaos Guide

The roguelike mode explained — permanent upgrades, sigil synergies, and how to clear all difficulty levels every week.

ENTRY_#0732026.07.02ABYSS

How to Read This: The Permanent Upgrades section applies to everyone — these carry across all runs. The Sigil Rankings section helps you pick between choices during a run. If you are struggling to clear a difficulty, skip to the strategy section.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Permanent Upgrades Priority

Abyssal Chaos has a permanent skill tree that upgrades your operatives inside the mode. These upgrades persist forever and make every run easier. Priority 1: ATK & HP nodes (the most universal stats). Priority 2: Ardent Oath generation (more Oath = faster clears). Priority 3: Resonance skill upgrades (specific to your main operative). Priority 4: Support/Survival nodes (healing, shield, dodge distance). Priority 5: Currency gain nodes (more chaos currency for permanent shop items). You earn permanent currency from first-time clears of each difficulty tier. Do not waste currency on cosmetic items until you have maxed the ATK and Oath nodes — those are the difference between clearing and wiping.

Sigil Rankings (Must-Pick vs Never-Pick)

Sigils are the power-ups you collect during a run. Not all sigils are equal. S-Tier (always pick): Oath Amplification (+200% Oath damage), Resonance Cascade (resonance procs twice), Critical Overload (guaranteed crit on resonance). These carry any team. A-Tier (strong picks): Attack Speed Up, Elemental Boost (matching your main DPS), Shield on Dodge (survivability = more uptime). B-Tier (situational): Healing sigils (if your team lacks sustain), HP-based damage (if your operatives have high HP). F-Tier (skip unless forced): Defense conversion (DEF is the least valuable stat in this mode), Enemy Slow (wasted sigil slot, does not increase your damage). General rule: damage > utility > defense in Abyssal Chaos. The fast clear bonuses are worth more than surviving longer.

Weekly Clear Route

Each week Abyssal Chaos resets with new modifiers. Here is the optimal route: (1) Check the weekly modifier — some weeks favor specific elements or team compositions. (2) Build your team around the modifier, not around your strongest operatives. (3) Take the most efficient path through the map — always choose combat nodes over event nodes unless the event is guaranteed to give a sigil. (4) Save your best sigils for the boss node — do not use consumables on trash waves. Time investment: Full clear takes 30-45 minutes at difficulty V. Partial clears are fine if you are short on time — prioritize boss kills over full exploration.

In Abyssal Chaos, prioritize energy regeneration and self-sustain picks in the first 3 stages. Damage-focused picks become more valuable in the last 2 stages. Balanced runs have the highest clear rate.

Team Building for Chaos: Abyssal Chaos uses a modified version of your operatives' stats. Your protocores and memories from outside the mode carry in, but sigils within a run can completely change your power level. Best team archetypes: (1) Hypercarry — one DPS operative with two supports stacking ATK buffs. (2) Elemental Synergy — two operatives sharing an element, one as sub-DPS. (3) Shield Sustain — defensive team for modifier weeks that reduce healing. Test all three in early weeks to see which fits your roster.

Advanced Strategies

Once you have the basics down, these advanced tactics separate good runs from great runs: Sigil Recycling: If you pick a weak sigil early, you can replace it later in a sigil fusion node. Do not be afraid to pick a temporary sigil for an immediate power boost and swap it out at the next opportunity. Oath Timing: Save your Ardent Oath for the second phase of boss fights. Many bosses become more aggressive below 50% HP, and Oath provides invincibility frames that can dodge telegraphed enrage attacks. Elemental Matching: If the weekly modifier gives +50% to fire damage, do not bring a water team. Elemental matching in Chaos is worth approximately 2 extra sigils worth of power. Reset Strategy: If your first three sigil choices are all F-tier, it is faster to reset and start a new run than to struggle through 15 minutes of underpowered combat.

Summary: Prioritize permanent ATK + Oath nodes first. Pick Oath Amplification and Resonance Cascade sigils whenever offered. Clear combat nodes over event nodes for more sigils. Build around the weekly modifier. Reset early if sigil RNG is bad. Full clear in 30-45 minutes per week for maximum rewards.
