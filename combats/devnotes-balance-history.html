DEV NOTES — BALANCE

Balance Change History

Every buff, nerf, and rework documented. Knowing what changed helps you understand why the current meta exists and what the devs value in character design.

ENTRY_#DEV_022026.07.02BALANCE

Dev Balance Philosophy: Love and Deepspace's developers rarely nerf characters directly. Instead, they buff underperformers and add new content that challenges dominant strategies. This means: (1) Your favorites will never be nerfed into uselessness. (2) But they may become less effective as new content favors different characters. (3) Investing in buffed characters is always safe — the devs do not reverse buffs.

Operative Balance Changes

Xavier — Light Seeker: No direct changes since launch. Xavier was the most-balanced operative from day 1. Minor adjustment (1.1): Light Slash projectile speed increased by 15% — quality of life, not DPS change. Xavier remains the consistent DPS benchmark. Verdict: The devs consider Xavier the "baseline" for DPS. Other operatives are balanced relative to him.

Sylus — Dark Champion: Major rework (1.1): Execute threshold increased from 20% HP to 30% HP. Execute damage increased from 30% bonus to 40% bonus. Cooldown reduced from 15s to 12s. Result: Sylus went from bottom tier to A-tier in a single patch. Minor fix (1.2): Execute now properly benefits from CRIT DMG (was bugged at launch — only base ATK was used). Effective damage increase: ~25%. Verdict: Sylus is the most-buffed operative. The devs clearly wanted him to be a viable alternative to Xavier, not a meme pick.

Zayne — Frostguard: Buff (1.1): Shield healing increased from 12% of max HP to 15% of max HP. Duration increased from 3s to 4s. Result: Zayne became the best sustain support. Buff (1.2): Frostguard 4pc set effect: Shield now also cleanses one debuff every 8s. Result: Zayne became mandatory for Abyss floors with debuff mechanics. Verdict: Zayne has been consistently buffed. The devs value support characters and want them to feel impactful.

Rafayel — Inferno Blaze: Major rework (1.2): Burn DoT tick interval reduced from 2.0s to 1.3s (with 4pc). Burn damage increased by 15% base. Result: Rafayel's total DPS increased by ~35%. He became the best AoE operative. Bug fix (1.3): Burn DoT now properly stacks with other fire damage sources (was overwriting instead of stacking). Effective increase: ~10% in team compositions with multiple fire sources. Verdict: Rafayel was a late bloomer. His 1.2 rework made him competitive with Xavier in AoE scenarios.

System Balance Changes

Protocore System (1.1): Added substat roll floor — protocores cannot roll below 50% of the maximum possible value for each substat. This was a massive buff to protocore farming quality. Before this change, a CRIT Rate roll could be as low as 1.2%. Protocore System (1.2): Added main stat guarantee — the first 10 protocore domain runs per week guarantee a correct main stat for the domain type. This made protocore farming significantly less frustrating. Memory Leveling (1.1): XP crystal drop rates in domains increased by 20%. Memory Leveling (1.2): Added "batch level" feature — feed multiple XP crystals at once (quality of life). Stamina System (Launch): Maximum stamina increased from 100 to 120. Event Shop (1.2): Added guaranteed protocore to event shops (previously only SSR shards were guaranteed).

Pattern Recognition: The devs make 3 types of balance changes: (1) Direct operative buffs — always happen in the first 2 patches after a character's release. If a character is not buffed in the first 2 patches, they are considered "balanced" and will not receive direct changes. (2) System improvements — happen every 2-3 patches. (3) Content shifts — new enemies or mechanics that indirectly shift the meta without changing any numbers. Watch for pattern (3) — it is the most common and most overlooked balance lever.

Lessons from Balance History

(1) Never judge a character in the first 2 weeks after release — they will almost certainly be buffed. (2) The devs value support characters (Zayne) and have consistently buffed them. Investing in supports is future-proof. (3) Protocore system improvements always benefit long-term players more than new players. The longer you farm, the more you gain from system changes. (4) Bug fixes = hidden buffs. After every patch, test your main's skills for undocumented changes. (5) No character has ever been nerfed. This means your investments are never wasted — but they may become less optimal as new characters release with stronger baseline kits.

Summary: Xavier — baseline, never nerfed. Sylus — major buff (1.1), now A-tier. Zayne — consistently buffed, best support. Rafayel — late bloomer (1.2 rework), AoE king. System changes favor long-term farmers. No character has ever been nerfed. Buffs always within first 2 patches. Bug fixes = hidden buffs. Read every patch note.
