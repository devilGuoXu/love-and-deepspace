The Honest Report Card

Love and Deepspace — Graded by Kaelen — July 2026

I have been playing this game for long enough that I feel qualified to hand out grades. These are subjective, obviously. But they are based on hundreds of hours of play, not first impressions. Here is how every major system measures up.

A
Combat System
Smooth, responsive, genuinely fun on mobile. The perfect dodge mechanic is one of the best implementations I have seen in any action game. Animations have weight. Sound design is punchy. The combat is the reason I stayed.
A-
Character Design & Art
The character models are gorgeous. The memory art is consistently stunning. The visual design carries the emotional weight of the story. Minor deduction for outfit recolors being sold as new content.
B+
Story & Writing
Genuinely better than expected. The main story has real emotional beats. The character-specific stories are well-paced. The writing is not literary fiction, but it does not need to be. It is warm, sincere, and effective. Loses points for occasional filler dialogue and translation inconsistencies.
B
Music & Soundtrack
The soundtrack is good. The battle themes are energetic. The character themes are fitting. Nothing particularly memorable, but nothing bad. Solid B work from the audio team.
B-
Event Design
Events are generally well-designed with good rewards. The minigames are hit or miss. The summer event was fantastic. The login events are boring but free. Deduction for event FOMO mechanics that pressure spending.
C+
Gacha & Monetization
Standard gacha rates with soft pity at 62 and hard pity at 70. The 50-50 mechanic is punishing. The gem economy is tight but manageable for F2P. The monthly pass is the best value. Direct top-up rates are terrible. Overall, middle of the pack for gacha games.
C
New Player Experience
The game dumps too many systems on new players too quickly. The tutorial explains the basic controls but leaves major systems like Stellactrum and protocores under-explained. New players are expected to learn from the community, which should not be the case for fundamental mechanics.
C-
Endgame Content Variety
The endgame is primarily Abyss floors and events. There is not enough variety in endgame activities. Once you clear Abyss, the weekly loop is identical every week. More endgame modes would significantly improve longevity.
D+
Social & Multiplayer Systems
Co-op exists but is basic. No guild system. No competitive leaderboards beyond personal rank. The social features feel like an afterthought. For a game with such strong character attachment, the lack of meaningful social features is a missed opportunity.
D
Protocore Enhancement
The worst system in the game by a wide margin. Four layers of RNG with no pity mechanism. The enhancement dust economy is stingy. The substat rolling system can waste hours of farming in seconds. This system needs a fundamental redesign, not minor adjustments.
C+
OVERALL GPA

A solid game held back by its worst systems. The highs are high. The lows are frustrating but manageable if you know what you are getting into. Would recommend to fans of action games and character-driven stories, with the caveat that the gear grind is painful.

Grades are subjective and based on personal experience. Your experience may vary depending on your tolerance for gacha mechanics and gear RNG.
