THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Nightmare Wraith

A creature of pure shadow that fights with clones, curses, and silence. Identify the real target — or die trying.

ENTRY_#1022026.06.29ELITE_BOSS
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Recommended Power: 82k+ | Element: Shadow | Weakness: Dark — Sylus has natural advantage + execute on clones.

Phase 1: Whispers (100%-65%)

Shadow Clones: Wraith spawns 3 copies. Only one is real. Hit the real one to dispel clones. Clones have slightly different transparency. Nightmare Curse: A DoT that stacks. Cleanse with Zayne or wait it out. Max 5 stacks. Silence Field: Ground zone that silences abilities. Avoid at all costs.

Phase 2: Descent (65%-30%)

Drain: Wraith tethers to you and drains HP. Break the tether by moving 15+ meters away. Shadow Surge: AoE darkness pulse. Dodge inward (toward the Wraith) to avoid the expanding ring. Hallucination: Screen distortion effect. Ignore the visual noise — focus on sound cues.

Phase 3: Void (30%-0%)

Void Zone: Arena shrinks. Stay in the light. Nightmare's End: Mass clone spawn + curse + silence combo. Prioritize survival over damage. True Form: Wraith reveals itself. All damage tripled. Burst immediately.

Recommended Team: Sylus (Main DPS) — Dark element destroys clones. Zayne (Support) — cleanse curses and shield. Xavier (Sub-DPS) — Light damage during True Form.

Clone Identification Techniques

Identifying the real Wraith among its shadow clones is the core skill required for this encounter. Three visual cues distinguish the real Wraith: the real one has a faint red aura at its base that clones lack, the real one casts a visible shadow on the ground while clones have no shadow, and the real one's movement animation has a 0.3-second delay after clone movements. Train your eye to check for the shadow cue first — it is the most reliable indicator regardless of arena lighting conditions or visual effects.

When the Wraith spawns clones in Phase 1, immediately stop all damage abilities and observe the clone formation for 1-2 seconds before attacking. Charging into the clone group without identification leads to wasted damage and potential curse stacks. If you are playing Sylus, use his enhanced perception passive ability to highlight the real target — the passive applies a subtle glow effect to the real Wraith that is visible through the clone visual noise. Xavier's light-based abilities also reveal the real target by casting stronger shadows from the real Wraith compared to clones.

Curse Stack Management

The Nightmare Curse DoT is the primary threat in Phase 1 and Phase 2. At zero stacks, the curse deals negligible damage. At three stacks, the damage becomes significant enough to require healing attention. At five stacks, the curse damage rivals the Wraith's direct attacks. Managing curse stacks is about awareness of positioning relative to the Curse Field zones. These zones appear on the ground as swirling purple circles with a 2-second delay before activation. Once you recognize the spawn animation, you have a 2-second window to move out of the affected area.

Zayne's shield provides a full cleanse of curse stacks when activated while cursed. Time your shield use specifically for when you reach three curse stacks, not before. Using the shield at zero stacks wastes the cleanse potential. The shield has a 15-second cooldown, which is approximately the time it takes to accumulate three curse stacks if you are playing aggressively. This creates a natural rhythm: play aggressively for 15 seconds, shield to cleanse at three stacks, play defensively for 5 seconds, then repeat. Xavier's self-heal passive also provides curse mitigation by offsetting the DoT damage, though it does not cleanse the stacks themselves.

The silence field targets the operative with the highest HP percentage. Keep your support slightly lower HP than your DPS to control where the field appears. The field lasts 8 seconds with a 4-meter radius.

Advanced Tip: The clones always move in perfect formation. The real Wraith has a 0.5s delay in its movement pattern. Watch for the straggler — that's the real target. Sylus's execute on the real Wraith dispels all clones instantly.

Summary: Find the real Wraith, avoid silence fields, and break tethers. Sylus makes this fight significantly easier with Dark element advantage and clone dispel. Phase 3 is chaos — prioritize survival.
