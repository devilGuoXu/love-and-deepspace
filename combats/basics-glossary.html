Deepspace Glossary

A comprehensive glossary of terms, abbreviations, and community slang used in Love and Deepspace. Essential reading for new and returning players.

ENTRY_#0822026.07.02GLOSSARY

How to Use This Glossary: Terms are organized alphabetically within categories. If you encounter an unfamiliar term in a guide or community discussion, look it up here. New terms are added as the game evolves.

Game Systems

Affinity: Relationship level with each operative. Unlocks bond memories, dates, Secret Times, and stat bonuses. Max level: 150. Bond Memory: Narrative-only memories that unlock at Affinity thresholds. No combat stats. Complete them for account-wide ATK and CRIT DMG bonuses. Destiny Cafe: The hub area where you interact with operatives. Access gifts, photo mode, and daily interactions. Memory: Equippable cards that determine your operative's stats, set bonuses, and resonance skills. SSR (highest rarity, level cap 80), SR (medium, cap 60), R (lowest, cap 40). Protocore: Secondary equipment system that provides stat bonuses and set effects. Farmed from Protocore Dust domains. Resonance: Each memory's active skill. Activated by the resonance button during combat. Different memories have different resonance effects. Set Bonus: Equipping 2 or 4 memories from the same set activates bonus stats. 2-pc: +15% ATK or similar. 4-pc: +30% elemental damage or unique effect. Stellactrum: The elemental alignment system. Matching element to enemy weakness doubles damage and enables weakness break. Six elements: Light, Fire, Ice, Dark, Physical, Cosmic. Weakness Break: Attacking a wanderer with its weakness element fills a hidden meter. When full, the enemy is stunned for 4 seconds and takes 1.5x damage.

Abbreviations & Acronyms

BiS: Best in Slot — the best possible memory for a given operative or role. F2P: Free to Play — a player who spends no real money on the game. Whale: A player who spends significant money. Dolphin: A player who spends moderately (typically battle pass + monthly pass). SHC: Senior Hunter Contest — the endgame PvE mode with floors of increasing difficulty. DoT: Damage over Time — effects that deal damage over several seconds (e.g., burn, frostbite). AoE: Area of Effect — attacks that damage multiple enemies in an area. ST: Single Target — attacks that damage one enemy. CD: Cooldown — the time before a skill can be used again. CDMG/CR: Critical Damage / Critical Rate — the two critical stats. ATK%: Attack Percentage — a main stat or substat that increases base ATK by a percentage. HP%: Health Percentage — similar for HP. DEF%: Defense Percentage. EM: Energy Recharge — how fast your resonance energy regenerates. Pity: The guaranteed SSR system. Hard pity at 70 pulls. Soft pity starts at 62 pulls. 50/50: The 50% chance to get the rate-up character when you pull an SSR. If you lose, your next SSR is guaranteed to be the rate-up.

Community Slang

Husbando: A player's favorite male character. Used endearingly. Best Boy: Similar to husbando, often used for Xavier. Otp: One True Pairing — a player's preferred romantic pairing (usually self-insert MC with a specific operative). Copium: Coping with bad gacha luck or disappointing news. "I'm on heavy copium after losing my 50/50." Diamond Poor: Having zero diamonds after pulling on a banner. Spook: Getting an off-rate SSR (not the featured character) from a banner. "I got spooked by a standard SSR." Dolphin Kick: A small spending spree, usually the monthly pass + battle pass. Reroll: Creating a new account to try for better initial pulls. Less common now that the game is established. Alt Account: A secondary account, often used to play a different operative or test strategies. Main: A player's primary operative. "I'm a Xavier main." Simp: Playfully used to describe devotion to a particular character. "I simp for Sylus." Dailies: Daily missions. Weeklies: Weekly missions.

Combat Terms

DPS: Damage Per Second — a role focused on dealing damage. Support: A role focused on healing, shielding, or buffing the team. Sub-DPS: A secondary damage dealer who provides utility or off-element coverage. Rotation: The sequence of skill activations for maximum damage. Burst Window: A period of time when all buffs are active and damage is maximized. Invincibility Frames (iFrames): Brief moments during dodges or skills where the character cannot take damage. Perfect Dodge: Dodging at the exact moment an attack hits. Triggers a slow-motion effect and bonus damage window. Aggro: Enemy aggression — which operative the enemy is targeting. Manage aggro to protect your support units. Stagger: Interrupting an enemy's attack animation with a heavy hit. HP Threshold: An enemy's HP percentage that triggers a phase change, add spawn, or new attack pattern. Soft Enrage: An enemy's damage or speed increasing over time, eventually becoming overwhelming. Common in endgame content.

Quick Tips for New Players: (1) Learn the abbreviations first — they appear in every guide and discussion. (2) Know your game systems: Affinity, Memories, Protocores, Stellactrum, Weakness Break. These are the core loops. (3) Understand pity and 50/50 before pulling on any banner. Budget 140 pulls for a guaranteed SSR. (4) Do not worry about community slang immediately. It comes naturally as you engage with the community. (5) Bookmark this glossary. As the game updates, new terms will be added. Check back when you encounter unfamiliar terminology. (6) Ask in the community if a term is not listed here. The Deepspace community is friendly to new players asking questions.

Currency & Items

Diamonds: Premium currency. Used for pulling on banners, buying stamina, and shop purchases. Earned through gameplay and events. Deepspace Wish (Limited): A limited pull ticket. Used on current event banners. Converts to standard wish after banner ends. Deepspace Wish (Standard): A standard pull ticket. Used on the standard banner. Earned through gameplay, events, and shop. Stardust: Currency earned from pulling duplicate memories. Used in the Stardust Shop for pulls and materials. Crew Coins: Earned from crew activities. Used in the Crew Shop for SSR shards and materials. Gold: Basic currency. Used for leveling, ascending, and shop purchases. Stamina: Energy resource. 1 per 6 minutes. Used to enter domains and stages. Stamina Potion: Restores 60 stamina. Earned from events and login. Protocore Dust: Material used to craft protocores. The endgame resource bottleneck. ASCension Materials: Used to increase memory level cap. Farmed from specific domains.

Summary: This glossary covers game systems (Affinity, Memory, Protocore, Resonance, Set Bonus, Stellactrum, Weakness Break), abbreviations (BiS, F2P, SHC, DoT, AoE, Pity, 50/50), community slang (husbando, copium, spook, main, simp), combat terms (DPS, iFrames, Perfect Dodge, Aggro, Stagger, HP Threshold), and currency (Diamonds, Deepspace Wishes, Stardust, Crew Coins, Gold, Stamina). New terms are added as the game evolves. Bookmark this page for quick reference.
