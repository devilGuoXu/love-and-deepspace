BASIC PROTOCOLS — REFERENCE

Combat Guide Glossary

Complete reference of Love and Deepspace combat terminology — from Abyss floors to Zero Energy, every term explained for players of all levels.

ENTRY_#0822026.07.03REFERENCE

Key Insight: Love and Deepspace uses a unique combination of gacha RPG terminology and original mechanics. Understanding the specific meaning of each term — especially the difference between similar concepts like "Resonance" (character skill) and "Resonance Chain" (memory dupe system) — prevents costly mistakes in build planning and resource allocation.

TABLE OF CONTENTS

  • A-D
  • E-H
  • I-L
  • M-P
  • Q-T
  • U-Z

A-D

Abyss (Deepspace Abyss): The primary endgame PvE content — a multi-floor combat tower with 12+ floors. Each floor has elemental restrictions and reward tiers. Clearing floors rewards gems, memories, and upgrade materials. Abyss progress resets periodically (monthly for rewards, permanent for floor unlocks).

Affinity: A relationship level system with each operative. Higher Affinity unlocks voice lines, base interactions, companion combat effectiveness, and story content. Affinity is increased through gifts, daily interactions, combat together, and story progression. Each operative has their own Affinity level (1-100).

Ardent Oath: A powerful consumable item that provides a temporary team-wide damage buff and energy regeneration. Crafted from materials dropped by weekly bosses. Essential for pushing Abyss floors and clearing time-limited challenge content.

ATK (Attack): The primary damage stat. Increases all skill damage. Sources: memory base stats, protocore main stats and substats, set bonuses, and temporary buffs. The baseline for all damage calculations.

Ascension: Level cap increase for memories. SSR memories ascend at levels 20/30/40/50/60/70 (max 80). SR at 20/30/40/50 (max 60). Ascension requires elemental crystals of matching type and increases base stats.

Aurum Pass: The monthly subscription ($4.99) that provides 100 gems per day for 30 days (3,000 total). Best value purchase in the game. Also called "Monthly Card."

Base / Dawnbreak Base: The Hunter's customizable living space. Rooms can be decorated with furniture, and operatives visit for interactions. Base progression rewards gems and exclusive items.

Battle Pass (BP): A 6-week progression system with free and paid tracks. Completing daily/weekly/event missions earns BP EXP and unlocks rewards: pull tickets, gold, protocore dust, memory chests, and exclusive cosmetics.

Breakpoint: A stat threshold where content becomes clearable or damage calculations shift significantly. Examples: 50% CRIT (reliable crits), 2,000 ATK (Abyss Floor 9 minimum), 120% ER (comfortable rotation energy).

CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG: Critical hit stats. CRIT Rate = chance to crit (cap 100%). CRIT DMG = damage multiplier when critting (base 100%, can exceed 250%). The effective damage increase is (1 + CRIT_Rate × CRIT_DMG).

Companion: The operative who fights alongside the Hunter in combat. The companion auto-attacks and can be commanded to use an active skill. Companion effectiveness scales with Affinity level and equipped memories.

Consumables: Craftable items (food, potions) that provide temporary combat buffs: ATK increase, CRIT increase, DEF increase, and energy regen. Crafted in the Home system using ingredients from daily gathering.

Co-op / Cooperative Battles: Multiplayer content where 2-3 players team up against bosses. Co-op has its own reward track and weekly caps. Companions are active in co-op and contribute to team DPS.

Daily Missions: 5 daily tasks (log in, spend stamina, do domains, etc.) that reward gems, BP EXP, and resources. Complete all 5 every day for optimal progression.

DEF (Defense): Damage reduction stat. Reduces incoming damage from enemies. Important for tank and support memories but generally low priority for DPS. Sources: memory base stats, protocore main stats.

Diminishing Returns: The principle that each additional point of a stat provides less benefit than the previous point. Example: going from 0-50% ATK bonus gives ~35% damage increase, while 100-150% gives only ~15%. Optimizing across stats avoids over-investment in any single category.

Domain: Repeatable PvE stages that drop specific resources: Protocore Dust, XP Crystals, Gold, Ascension Materials, and Skill Materials. Domains have difficulty levels (30/40/50/60/70) with better drop rates at higher levels.

DPS (Damage Per Second): A measure of combat effectiveness. Also used to describe a character whose primary role is dealing damage (as opposed to support or tank).

E-H

Element / Elemental Damage: Each operative and memory has an element (Light, Frost, Fire, Dark, Water, Wind). Matching elements in a team triggers elemental reactions that amplify damage. Elemental Damage stat increases damage of that element's skills.

Elemental Reaction: When two matching or complementary elements interact in combat. Examples: Frost + Light = Shatter (30% bonus damage), Dark + Water = Vortex (DoT), Fire + Wind = Combustion (AoE burst). Reactions are automatic when conditions are met.

Energy / Energy Regen (ER): Energy fuels Resonance skills. Characters generate energy through basic attacks and skill use. Energy Regen stat increases the rate of energy generation. Higher ER = more frequent Resonance uses = more damage.

Event: Time-limited content running 7-21 days with exclusive rewards: memories, cosmetics, upgrade materials, and premium currency. Events are the most stamina-efficient content in the game.

F2P (Free to Play): A player who spends no real money on the game. F2P accounts require disciplined resource management and selective pulling but can clear all content.

Farming: Repeatedly running domains or content to accumulate specific resources. "Farming dust" = running the protocore dust domain multiple times.

Gacha / Banner: The randomized reward system for obtaining memories. Limited banners feature rate-up memories available for 3 weeks. Standard banner (XSpace Echo) has a permanent pool. Pulls cost 150 gems each or tickets.

Gems / Diamonds: The premium currency. Used for pulls, stamina refills, BP level skips, and shop items. Earned through daily/weekly missions, Abyss, events, and purchases.

Gold: The basic currency used for leveling memories, enhancing protocores, and crafting consumables. Farmed from gold domain or obtained from events and missions.

Hard Pity / Pity Timer: A guaranteed SSR memory after 90 pulls on a banner. Soft pity begins at approximately pull 65 where SSR rates increase. Pity carries over between banners of the same type (limited to limited, standard to standard).

HP (Health Points): Character health. When HP reaches zero, the character is knocked out. Support memories often build HP to improve healing and shielding effectiveness.

Hunter: The player character. The Hunter is the primary combat unit, supported by operatives (companions) in battle.

I-L

Limited Banner: A time-limited gacha banner (usually 3 weeks) featuring a specific SSR memory at increased rate. Limited memories may get reruns but are not permanently added to the standard pool.

Main Stat: The primary stat on a protocore. Each slot has a pool of possible main stats. Correct main stat (e.g., CRIT Rate on Slot 4 for DPS) is essential for build effectiveness.

Memory: Equippable cards that determine a character's skills, stats, and set bonuses. Memories have rarity (SR/SSR), level (max 60 SR, 80 SSR), element, set, and equipped protocore slots.

Memory Set: A collection of memories sharing a theme. Equipping 2 pieces activates a 2-set bonus. Equipping 4 pieces activates a stronger 4-set bonus. Examples: Light Seeker (Xavier), Frost Weaver (Zayne).

Memory Shard: Fragments used to craft or ascend memories. Collected from gacha (duplicate memories convert to shards), events, and the Crew Shop.

Meta: The most effective strategies and builds at the current game state. The meta shifts with patches, new memories, and balance changes. "Meta" is short for "most effective tactics available."

Monthly Card: See Aurum Pass.

Operative: A main character companion (Xavier, Zayne, Rafayel, Sylus). Each operative has unique combat style, element preferences, story content, and Affinity progression. Players choose operatives to accompany the Hunter in combat.

M-P

Perfect Dodge: Dodging an attack at the exact moment of impact. Triggers a brief slow-motion effect, grants temporary invincibility frames, and generates bonus energy. Mastering perfect dodge is essential for high-level play.

Pity: See Hard Pity.

Premium Currency: See Gems.

Protocore: Equippable enhancement items that provide stat bonuses. Each memory has 6 protocore slots. Protocores have main stats (fixed per slot) and substats (randomized on enhancement). Protocores can be enhanced to +15 using dust.

Protocore Dust: The primary material for enhancing protocores. Farmed from the protocore dust domain. Required in increasing quantities per enhancement level.

Pull / Wish: A single attempt on a gacha banner. Costs 150 gems or one ticket. 10-pulls guarantee at least one SR or higher memory.

Pull Ticket / Limited Wish: A ticket that can be used on limited banners instead of gems. Obtained from events, BP, and login rewards.

Q-T

Resonance: Each operative's ultimate skill. Requires energy to use (usually 80-100% energy bar). Resonance skills have high damage multipliers and often trigger elemental reactions. The centerpiece of most combat rotations.

Resonance Chain (R-Level): A memory dupe system. Obtaining duplicate copies of a memory increases its Resonance Chain level (R0 to R3+). Higher R-levels unlock enhanced skill effects and stat bonuses. Important for min-maxing but not required for content clearing.

Rotation: The sequence of skills and actions used in combat to maximize damage. A good rotation aligns buffs, debuffs, Resonance cooldowns, and elemental reactions for optimal DPS.

SHC (Senior Hunter Challenge): Advanced combat challenges with specific conditions and leaderboard rankings. Endgame content for optimized accounts.

Skill Material: Items used to level up memory skills. Obtained from the skill material domain. Different materials are needed for different skill levels and memory rarities.

Stamina: Energy resource for entering domains and story stages. Regenerates at 1 per 5 minutes (288 per day). Capped at 120 base (can be expanded with leveling). Stamina potions provide bonus stamina.

Standard Banner / XSpace Echo: The permanent gacha banner. Does not feature rate-up memories. Standard wishes (blue tickets) are used here. Never spend gems on the standard banner — use only free tickets.

Stellactrum: A grid-based memory enhancement system. Each memory has a Stellactrum grid that can be filled with specific materials to unlock bonus stats and set effects.

Substat: Secondary stats on a protocore, revealed at enhancement levels +3, +6, +9, +12, +15. Quality depends on roll values (low/medium/high). Substats are where protocore optimization happens.

Support: A character role focused on healing, shielding, buffing allies, or debuffing enemies. Support memories prioritize HP, ER, and defensive stats over offensive stats.

Synergy: How well two or more characters' skills, elements, and sets work together. High synergy teams deal 20-60% more damage than the sum of their individual parts.

U-Z

Wanderer: Enemy types in Love and Deepspace. Each Wanderer has elemental weaknesses, attack patterns, and defense values. Learning Wanderer mechanics is essential for efficient domain farming and Abyss progression.

Weekly Boss: A powerful boss encounter that resets weekly (3 entries per week, shared across all bosses). Weekly bosses drop ascension materials, Ardent Oath ingredients, and have a chance to drop SSR protocores.

Weekly Missions: 5 weekly tasks that reset every Monday. Rewards gems, BP EXP, and resources. Higher value than daily missions. Complete before Monday reset each week.

Whale: A high-spending player who invests significant money into the game, typically pulling on every banner and aiming for R3+ memories and perfect protocores.

Wish: See Pull.

XP / EXP: Experience points for leveling memories. Obtained from XP crystal domains, event rewards, and mission rewards. SSR memories require 16.5M XP to reach level 80.

XP Crystal: Items that provide memory experience. Different tiers (Small, Medium, Large, XL) provide different amounts of EXP. Farmed from the XP crystal domain.

Zone / Domain Level: The difficulty tier of a domain (30, 40, 50, 60, 70). Higher levels have better drop rates and reward higher-tier materials. Unlocked by reaching certain player levels or Abyss milestones.
