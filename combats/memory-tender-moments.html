What Are Tender Moments

Tender Moments are Love and Deepspace's visual novel-style narrative content, attached to SSR memories as unlockable story segments. Unlike Secret Times (audio-only), Tender Moments are full visual presentations with character sprites, dialogue boxes, background art, and the memory card's illustration as a centerpiece. They represent the main narrative payoff of collecting and ascending SSR memories — the story that the card art is depicting.

Each SSR memory has exactly one Tender Moment, unlocked when the memory reaches ascension tier 1 (level 40+). The story is typically 10-20 minutes of reading, with branching dialogue options that influence the narrative flow (though not the ending). The writing quality across Tender Moments is consistently high, with some episodes rivaling the main story in emotional impact and character development.

How to Access

To access a Tender Moment: collect the SSR memory, ascend it to tier 1 (reach level 40, which requires the first duplicate or 30 memory shards), then navigate to Memories > SSR Memory > Tender Moment tab. The story plays automatically in visual novel format — tap to advance dialogue, make story choices when prompted, and enjoy the narrative payoff for your collection efforts.

Importantly, Tender Moments are permanently unlocked once you ascend the memory to tier 1. Even if you later use the memory as fodder (not recommended!), the story remains in your collection. There is no time limit on viewing them, and once unlocked you can rewatch any Tender Moment from the Memories archive as many times as you like.

Story Guide

Xavier — Stellar Contract
A stargazing date turns into a confession about Xavier's origins. The story explores his loneliness among the stars and his growing attachment to the Hunter. Features beautiful nighttime Observatory backgrounds and a particularly tender moment where Xavier admits he's been visiting the Hunter's dreams.
Zayne — Permafrost Covenant
During a research expedition to a frozen wasteland, Zayne's careful professionalism cracks when the Hunter is injured. The story balances Zayne's medical expertise with his hidden emotional depth. The ice cave backgrounds are stunning, and Zayne's internal monologue reveals more about his past with the Hunter.
Rafayel — Inferno Waltz
A masquerade ball at an art gallery. Rafayel and the Hunter share a dance while chaos unfolds around them. The story is equal parts romance and action, with Rafayel showcasing both his playful side and his fierce protectiveness. The ballroom backgrounds with stained glass lighting are a visual highlight.
Sylus — Twilight Oath
Sylus makes a risky deal to protect the Hunter, revealing the extent of his underworld connections. The story is the darkest Tender Moment, dealing with themes of sacrifice and loyalty. The shadowy Onychinus headquarters setting creates a tense atmosphere, and Sylus's vulnerability in the final scene is remarkably affecting.

Writing Quality

The writing in Tender Moments is a step above typical mobile game visual novels. The localization team handles cultural nuances well, preserving the poetic quality of the original Chinese while ensuring natural English dialogue. Each operative has a consistent voice — Xavier speaks with gentle melancholy, Zayne with clinical precision, Rafayel with theatrical flair, Sylus with measured intensity — and the Tender Moments reinforce these character voices through internal monologue and scene description.

Dialogue choices, while limited in their mechanical impact, offer meaningful roleplaying opportunities. Choosing to be direct versus evasive, or playful versus serious, changes how the operative responds in the moment — even if the overall story arc stays the same. For players invested in the relationship simulation aspects, these choices add replayability.

Art & Presentation

The visual presentation of Tender Moments is polished and cinematic. Character sprites use multiple expressions and poses, with smooth transitions that mimic visual novel pacing. Backgrounds are fully painted scenes that match the memory card's aesthetic — no reused assets from the main story. The memory card art itself appears as a climactic illustration, often with special effects (particle effects, lighting overlays) that enhance the moment.

The UI is clean and unobtrusive, with auto-play options, text log, and skip-read functionality. You can adjust text speed and choose between portrait or landscape orientation. The music that plays during each Tender Moment is unique to that memory, composed to match the emotional tone of the story.

Tip: You don't need to read Tender Moments in any order. Each story is self-contained and assumes only basic knowledge of the operative's personality. Feel free to start with whichever operative is your favorite. However, reading them in release order does reveal subtle callbacks and character growth across the narrative timeline.

Summary: Tender Moments are the narrative heart of the memory system. Each SSR memory unlocks a 10-20 minute visual novel story with professional writing, beautiful art, and meaningful character moments. Access requires ascending the SSR memory to tier 1. For players invested in Love and Deepspace's characters and story, Tender Moments are the primary reward of the collection system — more valuable than the stat bonuses for narrative enjoyment.
