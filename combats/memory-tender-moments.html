Tender Moments Guide

Tender Moments are interactive story scenes with branching dialogue, multiple endings, and exclusive CG artwork. This guide covers how to access and complete them.

ENTRY_#MEM_092026.07.02TENDER MOMENTS

What Are Tender Moments: Tender Moments are interactive visual novel-style scenes that function as dates with the operatives. Unlike bond memories (which are linear stories), Tender Moments feature branching dialogue choices that lead to different outcomes and rewards.

Unlocking Tender Moments

Tender Moments are tied to specific SSR memories. Each limited SSR memory includes a Tender Moments scene. Standard banner SSRs do not include Tender Moments. How to access: Obtain the associated SSR memory. Level the memory to at least 40. Complete the memory's story unlock requirements. Access Tender Moments from the Memory Detail screen (the heart icon). Each Tender Moment is a self-contained story that takes 10-20 minutes to complete. There is no stamina cost to replay Tender Moments once unlocked. You can replay them as many times as you like to see different dialogue branches.

Branching Dialogue System

Tender Moments use a dialogue tree system. Key choices have 2-4 options. Each choice affects the operative's response and can lead to different endings. Choice types: Affection choices (increase temporary affinity score), neutral choices (no effect), and risky choices (can lower affinity score or lead to early ending). Each Tender Moment has 2-3 endings: a standard ending, a good ending (requires 70%+ affinity choices), and sometimes a hidden ending (requires specific choices at specific decision points). The hidden ending often unlocks an exclusive CG or voice line. Reaching the good ending in a Tender Moment rewards 50 diamonds. Finding the hidden ending rewards an additional 30 diamonds plus a unique name card. Hidden ending conditions are not shown in-game — they must be discovered through experimentation or community guides.

Tender Moment Catalog

Twilight Oath (Xavier): A nighttime rooftop date under the stars. Choices revolve around whether to push the conversation toward romance or keep it friendly. Hidden ending: Choose the stargazing option at the second decision point, then select the romantic dialogue option at the fourth decision point. Rewards: Exclusive "Starlight" name card. Eternal Winter (Zayne): A winter market date with ice skating and hot drinks. Hidden ending: Help the market stall owner during the side event segment. Rewards: "Frost Blossom" chat bubble. Blazing Sacrifice (Rafayel): A festival date with fireworks and traditional games. Hidden ending: Win the ring toss game (requires choosing the practice option before the actual attempt). Rewards: "Firelight" profile frame. Midnight Sovereign (Sylus): A formal dinner date at an exclusive restaurant. Hidden ending: Correctly identify the wine vintage when tested. Rewards: "Shadow Court" exclusive CG. These hidden endings add significant replay value. The community has documented all hidden ending conditions on dedicated fan wikis.

Completionist Tips: Before starting a Tender Moment, save your game manually. If you make a wrong choice, reload and try again. There is no penalty for reloading. For hidden endings: always choose the option that seems most "in-character" for the operative, not the option that seems most "affectionate." The hidden endings often reward understanding the character rather than maximizing affection. For example, with Zayne, the logical/medical-choice option is often the hidden ending trigger, not the romantic option. If you want to see all content without a guide, plan for 3-4 playthroughs per Tender Moment. Each playthrough takes about 15 minutes, and you can skip already-read dialogue.

Rewards Summary

Each Tender Moment gives: Standard ending: 20 diamonds + affinity XP. Good ending: 50 diamonds + affinity XP + exclusive item (name card, chat bubble, or profile frame). Hidden ending: 30 bonus diamonds + exclusive CG or voice line. Replaying Tender Moments does not give repeat rewards. The rewards are one-time per ending. Achievement hunters should prioritize Tender Moments for the exclusive cosmetic items. The name cards and profile frames from hidden endings are rare status symbols — only players who completed the hidden ending can display them.

Summary: Tender Moments are interactive date scenes tied to limited SSR memories. Each has 2-3 endings with branching dialogue. Good endings reward 50 diamonds + exclusive items. Hidden endings reward bonus diamonds + unique CGs. Save before starting, replay to find all endings, and prioritize hidden endings for rare cosmetics. Tender Moments represent some of the best-written character content in the game and are well worth the effort to 100% complete.
