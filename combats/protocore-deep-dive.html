Protocore Deep Dive

Main stats, substat rolls, set bonuses, and the difference between a good protocore and a perfect one.

ENTRY_#083 2026.06.29 CORE_SYSTEM
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

One Sentence: Protocores are the single biggest damage multiplier in the game — a perfectly rolled set can increase your damage by 40%+ compared to an unoptimized one.

Protocore Basics

Each operative has 6 protocore slots. Each protocore has a main stat (fixed per slot), a set bonus (2-piece), and up to 4 substats (randomly rolled at +3/+6/+9/+12). Understanding what each slot does is the first step to optimization.

Slot Main Stat Options Priority
Slot 1 ATK (fixed) Always ATK
Slot 2 HP / DEF / ATK% ATK% for DPS
Slot 3 CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG / Elemental Mastery CRIT Rate (until 60%)
Slot 4 CRIT DMG / ATK% / Skill DMG% CRIT DMG (if CRIT > 60%)
Slot 5 Elemental DMG% / ATK% Elemental DMG% (matching operative)
Slot 6 Energy Regen / Healing Bonus / ATK% ATK% (Energy Regen if support)

Set Bonuses

A 2-piece set bonus activates when you equip two protocores of the same set on the same operative. You can mix two different sets for two bonuses, or use 4 of one set for an enhanced bonus.

Solar Set
2-piece: ATK +12%. 4-piece: ATK +25% and skill damage +10%.
Best for: Xavier, Sylus (general DPS). Universal set — works on every DPS operative.
Permafrost Set
2-piece: Frost DMG +15%. 4-piece: Freeze duration +1s and frozen targets take +15% damage.
Best for: Zayne. Only useful on Frost element. Synergizes with Zayne's CC kit.
Tidal Set
2-piece: Water DMG +15%. 4-piece: Healing received +20% and water element shields absorb 30% more.
Best for: Water-element memories. Niche — only use if building a water-focused support.
Shadow Set
2-piece: Dark DMG +15%. 4-piece: Below 50% HP, DMG +25% and DEF +15%.
Best for: Sylus. The below-50% bonus directly amplifies Sylus's execute phase. Excellent synergy.

Substat Priority

Substats are randomly rolled when you level a protocore to +3, +6, +9, and +12. Here's the priority order:

1 CRIT Rate — Most valuable substat. Caps at 60% for most builds.
2 CRIT DMG — Second most valuable. Scales with CRIT Rate.
3 ATK% — Flat percentage increase. Always useful.
4 Elemental Mastery — Only if your build relies on elemental damage.
5 ATK (flat) — Better than nothing, worse than percentage.
6 HP% / DEF% — Only for support builds.

The 60/120 Rule: Aim for 60% CRIT Rate and 120% CRIT DMG as your baseline for any DPS operative. Below 60% CRIT Rate, you're losing damage to non-crits. Every point of CRIT Rate below 60% should be your top priority to fix.

Substat Rolling Strategy

When leveling a protocore, you don't know which substat will be upgraded at +3/+6/+9/+12. Here's the optimal strategy:

①
Level to +3: Reveals the first substat. If it's CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG, continue. If it's flat DEF, salvage.
②
Level to +6: Reveals the second substat. If both are useful (CRIT, ATK%, Elemental Mastery), keep going. If either is flat/percent HP or DEF for a DPS, salvage.
③
Level to +9: Third substat revealed. If you have 3+ useful substats, this is a keeper. Otherwise salvage.
④
Level to +12: Final substat. If 3+ rolls went into CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG, this is a god-tier protocore. Lock it immediately.

Expected Rolls: On average, you will need 8-12 protocores to get one with 3+ useful substats. Farming protocores is a long-term game — don't expect perfect rolls quickly. A "good enough" protocore (2 useful substats with 1-2 rolls each) is fine for clearing all content.

Character-Specific Recommendations

Xavier — Full ATK
Slot 3: CRIT Rate. Slot 4: CRIT DMG. Slot 5: Light DMG%. Slot 6: ATK%. Set: 4-piece Solar. Xavier's damage is front-loaded burst. Stack ATK and CRIT equally. Stellar Contract memory gives ATK buff — CRIT becomes more valuable to multiply that buff.
Sylus — Execute Specialist
Slot 3: CRIT Rate. Slot 4: CRIT DMG. Slot 5: Dark DMG%. Slot 6: ATK%. Set: 4-piece Shadow. Sylus's execute can CRIT. Prioritize CRIT DMG over ATK% — the execute multiplier already gives huge base damage, and CRIT DMG multiplies it further.
Zayne — CC Support
Slot 3: CRIT Rate or HP%. Slot 4: ATK% or HP%. Slot 5: Frost DMG% or DEF%. Slot 6: Energy Regen. Set: 4-piece Permafrost. Zayne's damage is secondary to his CC. Energy Regen for faster ultimates is often better than raw damage stats.
Rafayel — Burn Stacker
Slot 3: Elemental Mastery. Slot 4: CRIT DMG. Slot 5: Flame DMG%. Slot 6: ATK%. Set: 2 Solar + 2 Permafrost (mix). Rafayel's burn damage scales with Elemental Mastery — prioritize this over CRIT Rate. Elemental Mastery increases burn tick damage and explosion damage.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Ignoring main stat. A gold protocore with perfect substats but wrong main stat is useless. Slot 5 Elemental DMG% is worth more than any substat combination. Always get the right main stat first, then worry about substats.

Mistake #2: Over-investing in purple (4-star) protocores. Purple protocores have lower stat ranges than gold (5-star). Leveling a purple to +12 is fine as a temporary measure, but don't spend your best substat rolls on purples. Save gold protocores for your main DPS.

Mistake #3: Not checking substat ranges. Gold protocores have stat ranges (e.g., CRIT Rate can roll 2.5%-4.2%). A "low roll" protocore with 4 useful substats is worse than a "high roll" protocore with 3. Check whether your substat rolls are near the maximum before building around them.

Mistake #4: Salvaging too aggressively. A protocore with 2 correct substats and 2 wrong ones is still usable. The "perfect" protocore (4 correct substats with high rolls) is a 1-in-1000 drop. "Good enough" clears all content. Don't salvage usable protocores chasing perfection.

The Bottom Line: Protocores are the deepest optimization system in Love and Deepspace. Get the right main stat first. Aim for CRIT Rate (60%) and CRIT DMG (120%) baseline. Use set bonuses that match your operative. Level protocores strategically — salvage early if substats are bad. And remember: a "good enough" protocore clears all content. Perfect ones are for leaderboard chasing, not Abyss clearing.
