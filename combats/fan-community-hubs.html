Platform Overview

The Love and Deepspace fan community is vast and decentralized, with major hubs across multiple platforms serving different purposes. Discord is the heart of real-time conversation, with dedicated channels for theorycrafting, fan art, build discussion, and general chat. The official server hosts developer announcements and feedback channels, while fan-run servers cater to specific interests like deep lore analysis or competitive combat optimization. Reddit offers structured, threaded discussions with subreddits for the main game, lore deep dives, fan art, and cosplay showcases — each with its own posting culture and content expectations.

Twitter/X serves as the primary platform for art sharing and creator networking, with operative-specific hashtags and a vibrant commission economy. Bilibili dominates the video content space, hosting livestreams, memory card showcases, and fan-made analysis videos with active comment sections. The official HoYoLAB-like community platform (accessible in-game) provides structured guides, official announcements, and a lightly moderated gallery space. Tumblr, while smaller, hosts a thriving writing community with detailed headcanons, fan fiction, and character studies that often seed ideas across other platforms.

Discord Server Directory

Discord is the most active real-time community space for Love and Deepspace. Each server has a distinct focus, culture, and set of channels. Here are the recommended servers sorted by purpose.

OFFICIAL SERVER
Love and Deepspace Official Discord
Focus: Developer announcements, patch notes, official event details, and player feedback. Invite culture: Verified through official social media links. Strict moderation ensures reliable information. Active channels: #announcements (news and patch notes), #feedback (constructive suggestions for developers), #general-chat (community discussion), #media-sharing (screenshots and clips). Best for staying up to date with official news and providing direct feedback to the development team.
THEORY SERVER
Deepspace Theory Collective
Focus: Deep lore analysis, narrative speculation, timeline reconstruction, and character motivation study. Invite culture: Fan-run with dedicated lore channels. Welcomes theorists of all levels and encourages evidence-based discussion with sourced arguments. Active channels: #lore-debates (structured lore discussions with citation expectations), #timeline-theory (chronological analysis of story events), #operative-analysis (character-specific deep dives), #evidence-board (screenshots and text references organized by topic).
ART SERVER
Love and Deepspace Art Hub
Focus: Fan art creation, critique exchange, collaboration projects, and art resources. Invite culture: Artist-friendly with constructive critique channels. All skill levels welcome. Credit and attribution rules strictly enforced. Active channels: #art-gallery (finalized pieces for showcase), #wip-feedback (work-in-progress critique requests), #art-resources (brushes, tutorials, reference packs), #collab-projects (zine signups, art trade matching).
BUILD SERVER
Abyss Build Lab
Focus: Combat team building, protocore optimization, Abyss floor strategies, and damage calculation. Invite culture: Data-driven with spreadsheets and damage formulas. Focus on practical advice with evidence. Active channels: #team-building (personalized team recommendations), #protocore-advice (stats and set optimization), #abyss-strategy (floor-by-floor guides), #dps-calc (custom calculation requests and formula discussion).

The official server is the only server directly affiliated with Papergames. Fan-run servers are independent community efforts. Always verify server legitimacy before sharing personal information.

Reddit Communities

Reddit hosts the largest structured discussion space for Love and Deepspace. Each subreddit has distinct rules, posting culture, and optimal content types. Use the right subreddit for your content to get the best engagement.

r/LoveAndDeepspace — Main Subreddit
Focus: General discussion, news, questions, and all-purpose community content. Rules: No low-effort posts, credit all fan art sources, spoiler tag story content for 30 days after release, no account trading. Posting style: Flaired posts mandatory (Discussion, Question, Media, Guide, Lore, Cosplay, Fluff). Text posts encouraged for build questions. Best content: Build guides with detailed reasoning, memory card reviews, story reaction threads, and high-quality fan art with proper credit.
r/LADS_Lore — Lore Subreddit
Focus: Deep lore analysis, timeline discussions, character backstory, worldbuilding, and narrative theorycrafting. Rules: Spoiler tags required for any content within 60 days of story release, sources must be cited for factual claims, speculative content must be clearly labeled. Posting style: Long-form text posts encouraged. Comment debates are expected to be civil and evidence-based. Best content: Timeline reconstruction posts, operative backstory analysis, Wanderer origin theories, and cross-referenced lore compilations.
r/LADS_Art — Fan Art Subreddit
Focus: Dedicated fan art showcase space separate from the main subreddit's general feed. Rules: Art must be original or properly credited to the original creator. AI-generated art is not permitted. Reposting requires explicit permission from the original artist. Posting style: Single image posts with the artist's social handle in the title. OC (Original Content) tag for self-made art. Best content: Complete illustrations, chibi comics, memory card redraws, and outfit design concepts. Progress shots and timelapse videos also perform well.
r/LADS_Cosplay — Cosplay Subreddit
Focus: Cosplay photography, prop making, costume construction guides, and convention meetup coordination. Rules: All cosplayers must be 18+ to post. Credit photographers and makeup artists. No self-promotion beyond cosplay content. Posting style: Multi-image albums preferred. Description should include costume source (which operative/outfit), event or shoot location, and credits. Best content: High-quality cosplay photos that capture each operative's distinctive style — Sylus's sharp tailoring, Xavier's celestial motifs, Zayne's elegant coats, and Rafayel's flowing silhouettes.

Reddit etiquette tip: Sort by New or Rising to engage with fresh content rather than upvote-farming on front page posts. Commenting on new posts is the best way to build community connections.

Cross-posting tip: If you create content that fits multiple subreddits (e.g., a lore-rich cosplay), post to the most specific subreddit first, then cross-post to the main sub with a note directing to the original post.

Content Sharing Best Practices

Each platform has its own culture and expectations. Following platform-specific best practices ensures your content reaches the right audience and is well received by the community.

Reddit — Text Posts with Sources
When posting guides or lore analysis on Reddit, use the Guide or Lore flair and include screenshots or links as supporting evidence. Text posts should be well structured with clear headings and bullet points for readability. Avoid linking exclusively to external sites — Reddit prefers self-contained content. For build advice, include your memory levels, protocore stats, and team composition so commenters can give targeted feedback. Flair correctly: Misflaired posts are often removed or downvoted. If unsure, check what flairs similar posts use.
Twitter/X — Hashtag Strategy & Art Etiquette
Use operative-specific hashtags alongside the main game tag. Recommended tags: #LoveAndDeepspace (main tag), #LoveAndDeepspaceArt (art-specific), #LaDS_Xavier, #LaDS_Zayne, #LaDS_Rafayel, #LaDS_Sylus (operative-specific), #LaDSFanart (discovery tag). When sharing art, tag the game's official account @LoveDeepspaceEN for potential retweets. Credit any collaborators in the first tweet of the thread. For commission posts, use #LaDSCommission and clearly state your status (open/closed) and pricing in your bio or pinned tweet.
Discord — Channel Etiquette & Attribution
Always share content in the correct channel — art in #art-gallery or #media-sharing, builds in #team-building or #abyss-strategy, lore in #lore-debates or #evidence-board. Credit and attribution: When sharing someone else's art or guide, include the creator's name or handle and a link to the original post. Discord servers have zero tolerance for uncredited reposts. Use thread replies instead of @mentions when responding to specific messages. Avoid pinging roles unnecessarily. Read the server's #rules and #faq channels before posting.
Bilibili — Tagging & Description Links
Bilibili's algorithm relies heavily on tags and descriptions. Use relevant Chinese-language tags: #恋与深空 (Love and Deepspace), #深空猎人 (Deepspace Hunter), plus operative names in Chinese. Include a detailed description with timestamps for long videos. Add links to your other platforms in the description or pinned comment. For guide videos, include a text summary in the description so viewers can quickly assess relevance. Engage with commenters — Bilibili's community values creator interaction highly.

Tip: Create a consistent handle across all platforms. Having the same username on Discord, Twitter/X, Reddit, and Bilibili makes it easy for community members to recognize you and find your content across platforms. Link your other accounts in each platform's bio or about section to build a cross-platform presence.

Community Events & Traditions

The Love and Deepspace community runs a rich calendar of events and traditions that bring fans together across platforms. Here's how to participate in the most notable ones.

Annual Operative Birthday Celebrations
Each operative's birthday is celebrated with community-wide events across all platforms. The schedule follows in-game canon birthdates: Xavier (February 18), Zayne (March 10), Rafayel (September 21), Sylus (November 15). Activities include: coordinated art shares on Twitter using #HappyBirthday[OperativeName], Discord voice chat parties with themed activities, Reddit megathreads for sharing birthday fan works, and community-organized birthday zines. To participate, prepare your contribution (art, fic, or message) in advance and post it on the day using the event hashtag. The community often creates collaborative birthday gifts like compilation videos or multi-artist illustration sets.
Subreddit Competitions
The main subreddit and art subreddit host regular competitions with community-voted winners. Fan art contests run quarterly with themed prompts (e.g., "Operatives in Modern AU," "Memory Card Redraw Challenge"). Winners receive Reddit awards and are featured in the subreddit sidebar. Theory writing challenges on the lore subreddit invite deep analysis on specific topics (e.g., "The Origin of Wanderers," "What Lies Beyond the Deepspace Tunnel"). Winning entries are archived in the subreddit wiki. Check pinned posts and community announcements for current and upcoming competition schedules.
Discord Watch Parties
When new story chapters, memory card stories, or major events release, community Discord servers host synchronized watch parties. Participants play through the content at the same time while discussing reactions in voice chat or dedicated text channels. Watch parties are announced in advance through #events channels. To participate: be in the server at the scheduled time, have the game ready on the relevant content, and join the designated voice channel. Spoiler channels are active during watch parties, so avoid main chat if you haven't completed the content yet. Post-watch discussion threads remain open for 48 hours for those in different time zones.

How to get started: Join at least one Discord server and one subreddit. Introduce yourself in the welcome channel. Comment on posts that interest you. Don't be afraid to ask questions — the Love and Deepspace community is known for being welcoming to new members. The more you engage, the more you'll discover, and the more connections you'll build across the fandom.

Summary: The Love and Deepspace fan community is a vibrant, multi-platform ecosystem. Discord offers real-time community across official and fan-run servers for theory, art, and builds. Reddit provides structured discussion across main, lore, art, and cosplay subreddits. Twitter/X is the hub for art sharing and creator networking with a robust hashtag system. Bilibili dominates video content with a tag-driven discovery system. Community traditions include operative birthday celebrations, subreddit competitions, and Discord watch parties. The key to a rewarding community experience is participation: join a server, introduce yourself, use flairs and hashtags correctly, and engage genuinely with other fans.
