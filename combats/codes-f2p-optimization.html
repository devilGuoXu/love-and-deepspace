F2P Philosophy: Abundance Within Limits

The first and most important thing to understand about F2P play is mindset. Love and Deepspace, like all gacha games, is designed to make you feel that spending will solve your problems. It will — to a degree. But the game is also designed to be completable by F2P players who play smart. The key is accepting what you cannot have and maximizing what you can.

As an F2P player, you will not get every SSR memory card. You will not max-rank every operative. You will not have unlimited stamina. These limitations are not failures — they are constraints that define your strategy. The F2P player's advantage is decision clarity: because you have fewer resources, every choice matters more, and the satisfaction of a well-executed plan is correspondingly greater.

The F2P golden rules are: never spend diamonds on standard banner pulls, never refresh stamina with diamonds except for event farming, always complete daily and weekly missions, always participate in limited events even if you only claim the free rewards, and save for banners that provide the most account value. These rules are not restrictions — they are the framework for F2P success.

Diamond Income: Every Source Analyzed

Understanding your diamond income is the foundation of F2P planning. Here is the complete breakdown of diamond sources available to F2P players.

Daily Sources. Daily missions reward 50 diamonds per day, totaling 1,500 per 30-day month. This is your most reliable income source and should never be missed — missing one day costs 50 diamonds, which translates to approximately 0.3 pulls on a banner.

Weekly Sources. Weekly missions provide 200 diamonds per week (800 per month). Abyssal Trials weekly rewards vary by floor cleared but average 150-300 diamonds per week for a mid-game account. Senior Hunter Contest provides 200 diamonds per week if you can clear at least 9 stars. Total weekly: 400-700 diamonds.

Event Sources. Limited events provide an average of 800-1,200 diamonds per event through event shops, milestone rewards, and mission completion. With 2-3 events per month, this represents 1,600-3,600 monthly diamonds. Major version updates also provide maintenance compensation of 300-600 diamonds.

One-Time Sources. Memory card stories (Tender Moments, Secret Times) provide 30-50 diamonds each for first-time completion. Operative affinity milestones reward diamonds at key thresholds. Achievement completion provides a significant one-time diamond injection. These sources are finite but substantial, totaling roughly 5,000-8,000 diamonds over the first few months of play.

Monthly F2P Diamond Estimate. A diligent F2P player can expect approximately 5,000-7,000 diamonds per month from all recurring sources combined. This translates to 31-43 pulls per month. Over a 3-month banner cycle, an F2P player can save 15,000-21,000 diamonds (94-131 pulls), which guarantees hitting soft pity once with good chance of a second pity.

Stamina Management: Daily & Weekly

Stamina (also called Energy or Resin depending on your server) is the most consistently undervalued resource by new F2P players. While diamonds are for pulling, stamina is for account growth — and account growth determines your ability to earn more diamonds through content clearing.

Daily Stamina Budget. An F2P player regenerates 240 stamina per day (10 per hour) plus the 60-stamina daily login reward, totaling 300 stamina per day. Additional stamina can be obtained from: event rewards (variable), level-up refills (one-time), and friendship points (20 per day from active friends). The total daily stamina budget for an established F2P player is approximately 320 stamina.

Spending Priority. The order of stamina spending should be: protocore farming for main team (highest long-term value), memory card ascension materials (as needed for priority cards), character level-up materials (until your main team reaches level 60), and gold/general materials (only when needed). Never spend stamina on standard memory card farming unless you have a specific need — the drop rates are too low for general farming.

Event Stamina. During events, the priority shifts. Event stages provide additional rewards beyond standard farming. As a general rule: if the event shop has memoria or special enhancement materials, farming the event stage is worth diverting 50-70% of your daily stamina. If the event shop only has standard materials, use only the free event attempts and spend stamina on normal farming.

Stamina Refresh Analysis. F2P players should NEVER refresh stamina with diamonds except in one specific scenario: during a limited event where the event shop has unique materials that cannot be farmed elsewhere, and you are confident you will not need those diamonds for an upcoming banner within the next 2 weeks. Even then, limit refreshes to one per day (50 diamonds). The value proposition of stamina refresh is poor for F2P players because 50 diamonds equals 0.3 pulls, and the expected return from those 60 stamina is rarely worth more than 0.3 pulls worth of account value.

Banner Strategy: When to Pull

Banner selection is the single most impactful decision an F2P player makes. One correct banner choice can save months of resource accumulation; one incorrect choice can set an account back significantly.

Never Pull on Standard Banner. The standard banner pity system is separate from limited banners, and the pool is diluted. Your free standard banner pulls (from leveling, events, and achievements) will accumulate naturally. Never spend diamonds on standard banner pulls.

Limited Banner Evaluation Framework. Before pulling on any limited banner, evaluate: how does this memory card improve my account? Does it enable a new team composition? Does it significantly upgrade an existing operative? Will it be useful in Abyssal Trials? Is the operative one I enjoy playing? An F2P player should only pull if at least 3 of these 5 questions are yes.

Save for Synergy. The most efficient F2P strategy is to save for memory cards that work together. A team with two synergistic SSR cards at R0 will outperform a team with four unrelated SSR cards at R0. Focus on building one strong team rather than spreading resources across all four operatives.

The 180-Pull Rule. Never start pulling on a banner unless you have at least 180 pulls saved (28,800 diamonds). This guarantees you can reach hard pity if necessary. Starting a banner with fewer pulls risks not getting the card and wasting your pity progress. The only exception is if you are okay with not getting the card and are building pity for a future banner — but this is not recommended for F2P players.

Banner Calendar Awareness. Pay attention to banner leak and prediction communities. Knowing what banners are coming in the next 4-8 weeks allows informed saving decisions. If a major banner is coming, skip the current banner even if it is decent. F2P success is built on patience and information.

Event Optimization: Max Value Per Hour

Events are the F2P player's primary source of bonus resources. Optimizing event participation is essential for account growth.

Always Complete Event Dailies. Most events have daily missions that take 5-10 minutes. These missions typically reward event currency, diamonds, and materials. The cumulative value over a 2-week event is substantial. Never skip event dailies.

Event Shop Priority. Event shops follow a predictable value hierarchy. First priority: limited items that cannot be obtained elsewhere (memory cards, exclusive materials, namecards). Second priority: high-value renewable materials (wishes, enhancement crystals, protocore packs). Third priority: standard materials (gold, exp books, ascension materials). Skip cosmetic items (frames, icons) unless you have surplus currency after claiming all high-value items.

Free Event Pass vs Paid. Most events have a free track and a paid track. As an F2P player, you only have access to the free track. However, the free track usually contains 60-70% of the total event value. Do not feel pressured to buy the paid track — the free track is designed to be worthwhile on its own.

Event Story Rewards. Limited event stories are only available during the event period. They typically reward 200-400 diamonds total for first-time completion. Even if you are not interested in the story, rush through it for the diamond rewards. These diamonds are time-limited and will not return (except in potential reruns).

Memory Card Investment Priority

F2P players cannot afford to level every memory card. Investment priority is critical.

Leveling Order. Main DPS SSR to 60 (highest priority), support SSR to 50, sub-DPS SSR to 50, then fill remaining team slots with level 40 cards that provide set bonuses. Never level a card past 60 as F2P — the cost per level increases dramatically beyond 60, and the returns diminish.

Skill Level Priority. Main DPS Ardent Oath (highest priority), Main DPS Resonance Skill, Support Resonance Skill, Sub-DPS Ardent Oath. Basic attack levels are lowest priority for most operatives. Only level skills that provide clear damage or utility improvements — read the skill descriptions carefully.

Memory Set Strategy. Prioritize completing 2-piece set bonuses over 4-piece sets. A 2+2 set configuration (two different 2-piece bonuses) is often comparable to a 4-piece set and is much easier to assemble with limited card pool. Only commit to a 4-piece set if you have the core cards identified.

Protocore F2P Strategy

Protocores are the great equalizer in Love and Deepspace. A well-farmed protocore setup can bridge the gap between a modest card collection and an expensive one.

Farm Strategy. Focus on one protocore set for your main DPS operative. Do not spread farming across multiple sets until your main team is fully equipped. The expected timeline to get a good main-stat set with acceptable substats is 2-4 weeks of dedicated farming.

Main Stat Priority. Slot Alpha (ATK% or Elemental DMG% depending on operative), Slot Beta (Crit Rate or Crit DMG based on ratio), Slot Gamma (ATK% or Energy Regen), Slot Delta (ATK% or Healing Bonus for supports). Getting the correct main stat is worth more than perfect substats.

Substat Strategy. As F2P, you cannot afford to be picky about substats. Acceptable substat thresholds: 2+ relevant substats is keepable, 3+ is excellent, 4 is perfect but not expected. Do not throw away a correct main-stat protocore because it has 2 bad substats — the main stat contributes 60-70% of the protocore's value.

Abyssal Trials: F2P Clearing Strategy

Abyssal Trials are the primary endgame content and a major diamond source. F2P clearing strategy differs significantly from spender strategy.

Team Depth Over Strength. Abyssal Trials require 2-3 teams. F2P players should prioritize having 6-8 usable characters at level 50+ rather than 2-3 hyper-invested characters at level 70+. Spread resources to meet team requirements.

Stellactrum Matching Is Non-Negotiable. The damage bonus from matching Stellactrum colors is too large to ignore. Build your teams around color coverage first, character preference second. An off-color team at 50% matching deals approximately 30% less effective damage.

Floor Target Strategy. Do not expect to clear floor 12 as F2P. Floor 9-10 clear is realistic. Floor 11 is achievable with excellent play and good luck. Floor 12 requires either specific meta cards or months of dedicated account building. Set realistic floor targets and celebrate progress.

Shop Guide: What to Buy, What to Skip

The in-game shop is designed to tempt F2P players into spending. Understanding what is actually worth purchasing with free currency is essential.

Monthly Packs. The free monthly pack (available in the shop) provides 10 standard pulls per month. Always claim this. The premium monthly pack requires Aurum Pass — skip as F2P.

Stardust Shop. The most important shop for F2P players. Save Stardust for: SSR memory card crates (highest priority), limited-time wishes, and enhancement materials. Never spend Stardust on standard wishes or cosmetic items.

Crystal Shop. The paid currency shop is irrelevant for F2P players. Ignore it completely.

Event Shops. As covered in the events section: priority is limited items, wishes, materials, then cosmetics last.

Long-Term Account Planning

F2P success is measured in months, not weeks. Long-term planning separates accounts that stagnate from accounts that grow.

3-Month Plan. Month 1-2: Build one functional team to level 50-60, clear story content, establish daily routine. Month 3: Begin Abyssal Trial attempts, expand to second team, start protocore optimization. This timeline assumes consistent daily play.

6-Month Plan. Two teams at level 60. Consistent Abyssal floor 9-10 clear. Senior Hunter Contest at 12+ stars. Ready to pull for targeted banner upgrades. At this point, the account is established and the F2P resource management skills should be second nature.

1-Year Plan. Three teams operational. Ability to clear floor 11 with favorable rotation. Deep protocore inventory for each team. Enough accumulated resources to guarantee a banner of choice on release. The 1-year F2P account should be competitive in most game modes.

Common F2P Myths Debunked

Several persistent myths about F2P play cause players to make suboptimal decisions. Here are the most common ones, corrected.

Myth: F2P cannot clear endgame content. False. F2P players can clear all story content, events, and Abyssal floors 9-10. Floor 11-12 are significantly harder but achievable with excellent play and targeted card investment.

Myth: You need to pull every banner to stay relevant. False. F2P success comes from skipping more banners than you pull on. Each pull on a non-essential banner delays your ability to guarantee an essential one.

Myth: Protocore farming is a waste for F2P. False. Protocore farming is the highest-ROI activity for F2P players because protocores are equally accessible to spenders and non-spenders. A F2P player with excellent protocores will outperform a spender with bad protocores.

Myth: You should always pull for your favorite character. This is a preference question, not a factual one. If pulling for favorites makes the game more enjoyable, do it. But be honest about the trade-off: pulling for favorites means accepting that you will clear less endgame content. Both choices are valid — just be intentional about yours.

The F2P Mindset Shift: The most important change an F2P player can make is moving from "I can't afford that" to "I choose to save for what matters." Every skipped banner is not a loss — it is a strategic reserve for the banner that will define your account. The F2P path is not deprivation; it is disciplined abundance.

Summary: F2P success in Love and Deepspace is built on four pillars: maximizing diamond income from all sources, disciplined banner selection and saving, optimal stamina allocation, and realistic goal-setting for endgame content. A F2P player who follows these principles can clear all story content, participate fully in events, and achieve competitive results in endgame modes. The gap between F2P and spending players exists, but smart play narrows it significantly. The most important resources are not diamonds or stamina — they are patience and information.
