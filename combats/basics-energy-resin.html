Energy & Resin Guide

Master the stamina economy — when to spend, when to save, and when to refresh for maximum account value.

ENTRY_#0722026.07.02ENERGY

How to Read This: If you are new, follow the Early Game section. If you are mid-game, read the Refresh Value analysis before spending any diamonds on stamina. Veterans should review the Endgame section for max-efficiency routing.

Stamina Basics

You regenerate 1 stamina per 6 minutes (240 stamina per day). Your cap is 180 stamina — anything above that is wasted. The game gives you two free stamina packs per day (120 stamina each) at specific times. Claim them every day without exception. You also earn stamina from level-ups, events, battle passes, and special codes. Total daily free stamina: 240 base + 240 lunch/dinner = 480 stamina per day baseline. Events can push this above 600.

Where to Spend: Priority Order

Not all stamina uses are equal. Here is the value hierarchy: 1) Weekly Boss ×3 (required for awakening materials, do this first every week). 2) Simulation Chamber / Protocore Domains (best value after you reach level 50 — protocores are the only infinite stat ceiling). 3) Bounty Hunt / Level-Up Materials (only spend what you need to ascend your top 4 memories). 4) Memory Domains (for shard farming — only if you have a specific awakening target). 5) Heartbreaker / Ascension Domains (farm only when you need specific awakening materials). 6) Deepspace Voyage (passive income — always keep expeditions running, but do not spend extra stamina here). Avoid: Spending stamina on gold domains. Gold income from events and battle pass is sufficient for most accounts.

Refresh Value: Should You Spend Diamonds?

A stamina refresh costs 60 diamonds for 120 stamina. The question: is this worth it? For new players (level 30-60): Yes — the accelerated progress is worth approximately 120 diamonds worth of stats. Refresh once per day. For mid-game players (level 60-70): Only if you have a specific goal (e.g., finishing a protocore set before Abyss resets). Otherwise, save diamonds for limited banners. For endgame players (level 70+): Generally not worth it. Your account gains more from an extra limited SSR pull per 9 days than from 120 stamina. Exception: during double-drop events, refresh up to 3 times per day. Double-drop stamina is 2x more valuable than regular stamina.

Resin Usage: Resin allows you to bank stamina beyond the 180 cap. You can hold up to 5 resin (each = 60 stamina, 18 minutes to charge). Best use cases: (1) Use before bed so you wake up with 300+ stamina instead of 180. (2) Save 5 resin for double-drop events to claim 600 stamina instantly. (3) Never use resin during normal play unless you are about to cap on banked resin charges. Resin is a convenience tool, not a power tool — it does not give you extra stamina, it just shifts when you can use it.

Early Game Routing (Level 1-50)

Your goal is to reach level 50 as fast as possible to unlock the best protocore domains. The fastest route: (1) Spend all stamina on Bounty Hunt for memory level-up materials. (2) Focus on 2 operatives, 4 memories each. (3) Do not spend stamina on protocores until level 45+. (4) Claim all achievement and code stamina rewards immediately — do not let them expire. Expected timeline: Level 50 in 14-18 days of active play. Speed depends on how many codes and event stamina packs you can claim.

Mid Game Routing (Level 50-70)

This is the longest phase. Your stamina priorities shift: (1) Simulation Chamber / protocore domains become your primary stamina sink. (2) Weekly bosses remain mandatory. (3) Memory shard domains only when you have a specific awakening target. (4) Skip level-up material farming unless you have a new memory to level. The trap: Farming too many different domains. Stick to 1-2 domain types per day. Domain hopping wastes stamina on load screens and encourages unfocused spending.

Summary: 480 free stamina per day baseline. Priority: Weekly bosses > Protocore domains > Memory level-up > Everything else. One daily refresh is good for early game, skip for endgame. Resin is for convenience, not extra stamina. Double-drop events are the best time to refresh. Never let stamina sit at cap — that is permanently lost progress.
