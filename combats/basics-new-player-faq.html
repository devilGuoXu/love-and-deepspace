New Player Mistakes FAQ

10 most common mistakes new players make — and how to avoid every single one of them.

ENTRY_#0712026.07.02FAQ

Read This If: You are in your first month of Love and Deepspace. Avoiding these 10 mistakes will save you weeks of wasted progress and hundreds of dollars in inefficient pulls.

#1 — Wasting Limited Pulls on Every Banner

The mistake: Pulling on every limited banner as soon as it drops, spreading wishes across 3-4 different banner characters. Why it hurts: You need 14,400 diamonds to guarantee a limited SSR. If you spend 5,000 on each of 3 banners, you get zero SSRs (or 1 at low pity). If you save 14,400 for one banner, you guarantee one SSR. The fix: Pick one limited banner that matches your main operative. Save until you can guarantee it. Pull. Repeat. Do not pull on a limited banner unless you have enough to hit hard pity.

#2 — Not Picking 2 Main Operatives

The mistake: Trying to level all 4 operatives equally in the first month. Why it hurts: Resources in the first month are extremely scarce. Spreading them across 4 operatives means none of them are strong enough to clear content. Content is designed for 2-person teams. The fix: Pick 2 operatives on day 1. Level them. Build teams around them. Ignore the other 2 until month 3-4. Your two operatives at level 70 will clear more content than 4 operatives at level 50. You can build the others later with surplus resources.

#3 — Skipping Events

The mistake: Ignoring event content because "it takes too long" or "I want to focus on the main story." Why it hurts: Events are the most stamina-efficient content in the game. A single event shop contains 2-3 weeks worth of normal farming materials. Event-only rewards (limited memories, exclusive cosmetics) are gone forever if you skip them. The fix: Always clear the event shop of high-value items (wishes, SSR crates, purple gems). Prioritize event stamina consumption during the event period. You can clear main story anytime — events are time-limited.

#4 — Ignoring Support Memories

The mistake: Only leveling DPS memories and ignoring support (healer, buffer, tank) memories. Why it hurts: Support memories at level 60-70 with correct gear provide massive survivability and damage amplification. A team with a well-built support clears 20-30% faster than a team of all DPS. The fix: Level at least 2 support memories (one per team) to level 60-70. Give them correct protocores (HP%/DEF% for tanks, Energy Regen for healers). Your support does not need to deal damage — they need to keep your DPS alive and generating energy.

#5 — Spreading Resources Too Thin

The mistake: Leveling every memory you own to level 30-40 "just in case." Why it hurts: Leveling a memory to 40 wastes resources that could have gone to taking one memory to 70. A level 70 memory + level 40 memory = less total power than two level 60 memories. The fix: Only level memories you actively use in your weekly boss teams and Abyss teams. Everyone else stays at level 1. When you need a new memory, power-level it from 1 to 60 in one session rather than spreading XP across 10 memories.

#6 — Pulling on Standard Banner with Diamonds

The mistake: Using diamonds on the standard banner (XSpace Echo) instead of limited banners. Why it hurts: The standard banner has no pity timer that carries over. You get free standard wishes from BP, events, and achievements. Using diamonds on standard is 50-60% less efficient than using them on limited banners. The fix: Only use free wishes on the standard banner. Save diamonds exclusively for limited banners. The standard banner is a trap for impatient players.

#7 — Neglecting Protocores

The mistake: Ignoring protocores until level 70+ content forces you to care. Why it hurts: Protocores are 40-50% of your total combat power. A character with good protocores at level 50 outperforms a character with bad protocores at level 70. The fix: Start farming protocores at domain level 50-60. Use ATK% main stat pieces even if substats are bad. Replace them with better pieces later. The difference between a character with +0 protocores and +12 protocores is approximately 2x damage.

#8 — Not Using Consumables

The mistake: Saving all consumables "for later" and never using them. Why it hurts: Consumables (food buffs, potions) give 10-15% free power that costs only gold and ingredients. Hoarding them is the same as throwing away free progress. The fix: Use ATK food before every weekly boss attempt. Use CRIT food before SHC runs. Use potions in Abyss attempts. The resource cost is minimal and the benefit is immediate. You get more ingredients every day from the Home system.

#9 — Ignoring Weekly Caps

The mistake: Letting weekly boss entries cap at 3/3 without running them. Why it hurts: Each missed weekly boss entry is lost upgrade materials and a chance at SSR protocores. Over a month, missed weekly bosses = missing 12+ runs = one full ascension tier worth of materials. The fix: Run your 9 weekly boss entries before Monday reset every week. Mark it on your calendar. Weekly reset is the most important recurring event in your routine.

#10 — Rushing Story Mode

The mistake: Speed-clicking through story dialogue to "get to the endgame." Why it hurts: Story progression gates many features (Home system, certain domains, some events). But the story is not going anywhere. Endgame content requires gear, not story completion. Rushing through means you miss lore, character development, and important context for events. The fix: Enjoy the story at your own pace. There is no race. Endgame content is gated by your account's power level, not how fast you cleared chapter 8. Take your time and build your account while enjoying the narrative.

Bonus Mistake — #11 Not Asking for Help: The LADS community is very helpful. If you are stuck, ask. Most problems (which memory to level, which banner to pull, which team to build) have clear answers. Do not struggle for days with a problem that someone else solved months ago.

Summary — The 10 Rules: (1) Save 14,400 diamonds for limited banners. (2) Pick 2 main operatives. (3) Always clear event shops. (4) Level support memories. (5) Focus resources, do not spread thin. (6) Never use diamonds on standard banner. (7) Farm protocores early. (8) Use consumables every fight. (9) Do not miss weekly bosses. (10) Do not rush the story. Follow these rules and your first 3 months will be smooth sailing.
