New Player Mistakes FAQ

Every new player makes the same mistakes. Here are the 10 most common ones — skip them and save yourself weeks of wasted progress.

ENTRY_#0712026.07.02FAQ

How to Read This: If you are a new player, read all 10 mistakes right now. If you have already made some of them, do not worry — the game forgives most mistakes. The only truly costly mistakes are wasting limited pulls and converting SSR duplicates. Everything else can be fixed with time.

1. Spending Limited Pulls on the Standard Banner

The Mistake: Using Deepspace Wishes (limited pink tickets) on the Standard Banner because "I want a specific standard character." Why It Is Wrong: Limited banners have a 50/50 rate-up. Standard banner has no rate-up. You are 50% less likely to get what you want on the standard banner. The Fix: Only use Deepspace Wishes on limited character event banners. Standard banner gets the free blue tickets you earn from gameplay. Never convert blue tickets to pink tickets or vice versa.

2. Leveling All Operatives Evenly

The Mistake: Leveling Xavier to 20, then Sylus to 20, then Zayne to 20, then Rafayel to 20. Why It Is Wrong: A team of 4 level-20 operatives cannot clear Abyss floor 3. A team of 2 level-50 operatives + 1 level-1 can clear Abyss floor 7. The Fix: Pick 2 operatives. Level them to 50+ before touching anyone else. Your third slot can be filled by any operative — they are just there for set bonuses.

3. Ignoring Protocores Until Level 50+

The Mistake: "Protocores are endgame content, I will worry about them later." Why It Is Wrong: Protocores give 40%+ of your total stats. A level-40 operative with +9 protocores is stronger than a level-50 operative with +0 protocores. The Fix: Start equipping protocores at level 30. Level them to +6 at minimum. The gold cost is negligible and the stat gain is enormous.

4. Converting SSR Duplicates to XP

The Mistake: "I already have this SSR memory, so I will convert the duplicate to XP crystals." Why It Is Wrong: SSR duplicates are used for Awakening (R1/R2). R1 gives a unique passive that is worth more than any amount of XP. The Fix: Never convert an SSR duplicate until you have reached R2 on that memory. Even then, converting should be a last resort.

5. Not Doing Mini-Game Events

The Mistake: "Mini-games are a waste of time. I will farm domains instead." Why It Is Wrong: Mini-game events give 800-1,200 free diamonds with zero stamina cost. That is 5-8 limited pulls for 45 minutes of play. Domains give zero diamonds. The Fix: Always clear mini-game events before they expire. They are the best diamond-per-minute activity in the game.

6. Sleeping on Perfect Dodge: "I can tank hits with Zayne's shield." Wrong — perfect dodging gives energy for Ardent Oath. Without perfect dodges, you use Oath once per fight. With perfect dodges, you use Oath 3-4 times. Practice perfect dodge on easy content until it becomes automatic. 7. Saving All Resources for "The Perfect Banner": "I will save every diamond for 6 months until my favorite character gets a rate-up." Wrong — you need a baseline team to clear content that gives diamonds. Spend enough to build a functional team, then save for your target banner. 8. Ignoring the Event Shop: "The event shop items are too expensive." Wrong — the event shop SSR memory (3,000 currency) is the best value in the game. An SSR memory costs 14,000 diamonds (worst-case pity) from banners. The event shop gives one for 3,600 stamina worth of farming. 9. Not Reading Enemy Attack Patterns: "I will just dodge when I see something coming." Wrong — every enemy has specific telegraphs. Learning them lets you perfect dodge reliably. Read the Wanderer Encyclopedia entries on this site. 10. Playing on Auto-Too Much: "Auto-battle is good enough." Wrong — auto-battle cannot perfect dodge, cannot use Ardent Oath optimally, and cannot focus-fire priority targets. Manual play is 30-50% more effective than auto in difficult content.

Bonus: The One Mistake That Actually Hurts

Most mistakes in Deepspace are fixable with time and stamina. The one mistake that can permanently hurt your account is: quitting the game for 3+ months and missing limited events. Limited SSR memories, outfits, and event protocores do not return for 6-12 months (or ever, for collabs). If you take a break, you will permanently miss some content. This is the only mistake that cannot be fixed. Play consistently, even if only 10 minutes per day.

Summary: 10 mistakes to avoid: (1) Limited pulls on standard banner. (2) Leveling everyone evenly. (3) Ignoring protocores. (4) Converting SSR dupes. (5) Skipping mini-games. (6) Not perfect dodging. (7) Oversaving for future banners. (8) Ignoring event shop. (9) Not learning attack patterns. (10) Relying on auto-battle. Bonus: Do not quit for extended periods — limited content is truly limited.
