Effective HP Formula

Key Concept: Effective HP (EHP) = HP / (1 − Damage Reduction). DEF, shields, and healing all feed into a single survivability metric. EHP tells you how much raw damage your operative can survive before factoring in any heals.

EHP is the true measure of survivability. Your displayed HP is misleading because DEF reduces all incoming damage by a percentage. Shield absorption and healing over the fight duration add additional layers. The unified formula is:

EHP = (HP + Shield) / (1 − DEF_DMG_Reduction)

DEF damage reduction is calculated using a diminishing-returns formula common to most RPGs: DEF_DMG_Reduction = DEF / (DEF + Constant). The constant varies by content level. At Abyss floor 10, the constant is approximately 4,000. At floor 12, it is 5,200. Higher content = higher constant = less effective DEF.

1,000 DEF at floor 101000 / (1000 + 4000) = 20% reduction → EHP ×1.25
2,000 DEF at floor 102000 / (2000 + 4000) = 33% reduction → EHP ×1.50
3,000 DEF at floor 103000 / (3000 + 4000) = 43% reduction → EHP ×1.75
4,000 DEF at floor 104000 / (4000 + 4000) = 50% reduction → EHP ×2.00

Example: An operative with 20,000 HP, 2,000 DEF, and a 5,000 shield at Abyss floor 10 has EHP = (20000 + 5000) / (1 − 0.33) = 25,000 / 0.67 = 37,313 EHP. That is nearly double the raw HP pool. Every point of DEF and every shield point multiplies your survivability.

Rule of Thumb: 1,000 DEF ≈ 25% EHP multiplier. 2,000 DEF ≈ 50% EHP multiplier. Every additional 1,000 DEF past 2,000 gives diminishing returns — the multiplier gain shrinks from +25% to +17% to +12%. Past 3,000 DEF, each 1,000 DEF is worth less than 12% EHP increase. Invest in DEF only until the next HP% roll gives more EHP.

DEF vs HP% on Protocores

One of the most common build questions: should a main stat slot use DEF% or HP%? The answer depends entirely on your operative's base DEF. The two stats scale differently and have a mathematical crossover point.

600 +180 DEF → +3.6% EHP +3,000 HP → +4.8% EHP HP% wins

1,200 +360 DEF → +5.1% EHP +3,000 HP → +3.5% EHP DEF% wins

Crossover Point: At approximately 800 base DEF, a DEF% main stat roll gives the same EHP increase as an HP% roll. Below 800 base DEF, HP% is strictly better. Above 800, DEF% pulls ahead. Most DPS operatives have 500–700 base DEF at level 60 — meaning HP% is better for them. Zayne (support build) has ~900 base DEF, making DEF% slightly more EHP-efficient, but HP% also increases his shield healing, which may outweigh raw EHP.

Practical Decision: For DPS operatives: always HP% over DEF%. The 500–700 base DEF range means HP% gives 10–30% more EHP per roll. For Zayne: HP% is still preferred because it scales both shield absorption and healing simultaneously. For any operative above 1,000 base DEF (none currently exist at level 60), DEF% would be the better pick. This may change with future level cap increases.

Shield Strength Math

Shields are the only form of pre-mitigation damage prevention in Deepspace. They absorb damage before DEF reduction applies, making them extremely efficient for high-burst content. Shield formulas vary by source, but the general structure is consistent.

Base Shield Formula: Base Shield = ATK% of Shield Caster × Shield Multiplier. Most shield skills use a percentage of the caster's ATK (for DPS shields) or HP (for support shields like Zayne). The shield multiplier is a skill-specific number — typically 150–300% of the relevant stat.

Shield Strength Bonuses: Set bonuses and passive skills increase shield effectiveness multiplicatively. The Tidal set gives +20% shield strength. Frostguard gives +15% shield strength. These bonuses stack additively with each other but multiplicatively with base shield.

Effective Shield FormulaBase Shield × (1 + Total Shield Strength%)
Tidal Set (2pc)+20% shield strength → 1.2x multiplier
Frostguard (2pc)+15% shield strength → 1.15x multiplier
Tidal + Frostguard stacked1 + 0.20 + 0.15 = 1.35x multiplier

Shield stacking rules: Most shields from different sources stack (e.g., Zayne's skill shield + memory set shield). Shields from the same source overwrite — casting a new shield before the old one expires replaces it. The exception is passive shield procs (e.g., from set bonuses), which typically add to existing shields rather than overwriting.

Shield Efficiency Ranking: Zayne's Frostguard shield is the most EHP-efficient in the game because it scales off HP (which also increases his healing). Rafayel's Tidal shield is second-best — scales off ATK but has high uptime (66%). Generic memory set shields are a baseline — useful but not build-around material. For co-op content, at least one shield source per team is recommended.

Healing Per Second (HPS)

HPS is the measure of sustained recovery. Unlike shields (pre-mitigation), healing recovers HP after damage has been reduced by DEF — meaning healing is less efficient per raw point than shielding, but it can sustain through long attrition fights where shields would be overwhelmed.

Zayne Healing Formula: Zayne's 4pc Frostguard heal = 15% of max HP over 4 seconds. At 35k HP: 5,250 total HP / 4s = 1,312 HPS. Healing Bonus% from substats increases this: Heal = Base Heal × (1 + Healing Bonus%). A +15% Healing Bonus roll increases Zayne's HPS to 1,312 × 1.15 = 1,509 HPS.

Rafayel Self-Heal Formula: Rafayel's Inferno Blaze 4pc grants life steal on burn ticks: 3% of damage dealt as HP. If Rafayel deals 8,000 DPS with burn active, his self-heal is 240 HPS. This is lower than Zayne's output but requires no dedicated support slot.

Zayne 4pc HPS (35k HP)1,312 HPS (base) — 1,509 HPS (with +15% Healing Bonus)
Zayne 4pc HPS (45k HP)1,687 HPS (base) — 1,940 HPS (with +15% Healing Bonus)
Rafayel self-heal HPS~240 HPS (8k DPS sustain) — ~360 HPS (12k DPS burst)

Healing vs Shield Comparison: A shield that absorbs 10,000 damage prevents 10,000 HP loss. A heal that recovers 10,000 HP after damage requires your operative to survive the hit first. In one-shot content (Abyss floors 10–12 boss nukes), shields are essential because they prevent the lethal hit entirely. In attrition content (Domain farming, boss rush), healing is superior because shields cannot keep up with sustained damage over 2+ minutes.

Content Decision Tree: Is there a one-shot mechanic? → Use shields (Zayne or Tidal set). Is the fight longer than 90 seconds? → Use healing (Zayne 4pc or life steal). Is it both? → Use both — shield for the one-shot, healing for attrition recovery. For Abyss floors 7–9, one shield source is usually enough. For floors 10–12, you want shields on the support AND self-heal on the DPS.

Survivability per Content Type

Different content types require very different survivability thresholds. Over-investing in survivability costs you DPS; under-investing costs you the clear. Here are the recommended EHP and HPS thresholds for every major content type, based on observed enemy damage output at each tier.

ABYSS FLOORS 1–6 — MODERATE
EHP: 20k–28k | HPS: Minimal (0–200)
Story-level enemy damage. Shields from memory set passives are sufficient. No dedicated healer needed. Focus on DPS stats — survivability is a non-issue at this tier. Zayne's base shield without any HP investment is enough.
ABYSS FLOORS 7–9 — MODERATE+
EHP: 28k–35k | HPS: 200–600
Enemy damage starts to matter. One shield source (Zayne or Tidal) recommended. If running double DPS, at least one memory set with a shield passive. Healing from set bonuses is sufficient. Zayne at 30k HP handles this tier comfortably.
ABYSS FLOORS 10–12 — HIGH
EHP: 35k–50k | HPS: 600–1,500
Boss nukes hit for 25k+ before DEF reduction. Shields are mandatory. Zayne (35k HP / 4pc Frostguard) or Tidal set on a support. Healing active throughout the fight. DPS operatives should have at least 30k EHP self-sufficiently. Cooldown management of shields is critical — mistiming a shield cast means a oneshot.
CO-OP (ALL TIERS) — VERY HIGH
EHP: 40k+ | HPS: 1,000+
Multiplayer enemy scaling means damage comes from multiple sources simultaneously. Both shields and healing are required. Team composition should include at least one dedicated shielder (Zayne) and one sustain healer. DPS operatives should bring self-shield or life steal as a backup. Communication of shield cooldowns helps prevent overlapping.
DOMAIN FARMING — LOW
EHP: 18k–22k | HPS: 0–100
Domain enemies hit softly. No survivability investment needed. Speed is the only metric that matters. Equip full DPS sets. If your operative dies in domains, the problem is not survivability — it is failing to dodge telegraphed attacks. Practice the dodge timing instead of sacrificing DPS for HP/DEF.

Summary: EHP = (HP + Shield) / (1 − DEF_Reduction). DEF reduction = DEF / (DEF + Constant). Crossover for DEF% vs HP% is ~800 base DEF — below that, HP% wins. Shields are pre-mitigation (better for one-shots). Healing is post-mitigation (better for attrition). Survivability thresholds: Abyss 1–6 (20k EHP), Abyss 7–9 (28k+ EHP), Abyss 10–12 (35k+ EHP + mandatory shields), Co-op (40k+ EHP + both shield and heal), Domains (negligible — focus on speed). Build shields for burst content, healing for sustained content, and both for endgame.
