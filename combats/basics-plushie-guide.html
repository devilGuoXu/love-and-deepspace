Plushies & Mini-Games Guide

Crane machine, arcade games, and collection bonuses — everything outside combat that rewards progression materials.

ENTRY_#0582026.07.01LEISURE

Not Just Cosmetic: Plushie collection and mini-game achievements reward diamonds, affinity items, and exclusive furniture. The crane machine alone has hidden achievements worth 300+ diamonds. This content is easy to dismiss but rewards are real.

Crane Machine — Mechanical Tips

The crane machine in the base has two claw strengths per session (strong and weak). The first grab is always strong claw — use it on the largest prize near the drop chute. The second grab is weak claw — only attempt if a prize is already positioned over the chute. If you fail both, exit and re-enter to reset the claw strength sequence.

Prize selection: Avoid round plushies (they roll unpredictably). Target flat/oblong plushies (they slide into the chute). Target plushies that are already partially over the chute edge — the claw just needs to nudge them. Never grab plushies from the back wall — the claw swing arc won't reach the chute.

Plushie Collection Bonuses

Plushie collections unlock passive stat bonuses when displayed on shelves. Each shelf holds 3 plushies. Full shelf bonus: +2% ATK for the affiliated operative. 3 shelves (9 plushies): +5% ATK. 5 shelves (15 plushies): +10% ATK. These bonuses are permanent and apply to all content.

Rare plushies (gold border) give double bonus. Priority: Complete 1 shelf for your main DPS (+2% ATK) > Complete 3 shelves (+5% ATK) > Fill all available shelves. The +10% ATK bonus from 15 plushies is equivalent to an extra memory substat — significant for zero stamina cost.

Arcade Mini-Games

Three mini-games rotate weekly: Target Shot (aiming, 3 attempts daily), Rhythm Tap (timing, unlimited attempts), and Memory Match (pairs, 1 attempt daily). Each has achievements for score thresholds. Total diamond rewards from all mini-game achievements: approximately 500 diamonds one-time.

Efficiency: Do Target Shot daily (3 attempts = 30 seconds). Skip Rhythm Tap unless you enjoy it (rewards are minimal). Do Memory Match daily during events (event currency rewards are significant).

Pro Tip: The crane machine has a hidden pity system. After 10 unsuccessful grabs, the 11th grab is guaranteed strong claw regardless of positioning. Use this to secure a difficult plushie. The counter resets when you successfully grab a prize.

Summary: Crane machine — first grab is strong, grab flat plushies near the chute. Plushie shelves give +2-10% ATK — fill them. Mini-game achievements = 500 diamonds one-time. Hidden pity on 11th crane fail.
