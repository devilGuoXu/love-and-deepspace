Key Concept: Protocore set bonuses provide 8–15% damage uplift depending on the set, operative, and content type. Knowing which set to use per operative per content maximizes your effective stats by up to 20% compared to random equipping. The math behind set selection is deterministic — this guide breaks it down.

All Protocore Sets Catalog

There are six protocore sets in the current game meta. Each set provides a 2-piece bonus and a 4-piece bonus. The 2-piece is always a flat stat increase; the 4-piece provides a unique effect or a stronger stat multiplier. Below is the complete catalog with detailed breakdowns.

Solar — Raw Power
The universal ATK set. Solar has no conditional triggers and no elemental alignment. It works on every operative in every content type. The most farmed set in the game for good reason.
2pc: ATK +12% 4pc: ATK +25% (stacks with 2pc for +37% total) DPS Uplift: 12–15% raw (no conditions) Best For: All operatives, all content
Permafrost — Frost Domain
The premier elemental set for Frost-aligned operatives. Permafrost not only increases Frost damage but extends freeze duration, creating additional CC windows. Excellent in freezeable content.
2pc: Frost DMG +15% 4pc: Freeze duration +1s (on top of Frost DMG+15%) DPS Uplift: 18–22% (if freezeable content) Best For: Zayne, Frost-team comps
Tidal — Ocean's Embrace
The support-oriented Water set. Tidal provides healing amplification and Water damage. This is the primary set for healers and off-support operatives who contribute both damage and sustain.
2pc: Water DMG +15% 4pc: Healing +20% (on top of Water DMG+15%) DPS Uplift: 5–8% (healers) / 12–15% (damage) Best For: Support operatives, healer builds
Shadow — Abyssal Execute
The conditional high-risk set. Shadow provides a massive damage bonus when enemies are below 50% HP. This makes it a powerful execute set, but the conditional nature means its average value depends entirely on fight length and enemy durability.
2pc: Dark DMG +15% 4pc: Below 50% HP: DMG +25% (stacks with Dark DMG) DPS Uplift: ~12.5% average (see uptime section) Best For: Sylus, execute phase carries
Storm — Thunderous Wrath
An Electro-based set focused on burst windows and energy regeneration. Storm excels in short fights where the 4pc burst can be aligned with resonance rotations for maximum impact.
2pc: Electro DMG +15% 4pc: On resonance: next 3 attacks deal +30% Electro DMG (8s ICD) DPS Uplift: 10–16% (depends on rotation alignment) Best For: Rafayel, burst-window comps
Ember — Scorched Earth
A Fire DoT and debuff set. Ember excels in prolonged fights where burn stacks can ramp up. The 4pc applies a stacking defense reduction that benefits the entire team, making it a hybrid DPS/support set.
2pc: Fire DMG +15% 4pc: Burn ticks reduce DEF by 3% per stack (max 5 stacks = 15%) DPS Uplift: 9–14% (personal) + 10–15% teamwide Best For: Rafayel, team-debuff strategies

Key takeaways: Solar dominates because it has zero conditions. A set that is active 100% of the time with a flat 12–15% uplift mathematically outperforms conditional sets whose average is dragged down by uptime. Permafrost is the only elemental set that can rival Solar, and only in freezeable content. Shadow's 25% bonus below 50% HP sounds impressive, but its average across a full fight is only ~12.5% because half the fight happens above 50% HP.

Rule of thumb: If a set's peak bonus is active less than 60% of the fight, a universal ATK set (Solar) will almost always outperform it on average. Always calculate effective uptime before choosing a conditional set over Solar.

2pc vs 4pc Math

One of the most common questions: is it better to run a full 4-piece set or combine two 2-piece bonuses? The answer depends on stat dilution — the principle that stacking more of a stat you already have gives diminishing percentage returns.

Example A: 2 Solar + 2 Permafrost

2 Solar = ATK +12%. 2 Permafrost = Frost DMG +15%. Combined: +12% ATK and +15% Frost DMG.

If Zayne has +80% ATK from other sources and +40% Frost DMG from his kit: +12% ATK is worth 12 / 180 = 6.7% total damage. +15% Frost DMG is worth 15 / 140 = 10.7% total damage. Combined: ~17.4% total damage increase.

Example B: Full 4 Solar

2pc: ATK +12%. 4pc: ATK +25%. Total: ATK +37%.

Same Zayne with +80% ATK: 37 / 180 = ~20.6% total damage increase.

Winner: 4 Solar by ~3.2%. The 4-piece ATK% bonus is undiluted because ATK% is a broader damage multiplier than elemental DMG%.

The math flips when base ATK is already very high. If Zayne already has +120% ATK from gear and memories, the 4 Solar bonus of +37% is worth 37 / 220 = 16.8%. The 2+2 hybrid gives 6.7% + 10.7% = 17.4% — now the hybrid wins. This is the dilution crossover point.

The 2+2 Dilution Formula

When 2+2 beats 4-piece: (ATK% from other sources) / (Elemental DMG% from other sources) > (4pc ATK% gain) / (2pc Elemental gain). Simplified: if your ATK% is more than ~2.5x your elemental DMG% from other sources, 2+2 is mathematically superior. Otherwise, full 4-piece wins.

General rule: Full 4-piece Solar is almost always better for main DPS operatives unless your ATK% surpasses +100%. 2+2 hybrids are better for sub-DPS and support roles where you want balanced stat distribution. Mixed sets (one elemental + one ATK) excel when the elemental matches your operative's damage type.

Uptime Modeling

Conditional set bonuses like Shadow's below-50% execute and Storm's resonance-window burst have effective uptimes that vary dramatically by content type. A 25% damage bonus sounds incredible until you realize it only works for a fraction of the fight. Below is the uptime modeling per content category.

Shadow 4pc — Below 50% HP Bonus
Abyss Floors 1–6~10% uptime
Enemies die in 10–15 seconds. The bonus is active for roughly the last 1–2 seconds. Shadow is essentially wasted here. Use Solar or elemental matching.
Abyss Floors 7–9~25% uptime
Elite enemies live 30–45 seconds. The below-50% window is approximately 8–12 seconds. Shadow starts to become viable.
Abyss Floors 10–12~40% uptime
Bosses live 60–90 seconds. The execute window (below 50% HP) spans 25–35 seconds. Shadow's effective DPS uplift reaches ~10% — competitive with Solar.
Co-op Bosses~50% uptime
Multi-phase boss fights lasting 2–3 minutes. The below-50% phase is roughly half the fight. Shadow's effective ~12.5% average matches Solar's raw 12%.
Storm 4pc — Resonance Burst Window
Short Fights (<30s)~60% alignment
With 8s ICD and 10–15 second fights, you can align 1 burst window. Storm's peak 30% Electro DMG on 3 attacks translates to ~15% average per fight.
Medium Fights (30–60s)~45% alignment
Multiple resonance cycles mean some burst windows will overlap with low-damage phases of your rotation. Effective average drops to ~10%.
Long Fights (60s+)~35% alignment
Over extended fights, the 8s ICD and rotation drift reduce Storm's effective uptime. Average drops to ~8–10% — below Solar's unconditional 12%.

Uptime is everything. A set with 100% uptime and a 12% bonus mathematically beats a set with 50% uptime and a 25% bonus (12% vs 12.5% — barely). But when you factor in the consistency of Solar (no rotation adjustments needed), the unconditional set is nearly always preferred for general content. Conditional sets excel only when you can specifically tailor content around their condition.

Content-Specific Recommendations

Different content types demand different set priorities. Below are the recommendations per content tier, ranked by optimal set choice for each operative role.

ABYSS FLOORS 1–6
Solar (any operative) > Storm > Elemental Match
Enemies die too fast for conditional sets. Solar's raw ATK% clears waves fastest. Storm can work if you need energy burst for the final wave.
ABYSS FLOORS 7–9
Elemental Match > Solar > Permafrost (if freezeable)
Elites have enough HP to justify elemental matching. Frost teams gain significant value from Permafrost's freeze extension. Shadow starts being viable but not optimal.
ABYSS FLOORS 10–12
Shadow (execute) > Permafrost > Solar
Long boss fights make Shadow's execute mathematically competitive or superior. Permafrost's freeze duration gives 1–2 extra CC windows, which can prevent one-shot mechanics.
DOMAINS (Farming)
Solar > Ember > any set
Consistency is king in farming. Solar's unconditional ATK% ensures every run has the same clear time. Ember provides team debuffs for co-op domains.
EVENTS
Varies by event buff > Solar > Elemental Match
Most events provide a specific buff that favors one element or mechanic. Check the event details before equipping. Solar is a safe default if you don't want to swap.

Per-Operative Quick Reference: Zayne (Permafrost 4pc in freezeable content, Solar otherwise). Rafayel (Storm 4pc for burst, Ember 4pc for debuff teams). Sylus (Shadow 4pc on Abyss 10–12, Solar elsewhere). Xavier (Solar 4pc always — no elemental set matches his Light damage type). A universal Solar set at +12/16 is sufficient to clear all Abyss content, but optimal element matching shaves 10–20 seconds per floor at high investment.

Summary: Protocore set selection is a math problem with four variables — set bonus magnitude, uptime percentage, stat dilution from existing gear, and content type. Solar is the universal best-in-slot for any operative in any content where you are unsure. Permafrost is best for Zayne in freezeable Abyss floors 7+. Shadow is only competitive in long fights (Abyss 10–12, co-op). Storm and Ember fill niche burst and debuff roles respectively. Tidal is for healers only. When in doubt: farm Solar and build a +12/16 set — it will never be wrong.
