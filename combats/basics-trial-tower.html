BASIC PROTOCOLS — TRIAL TOWER

Deepspace Trial Tower

A permanent challenge mode with 100+ floors that resets monthly. The Trial Tower is the best source of limited pulls for F2P players who can climb.

ENTRY_#0622026.07.02TRIAL_TOWER

What It Is: The Trial Tower is a monthly-reset gauntlet of combat floors. Each floor has increasing difficulty and a fixed elemental requirement. You cannot brute-force with overleveled characters — you must match the element. The tower has 100 floors total, but most players plateau around floor 50-60.

Floor Structure

Floors 1-20 (Easy): Single-element requirement. Standard wanderer waves. Recommended power: 30k-50k. Any team with the correct element clears these easily. Floors 21-50 (Medium): Dual-element requirement. Mixed wanderer types. Recommended power: 50k-80k. You need two teams or a hybrid team with both elements. Floors 51-80 (Hard): Single-element with elite modifiers. Recommended power: 80k-120k. Each floor has an elemental shield that requires precise breaking. Floors 81-100 (Extreme): Dual-element with boss floors every 5 floors. Recommended power: 120k+. Gear check. Protocore optimization required. Monthly reset: Your progress resets to floor 1 every month. You keep the rewards permanently. Maximum reward per month: ~1,200 diamonds + 10 limited pulls + materials.

Climb Strategy

First week of the month: Rush floors 1-50 in one session (2-3 hours). These floors are easy and give the bulk of the rewards. Second week: Push floors 51-70. If you get stuck, check the elemental requirement and adjust your protocores. Third week: Attempt floors 71-90. This is where most F2P players top out. Reward difference between 71-80 and 81-90 is small — do not stress over it. Fourth week: Final push for floors 91-100 if you have the gear. Otherwise, accept your current floor and save energy for next month's reset. Optimal stopping point: Floor 70 gives 85% of the total reward value. The last 30 floors give only 15% of rewards but require 200% more effort.

Element Requirements by Floor Range

Floors 1-20 cycle through Light, Dark, Fire, Ice (5 floors each). Floors 21-50 require two elements. Floors 51-80 require one element but with a 2x shield durability. Floors 81-100 require two elements and have rotating modifiers (increased enemy ATK, reduced healing, time limit). Preparation: Have at least 2 operatives of each element at level 50+ before attempting floors 51+. If you lack elemental coverage, the tower will block your progress regardless of your main team's power. Priority investment: Light (Xavier) and Fire (Rafayel) appear most frequently. Invest in these two elements first.

Pro Tip: The Trial Tower does NOT consume stamina. You can retry as many times as you want. Use this to practice difficult fights without penalty. If a floor has a time limit modifier, record your clear time and optimize your rotation. The tower is the best place to practice advanced combat mechanics because failure has no cost.

Reward Efficiency

Compared to other diamond sources: Trial Tower gives ~1,200 diamonds/month for a floor-70 clear. This is equivalent to 8.5 days of daily mission diamonds. It takes ~5 hours to clear floors 1-70. Time efficiency: 240 diamonds/hour. This is better than Abyss (200 diamonds/hour) and worse than mini-game events (400+ diamonds/hour). Ranking: Do the tower if you have time. Skip it if you only have 30 minutes/day — mini-games are more time-efficient. But if you enjoy combat, the tower is the most fun diamond source.

Summary: 100 floors, monthly reset. 1-20 easy, 21-50 medium, 51-80 hard, 81-100 extreme. Floor 70 = 85% of total rewards. No stamina cost — unlimited retries. Light and Fire elements are most important. Do not stress over floors 81-100 — the extra 15% reward is not worth the frustration.
