Boss Summon Mechanics

Some wanderer bosses must be summoned rather than found. This guide covers every summon condition, boss type, and optimal summoning strategy.

ENTRY_#WDR_092026.07.02BOSS SUMMON

What Are Summonable Bosses: Certain elite wanderers and bosses require specific actions to summon. They do not appear in the world naturally. Summoning them requires meeting specific conditions — kill counts, item usage, time of day, or event triggers.

Standard Summon Conditions

Most summonable bosses follow one of these patterns: Kill Count Threshold: Kill X number of a specific wanderer type within a region. The boss spawns after the threshold is met. Example: Kill 20 Flame Imps in Scorched Expanse to summon the Inferno Colossus. The kill count is cumulative across sessions — you do not need to do it in one visit. Item Offering: Use a specific item at a designated summoning point. Items are obtained from elite drops, event rewards, or the shop. Example: Use a "Frost Core" at the Tundra Summoning Circle to summon the Frost Tyrant. One item = one summon attempt. Time/Condition Based: The boss only appears during specific in-game conditions. Example: The Nightmare Wraith only spawns between 12 AM and 4 AM in-game time. Event Trigger: The boss spawns after completing a specific sequence of actions. Example: Activate 3 ancient altars in the correct order to summon the Ancient Treant Lord.

Boss List and Summon Requirements

Inferno Colossus (Scorched Expanse): Kill 20 Flame Imps. Spawns at Caldera Summit. Recommended level: 55+. Drops: Hellfire Core (SSR ascension material), Ember Shards. Respawn after defeat: 24 hours real time. Frost Tyrant (Frostwind Tundra): Use 1 Frost Core at Summoning Circle. Spawns at Frozen Pass. Recommended level: 50+. Drops: Permafrost Heart (SSR ascension material), Ice Crystals. Respawn: immediate if you have another Frost Core. Abyssal Siren (Abyssal Depths): Kill 15 Void Shades. Must be done within a single visit (cumulative count resets on exit). Spawns at Lower Depths. Recommended level: 70+. Drops: Abyss Core (SSR ascension material), Void Fragments. Respawn: 24 hours. Ancient Treant Lord (Verdant Woodlands): Activate 3 altars in order: North Altar, East Altar, West Altar. Each altar has a 5-second activation channel. Spawns at Ruined Temple. Recommended level: 45+. Drops: Ancient Bark (SR ascension material), Wood Cores. Respawn: 12 hours. Phantom Stalker (Urban District — Night Only): Kill 10 Phantom Scouts during nighttime. Spawns at Central Plaza. Recommended level: 30+. Drops: Shadow Gem (SR material), Scout Insignias. Respawn: next in-game night cycle. Void Lord (Abyssal Depths — Rare): Requires Abyssal Siren kill first, then use 3 Void Sigils at the Deepest Point. Void Sigils drop from Abyssal Siren (20% chance). Recommended level: 75+. Drops: Lord's Core (Premium material), Abyss Essence, exclusive name card. Respawn: requires another Abyssal Siren kill + 3 more Sigils.

Summoning Strategy

Efficiency Approach: (1) Farm the required kill-count wanderers while doing your normal material farming circuits. Do not go out of your way to hit thresholds — they will accumulate naturally. (2) Save summoning items (Frost Cores, Void Sigils) until you have at least 3. This lets you chain-summon the same boss for efficient farming. (3) Check the in-game time before attempting time-gated bosses. Adjust your play schedule accordingly. (4) For multi-altar summons (Ancient Treant Lord), memorize the activation order. A wrong order resets all altars and you must wait 10 minutes before trying again. (5) For rare spawn chains (Void Lord), track your drop rates. If you have not gotten a Void Sigil after 5 Abyssal Siren kills, consider using a luck boost item if available.

Advanced: Boss Rotation Planning: Create a weekly boss summoning rotation: Monday: Inferno Colossus + Frost Tyrant. Tuesday: Abyssal Siren (farm Void Shades). Wednesday: Ancient Treant Lord + Phantom Stalker. Thursday: Abyssal Siren (kill attempt). Friday: Void Lord (if sigils obtained). Saturday: All remaining bosses. Sunday: Rest/reset. This rotation ensures you hit every boss at least once per week. Adjust based on your material needs. If you need Fire materials, prioritize Inferno Colossus. If you need Dark materials, prioritize Abyssal Siren and Void Lord. Boss materials are used for SSR memory ascension — farming bosses efficiently accelerates your memory progression significantly.

Boss Reward Table

Each boss has a fixed drop table: Common Drops (100%): Regional materials (8-12 pieces), gold (5,000-20,000), affinity XP (50-100). Uncommon Drops (50-70%): Blueprint fragments (crafting), protocore dust boxes (2-5), stamina potions (1-2). Rare Drops (20-30%): SSR ascension materials, exclusive cosmetics, limited-use skill books. Very Rare (5-10%): Premium materials (Lord's Core), name cards, profile frames. Tip: Boss drop rates are NOT affected by luck or difficulty modifiers. Every kill has the same chances. The only way to increase rare drops is to kill more bosses. This makes efficient summoning (multiple summons per session) more valuable than difficult-summon bosses.

Summary: Summonable bosses require specific conditions: kill counts, item offerings, time conditions, or event triggers. Six bosses: Inferno Colossus (20 Flame Imps), Frost Tyrant (Frost Core), Abyssal Siren (15 Void Shades in one visit), Ancient Treant Lord (3 altars in order), Phantom Stalker (10 scouts at night), Void Lord (Abyssal Siren kill + 3 Void Sigils). Strategy: accumulate kills naturally, save summon items, chain-summon for efficiency, follow a weekly rotation. Bosses drop essential ascension materials and exclusive cosmetics. Efficient boss summoning is a key part of late-game account progression.
