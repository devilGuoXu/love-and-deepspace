Boss Summoning Guide

World bosses in Deepspace are summoned using specific items obtained from exploration and domain farming. Learn how to summon every boss, where to fight them, and how to prepare.

Summoning System Overview

How Summoning Works: World bosses are not always present in the open world. They must be summoned using specific items at designated summoning altars. Each boss requires a unique summoning item obtained from exploration, elite enemy drops, or domain completion. The summoning item is consumed on use — one item = one boss fight. Bosses can be fought solo or in co-op (up to 3 players).

The summoning system has two key components: Summoning Altars (fixed locations in each region where bosses are summoned) and Summoning Items (consumable items that activate the altars). Altars are marked on the map with a unique icon (a glowing circle with runes). When you approach an altar with the correct summoning item in your inventory, an interaction prompt appears. Using the item spawns the boss immediately — there is no cast time or delay. The boss spawns at the altar location and immediately aggroes.

Summoning etiquette: If you summon a boss in co-op mode, all party members can participate but only the summoner consumes the item. Party members do not need their own summoning items. Common etiquette: party members help the summoner clear the boss, and the summoner shares the rewards (materials are instanced — each player gets their own drops). If you join a co-op boss fight, always ask before using your own summoning item — the party may want to fight a different boss.

Important restriction: Bosses have a weekly lockout. Each boss can be fought once per week for full rewards. After the first kill, you can still summon and fight the boss, but rewards are reduced to 10% (gold only, no materials). The lockout resets on Monday (server time). Plan your boss summoning around the weekly reset — do not summon a boss on Sunday if you can wait until Monday for the new lockout cycle.

Boss List & Summon Items

ANCIENT DRAGON
Mountain — Summon: Dragon's Scale — Drop rate: 20% from Stone Golems
The Ancient Dragon is the first world boss most players encounter. Summoning item: Dragon's Scale (drops from Mountain Stone Golems at 20% rate). The boss is a large dragon with fire breath (frontal cone), tail sweep (360° AoE), and wing gust (knockback). Recommended team: 1 tank (Zayne shields) + 2 DPS. Bring fire resistance if available. Phase 2 (50% HP): dragon flies up and rains fire — hide behind the pillars in the arena. Fight duration: 4-6 minutes.
ABYSSAL LEVIATHAN
Coast — Summon: Leviathan's Tear — Drop rate: 15% from Tide Striders
Abyssal Leviathan — coastal region. Summoning item: Leviathan's Tear (Coast Tide Striders, 15% drop). The boss is a massive sea serpent that emerges from the water. It has water jet attacks (ranged, applies slow), a dive attack (AoE with knockup), and a tidal wave (arena-wide, must dodge through the wave). The arena is a floating platform — falling into the water is instant death. Recommended team: ranged DPS preferred — melee has difficulty hitting the head weak point. Fight duration: 5-7 minutes.
SHADOW LORD
Urban — Summon: Shadow Core — Drop rate: 10% from Shadow Stalkers (night only)
Shadow Lord — urban region. Summoning item: Shadow Core (Urban Shadow Stalkers, 10% drop, night only). The Shadow Lord is a fast, aggressive boss that teleports frequently. It has shadow clones (creates 2 decoys), darkness bolts (homing projectiles), and a execute attack (instant kill below 30% HP — dodge at the right moment). The dark arena reduces visibility — bring a light source (Xavier's Light element skills reveal the boss). Recommended team: 1 DPS + 1 support + 1 flex. Fight duration: 6-8 minutes.

ENDGAME BOSS — VOID LORD
The Void Lord (void region) requires a different summoning method. Instead of a drop from normal enemies, the Void Lord summoning item (Void Sigil) is crafted from 10 Void Core Fragments. Fragments drop from Void region elite enemies (Void Spawns and Void Weavers) at a 30% rate. The crafting recipe is unlocked after defeating the Void Lord once (the first encounter is story-mandatory — does not require summoning). After the first kill, you must summon subsequent fights using crafted Sigils.

Summon Item Farming

Efficient summon item farming requires targeting the right enemies with the right strategy.

1
Dragon's Scale (Mountain)
Farm Stone Golems at Mountain base camp (spawns 1-3). Drop rate: 20%. Average: 1 scale per 5 kills. Efficient route: clear base camp (3 Stone Golems), teleport to entrance, re-enter, repeat. 20-minute farming session = 6-8 scales. Recommended for mid-game players (level 35+).
2
Leviathan's Tear (Coast)
Farm Tide Striders at Coast beachfront (spawns 1-4). Drop rate: 15%. Average: 1 tear per 6-7 kills. Tide Striders have annoying slow debuffs — bring Zayne for debuff cleanse. Efficient route: beachfront clear → teleport → repeat. Coast farming is harder than mountain — the slow debuff reduces clear speed.
3
Shadow Core (Urban)
Farm Shadow Stalkers in Urban region at night (in-game time 8PM-6AM). Drop rate: 10%. Highest difficulty due to time constraint and low drop rate. Use Xavier (Light element) for extra damage against Shadow enemies. Night-only restriction means you must manage in-game time — use the wait function strategically.
4
Void Sigil (Crafted)
Requires 10 Void Core Fragments per Sigil. Fragments drop from Void Spawns and Void Weavers at 30% rate. Average fragments per full void clear: 2-3. You need 3-4 full clears for one Sigil. This is the most expensive summoning item — only summon the Void Lord with a full co-op party to maximize value.
5
Alternative acquisition
Summoning items can also be obtained from: domain completion rewards (small chance), event shops (limited quantity), and Abyss cycle rewards (guaranteed). Use event currency for summoning items only if you have bought all limited items first — they are not high priority.

Farming efficiency: Do not farm summoning items exclusively. Integrate summon item farming into your daily material farming routes. For example: farm mountain for gold (which you need anyway) and collect Dragon's Scales as a bonus. Farming specifically for summoning items is inefficient unless you are preparing for a specific boss fight. A balanced farming approach yields enough summoning items for 2-3 boss fights per week — which is the maximum you can do with weekly lockouts anyway.

Boss Preparation Checklist

Before summoning any boss, run through this checklist to ensure you are ready:

Is your weekly lockout available?
Check that you have not already killed this boss this week. If you have, wait for the Monday reset. Fighting a boss on reduced rewards is a waste of a summoning item.
Do you have the correct element team?
Check the boss's weakness. Ancient Dragon is weak to Ice (Zayne). Abyssal Leviathan is weak to Lightning (if available) or neutral. Shadow Lord is weak to Light (Xavier). Void Lord has no weakness but resists all elements equally — pure DPS check.
Are your consumables ready?
Bring healing items, element resistance potions (if available), and revive items. Boss fights consume resources — do not start without a full inventory of healing items.
Have you studied the boss patterns?
Watch a video guide or read the boss guide before summoning. Wiping wastes your summoning item. The first time you fight a boss, expect to die — learn the patterns, then summon again for the real attempt.
Is your co-op party ready?
If summoning in co-op, confirm party members have prepared (correct elements, consumables, knowledge of mechanics). A disorganized co-op party will fail more often than a well-prepared solo player.
BRING
  • Correct element team (exploit weaknesses)
  • Healing items (10+ medium potions minimum)
  • Element resistance potions (if available for the boss)
  • Revive items (1-2 for emergency)
  • Knowledge of boss attack patterns
DON'T BRING
  • Wrong element team (reduced damage)
  • Unleveled characters (waste of summon)
  • No consumables (you will run out of healing)
  • Disorganized co-op group
  • Bad attitude — wipes happen, learn from them

Co-op Boss Summoning

Co-op boss fights are more efficient for summoning item usage — you get help clearing the boss without consuming additional items. However, co-op coordination requires practice.

CO-OP ROLE ASSIGNMENT
Host (Summoner): Controls the fight. Use ping system to mark targets and warn about mechanics. The host should be the most experienced player.
DPS (2 players): Focus on damage. Stay alive, but prioritize dealing damage. One DPS should bring sustained damage (Xavier), the other burst damage (Sylus for execute phase).
Support (1 player, optional): Bring Zayne for shields and debuff cleansing. The support player focuses on keeping the team alive, not dealing damage. Support is recommended for first-time clears and difficult bosses.

Co-op matching: Use the in-game matching system (available for all world bosses). Matching finds players from your server who are also looking for boss fights. The matching system prioritizes: same boss, similar power level, and role balance. Average matching time: 30-60 seconds during peak hours, 2-5 minutes during off-peak. If matching takes too long, use the community Discord to find co-op partners.

"I was terrified of summoning my first Void Lord. The summoning item took 3 days of farming to craft. I joined a co-op group through Discord, told them it was my first time, and they carried me through the fight. The community is incredibly helpful — do not be afraid to ask for help with your summons."

Co-op etiquette summary: Greet the party. Confirm boss and difficulty. Share roles. Do not waste the host's summoning item by being unprepared. Say thank you after the kill. Offer to host the next summon if you have the item. A positive co-op experience leads to repeat invitations and a reliable boss-fighting network.

Boss Reward Analysis

Each boss offers different rewards. Understanding the reward structure helps you prioritize which bosses to summon.

Ancient DragonDragon materials (rank-up), gold (20K+), SSR memory chance (5%), exp items
Abyssal LeviathanLeviathan materials (rank-up), premium currency (100-200), SSR memory chance (8%), exclusive costume materials
Shadow LordShadow materials (rank-up), protocore dust (500+), SSR memory chance (10%), exclusive weapon skin
Void LordVoid materials (R2+ rank-up), premium currency (200-400), guaranteed SSR memory fragment (1 per kill), exclusive title

Reward priority: If you have limited summoning items, prioritize: Void Lord (premium currency + guaranteed SSR fragment) > Shadow Lord (protocore dust + highest SSR chance) > Abyssal Leviathan (premium currency) > Ancient Dragon (basic rank-up materials). The Void Lord is the best value per summon if you can clear it. The Shadow Lord is the best value if you need protocore dust (which you always need).

SSR memory drop rates: Each boss has a small chance to drop an SSR memory on kill. The rate is low (5-10%) but the memories are exclusive — they only drop from bosses, not from gacha. Check the community tier list before farming a specific boss memory — some are excellent, others are outclassed by standard memories. The Void Lord guaranteed SSR fragment (1 per kill, 20 fragments to craft the memory) makes the Void Lord the most reliable source of SSR memories from boss fights.

Summary: Summoning system: summoning items are consumed at altars to spawn bosses. Weekly lockout per boss (reset Monday). Four bosses: Ancient Dragon (Dragon's Scale — Mountain Stone Golems), Abyssal Leviathan (Leviathan's Tear — Coast Tide Striders), Shadow Lord (Shadow Core — Urban Shadow Stalkers at night), Void Lord (crafted Void Sigil — 10 Void Core Fragments). Item farming: integrate into daily material routes for efficiency. Preparation: check weekly lockout, bring correct elements, prepare consumables, learn boss patterns, coordinate co-op roles. Rewards: Void Lord (best value — premium currency + guaranteed SSR fragment), Shadow Lord (protocore dust + highest SSR chance), Abyssal Leviathan (premium currency), Ancient Dragon (basic materials). Co-op etiquette: be prepared, communicate, share roles, show gratitude.
