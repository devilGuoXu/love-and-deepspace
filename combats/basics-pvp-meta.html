PvP Team Building & Meta

Current meta compositions, operative synergies, and how to counter the most popular defenses.

ENTRY_#0352026.06.30PVP

Current Meta: Burst-heavy compositions dominate. Matches are decided in the first 30 seconds. Teams that can delete one enemy operative before they act have a 70% win rate.

Top Meta Compositions

Sylus Burst: Sylus (DPS) + Zayne (Support) + Xavier (Flex). Sylus deletes one target with execute. Zayne shields through retaliation. Xavier cleans up. This is the most consistent composition at Diamond+. Rafayel Burn: Rafayel (DPS) + Xavier (Sub-DPS) + Zayne (Support). AoE burn pressure. Strong against tanky teams. Weak against high-burst compositions that kill Rafayel first. Zayne Stall: Zayne (Main) + Xavier (DPS) + Sylus (Execute). Focus on survival. Outlast the enemy burst then counter-attack. Good against Rafayel burn comps.

Counter Picking

If the enemy has Sylus, bring Zayne with high HP. Sylus's execute is based on max HP percentage — high HP reduces its effectiveness. If the enemy has Rafayel, bring burst to kill him before his burn stacks. If the enemy has Zayne, ignore him and kill his teammates first. Zayne alone has low kill pressure.

Defensive Team Setup

Your defensive team is controlled by AI. The AI is predictable — it uses Ardent Oath immediately when available, prioritizes the lowest HP target, never dodges perfectly. Build your defense to exploit these patterns: put your tankiest operative first (AI targets lead), avoid glass cannons on defense (AI can't protect them), use Zayne or Rafayel on defense (AI plays them best).

Key Insight: The AI defense has a tell: it always targets the operative with the lowest DEF stat first. Put your highest DEF operative as the team leader to absorb the initial burst. This single swap can increase your defense win rate from 20% to 35%.

Summary: Burst meta = Sylus + Zayne + Xavier. Counter-pick based on enemy lead. Defense AI is dumb — exploit its patterns. Build offense first, defense second. A good offense with a mediocre defense beats a balanced team.
