PvP Team Building & Meta

Current meta compositions, operative synergies, and how to counter the most popular defenses.

ENTRY_#0352026.06.30PVP

Current Meta: Burst-heavy compositions dominate. Matches are decided in the first 30 seconds. Teams that can delete one enemy operative before they act have a 70% win rate.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Top Meta Compositions

Sylus Burst: Sylus (DPS) + Zayne (Support) + Xavier (Flex). Sylus deletes one target with execute. Zayne shields through retaliation. Xavier cleans up. This is the most consistent composition at Diamond+. Rafayel Burn: Rafayel (DPS) + Xavier (Sub-DPS) + Zayne (Support). AoE burn pressure. Strong against tanky teams. Weak against high-burst compositions that kill Rafayel first. Zayne Stall: Zayne (Main) + Xavier (DPS) + Sylus (Execute). Focus on survival. Outlast the enemy burst then counter-attack. Good against Rafayel burn comps.

Counter Picking

If the enemy has Sylus, bring Zayne with high HP. Sylus's execute is based on max HP percentage — high HP reduces its effectiveness. If the enemy has Rafayel, bring burst to kill him before his burn stacks. If the enemy has Zayne, ignore him and kill his teammates first. Zayne alone has low kill pressure.

Defensive Team Setup

Your defensive team is controlled by AI. The AI is predictable — it uses Ardent Oath immediately when available, prioritizes the lowest HP target, never dodges perfectly. Build your defense to exploit these patterns: put your tankiest operative first (AI targets lead), avoid glass cannons on defense (AI can't protect them), use Zayne or Rafayel on defense (AI plays them best).

Memory Optimization for PvP

PvP memory optimization differs from PvE in one critical way: HP and DEF substats are significantly more valuable because player-controlled operatives deal higher burst damage than AI enemies. A memory with HP percentage and DEF percentage substats can triple your survivability in PvP at the cost of roughly 15% damage output. For your defensive team, prioritize HP over ATK on all Solar pair main stats. Your offense team can lean heavier into damage stats because you control the engagement timing.

Energy regeneration rate is the most undervalued stat in PvP builds. A single Energy Regen protocore substat can shift your ultimate timing by one full rotation, giving you the first ultimate in a mirror matchup. In Sylus versus Sylus matchups, the player who gets execute first wins approximately 80% of the time. If you are running Sylus in your PvP lineup, reroll protocore substats until you have at least one Energy Regen roll on your main DPS memory set.

Banned and Meta Shifts

The PvP meta shifts with each balance patch and new operative release. Currently banned operatives cycle weekly, and the ban phase at the start of each match determines which compositions are viable. Always have at least two complete team compositions ready before entering ranked matchmaking. If Sylus is banned, your backup composition should not rely on execute mechanics. If Rafayel is banned, pivot to a Zayne stall composition instead.

Track the weekly ban rates on community spreadsheets to anticipate which operatives will be popular in your rank bracket. Diamond and above has a distinct meta that differs significantly from Platinum. In Diamond, Sylus burst comps dominate with roughly 60% play rate. In Platinum, Rafayel burn comps are more common at approximately 45% play rate. Build your counter team based on the meta at your specific rank rather than the global meta.

Currently, Sylus dominates Diamond rank with 55% win rate. Rafayel has 48%, Zayne stall wins 52%, Xavier has 45%. Track the meta on community resources to prepare counter strategies.

Key Insight: The AI defense has a tell: it always targets the operative with the lowest DEF stat first. Put your highest DEF operative as the team leader to absorb the initial burst. This single swap can increase your defense win rate from 20% to 35%.

Summary: Burst meta = Sylus + Zayne + Xavier. Counter-pick based on enemy lead. Defense AI is dumb — exploit its patterns. Build offense first, defense second. A good offense with a mediocre defense beats a balanced team.
