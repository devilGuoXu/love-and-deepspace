Abyssal Trials: Floor 3

First elemental boss — match your element or struggle.

ENTRY_#1322026.06.30FLOOR_3

Floor 3 Threshold: Recommended power level 12,000. First floor requiring elemental coverage. Boss has a Stellactrum-locked phase.

Boss — Elemental Harbinger

A medium-sized Wanderer that cycles elemental affinities. The Harbinger starts with a random element (Light, Dark, Frost, or Fire) and switches every 20 seconds. While aligned with an element, it has 60% damage reduction against non-matching elements and deals 30% increased damage of its aligned type.

The Harbinger has three attacks: Elemental Bolt (ranged projectile, dodge sideways), Elemental Wave (wide AoE, dodge through or iframe), and Elemental Prison (targeted circle AoE, step out). The Prison is the most dangerous — it roots you for 3 seconds if hit, leaving you vulnerable to follow-up damage.

Team Building

You need at least two elements covered between your two operatives. Recommended: Xavier (Light) + Rafayel (Fire) covers half the rotation. If the boss cycles to an uncovered element, focus on dodging and basic attacks until it cycles back. Do not waste resonance skills during resistance phases.

If possible, bring a third element via your support operative's Stellactrum. A 2-element team can cover 50% of the rotation. A 3-element team covers 75%. A 4-element team (all operatives with different elements) covers 100%.

Rewards

First clear: 100 diamonds, 5 Advanced Resonance Crystals. Weekly reset: 40 diamonds. 3-star clear bonus: 50 diamonds.

Pro Tip: Watch the Harbinger's color aura — it glows the color of its current element. Light = yellow glow, Dark = purple, Frost = blue, Fire = red. Match your active operative to the glow color. When the glow changes, swap operatives immediately.

Summary: Elemental cycling boss. Cover 3+ elements. Watch the color aura. Save burst for matching phases. 12,000 power threshold.
