Abyssal Trials: Floor 3

First elemental boss — match your element or struggle.

ENTRY_#1322026.06.30FLOOR_3
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Floor 3 Threshold: Recommended power level 12,000. First floor requiring elemental coverage. Boss has a Stellactrum-locked phase.

Boss — Elemental Harbinger

A medium-sized Wanderer that cycles elemental affinities. The Harbinger starts with a random element (Light, Dark, Frost, or Fire) and switches every 20 seconds. While aligned with an element, it has 60% damage reduction against non-matching elements and deals 30% increased damage of its aligned type.

The Harbinger has three attacks: Elemental Bolt (ranged projectile, dodge sideways), Elemental Wave (wide AoE, dodge through or iframe), and Elemental Prison (targeted circle AoE, step out). The Prison is the most dangerous — it roots you for 3 seconds if hit, leaving you vulnerable to follow-up damage.

Team Building

You need at least two elements covered between your two operatives. Recommended: Xavier (Light) + Rafayel (Fire) covers half the rotation. If the boss cycles to an uncovered element, focus on dodging and basic attacks until it cycles back. Do not waste resonance skills during resistance phases.

If possible, bring a third element via your support operative's Stellactrum. A 2-element team can cover 50% of the rotation. A 3-element team covers 75%. A 4-element team (all operatives with different elements) covers 100%.

Rewards

First clear: 100 diamonds, 5 Advanced Resonance Crystals. Weekly reset: 40 diamonds. 3-star clear bonus: 50 diamonds.

Energy and Resonance Management

Floor 3 rewards disciplined energy management. The Elemental Harbinger's 20-second cycle window is exactly enough time to build one full resonance charge and use it before the element switches. Your rotation should follow this rhythm: basic attacks for the first 10 seconds to build energy, then resonance skill during the 10-15 second window, followed by basic attacks until the element switch. Using resonance immediately at the start of a cycle is a common mistake — you waste the burst because the boss will switch elements before you can follow up with damage.

Energy regeneration rate varies between operatives. Xavier generates energy fastest through light attack chains, making him the best choice for the flex slot. Rafayel's heavy attacks generate energy more slowly but deal higher burst damage per skill use. Adjust your rotation based on which operative is your main DPS. If you are using Xavier as primary, you can fit two resonance skills per cycle. If using Rafayel, one well-timed resonance per cycle is the realistic target.

Protocore Optimization for Elemental Coverage

The Elemental Harbinger does not have a specific elemental weakness — it is equally vulnerable to all elements during its matched phase. This makes ATK percentage and crit damage the optimal protocore stats for this encounter rather than elemental damage bonuses. Flat ATK protocores on your bottom-right slots provide consistent damage across all element cycles without the risk of wasted stats during resistance phases.

Energy regeneration protocores are surprisingly effective on this floor. A single Energy Regen substat on your main DPS memory can shift your rotation timing, allowing you to fit an extra resonance skill within each 20-second cycle. Test your energy regeneration by running the fight once and counting how many basic attack chains you complete per cycle. If you are halfway to full energy when the element switches, an Energy Regen protocore will solve the gap.

Floor 3's Elemental Harbinger cycles through elements in a fixed order: Light, Dark, Frost, Fire, then repeats. The cycle resets at each wave, meaning the Harbinger always starts with Light element at the beginning of each wave. Pre-position your matching-element operative as the active character when the wave starts. If your matching operative needs to be swapped in, do so during the 2-second transition period between element cycles rather than during active combat.

Floor 3's boss has 40% more HP than Floor 2 — the largest jump between consecutive Abyss floors. If failing the DPS check, spend 1-2 weeks farming enhancement materials. The gear gap is approximately 5,000 power.

Pro Tip: Watch the Harbinger's color aura — it glows the color of its current element. Light = yellow glow, Dark = purple, Frost = blue, Fire = red. Match your active operative to the glow color. When the glow changes, swap operatives immediately.

Summary: Elemental cycling boss. Cover 3+ elements. Watch the color aura. Save burst for matching phases. 12,000 power threshold.
