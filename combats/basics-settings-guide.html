Settings & Optimization

Best settings for performance, visibility, and comfort across all devices.

ENTRY_#026 2026.06.29 SETTINGS

Why Settings Matter: The right settings can mean the difference between smooth 60fps gameplay and stuttery lag. More importantly, visibility settings affect your combat performance — you can't dodge what you can't see.

Graphics Settings (Performance Priority)

Low-End Devices: Resolution 720p, Shadows Off, Effects Low, FPS cap 30. Disable bloom and motion blur. This gives stable 30fps on most devices.
Mid-Range Devices: Resolution 1080p, Shadows Low, Effects Medium, FPS cap 60. Keep bloom on (visibility aid). Disable motion blur.
High-End Devices: Resolution 1440p+, Shadows High, Effects High, FPS uncapped. All visual effects on. Consider VSync for screen tearing.

Combat Settings

Camera Sensitivity: 60-80% recommended. Too low and you can't track fast enemies. Too high and you overshoot.
Target Lock: Enable soft lock. Hard lock can disorient in multi-target fights.
Dodge Button Size: Increase by 20%. You will mash this more than any other button.
Skill Indicators: Always on. Telegraphed attacks are the only way to survive high floors.

Audio & Accessibility

Voice Lines: Keep at 100%. Many boss mechanics have audio cues before visual indicators.

SFX: 80-100%. Critical for dodge timing.

BGM: 30-50%. Atmosphere is nice but SFX priority.

Subtitles: Enable. Story context helps with combat mechanics.

The Bottom Line: Prioritize stable framerate over visual quality. Combat settings (sensitivity, lock-on, dodge button) matter more than graphics. Test your settings in a low-stakes fight before taking them into Abyss or N109.
