Story Mode Completion Guide

Maximize rewards from Deepspace story mode — chapter completion, exploration chests, and all missable content.

ENTRY_#0792026.07.02STORY

How to Read This: New players should read this before starting the story to avoid missing rewards. Returning players can check the "Missable Content" section for anything they may have missed in earlier chapters.

Chapter Rewards Overview

Each story chapter gives first-time completion rewards: Diamonds: 200-400 per chapter (scales with chapter number). Total from all chapters: approximately 8,000-10,000 diamonds. Standard Banner Tickets: 5-10 per chapter. Total: approximately 100+ tickets across all chapters. Memory Level-Up Materials: A significant amount — enough to level 2-3 memories from 1 to 50. Character Bond Materials: Used to unlock bond conversations and permanent stat bonuses. Stamina: Each chapter completion gives a small stamina pack. Claim immediately — these expire. Total value: The story mode is worth approximately 60+ limited pulls worth of resources. Do not skip story or auto-skip cutscenes without claiming rewards first. Some chapters have post-cutscene dialogue that gives additional resources — wait for the auto-advance to fully finish before exiting.

Hidden Chests & Exploration

Story chapters contain hidden exploration chests that are not marked on the minimap. Where to look: Behind waterfalls (common), inside environmental objects (breakable crates, suspicious walls), at dead ends in non-linear chapters, and after optional dialogue sequences where NPCs walk away from a location. How many: Each chapter has 2-5 hidden chests. Total across all chapters: approximately 40-60 chests. Each gives 20-50 diamonds. Total hidden diamonds: 1,000-3,000 diamonds from exploration alone. This is equivalent to 6-18 limited pulls. Tracking: Unfortunately, there is no in-game tracker for hidden chests. Your best resource is community-made chapter maps. Search for each chapter name + "hidden chests" on your favorite content platform. Mark chapters you have fully explored so you do not revisit. Some chests are permanently missable (see below).

Missable Content

Certain story content is permanently missable if you progress past a chapter without completing it: Chapter-specific achievements: Some chapters have time-limited achievements that disappear after the next chapter is released. These give profile namecards. Exploration chests in limited-time story events: Event-based story chapters often have chests that disappear when the event ends. Always fully explore event stories before the event ends. Dialogue choices: Some dialogue choices give affinity points with specific operatives. If you care about maximizing affinity, check a guide for each chapter's optimal dialogue choices. These are not permanently missable — you can revisit chapters from the menu — but many players forget and lose the affinity points. Post-completion exploration: After finishing a chapter, the area remains explorable for 48 hours before locking. During this window, revisit the chapter map and check every corner for chests you missed.

Post-Completion Content: After finishing the main story chapters, you unlock: (1) Deepspace Voyage — permanent endgame story-lite content with new chapters released monthly. (2) Bond episodes — each operative has 4 bond chapters unlocked through gift-giving. These give thousands of diamonds total. (3) World exploration — free-roam maps that become available after certain chapter thresholds. These contain repeatable resource nodes. (4) Challenger mode — replayable boss fights with score tracking. Not all of these are strictly "story," but they are locked behind story progression. Complete the main story as fast as possible to unlock the full breadth of daily activities and resource income.

Speedrunning the Story

Should you rush through story content or take your time? For new players: Rush. Story chapters unlock domains, weekly bosses, and the battle pass. Getting access to these activities as early as possible gives you more daily value. You can re-read the story later from the archive. For lore enthusiasts: Take your time. The story is the main draw of Deepspace. Read all dialogues, explore all areas, and let the atmosphere sink in. The gameplay loop will still be there when you finish. For alts/multi-account players: Install a dialogue auto-clicker if your platform allows it. Skipping story on alt accounts saves approximately 8-12 hours per account. The diamonds are worth running through the story, even if you skip the cutscenes.

Summary: Story mode gives 8,000-10,000 diamonds plus 100+ standard tickets. Hidden chests add 1,000-3,000 more diamonds. Some chests are permanently missable — check community maps. Rush the story for gameplay unlocks, savor it for lore. Post-story content (Voyage, Bonds, exploration) gives even more rewards. The story is the best non-repeatable diamond source in the game.
