The Protofield Records

Origin. The Protofield records were discovered in the deepest surveyed layers of the Deepspace Tunnel—a dimensional corridor that connects Earth to what remains of the Philos dimension. The recordings are not written text but resonant data imprints, stored in crystallized Protocore formations that have survived for over five thousand years. They were first detected by deep-space resonance scanners during an expedition to map the Tunnel's lower strata. Only three personnel were present when the data was recovered. Two of them have since been reassigned to classified positions. The third—a lead decoding specialist—submitted their resignation within a week of completing the translation.

Content. The records describe a repeating pattern that the Philos civilization observed across multiple dimensional boundaries: dimensional thinning followed by protocore decay, followed by Wanderer proliferation, followed by eventual collapse. The Philos referred to this pattern as the "Great Cycle." Their own civilization experienced it. Their records indicate they attempted to stop it. Those attempts failed. The data suggests that Earth is now in the early stages of the same cycle, with one critical difference: the Philos had five hundred years between their first warning and their collapse. Earth has approximately fifty.

Verification. Independent analysis by the Hunter Corps Research Division, Onychinus-affiliated dimensional physicists, and a third party whose identity remains compartmentalized has confirmed the records' authenticity. The 50-year projection is a median estimate. Optimistic models give Earth seventy-five years. Pessimistic models give it twenty-seven. All models agree on one point: the collapse cannot be prevented entirely. The only question is whether it can be mitigated.

The Five Stages of Collapse

The Protofield records divide the collapse process into five distinct stages. Each stage represents a threshold beyond which the dimensional degradation accelerates and becomes exponentially harder to reverse. Understanding where we are in this progression is the single most important strategic question facing every faction—and the answers are not encouraging.

STAGE 1
Dimensional Weakening
The boundary layers between dimensions begin to thin. Small rifts appear in areas of high dimensional stress. Wanderers emerge from these rifts in isolated incidents. The initial response is confusion—no one understands the source of the threat. Global governments dismiss the early signs as natural phenomena. Estimated duration: 5–8 years. Status: PASSED. This stage concluded approximately 12 years ago, marked by the first verified large-scale Wanderer incursion.
STAGE 2
Ecosystem Breakdown
Deepspace energy leaks into the terrestrial environment at an accelerating rate. The energy does not behave like any known radiation. It alters biological structures at the cellular level, causing mutations in flora and fauna. Coral reefs bleach at ten times the historical rate. New plant species emerge that have no evolutionary predecessors. The energy accumulates in the atmosphere, the soil, and the water table. Food chains begin to destabilize. Agriculture yields decline by 15–30% in affected regions. Wanderer spawn rates increase by approximately 40% year-over-year. Status: CURRENT. Current projections place us in the 6th–9th year of this stage. Transition to Stage 3 is expected within 2–4 years.
STAGE 3
Protocore Instability
The energy sources that power both human technology and Wanderer biology become volatile. Protocores begin to exhibit unpredictable behavior: spontaneous discharge, resonant feedback loops, and in extreme cases, localized detonations. The energy grid that supports the Hunter Corps and civilian infrastructure becomes unreliable. Evol users experience fluctuations in their abilities. Wanderers become more aggressive as their core energy sources destabilize. The casualty rate from Protocore-related incidents is projected to increase by 300%. Status: APPROACHING. Early indicators—including increased Protocore fluctuation readings from the N109 monitoring station—suggest this stage will begin within 2–4 years. Preparations are critically behind schedule.
STAGE 4
Mass Corruption
Wanderer outbreaks become uncontrollable. The dimensional barriers that currently limit Wanderer incursions to localized events fail in multiple locations simultaneously. Major population centers face sustained siege conditions. The Hunter Corps, already stretched thin, is forced to triage. Some cities will be abandoned. The corruption spreads beyond physical territory—the dimensional energy begins to alter the fundamental laws of physics in affected areas. Gravity fluctuates. Time moves at inconsistent rates. Communication networks collapse as signals degrade in the corrupted zones. Status: PROJECTED. Estimated onset: 10–18 years from current date. Civilization as we know it will not survive this stage intact.
STAGE 5
Singularity — Dimensional Collapse
The final stage. The dimensional barriers dissolve entirely. Earth and the Philos dimension—and potentially additional dimensions beyond both—merge in an uncontrolled collapse event. The result is not a peaceful fusion but a catastrophic annihilation of both realities. All matter, all energy, all consciousness within the affected dimensions is reduced to a singularity point. This is what happened to the Philos civilization. The Protofield records describe it as "the silence after the last note." There is no recovery from Stage 5. There is no coming back. Status: INEVITABLE WITHOUT INTERVENTION. Estimated onset: 30–50 years from current date. Every effort must be directed at preventing this outcome.

Who Knows the Truth

The full content of the Protofield records is among the most closely guarded information in existence. No single faction possesses the complete picture—each holds fragments, and the fragments tell different stories.

Hunter Corps High Command
ACCESS LEVEL: LIMITED
The upper echelons of the Hunter Corps are aware of the Protofield records and the 50-year projection. However, the information has been compartmentalized. Only the Commander and two senior research directors have reviewed the full data. The operative corps has been told that the threat level is "elevated but manageable." The true scope of the collapse prediction has been withheld to prevent panic and maintain operational effectiveness. Select field commanders have been briefed on a need-to-know basis, but none have been shown the complete records.
Onychinus — Sylus's Network
ACCESS LEVEL: EXTENSIVE
Sylus has assembled the most comprehensive intelligence network on the Protofield records outside of the Hunter Corps' classified archives. Through a combination of infiltrated personnel, intercepted transmissions, and independent research by Onychinus-affiliated dimensional physicists, he has reconstructed approximately 80% of the records' content. His knowledge of the collapse timeline is believed to be more accurate than the Hunter Corps' own projections in certain areas. What he plans to do with this information—and why he has not shared it with the Corps—remains unknown.
Aether Core Researchers
ACCESS LEVEL: PARTIAL
The scientific teams studying the Aether Core in Linkon City have independently verified aspects of the Protofield data through their own research. Their access is limited to the Core's energy signature records, which correlate with the collapse timeline described in the Protofield data. They do not have access to the full records and are unaware of the complete five-stage model. Their research has led them to believe that the Aether Core may be the key to stabilizing the dimensional boundary—a conclusion that aligns with the MC's unique resonance abilities.
The MC — Dreams & Resonance
ACCESS LEVEL: FRAGMENTED (INSTINCTIVE)
The MC has been receiving fragments of the Protofield records through dreams and resonance episodes since childhood. These fragments are not organized chronologically or thematically—they come as disconnected images, sensations, and impressions. The MC does not possess the intellectual framework to decode these fragments, but their resonance with the Aether Core provides an intuitive understanding of the dimensional mechanics involved. Several key predictions from the Protofield records have appeared in the MC's dreams before they were verified by scientific analysis. This suggests that the MC's connection to the records is deeper than anyone currently understands.

Critical Observation: The fragmentation of information is not accidental. The Protofield records appear to have been designed to prevent any single entity from holding the complete picture. The Philos civilization encoded their warning across multiple data formats and resonance frequencies, perhaps anticipating that their knowledge would be misused if it fell into the wrong hands. The result, five thousand years later, is that humanity faces its greatest threat armed with incomplete intelligence.

Attempts to Stop It

Three broad approaches to preventing or mitigating the collapse have been identified. All three have limitations. None offers a guaranteed solution. The choice of approach may determine the future of human civilization.

S
Sealing
PRIMARY STRATEGY — Hunter Corps
Sealing involves closing dimensional rifts as they appear, preventing Wanderer incursions and slowing the leakage of Deepspace energy into the terrestrial environment. This is the Hunter Corps' primary operational doctrine. It is effective as a short-term containment measure but does not address the underlying dimensional degradation. Sealing a rift is like plugging a leak in a dam that is structurally failing—it buys time but does not prevent the eventual collapse. The Corps has become highly efficient at sealing operations, closing an average of 14 rifts per month across the globe. However, new rifts are appearing at an accelerating rate of approximately 18% year-over-year. The math is unsustainable.
P
Purification
SECONDARY STRATEGY — Independent Research
Purification targets the corrupted dimensional energy itself, using resonance-based techniques to cleanse the Deepspace energy and restore dimensional stability. This approach is slower than sealing but addresses the root cause rather than the symptoms. The Aether Core is believed to be the most powerful purification instrument available, and the MC's resonance abilities may be the key to activating it. Limited purification efforts have been attempted in controlled environments, with promising but inconclusive results. The purification rate currently lags behind the corruption rate by a factor of approximately 1:7. Scaling purification to the level required would demand energy output that exceeds current technological capabilities.
T
Transcendence
FAILED PRECEDENT — Philos Solution
Transcendence was the Philos civilization's attempted solution to the Great Cycle. Rather than preventing the collapse, they sought to escape it—to transfer their civilization to another dimension entirely. The Protofield records indicate that the Philos succeeded in creating dimensional gate technology capable of cross-dimensional transit. However, the solution failed: the destination dimension was incompatible with Philos biology, and the transition itself caused catastrophic damage to the Philos civilization's physical and cultural continuity. The survivors who reached Earth were irrevocably changed. The modern implications of Transcendence are deeply troubling, as Sylus has shown significant interest in dimensional gate research—raising the question of whether he is planning a similar escape.

What the Warning Means for the Future

The 50-year warning is not just a prophecy of doom—it is a call to action. Every faction, every alliance, and every individual must confront the implications of the Protofield records and decide how to respond.

The Countdown Creates Urgency. The 50-year timeline compresses every strategic decision. Research that would normally take decades must be completed in years. Alliances that would normally be forged through gradual diplomacy must be formed immediately. The luxury of incremental progress has been revoked. Every month of delay reduces the available options. The Hunter Corps has begun restructuring its operations with this timeline in mind, but the pace of change is still too slow. Onychinus, operating without bureaucratic constraints, has adapted more quickly—but to what end remains unclear.

The MC Must Awaken Before Stage 3. The transition from Stage 2 to Stage 3 is the critical inflection point. Once Protocore instability reaches critical mass, the dimensional environment will become too volatile for precise resonance work. The MC's ability to achieve full resonance with the Aether Core—and potentially reverse or mitigate the collapse—depends on training and awakening before this threshold is crossed. Current projections give a window of 2–4 years. If the MC has not achieved resonance mastery within that window, the purification approach becomes functionally impossible, and only sealing or transcendence remain viable.

The Connection Between Resonance and Reversal. The Protofield records contain a crucial detail that has only recently been decoded: the Great Cycle can be interrupted at the dimensional level through precise resonant interference. The Aether Core is the instrument. The MC is the operator. The theory suggests that a sufficiently powerful resonance pulse, properly tuned to the dimensional frequency, could reinflate the thinning boundary layers and reset the collapse countdown. This has never been attempted. The Philos records do not indicate whether they possessed a Resonance operative of sufficient power. Earth may have one chance. The timing of that chance is measured not in years but in the resonance synchronization between the MC and the Aether Core.

The Final Assessment: The 50-Year Warning is real. The Protofield records have been verified. The collapse is coming. But the warning itself is a gift—a gift of time, however limited, and of direction, however uncertain. The Philos had five centuries and failed. Earth has five decades and cannot afford to fail. The countdown is not a death sentence. It is a deadline. And deadlines, unlike endings, can be met. — Protofield Analysis Team, Eyes Only
