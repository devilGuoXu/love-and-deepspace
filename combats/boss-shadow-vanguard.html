⚔️ The Core Insight: Shadow Vanguard isn't about damage — it's about reaction time. He teleports constantly. His attacks come from nowhere. The key is learning where he'll appear before he appears. Pattern recognition beats reflexes.

Boss Overview — The Shadow Vanguard

The Shadow Vanguard is an elite assassin boss encountered in Chaos Abyss Floors 8-10. He specializes in teleportation attacks, appearing behind you, above you, or from the shadows. Most players struggle because they can't track his movement.

Phase 1 — Teleportation Patterns (100% - 50% HP)

The Shadow Vanguard has four teleport attacks in Phase 1. Learn each tell.

🌀 Behind You Slash
Teleports directly behind you and slashes.
Tell: Dark smoke swirls around your feet
Dodge Timing: 0.3s after smoke appears
Punish Window: 1.5 seconds — short!
💨 Shadow Dash
Becomes a shadow on the ground, dashes toward you, then appears and slashes.
Tell: Shadow on ground moving toward you
Dodge Timing: When shadow reaches your feet
Punish Window: 2 seconds
⬆️ Dive Bomb
Teleports high above, then drops down with both blades.
Tell: Shadow circle appears on ground below
Dodge Timing: 0.2s before impact, dodge out of circle
Punish Window: 2.5 seconds after he lands
🌀 Triple Teleport Combo
Teleports three times in quick succession, slashing each time.
Tell: First teleport, then second, then third
Dodge Timing: Dodge each slash individually
Punish Window: 3 seconds after third slash

💡 Pro Tip: After any teleport, Shadow Vanguard has a 1-2 second recovery animation where he can't attack. This is your punish window. Don't waste it.

Phase 2 — Enraged Patterns (50% - 0% HP)

At 50% HP, Shadow Vanguard enters Phase 2. Attack speed increases by 40%. New patterns emerge.

🌀 Shadow Clone
Creates a clone that mimics his attacks. Two enemies to track.
Tell: Boss splits into two shadows
Strategy: The clone has less HP. Kill it first.
Danger: High — double the attacks
🌀 Phantom Rush
Teleports 5 times rapidly, slashing randomly around the arena.
Tell: Boss disappears for 1 second
Strategy: Keep moving. Don't stand still.
Punish Window: After 5th teleport — 2 seconds
🌑 Shadow Storm
Creates a large AoE circle that damages over time.
Tell: Dark circle expands from boss
Strategy: Run out immediately. Stay outside.
Duration: 8 seconds — don't re-enter
💀 Execution Strike
Below 20% HP, massive damage single strike.
Tell: Boss glows dark purple
Dodge Timing: Exactly when he raises both blades
Note: This is a one-shot. Don't miss the dodge.

Common Mistakes (I Made All of These)

❌ Standing still during teleport combos
Keep moving. A moving target is harder to hit. Shadow Vanguard tracks your position at the moment of teleport.
❌ Ignoring bleed stacks
Each hit increases damage taken. After 3 stacks, heal immediately. Don't let it reach 5.
❌ Not punishing after teleports
His recovery window is short but real. Use it. Don't just dodge — counter-attack.
❌ Killing the clone first in Phase 2
Actually, that's correct. Kill the clone first. But don't ignore the main boss completely.
❌ Panic dodging
His attacks are telegraphed. Watch for the tells. Panic dodging wastes stamina and misses the timing.

🎯 The Bottom Line: Shadow Vanguard is a pattern recognition boss, not a DPS check. Learn his tells. Practice the dodge timings. Punish after every teleport. With Xavier (Light element) and good reactions, this becomes one of the most satisfying fights in the game.

The key is staying calm. His teleports are designed to disorient you. Don't chase him — let him come to you. Watch. Wait. Punish.
