Frost Tyrant

The ancient ice dragon that nearly destroyed the Hunter Corps. Arena-wide blizzards and one-shot mechanics.

Phase 1: Hoarfrost (100%-70%)

Frost Breath: Wide ice cone. Dodge sideways. The breath lingers on the ground for 5 seconds. Tail Sweep: 270-degree arc behind the Tyrant. Stay near the head 閳?safe zone. Glacial Shard: Summons icicles that fall on marked locations. 3 waves, increasing speed.

Phase 2: Blizzard King (70%-35%)

Blizzard: Arena-wide storm. Vision reduced. Move toward the Tyrant's roars. Ice Tomb: Freezes one operative in a crystal. Break them out with fire damage. Frozen Roar: AoE freeze wave. Jump to avoid. Getting frozen = immediate Blizzard follow-up.

Phase 3: Permafrost (35%-0%)

Permafrost Aura: Tyrant pulses constant frost damage. Range attacks only. Absolute Zero: Massive channeled freeze. Run behind ice pillars for cover. Dragon's End: Tyrant crashes to the ground. All damage windows open. This is the kill phase.

Fire Element Stacking Strategy

Rafayel's +50% Fire damage advantage against the Frost Tyrant is the largest elemental bonus in any boss encounter. To maximize this advantage, build Rafayel with full Fire Damage Bonus protocores on all available slots. The Fire Damage Bonus stat stacks additively with the natural advantage, meaning a Rafayel with 20% Fire Damage Bonus from protocores effectively deals +70% damage to the Frost Tyrant. This level of damage output allows you to skip entire mechanics in Phase 1 and Phase 2 by pushing HP thresholds before the Tyrant executes its dangerous attacks.

Break the Frost Tyrant's Ice Tomb mechanic by ensuring Rafayel's Resonance skill is always available when a teammate gets frozen. The Ice Tomb has 15 hits of health and can only be damaged by Fire element. Rafayel's Flame Whip destroys it in approximately 3 seconds. Xavier and Zayne deal zero damage to the Ice Tomb. If Rafayel is the one frozen, your team must wait the full 10-second duration for the Ice Tomb to expire naturally. This makes Rafayel's positioning critical 閳?keep him at mid-range rather than close range to reduce the chance he gets caught by the targeted freeze.

Phase 3 Pillar Positioning

Absolute Zero creates ice pillars around the arena that provide the only safe cover from the channeled freeze. Three pillars spawn in a triangle pattern. The pillar closest to the Tyrant is the safest because the Absolute Zero AoE originates from the Tyrant's position, making the farthest pillar the most exposed to the edge of the blast radius. Memorize the pillar spawn positions during Phase 2 so you can pre-position near the closest pillar before Phase 3 begins.

When hiding behind a pillar during Absolute Zero, crouch to minimize your hitbox exposure. The pillar's cover arc is approximately 120 degrees. If you stand at full height, your head hitbox extends above the pillar's top edge and the freeze can still affect you. Crouching reduces your total hitbox by approximately 40%, ensuring complete cover behind the pillar. This is a common source of unexpected deaths during Phase 3 閳?players assume full cover from the pillar but take freeze damage because they are standing.

During Blizzard phase, the Frost Tyrant roars every 10 seconds. The roar volume increases as you face toward the Tyrant. If the roar gets quieter, rotate. Use audio cues rather than reduced visibility to navigate.

Advanced Tip: The Frost Tyrant's Blizzard phase has a hidden mechanic 閳?the roars are directional. The Tyrant always roars toward the nearest target. Follow the roars to navigate through the storm. Don't rely on visual cues during Blizzard 閳?sound is your guide.

Summary: The Frost Tyrant is the hardest standard boss. Arena awareness is critical 閳?Blizzard disorients, Ice Tomb requires fire damage, and Absolute Zero needs cover. Rafayel is mandatory for breaking frozen teammates. Save burst for Phase 3.
