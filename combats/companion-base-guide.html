Base Overview

The Dawnbreak Base is the Hunter's personal living space, shared with occasional visits from the operatives. The base consists of multiple rooms that can be decorated with furniture, each room having its own level and Affinity rating. The base serves as a social hub where you can interact with operatives outside of combat and story contexts, building Affinity through casual interactions rather than structured content.

Base progression is separate from operative Affinity but feeds into it — placing furniture an operative likes increases their Visit Chance, and successful interactions during visits award Affinity EXP. The base also has its own leveling system that rewards gems, exclusive furniture, and base expansions. Think of it as a secondary progression track that complements the main Affinity system.

Room System

The base starts with a Living Room and a Bedroom. Additional rooms unlock as you level the base: Kitchen (level 3), Study (level 5), Balcony (level 7), and Garden (level 10). Each room can hold a limited number of furniture items based on its level. Rooms also have a Comfort rating that increases when you place furniture matching the room's theme — higher Comfort increases the chance of operative visits to that specific room.

Furniture & Decor

Furniture is obtained through the Furniture Shop (using base coins earned from daily check-ins and interactions), Event rewards, Battle Pass milestones, and gem purchases. Furniture items have rarity tiers matching the gift system: Common, Uncommon, Rare, and Epic. Higher rarity furniture provides more Comfort points and has a greater effect on operative visit rates.

Each operative has preferred furniture styles. Xavier prefers elegant, celestial-themed decor. Zayne likes clean, minimalist furniture with natural elements. Rafayel favors artistic, colorful pieces with ocean motifs. Sylus appreciates dark, luxurious, and imposing furniture. Matching furniture to operative preferences increases their happiness during visits, which increases the Affinity EXP earned from interactions.

Operative Visits

Operatives visit the Dawnbreak Base based on Base Level, Room Comfort, and Furniture Preferences. Visits are random but influenced by these factors. During a visit, you can interact with the operative through various room-specific activities: cooking together in the Kitchen, reading in the Study, gardening on the Balcony, etc. Each interaction awards Affinity EXP (50-200 depending on the activity and furniture quality).

Visits happen when you enter the base — there's no notification system. Check your base multiple times per day, especially after completing other activities. The visit cooldown is approximately 4 hours, meaning you can potentially get 3-4 visit windows per day if you check regularly.

Progression Rewards

Base leveling rewards include gems, furniture shop currency, exclusive furniture items, and room expansions. Key milestones: Base Level 3 (Kitchen unlock, 50 gems), Base Level 5 (Study unlock, 80 gems), Base Level 7 (Balcony unlock, 100 gems), Base Level 10 (Garden unlock, 150 gems, exclusive decor set). The total gem payout from maxing the base is substantial and should not be ignored.

Tip: Focus furniture purchases on a single room to maximize its Comfort level quickly. A Level 5 Living Room with full Rare furniture will attract more operative visits than spreading furniture across 3 rooms at Level 2 each. Build one room at a time for optimal visit rates and interaction rewards.

Summary: The Dawnbreak Base is a secondary Affinity progression system that rewards daily interaction and smart furniture investment. Level your base to unlock rooms (prioritize Living Room for most visit opportunities), buy furniture matching operative preferences, and check for visits every 4 hours. Base leveling provides gems and exclusive items that make it worth the time investment even for combat-focused players.
