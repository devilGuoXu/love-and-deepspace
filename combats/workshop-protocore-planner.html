Protocore Farming & Upgrade Planner

Protocore farming isn't random if you plan it. This guide provides tools and frameworks to plan your protocore farming efficiently — drop rate tables, stamina budgets, and upgrade priority matrices.

Overview

Most players treat protocore farming as an endless grind — run the highest domain available, hope for good drops, repeat. This approach wastes stamina and delays your progression. A structured plan transforms farming from luck-based gambling into a predictable resource pipeline. When you know exactly which domain to run, which stats to target, and when to stop farming a given slot, you cut your time-to-target by more than half.

Core Principle: 90% of protocore farming results come from planning, not grinding. The 10% that requires grinding goes toward targeted substat refinement, not random main stat fishing. A player with a plan who runs Domain 6 thirty times will have a better team than a player who runs Domain 6 one hundred times without direction.

This planner covers every aspect of protocore farming optimization: the actual drop probabilities so you know what you are rolling for, domain routing based on your account stage, a stamina budget template to allocate your weekly energy, a priority matrix for upgrade order, and milestone checkpoints to track progress. Use the sections below to build your personal farming roadmap.

Early Game (Account Level 1-44)
Run Domain 3 exclusively. It drops all three shapes evenly and has the lowest stamina cost per run. Your goal at this stage is to get any SSR protocore with a correct main stat on each of the 6 slots. Do not worry about substats yet. Focus on clearing the domain quickly with the units you have leveled.
Mid Game (Account Level 45-59)
Transition to Domain 5. The substat pool is weighted toward offensive rolls — ATK%, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG appear more frequently in upgrade rolls. Farm Domain 5 until all six DPS slots have usable main stats AND at least two substat upgrades that are not flat DEF or flat HP. Domain 5 is your home for approximately 3-4 weeks.
End Game (Account Level 60+)
Domain 6 becomes your primary domain when you need specific slot shapes. Want a Triangle for Slot 2? Domain 6 has weighted Triangle drops. Use Domain 6 for targeted shape farming and Domain 5 for general substat improvement. Alternate between them based on your immediate need.

Efficiency Rule: Do not farm Domain 6 until your main DPS has all six slots at +12. Domain 6 is for refinement, not foundation. Premature Domain 6 farming slows your overall progression because you miss the substat value that Domain 5 provides in bulk.

Protocore Domains (600 stamina / ~39%) — This is your primary investment. Run 8-9 protocore domains per day. Focus on the domain stage appropriate for your progression level (see Farming Route Planner above). This allocation guarantees at least 75 runs per week, yielding approximately 6 SSR protocores on average.

Memory Domains (450 stamina / ~29%) — Memory experience and upgrade materials. Running memory domains every other day ensures your equipped memories stay leveled. Neglecting memory levels is a common mistake — a well-leveled memory with bad protocores outperforms a max-level protocore set on level 1 memories.

Credit / EXP Domains (360 stamina / ~23%) — Gold and hunter EXP. You will always need more credits than you think. Leveling protocores from 0 to 15 costs approximately 270,000 credits per piece — over 1.6 million for a full team of 6. Run credit domains at least every third day.

Event Reserve (150 stamina / ~10%) — Keep this buffer for limited-time events, double-drop campaigns, or emergency upgrade needs. If events are quiet, funnel this into protocore domains instead. Never let your stamina cap overflow.

Budget Adjustment: If you are in an active event week, swap the reserve allocation with credit allocation — events usually provide enough credits. Conversely, during double-drop protocore events, push protocore allocation up to 50% and reduce memory domains. The template is a baseline, not a rule — adjust based on the current event calendar.

Upgrade Priority Matrix

Not all protocore slots are equal. Upgrading in the wrong order wastes resources. Use this priority matrix to decide which slot to upgrade next on each team member.

S-TIER
DPS Slot 4 (CRIT) + Slot 6 (ATK%)
These two slots determine 60% of your total DPS. Slot 4 CRIT main stat (Rate or DMG) and Slot 6 ATK% are the highest-impact upgrades in the entire game. Prioritize getting both to +12 before touching any other protocore upgrade on your DPS.
A-TIER
DPS Slot 1 (ATK)
Slot 1 is pure ATK main stat. Every upgrade level adds raw attack power. After your Slot 4 and Slot 6 are +12, bring Slot 1 to +12. This is your third-highest priority for any DPS character.
B-TIER
DPS Slot 2, 3, 5 (Substats)
Slots 2, 3, and 5 provide main stats that are less impactful (HP%, DEF%, DMG Bonus, flat stats). Their value comes from substat rolls. Upgrade these to +9 first, then push to +12 only after your S and A tier pieces are at +15. The substat rolls at +3, +6, +9 matter more than the main stat on these slots.
C-TIER
Sub-DPS & Support Slots
Sub-DPS characters follow the same priority as DPS but two tiers lower — get Slot 4/6 to +9, everything else to +6. Support characters should have HP% or ER main stats on Slot 6 at +6, and all other slots at +3. Support protocores are for enabling — they do not need high levels to function.

Upgrade Order for a New DPS: Slot 4 to +12 > Slot 6 to +12 > Slot 1 to +12 > Slot 3 to +9 > Slot 2 to +9 > Slot 5 to +9 > all six to +15 in the same order. This sequence gives you the highest DPS return per credit spent at every step.

Milestone Planner

Track your protocore progression against these milestones. Each milestone represents a tangible power gain that unlocks harder content.

1
Week 1 — Foundation
Correct main stat on all 6 DPS slots. Do not worry about set bonuses or rarity. Even SR protocores with the right main stat are acceptable at this stage. Your goal is to have a functioning stat layout. Recommended domain: Domain 3 for fastest SSR rate vs stamina.
2
Week 2-3 — Core Upgrade
DPS slots 4, 6, and 1 at +9. Support slots at +6. Start replacing SR pieces with SSR pieces as they drop. At this stage, your DPS should have approximately 1,800 ATK, 45% CRIT Rate, 100% CRIT DMG. This is enough to clear Domain 5 comfortably.
3
Month 1 — Full +12
All six slots on your main DPS at +12. Sub-DPS at +9. Support at +6. Your main team is now ready for Abyss floors 1-8. At this milestone, stop farming your main DPS protocores and shift focus to your second team. The second team only needs to reach the Week 2-3 standard to be Abyss-viable.
4
Month 2 — Substats Refinement
Return to your main DPS and start refining substats. Goal: at least 2 useful substat rolls (CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ATK%) on slots 4, 6, and 1. Slots 2, 3, 5 should have at least 1 useful roll. Average DPS CRIT Rate should be 55%+. Average CRIT DMG 130%+.
5
Month 3+ — God-Roll Chasing
Now you are in the endgame. Target: triple or quad-roll CRIT substats on slots 4 and 6. Slot 1 with ATK% + CRIT Rate + CRIT DMG. This is the 80/20 zone — the last 20% of optimization takes 80% of the time. Only chase these if you are clearing all current content comfortably.

Progression Tip: Do not linger on milestone 1. Getting correct main stats is the quickest power spike in the game. You can go from zero to full correct main stats in one focused farming session (approximately 400 stamina). The real time gate starts at milestone 3 when you need upgrade materials and credits. Budget your stamina accordingly.

Common Planning Mistakes

Mistake 1: Farming Without a Target
The most common mistake is running domains without knowing what you need. You do Domain 6 because it is the highest unlocked, get random protocores, and check if they are good. This is aimless grinding. Always enter a domain knowing exactly which slot and main stat you are targeting. If you need Slot 4 CRIT Rate, count how many runs you are willing to dedicate before pivoting. Unplanned farming is the largest stamina sink in the game.
Mistake 2: Over-Farming Early
Players below level 45 who spend 100% of their stamina on protocore domains are delaying their account progression. Memory levels, character ascension, and skill upgrades all multiply your protocore effectiveness. A level 60 character with +9 protocores outperforms a level 40 character with +15 protocores. Level your account first, then optimize protocores.
Mistake 3: Ignoring Support Protocores
Support protocores are treated as an afterthought by most players. This is a mistake. A support with HP% Slot 6 at +12 enables your DPS to stay on field longer. A support with Energy Recharge main stat on Slot 6 feeds your DPS ultimate uptime. Investing 200 stamina into support protocores can increase your team DPS by 15-20% simply by keeping your DPS alive and ulting more often.
Mistake 4: Chasing Perfect Pieces
The 80/20 rule applies brutally to protocore farming. Getting a usable piece takes 1-2 domain sessions. Getting a great piece takes 1-2 weeks. Getting a perfect piece (correct main stat, triple-roll CRIT, matching set) can take months. The time cost of perfection is rarely worth it. Settle for "great" on your main DPS and "good" on everyone else. The content does not require perfect pieces — it requires consistent, planned farming.

Summary: Protocore farming efficiency comes from planning, not grinding. Key rates: SSR 8% baseline, CRIT main stats ~8% per slot-4 drop. Domain routing: Domain 3 early, Domain 5 mid, Domain 6 end. Weekly stamina budget: 600 protocore, 450 memory, 360 credit, 150 reserve. Upgrade priority: DPS Slot 4/6 (S-tier), Slot 1 (A-tier), Slots 2/3/5 (B-tier), support slots (C-tier). Milestones: Week 1 (main stats), Week 2-3 (+9 core), Month 1 (all +12), Month 2 (substat refinement), Month 3+ (god-rolls). Avoid aimless farming, early over-farming, support neglect, and perfection chasing. Follow the plan, adjust for events, and your protocore progression will outpace players who grind without direction.
