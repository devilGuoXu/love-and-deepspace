PROTOCORE

Protocore 成长路线图：从新手到毕业

A phase-by-phase progression roadmap covering exactly which protocores to keep, level, salvage, and chase from your first day to endgame farming. Follow the phases in order — skipping ahead wastes months of stamina.

2026.07.11 ENTRY_#142 ROADMAP
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Why a Roadmap: Most players waste their early stamina by farming the wrong domains, leveling bad protocores, or salvaging pieces they should have kept. This guide tells you exactly what to do at each level bracket so you never waste a single stamina point on a dead-end protocore.

Phase 1 新手期 (1-40级)

Focus: Survival & Basic ATK — Your goal during this phase is simple: clear the story and unlock all domains. Protocores are secondary to character levels and memory ascension. Use whatever drops — you will replace everything later.

Equip ATK% main stat on every slot. Do not worry about set bonuses. A pair of mismatched protocores both with ATK% main stat will outperform a matched set with wrong main stats. Level protocores to +6 maximum. Never take anything to +9 or higher — the material cost is not worth it for pieces you will salvage in a week.

Salvage everything that does not have ATK% as its main stat. Flat stats are traps. DEF% and HP% are useless at this stage. Delete them without hesitation. Your inventory should be ATK% pieces only.

ATK% on all slots +6 max level No set bonus chasing
Stamina allocation: 80% Story / Progression 20% Protocore Domains
Phase 2 发展期 (40-55级)

Focus: First Set Bonuses — You now have access to domain levels that drop SSR protocores. Start chasing 2-piece set bonuses. The Solar set (ATK +12%) is the universal choice for all DPS operatives. Two Solar pieces equipped = immediate 12% ATK increase over mismatched pieces.

Correct main stats remain priority #1. A Solar piece with the wrong main stat is not worth equipping. Level your main DPS character's protocores to +9 (all 6 slots). Sub-DPS stays at +6. Support operatives stay at +3. This tiered investment ensures your main damage dealer gets the best stats while your supports get functional minimums.

Your CRIT rate target is a minimum of 30%. Begin checking substats when pieces drop, but do not chase substats heavily. The priority order is: correct main stat > set bonus > substats. Salvage guideline: keep only pieces that have 2 or more usable substats (CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ATK%).

2-piece Solar set (DPS) +9 main DPS, +6 sub-DPS, +3 support CRIT Rate 30% minimum
Stamina allocation: 50% Protocore 50% Other (Memories / Credits / EXP)
Phase 3 成型期 (55-65级)

Focus: Complete 4-Piece Sets — Your main DPS should now have a full 4-piece set bonus. Solar set is the general-purpose choice for all operatives. Permafrost set is for Zayne's frost specialty. Shadow set is for Sylus's execute-focused build. The 4-piece bonus provides a significant power spike that cannot be ignored.

Your CRIT rate breakpoint is 50% for your main DPS. Level main DPS protocores to +12. Sub-DPS goes to +9. Support stays at +6. This is also the phase where you begin building a second team's protocores. You need 8 operatives for Abyss — start farming toward that now.

Use the +3 filter strategy: level all promising pieces to +3 and check the first substat. If it is CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ATK%, or ER, continue leveling. If it is flat DEF or flat HP, salvage immediately. The +3 filter is the single most important resource-saving technique in the game. A 10-second decision at +3 saves thousands of enhancement materials.

4-piece set (main DPS) +12 main DPS, +9 sub-DPS, +6 support CRIT Rate 50% breakpoint
Stamina allocation: 40% Protocore 30% Memories 30% Credits / EXP
Phase 4 精进期 (65-75级)

Focus: Substat Refinement — You are now in the mid-to-late game. Your main DPS should have +12 on all slots with 2 or more good substats each. CRIT rate target: 60% or higher. CRIT DMG target: 140% or higher. These are the baseline numbers for clearing Abyss floors 10-12.

Sub-DPS: +12 on 3-4 key slots with a complete 4-piece set. Support: +9 on all slots with Energy Recharge or HP main stats. Energy Recharge breakpoints become important now. Sylus operates best at 145% ER for his execute rotation. Zayne needs 140% ER for consistent CC uptime. Rafayel functions well at 120% ER. Xavier does not need ER — he should focus CRIT stats exclusively because his burst rotation is cooldown-gated, not energy-gated.

Start salvaging +9 pieces that roll badly. If a protocore reaches +9 with only 1 useful substat, salvage it. Do not fall for the sunk cost fallacy — you will find better pieces. At this stage, you are refining, not building from scratch.

CRIT Rate 60%+ CRIT DMG 140%+ ER breakpoints per operative
Stamina allocation: 30% Protocore 30% Memories 20% Events 20% Credits / EXP
Phase 5 毕业期 (75+级)

Focus: Min-Max Substats & God-Roll Chasing — Welcome to the endgame. Every DPS protocore should be at +15. Every sub-DPS and support protocore should be at +12 minimum. Your CRIT rate target is 70% or higher. Your CRIT DMG target is 180% or higher.

Begin pushing for CRIT DMG body pieces (Slot 4) with CRIT Rate substats. This is the most efficient stat combo in the game — a CRIT DMG main stat with high CRIT Rate rolls gives you both damage multiplier and consistency in a single slot. Chase specific substat distributions per operative. Sylus needs CRIT DMG subs on every piece. Zayne wants HP% and ER subs. Xavier wants pure CRIT and ATK.

Accept nothing below 3 useful substats for new pieces. If a protocore has 3+ substats that match your operative's needs, level it. If it has 2 or fewer, salvage it immediately. This is the domain of incremental gains — each upgrade might take weeks of farming. A 2% CRIT rate improvement is a good week. Accept the grind or stay at phase 4 levels.

+15 all DPS, +12 supports CRIT Rate 70%+ CRIT DMG 180%+
Stamina allocation: 20% Protocore 30% Events 30% Limited Farming 20% Whatever you need

常见失误 — Common Mistakes by Phase

Each progression phase has its own traps. Here is what to watch out for at every stage.

Phase Common Mistake Impact Fix
1-2 Leveling bad main stat to +12 Wastes 80% of enhancement materials Never exceed +6 on pieces you will replace
2-3 Overvaluing set bonuses over substats Wrong set with good substats beats right set with bad substats Prioritize correct main stat first, then substats, then set
3-4 Not using the +3 filter Blindly leveling to +12 wastes thousands of materials Level every piece to +3 first — salvage if first substat is bad
4-5 Chasing perfect pieces while other characters are naked One god-rolled DPS with naked supports cannot clear Abyss Equip all 8 Abyss operatives before refining any single one
5+ Salvaging "good enough" pieces chasing perfection You salvage usable gear and end up with worse stats overall Keep pieces with 3+ useful substats. Only salvage if below threshold

The +3 Rule: Level every new protocore to +3 immediately. If the first substat is CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ATK%, ER (Sylus), or HP% (Zayne), keep it. If it is flat DEF, flat HP, or flat ATK, salvage it. This 5-second check saves 80% of enhancement material waste across your entire account.

Do This
  • Follow phases sequentially — do not skip
  • Salvage early and often
  • Use the +3 filter on every piece
  • Invest tiered: main DPS first, then sub-DPS, then support
  • Build second team before perfecting first team
Avoid This
  • Leveling bad main stats to +12
  • Chasing set bonuses before correct main stats
  • The sunk cost fallacy on bad rolls
  • Leaving supports naked while chasing god rolls
  • Using flat stat protocores past Phase 2

The Marathon, Not the Sprint: The protocore progression path is a long journey. Each phase builds on the foundation of the previous one. Do not try to farm Phase 5 protocores at level 45 — you will burn out and waste resources. Follow this roadmap in order, check off milestones as you complete them, and trust the process. Every endgame player started exactly where you are now. The difference is they followed a plan. Now you have one too.
