Boss Dodge Pattern Reference

Exact dodge timing for every boss — parry windows, safe zones, and attack telegraphs for endgame encounters.

Titan

Titan has 3 distinct phases with different attack patterns. Phase 1 (100-60% HP): Slow overhead slam — dodge when the arm reaches its highest point (0.4s window). Ground pound — dodge as the fist hits the ground (0.2s window). Charge — dodge perpendicular to charge direction. Phase 2 (60-25% HP): Adds shockwave attack — jump over the wave or dodge through it (0.3s window). Double slam — two hits, dodge the first then immediately dodge again. Phase 3 (25-0% HP): Enrage mode — all attack speeds increase by 40%. Triple slam with shockwave — three dodges in sequence. Core exposed — massive damage window after the third slam.

Crystal

Crystal is a ranged boss with projectile-heavy patterns. Phase 1 (100-50% HP): Homing crystals — dodge right as they fire (0.3s window). Crystal rain — run to the edge, dodge the last wave. Ground spike — dodge when the ground cracks (0.5s window). Phase 2 (50-0% HP): Adds tracking laser — strafe continuously, dodge at the pulse audio cue. Crystal prison — dodge out before it forms. Summon adds — kill adds immediately or they explode. Crystal's tells are audio-based — listen for the chime before attacks.

Phantoms

Phantom bosses have delayed attack timing designed to catch early dodgers. Xavier Phantom: Dagger throw — dodge 0.3s after the flash (not on flash). Teleport strike — dodge the instant he appears behind you. Sylus Phantom: Scythe sweep — wait for the weapon glow, then dodge (0.5s delay). Dark orbs — dodge through, not away from them. Zayne Phantom: Ice shards — dodge right as his hand extends. Frost ring — dodge inward through the ring. Rafayel Phantom: Water cutters — dodge perpendicular. Bubble trap — dodge back twice.

General rules: Dodging too early is worse than dodging too late (enemies track). The dodge i-frames start on frame 1 of the dodge animation. Perfect dodges (dodging into attacks) give you a longer window than dodging away. Against most bosses, dodge toward the attack for better positioning and the perfect dodge damage bonus.

Safe Zones by Boss

Titan: Behind the legs during slams, under the body during charge. Crystal: Corners of the arena avoid most projectiles. Phantoms: Close range is safer than long range (most Phantom attacks are mid-range). Co-op bosses: Stack with the boss's facing direction (behind is safe from frontal attacks). General: Most bosses have a 30-60 degree safe arc behind them. Use this for free damage windows.

Important: Boss attack patterns change at HP thresholds (usually 60% and 25%). Do not assume the pattern you learned in phase 1 applies to phase 2. Re-learn the tells for each phase.

Parry Windows

Parrying (Ardent Oath parry) has a tighter window than dodging but rewards you with a stun and damage multiplier. Parryable attacks: All physical melee swings (not projectiles or magical attacks). The parry window is the last 0.2s of the attack animation — right before the hit connects. Visual cue: When the boss's weapon glows just before impact. Audio cue: A sharp "click" sound 0.1s before the parry window. Practice parrying on Titan slams — they have the most generous parry timing. Parrying Crystal attacks is not recommended (projectiles cannot be parried).

Pro Tip: For each boss you farm weekly, spend 5 minutes at the start of the week doing dodge-only runs. No attacking — just dodge for 5 minutes. This retrains your muscle memory at the start of every week and dramatically improves consistency.

Summary: Each boss has 2-3 phases with different tells. Dodge windows range from 0.2s (fast) to 1.5s (telegraphed). Dodge toward attacks for perfect dodge bonuses. Practice each pattern 50 times. Parry physical melee attacks for stun damage windows. Safe zones are typically behind or under the boss.
