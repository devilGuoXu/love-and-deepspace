BASIC PROTOCOLS — DODGE REFERENCE

Boss Dodge Pattern Reference

Every boss attack has a tell. Some are obvious (1.5s wind-up). Some are frame-perfect (0.2s). Memorize these timings and no boss attack will ever hit you.

ENTRY_#0652026.07.02DODGE

Universal Rule: Every attack in Deepspace has a sound cue + visual flash + animation telegraph. Use all three senses to dodge — if you only watch the animation, you will miss attacks with subtle tells. The sound cue is often the fastest indicator (0.1s reaction advantage over visual).

Titan Sentry

Club Slam (AoE): Telegraph: raises club overhead (1.2s). Sound: deep rumble. Perfect dodge: when the club reaches its highest point. Safe zone: behind the Sentry. Charge: Telegraph: lowers shoulder, digs in (0.8s). Dodge: perpendicular, not backward. The charge hitbox extends 2 units wide. Laser Beam (Phase 3): Telegraph: chest crystal glows white (1s). Dodge: perpendicular. Do not dodge backward — the beam sweeps. Safe zone: close to the Sentry's feet (beam cannot hit directly below).

Crystal Monarch

Ice Shard Rain: Telegraph: raises both arms, ice gathers above (0.8s). Sound: shattering glass. Dodge: keep moving in a tight circle — shards target your position with a 0.5s delay. Frost Breath (Cone): Telegraph: leans forward, mouth opens (1s). Dodge: run behind the Monarch. The cone is 120 degrees frontal only. Ground Spikes: Telegraph: ground glows blue in a line (0.6s). Dodge: step sideways off the glow. Spikes always target your current position — never stand still for more than 2 seconds.

Phantom Sylus

Dark Slash: Telegraph: raises arm high (0.8s). Parry window: generous (0.3s when the arm starts descending). This is the easiest parry in the game — practice it first. Execute (Below 50% HP): Telegraph: eyes flash red (0.2s). Cannot be parried — must perfect dodge. The red flash is the only tell. If you see red, dodge immediately. Shadow Pool: Telegraph: Sylus crouches, shadow spreads (0.5s). Dodge: run out of the 4-unit circle. Do not dodge-roll — just sprint.

Phantom Rafayel

Flame Wave: Telegraph: sweeps arm horizontally (0.6s). Parry window: when the fire wall is 1 unit away — counts as a projectile parry. Explosive Teleport: Telegraph: laughter sound cue (0.3s) + vanishing animation (0.4s). Perfect dodge: immediately after the laughter, dodge in any direction. The explosion is centered on his landing point, not your position. Burning Ground: Telegraph: stomps foot, fire spreads (0.5s). Dodge: move to unburned ground. Never stand in burning ground — the DoT stacks if you stand in overlapping circles.

Pro Tip: If you are struggling with a specific dodge, record your gameplay and watch it in slow motion. Count the frames between the telegraph and the damage. Write down the count. Next attempt, count in your head — dodge when you hit the frame count. Muscle memory takes ~50 repetitions to form. Most players give up after 5-10 attempts. Persist through 50 and the dodge becomes automatic.

Quick Reference — Dodge Timings

1.5s+ (Easy): Titan Sentry Club Slam, Crystal Monarch Frost Breath. You have plenty of time — do not dodge early. 0.8-1.2s (Medium): Most boss attacks. Watch the telegraph animation and count. 0.4-0.7s (Hard): Phantom Xavier Teleport Strike, Phantom Rafayel Explosive Teleport. Use audio cues. 0.2-0.3s (Extreme): Phantom Sylus Execute, Storm Weaver Lightning Bolt. These require reflex, not anticipation. The only defense is to always be moving. Rule: If you are consistently dodging too early, wait one more beat. If you are consistently dodging too late, dodge at the first visual change. Early dodging is more punishable than late dodging.

Summary: Use sound + visual + telegraph to dodge. 1.5s+ = easy, 0.8-1.2s = medium, 0.4-0.7s = hard, 0.2-0.3s = extreme. Titan Sentry: behind or perpendicular. Crystal Monarch: keep moving, behind for breath. Phantom Sylus: parry slash, dodge red flash. Phantom Rafayel: dodge on laughter. Record and review your gameplay. 50 reps per dodge pattern.
