Team DPS Synergy Math

Three strong operatives do not make a strong team. Synergy — how their skills interact and how their rotations align — is the difference between a team that clears floor 12 and a team that wipes at floor 8.

ENTRY_#LAB_062026.07.02TEAM_SYNERGY

Core Concept: Team DPS is not the sum of three individual DPS values. It is the product of three factors: (1) Individual operative DPS. (2) Rotation alignment (how well their skill cycles overlap). (3) Synergy multiplier (how much one operative's skills amplify another's). A well-synergized team can deal 2x the damage of a poorly-synergized team with the same total power.

Synergy Multiplier Framework

Type A — Buff Stacking: One operative buffs another's damage. Example: Zayne's shield gives ATK% buff → Switched operative deals 20% more damage for 8 seconds. Value: 1.2x multiplier on the switched operative's damage during that window. Type B — Rotation Alignment: Two operatives whose skill cooldowns end at the same time, enabling simultaneous burst windows. Example: Xavier's 12s cooldown + Sylus's 12s cooldown = both burst at the same time. Value: Tight rotations compress damage into shorter windows, reducing exposure to boss mechanics. Type C — Element Chain: One operative's element application enables another's passive. Example: Rafayel's burn (Fire DoT) enables Sylus's Dark execution bonus against burning targets (if such a passive exists). Value: Variable — check specific passive interactions. Type D — Energy Battery: One operative generates energy faster, enabling the team to use Ardent Oath more frequently. Value: Each extra Ardent Oath per fight = one extra burst rotation = ~30% damage increase for that fight segment.

Team Archetype Analysis

Hypercarry (Xavier + Zayne + Flex): Xavier deals 70%+ of total team damage. Zayne provides shield + ATK buff. Flex (Sylus or Rafayel) provides utility. This team has the highest theoretical single-target DPS because all buffs stack on one operative. Synergy math: Zayne's ATK buff (1.2x) × Xavier's 70% damage share = 14% total team damage increase. Flex's 15% damage share + 14% from buff = 29% total team damage. This beats a "balanced" team by ~5-8% in single-target fights.

Balanced Dual DPS (Xavier + Sylus + Zayne): Two DPS operatives each deal ~40% of damage. Zayne provides sustain. This team has the highest consistent DPS because two DPS operatives can rotate their burst windows — when Xavier's skills are on cooldown, Sylus is active, and vice versa. Synergy math: No single operative is the bottleneck. DPS uptime is higher because you always have an active skill rotation. This team is 10-15% better in multi-target or prolonged fights where hypercarry cooldown windows would be wasted.

Full AoE (Rafayel + Xavier + Sylus): Three DPS operatives, no sustain. This team only works in content where you can kill enemies before they damage you (Abyss floors 1-6, resource domains). Synergy math: Three individual DPS values add directly but there is no buff multiplier. Total team DPS = sum of three individual DPS. Without sustain, this team has zero error margin. One mistake = death. This team is 20% stronger on paper than hypercarry but 50% less consistent in practice.

Rotation Alignment Principle: The most important synergy factor is cooldown alignment. If your main DPS has a 12s cooldown on their best skill, and your support's buff lasts 8s with a 15s cooldown, you have a 3s gap where the support's buff expires before the DPS's skill is ready. This gap reduces team efficiency by ~15%. Solution: Adjust protocores to add Energy Recharge on the support until their cooldown cycle matches the DPS's cooldown cycle perfectly. The goal is to have all three operatives' burst windows overlap within the same 3-second window every 12-15 seconds.

Team DPS Formula

Team DPS = (DPS × Rotations per fight × Synergy Multiplier) / Fight duration. Synergy Multiplier = 1 + (Buff Value × Uptime) + (Rotation Alignment bonus) + (Element Chain bonus). For a hypercarry team with perfect alignment: Synergy Multiplier = 1 + (0.2 × 0.66) + (0.1) + (0) = 1.23. For an unoptimized team with zero synergy: Synergy Multiplier = 1.0. The difference between a 1.23 and a 1.0 multiplier is 23% total team damage — more than any individual protocore or memory upgrade can provide.

Summary: Team DPS = individual DPS × synergy multiplier. Hypercarry = best single-target (buff stacking). Balanced Dual DPS = best sustained (rotation overlap). Full AoE = fastest clear (zero margin). Cooldown alignment is the single most important synergy factor. Aim for all three burst windows to overlap every 12-15s. The 23% synergy gap between optimized and unoptimized teams is bigger than any gear upgrade.
