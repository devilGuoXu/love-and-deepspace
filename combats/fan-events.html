Fan Event Calendar

A calendar of community-run fan events for Love and Deepspace. Find art jams, writing exchanges, co-op sessions, and celebration events.

About Fan Events: Fan events are organized by community members for the community. They are separate from official Papergames events. Fan events focus on creative expression, community bonding, and celebrating shared love for the game.

Regular Recurring Events

Monthly Subreddit Art Contest (r/LoveAndDeepspace): First week of every month. Theme announced on the 1st. Submissions due by the 21st. Winners announced on the last day. Prizes: Reddit awards, featured banner spot, community recognition. Participation is open to all skill levels. Weekly Co-op Sessions (Discord): Every Saturday, 8 PM EST. Community members form co-op groups for boss runs. Voice chat available. All levels welcome. Organized by server moderators. Bi-Weekly Fan Fiction Exchange (AO3 Collection): Every two weeks, a new prompt is posted. Writers create short stories (500-2,000 words) based on the prompt. Stories are collected in an AO3 collection. Monthly Screenshot Contest (Twitter): Hosted by fan account @DeepspaceScreens. Theme changes monthly. Best in-game screenshot wins retweet and feature. Operative Birthday Celebrations: Xavier (Dec 25), Zayne (Feb 14), Rafayel (Oct 3), Sylus (Apr 17). Each birthday features: fan art collections, birthday message threads, community collages, and special Discord events. These are the largest fan-run events of the year.

Upcoming Special Events

Winners featured on community showcase. August 2026 — 1.5 Year Anniversary Fan Celebration: Multi-event celebration. Winter Festival is the largest fan event of the year, often with hundreds of participants.

How to Participate

Joining fan events is simple: (1) Follow the organizing account (subreddit, Twitter fan account, Discord server). (2) Check the event announcement for rules, theme, and deadline. (3) Create your entry. (4) Submit before the deadline using the required format (hashtag, submission form, Discord channel). (5) Engage with other entries — comment, like, and share. (6) Attend the winner announcement or reveal event. Participation Tips: Read all rules carefully. Submission format requirements (resolution, word count, file type) are strict. Late entries are usually not accepted. If you cannot create, participate by voting or commenting. Community judges often consider audience engagement when selecting winners. Do not be discouraged if you do not win. Fan events are about community participation, not competition. Every entry adds to the celebration.

Organizing Your Own Event: If you want to host a fan event: (1) Choose a clear theme and format. (2) Set realistic deadlines (2-4 weeks for art, 1-2 weeks for writing). (3) Create a submission system (Google Forms, Discord channel, hashtag). (4) Advertise on Reddit, Twitter, and Discord. (5) Recruit judges from the community. (6) Arrange prizes (community recognition, shoutouts, artwork from other participants). (7) Communicate clearly with participants. (8) Follow through on all promises. Fan events run on trust. Successful events build community reputation. Start small — a screenshot contest or writing prompt is easier to organize than a multi-week art jam. The Deepspace community is supportive of fan organizers. Do not be afraid to ask for help.

Community Discord Servers

Fan events are organized across several Discord servers. The largest event-hosting servers include the official Love and Deepspace Discord, Deepspace Fan Creators Hub (dedicated fan server for artists and writers), and The Destiny Cafe (community server focused on socializing). Each server has its own events channel. Join the servers that match your interests. The Fan Creators Hub is best for art and writing events. The official Discord is best for large-scale celebrations. Smaller servers often have more intimate, personalized events. Being active in a server increases your chances of hearing about events early — many events fill up quickly or have limited participation slots.
