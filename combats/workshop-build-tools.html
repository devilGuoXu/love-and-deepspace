Build Templates & Tools

Standardized templates for sharing your builds, recommended theorycrafting tools, and how to get feedback on your setups from the community.

ENTRY_#WRK_022026.07.02TOOLS

Why Templates Matter: When you ask "is my Xavier build good?" without sharing your stats, nobody can help you. A standardized build template makes it easy for others to evaluate your build and give specific advice. Use the template below whenever you ask for build feedback on community platforms.

Build Share Template

Operative: [Name] — [Level/Rank]. Memory Set: [4pc Name / 2+2 Sets]. Memory Levels: Slot 1: 60 — Slot 2: 50 — Slot 3: 50 — Slot 4: 50 — Slot 5: 50 — Slot 6: 40. Protocore Main Stats: Slot 1: ATK% +12 — Slot 2: ER% +12 — Slot 3: ATK% +15 — Slot 4: CRIT Rate +15 — Slot 5: CRIT DMG +12 — Slot 6: ATK% +9. Final Stats: ATK: [number] | CRIT Rate: [%] | CRIT DMG: [%] | ER: [%]. Content Goal: [Abyss Floor X / Co-op / Domain]. Issue: [What you feel is wrong]. Example: Xavier Lv60/R1 — 4pc Light Seeker — ATK 2,410 | CRIT 58% | CRIT DMG 132% | ER 118% — Struggling on Abyss Floor 10 boss phase. This template gives the community everything they need to diagnose your build in 10 seconds.

Recommended Theorycrafting Tools

Spreadsheet (Personal): Keep a personal build tracker. Columns: Operative, Memory Set, Memory Levels, Protocore Main Stats, Final Stats (ATK/CRIT/DMG/ER), Content Cleared. Update this sheet whenever you change a protocore or level a memory. A personal tracker reveals your weak spots over time. Google Sheets Community Build Database: Several community-maintained sheets track optimal builds for each operative. These are updated every patch. Search for "Deepspace [Operative Name] Build Sheet" to find the latest version. Screenshot Journal: Take screenshots of your protocore substats before enhancing. If a protocore rolls poorly, you can review the before/after and learn which protocores to skip enhancing in the future. This is the best way to improve your protocore evaluation skills.

How to Get Build Feedback

Step 1 — Use the template above: Post your build in the format shown. Include your content goal. Step 2 — Share specific screenshots: Screenshot your stat screen (press C in combat) and your protocore enhancement screens (all 6 slots). Do not ask "is this good?" without screenshots. Step 3 — State your budget: Are you F2P, dolphin, or whale? A build that is good for F2P may be bad for a whale (who has access to better protocores). Step 4 — Listen to consensus: If 3 people tell you your CRIT rate is too low, it is too low. Do not argue with feedback you asked for. Step 5 — Iterate: Post your updated build after making changes. Community members who helped you will appreciate seeing the improvement and will be more likely to help you again in the future.

Pro Tip: When sharing screenshots on Discord/Reddit, use the "hide UID" option in settings. Your account UID is visible in screenshots and can be used to look up your account. Privacy first — hide your UID, hide your friend list, hide your purchase history. Share only what is necessary for build evaluation.

Build Feedback Checklist

Before asking for feedback, check these common issues yourself: (1) Is your main stat correct for your operative? (Xavier Slot 4 = CRIT Rate, not HP%). (2) Are your memory set bonuses active? (3 are required for 4pc, 2 for 2pc). (3) Are all 6 memories at level 40+? (4) Is your CRIT Rate above 50% for DPS? (5) Is your ER above 120% for DPS / 150% for support? If you identify the issue yourself, you do not need to ask — and you learn faster.

Summary: Use the build template when asking for feedback — include operative, set, levels, main stats, final stats, and goal. Keep a personal spreadsheet. Screenshot protocores before enhancing. Hide your UID in screenshots. Check the 5 common issues before asking. Community feedback is a gift — respond to it with gratitude, not defensiveness.
