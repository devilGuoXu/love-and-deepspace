Stamina Efficiency by Content

Not all stamina is created equal. Spending 45 stamina on a protocore domain gives different long-term value than 45 stamina on a gold domain. Here is exactly how much each activity is worth.

Core Metric

Core Metric: Stamina efficiency = (reward value in diamonds) / (stamina cost). Every activity in Deepspace can be converted to a diamond-equivalent value. This lets you compare farming protocore dust vs farming event currency vs farming gold — and choose the most efficient option every time.

By converting everything to diamonds, we remove the confusion of comparing different resource types. A protocore dust run gives "dust" and a gold domain gives "gold" — which is better? Convert both to diamonds and the answer is clear. This system works because diamonds are the universal currency: they can buy stamina, pulls, and shop items.

The 60 Stamina Rule

The 60 Stamina Rule: Never let your stamina sit at 60/120 or above. Stamina regeneration stops when you are capped. 60 wasted stamina = 20 diamond value lost. If you cannot play, log in for 2 minutes, spend your stamina on ANYTHING (even the worst domain), and log out. Letting stamina cap is the single biggest resource loss in the game — worse than any bad purchase decision.

Let us quantify the loss: If you cap stamina once per day (wasting 60 stamina), that is 20 diamonds lost per day. Over a month = 600 diamonds = 4 limited pulls. Over a year = 7,300 diamonds = 48 pulls. That is nearly half a pity timer — lost because you did not log in for 2 minutes to spend stamina. Set a phone notification if you need to. The 60 stamina rule is non-negotiable for efficient play.

Gold Farming Alternatives

Never farm the gold domain directly. Instead, use the "sell protocores" method:

Farm the protocore dust domain (efficiency: 0.44). Sell all blue and purple protocores with bad substats. Blue protocore = ~500 gold. Purple = ~1,000 gold. After a week of dust farming, you will have accumulated 20–30 sellable protocores = 15k–30k gold. This is equivalent to 3–6 gold domain runs but costs you 0 extra stamina — you were going to farm dust anyway.

Bonus tip: During double drop weekends for protocore dust, the sell value increases proportionally because you get more protocores per run. A double drop weekend effectively gives you double gold from selling, making it 4x more efficient than the gold domain.
