Community Art Spotlight

Showcasing the incredible fan art created by the Love and Deepspace community. Learn where to find art, how to share your own, and how to participate in art events.

About Fan Art: The Love and Deepspace fan art community is one of the most active in mobile gaming. Thousands of artists create illustrations, comics, chibi art, and animations featuring Xavier, Zayne, Rafayel, Sylus, and original characters.

Where to Find Fan Art

Pixiv (Best Quality): The primary platform for high-resolution Deepspace art. Search for "ラブアンドディープスペース" or character names in Japanese. Pixiv has the highest concentration of professional-grade artwork. Many artists post at 4K resolution. Twitter/X (Most Active): Hashtags: #LoveAndDeepspace, #LoveAndDeepspaceFanArt, #DeepspaceArt, #Xavier, #Zayne, #Rafayel, #Sylus. Twitter has the most daily posts. Follow artists you like and turn on notifications. Reddit — r/LoveAndDeepspace: Weekly fan art threads. Monthly contest winners are pinned. Sort by "Top — All Time" for the best collection. Instagram: Hashtag: #loveanddeepspaceart. More casual art and edits. Good for story-style posts and carousel illustrations. DeviantArt: Smaller but active community. Good for finding high-resolution downloads. Lofter: Chinese platform. The origin of much of the fan art that gets reposted. Harder to navigate without Chinese language skills but has exclusive content. Pinterest: Aggregated collections. Good for discovery but always check original sources before reposting.

How to Support Artists

The best way to support fan artists: (1) Like, comment, and share their posts. Engagement helps their content reach more people. (2) Follow their social media accounts — do not just save and leave. (3) Credit artists when sharing their work elsewhere. Include the artist's handle in your caption. (4) Do not remove watermarks. Watermarks protect artists from art theft. (5) Do not repost art claiming it as your own. This is art theft and is taken seriously by the community. (6) Commission artists if you want custom artwork. Many artists have open commissions. Prices range from $20 for simple sketches to $200+ for full illustrations. (7) Report art theft when you see it. If someone reposts art without credit or claims it as their own, report the post and notify the original artist if possible. (8) Consider supporting artists on Patreon, Ko-fi, or Fantia if they offer membership tiers. Many artists post exclusive content and high-resolution downloads for supporters.

Art Contests & Events

Official and community art contests run regularly: Official Papergames Contests: Announced on Twitter and Discord. Prizes: in-game currency, exclusive in-game items, merchandise. Frequency: every 2-3 months. Past themes: "My Favorite Operative," "Summer Memories," "Anniversary Tribute." Subreddit Monthly Contests: r/LoveAndDeepspace hosts monthly fan art contests. Winners are featured in the subreddit banner and receive Reddit awards. Theme changes monthly. Discord Server Events: The official Discord has art showcases and themed events. Participation rewards often include Discord roles and occasionally in-game prizes. Community Challenges: Fan-run hashtag challenges on Twitter and Instagram. Example: #DeepspaceOCWeek (original character week), #SylusWeek, #RafayelMonth. These are organized by fans, not officially. Participation is free and encouraged. Tips for entering contests: Read the rules carefully — some have resolution requirements, watermarks must be included/excluded, and usage rights may be claimed by the host for promotional purposes. Submit early — late entries are often disqualified. Follow the theme — off-theme entries are judged less favorably even if the art quality is high.

Getting Started as an Artist: If you want to create and share Deepspace fan art: (1) Start with what you enjoy. Draw your favorite operative in a pose or setting you love. (2) Use references. Screenshot in-game scenes for poses, lighting, and color schemes. The in-game photo mode is an excellent reference tool. (3) Study the art style. Deepspace uses cel-shading with soft lighting. Practice replicating the official style or develop your own interpretation. (4) Share your work. Post on Twitter with relevant hashtags. The community is supportive of artists at all skill levels. (5) Accept criticism gracefully. The Deepspace art community gives constructive feedback. Use it to improve. (6) Do not compare yourself to others. Focus on your own progress. The art community grows together. (7) Be patient. Building a following takes time. Consistent posting and engagement matter more than individual viral posts. (8) Protect your art. Add watermarks. Register your work if possible. The internet is large — art theft happens. Prepare for it proactively.

Art Style Guide

Fan art in the Deepspace community falls into several style categories: Official-style replicas: Artists who closely mimic the game's cel-shaded semi-realistic style. These are the most technically impressive. Chibi/cute style: Simplified, adorable versions of characters. Very popular for stickers and emoji. Semi-realistic: More painterly style with softer edges. Common on Pixiv. Anime style: Standard anime aesthetics applied to Deepspace characters. Comic/cartoon: Sequential art telling short stories or comedic scenarios. Popular on Twitter. Traditional media: Physical paintings, sketches, and digital art that emulates traditional tools. Each style has its own community and audience. Experiment with different styles to find what suits you best as an artist.

Summary: Deepspace fan art is found on Pixiv, Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, DeviantArt, Lofter, and Pinterest. Support artists by liking, sharing with credit, and commissioning them. Art contests run monthly on Reddit and every 2-3 months officially. To start creating: use references, study the art style, share with hashtags, accept feedback, and protect your work with watermarks. The Deepspace art community is welcoming and talented. Whether you are a viewer or a creator, there is a place for you.
