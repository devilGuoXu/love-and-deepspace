Missable Content Overview

Love and Deepspace is designed around the live-service model, which means not all content is permanently available. The companion system — encompassing Tender Moments, Secret Times, phone calls, text messages, base interactions, and gift items — has multiple layers of missable content that can trap unsuspecting players. Unlike combat content which is always accessible, companion content often comes with time gates, event windows, or one-time unlock conditions that, once passed, cannot be revisited.

This guide catalogs every type of missable companion content in the game, organized by operative and by content category. We cover what you can still obtain, what is permanently gone, and how to plan your gameplay to minimize losses. Whether you are a casual lore enjoyer or a dedicated collector aiming for 100% completion, understanding the missable landscape is the first step toward a stress-free experience.

The core principle is simple: if it came from a limited-time event banner or event shop, assume it is gone forever when the event ends. If it is tied to Affinity or story progression, it is usually permanent. However, there are edge cases — one-time story choices and unique furniture-triggered dialogue — that require special attention even for veteran players.

Missable Content Types

We have identified five distinct categories of missable companion content. Each category has different rules, availability windows, and recovery options.

LIMITED Event Companion Content
Event-exclusive Tender Moments, Secret Times episodes, phone calls, and text messages that are tied to specific limited-time banners. When the banner event ends, these become permanently inaccessible for new players. Examples include the "Night of Seagull" Rafayel Tender Moment from his launch banner and the "Snowfall Promise" Zayne Secret Times from the winter event. These are the most painful misses for completionists because they represent unique story content that cannot be re-experienced. No reruns or second chances have been confirmed for any of these.
WINDOW Affinity-Gated Dialogue Changes
Certain story nodes and base interactions have dialogue that changes based on your current Affinity level with an operative. If you replay a story node at a higher Affinity, you will see different dialogue than what you saw the first time. However, if you never trigger the first version, both versions remain accessible. The missable aspect is subtle: if you rush through story content at low Affinity, you will not see the higher-Affinity variant until you replay — but the lower-Affinity variant is lost once your Affinity increases past the threshold. This is more of a "completion window" than a hard miss, but for dialogue collectors it matters.
PERMANENT One-Time Story Choices
During main quest chapters and certain companion story events, you are presented with dialogue choices. These choices affect which lines the operative says in response and can also affect which voice lines are unlocked in the companion mode's voice line collection. Once you make a choice, the unchosen path's dialogue is locked out for that playthrough. Because there is no chapter select or NG+ feature, these choices are effectively permanent — you see one branch per playthrough. To see all branches, you would need to replay on a different account.
PERMANENT Missable Base Interactions
The Dawnbreak Base has furniture combos that trigger unique dialogue when certain items are placed together for the first time. If you place a full furniture set all at once, you may only trigger the final set bonus dialogue, missing the individual pair-triggered interactions. Worse, if you rearrange furniture without ever having triggered a specific combo, the chance to trigger that interaction may be lost. These are permanent in the sense that the triggers are one-time — once fired, they never fire again. Plan your furniture placement carefully and trigger combos one at a time.
LIMITED Time-Limited Gift Shop Items
Event-exclusive gift types sold in limited-time shops. These gifts often give bonus Affinity EXP when given to a specific operative during the event period. After the event ends, these gift types are removed from the shop and cannot be obtained elsewhere. While they do not permanently affect your completion percentage, they represent a loss of efficient Affinity EXP that can never be recovered.

Tracking Tools

Keeping track of what you have completed and what you still need is critical for anyone aiming beyond the Casual tier. The game provides several in-tracking tools, but external tracking is recommended for the dedicated completionist.

In-Game Tracking
The Affinity progress bar shows your current level and EXP toward the next milestone for each operative. The Tender Moments completion tab (accessible from the main menu) shows which Tender Moments you have unlocked and which remain locked, though it does not indicate whether locked ones are permanently missed or simply require higher Affinity. The Secret Times library offers similar functionality. The voice line collection percentage on each operative's profile page gives a rough idea of dialogue completion, but it does not distinguish between permanently missable lines and lines you can still unlock.
External Tracking (Recommended)
We strongly recommend maintaining a personal spreadsheet to track limited content completion per operative. Create columns for: Tender Moments (permanent vs. limited), Secret Times, Phone Calls, Text Messages, Base Interactions, and Voice Lines. Mark each item as "unlocked," "still available," "missed permanently," or "waiting for event." Update this spreadsheet at the end of every event banner. This is the only reliable way to know your true completion percentage, as the in-game tools do not differentiate between content you can still obtain and content that is gone forever.

Recommendation: Set a recurring calendar reminder for the last 48 hours of every event. This is your final window to check for any missed companion content tied to that event. Many players discover missed interactions only after the event shop has closed.

Planning for Future Content

While you cannot recover past missable content, you can prepare for future companion updates to ensure you never miss another window. Strategic resource management is the key.

1
Save Gift Boxes for New OperativesWhen a new operative is announced, start stockpiling gift boxes 2-3 weeks before their release. The daily gift cap of 5 per operative means you cannot rush Affinity on launch day without reserves. Having 100+ gift boxes ready lets you front-load Affinity to unlock their Tender Moments and Secret Times immediately.
2
Reserve Stamina for Double-Affinity EventsOccasional events double the Affinity EXP earned from combat for specific operatives. Stockpile your reserve stamina items (energy cans) at least one week before expected double-affinity events. A full 480 stamina spent during a double-affinity window equals 960 Affinity EXP instead of 480 — essentially a free day of progress.
3
Budget Gems for Limited Companion MemoriesLimited-time companion memories (the pink gem banners) are the primary source of event-exclusive Tender Moments and Secret Times. Maintain a gem reserve of at least 7,000 to 10,000 gems for unexpected limited banners. This gives you enough pulls to hit soft pity and secure the memory before the event ends.
4
Maintain a Completion CalendarTrack upcoming events on a calendar. Mark event start and end dates, expected banner types (new operative vs. rerun), and double-affinity windows. Review this calendar weekly and set reminders 3 days before each event end to tie up loose ends.

Urgency Guide

Understanding which content demands immediate action and which can wait is the cornerstone of effective completion planning. We categorize all companion content into three urgency levels to help you prioritize your limited playtime.

LIMITED Do now or miss forever. Event-exclusive content, limited banner memories, time-gated interactions. This content has a hard deadline and will never return.
WINDOW Available for a limited time but may return. Event shop items, double-affinity events, seasonal interactions. Prioritize during the window but do not panic — some may rerun.
PERMANENT Always accessible. Base Tender Moments, main story dialogue, Affinity rewards, permanent phone calls and texts. No deadline pressure — progress at your own pace.

Our recommended daily and weekly priority checklist: check active events first for any limited companion content you have not yet claimed or triggered. Review the event shop for time-limited gifts. Do your daily Affinity interactions (greetings and gifts) for all operatives — these are permanent but have daily caps, so missing a day is lost progress. Use your stamina with event-relevant operatives to maximize Affinity during bonus windows. At the end of each event, update your external tracker and note any content that became permanently unavailable.

Final Word: Completion anxiety is real in live-service games, but Love and Deepspace is generous with permanent content compared to many gacha titles. The vast majority of companion interactions — all base Tender Moments, Secret Times, phone calls, and messages — are permanently available as long as you meet the Affinity requirements. The truly missable content is limited to event-exclusive memories and their associated interactions. Focus on building consistent daily habits, maintain an external tracker, and do not let FOMO dictate your spending. A Casual or Dedicated completion tier is achievable for free-to-play players; the Collector tier is a marathon that requires significant investment and discipline.
