Social Media Rewards Guide

Love and Deepspace rewards players for engaging with official social media channels. This guide covers every platform and how to maximize your free rewards.

ENTRY_#CDE_042026.07.02SOCIAL

Why Social Media Rewards Matter: Social media campaigns are one of the easiest free reward sources. They require no stamina, no combat, and no spending. Just a few minutes of engagement per week can net 100-300 diamonds per campaign.

Platform Overview

Twitter/X (@LoveDeepspace_EN): The primary reward distribution platform. Follow for: Livestream code announcements, milestone celebration codes (follower count milestones), fan art contest announcements, maintenance schedule updates. Typical rewards: 100-300 diamonds per campaign. Frequency: 2-4 campaigns per month. Discord (Official Server): The most active community hub. Join for: #announcements channel posts all codes immediately, exclusive Discord-only events and giveaways, developer Q&A sessions with reward drops, community contests with in-game prizes. Typical rewards: 50-200 diamonds per event. YouTube (Official Channel): Subscribe for: New character trailer premieres with time-limited codes in the description, livestream VODs with pinned code links, patch preview videos with embedded codes. Typical rewards: 100-150 diamonds per video campaign. Reddit (r/LoveAndDeepspace): Official subreddit. Follow for: Community managers post codes, fan events with reward participation, AMA sessions with the development team. TikTok (@LoveDeepspace): Short-form content platform. Rewards: Limited campaigns during major events. Challenges with in-game currency prizes. Instagram (@LoveDeepspace): Visual content platform. Rewards: Story-exclusive codes during events. Milestone celebration codes.

Campaign Types

Follow Milestones: When official accounts hit follower targets (500K, 1M, 2M), they release global reward codes. These are the highest-value social rewards. The 2M follower campaign gave 200 diamonds + 5 limited pulls. Share Campaigns: Share a specific post to unlock community-wide rewards. When total shares hit a target, all players receive rewards. Fan Art Contests: Submit fan art for a chance to win in-game currency and exclusive cosmetics. Even participation rewards (non-winning entries) often include 50-100 diamonds. Retweet/Like Campaigns: Like and retweet a specific post. When targets are met, codes are released. Hashtag Campaigns: Post with a specific hashtag during events. Milestone-based rewards. Livestream Drops: Watch official livestreams for a minimum duration (usually 15 minutes) to receive a code. Survey/Feedback Rewards: Complete official surveys posted on social media. Rewards: 100 diamonds per survey.

Maximizing Social Rewards

Setup: Create accounts on all platforms (Twitter, Discord, YouTube, Reddit). Enable notifications for the official accounts. Join the official Discord server and assign yourself the @announcements role. Follow the subreddit and enable push notifications. Subscribe to the YouTube channel with the bell icon. Weekly Routine: Monday: Check Twitter for weekly campaign announcements. Wednesday: Check Discord #announcements for mid-week updates. Friday: Check Reddit for weekend event announcements. Saturday: Watch any new YouTube videos for description codes. Sunday: Claim all accumulated codes. Monthly Routine: Check for follower milestone campaigns (usually announced when 50K-100K followers from the next milestone). Participate in at least one fan event per month. Complete all official surveys. Campaign Participation Tips: Like and retweet campaign posts within the first hour — early engagement helps campaigns reach targets faster. When share/like targets are announced, complete your share immediately and encourage friends to do the same. For fan art contests, even simple entries qualify for participation rewards. For livestream drops, open the stream on a second device and mute it — you do not need to actively watch.

Community Multiplier Effect: Social media rewards often have community-wide unlock conditions. When everyone participates, everyone benefits. Share campaign announcements in your crew/guild chat. Post reminders on community forums. The faster a campaign reaches its target, the sooner the rewards are distributed. Some campaigns have multiple reward tiers — higher engagement unlocks better rewards. A campaign with 10,000 shares might give 100 diamonds, but 50,000 shares could give 300 diamonds. Community coordination turns good rewards into great rewards. If you see a campaign stalling near a target, share it again to push it over the line.

Reward Tracking

Keep track of claimed and pending rewards: (1) Screenshot each code as soon as it is posted. (2) Maintain a note in your phone with unredeemed codes. (3) Check your in-game mailbox daily for social reward deliveries. (4) Cross-reference this guide's active codes page for the latest campaigns. Social rewards are often time-sensitive. A code from a livestream might expire in 24 hours. A share campaign reward might need to be claimed within 48 hours of the target being met. Delaying can mean missing out entirely. Set reminders for yourself when a major campaign is active.

Summary: Follow official accounts on Twitter (@LoveDeepspace_EN), Discord, YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, and Instagram. Typical rewards: 100-300 diamonds per campaign. Campaign types: follower milestones, share campaigns, fan art contests, like/retweet campaigns, hashtag campaigns, livestream drops, surveys. Weekly routine: check all platforms 3x per week. Monthly: participate in at least one fan event. Social rewards are free, fast, and add up to 500-1,000 diamonds per month. Set up notifications and check this site for active campaigns.
