THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Stormcaller

Speed incarnate. The Stormcaller teleports, strikes from nowhere, and electrifies the entire arena.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Recommended Power: 80k+ | Element: Lightning | Weakness: Ice — Zayne's freeze is the only reliable CC against this boss.

Phase 1: Lightning Rod (100%-70%)

Teleport Strike: Stormcaller vanishes and reappears behind you. 0.3s dodge window. Turn and dodge immediately. Chain Lightning: Hits one person then jumps to others. Spread out 10+ meters apart. Static Field: Ground zones that deal damage and slow. Avoid at all costs.

Phase 2: Eye of the Storm (70%-35%)

Tempest: Stormcaller creates a tornado that pulls you in. Run away from the center. Thunderclap: Massive AoE after a lightning flash. Dodge when you see the flash, not when you hear the sound. Overcharge: Stormcaller becomes surrounded by lightning orbs. Close-range damage. Keep distance.

Phase 3: Cataclysm (35%-0%)

Storm's End: Arena-wide lightning storm. Safe zones appear randomly. Move from zone to zone. Final Bolt: Single target nuke on the aggro holder. Use Zayne's shield or perfect dodge. Calm Before: Brief pause then massive explosion. Use this pause to heal and reposition.

Recommended Team: Zayne (Main/Survivor) — Frost element + shields. Sylus (Sub-DPS) — execute punishes teleport patterns. Xavier (Flex) — consistent damage during safe windows.

Positioning and Team Coordination

The Stormcaller's teleport mechanic makes positioning a team-wide concern. Designate one player as the "bait" before the fight begins. This player stays at maximum range from the Stormcaller at all times, ensuring the teleport targets them predictably. The other two operatives position themselves mid-range to punish the Stormcaller immediately after it teleports. Rotate the bait role if the designated player's HP drops below 50% — the teleport follow-up attack deals significant damage and can kill a wounded operative in one combo.

Chain Lightning requires the team to spread at least 10 meters apart. Pre-assign positions around the arena in a triangle formation before the fight starts. Each player claims one vertex of the triangle and returns to their position immediately after damage phases. If a player is caught too close to another when Chain Lightning activates, the lightning jumps deal increasing damage with each jump. The first jump deals 100% damage, the second 150%, the third 200%, which can one-shot a squishy operative.

Zayne Freeze Timing Guide

Zayne's freeze ability is the most reliable tool for controlling the Stormcaller's mobility. The freeze window is 2 seconds long with a 12-second cooldown. Use freeze specifically to interrupt the Tempest tornado summon in Phase 2. The Tempest has a 1-second cast animation visible as a swirling wind effect around the Stormcaller's hands. Freezing during this animation cancels the Tempest entirely and puts it on a 10-second cooldown instead of letting it activate.

Do not use freeze on cooldown. Save it for the Overcharge phase when the Stormcaller becomes surrounded by lightning orbs. Freezing the Stormcaller during Overcharge causes the lightning orbs to dissipate, creating a 3-second safe window for your team to deal damage at close range. This is the highest damage window in the entire fight. Coordinate with your team to use resonance skills during this frozen Overcharge window for maximum burst output.

The Stormcaller's chain lightning damage increases by 50% with each jump. The first target takes 100% damage, the second takes 150%, and the third takes 200%. If your team of three is clustered together, the third target takes double damage, which can one-shot a squishy operative. The minimum safe distance between operatives to prevent chain lightning jumps is 8 meters. Pre-assign positions in a triangle formation with 10-meter spacing before the fight starts to ensure safe positioning.

The Stormcaller's teleport has a static electricity sound cue 1 second before impact. Learn to dodge using only audio. Once mastered, the teleport never hits you regardless of visual distractions.

Advanced Tip: The Stormcaller's teleport always lands behind the furthest target. Use this to predict and pre-position. If you time a Zayne freeze as the teleport lands, you cancel the follow-up attack.

Summary: Prediction is everything. Learn the teleport pattern — it always targets the furthest player. Spread out for chain lightning. Zayne's ice is your best friend. This fight tests your reaction time more than your DPS.
