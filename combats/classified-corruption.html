What Is Corruption?

Corruption occurs when Deepspace energy infuses a living being beyond its tolerance threshold. Unlike ordinary injuries or energy burns, corruption does not simply damage tissue — it rewrites the fundamental structure of the victim at a cellular and metaphysical level. The process follows a predictable sequence that researchers have documented across hundreds of cases.

Phase 1: ExposureDeepspace energy enters the body through open wounds, respiration, or prolonged proximity to the rift.
Phase 2: TaintEnergy bonds with the host's Evol signature. Behavioral changes begin. The victim's own energy works against them.
Phase 3: MutationPhysical restructuring. Organs shift. Consciousness fragments. The Wanderer's hunger awakens.
Phase 4: ConversionThe host ceases to exist. What remains is a Wanderer — a creature of pure Deepspace energy wearing the victim's form.

Unlike standard damage which can be healed through medical treatment or Evol-based recovery, corruption permanently alters the victim's structure. Tissue regenerated after corruption retains the corrupted pattern. This is why the Hunter Corps classifies corruption as a terminal condition beyond Stage 1. The body remembers the corruption even after the energy is removed.

Critical Note: The tolerance threshold varies dramatically between individuals. Subjects with strong Evol affinity show higher resistance. Sylus, for example, has spent more time in high-corruption zones than any documented Hunter and shows zero signs of corruption. His Dark element Evol appears to grant him a natural immunity — or he has already been corrupted and we simply cannot tell the difference.

Stages of Corruption

Corruption progresses through four distinct stages. Each stage represents a point of no return for specific biological and cognitive functions. Early detection is the only factor that improves outcomes.

Stage 1TaintReversible

Minor behavioral changes — increased aggression, shortened temper, subtle fixation on the Deepspace. Physical marks appear: faint dark veins along the torso and limbs, often mistaken for bruising. The subject retains full consciousness and free will. Most victims do not realize they are corrupted at this stage.

  • Symptoms: mood swings, vivid dreams of the rift, mild photophobia
  • Duration: days to weeks depending on exposure level
  • Detection: requires specialized Deepspace energy scan
Stage 2MutationCritical

Visible physical transformation begins. Fingers elongate. Skin takes on a pale, waxy texture. Dark veins spread across the face. The subject experiences periods of lost time — minutes to hours where they are not in control. The Wanderer's hunger begins: an insatiable craving for Evol energy. Subjects at this stage are dangerous and should be restrained.

  • Symptoms: memory gaps, heightened strength, attraction to Evol sources
  • Duration: weeks to months
  • Detection: visible to naked eye; confirmed via blood analysis
Stage 3ConversionTerminal

The subject becomes a full Wanderer. No trace of the original identity remains — not memories, not personality, not consciousness. What walks in their place is a creature of pure Deepspace energy wearing their form as a vessel. The conversion is accompanied by a violent energy release that often injures anyone nearby. The newly formed Wanderer retains the victim's combat skills and muscle memory but operates on pure predatory instinct.

  • Symptoms: complete identity loss, energy emission, hostile to all life
  • Duration: permanent
  • Detection: confirmed via metaphysical resonance scan
Stage 4DissolutionTerminal

The final stage. The Wanderer breaks down into ambient Deepspace energy. This typically occurs after the Wanderer has been active for an extended period or has suffered sufficient damage. The dissolution releases all accumulated energy back into the environment, sometimes creating corruption hotspots that persist for years. In rare cases, the dissolution is incomplete and the energy coalesces into a new, more dangerous entity.

  • Symptoms: body disintegration, energy surge, residual contamination
  • Duration: hours to days during the dissolution process
  • Detection: energy signature decay pattern

Known Cases

Documented corruption cases span every category of Deepspace exposure. The following represents the Hunter Corps' classified case registry.

Field Researchers
Laboratory personnel exposed during Deepspace energy experiments. The highest survival rate due to immediate containment protocols. Case #431: Dr. Elena Vasquez, exposed during a containment breach at N109 Research Station 7. Detected at Stage 1 within 6 hours. Received purification treatment. Full recovery after 14 days. Case #512: Dr. Marcus Chen, refused evacuation during a Level 3 energy spike. Reached Stage 3 in 72 hours. Terminated by security forces.
Hunters
Combat personnel with prolonged exposure in high-corruption zones. Hunters account for 47% of all documented corruption cases. The average Hunter lasts 8 months of active duty before showing Stage 1 symptoms. Elite Hunters with high Evol resistance may last 2-3 years. The Hunter Corps rotates personnel out of high-corruption zones every 6 months to minimize risk, but combat exigencies often prevent timely rotation.
Corrupted Operatives
The Phantom variants of Xavier, Sylus, Zayne, and Rafayel raise a disturbing question: are these alternate versions of the operatives from a parallel timeline, or are they corruption victims who reached Stage 3 Conversion? Evidence supports both theories. The Phantom operatives retain combat skills identical to their uncorrupted counterparts but display no recognizable personality traits. Their energy signatures match the Wanderer profile exactly. If they are corruption victims, then every known Hunter is at risk of becoming a Phantom variant given sufficient exposure.
Civilians
Zone residents with chronic high exposure. These cases are the most tragic because they are almost never detected early. Civilians in the N109 Zone and Deepspace-adjacent settlements lack access to energy scanning equipment. By the time symptoms become visible, the corruption has usually reached Stage 2. The Hunter Corps conducts mandatory screening of all residents within the 50-kilometer quarantine zone, but compliance is low and enforcement is difficult.

Can Corruption Be Reversed?

The question that drives Deepspace research funding worldwide. The answer depends entirely on the stage at which treatment begins.

Stage 1 — Partially Reversible: Intensive purification treatment can remove Deepspace energy from the host if applied within the first 72 hours of taint. The process requires daily sessions of resonant energy cleansing combined with Evol-suppressing medication. Success rate: approximately 60%. The treatment is brutal — subjects report feeling their own cells being torn apart and reassembled. Some choose to decline treatment after the first session.

Stage 2 — Irreversible (Corps Stance): The Hunter Corps officially considers Stage 2 corruption irreversible. No subject has ever been returned to baseline after visible mutation begins. However, Onychinus may have alternative methods. Intelligence suggests Sylus has access to experimental purification techniques that the Corps does not. Whether these methods actually work or simply prolong the inevitable is unknown. Onychinus has not shared their data.

Stage 3 & 4 — Terminal: No known method can reverse Conversion or Dissolution. Once the original consciousness is gone, there is nothing to save. Some researchers have proposed radical theories involving consciousness transfer or memory reconstruction, but these remain theoretical. The ethical implications alone prevent funding.

The MC Factor: The protagonist's Resonance ability appears to be uniquely effective against Deepspace corruption. Records indicate the MC can purify corruption at rates that exceed standard treatment by orders of magnitude. This is why both the Hunter Corps and Onychinus seek to control the MC — whoever holds the key to purification holds the key to the future of humanity. If the MC's Resonance can be weaponized, Stage 2 corruption may no longer be a death sentence.

Connection to the Wanderer Plague

Corruption is not a side effect of the Wanderer phenomenon. Corruption is the mechanism. Every Wanderer that has ever existed was created through the corruption process. There is no other origin. The Void Spawn, the Elemental variants, the Ancient Wanderers — all began as something else, something that was infused with Deepspace energy beyond its tolerance and converted.

This understanding reframes the entire conflict. The Hunters are not fighting an invading army from another dimension. They are fighting the victims of an environmental catastrophe that is still ongoing. Every Wanderer killed is a being that could not be saved. This is the central tragedy of the Deepspace crisis.

Research Directive: If a reliable cure for Stage 1 corruption existed and could be deployed at scale, the formation of new Wanderers could be prevented entirely. The Wanderer population would decline naturally over time as existing Wanderers dissolve. This is the holy grail of Deepspace research — a cure for corruption would mean the end of the Wanderer plague within a generation.

The Hunter Corps has allocated 60% of its research budget to corruption reversal studies. Onychinus has allocated an unknown but presumably larger percentage through covert channels. The race for a cure is not a race between organizations — it is a race against the expanding Deepspace rift, which continues to increase energy levels worldwide. Every year the cure remains undiscovered, the tolerance threshold of every living human decreases.

Classified Final Assessment: Corruption is the single most important phenomenon in the Deepspace crisis. It is the origin of every enemy, the fate of every fallen Hunter, and the key to humanity's survival. The MC's unique Resonance may be the only hope for a cure — and both the Corps and Onychinus know it. The battle for the future will be fought over the MC's ability to purify what the Deepspace has tainted.
