Challenge Mode & Boss Rush

Beyond Abyss — the hardest content the game offers and how to conquer every format.

ENTRY_#121 2026.06.29 CHALLENGE

One Thing to Know: Challenge modes are not DPS checks — they are TEAM checks. Each format demands different team compositions, element coverage, and playstyles. A team that dominates Abyss may fail in Boss Rush.

Boss Rush Mode

Boss Rush is a limited-time mode where you fight 3-5 bosses in sequence. Score is based on clear time, damage taken, and team vitality remaining.

Scoring System

Base Score: 1,000 points per boss defeated.

Time Bonus: Remaining time × 10 points (up to 500 per boss).

Health Bonus: Remaining HP % × 200 points. Losing an operative = -200 points.

Combo Bonus: Consecutive resonance triggers increase combo multiplier (max 2.0×).

Do NOT use the same team for all bosses. Each boss has different weaknesses. Swap teams between rounds.

Prioritize survival over speed. Losing an operative costs 200 points. A safe 60s clear beats a risky 40s one.

Open with all ultimates. Use ults in the first 3 seconds of each boss for best time bonus.

Challenge Stage Modifiers

Challenge Stages are permanent high-difficulty content with rotating modifiers:

Time Warp

All actions are 50% faster — enemy attacks AND yours. Game speed increases. Use short-animation skills; long casts become risky.

Best Team: Rafayel (fast animations, DoT doesn't need long casts). Avoid Xavier's Stellar Burst (long cast is a liability).

Elemental Surge

One element deals 100% bonus damage. All others deal 50% reduced damage. Forces you to build around the featured element. Rotates weekly.

Best Team: Build one team per element. Surge Light week = Xavier. Surge Frost week = Zayne. Do not brute force off-element.

Glass Cannon

Operatives deal 100% more damage but take 200% more damage. Any hit can be lethal. Rewards perfect play.

Best Team: Zayne is mandatory. Freeze prevents the boss from hitting you. Ranged combat (Xavier projectiles) is safer than melee.

Endless Wave

Enemies spawn in infinite waves. Survive as long as possible. Score increases per wave cleared. Difficulty ramps every 5 waves.

Best Team: Rafayel (AoE burn clears waves). Sylus (execute eliminates high-HP elites fast). Sustain > burst in this mode.

Orbit Mode

Orbit is the permanent high-difficulty tower. Each floor has specific conditions and a boss. Key differences from Abyss:

No Team Lock: You can retry with different teams. This makes Orbit more forgiving than Abyss — experiment freely.

Fixed Order: Bosses in Orbit are always the same per floor. You can pre-plan counters for each floor.

Higher Rewards: Orbit gives exclusive name cards, titles, and memory shards. Worth pushing as high as you can.

Strategy: Check the floor's boss and condition before building your team. Orbit Floor 30+ often has Elemental Surge or Glass Cannon modifiers stacked on top of the boss.

General Challenge Mode Tips

Build for the modifier, not the boss. In Time Warp, animation speed matters more than element advantage. In Glass Cannon, survival matters more than DPS. Read the modifier first.
One team cannot do everything. The best challenge mode players have 4+ teams leveled. Depth is the key to high scores in every format.
Study the leaderboard. Top scorers publish their team comps. Check what they used and copy their element/character choices. The leaderboard is a free strategy guide.
Reset liberally. If a run starts badly (you get hit in the first 5 seconds), restart. Challenge modes have no entry cost. A clean run always scores higher than a messy completion.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Using Abyss teams in challenge modes. Your Abyss Floor 12 team was built for endurance (3 floors). Challenge modes are about specialization (1-2 bosses per run). Swap to focused burst teams.

Mistake #2: Ignoring modifiers. "I'll just out-DPS the modifier" works until Elemental Surge reduces your damage by 50%. Respect the modifiers and build around them.

Mistake #3: Tunnel vision on score. Higher risk does not mean higher score. A clean, slow clear with full HP bonus often out-scores a fast, sloppy clear with dead operatives. Survive first, speed second.

The Bottom Line: Challenge modes test your depth, not your peak. Build 4+ teams. Respect the modifiers. Study the leaderboard. Reset bad runs. And remember: survival scores points. The player who finishes every boss alive will out-score the player who finished one boss 10 seconds faster but lost an operative. Build wide, play safe, and climb.
