Abyss Star Optimization Guide

Clearing a floor is step one. Three-starring it is the real achievement. Every star lost is gems left on the table.

ENTRY_#1342026.07.11STAR_OPTIMIZATION
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Total Star Value: 180 bonus gems per full reset if you 3-star every floor 7-12. That is 720 gems per month from stars alone. Each star you miss costs you 10 gems per reset.

How Star Scoring Works

Each Abyss floor awards up to 3 stars based on three criteria: clear time, damage taken, and team survival. The exact thresholds vary by floor, but the pattern is consistent across all twelve floors. Understanding how each star is earned lets you target your improvements instead of banging your head against the same fight hoping for a better outcome.

First Star: Beat the floor within the base clear time. This is the easiest star and the one most players earn on their first successful clear. The base time is generous enough that even messy runs with mistakes will still secure the first star as long as you finish the fight.

Second Star: Beat a tighter clear time threshold. This is the point where you need solid execution. The time is roughly 75-80% of the base clear time, meaning you need to stay on the offensive and minimize downtime. Players who rely heavily on defensive play or who spend too much time dodging will struggle with the second star.

Third Star: Beat an even tighter time OR take minimal damage. The third star threshold varies by floor. On pure DPS check floors like Floor 11, it is a time-only requirement. On survival floors like Floor 8, it requires staying above a certain HP threshold. On wave-clear floors, it requires clearing both waves fast enough. Always check which specific condition applies to the floor you are attempting.

Per-Floor Star Thresholds

Floors 1-3: These are effectively free stars. Any account above level 20 can 3-star these floors without specific strategy. The time thresholds are extremely generous. If you cannot 3-star Floor 1, your account may have a fundamental issue with how you are building memories or executing basic combat. The time requirement is roughly 5 minutes for a floor that should take 90 seconds at most.

Floors 4-6: Moderate thresholds that require a competent team but not specific optimization. The second star typically requires finishing within 3 minutes. The third star requires finishing within 2 minutes and taking fewer than 5 significant hits. These floors have no enrage mechanic, so you can take as long as you want for the first clear, but stars require speed.

Floors 7-8: The first real star challenge. Floor 7's third star requires clearing the Commander wave within 90 seconds of the Sentinel wave. Floor 8's third star requires finishing with at least 60% of your team's total HP remaining across both operatives. This is why Zayne is recommended for Floor 8 — his shield directly contributes to the survival star. Players who clear Floor 8 without Zayne often miss the third star due to accumulated DoT damage.

Floors 9-10: These floors test your roster depth. The third star on Floor 9 requires both elites to die within 10 seconds of each other, incentivizing balanced damage. The third star on Floor 10 requires breaking both elemental shields within 15 seconds of each phase start. Multi-hit abilities like Xavier's light attack chain excel here because they shred shield gauge regardless of element matching.

Floors 11-12: These are the hardest star thresholds in the game. Floor 11 requires a sub-3-minute clear for the third star, which is the tightest DPS check in the Abyss. Floor 12 requires a sub-3-minute clear AND taking no more than 3 hits from the boss's enrage phase attacks. At this level, the difference between 2 stars and 3 stars is often less than 10 seconds or a single avoidable hit. Small optimizations matter more than raw power.

Optimization Strategies by Floor Category

Wave Floors (1, 2, 7): Group enemies before using AoE. Running a tight circle gathers all wandering enemies into a cluster that a single Resonance can hit. Xavier's skill covers the widest AoE arc for this purpose. Do not use Ardent Oath on the first wave unless doing so would save significant time. Energy carries over between waves, and using Oath on the first wave often leaves you energy-starved for the second wave where a faster clear matters more.

Elite Floors (3, 4, 5, 9): Target the elite's weak point first. Elites have specific stagger thresholds that, when reached, stun them for 3-4 seconds of free damage. Memorize which attacks contribute the most stagger. Sylus's fully charged skill does the highest stagger of any single ability in the game. A staggered elite is a dead elite, and stagger windows are your best opportunity to shave time off your clear.

Survival Floors (6, 8): Prioritize not getting hit over dealing damage. This seems counterintuitive for a star guide, but the survival floors have tight HP thresholds for the third star. Each hit you take costs you not only the HP but also the stagger recovery time. A clean run that takes 30 seconds longer will often earn more stars than a reckless run that is fast but takes damage. Reset runs where you take early damage rather than trying to salvage a losing star condition.

Boss Floors (10, 11, 12): These are purely about optimizing your damage window. Boss phases are time-gated or HP-gated. Memorize each phase transition and pre-position before it triggers. Save all Resonance skills for when the boss is vulnerable or staggered. The first 10 seconds of each phase are the most important because that is when the boss is setting up its attack pattern and dealing the least damage to you. Use this window aggressively.

Damage Benchmark Targets

The following benchmarks apply to your main DPS operative for 3-star clears. These are minimums, not recommendations. Having more than these thresholds will make the runs significantly more comfortable. The ATK values assume level 70 memories with +12 protocores. The CRIT values assume a 2-piece CRIT rate set and CRIT rate main stat on your Solar protocore.

Floor 7: 2,500 ATK / 25% CRIT / 140% CRIT DMG. This threshold is achievable with a single built SSR and +9 protocores. You do not need perfect gear. You just need a functioning main DPS and a support that stays alive.

Floor 8: 3,500 ATK / 35% CRIT / 160% CRIT DMG. The survival star requirement means you want Zayne as support even if he is not built as a DPS. His shield and healing give you the HP buffer needed for the third star.

Floors 9-10: 5,000 ATK / 45% CRIT / 180% CRIT DMG. These floors require focused investment. Your main DPS should have +12 protocores with at least 2 usable substats each. Your second team does not need to match the first team's power, but they should be able to survive their assigned phase without dying.

Floors 11-12: 7,000+ ATK / 60% CRIT / 200%+ CRIT DMG. These are endgame thresholds. Your main DPS needs optimized gear. Your support needs offensive protocores as well because the 3-star clear window requires both operatives contributing damage. If your support is running full defensive gear, you will not meet the sub-3-minute clear threshold.

Star Reset Strategy: If your first clear of a floor earns 1 or 2 stars, do not immediately retry. Finish all your first clears first, then go back and optimize. The reset costs stamina, so make each retry count. Review your run recording and identify the specific section where you lost time or took damage before attempting again.

Team-Specific Star Strategies

Xavier Teams: Xavier's strength is burst damage within short windows. For star optimization, save his skill charges for when the boss or elite is staggered. Xavier can kill an elite within a single stagger window if his skill and Resonance are both available. This makes him the best operative for Floors 9 and 11 where burst windows are the primary star constraint.

Sylus Teams: Sylus excels at sustained damage and execute. His execute threshold at 30% HP is particularly valuable for star optimization because the final phase of most bosses is the most dangerous. The faster you finish the boss, the less time you spend in the enrage phase. Sylus's execute can shave 15-20 seconds off your clear time on Floors 11 and 12 compared to other operatives with equal gear.

Zayne Teams: Zayne is the survival star specialist. His shield and freeze create breathing room that no other operative can replicate. On Floor 8, his shield directly contributes to the HP threshold star. On Floor 12, his freeze can interrupt the boss's enrage phase attacks, preventing the hits that would cost you the third star. If you are struggling with the survival star on any floor, Zayne is the answer.

Rafayel Teams: Rafayel is the wave-clear king. His AoE damage is unmatched, making him the best choice for Floors 1, 2, and 7 where grouping and clearing multiple enemies is the star bottleneck. His burn spread can also contribute meaningfully to boss damage, though his single-target output is lower than Xavier or Sylus. Use Rafayel on the wave-clearing assignment and save your other DPS for the boss wave.

Common Star-Loss Causes and Fixes

Too much time spent dodging: If you are spending more than 30% of the fight in dodge animation, you are over-dodging. Learn the boss's attack patterns well enough to stand in safe zones instead of dodging every attack. Positioning is a form of damage mitigation that does not interrupt your DPS. If you find yourself dodging more than attacking, watch a 3-star clear video of the same floor and study where the player stands.

Wrong support protocores: Many players run full support protocores on their second operative (full HP/DEF sets). This is correct for the survival star but wrong for the time star. A support that contributes no damage makes the time star significantly harder. Find a balance: give your support offensive protocores in slots 3-6 (ATK, CRIT, DMG) and defensive protocores in slots 1-2 (HP). This split provides enough damage contribution for the time star while keeping your support alive for the survival star.

Wasting Ardent Oath charges: Ardent Oath charges carry over between waves. Using Oath on the last enemy of a wave wastes the charge. Check your energy bar before each wave transition. If you are above 70% energy, use Oath during the wave. If you are below 70%, save it for the next wave. This simple rule fixes the majority of star-loss issues on wave-based floors.

Not resetting fast enough: The most efficient star farmers reset aggressively. If you take significant damage in the first 15 seconds of a floor, reset. If you miss an important stagger window, reset. A fresh run takes 2 minutes. A salvage attempt takes 4 minutes and often still misses the star. The math favors resetting early and often.

Summary: Stars are worth 10 gems each per reset. Floor 7-12 full 3-star = 180 bonus gems per reset. Learn the per-floor star conditions. Match your team to the star type (burst for time, sustain for survival). Reset aggressively. Keep a notepad with your recorded clear times so you know which floors need more work. The gems from stars add up to free pulls over time.
