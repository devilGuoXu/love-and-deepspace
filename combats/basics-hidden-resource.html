Energy Management: The Hidden Resource

Stop wasting energy. Learn how to track, generate, and optimize Resonance Energy.

The Energy System — How It Works

Every character has a hidden Resonance Energy bar. You can't see it directly — but you can feel it. It fills when you attack. It empties when you use Resonance Skills.

The max capacity is 100 energy. A Resonance Skill costs exactly 100 energy. If you use it at 80 energy, you're wasting 20 energy worth of generation.

↑ Resonance Skill costs 100 energy. Using it early = wasted potential.

Energy Generation — Where It Comes From

Basic Attack (per hit) 8-12 energy
Perfect Dodge +15 energy
Resonance Skill -100 energy (consumes)
Ardent Oath 0 energy (separate resource)
Sylus Dark Energy State +15 on perfect dodge (bonus)

Math Check: A full basic attack chain (5 hits) generates 40-60 energy. That's almost half a Resonance Skill. Complete your chains — don't cancel early.

The Waste — What Most Players Do Wrong

Mistake #1: Using Resonance Skill on cooldown — even when energy isn't full. You're losing 20-30 energy every time.

Mistake #2: Letting energy sit at 100 — every basic attack after cap is wasted. Use the skill before you overcap.

Mistake #3: Ignoring perfect dodge energy — 15 free energy is huge. Practice your dodge timing.

Mistake #4: Using Ardent Oath when energy is low — you can't follow up with Resonance. Save it for when you have energy to spend.

Character-Specific Energy Notes

Sylus
Perfect dodge in Dark Energy state refunds 15 energy. This makes his loop infinite at 145% ER. Prioritize energy regen substats until breakpoint.
Xavier
Energy is less critical. Focus on crit rate and burst timing. Use resonance only during vulnerability windows.
Zayne
Energy regen helps freeze uptime. 140% ER is the sweet spot for perma-freeze rotations.
Rafayel
Energy maintains burn stacks. 120% ER is enough for permanent DOT uptime.

Energy Regen Breakpoints

0-100% ER You need 2-3 full combos per Resonance. Suboptimal.
100-120% ER Decent. 2 combos per Resonance. Acceptable for most content.
120-140% ER Good. 1.5 combos per Resonance. Feels smooth.
140%+ ER Excellent. 1 combo + dodge = full Resonance. Optimal for Sylus and Zayne.

How to get ER: Substats on protocores. Aim for 10-12% per piece. Don't use main stat ER — you lose too much damage.

Energy Tracking — The Skill

You can't see your energy bar. You have to feel it. This takes practice.

After a few hours of focused play, you'll develop an internal sense of when you're at 50, 70, 90 energy. Trust it.

Training Drill
Go to Floor 1 of Abyss. Fight only with basic attacks. Count your hits. After 8-10 hits, use Resonance Skill. See if you feel the difference between using it at 70 energy vs 100 energy. Repeat until you can "feel" your energy level without thinking.

Summary — Energy Golden Rules

DO
  • Complete your basic attack chains

  • Feel your energy level
DON'T

  • Cancel basic attack chains early
  • Ignore dodge energy
  • Use main stat ER protocores
