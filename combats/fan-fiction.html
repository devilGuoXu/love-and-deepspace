Fan Fiction Recommendations

The Love and Deepspace fan fiction community creates incredible stories. This guide highlights recommended reads and top platforms.

About Fan Fiction: Fan fiction expands the Deepspace universe through original stories featuring the game's characters. The community spans multiple platforms and languages. Thousands of stories have been written since launch.

Best Platforms for Deepspace Fan Fiction

Archive of Our Own (AO3): The primary platform for Deepspace fan fiction. AO3 has the best tagging system, making it easy to find stories by character, relationship, rating, and content warnings. Search tags: "Love and Deepspace" (fandom tag), individual character tags (Xavier, Zayne, Rafayel, Sylus), relationship tags. AO3 is recommended for its advanced filtering, no ads, and supportive community. Wattpad: Larger platform but less organized for Deepspace content. Good for discovering new writers. Search for "Love and Deepspace" in the fan fiction category. Wattpad has a mobile app with offline reading. FanFiction.net: Smaller Deepspace presence. Older UI but has some classic stories. Reddit (r/LoveAndDeepspace): Fan fiction posts appear regularly. Search for the "Fan Fiction" flair. Shorter stories and one-shots are common. Twitter: Micro-fiction and thread fics. Follow Deepspace fan fiction writers and search for #DeepspaceFic, #XavierFic, #ZayneFic, #RafayelFic, #SylusFic.

Genres and Tropes

The Deepspace fan fiction community explores many genres: Canon Compliant: Stories that fit within the game's established lore. These often fill gaps in the story or expand on hinted backstory. Alternate Universe (AU): Characters placed in different settings. Popular AUs: Modern, Fantasy, Historical, Sci-fi, College, Royalty. Fluff: Light, feel-good stories focusing on romance and domesticity. Low conflict, high comfort. Angst: Emotional stories exploring conflict, separation, and difficult choices. Often has happy endings but the journey is painful. Crack: Humorous, absurd, or meta stories. Not meant to be taken seriously. Crossovers: Characters from Deepspace interacting with characters from other fandoms. Rare but exists. Reader-Insert (Y/N): Stories written in second person where the reader is the main character. Very popular on Wattpad.

How to Find Quality Fiction: (1) Use AO3's filter system: select the character or relationship you want, sort by kudos or bookmarks (indicators of quality). (2) Check the comments section. Engaged comment sections usually indicate quality writing. (3) Look for beta-read credits. Stories that have been beta-read tend to have better grammar and pacing. (4) Read the summary carefully. A well-written summary usually correlates with well-written content. (5) Check the word count. For multi-chapter stories, look for 3,000+ words per chapter as a baseline. (6) Use collections. On AO3, themed collections curate high-quality stories around specific topics. (7) Ask for recommendations on the subreddit or Discord. The community is happy to share favorites. (8) If you enjoy a particular writer, check their bookmarks and other works. Writers often recommend each other. (9) Do not judge by tags alone. Some of the best stories have unusual tags that scare off readers. Give them a chance. (10) Be patient. The Deepspace fan fiction community is still young. New masterpieces are being written every day.

Writing Your Own Fan Fiction

Tips for aspiring Deepspace fan fiction writers: (1) Know the characters. Replay their story chapters and bond memories. Understand their voice — how they speak, what they value, how they react to situations. (2) Start with one-shots (2,000-5,000 words) before attempting multi-chapter epics. (3) Use beta readers. Ask a trusted friend or community member to review your work before posting. (4) Tag accurately. Proper tagging helps readers find your work and sets correct expectations. (5) Respond to comments graciously. Reader engagement is what keeps the fan fiction community alive. (6) Do not be discouraged by low engagement. Many masterpieces start with few readers and grow over time. (7) Write what you love. The best fan fiction comes from genuine passion for the characters and world.

Summary: Deepspace fan fiction is found on AO3 (best), Wattpad, FanFiction.net, Reddit, and Twitter. Recommended reads for each operative are listed above. Genres range from canon-compliant to alternate universes, fluff to angst. Use AO3's filter system sorted by kudos to find quality stories. Support writers by commenting and sharing. To write your own: know the characters, start small, use beta readers, tag accurately. The Deepspace fan fiction community is welcoming to readers and writers of all experience levels.
