Protocore Enhancement Guide

How to roll substats, when to lock, when to salvage, and how to maximize your enhancement materials.

ENTRY_#0892026.06.30CORE_SYSTEM
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Enhancement Basics: Protocores gain a new substat at +3, +6, +9, +12 (4 total). Each subsequent enhancement to +15 boosts an existing substat. The first 4 rolls determine whether a protocore is good or trash — never take a protocore to +15 without checking the +12 rolls.

Rolling Strategy: Check at +3

Take every promising protocore to +3 and check the first substat. If it's a useful stat (CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ATK%, ER, HP% for Zayne), continue rolling. If it's a dead stat (flat DEF, flat HP, flat ATK), salvage immediately. The +3 roll costs very little materials. This "filter" strategy saves 80% of your enhancement material waste.

Stop Points

+6 with 2 good substats: Continue. This protocore has potential. +9 with 3 good substats: Good piece. Take to +15. +12 with 3-4 good substats: God roll. Lock immediately and max to +15. +12 with 0-1 good substats: Salvage. Don't waste more materials chasing good rolls. The sunk cost fallacy is real.

Locking & Stats Priority

The lock feature prevents accidental salvage. Lock any protocore that hits 3+ good substats at +9 or above. For main stat priority, refer to the character-specific protocore guides (#084-#087). General rule: CRIT substats are valuable on every DPS. ER is Sylus-specific. HP% is Zayne-specific.

Material Efficiency and Salvage Strategy

Enhancement materials are a finite resource during mid-game progression. The most efficient material usage follows the 80-20 rule: 80% of your enhancement materials should be spent on your two main DPS operatives' protocores, and 20% on support operatives. Support operatives benefit less from enhanced protocores because their damage contribution is secondary to their utility skills. A support operative with +6 protocores performs adequately in most content. A main DPS with +12 protocores is mandatory for end-game floors.

Salvage returns vary by enhancement level. A +3 protocore salvages for 80% of materials invested. A +6 salvages for 60%. A +9 salvages for 40%. A +12 salvages for 20%. This diminishing return curve means you should salvage early and often. Taking a protocore to +12 and then salvaging it wastes 80% of your invested materials. The optimal salvage point is +3 if the first substat is bad, or +6 if the second substat fails. By +9, you are committed — only salvage if the third substat is catastrophically bad.

Protocore Sourcing and Daily Routine

Protocores drop from four sources: Abyss floor rewards, domain farming, event shops, and battle pass. Abyss floors 7-12 drop the highest-tier protocores with guaranteed SSR quality. Domain farming is the most consistent source for SSR protocores, costing 40 stamina per run with a roughly 20% SSR drop rate. Event shops offer selectable protocores — always purchase these first as they allow you to choose the main stat you need.

Your daily protocore routine should follow this structure: spend 80 stamina on protocore domains (two runs) to fulfill daily mission requirements. Save all Abyss protocore rewards for weekly collection — they accumulate in your mailbox until claimed. On weekends when all domains are open, run the protocore domain that drops the slot you need most. Rotate your target slot weekly to build a balanced inventory. After four weeks of this routine, you will have enough protocores to outfit two full teams.

Domain level 5 provides the best balance of enhancement material quality and quantity. Level 7 drops purple materials at 20% rate. Level 3 drops green at 60% rate.

Key Insight: Most players waste 60% of their enhancement materials by taking bad protocores to +12 hoping they'll magically get good rolls. The +3 substat reveal is the most important roll in the game. If the first substat is bad, stop. Salvage. Try again. A 5-second decision at +3 saves 20 minutes of wasted enhancement.

Summary: +3 reveals first substat — filter here. +9 with 3 good = lock and max. +12 with 0-1 good = salvage. Never take a protocore to +15 without good rolls at +12. The +3 filter saves the most materials. Be ruthless with your standards.
