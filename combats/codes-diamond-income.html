Understanding the Diamond Economy

Diamonds are the premium currency in Love and Deepspace. They are used primarily for limited banner pulls (160 diamonds per pull), stamina refreshes (50-100 diamonds per refresh), and occasional shop purchases. Understanding the diamond economy means knowing not just how many diamonds you have, but how many you can expect to earn and where they come from.

The diamond economy has three tiers of income: recurring (income that repeats predictably — daily missions, weekly missions), semi-recurring (income that comes from sources with predictable frequency but variable amount — events, maintenance compensation), and one-time (finite sources that eventually deplete — achievements, story completion, affinity milestones). Successful planning requires modeling all three tiers.

Diamonds are the most tracked and discussed resource in the community because they directly determine pulling power. However, diamonds are not the only valuable resource — stamina, enhancement materials, and wish cards all contribute to account power. Do not optimize diamonds at the expense of other resources.

Daily Diamond Sources

Daily sources form the reliable foundation of your diamond income. These sources are predictable and should never be missed.

Daily Missions. Completing all daily missions rewards 50 diamonds. This requires approximately 10-15 minutes of gameplay. Monthly total: 1,500 diamonds. Daily missions also provide stamina, gold, and materials — the diamonds are the headline reward, but the ancillary resources are also significant.

Daily Login Rewards. The standard login calendar provides diamonds on specific days — typically 30-50 diamonds every 2-3 days. Monthly total from login calendar: approximately 400-500 diamonds.

Aurum Pass (Monthly Card). If purchased, provides 100 diamonds per day for 30 days plus 300 instant diamonds. Monthly total: 3,000 diamonds from daily claim + 300 instant = 3,300 total. This is the single largest daily diamond source but requires spending $4.99.

Protector Pass (Monthly Pass). A separate monthly purchase that provides 60 diamonds per day plus other rewards. Monthly total: 1,800 diamonds. Also requires spending — approximately $2.99.

Daily Diamond Summary. F2P: 50-65 diamonds per day (1,500-1,950 per month). Monthly Card holder: 150-165 diamonds per day (4,500-4,950 per month). Both passes: 210-225 diamonds per day (6,300-6,750 per month).

Weekly Diamond Sources

Weekly sources require more engagement than daily sources but provide larger diamond amounts per payout.

Weekly Missions. Completing weekly mission progress rewards 200 diamonds. The missions require cumulative activity across the week (stamina spending, bounty completion, boss fights). Weekly total: 200 diamonds. Monthly total: 800-1,000 diamonds (accounting for variance in weekly reset timing).

Senior Hunter Contest. The PvP-adjacent weekly mode rewards diamonds based on star achievement. At 0-3 stars: 50 diamonds. At 4-6 stars: 100 diamonds. At 7-9 stars: 150 diamonds. At 10-12 stars: 200 diamonds. At 13-15 stars: 250 diamonds. At 16-18 stars: 300 diamonds. Monthly total: 200-1,200 diamonds depending on performance.

Abyssal Trials Weekly. Weekly clear rewards from Abyssal Trials provide diamonds based on floors cleared. Floor 1-3: 60 diamonds. Floor 4-6: 80 diamonds. Floor 7-9: 100 diamonds. Floor 10-12: 120 diamonds. Monthly total: 240-480 diamonds.

Weekly Diamond Summary. Minimum (low engagement): 400-500 diamonds per week (1,600-2,000 per month). Maximum (high engagement): 800-900 diamonds per week (3,200-3,600 per month).

Monthly Diamond Projections

Combining daily and weekly sources provides the baseline for monthly income projections. These projections assume consistent (not perfect) engagement.

F2P — Low Engagement. Daily missions completed sporadically, weekly missions partially completed, minimal PvP participation. Estimated: 2,500-3,500 diamonds per month. This level of play will struggle to save for banners and is not recommended for players who want to pull regularly.

F2P — Moderate Engagement. Daily missions completed most days, weekly missions fully completed, basic PvP and Abyssal participation. Estimated: 4,500-5,500 diamonds per month. This is the minimum viable engagement for sustainable banner pulling (one guaranteed SSR every 3-4 months).

F2P — High Engagement. Daily missions completed every day, weekly missions maximized, strong PvP and Abyssal performance, all event diamonds claimed. Estimated: 5,500-7,000 diamonds per month. This is the optimal F2P income and enables pulling on approximately 1 of every 3 banners.

Monthly Card — High Engagement. All of the above plus the 3,300 diamonds from Aurum Pass. Estimated: 8,500-10,000 diamonds per month. This is the most popular spender tier and enables pulling on most banners with selective skipping.

Both Passes — High Engagement. Monthly Card + Protector Pass. Estimated: 10,500-12,500 diamonds per month. This tier provides significant pulling flexibility and enables competitive account growth.

Event Diamond Sources

Event diamond income is variable but substantial. This section provides average estimates based on historical event data.

Major Banner Events (14-21 days). Event shop diamonds: 200-400. Event story completion: 200-400. Event mission milestones: 300-500. Event login bonuses (if applicable): 200-300. Total per major event: 900-1,600 diamonds. With 1-2 major events per month: 900-3,200 diamonds.

Minor Events (7-14 days). Minigame rewards: 100-300. Story event rewards: 200-400. Total per minor event: 300-700 diamonds. With 1-2 minor events per month: 300-1,400 diamonds.

Maintenance & Update Compensation. Standard maintenance: 300 diamonds. Major version update: 600 diamonds. Bug fix compensation: 100-200 diamonds. Monthly total: 500-1,000 diamonds on average.

Social Media & Community Events. Official social media campaigns: 50-200 diamonds per campaign. Community milestone rewards: 100-300 diamonds. Redemption codes (active): 100-500 diamonds per month. Monthly total: 200-800 diamonds.

Event Diamond Summary. Low event month: 1,900-3,200 diamonds. High event month: 5,000-6,400 diamonds. Average event month: 3,500-4,800 diamonds.

One-Time Diamond Sources

These sources are finite — they provide a substantial initial diamond injection but do not renew. Tracking your progress through one-time sources helps you understand your account's remaining diamond potential.

Main Story Completion. Each story chapter rewards 100-200 diamonds for first completion. Total main story diamonds: 2,000-3,000 diamonds (estimate based on current chapter count).

Memory Card Stories. Each SSR memory card provides 30-50 diamonds for first-time Tender Moments completion. Each SR provides 20-30 diamonds. Total memory card story diamonds: highly variable based on collection size — potentially 3,000-5,000+ diamonds for complete collections.

Secret Times. Each Secret Times episode provides 30-50 diamonds. Total: 500-1,000 diamonds depending on available episodes.

Ancillary Content. In-game messages and phone calls sometimes reward diamonds. Social media posts (in-game) occasionally provide rewards. Total: 200-500 diamonds from miscellaneous one-time sources.

Total One-Time Diamond Estimate. New account: 5,000-8,000 diamonds available from all one-time sources. Established account: 1,000-3,000 diamonds remaining (most claimed). Mature account: 0-500 diamonds remaining (nearly all claimed).

Hidden & Achievement Diamonds

Achievements are the most commonly overlooked diamond source in the game. The achievement system is extensive and rewards account development across multiple dimensions.

Combat Achievements. Clear specific floors in Abyssal Trials, reach score thresholds in Senior Hunter Contest, complete bounty stages without taking damage. Diamond rewards range from 50-200 per achievement. Estimated total from combat achievements: 1,500-2,500 diamonds.

Collection Achievements. Collect specific numbers of memory cards, reach specific collection thresholds, complete memory card sets. Diamond rewards: 50-300 per achievement. Estimated total: 1,000-2,000 diamonds.

Level Achievements. Reach specific Hunter levels, operative affinity thresholds, and account milestones. Diamond rewards: 100-500 per achievement. Estimated total: 2,000-3,000 diamonds.

Exploration Achievements. Complete specific exploration milestones in various game modes. Diamond rewards: 30-100 per achievement. Estimated total: 500-1,000 diamonds.

Hidden Achievements. Some achievements are not displayed until completed. These are discovered through specific actions — talking to NPCs on specific days, completing specific memory card combinations, interacting with environment objects. Diamond rewards: 30-50 per hidden achievement. Estimated total: 300-500 diamonds.

Total Achievement Diamonds. Estimated 5,000-9,000 diamonds available through the achievement system, with most achievements completable through normal gameplay over 3-6 months.

Senior Hunter Contest Diamonds

The Senior Hunter Contest deserves special attention because it is both a major diamond source and a skill-based mode where optimization directly translates to income.

Scoring System. Each season (2 weeks) has three stages with 6 stars available per stage (18 total). Stars are earned by meeting clear time and survivability thresholds. Higher star counts unlock higher diamond rewards at season end.

Diamond Reward Tiers. 0-3 stars: 100 diamonds per season. 4-6 stars: 200 diamonds per season. 7-9 stars: 300 diamonds per season. 10-12 stars: 400 diamonds per season. 13-15 stars: 500 diamonds per season. 16-18 stars: 600 diamonds per season.

Seasonal Diamond Income. With 2 seasons per month: 200-1,200 diamonds per month. The difference between low and high performance is 1,000 diamonds per month — equivalent to 6 limited banner pulls. Improving your Senior Hunter Contest performance by even a few stars has a measurable impact on your pulling power.

Investment Tip. The Senior Hunter Contest rewards team diversity — each stage favors different Stellactrum colors and operative types. Investing in a broad roster of 6-8 characters at level 50-60 produces better contest results than hyper-investing in 3 characters. The diamond return on broad roster investment is concrete and calculable.

Abyssal Trial Diamonds

Abyssal Trials provide both one-time and recurring diamond income. Understanding both components is essential for accurate income modeling.

First-Clear Diamonds. Each floor provides 100-200 diamonds on first clear. Total first-clear diamonds: 1,200-2,400 for floors 1-12. These are one-time but substantial.

Weekly Clear Diamonds. As covered in the weekly section: 60-120 diamonds per week based on highest floor cleared. Monthly: 240-480 diamonds.

Season Rewards. Abyssal Trials seasons (typically monthly) provide bonus diamonds based on cumulative performance. Season rewards: 200-500 diamonds per season.

Total Abyssal Diamond Value. First month (all first clears + weekly + season): approximately 2,000-3,500 diamonds. Ongoing monthly: approximately 500-1,000 diamonds from weekly + season rewards.

Affinity & Social Diamonds

Affinity systems and social features provide a steady trickle of diamonds that many players overlook.

Operative Affinity Levels. Each affinity level (1-50+ per operative) rewards diamonds at key thresholds. Typical rewards: 50-200 diamonds per milestone. Total from all operatives: 2,000-4,000 diamonds over the life of the account.

Daily Social Interactions. Visiting operatives in the Hub (daily): 10-20 diamonds per interaction. Weekly total from social interactions: 50-100 diamonds. Monthly: 200-400 diamonds.

Photo Studio. First photo with each outfit or location sometimes rewards diamonds. Weekly photo challenges occasionally include diamond rewards. Monthly: 50-150 diamonds.

Friend System. Sending and receiving friend stamina (not diamonds directly, but stamina enables more farming which enables more progression which enables more diamond-earning content). No direct diamond income but indirect value.

Savings Planning Tools

Putting this knowledge into practice requires a savings plan. Here is a framework for planning your banner pulls with precision.

Step 1: Know Your Baseline. Calculate your monthly diamond income using the projections above, adjusted for your actual engagement level. Be honest about your consistency — if you skip dailies on weekends, adjust downward.

Step 2: Identify Your Target Banners. Based on prediction sources, identify which upcoming banners are your priorities. Note the expected release dates. A banner typically requires 14,400 diamonds (90 pulls for soft pity) or 28,800 (180 pulls for hard pity).

Step 3: Calculate Savings Timeline. Divide the diamond cost of your target banner by your monthly income to determine how many months of saving you need. Add 1 month of buffer for unexpected expenses (impulse pulls, event passes).

Step 4: Set Hard Rules. Rules like "never drop below 14,400 diamonds" or "only pull on the first day of a banner" prevent impulse spending between your target banners. Write your rules down and commit to them.

Step 5: Track Actual Income. Use a simple spreadsheet or note: at the start of each month, record your diamond balance. At the end of each month, record again and calculate the difference. Compare actual income to your projections and adjust as needed.

Banner Readiness Check: Before pulling on any banner, run this checklist: Do I have 14,400+ diamonds saved? (If no, do not pull unless you accept the risk of missing pity.) Is this banner for an operative I actively use? Does this memory card fill a gap in my account? Will I regret skipping this when it reruns in 6 months? If at least 3 answers are yes, the pull is justified. If 2 or fewer, save your diamonds.

Summary: The Love and Deepspace diamond economy is complex but predictable. Daily and weekly sources provide the reliable foundation: 2,500-4,000 diamonds per month from routine activities. Events add 3,500-5,000 diamonds per month on average. One-time sources provide an initial injection of 5,000-8,000 diamonds for new accounts. Achievements contribute another 5,000-9,000 diamonds over the account's lifetime. PvP and Abyssal performance can add 500-1,500 diamonds per month at high engagement levels. A F2P player with optimized play earns 6,500-8,000 diamonds per month — enough for a guaranteed SSR every 2-3 months. A Monthly Card holder earns 9,500-11,500 per month — enough for a guaranteed SSR every 1.5-2 months. The key to successful savings is accurate income projection, clear banner prioritization, and strict spending discipline between target banners.
