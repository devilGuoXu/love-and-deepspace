THREAT LEVEL: EXTREME

Abyssal Floor 10

Two elite Wanderers with complementary elements. Split your damage and watch the clock.

ENTRY_#1222026.06.29FLOOR_10
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Required Power: 90k+ | Clear Time: 4:00 | Elements: Fire + Ice. Bring a balanced team — don't go mono-element.

Wave 1: Fire + Ice Elite

Inferno Guard: A fire-themed elite with AoE burns. Dodge the flame pillar attack — it tracks. Frost Reaver: An ice-themed elite that freezes and charges. Break line of sight to avoid freeze. Strategy: Pull them together. Use Rafayel for Inferno Guard and Zayne for Frost Reaver. Focus Inferno Guard first — his burns are harder to manage.

Wave 2: Boss — Dual Element

Phase 1 (100%-60%): Boss alternates between fire and ice forms. Fire form = stay mobile. Ice form = stay close. React to the element switch. Phase 2 (60%-30%): Boss gains an elemental shield that must be broken with the opposite element. Fire shield = ice damage. Ice shield = fire damage. Phase 3 (30%-0%): Both elements simultaneously. Arena splits into hot/cold zones. Stand in neutral zones.

Recommended Team: Rafayel (Fire DPS — breaks ice shield) + Zayne (Ice DPS — breaks fire shield) + Xavier (Flex DPS — consistent damage to both). Rotate between fire and ice as shields appear.

Memory Loadout Strategy

Floor 10 demands optimized memory setups for both fire and ice damage dealers. Rafayel should run a full Fire Solar set with attack percentage main stats on your Solar pair and HP percentage on the Lunar pieces. Zayne benefits most from an Ice Solar set with similar stat priority. Xavier can run a generic damage set since his role is neutral flex — focus on crit rate and crit damage substats to maximize consistent output during both phases.

Protocore selection is critical at this power level. Rafayel needs a Fire Damage Bonus protocore on the bottom-right slot. Zayne requires an Ice Damage Bonus in the same position. Xavier can run a flat ATK protocore for flexibility. The shield-breaking mechanics do not scale with damage stats, so your protocore choices should prioritize raw power over specialized bonuses to meet the 90k battle rating threshold.

Positioning and Movement

Wave 1 positioning determines your success rate. Pull the Inferno Guard and Frost Reaver to the center of the arena so your AoE abilities hit both simultaneously. Kite around the central pillar to break line of sight on the Frost Reaver's freeze attack. The pillar provides complete cover from the freeze mechanic. Use it liberally during Wave 1 to conserve HP for the boss fight.

During the boss encounter, position yourself at the edge of the arena during Phase 1 to give yourself maximum room to react to the element switch. When the boss shifts to fire form, move to the outer ring and maintain distance. When it shifts to ice form, close to mid-range. Phase 3 positioning is the most demanding — identify the neutral zones as soon as they appear and claim your position immediately. The hot and cold zones deal damage over time that can kill in under 10 seconds.

Floor 10 rewards elemental coverage over raw power. The dual-element boss requires both Fire and Ice damage to break shields efficiently. If your Ice DPS is significantly weaker than your Fire DPS, consider using Xavier as your primary damage dealer for both phases. Xavier's Light element deals neutral damage to both shield types at 70% effectiveness, which is better than bringing a weak Ice operative that deals 100% damage to Ice shields but only 20% to Fire shields.

Floor 10's boss alternates elements every 20 seconds. A 2-second body glow precedes each switch. Use this window to swap to your matching-element operative before the shield activates.

Key Mechanic: The boss's element shield has 10 hits regardless of damage. Fast-hitting operatives break shields faster than slow, hard-hitting ones. Xavier's multi-hit light attacks are excellent for shield breaking.

Summary: Bring both fire and ice. Break shields with the opposite element. Focus Inferno Guard first. Phase 3 is about positioning in neutral zones. Clear time is tight — maximize uptime.
