The Optimal Week

A 7-Day Farming Schedule — Based on Domain Rotations & Community Data

Every week, the same question appears in Discord: "What should I farm today?" This is my answer. A day-by-day schedule optimized for domain rotations, resource efficiency, and mental sustainability. I have been running this schedule for six months and it has never let me down.

Day 1 — Monday

Abyss Reset & Protocore Domain

Monday is Abyss reset day. Before you do anything else, check your new Abyss floors. The weekly reset happens at server midnight, and the first clear of each floor gives bonus rewards. Run your highest cleared floor first to gauge where your account is this week. If you can push one floor higher than last week, that is progress.

After Abyss, hit the protocore domain. Monday is the ATK/CRIT domain rotation. This is the most universally useful domain because ATK% and CRIT pieces work on every DPS operative. Run it until you run out of energy or patience, whichever comes first.

Pro tip: save your Abyss sweep for Tuesday, when you have a better sense of which floors you can push. The sweep auto-clears your highest completed floor.
Day 2 — Tuesday

Weekly Boss & Ascension Domain

Tuesday is weekly boss day. The three weekly boss kills reset every Tuesday at server time. Use them on the boss that drops materials for the operative you are currently building. If you are between builds, just kill whichever boss is fastest to clear. The materials stockpile is never wasted.

After bosses, switch to the ascension domain that matches the element of your main DPS. Tuesday is the HP/DEF domain rotation, which is less useful for DPS, but ascension materials are element-locked, not domain-locked. Farm whatever element you need.

The weekly boss materials are account-bound but cross-operative. You can funnel all three kills into a single operative's ascension if you are focusing one character.
Day 3 — Wednesday

Protocore Cleanup & Enhancement Day

Wednesday is my favorite day of the week, which is a sad thing to say about a mobile game, but here we are. Wednesday is enhancement day. I take all the protocores I collected on Monday and Tuesday, sort through them, and enhance anything that looks promising. No new farming. Just processing existing inventory.

The Wednesday domain rotation is elemental damage, which is niche but valuable if you are building a specific operative. I usually skip the domain on Wednesday and focus on using what I already have. The urge to keep farming without ever using what you farm is the hoarding trap I fell into before.

Rule of thumb: if a protocore has been sitting in your inventory for more than 7 days unenhanced, you are never going to use it. Trash it.
Day 4 — Thursday

XP & Gold Domain

Thursday is the XP and gold domain rotation. I know, it is not exciting. But running these domains consistently is what separates accounts that have resources when they need them from accounts that are always scraping. I run the XP domain until I have enough bottles to level one memory from 1 to 80. That is my minimum target.

The gold domain is less urgent because events give a lot of gold, but if you are below 500k gold, run it until you hit that threshold. Gold is the resource that seems abundant until you suddenly need 300k for a single enhancement and you are at 40k.

Day 5 — Friday

Protocore Domain (Second Wave)

Friday is the second protocore domain rotation. The available set changes from Monday's rotation. If Monday was ATK/CRIT, Friday is usually HP/DEF or status effect sets. Even if you do not need the Friday sets, run the domain anyway and stockpile. You never know when a future operative will need a set you ignored.

I keep a running list of which operatives need which sets. When I get a promising piece for an operative I am not currently building, I save it with a note. This has saved me weeks of farming when I eventually got around to building that operative.

Day 6 — Saturday

Event Content & Achievement Hunting

Saturday is for events and achievements. Most limited events have weekly missions that reset on Saturday or Sunday. Knock those out first. Then look at your achievement list and pick one that is within reach. Achievements are a finite gem source, and clearing them out systematically is more efficient than panic-farming them when a banner is about to end and you need 10 more pulls.

I also use Saturday to clean up any content I missed during the week. Story missions, side content, minigames. Whatever I did not get to.

Day 7 — Sunday

Rest Day — Do Your Dailies and Log Off

Sunday is the most important day of the week. Do your dailies. Spend your stamina on whatever domain feels least tedious. Then close the game. Go outside. Read a book. Play a different game. Touch some grass, as the kids say.

I used to treat Sunday as a catch-up day where I would grind everything I missed during the week. That led to burnout. Now I treat Sunday as a deliberate low-effort day. I do the bare minimum and log off. My Monday performance is noticeably better after a low-effort Sunday than after a full-grind Sunday. Rest is part of the schedule.

The game will always have more content. You will never catch up completely. Accepting that is the key to long-term enjoyment.
