Photo Mode Guide

Camera angles, poses, filters, and lighting 閳?capture your operatives at their best.

Camera Controls

Zoom: Scroll wheel / pinch. Use 1.5x-2x zoom for portraits. Avoid max zoom (distortion).
Angle: Drag to orbit. Eye-level for natural shots. Low angle for dramatic power shots.
Depth of Field: Adjust blur intensity. Low blur for full-body, high blur for close-up portraits.
Lighting: 3-point lighting system. Key light, fill light, backlight. Experiment with warm/cold tones.

Pose & Expression Guide

Casual: Best for daily life settings. Natural expressions. Use with outdoor lighting.
Combat: Action poses. Best with dramatic backlighting and motion blur effects.
Romantic: Intimate poses. Soft lighting, warm filters. Available at higher affinity levels.
Formal: Elegant poses. Best with clean backgrounds and symmetrical framing.

Location Tips

Hunter Corps HQ: Clean architectural lines. Best for formal and group shots.

City Streets: Urban backdrop. Use golden hour (warm) lighting for best results.

N109 Zone: Dramatic ruins. High contrast. Best for combat and edgy poses.

Home/Base: Cozy indoor settings. Best for casual and romantic shots.

Event Photo Requirements

Many limited events require specific photo types to earn full rewards. Event photo missions typically specify a required operative, location tag, and expression type. Before taking an event photo, check the mission requirements and set up your shot to match all three criteria simultaneously. A photo that meets only two of the three conditions earns partial rewards. Common event requirements include sunset lighting in Hunter Corps HQ with a happy expression, or combat poses in the N109 Zone with a serious expression.

Keep a mental checklist of the four key photo elements before each event shot: operative (use the correct companion), location (stand in the marked zone), expression (select from the required category), and lighting (adjust to match the specified time of day). The lighting condition is the most commonly missed requirement 閳?most event photos require natural outdoor lighting rather than the studio lighting preset. If your photo fails the event check, review which element was missing and adjust before retaking.

Affinity Photo Bonuses

Taking photos with operatives in your base generates small affinity bonuses. Each operative has a daily photo affinity cap of 20 EXP, earned by taking one photo per unique location in your base. The base's living room, garden, and training room each count as separate locations. Taking a photo with the operative in all three locations in one session caps their daily photo affinity. This is a small but free source of affinity that requires no gifts or stamina.

Operative photo preferences affect the quality rating of your photos, which in turn affects how much affinity bonus you receive. Photos rated gold quality by the game's evaluation system give double the base affinity compared to silver-rated photos. To achieve gold rating consistently, ensure good lighting (avoid backlighting), keep the operative centered in the frame, and select an expression that matches the location's mood. A neutral expression in a formal location like the living room usually scores better than a playful expression in the same setting.

Photo mode achievements are one-time gem sources that many players miss. The 'Master Photographer' achievement requires taking 100 photos total across all modes. The 'Location Scout' achievement requires taking a photo in each of the 12 base locations. The 'Expression Collection' achievement requires capturing all 8 expression types on one operative. These three achievements alone reward 250 total gems for activities you would likely do anyway.

Event photo missions often require specific lighting tied to the real-time clock. Sunrise: 5-7 AM. Sunset: 5-7 PM. Night: 8 PM-4 AM. Check time requirements at the start of the event and set an alarm.

The Bottom Line: Great photos come from lighting + angle + expression, in that order. Master the 3-point lighting system. Keep the camera at eye level. Choose poses that match the location. And experiment 閳?the worst that happens is a bad screenshot.
