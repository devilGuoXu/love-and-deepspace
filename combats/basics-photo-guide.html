Photo Mode Guide

Camera angles, poses, filters, and lighting — capture your operatives at their best.

ENTRY_#025 2026.06.29 PHOTO

Why Photo Mode Matters: Photo mode is not just for screenshots — it's used in events, dating mechanics, and social features. Knowing how to frame a good shot helps in both creative and gameplay contexts.

Camera Controls

Zoom: Scroll wheel / pinch. Use 1.5x-2x zoom for portraits. Avoid max zoom (distortion).
Angle: Drag to orbit. Eye-level for natural shots. Low angle for dramatic power shots.
Depth of Field: Adjust blur intensity. Low blur for full-body, high blur for close-up portraits.
Lighting: 3-point lighting system. Key light, fill light, backlight. Experiment with warm/cold tones.

Pose & Expression Guide

Casual: Best for daily life settings. Natural expressions. Use with outdoor lighting.
Combat: Action poses. Best with dramatic backlighting and motion blur effects.
Romantic: Intimate poses. Soft lighting, warm filters. Available at higher affinity levels.
Formal: Elegant poses. Best with clean backgrounds and symmetrical framing.

Location Tips

Hunter Corps HQ: Clean architectural lines. Best for formal and group shots.

City Streets: Urban backdrop. Use golden hour (warm) lighting for best results.

N109 Zone: Dramatic ruins. High contrast. Best for combat and edgy poses.

Home/Base: Cozy indoor settings. Best for casual and romantic shots.

The Bottom Line: Great photos come from lighting + angle + expression, in that order. Master the 3-point lighting system. Keep the camera at eye level. Choose poses that match the location. And experiment — the worst that happens is a bad screenshot.
