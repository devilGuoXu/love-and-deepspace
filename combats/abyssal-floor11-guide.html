THREAT LEVEL: EXTREME

Abyssal Floor 11

A pure DPS check. Three phases, no breaks, and a tight 3:30 clear window.

ENTRY_#1232026.06.29FLOOR_11

Required Power: 95k+ | Clear Time: 3:30 | Element: Dark — Sylus is strongly recommended for execute damage.

Phase 1: The Gauntlet (100%-70%)

Horde Wave: 12 small Wanderers spawn. Kill them quickly to trigger the elite spawn. Elite — Void Caster: A mage that channels AoE void damage. Interrupt the channel or eat heavy damage. Sylus's execute is perfect here. Timer Check: Phase 1 must be cleared in under 1 minute or you won't meet the DPS check.

Phase 2: The Duel (70%-35%)

Shadow Knight: A single, powerful elite with fast combos. Learn the 3-hit pattern: slash, slash, overhead. Perfect dodge the overhead for a long punish window. Void Pools: Ground zones that drain energy. Avoid them — losing energy means losing DPS. Enrage Mechanic: If Phase 2 takes longer than 1:30, the Knight enrages and one-shots with every hit.

Phase 3: The Reaper (35%-0%)

Death's Herald: The floor boss. Massive AoE scythe sweeps. Stay behind at all times. Soul Harvest: Tethers to the furthest operative and drains HP. Break tether by dealing 20% of the boss's HP in damage. Final Reckoning: Arena-wide execution attack. Only perfect dodge or Ardent Oath i-frames survive.

Recommended Team: Sylus (Main DPS/Execute) — execute phases elites faster. Xavier (Sub-DPS) — consistent damage during Knight duel. Zayne (Support/Sustain) — HP sustain through Phase 3.

Key Mechanic: Floor 11 is a hard DPS check. If you can't clear Phase 1 in 1 minute, your gear isn't ready. If you clear Phase 1 but fail Phase 2, you need better dodging — not more damage. Phase 3 is a gear + skill combined check. Save all ults for the Soul Harvest tether break.

Summary: Floor 11 tests your DPS first, your dodging second, and your patience third. Clear Phase 1 fast, dodge the Knight perfectly, and burst the Reaper. Sylus's execute makes every phase easier. This floor separates mid-game from end-game players.
