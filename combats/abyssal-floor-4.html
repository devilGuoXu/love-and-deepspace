Abyssal Trials: Floor 4

The first major difficulty spike. Learn the mechanics and build right.

ENTRY_#1282026.06.30ABYSS

Floor 4 Threshold: Recommended power level 18,000. First floor requiring two teams. Boss has 2-phase fight with escalating damage.

Phase 1 — Elemental Sentry

An armored construct that cycles through elemental shields. The shield rotates through Light → Dark → Frost → Fire every 15 seconds. While a shield is active, it has 80% damage reduction against non-matching elements. Break the shield by hitting it with the matching element. Once broken, the sentry is stunned for 5 seconds and takes 50% increased damage.

Strategy: Rotate your operatives to match the current shield. If you don't have an operative of the active element, use neutral damage (basic attacks) until the shield cycles. Never use non-matching resonance skills during shield phase — they get reduced to 20% effectiveness.

Phase 2 — Corrupted Colossus

After Phase 1's sentry is destroyed, the Corrupted Colossus spawns. This boss has two dangerous attacks: Ground Slam (AoE circle, telegraphed by 2s charge) and Energy Beam (line AoE, telegraphed by 1.5s charge). The Ground Slam can be dodged or iframed. The Energy Beam must be dodged — it has too many hit ticks for iframes.

Positioning tip: Stay at mid-range (not melee, not far). Melee range triggers Ground Slam more frequently. Far range triggers Energy Beam more frequently. Mid-range gives you reaction time for both attacks.

Team Composition

Team 1: DPS (high burst, any element), Flex (shield breaker, ideally one of each element), Support/Healer. Team 2: DPS (elemental coverage), Flex, Support. Minimum 2 operatives per team. Shield rotation means you need at least 3 elements covered across both teams. Recommended: Xavier (Light) + Rafayel (Fire) on Team 1, Zayne (Frost) + Sylus (Dark) on Team 2.

Rewards

First clear: 300 diamonds, 5 Advanced Resonance Crystals, 10 Purple EXP Books. Subsequent clears: 50 diamonds weekly. The 300 diamond first-clear bonus makes this floor a high priority for all accounts.

Pro Tip: Save your resonance skills for shield breaks. The 50% increased damage window after breaking the sentry's shield is the best time to burst. If you use resonance off cooldown, you'll have nothing when the shield breaks.

Summary: Match elements to break shields. Mid-range positioning. Save burst for stun windows. Two teams with at least 3 elements covered. First-clear diamonds are worth the investment.
