强化材料圣经：+0 到 +15 的成本全解析

The Protocore Enhancement Material Bible — every ore, every cost, every efficiency ratio. If you have ever wondered "should I take this to +12?" the answer is here.

The One Number You Need to Know: Taking a single SSR protocore from +0 to +15 costs the equivalent of 420 green enhancement ores — or roughly 6,720 stamina worth of domain farming. Plan accordingly.

01 MATERIAL TYPES

强化材料类型

Conversion ratio: 1 purple = 3 blue = 9 green (in XP terms). However, material type restricts what level range each can be used for. Green ore cannot be used past +6 enhancement. Blue ore caps at +12. Purple ore is required for the final +12 to +15 push. You cannot bypass this by using more lower-tier ore — the game enforces material tiers per level range.

Key observation: The +9 to +12 bracket (levels 10-12) costs 144 green-equivalent — that is 24% of the entire +0 to +15 cost for just 3 levels. The final +12 to +15 push costs 216 green-equivalent (36%). Over half your total material investment goes into the last 6 levels. This is why a +12 protocore is considered "good enough" for most content — you get 64% of the stat value for 64% of the cost, but the final 36% investment only yields 36% more stats.

03 RARITY COMPARISON

SR vs R vs SSR 成本对比

Rarity dramatically affects enhancement cost. SSR protocores cost the most to max. Here is the total material cost to reach +15 for each rarity:

Rarity Green Blue Purple Total (green eq.) % of SSR Cost
SSR 28 26 43 600 100%
SR 12 10 17 240 40%
R 6 5 9 120 20%

When to use each rarity: R protocores are temporary filler only — never take an R beyond +6. SR protocores are viable budget options for support operatives; a +12 SR is cheaper than a +9 SSR and provides comparable utility stats. SSR protocores should be reserved for your main DPS operatives where the higher stat ceiling actually matters for damage output.

Rule of thumb: If an operative's damage contribution is less than 20% of team DPS (pure support), an SR protocore set at +9 is sufficient. If the operative is your main damage dealer, invest in SSR and push to +12 minimum.

Efficiency analysis: Domain Level 7+ gives the best green-equivalent ore per stamina at 4.2 per 20 stamina. However, if you specifically need green ore for low-level enhancement, Domain Level 3 is more efficient (1.5 green per 20 stamina vs 0.6 green from Level 7). The opportunity cost of running lower domains is the lost purple ore — each Level 7 run has a 20% chance to drop a purple ore, worth 9 green equivalent. Over 100 runs, Level 7 yields roughly 420 green-equivalent vs Level 3's 150 green-equivalent. Always run the highest domain you can clear efficiently.

Weekly routine: Run Domain Lv.7 five times per day (100 stamina) for consistent purple ore income. Over a 7-day week, this yields approximately 7-10 purple ores, 15-20 blue ores, and 30-40 green ores — enough to take one SSR protocore from +0 to +9 every 10 days.

05 SALVAGE RETURNS

分解回收曲线

The salvage return rate is not linear. Understanding the "salvage cliff" is critical to efficient material management. Here is the exact return rate per enhancement level bracket:

Enhancement Level Return Rate Recommendation
+0 to +5 80% Safe to salvage freely
+6 to +9 60% Salvage only if substats are bad
+10 to +12 40% Think carefully before salvaging
+13 to +15 20% Never salvage — keep or lock

The salvage cliff: At +10, the return rate drops from 60% to 40%. This is the inflection point where the math changes. A protocore taken to +9 and salvaged loses 40% of materials. A protocore taken to +10 and salvaged loses 60%. The incremental cost of going from +9 to +10 is 39 green-equivalent, but the salvage loss increases by 20% of the total invested (600 green eq. x 20% = 120 green eq. loss). In other words, the +9 to +10 step alone costs 39 + 120 = 159 green equivalent in combined materials and lost salvage value. This is why you should never take a protocore past +9 unless you are committed to keeping it.

Practical rule: Salvage at +3 if the revealed substat is bad (90% material recovery including the protocore base). Salvage at +6 if two substats are bad (70% recovery). At +9, you are committed — only salvage if all three substats are unusable (50% recovery). Never salvage past +10.

06 OPPORTUNITY COST

机会成本分析

Every stamina spent on protocore domains is stamina not spent on other vital resources. One week of natural stamina regeneration (720 stamina) spent entirely on protocore farming has a significant opportunity cost. Here is what 720 stamina could have bought instead:

Resource Domain Yield per 720 Stamina Value Equivalent
Protocore Ores Domain Lv.7 ~150 green eq. 1/4 of a +15 protocore
SSR Memories Memory Domain 6 SSR shards 60% of a memory shard
Credits Credit Domain ~1.2M credits 12 protocore leveling costs
EXP Bottles EXP Domain ~120 purple bottles 2 protocores to +12

The real cost: If you spend one full week of stamina on protocores, you delay memory leveling by 6 SSR shards (roughly one SSR memory ascension tier). You lose 1.2M credits that could have upgraded 12 protocores' level-up costs. And you miss 120 EXP bottles that could have taken 2 protocores to +12. The decision to farm protocores is a decision to slow down every other progression system.

This is why the optimal strategy is balanced farming: spend 2-3 days per week on protocores (about 200-300 stamina), 2 days on memories, 1 day on credits, and 1 day on EXP. Over a full patch cycle (6 weeks), this balance yields enough materials to maintain 3 operatives without sacrificing overall progression speed.

07 F2P BUDGETING

F2P vs 付费玩家的强化预算

How many +15 protocores can different player segments afford per patch? The answer depends on stamina allocation, event participation, and battle pass tier.

Light Spender 2-3 5-6 10-12 ~6,720 / patch
Whale Unlimited Unlimited Unlimited Infinite (stamina refreshes)

F2P budgeting advice: As a free player, you can afford roughly 2-3 +15 protocores per patch (6 weeks). That is enough for one main DPS operative (6 slots, 2 of which should be +15, 2 at +12, 2 at +9). Do not spread materials thin across multiple operatives. Focus on one DPS per team and give them the best protocores. Your second and third operatives can function with +9 sets.

Light spenders (monthly card + battle pass) get approximately 30% more stamina from the battle pass ore rewards. This translates to one additional +15 protocore per patch. With 3-4 +15 protocores per patch, a light spender can maintain two strong DPS operatives simultaneously — a significant advantage for Abyss team building.

Whales who refresh stamina daily can farm indefinitely. However, even whales face the bottleneck of purple ore from Domain Lv.7 — stamina refreshes increase green/blue ore income but purple ore drop rates cap at 20% per run. The purple ore gate is real for everyone.

Patch budget: A typical patch gives F2P players ~5,040 stamina (720/day x 7 days x 6 weeks). If you spend 30% of that on protocores (1,512 stamina), you get roughly 315 green-equivalent ore — enough for one +15 and one +12 SSR protocore. Plan your protocore upgrades around this hard constraint.

FINAL PRIORITY ORDER

强化优先级总结

When resources are tight (and they always are), follow this priority order for a 3-team roster of 9 operatives:

1
Main DPS Slot 3 (CRIT Rate) — +15 first. This is your single biggest damage multiplier. Every DPS operative needs CRIT Rate as high as possible.
2
Main DPS Slot 4 (CRIT DMG) — +15 second. Pairs with CRIT Rate. The 60/120 rule applies here.
3
Main DPS Slot 5 (Elemental DMG%) — +12 minimum. Elemental damage is multiplicative with crit.
4
Sub-DPS Slot 3 — +12. Sub-DPS benefits from CRIT but not as urgently as main DPS.
5
Main DPS remaining slots — +12. ATK%, Energy Regen, and filler slots.
6
Support Slot 6 (Energy Regen) — +9. Supports need ER for faster ultimates. +9 is sufficient.
7-9
Remaining support slots — +6. Anything beyond +6 on pure supports is diminishing returns.

The Bottom Line: A +12 on your main DPS is worth more than a +15 on your support. Always prioritize damage-dealing slots first. The material cost to go from +12 to +15 on a single protocore (216 green eq.) could instead take two support protocores from +0 to +9. Allocate materials where they deal damage.

Remember: 28 green + 26 blue + 43 purple = 600 green eq. = one +15 SSR protocore. Spend wisely.
