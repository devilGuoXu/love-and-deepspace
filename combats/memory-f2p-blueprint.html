1 — F2P Reality: The Math of Free

Here is the number that defines your entire F2P experience: 35,000. That is roughly how many gems a free player earns per patch when clearing all sources — dailies, events, Abyss resets, log-in bonuses. Converted to pulls, that is about 30-40 wishes every six weeks.

Do the math: at 75 wishes for soft pity and 140 for hard pity, an F2P account hits SSR pity roughly every 2 patches. That means in a year of consistent play, you will own about 6-8 SSR memories. That is your budget. Every pull you spend on a banner you do not need is a pull you cannot use for a banner that transforms your account.

The goal of this guide is simple: turn those 6-8 SSRs into three functional Abyss teams using free SSR memories, high-rank SR replacements, and the discipline to skip everything that is not part of the plan. This is not a guide to having the best account. It is a guide to having an account that can clear content.

2 — Free SSR Memories: What's Actually Good

Not all free SSRs are created equal. Some are account-defining cornerstones. Others are stat sticks you build only if forced. Here is every source of free SSR memories and the ones worth your resources.

Standard Banner SSRs: You get one free SSR from the beginner banner (50 pulls). After that, standard banner pity builds slowly through free standard tickets from events, leveling, and the exchange shop. Expect one standard SSR every 3-4 months. The standard pool includes most launch SSRs. Priority: Solar pairs for your main operative first, then Lunar memories that match your primary element.

Event SSRs: Limited events often include a free SSR memory as a milestone reward. These are guaranteed, time-gated, and generally well-tuned. Always complete the event to claim these. They are the highest-value free resources in the game because they cost zero pulls.

Shop SSRs (Wishing Well): The Wishing Well exchange allows you to purchase one SSR memory per rotation using duplicate currency. These are expensive (180 Starlight) but represent a guaranteed, targeted SSR. Save your Starlight for Solar pair completion.

S-TIER — BUILD FIRST
Standard SSR Solar Pair (any operative)
The 2-set Solar bonus is the single biggest power spike in the game. Whichever Solar pair drops first becomes your main team's core. Level to 70, protocore to +12, and this clears every floor that supports that element.
Viability: 95%
A-TIER — STRONG FREEBIE
Event SSR Lunar (matching element)
Event SSRs usually feature a meta Lunar memory with solid base stats and a usable skill. They cost no pulls and fill a critical slot in your second or third team. Check the passive before building — some event SSRs have generic buffs that fit any team.
Viability: 85%
B-TIER — FILLER ONLY
Shop SSR (off-element / support)
Shop SSRs are expensive and usually feature off-element or support-focused memories. Only buy these when you need exactly one more memory to complete a Solar set for your third team. Do not buy shop SSRs proactively.
Viability: 65%
C-TIER — AVOID IF POSSIBLE
Standard Banner SSR (wrong element)
Pity can give you an SSR that does not match any of your operatives' primary elements. Level it to 50 for the stat stick and stop. Do not invest protocores. It will be replaced by the first on-element SSR or high-rank SR you pull.
Viability: 40%

3 — SR Memory Value: The F2P Backbone

Here is the truth that spenders do not want to admit: some SR memories at R3 outperform R0 SSR memories. The reason is simple. At R3 (four copies), an SR's passive and stat bonuses often eclipse an SSR's base values. And SR memories are vastly easier to rank up because they share a pull pool with higher drop rates.

Every F2P player should treat the following SRs as irreplaceable core memories, not temporary fillers. An R5 SR Solar pair at level 70 with +12 protocores clears content that a level 60 R0 SSR Solar pair cannot touch.

4 — 3-Team Blueprint: 6-Month Milestone Plan

Building three Abyss teams as F2P is not about having all three at once. It is about sequenced investment. Month 1: one strong team. Month 3: a second functional team. Month 6: a third coverage team. Here is the exact path.

M1
Month 1 — Team 1: Core Operative
Build your main DPS operative with the best memories available. Use your first SSR (standard beginner banner) as the Solar pair anchor. Fill Lunar slots with the best SRs you have, prioritizing on-element and ATK-scaling memories. Get this team to level 60 minimum. This single team clears Floors 1-6 alone.
Target: 1 SSR Solar pair (level 60), 2-4 SR Lunar (level 50-60), +9 protocores on DPS. Investment: ~60% of monthly resources.
M3
Month 3 — Team 2: Second Operative
By now you have either pulled or purchased a second SSR. This is your second team's core. The rest of the team is SR backbone — R3+ SR Solar pairs and Lunar SRs. This team does not need to be as strong as Team 1. It needs to survive its assigned Abyss wave and contribute damage.
Target: 1 SSR or SR R5 Solar pair (level 60-70), 2-3 SR Lunar (level 50), +9 protocores on DPS, +6 on supports. Investment: ~30% of monthly resources.
M6
Month 6 — Team 3: Coverage Filler
The third team is your flex slot, built to cover Stellactrum weaknesses on Floor 9-10. This team uses whatever SSRs you have accumulated plus high-rank SRs. Elemental coverage matters more than raw power here. If you have an off-element SSR that fits a missing color slot, this is where it goes.
Target: Any SSR Solar or SR R5 pair (level 50-60), fill Lunar with correct element, +6-9 protocores. Investment: ~10% of monthly resources.
Month 1: Team 1 Month 3: Team 2 Month 6: Team 3

5 — Limited Banner Strategy: Pick Your Spots

This is the most important section of this guide. Your entire F2P account lives or dies by your limited banner decisions. Every pull spent on the wrong banner is a month of progress delayed. Here is the exact discipline:

Save for 1-2 S-tier limited banners per year. An S-tier limited banner is one where the limited SSR is either: (1) the best-in-slot Solar memory for your main operative, or (2) a universally strong Lunar memory that fits every team. That is it. Everything else is a skip.

Always have 140 pulls saved before pulling on any limited banner. Hard pity at 140 pulls is your guarantee. If you do not have 140 pulls banked, do not pull. The worst feeling in the game is hitting hard pity at 140 and coming up short. Save first. Pull second.

Never pull on the standard banner with gems. Standard banner tickets come free from events, leveling rewards, and monthly shop. Using paid gems on the standard banner is the single most wasteful thing an F2P player can do. Those 140 pulls on standard could have been a guaranteed limited SSR.

Pro tip: Track your pity counter. If you are at 65+ pulls on a limited banner with no SSR, the next 10-pull has a dramatically increased SSR rate. Use this knowledge to decide whether to spend or skip — but only if you have the full 140 guarantee banked.

Memory Blueprint Summary: Build one team at a time, not three at once. Free SSRs from events and standard pity are your cornerstones. R3+ SR memories are your reliable backbone. Save 140 pulls for exactly 1-2 limited banners per year. Follow this plan for 6 months and you will have three Abyss-ready teams — without spending a single dollar.
