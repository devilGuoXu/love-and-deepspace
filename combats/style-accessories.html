STYLE GUIDE — COSMETICS

Accessories & Makeup Guide

Complete guide to cosmetic customization — how to unlock, combine, and apply accessories and makeup for perfect photo mode shots.

ENTRY_#STY_052026.07.02COSMETICS

How to Read This: New players should focus on the unlock priority section. Veterans can jump to combination guides for advanced styling.

Accessory Types & Unlock Methods

Accessories fall into categories: Headwear: Hats, crowns, hair clips, animal ears. Most are event-limited. Unlock via event shops or battle pass tiers. Eyewear: Glasses, monocles, eye patches. Some are permanent (battle pass), most are event-limited. Jewelry: Necklaces, earrings, bracelets. Usually tied to outfit sets — buying an outfit often includes matching jewelry. Held Items: Fans, books, flowers, weapons (non-combat). Event and battle pass rewards. Special Effects: Aura glows, particle effects, floating elements. The rarest category — usually anniversary or high-tier battle pass rewards. Unlock priority: Special effects > Headwear > Held Items > Jewelry > Eyewear. Special effects are used in every photo; eyewear is situational. Prioritize items that work across multiple outfits and operatives.

Makeup System Guide

The makeup system lets you customize: Lip Color: 12 shades across red, pink, nude, and bold categories. Each has matte, gloss, and satin finishes. Eye Shadow: 8 palettes with 3-color combinations. Unlocked through event shops and battle pass. Blush: 6 shades placement options (cheeks, nose, temples). Subtle but impactful for warmth. Eyelashes: 5 styles from natural to dramatic. Event rewards only. Color theory tips: Warm lip colors (coral, peach) pair with cool eye shadows (taupe, lavender). Cool lip colors (berry, rose) pair with warm eye shadows (bronze, gold). Neutral makeup (nude lips, brown shadow) works with any outfit. Match makeup intensity to the photo mood — bold makeup for dramatic shots, natural for casual scenes. The most versatile makeup setup: nude matte lip, warm brown eye shadow, peachy blush.

Operative Color Palettes: Xavier — cool tones (silver, lavender, pale blue) match his light aesthetic. Zayne — icy tones (white, pale pink, frost blue) complement his ice theme. Rafayel — warm tones (gold, coral, deep red) echo his fire element. Sylus — dark tones (burgundy, charcoal, deep purple) match his dark aesthetic. Using the correct color palette for each operative makes photos look 2x more cohesive. Bonus: matching accessory metal (gold for Rafayel, silver for Xavier/Zayne, gunmetal for Sylus) adds a professional polish.

Accessory Investment Guide

Accessories cost money or event currency. Here is the value ranking: S-Tier (must buy): Aura effects, universal headbands, classic jewelry sets — used in 80%+ of photos. A-Tier (recommended): Themed headwear (crowns, animal ears), statement necklaces, handheld flowers — used in 50% of photos. B-Tier (situational): Eyewear, niche headwear (Santa hat, bunny ears), specialty held items — used in 20% of photos. C-Tier (skip): Recolor accessories, items locked to one operative, items that clip through outfits. Free alternatives: Many basic accessories are unlockable through companion affinity rewards. Leveling affinity to 30+ with each operative gives 2-3 free accessories. Do not spend event currency on accessories that you can unlock through gameplay.

Photo Mode Application

How to apply accessories and makeup effectively: Accessory placement: Accessories should frame the face, not obscure it. Place headwear above the hairline, not across the forehead. Earrings should be visible — adjust the head angle to show them. Makeup visibility: Makeup shows best in close-up and medium shots. Full-body shots do not display makeup details. Take separate close-up and full-body shots rather than trying to do everything in one frame. Lighting interaction: Makeup reflects light differently depending on the finish. Glossy lips need direct light to shine. Matte makeup works in any lighting. Test your makeup setup under 2-3 different lighting presets before settling on a look. Save preset: The game lets you save accessory and makeup presets. Create one preset per operative with their best look. This saves 5 minutes of setup time per photo session.

Summary: S-Tier accessories = auras + universal headwear. Neutral makeup (nude lip, brown shadow, peach blush) works on everyone. Match color palettes to each operative. Create one preset per operative. Accessories should frame the face — not obscure it. Free accessories from affinity rewards are value kings.
