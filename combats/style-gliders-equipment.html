Gliders & Equipment Style Guide

This guide covers every customizable piece of equipment — how to acquire skins, how to coordinate them with outfits, and how to build a cohesive combat look.

Equipment Customization Overview

Equipment customization in Love and Deepspace exists at the intersection of combat and fashion. Unlike outfits, which are primarily cosmetic with no gameplay impact, equipment skins are pure visual customization applied to functional items. This distinction makes them a unique category in the style system.

The equipment customization system includes two main categories: glider skins (which change the appearance of your glider during flight and combat) and weapon skins (which change the visual appearance of your equipped weapon). Both categories are acquired through separate means — some overlap with outfit acquisition methods, but many equipment skins have their own dedicated sources.

Equipment skins are character-independent — when you acquire a glider or weapon skin, it is available for use by any operative (including the Hunter). This makes them an efficient style investment: one skin purchase benefits your entire roster rather than a single character.

Glider Skins Complete Catalog

Glider skins are the most visible equipment customization in the game. Your glider appears in every combat encounter and during overworld exploration, making it the equipment piece with the highest screen time.

Standard Glider Skins. The base glider skin is unlocked by default. Additional standard-glide skins have been released through various methods. Standard glider skins typically change the color and material of the glider wings while maintaining the same basic shape. They are the most affordable glider customization option and often the most accessible.

Special Glider Skins. Limited-edition glider skins released during events feature unique shapes and animations beyond simple color changes. These are the most desirable glider skins due to their visual distinctiveness. A special glider skin might transform the standard wing silhouette into a completely different shape — ethereal energy wings, mechanical constructs, organic forms that match event themes.

Operative-Specific Glider Effects. While most glider skins are universal, certain glider skins feature effects that respond differently depending on which operative is using them. These subtle interactions reward players who pay attention to detail and add replay value to glider skin ownership.

Rarity and Collectibility. Glider skins follow a rarity structure similar to outfits: standard (permanently available), limited (event-exclusive), and battle pass (seasonal). Limited glider skins are the most collectible because they may never return. Battle pass glider skins are the best value because they are included in the pass alongside other rewards.

Weapon Skins Complete Catalog

Weapon skins modify the visual appearance of your equipped weapon. Unlike glider skins, which are always visible, weapon skins are most noticeable during combat — which is also when you are most focused on gameplay rather than appearance. However, for style-conscious players, a coordinated weapon skin completes the combat look.

Weapon Skin Categories. Weapon skins fall into several categories: color variants (recolor of the base weapon with different trim and accent colors), material variants (same shape but different visual materials — metallic, crystalline, energy-based), and complete redesigns (entirely new weapon models with unique animations and effects). Most weapon skins released to date are color or material variants, with complete redesigns being rare and typically tied to major events.

Weapon Skin Visibility. Weapon skins are visible in third-person combat view, in the equip screen, and in photo mode when the weapon is drawn. They are not visible in menu screens or most memory card illustrations. This limited visibility makes weapon skins a lower priority for players who style primarily for menus and photos rather than combat.

Operative Weapon Differences. Each operative has a unique weapon model, so weapon skins are operative-specific rather than universal. This means that equipping a weapon skin for Xavier does not affect Zayne's weapon appearance. This oper-specifc nature reduces the value proposition of weapon skins compared to glider skins, which benefit all characters.

Outfit-to-Equipment Coordination

The highest expression of style in Love and Deepspace is full coordination: outfit, glider skin, and weapon skin that work together as a cohesive look. Achieving this requires understanding how different visual elements interact.

Color Coordination. The simplest coordination approach is matching color palettes. A dark outfit pairs with a dark glider and weapon. A light outfit pairs with light equipment. Metallic accents should coordinate — gold-trimmed outfits look best with gold-accented equipment, while silver-trimmed outfits suit silver equipment. This color matching creates a unified visual presentation that reads as intentional and polished.

Thematic Coordination. Beyond basic color matching, thematic coordination creates the most impressive looks. An outfit with celestial motifs (stars, moons, cosmic patterns) pairs best with a glider skin that has celestial effects. A formal outfit looks best with a sleek, minimal glider and refined weapon. An event-themed outfit pairs with event-themed equipment. Thematic coordination requires more investment (you need both the outfit and the matching equipment) but produces the most satisfying results.

Operative Personality Coordination. The most nuanced approach to coordination considers the operative's personality and fashion identity. Xavier's celestial elegance calls for luminous glider skins and refined weapon skins. Zayne's clinical precision suits clean, sharp equipment designs. Rafayel's artistic flamboyance pairs with dramatic, unconventional equipment. Sylus's dark authority commands imposing, powerful equipment aesthetics. Matching equipment to operative personality rather than outfit color creates looks that feel character-authentic.

Photo Mode Considerations. In photo mode, equipment is often less visible than outfits and accessories, but it still contributes to the overall frame. A glowing glider in the background, a weapon resting at the character's side — these details add depth to photos. Consider equipment visibility in your photo composition when styling for screenshots.

Acquisition Methods & Costs

Equipment skins are acquired through several methods, each with different cost structures and accessibility levels.

Battle Pass (Best Value). The battle pass is the most cost-effective source of equipment skins. Each season typically includes one glider skin and one weapon skin at the premium tier. At approximately $10 per season, the battle pass delivers equipment skins at a fraction of their standalone cost while also providing other resources.

Limited Shop (Moderate Value). Equipment skins occasionally appear in the limited shop alongside event outfits. A standalone glider skin typically costs 980–1680 crystals ($10–20 equivalent if purchasing top-up). Weapon skins are slightly cheaper at 680–1280 crystals. Buying equipment from the limited shop during an event you are already pulling on adds marginal value.

Event Rewards (Free). Some events include equipment skins as free rewards in the event shop or as milestone rewards for event participation. These are the most valuable equipment skins because they cost nothing beyond playing the event. Always check event reward lists for equipment skins before spending crystals.

Gacha (Variable Value). Equipment skins can appear in limited banners as secondary prizes alongside memories. Pulling specifically for equipment skins is not recommended due to the unpredictable cost. However, if you are pulling for memories anyway, receiving equipment skins as side rewards is a pleasant bonus.

Permanent Shop (Standard Value). Standard equipment skins are available in the permanent shop for Stardust or other permanent currencies. These are the lowest priority because they are always available and do not offer the exclusivity or visual impact of limited skins.

Purchase Priority Ranking

With limited style resources, prioritizing equipment skin purchases is important. This ranking helps you decide which equipment skins to acquire and in what order.

Tier 1 — Battle Pass Equipment. Always acquire battle pass equipment skins if you purchase the pass. They represent the best value and often have high-quality designs. The battle pass glider skin alone is worth a significant portion of the pass cost.

Tier 2 — Free Event Equipment. Never skip free equipment skins from events. Even if you do not plan to use them immediately, free equipment skins add to your collection and may become valuable for future coordination.

Tier 3 — Limited Shop Glider Skins. Limited shop glider skins are worth purchasing if (a) they coordinate with an outfit you own or plan to own, (b) they are exclusive and unlikely to return, and (c) you have the crystals to spare after securing your high-priority pulls.

Tier 4 — Battle Pass Weapon Skins. Weapon skins have lower visibility than glider skins, making them a lower priority within the battle pass. However, they are still good value as part of the pass bundle.

Tier 5 — Limited Shop Weapon Skins. Weapon skins purchased separately are the lowest priority equipment investment. Only purchase standalone weapon skins if you have surplus crystals and a specific coordination need.

Tier 6 — Permanent Shop Equipment. Standard equipment skins that are permanently available should be the last priority. They will always be there when you have excess currency, and they lack the visual impact of limited designs.

F2P-Friendly Options

Free-to-play players need not abandon equipment style entirely. Several options exist for building a respectable equipment collection without spending money.

Free Event Equipment. As mentioned, many events include equipment skins in the free reward track. Participating in every event ensures you collect these over time. A dedicated F2P player can accumulate 3–5 free glider skins and 4–6 free weapon skins per year through events alone.

Standard Currency Equipment. Equipment skins purchased with Stardust or other free currencies are accessible to F2P players who save their standard currency. While these skins are not as visually exciting as limited skins, they provide variety and coordination options.

Battle Pass Free Track. Even the free track of the battle pass occasionally includes equipment skins, though less frequently than the premium track. Always claim these if available.

Coordination with Free Outfits. The best F2P strategy is coordinating free equipment with free outfits. The standard glider skin paired with a well-accessorized free outfit can look intentional rather than default. Adding a single free event equipment skin to a free outfit creates a coordinated look at zero cost.

Future Equipment Style Potential

The equipment customization system has significant room for expansion. Based on industry trends and game trajectory, here are likely future developments.

Glider Animation Effects. Future glider skins may include unique movement animations — trail effects, wing transformations during specific moves, or reactive effects that respond to combat conditions. These would increase the visual appeal and purchase motivation for glider skins.

Weapon Combat Effects. Weapon skins may evolve to include unique attack effects — different projectile colors, hit effects, or kill animations. This would significantly increase weapon skin desirability by making them visible during the most engaging parts of combat.

Equipment Sets. A potential future system could offer set bonuses for equipping matching outfit, glider, and weapon skins. These bonuses could be cosmetic (special transformation on full set) or gameplay-adjacent (minor effects in photo mode). Set bonuses would create strong purchase incentives for coordinated equipment collections.

Interactive Equipment. Future equipment may include interactive elements — gliders that respond to terrain, weapons that change appearance based on combat performance, or equipment that evolves over time with usage. These would add depth to equipment ownership beyond one-time purchase.

Pro Styling Tip: When building a coordinated look, start with the outfit, then choose a glider skin that matches its dominant color accent, then select a weapon skin that shares at least one color with the glider. This three-layer coordination creates visual harmony without requiring exact matching — and works even with free equipment.

Summary: Equipment customization in Love and Deepspace includes glider skins and weapon skins that extend your fashion identity into combat. Glider skins are higher priority due to greater visibility and universal character compatibility. Weapon skins are operative-specific and less visible, making them lower priority. Full coordination — outfit, glider, and weapon — creates the most impressive looks through color, thematic, or personality matching. Battle pass equipment offers the best value, followed by free event equipment, limited shop gliders, and finally weapon skins. F2P players can build a respectable collection through event participation and standard currency purchases. The future of equipment style includes animation effects, combat effects, equipment sets, and interactive features that will increase the depth and appeal of equipment customization.
