Prediction Methodology

Our predictions are based on four data sources: (1) Historical event cadence — measuring the average gap between event types across all of 2025-2026. Major events occur every 31.4 days on average (standard deviation: 4.2 days). Seasonal events align with real-world holidays. Anniversary events occur on fixed dates. (2) Banner schedule patterns — limited banners run for 21 days, with the next banner announced 3-5 days before the current one ends. This creates predictable windows. (3) Developer communication cadence — major updates are announced 2-3 weeks in advance via livestreams, usually on the last Friday of the month. (4) Industry benchmarks — comparing against similar gacha games' event schedules for holiday planning.

Q3 2026 Forecast (Jul-Sep)

Late July — Summer Event (Confidence: 95%). The annual summer event is the most predictable content in LADS. Expect: exclusive swimsuit outfits (3-4 outfits in the shop), a free SSR memory with water/ocean theme, beach volleyball minigame, enhanced stamina regeneration (summer bonus), and 1000+ gems across the event duration. The summer event historically runs 21 days and offers the best non-anniversary rewards of the year. Save 14,400+ diamonds for this banner — summer limited memories are consistently meta-relevant.

Late August — Story Chapter Update (Confidence: 85%). Version 2.2 or 2.3 will include a new main story chapter. Story chapter updates always include: 5-7 new story stages, 1-2 new domain difficulty levels, a new SSR memory for the featured operative, and 500-700 gems from story completion. Story chapters are permanent content but the accompanying event (login bonuses, limited missions) runs for 14 days. Prioritize the limited missions before they expire — they offer 200-300 bonus gems.

Mid-September — 2.5 Anniversary / Festival Event (Confidence: 75%). The 2.5 version update is expected to be a mid-cycle celebration similar to the 1.5 update in March 2026. Expected content: a new event format (potentially the first "event archive" system), a special banner with rate-up on older limited memories, login bonus with 10 free pulls, and QoL updates based on community feedback. The 2.5 update is likely the most unpredictable on this list — confidence is lower because the developers may try something entirely new.

Q4 2026 Forecast (Oct-Dec)

October — Halloween Event (Confidence: 90%). Halloween events have been consistent across both years. Expect: dark/cosmic-themed limited outfits, a spooky minigame (haunted house exploration), a dark-element SSR memory (likely Sylus or Xavier), and 800-900 gems. Halloween events are mid-sized — not as big as summer or winter but larger than standard story events. The limited outfits are usually among the most popular cosmetics in the game.

November — Thanksgiving / Harvest Event (Confidence: 70%). The November slot is the least consistent historically. In 2025, November had a rerun event + a small login event. There is no clear Thanksgiving theme in LADS (the game is Chinese-developed), but November often features a "harvest" or "gathering" themed event with boosted material drops and a login campaign. Expect a moderate event at best — this is a good month to save resources for the December winter event.

December — Winter Event (Confidence: 95%). The winter event is the second most important seasonal event after anniversary. Historically: exclusive winter outfits (often xia and elegant designs), a free SSR memory (ice or light element), a snow-themed exploration zone (limited time), holiday login bonus with 5-10 free pulls, and 1000+ gems. The winter event is also when the developers typically release the year's most powerful memories, anticipating the anniversary spending season. Save aggressively for December.

Q4 strategy: October is a moderate spending month (Halloween cosmetics are popular). November is a save month — do not pull on November banners unless you have a guaranteed pity ready for December. December is a major spending month — expect the winter limited SSR to be top-tier. Plan your diamond budget to have 28,800+ diamonds banked before December 1st (enough for hard pity on two limited banners if needed).

H1 2027 Outlook (Jan-Jun)

January — New Year / Anniversary Prep (Confidence: 80%). January events are typically smaller as the developers prepare for the anniversary. Expect: a login event (10 free pulls cumulative), a small story epilogue, and a "pre-anniversary" limited banner with a high-value memory. January is a hoarding month — do not spend diamonds unless the banner is absolutely essential for your main operative.

February — 3rd Anniversary (Confidence: 90%). The anniversary is the biggest event of the year. Based on the 2nd Anniversary template, expect: 21-day multi-stage event (story → challenge → co-op raid), 1200+ gems, free SSR selector (all standard pool SSRs), double top-up reset, enhanced login rewards (20+ free pulls over the duration), exclusive anniversary outfit, limited furniture set, and a new game mode announcement. The anniversary is when you spend everything — this is the best value content of the entire year.

March-June — Standard Cycle (Confidence: 75%). Post-anniversary, the event cadence returns to the standard 28-35 day cycle. Expect: 1-2 story chapter updates, 1 seasonal event (Spring or Cherry Blossom themed), 1-2 challenge events, and 1-2 rerun events. The developers often test new event formats in these quieter months. Watch for experimental events in March-April — they indicate what may become standard in the next anniversary cycle.

Long-term outlook: Based on industry trends and the game's growth trajectory, we expect 2027 to introduce: cooperative raid events (guild-based), an event archive system for permanent access to past event stories, increased rewards (targeting 1500 gems per major event by end of 2027), and potentially a new permanent game mode tied to the event system. The developers have indicated interest in expanding the social features of events, so look for more co-op and competitive elements in future events.

Banner & Rerun Projections

Limited banner reruns follow a predictable pattern: initial banner → 6-8 month gap → first rerun. Second reruns are rarer and occur 12+ months after the initial run. Based on this pattern, the following limited memories are due for reruns in the forecast period: "Ocean's Embrace" (Jul 2025 original — rerun likely Jul-Aug 2026), "Frost Monarch" (Dec 2025 original — rerun likely Nov-Dec 2026), "Starlight Serenade" (Feb 2026 original — rerun likely Sep-Oct 2026).

New limited banners rotate through the four operatives in a predictable sequence: Xavier → Zayne → Rafayel → Sylus → (repeat). Each operative typically receives 3-4 new limited banners per year. The summer event will likely feature a new Sylus or Xavier water-element memory. The winter event will likely feature a new Zayne or Rafayel ice/light-element memory. The anniversary banner traditionally offers a rerun of the previous year's anniversary limited memory at rate-up.

Resource Hoarding Plan

1
Set your pull budget
Decide how many limited banners you want to pull on per quarter. F2P: 1 banner every 2-3 months (save 14,400 diamonds). Dolphin: 1 banner per month (monthly pass + BP). Whale: unlimited. Be realistic about your income — do not plan for banners you cannot guarantee.
2
Mark priority banners
The summer banner (Jul), winter banner (Dec), and anniversary banner (Feb) are the three most important limited banners each year. These memories are consistently meta-relevant and have the best banner bonuses. Everything else is secondary — skip them if you need to save.
3
Maintain a diamond reserve
Always keep 5,000 diamonds as an emergency reserve. This covers: unexpected favorite character banners, limited outfits you really want, stamina refills during double-drop events, and shop reset purchases. Never spend below 5,000 diamonds — this is your safety net.
4
Track your pity
Keep a running tally of your pity counter on the limited banner. At 60+ pity (soft pity range), you are close to a guaranteed SSR. If you are at high pity when a priority banner arrives, you can secure the limited SSR with minimal additional investment. Never waste high pity on non-priority banners.
5
Review monthly
The event forecast updates monthly. Review your hoarding plan against the updated forecast. Adjust your pull targets based on new information. The key to successful diamond management is flexibility — plans change when new data arrives.

The 14,400 rule: Never start pulling on a limited banner unless you have 14,400 diamonds saved. It takes 14,400 to guarantee hard pity (90 pulls × 160 diamonds). If you start pulling without a guarantee and run out of diamonds, you waste your pity progress when the banner ends. 14,400 is your go/no-go threshold. If you do not have it, do not start. This single rule is the difference between disciplined saving and wasteful spending.

Wildcards & Unknowns

Several factors could shift the predicted schedule. A surprise collaboration event (with another game or franchise) would insert an unscheduled event into the calendar. The developers have hinted at interest in collaborations, and the anniversary period is the most likely window. Game balance patches that significantly change the meta could delay or reshape planned events. Server stability issues or development delays (both common in live service games) could push events back by 1-3 weeks.

The most significant wildcard is the rumored "Event Archive" system. If implemented, this system would change how reruns work — potentially eliminating the need for rerun events entirely by making past event stories permanently accessible. This would free up 2-3 event slots per year currently occupied by reruns, potentially increasing the frequency of new events or introducing a new event type. Watch the 2.5 and 3.0 livestreams for announcements on this system.
