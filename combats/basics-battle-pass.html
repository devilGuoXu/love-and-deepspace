Battle Pass Guide

Free vs premium. What you get, what it costs, and whether it's worth your money.

ENTRY_#0302026.06.29BATTLE_PASS

Battle Pass Cost: $9.99 USD per season (60 days). Free track is available for all players. This guide assumes you want to know if the premium pass is worth it.

Free Track Rewards

The free track gives approximately 40% of total pass rewards. Key items: 10 limited tickets, 500 gems, memory XP packs, gold. You can reach max level on free track — premium players just get more per level.

Premium Track Rewards

Additional items over free: 15 limited tickets (total 25), 1,200 gems (total 1,700), exclusive memory, 2x all resources, profile frame. Total value: The premium track gives roughly $30 worth of gems and tickets for $10. It's the second-best value after the monthly pass.

Leveling Efficiency

Complete daily missions (200 XP/day) and weekly missions (1,500 XP/week). Battle Pass-specific missions give bonus XP. Playing 20 minutes/day will max the pass in 35-40 days. You have 60 days — plenty of time.

The Bottom Line: Battle Pass is good value if you play consistently. The exclusive memory alone is worth consideration — some pass memories are meta-defining. If you only want gems, the monthly pass is better. If you want everything, get both. Free track is viable for F2P but you miss the exclusive memory.
