Creator Spotlight Series

Community content creators who consistently produce high-quality guides, lore analysis, and entertainment content for Deepspace.

How to Read This: Browse by category — guide creators for gameplay help, lore creators for story analysis, and entertainment creators for fun content. All creators listed are active as of this writing.

Guide & Build Creators

These creators focus on gameplay mechanics, protocore math, and team building: Deepspace Theorycrafters (YouTube): The most detailed build guides with actual damage testing. They publish weekly tier lists and protocore efficiency rankings. Best for: endgame optimization. Stellactrum Scholars (Bilibili): Chinese-language creator with the fastest patch analysis. They datamine new memory sets and protocore values within hours of a patch dropping. Best for: early meta read. Hunter's Log (Twitter/X): Infographic-style quick guides. Perfect for a 30-second summary of a new memory or operative. Best for: quick reference. F2P Framework (YouTube): Dedicated F2P content. Zero paid memory reviews, budget team comps, and pull planning. Best for: F2P and low-spender accounts.

Lore & Story Creators

Deepspace has rich lore that rewards deep analysis: Mythos Weaver (YouTube): Timelines, character analysis, and theory videos. They connect seemingly unrelated story beats into coherent theories. Their series on the Protofield Era is essential reading for lore enthusiasts. Echoes of N109 (Podcast): Weekly podcast discussing story updates, character development, and speculation. Episodes run 45-60 minutes. Best for: commuters and multitaskers. Divided Light (Blog/Substack): Written lore analysis in long-form essays. Covers symbolism, literary references, and mythological parallels in the story. Best for: deep readers who prefer text over video.

Entertainment & Community: Photo Mode Masters (Instagram/Pixiv): The best photo mode creators. They share lighting setups, pose combinations, and edit tutorials. Follow for daily photo inspiration. Comic Dubs (YouTube Shorts/TikTok): Funny voice-over dubs of in-game scenes. Light entertainment for when you need a break from optimization. Collection Showcase (YouTube): Whale-level collection showcases. Useful for seeing how memory sets look at max level before investing. Also runs a "build rating" series where they review viewer-submitted builds. Supporting creators: All of these creators produce content for free. If you find their work valuable, consider: subscribing, leaving a comment, sharing their content, or supporting through membership/patreon if available.

How to Submit a Creator

This directory is community-maintained. If you know a creator who consistently produces high-quality Deepspace content, you can submit them for inclusion. Requirements: (1) Minimum 3 months of consistent Deepspace content. (2) Original analysis — not just reading patch notes. (3) Active within the last 30 days. (4) No toxic behavior, misinformation, or harassment. To submit, use the contact form with the creator's name, platform, content focus, and a sample of their best work. The directory is updated monthly. Inclusion is not guaranteed — quality over quantity.

Creator Ethics & Disclaimer

This directory is a fan resource. No creators have paid for inclusion. All listings are based on editorial assessment of content quality and consistency. Some creators may have affiliate links in their descriptions — this is standard practice and does not influence their inclusion here. If a listed creator becomes inactive or produces harmful content, they will be removed. This directory aims to highlight the best of the community, not to endorse every opinion a creator holds.
