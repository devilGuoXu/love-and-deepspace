Achievement Guide

Every achievement, every reward, every hidden unlock condition.

ENTRY_#023 2026.06.29 ACHIEVEMENTS

Achievement Value: Total one-time rewards from achievements exceed 3,000 gems, 15+ blue tickets, and exclusive titles. This is the largest one-time gem source in the game — completing the achievement list is a top priority for F2P players.

Achievement Categories

Battle Achievements
Clear Abyss floors, complete challenge modes, defeat bosses without taking damage. Total reward: ~1,200 gems. Priority: High. These come naturally from Abyss progression.
Collection Achievements
Collect memories, reach memory level milestones, unlock set bonuses. Total reward: ~800 gems. Priority: Medium. These accumulate over time — don't force them.
Affection Achievements
Reach affinity milestones with each operative, complete date events. Total reward: ~600 gems. Priority: Medium. Comes naturally from gift giving.
Exploration Achievements
Visit locations in the home/base, interact with objects, find hidden areas. Total reward: ~400 gems + exclusive titles. Priority: Low. Do these when you're bored.

Hidden Achievements

Untouchable: Clear a boss fight without taking any damage. Use Zayne's freeze + ranged combat. Easiest on Phantom Zayne (slow projectiles are easy to dodge).
Speed Demon: Clear Abyss Floor 1 in under 30 seconds. Use your best team with all ultimates on the first wave.
Collector's Instinct: Equip 6 memories from different sets on one operative. Easy to trigger — just mix sets for one battle.
Perfect Resonance: Trigger 3 different elemental resonances in one battle. Bring Xavier+Sylus+Zayne to a multi-wave fight and alternate pairs.

The Bottom Line: Achievements are the best one-time gem source in the game. Focus on battle achievements first (they pay the most). Hidden achievements are optional but rewarding. Use the achievement list as a progress tracker — if you're ever bored, check which achievements you're closest to completing.
