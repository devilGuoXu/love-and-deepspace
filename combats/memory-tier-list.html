Why Pull Priority Matters

Every limited banner asks the same question: do you pull or save? The answer depends on knowing which memories deliver account-defining power and which are skippable luxuries. Pull priority is the single most important concept for F2P and low-spending players because wishes are the scarcest resource in Love and Deepspace. You get roughly 80-100 limited wishes per patch as a F2P player, and hard pity for a single SSR is 70 wishes. That means you can guarantee at most one limited SSR every patch, sometimes two if you win the 50/50.

A well-planned pull strategy can double your account's combat effectiveness compared to impulsive pulling. The difference between an account that pulls strategically (S-tier memories for core operatives) and one that pulls for collection (any banner that looks appealing) is approximately 3-4 additional Abyss floors cleared per rotation, translating to 600-800 extra diamonds monthly. Over six months, that gap widens to 5,000+ diamonds — enough for an entire extra guaranteed SSR. Pull priority is not about being meta-slave; it is about maximizing your limited resources to clear content and earn more resources in a virtuous cycle.

Tier System

Memories are ranked across five tiers using three criteria: meta impact (how much the memory changes the operative's performance in endgame content), versatility (how many team compositions and content types the memory works in), and replacement availability (how easy it is to substitute the memory if missed).

S
Account-Defining — Must-PullLegendary memories that define entire team archetypes. They enable unique mechanics no other memory provides and elevate the operative to a new performance tier. If you skip these, your account will consistently underperform in content where they excel.
A
Excellent — Highly RecommendedPowerful memories that provide substantial combat upgrades. They have strong alternatives, so missing them is not account-breaking, but having them makes content noticeably easier. Pull if your main operative needs the upgrade.
B
Solid — Good ReplacementReliable memories that fill specific roles. They are outclassed by S and A tier options but serve as decent substitutes when better memories are not available. Not worth chasing, but worth building if acquired as a spook or early pity.
C
Niche — Situational OnlyMemories with narrow use cases. They excel in specific scenarios but fall short in general content. Only consider if you have excess wishes and want to min-max for a specific Abyss rotation or event boss.
D
Weak — SkipMemories that underperform relative to alternatives. They provide marginal upgrades at best and are often outclassed by standard-pool memories. Spend your wishes elsewhere.

S-Tier Memories

These memories stand above all others. If you see one of these on a limited banner, your default decision should be to pull unless you have a specific reason not to.

S-TIER MUST PULL
Xavier — Stellar Contract
Set Bonus: Light Seeker 4-piece. Why S-tier: Stellar Contract provides a 40% ATK buff stacking with skill use that transforms Xavier into the highest sustained DPS in the game. The buff has 100% uptime in combat, making it effectively a permanent damage multiplier. No other memory provides this magnitude of unconditional stat amplification. It enables Xavier to function as both main DPS and burst support in any team composition. Replacement if missed: Xavier's standard-pool memories provide approximately 60% of Stellar Contract's damage contribution. You will notice the gap in every Abyss rotation where Light is favored.
S-TIER MUST PULL
Sylus — Twilight Oath
Set Bonus: Execute Protocol 4-piece. Why S-tier: Twilight Oath increases Sylus's execute threshold by 15% and applies a team-wide 10% damage buff when the execute triggers against elite and boss enemies. This memory single-handedly makes Sylus the best Abyss boss killer in the game. The execute mechanic bypasses traditional DPS checks and creates a safety margin on tightly-timed floors. No other memory offers boss-execute mechanics — it is a unique effect with zero substitutes. Replacement if missed: Sylus without Twilight Oath lacks the execute breakpoint needed for floor 12 clears. His damage drops by approximately 25% against endgame bosses.
S-TIER MUST PULL
Zayne — Permafrost Covenant
Set Bonus: Frost Weaver 4-piece. Why S-tier: Permafrost Covenant extends freeze duration by 2 seconds, applies freeze to nearby enemies within a 5-meter radius, and increases Frost damage taken by 20% for 8 seconds after freeze. This memory converts Zayne from a single-target CC specialist into an AoE CC monster with team-wide damage amplification. In Abyss floors with multiple elite enemies, Permafrost Covenant provides more clear-speed value than any other memory in the game. Replacement if missed: Zayne's base kit still freezes single targets, but the AoE extension and damage amp are irreplaceable. Expect 2-3 fewer Abyss floors cleared without it.

A-Tier Memories

High-value memories that are excellent additions to any account. They have alternatives available, so they are not mandatory, but they will noticeably improve your account if pulled.

A-TIER HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
Rafayel — Inferno Waltz
Set Bonus: Inferno Waltz 4-piece. Why A-tier: Inferno Waltz doubles burn damage and spreads burn to 2 additional nearby targets within 4 meters. This memory makes Rafayel the fastest wave-clearer in the game, excelling in Bounty missions, event stages, and Abyss add-floors. It drops from S-tier because single-target boss performance remains Rafayel's weakness even with the memory. The burn spread is invaluable for farming efficiency but does not solve his boss DPS ceiling. Replacement if missed: Crimson Oath 4-piece + Storm Chaser 2-piece provides roughly 85% of the clear speed. Inferno Waltz is a luxury for players who value farming speed.
A-TIER HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
Xavier — Radiant Edge
Set Bonus: Light Seeker 4-piece. Why A-tier: Radiant Edge provides a 25% Light damage boost on Resonance skill use and reduces enemy Light resistance by 15% for 6 seconds. It is an excellent damage memory that competes directly with Stellar Contract. The resistance reduction is valuable for team compositions with multiple Light damage dealers. It drops to A-tier because Stellar Contract exists — if you already have Stellar Contract, Radiant Edge provides diminishing returns as the ATK buff and resistance shred do not stack optimally. Replacement if missed: Any standard-pool Light damage memory substitutes at approximately 80% effectiveness.
A-TIER HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
Sylus — Shadow Sovereign
Set Bonus: Execute Protocol 4-piece. Why A-tier: Shadow Sovereign grants Sylus an additional dodge charge and makes his next attack after dodging deal 30% increased Dark damage. It significantly improves Sylus's mobility and burst window damage. The additional dodge charge is particularly valuable against highly aggressive bosses in Abyss floor 11 and above. It drops to A-tier because Twilight Oath's execute mechanic is more impactful for boss clear times. Replacement if missed: Sylus's base dodge kit works fine; Shadow Sovereign is a comfort-and-damage upgrade rather than a necessity.
A-TIER HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
Zayne — Frost Barrier
Set Bonus: Frost Weaver 4-piece. Why A-tier: Frost Barrier creates a shield equal to 15% of Zayne's max HP when freeze is applied and increases party defense by 20% while the shield is active. It transforms Zayne into a sustain-support hybrid, enabling safer clears of high-damage Abyss floors. It drops to A-tier because Permafrost Covenant's offensive value generally outperforms defensive utility in a DPS-focused endgame. Replacement if missed: Standard support memories provide comparable defensive value. Frost Barrier is best for players who prioritize survival over speed.

B-Tier Memories

Solid memories that fill specific slots in team compositions. They are serviceable when you have spare wishes but should never be your primary pull target during a banner.

Rafayel — Flame Dance B TIER
Set Bonus: Inferno Waltz 4-piece. Why B-tier: Flame Dance grants 25% dodge recovery speed and increases burn damage by 10% for 4 seconds after a perfect dodge. The dodge recovery is a quality-of-life improvement that helps Rafayel maintain burn uptime against aggressive bosses. However, the conditional requirement (perfect dodge) makes the buff inconsistent, and the 10% burn damage increase is modest compared to Inferno Waltz's unconditional burn spread. Replacement if missed: Inferno Waltz (A-tier) provides better overall value. Flame Dance is a comfort pick that does not substantially change Rafayel's performance.
Xavier — Light's Guard B TIER
Set Bonus: Light Seeker 4-piece. Why B-tier: Light's Guard provides a 20% DEF boost and converts 10% of DEF to ATK for 8 seconds after using a defensive skill. The DEF-to-ATK conversion is an interesting mechanical twist that rewards defensive play, but Xavier thrives on aggressive burst rotations, not defensive waiting. The memory's value depends entirely on content — it excels in survival-oriented boss fights but underperforms in timed DPS checks. Replacement if missed: Stellar Contract and Radiant Edge both outperform Light's Guard in general content. Only consider if you consistently run content where survivability is the bottleneck.
Sylus — Night Stalker B TIER
Set Bonus: Execute Protocol 4-piece. Why B-tier: Night Stalker increases Sylus's movement speed by 15% out of combat and grants 15% CRIT rate for 5 seconds after entering combat from stealth. The movement speed is valuable for exploration and Bounty missions but irrelevant in Abyss. The CRIT rate buff is a genuine damage increase, but the stealth requirement makes it unreliable in group content where aggro is unpredictable. Replacement if missed: Twilight Oath and Shadow Sovereign both provide more reliable combat value. Night Stalker is a situational pick that rewards specific playstyles.
Zayne — Glacial Shard B TIER
Set Bonus: Frost Weaver 4-piece. Why B-tier: Glacial Shard causes frozen enemies to take 15% increased damage from all sources and explode on death, dealing 30% AoE Frost damage. The on-death explosion provides decent wave clear for add-packed floors, and the damage amp is universal. However, the explosion only triggers on kill, making it useless against bosses and single elite enemies. Replacement if missed: Permafrost Covenant and Frost Barrier both offer more consistent value across content types. Glacial Shard is a wave-clear luxury for players who already have Zayne's core memories.

New Player Priority Path

If you are starting fresh or within your first three months, follow this pull priority roadmap to maximize account growth efficiency. Each phase builds on the previous one, ensuring you always have a combat-ready team while saving for the next milestone.

1
Month 1: Main Operative S-Tier Memory
Choose your preferred operative (Xavier recommended for beginners) and pull for their S-tier memory. Xavier's Stellar Contract or Sylus's Twilight Oath are the strongest starting points. Do not split wishes across multiple operatives — focus everything on one S-tier memory. This single memory will carry you through story content, early Abyss floors 1-6, and Bounty missions. Level it to max as soon as possible. Your entire account power in month 1 revolves around this one memory.
2
Month 2: Second Operative S-Tier Memory
After securing your main operative's S-tier memory, switch focus to a second operative who complements your main. If you started with Xavier (Light), consider Sylus (Dark) or Zayne (Frost) for elemental coverage. Pull for their S-tier memory to give you a second powerful team core. With two S-tier memories, you can clear Abyss floors 7-9 and begin tackling Abyss floor 10. Your roster now has two combat-ready operatives covering at least two elements.
3
Month 3+: Fill Gaps with A-Tier Memories
With both team cores established, shift to filling gaps with A-tier memories that address specific weaknesses. If your Abyss progression is blocked by lack of AoE clear, pull Rafayel's Inferno Waltz. If you need more survivability, Zayne's Frost Barrier. This phase is about rounding out your roster rather than chasing power spikes. Each A-tier memory should solve a specific problem you are encountering in content. Avoid pulling for C-tier or D-tier memories even if you like the operative — the wish cost is too high for marginal gains.

New player rule: Do not pull on standard banner with diamonds. Standard banner wishes come from free sources (BP, events, achievements). Save every diamond for limited banners. A single limited S-tier memory is worth more than ten standard SSR memories combined for account progression.

Summary: Pull priority determines account progression speed. S-tier memories (Stellar Contract, Twilight Oath, Permafrost Covenant) are account-defining and should be your primary targets. A-tier memories (Inferno Waltz, Radiant Edge, Shadow Sovereign, Frost Barrier) are excellent upgrades with alternatives. B-tier memories are solid fillers but not pull targets. New players should follow a 3-month roadmap: month 1 main operative S-tier, month 2 second operative S-tier, month 3+ A-tier gap fillers. Remember that wishes are the scarcest resource — spend them where they create the most value. A focused account with two S-tier memories will outperform a scattered account with five A-tier memories every time.
