Memory Awakening Priority

Which memories to awaken first — R1 vs R2 value, duplicate usage, SR awakening strategy, and material efficiency.

Golden Rule: R1 your main DPS SSR first, always. R1 gives the largest power spike per duplicate spent. Only push to R2-3 for specific memory breakpoints.

R1 vs R2 Value

The power gain from awakening is not linear. R0 to R1: ~25-35% stat increase + unlock passive effect. This is the most efficient awakening by far. R1 to R2: ~10-15% stat increase + upgrade passive effect. Significantly less value per duplicate. R2 to R3: ~8-12% stat increase + passive enhancement. Only worth it for main DPS signature memories. The general rule is that one R1 SSR is worth more than two R0 SSRs in practical combat performance. Do not spread your duplicates thin.

SSR Awakening Priority

Tier 1 (Awaken first — R1 minimum): Your main DPS's signature SSR memory. This is the memory they use in Abyss, SHC, and weekly bosses. The passive effect at R1 is usually a damage multiplier or energy regen buff that dramatically changes how the operative plays. Tier 2 (Awaken after main DPS): Secondary DPS SSR (your second team's main memory). Support SSR that gives team-wide buffs at R1. Tier 3 (Awaken last): Non-signature SSR memories that are used for stellactrum coverage. Standard banner SSR that you rarely use. A general priority: Main DPS R1 > Support SSR R1 > Second DPS R1 > Main DPS R2 > all other R1 > R3 for signature memories.

SR Awakening Strategy

SR memories are easier to awaken because you get more duplicate drops. However, investing in SR over SSR is a common trap. R3 SR vs R0 SSR: An R3 SR performs roughly equal to an R0 SSR in its specific role. If you lack an SSR for a stellactrum color, an R3 SR is a perfectly viable substitute. R5 SR: The maximum awakening for SR. At R5, some SR memories outperform R1 SSRs of the same role — but this is rare and depends on the specific memory. General SR rule: Do not intentionally pull for SR duplicates. Take the ones you get naturally and awaken them as they come. Only invest awakening materials into SR if you are actively using that memory in your weekly boss or Abyss team.

Tip: Some SR memories have deceptive power at high awakening. Check the passive effect description — if it mentions "team-wide" or "all operatives," it can be worth prioritizing even over a lower-awakened SSR.

Duplicate Usage

When you pull a duplicate SSR, you have two choices: awaken the memory (recommended in most cases) or convert to shards for the wishing well (only if the memory is fully awakened or completely useless). The wishing well conversion rate is poor — 1 duplicate SSR = 30-50 shards, which translates to roughly 1/5th of a new SSR. Only convert duplicates for the following cases: The memory is R3 already. The memory is a standard banner SSR you will never use. You are exactly 30-50 shards away from a pity pull on a limited banner you need. In all other cases, awakening the memory gives more immediate combat power.

Material Efficiency

Awakening materials are scarce — especially the purple/gold tier gems. Green gems: Use freely on SR and SSR awakening. You will have enough for all your needs. Blue gems: Spend only on SSR awakening. Do not use blue gems on SR unless you have a massive surplus. Purple gems: The bottleneck. Only use on your top-priority SSR awakenings. Gold gems: Extremely rare. Save for R3 awakening on your main DPS signature memory. Weekly farming: Awakening gem stages are available daily but have different element rotations. Plan your farming around what gems you need for your current awakening target.

✅ DO
  • R1 your main DPS SSR first
  • Use green/blue gems freely
  • Awaken SR that you actively use
  • Save purple gems for priority targets
  • Consider R3 SR as SSR replacement
❌ DON'T
  • Spread duplicates across multiple SSRs
  • Convert useful duplicates to shards
  • Use purple/gold gems on SR memories
  • Awaken non-signature SSRs before main DPS
  • Pull on banners just for SR duplicates

Key Breakpoints

Some memories have specific awakening breakpoints that unlock game-changing effects. R1 that adds energy regen: The most valuable breakpoint — allows faster Ardent Oath cycling. R2 that adds a second charge: Very strong for skills that have long cooldowns. R3 that adds team-wide effect: Makes a support memory much more valuable. R0 that is good enough: Some memories are perfectly functional at R0 — their awakened passives are small enough that they can stay at base awakening until you have nothing better to invest in.

Summary: R1 > all other priorities. R1 main DPS SSR first, then support SSR, then second DPS. Save purple/gold gems for priority memories. R3 SR = R0 SSR in performance. Only convert duplicates to shards if the memory is R3 or completely unusable. The material bottleneck is purple gems — plan around this.
