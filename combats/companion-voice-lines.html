Voice Line Collection Guide

Complete guide to unlocking every companion voice line — dialogue conditions, hidden triggers, and a completion tracker for the dedicated collector.

Many voice lines have one-time triggers that occur at specific Affinity thresholds on specific days in specific locations. Voice Line System Overview

Each operative has approximately 50-70 unique voice lines (dialogues) categorized into: Affinity lines (triggered at specific Affinity thresholds — these are the most common and represent the bulk of the collection), Daily lines (random voice lines that play during specific interactions — feeding, petting, base interaction), Story lines (voice lines from main story chapters and event stories — these are auto-unlocked by progression), Combat lines (triggered during specific combat actions — dodge, skill use, companion skill command), Secret lines (special conditions — specific date, time, location, equipment combinations).

The voice line collection screen (accessible from the Companion menu > Voice Gallery) tracks completion per operative. It shows: total voice lines (X/70 unlocked), category breakdown (Affinity X/25, Daily X/18, Story X/12, Combat X/10, Secret X/5), and a "last heard" timestamp for each unlocked line. The gallery also has a playback feature — once you unlock a line, you can replay it anytime. This means you only need to unlock each line once; you do not need to maintain conditions for repeated listening. The completionist goal is to fill all 70 slots per operative.

Trigger Types & Conditions

Affinity lines are the most straightforward — each line is tied to a specific Affinity level range. For example, Xavier's "Morning Greeting" line triggers at Affinity 15-20, but only between 6:00-9:00 AM in-game time. If you reach Affinity 20 without triggering it (because you never logged in during morning hours), the line becomes locked until you either reset your companion schedule or level another operative's Affinity past the range. This "missed window" mechanic is the most common frustration for collectors.

AFFINITY LINES
~25 lines per operative
Triggered at specific Affinity ranges (e.g., 1-10, 11-20, 21-30, etc.). 90% are unconditional within their range. 10% have additional conditions (time of day, base room, clothing). Focus on these first — they are the bulk of the collection and the most predictable.
DAILY LINES
~18 lines per operative
Triggered by specific daily actions: feeding (6 lines — 2 per food type), petting (4 lines — different body areas), base interaction (5 lines — different rooms), leaving/returning (3 lines — entering/exiting base). Each action has a small chance (10-20%) to trigger an unlocked line. RNG-based — the most tedious to complete.
STORY LINES
~12 lines per operative
Auto-unlocked by completing story chapters and event stories. These are the easiest to collect — you get them by playing through the game. The only risk is if you skip dialogue (the game warns you that skipping may permanently miss voice lines). Never skip event story dialogue if you care about voice line completion.
COMBAT LINES
~10 lines per operative
Triggered by specific combat actions: perfect dodge (2 lines), critical hit with specific element (3 lines), companion skill use (2 lines), boss kill (1 line), taking lethal damage (1 line), co-op revive (1 line). These require combat-specific conditions. Use the Training Room to farm combat lines without stamina cost.
SECRET LINES
~5 lines per operative
The rarest and most sought-after. Conditions include: specific date (birthday, anniversary), specific weather (storm, clear night), specific base setup (all furniture of one style), specific outfit combinations, and hidden interaction spots (interacting with a specific object in a specific room at a specific time). Secret lines require guide assistance — very few are discovered organically.

By Operative: All Voice Lines

Xavier (• lines): Affinity lines are heavily tied to time of day — he has distinct morning (6-9 AM), afternoon (12-3 PM), evening (6-9 PM), and night (10 PM-2 AM) sets. His combat lines focus on light-element execution and team protection. Secret lines: "Stargazing" (interact with telescope in Study on clear nights), "Anniversary Promise" (anniversary date with specific outfit), "Training Partner" (1000 perfect dodges with Xavier companion).

Zayne ( lines): Affinity lines are tied to medical and research themes — many trigger in the Study room with specific furniture. His daily lines have the most variety in feeding responses (he comments on each food type differently). Combat lines emphasize crowd control and team healing. Secret lines: "Prescription" (interact with the medicine cabinet in base with winter outfit), "Frost Research" (enter Frost domain with Zayne companion 10 times), "Tea Time" (specific tea set furniture + afternoon time window).

Rafayel (~ lines): Affinity lines are the most emotional — Rafayel's voice lines cover a wide range from playful to vulnerable. His daily lines trigger most reliably from petting (he enjoys physical affection more than the other operatives). Combat lines are artistically themed — he has unique lines for fire-element kills and burn damage ticks. Secret lines: "Ocean's Heart" (beach/pool furniture set + swimsuit outfit), "Art Critique" (interact with painting furniture 50 times), "Improvisation" (play specific music box furniture item).

Sylus (≈ lines): Affinity lines are the most dramatic — Sylus's voice acting is intense and theatrical. His lines often trigger in dark lighting conditions (night time, dark-element domains). Combat lines are the most varied — he has 4 unique combat lines versus the standard 2 for other operatives. Secret lines: "Shadow Dance" (dodge 3 attacks in rapid succession 10 separate times), "Onychnus Oath" (Onychnus outfit + throne furniture), "Night's Edge" (interact with weapon display in base at midnight exactly).

Optimal collection order: Start with story lines (free, guaranteed, no RNG). Then work on Affinity lines (predictable, can be scheduled). Then combat lines (use Training Room for efficient farming). Then daily lines (most RNG, requires patience). Finally, secret lines (save these for last — they require the most setup and guide reference). This order minimizes frustration — you build momentum with easy completions before tackling the RNG-heavy categories.

Hidden & Secret Voice Lines

Hidden voice lines are those not shown in the Voice Gallery count — they are Easter eggs outside the collection system. Currently discovered hidden lines include: "Loading Screen Whispers" (random 1-second voice clips during loading screens — 3 per operative, purely cosmetic), "Home Screen Reactions" (operatives react to specific home screen decorations — 2 per operative), "Error Message Voice" (extremely rare — operative voice line plays during a game error, only 1 reported occurrence per operative), and "Collaboration Lines" (cross-game references if you have specific other games installed — very rare).

Secret lines (counted in the collection) require specific, often arduous conditions. The most infamous secret line is Sylus's "Onychnus Oath" — to trigger it, you need: Sylus at Affinity 80+, the Onychnus outfit unlocked and equipped, the "Dark Throne" furniture item placed in the Living Room, and you must interact with the throne at exactly midnight in-game time (which corresponds to midnight in your device's timezone). Missing any of these conditions means the line does not trigger. The community maintains a shared document of all verified secret line triggers — consult it before attempting secret line collection.

Secret line safety: Before attempting a secret line trigger, save your game (manual save available in Settings > Account > Save Data). Some secret line triggers are buggy and may consume resources (gifts, furniture tokens) without triggering the line. A manual save lets you reload and retry without losing resources. If a secret line does not trigger after 3 attempts with correct conditions, check the community guide for that specific line — the conditions may have changed in a recent patch. Report outdated information to help other collectors.

Completion Tracker System

Use a voice line tracker to monitor your progress across all operatives. For each operative, record: total lines unlocked (from gallery screen), lines per category (tap each category in gallery to see individual counts), last heard timestamps (helps identify if you are stuck on a specific line), attempted triggers for stuck lines (which conditions you have tried — prevents repeating failed attempts), and priority next targets (which line to farm next based on easiest remaining). A simple spreadsheet with these fields takes 10 minutes to set up and saves hours of confused farming.

The tracker also helps identify "stuck" lines — lines that should have triggered based on your conditions but did not. If you are at Affinity 65 with Xavier and the "Morning Greeting" line (Affinity 15-20 range) is still locked, you either: (1) leveled past the range without triggering it (the line is now on the "missed" list), (2) have an unmet sub-condition (wrong time, wrong outfit), or (3) the line is bugged. The tracker helps you diagnose which scenario applies. For scenario 1, the only fix is using the "Schedule Reset" item (available from the Shop for 500 gems) or leveling another operative's Affinity to clear the range again on your target operative.

Schedule reset strategy: If you miss a time-gated line, do not panic-buy a Schedule Reset (500 gems each). Instead, use the "alt operative" method: level another operative's Affinity to push through the range that the first operative is stuck in. For example, if Xavier's "Morning Greeting" line did not trigger at Affinity 15-20, level Zayne to Affinity 20+. This "resets" the voice line cycle for all operatives, giving Xavier a new chance to trigger the line next time his schedule refreshes (weekly on Monday). The alt operative method takes longer (1-2 weeks) but costs 0 gems versus 500 gems for the instant reset. Only buy the Schedule Reset for your main operative during the last week before a new event.

Voice Line Farming Strategy

1
Daily check-in
Log in at 3 different times of day (morning, afternoon, night) to check daily voice line triggers. Each time window unlocks different Daily and Affinity lines. Set a phone reminder for each window. 2 minutes per check.
2
Base rotation
Cycle through all base rooms each check-in. Different rooms have different line triggers. Spend 30 seconds in each room. Interact with at least 1 furniture item per room. 3 minutes total.
3
Combat farming
Spend 10 minutes in the Training Room (no stamina cost) triggering specific combat conditions: perfect dodges, skill uses, CRIT hits. Combat lines have a 10-15% trigger chance per relevant action. 10 minutes.
4
Feeding & petting
Feed the companion 3 times (different food types) and pet them in 3 different body areas. Each action has a 10-20% trigger chance for daily lines. 5 minutes.
5
Weekly secret attempts
Pick 1 secret line per week to attempt. Set up the conditions (furniture, outfit, time) and trigger the interaction. If it does not trigger, try again next day. Focus on 1 line per week to avoid burnout.

Total daily time: approximately 20 minutes for voice line farming. This is in addition to normal gameplay. If you have limited time, prioritize the combat farming step (highest new-line rate per minute) and skip the daily check-in rotation (lowest rate). On weekends, double the time spent — voice lines have a higher trigger rate during weekends according to community data (approximately 25% higher across all categories). Weekend farming is 25% more efficient than weekday farming.

Efficiency note: Voice line farming is a collector's activity with no combat benefit. If you do not enjoy collecting voice lines, do not feel obligated to farm them. The Voice Gallery has no progression rewards or stat bonuses — it is purely cosmetic and narrative. Many players never unlock more than 50% of voice lines and that is perfectly fine. Voice lines are for lore enthusiasts and completionists. Spend your game time on activities you enjoy.

Missable & Limited Lines

Some voice lines are permanently missable if not collected during a specific event. Event story lines from limited-time events (collaborations, seasonal events) are only available during the event period. If the event has not had a rerun, those lines are currently unavailable. The developers have stated they are working on an Event Archive system that would make event story lines permanently accessible, but no release date has been announced. Currently, event lines from the Summer 2025, Halloween 2025, and Winter 2025 events are not available to new players.

Additionally, two voice lines per operative are tied to Birthday events. These are only triggerable during the operative's birthday week (Xavier: Mar 15-21, Zayne: Jun 10-16, Rafayel: Sep 22-28, Sylus: Dec 5-11). If you miss an operative's birthday, you must wait a full year for the next opportunity. Set calendar reminders for your favorite operative's birthday week at least 1 month in advance. The birthday lines are among the highest-quality voice acting in the game — many players consider them the best content in the voice line system. Do not miss them.

Voice line community resources: The LADS Voice Line Community maintains a shared spreadsheet with: all known voice lines per operative, verified trigger conditions, community-reported bugs, and timestamped audio previews of each line. The spreadsheet is linked in the Companion Guide section of the Workshop. If you discover a new secret line or a trigger condition that works differently than documented, please update the spreadsheet. The voice line community relies on collective knowledge — every contribution helps fellow collectors.
