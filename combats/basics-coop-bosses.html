Co-op Boss Mechanics Guide

Every co-op boss, every mechanic, every role — master team play.

ENTRY_#0462026.06.30COOP

Co-op vs Solo: Co-op bosses have different HP pools, attack patterns, and mechanics than their solo versions. Bosses scale with party size (3 players = 3x HP, modified attack patterns). Coordination becomes essential — especially for phase transitions and interrupt mechanics.

Common Co-op Mechanics

Shared Aggro: Bosses switch targets based on threat. Damage generates threat. Healing generates half threat. Taking damage does not generate threat. The highest-threat player becomes the tank. If you're not the tank, stay behind the boss. If you are the tank, face the boss away from the group.

Shared Interrupts: Some boss attacks can be interrupted. If any player uses a resonance skill during the boss's cast animation, the cast is interrupted and the boss is staggered for 3s. Coordinate who interrupts — two players interrupting the same cast wastes the cooldown.

Revive System: Downed players can be revived by any teammate within 4m. Revive takes 3 seconds. During revive, both the reviver and the downed player are vulnerable. Only revive during safe windows (boss attack recovery phases).

Void Colossus (Tier 1)

Simplest co-op boss. Role: 1 tank, 2 DPS. Tank holds aggro at north wall. DPS attack from behind. Avoid the laser sweep (strafe left). Interrupt the ground pound (charged attack, 2s cast). Mechanics: Phase 1 (100%-60%) — standard attacks + laser sweep. Phase 2 (60%-0%) — adds spawn, kill adds immediately. There is no enrage.

Frost Ancient (Tier 2)

More complex. Role: 1 tank, 1 DPS, 1 support/healer. Frost Ancient has an AoE freeze pulse every 20s. The support must shield or heal through the freeze. DPS must break the ice crystals that spawn during freeze — if crystals reach the boss, it heals for 10% HP per crystal. Tank must position the boss away from crystals.

Chaos Wyrm (Tier 3)

Hardest co-op boss. Role: 1 tank, 1 DPS, 1 flex (interrupt + mechanics). Phase 1: Wyrm flies — DPS must destroy its wings with ranged attacks (2 wings, each 30% HP). Phase 2: Wyrm grounded — tank holds aggro, flex interrupts the fire breath (3s cast, wipes party if not interrupted). Phase 3 (30%): Wyrm enrages — all attacks AoE, 2x damage. Burn phase — all DPS must be maximized.

Pro Tip: Communication is everything in co-op. Use the ping system to mark: "Attack" (focus target), "Danger" (incoming AoE), "Help" (need revive). Pre-assign roles before the fight starts. A coordinated team clears 50% faster than an uncoordinated one.

Summary: Know your role (tank/DPS/support). Interrupt big casts. Stay behind the boss if not the tank. Revive during safe windows. Void Colossus = easy, Frost Ancient = medium, Chaos Wyrm = hard.
