DPS Calculation Framework

The math behind every number that appears on screen. Understand this framework and you can evaluate any build, any rotation, any team composition without testing it.

ENTRY_#LAB_012026.07.02DPS

Core Formula: Final Damage = Base ATK × Skill Multiplier × (1 + DMG Bonus%) × CRIT Multiplier × Element Multiplier × Defense Factor. Every DPS question reduces to: which variable in this formula can I increase the most with the least investment? The answer changes per operative and per content type.

Damage Formula Breakdown

Base ATK: Operative base ATK (level-dependent) + memory flat ATK + protocore flat ATK. Multiplied by (1 + total ATK% from all sources). ATK% has diminishing returns above +100% because it saturates the multiplier bracket. Rule: 1 point of ATK% before +100% is worth ~2x more than 1 point after +100%.

Skill Multiplier: Each skill has a fixed multiplier (e.g., Xavier's Light Slash = 180% ATK at level 6). Skill levels increase this multiplier. Leveling a skill from 6 to 7 increases the multiplier by ~8-12%. This is the single most stamina-efficient damage increase — one skill level up beats 5 protocore upgrades.

DMG Bonus%: Elemental damage bonus, skill type bonus, set bonus. This bracket is additive with itself (30% Light DMG + 20% skill DMG = 50% total). It is multiplicative with ATK%. DMG Bonus% is the least saturated bracket for most builds — getting 30% elemental DMG is often a bigger increase than 30% ATK.

CRIT Multiplier: 1 + (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG). If you have 60% CRIT Rate and 150% CRIT DMG, your CRIT multiplier is 1 + (0.6 × 1.5) = 1.9x average. Improving CRIT Rate from 60% to 70% at the same CRIT DMG increases this to 2.05x — an 8% damage increase. The CRIT multiplier is the most tunable bracket but has the highest opportunity cost (you sacrifice ATK% or ER to stack it).

Element Multiplier: 1.5x if the enemy is weak to your element. 0.5x if resistant. 1.0x neutral. This is a hard multiplier that cannot be modified by stats. It is the single biggest damage factor in the game — a 1.5x multiplier is worth more than any single substat roll. Always match elements.

Defense Factor: 1 / (1 + enemy DEF / (ATK × penetration)). Enemy DEF scales with floor level. At Abyss floor 10, enemy DEF reduces damage by ~35%. At floor 12, ~50%. Defense penetration stats (if available) are more valuable at higher floors. Flat DEF penetration > DEF% penetration against high-DEF enemies.

Rotation DPS Model

Rotation DPS = (Skill 1 DMG + Skill 2 DMG + Ardent Oath DMG + Auto-attack DMG during cooldowns) / Rotation duration. A standard rotation cycle is 12-15 seconds (skill cooldown window). Key insight: Auto-attack damage during cooldowns accounts for 25-35% of total DPS. Protocores that boost auto-attack damage (if available) are undervalued by the community. A 15% auto-attack DMG bonus is worth approximately 5% total DPS increase — equivalent to a CRIT Rate substat roll.

Formula in practice: To estimate your DPS without testing: Take your highest skill's damage number (from a training dummy or domain run). Multiply by 3.5 (average skills per rotation). Add 30% for auto-attack fill. Divide by 13 (seconds per rotation). This gives a rough Rotation DPS. Example: Xavier Light Slash hits for 15k → 15k × 3.5 = 52.5k → +30% = 68.25k → /13s = 5,250 Rotation DPS. Compare this number across builds — the build with higher Rotation DPS wins regardless of peak damage numbers.

Practical Application

When comparing two protocore builds: (1) Calculate your CRIT Multiplier for both builds. (2) Calculate your ATK% bracket. (3) Multiply CRIT Multiplier × ATK multiplier × DMG Bonus multiplier. (4) The build with the higher product is better. This calculation takes 30 seconds and is more accurate than "feeling" which build is stronger. Spreadsheet recommendation: Keep a simple DPS calculator in a spreadsheet with your operative's stats. Update it whenever you change protocores. Do not trust "bigger number = better" — burst damage and sustained DPS are different metrics.

Summary: Final DMG = Base ATK × Skill Mult × (1+DMG Bonus) × CRIT Mult × Element × DEF Factor. ATK% saturates after +100%. Skill level up is the most efficient upgrade. Element match = 1.5x, bigger than any substat. Rotation DPS = total damage / 13s. Use the multiplier product method to compare builds. Test with math, not feelings.
