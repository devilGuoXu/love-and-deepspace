DPS Calculation Framework

The math behind every number that appears on screen. Understand this framework and you can evaluate any build, any rotation, any team composition without testing it.

Core Damage Formula

Core Formula: Final Damage = Base ATK × Skill Multiplier × (1 + DMG Bonus%) × CRIT Multiplier × Element Multiplier × Defense Factor. Every DPS question reduces to: which variable in this formula can I increase the most with the least investment? The answer changes per operative and per content type.

This formula is the foundation of everything in this guide. Every variable is a multiplier. Increasing any one variable by 10% increases your final damage by 10% — but only if you increase the right variable for your current build state. The art of build optimization is identifying which variable is the lowest and raising it.

Base ATK

Base ATK = Operative base ATK (level-dependent) + memory flat ATK + protocore flat ATK. This sum is then multiplied by (1 + total ATK% from all sources).

Lv50 Xavier base ATK~850 (varies by memory and breakthroughs)
Lv60 Xavier base ATK~1,050 (max base)
Flat ATK from 6 memories~300–500 depending on memory levels
Flat ATK from protocores~100–300 from substat rolls
Total ATK% target+80% to +100% before diminishing returns

Rule: 1 point of ATK% before +100% is worth ~2x more than 1 point after +100%. The ATK% bracket saturates above +100% because it becomes an additive multiplier on an already-large base. If you have +80% ATK and add +20% more, your ATK multiplier goes from 1.8 to 2.0 — an 11% increase. But if you have +120% ATK and add +20% more, it goes from 2.2 to 2.4 — only a 9% increase. The same 20% ATK roll is worth less when you already have high ATK%.

Skill Multiplier

Each skill has a fixed multiplier (e.g., Xavier's Light Slash = 180% ATK at level 6). Skill levels increase this multiplier. Leveling a skill from 6 to 7 increases the multiplier by ~8–12%.

Key insight: This is the single most stamina-efficient damage increase. One skill level up beats 5 protocore upgrades. Priority order: (1) Level your main DPS's primary skill to max. (2) Level their secondary skill. (3) Level support skills (Zayne shield). (4) Protocore upgrades. Most players invest in protocores before skills — this is backwards. Skills are guaranteed damage; protocores are RNG.

Skill Leveling Priority: For Xavier: Light Slash (main DPS) → Resonance → Auto-attack. For Zayne: Shield → Healing skill → anything else. For Sylus: Execute passive → everything else. For Rafayel: Burn DoT enabler → Fire skill. A level 8 main skill on a level 50 operative out-damages a level 6 main skill on a level 60 operative in most content.

DMG Bonus%

Elemental damage bonus, skill type bonus, set bonus — all go into the same additive bracket. 30% Light DMG + 20% skill DMG = 50% total DMG Bonus. This bracket is multiplicative with ATK%.

Typical F2P DMG Bonus15–30% (from set bonus + element matching)
Typical Dolphin DMG Bonus30–50% (set + element + some substat rolls)
Whale DMG Bonus50–70% (everything + signature memory bonuses)

DMG Bonus% is the least saturated bracket for most builds. Getting 30% elemental DMG is often a bigger increase than 30% ATK, because most players already have +80%+ ATK but only +15–20% DMG Bonus. Check your own stats — if your DMG Bonus is below 20% and your ATK% is above +80%, you should prioritize DMG Bonus sources over more ATK%.

CRIT Multiplier

CRIT Multiplier = 1 + (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG). If you have 60% CRIT Rate and 150% CRIT DMG, your CRIT multiplier is 1 + (0.6 × 1.5) = 1.9x average damage.

50% Rate / 100% DMG1.50x — minimum viable
60% Rate / 130% DMG1.78x — comfortable
65% Rate / 150% DMG1.975x — endgame target
70% Rate / 170% DMG2.19x — min-maxed
80% Rate / 200% DMG2.60x — whale territory

Improving CRIT Rate from 60% to 70% at the same CRIT DMG (150%) increases the multiplier from 1.9 to 2.05 — an 8% damage increase. The CRIT multiplier is the most tunable bracket but has the highest opportunity cost (you sacrifice ATK% or ER to stack it). Every CRIT roll is a roll not spent on ATK% or ER.

This is a hard multiplier that cannot be modified by stats. A 1.5x multiplier is worth more than any single substat roll. Matching elements is the single most impactful decision you make before entering combat. An off-element Xavier (1.0x) with perfect stats will deal less damage than an on-element Xavier (1.5x) with mediocre stats.

Always match elements. The difference between weak (1.5x) and resistant (0.5x) is a 3x damage gap. No amount of protocore farming can close a 3x gap. If your only built DPS is Xavier (Light) and the boss resists Light, you need to build a second DPS of a different element. This is why endgame players have 2–3 fully built DPS operatives of different elements.

Defense Factor

Defense Factor = 1 / (1 + enemy DEF / (ATK × penetration)). Enemy DEF scales with floor level.

Abyss floor 5~20% damage reduction from DEF
Abyss floor 7~30% damage reduction
Abyss floor 10~35% damage reduction
Abyss floor 12~50% damage reduction

Defense penetration stats (if available) are more valuable at higher floors. Flat DEF penetration > DEF% penetration against high-DEF enemies. At floor 12, a 10% DEF penetration substat can increase your effective damage by ~8–10% — more than a similar investment in ATK% would give, because DEF penetration reduces the impact of the steep DEF curve at high floors.

DEF Penetration Note: Currently, DEF penetration is rare and mostly tied to specific memory set bonuses or limited-time events. If you have access to DEF penetration (e.g., from a set bonus), prioritize it highly for Abyss floors 10+. It becomes more valuable the higher the floor. For most players, simply having high ATK (2,400+) is sufficient to overcome DEF at floor 10 — DEF penetration becomes relevant when pushing floor 12+.

Rotation DPS Model

Rotation DPS = (Skill 1 DMG + Skill 2 DMG + Ardent Oath DMG + Auto-attack DMG during cooldowns) / Rotation duration. A standard rotation cycle is 12–15 seconds (skill cooldown window).

Key insight: Auto-attack damage during cooldowns accounts for 25–35% of total DPS. Protocores that boost auto-attack damage (if available) are undervalued by the community. A 15% auto-attack DMG bonus is worth approximately 5% total DPS increase — equivalent to a CRIT Rate substat roll. Do not ignore auto-attack bonuses when evaluating protocores.

Quick DPS Estimator: Take your highest skill's damage number (from a training dummy or domain run). Multiply by 3.5 (average skills per rotation). Add 30% for auto-attack fill. Divide by 13 (seconds per rotation). Example: Xavier Light Slash hits for 15k → 15k × 3.5 = 52.5k → +30% = 68.25k → /13s = 5,250 Rotation DPS. Compare this number across builds — the build with higher Rotation DPS wins regardless of peak damage numbers. This takes 10 seconds and is more reliable than "it feels stronger."

Practical Application

When comparing two protocore builds, follow this process:

1
Calculate CRIT Multiplier
CRIT Mult = 1 + (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG). Do this for both builds.
2
Calculate ATK% bracket
ATK Mult = 1 + (total ATK% / 100). Include set bonuses.
3
Calculate DMG Bonus bracket
DMG Mult = 1 + (total DMG Bonus% / 100). Include elemental and skill bonuses.
4
Multiply all three
Total = CRIT Mult × ATK Mult × DMG Mult. Higher product = better build.

This calculation takes 30 seconds and is more accurate than "feeling" which build is stronger. Keep a simple DPS calculator in a spreadsheet with your operative's stats. Update it whenever you change protocores. Do not trust "bigger number = better" — burst damage and sustained DPS are different metrics.

Spreadsheet recommendation: Create a Google Sheet with columns: Build Name | ATK% | CRIT Rate | CRIT DMG | DMG Bonus | CRIT Mult | ATK Mult | DMG Mult | Total Product. For each protocore change, update the sheet. The sheet removes all subjectivity from build evaluation. If you share your sheet with the community, use the standard template from the Workshop tools article.

Summary: Final DMG = Base ATK × Skill Mult × (1+DMG Bonus) × CRIT Mult × Element × DEF Factor. ATK% saturates after +100%. Skill level up is the most efficient upgrade (out-damages protocore investment early). Element match = 1.5x, bigger than any substat. DEF at floor 12 = 50% reduction. Rotation DPS = total damage / 13s. Use the multiplier product method to compare builds. Test with math, not feelings.
