Lucky Dan: Gacha Mechanics

The in-game Lucky Dan minigame simulates banner pulls. It is not random — it follows a fixed pattern tied to your account's seed. Understanding it helps you plan real pulls.

ENTRY_#0612026.07.02GACHA

What Is Lucky Dan: A free daily simulation of the gacha system. It uses the same pity and rate-up logic as real banners but with free "fake" tickets. It does not cost diamonds. It does not give real memories. Its purpose is to teach you how the gacha works — and to confirm that the rates are real.

How It Works

Lucky Dan offers 1 free pull per day. You can simulate up to 1,000 pulls total (about 3 years of dailies). The simulation uses your account's hidden seed — a number assigned at account creation that determines your "luck pattern." The seed does NOT affect real banner pulls (real pulls use server-side RNG). Lucky Dan is purely educational. Pity rules in Lucky Dan: Same as real banners — 70-pull hard pity, 62-pull soft pity start, 50/50 rate-up. The only difference is there is no pull cost.

What Lucky Dan Teaches

Lesson 1 — Pity is real: Most players get their first SSR between pull 65-70. Lucky Dan confirms this. If you simulate 1,000 pulls, you will see that ~80% of SSRs land between pull 62 and 70. Lesson 2 — Early SSRs are rare: Only ~5% of SSRs come before pull 20. Never expect an early SSR. Budget for 70 pulls every time. Lesson 3 — The 50/50 is fair: Over 1,000 simulated pulls, you will win and lose the 50/50 at approximately equal rates. Confirmation bias makes you remember losses more — Lucky Dan shows the actual math.

Pull Planning with Lucky Dan

Use Lucky Dan to test your pull plan before spending real diamonds. Example: If a banner costs 140 pulls for a guaranteed rate-up (worst case), simulate 140 pulls in Lucky Dan. If you get the rate-up before 70, you "won" the simulation. Do this 10 times to see how often your budget is enough. Rule: If you can simulate 140 pulls and win 7+ out of 10, your budget is realistic. If you win fewer than 5, you need more savings.

Pro Tip: Lucky Dan resets your seed every time you close and reopen the game? No — the seed is permanent. But redraws are possible: if you get a character you already have, the game records it differently. Some players claim refreshing the Lucky Dan screen changes the outcome. This is a placebo. The result is fixed at the moment of the pull animation.

Advanced: Lucky Dan Patterns

Players have observed that Lucky Dan tends to cluster SSR drops. If you get an SSR early (before pull 30), the next SSR often comes late (after pull 65). This creates a "balancing" pattern but is purely coincidental — each pull is independent. Misconception to avoid: Some players believe Lucky Dan predicts real banner luck since both use the same account seed. This is FALSE. Real banners use server-side RNG independent of your seed. Lucky Dan outcomes have zero correlation with real pull outcomes.

Summary: Lucky Dan is a free simulator that teaches you how pity works. 70-pull hard pity, 62-pull soft pity, fair 50/50. Use it to confirm the rates and test your budget. Do NOT use it to predict real banner outcomes — there is no correlation. Always budget 140 pulls for a guaranteed rate-up. Lucky Dan proves the rates are real, not rigged.
