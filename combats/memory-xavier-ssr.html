Standard Banner SSRs (Permanent)

These Xavier SSRs are available on the standard banner and may spook you on limited banners. Heartfelt Accord (Radiance Set): ATK% main. Resonance skill: AoE light damage + party ATK buff for 8s. Tier: A — solid all-rounder, pairs with any team composition. Light Seeker (Lunar Set): CRIT% main. Resonance: single-target light damage. Tier: B — outclassed by limited SSRs but functional for F2P. Starlight Promise (Radiance Set): HP% main. Resonance: shield + self-heal. Tier: C — survival focus, use only on survivability-heavy floors. Standard banner tip: Do not pull specifically for standard SSRs. They will come naturally through lost 50/50s and standard tickets. The most valuable standard Xavier SSR is Heartfelt Accord — if you get it from a lost 50/50, consider it a win.

Limited SSRs (Event Banners)

These Xavier SSRs were available on limited time banners. Most will rerun eventually. Twilight Oath (Radiance 4-pc): The current best Xavier SSR. ATK% main. Unique effect: enhanced combo attacks reduce resonance cooldown by 2s. Tier: S — enables a completely different playstyle. Must-pull on rerun. Celestial Blade (Radiance Set): ATK% main. Resonance: massive single-target burst + CRIT damage self-buff. Tier: A — burst king. Excellent for SHC speed clears. Dawnbreaker (Lightforge Set): CRIT DMG% main. Resonance: multi-hit AoE with stun. Tier: A — strong AoE for trial tower floors. Phantom Radiance (Radiance Set): ATK% main. Resonance: teleport strike + invincibility frames. Tier: B — utility focus, the invincibility frames are situationally amazing but the damage is below average. Limited pulls strategy: Twilight Oath is Xavier's current BiS. Save 140 pulls for its rerun. Celestial Blade and Dawnbreaker are interchangeable as second-best depending on content.

Event & Free SSRs

Free Xavier SSRs from events and login campaigns: First Anniversary SSR (Radiance Set): ATK% main. Free from first anniversary login. Tier: B — solid stat stick, no unique effects. Good placeholder until you get a limited SSR. Valentine's Event SSR (Lunar Set): HP% main. Free from Valentine's event shop. Tier: C — HP% main is suboptimal for Xavier. Use only if you have nothing else. Battle Pass SSR (Radiance Set): ATK% main. Tier: B — identical kit to the standard banner Heartfelt Accord. Useful for building a second Radiance set for abyss teams.

Memory Investment Priority for Xavier: Twilight Oath (S) > Celestial Blade / Dawnbreaker (A) > Heartfelt Accord (A) > Anniversary SSR (B) > everything else. If you have Twilight Oath, invest all resources into it. If you do not have Twilight Oath, Celestial Blade or Heartfelt Accord will carry you through 95% of content. Do not invest in HP% main stat SSRs for Xavier — they waste his ATK-scaling kit. The difference between an S-tier and B-tier Xavier SSR is approximately 25% damage output. This makes Twilight Oath a high-value rerun target.

Memory Set Activation Notes

Xavier's best sets: Radiance 4-pc: +30% light damage for 12s after resonance. This is Xavier's default best set. The activation condition (use resonance) is trivially easy to maintain. Lightforge 2-pc + Radiance 2-pc: +15% ATK + +15% light damage. Use this if you cannot maintain Radiance 4-pc uptime (e.g., co-op where resonance cooldown is longer). Lunar 2-pc + ATK% 2-pc: F2P budget option. 10-15% worse than Radiance 4-pc but much easier to assemble. Set recommendation: Farm Radiance 4-pc as your primary set. Use the 2+2 hybrid as a temporary bridge. The 4-pc bonus is worth the additional farming time.

Summary: 4 standard SSRs (Heartfelt Accord A, Light Seeker B, Starlight Promise C). 4 limited SSRs (Twilight Oath S, Celestial Blade A, Dawnbreaker A, Phantom Radiance B). 3 free SSRs (Anniversary B, Valentine C, Battle Pass B). Invest in Twilight Oath first, Radiance 4-pc set. The S-to-B tier gap is ~25% damage. Pull on reruns, not standard banner.
