SSR Pool Overview

Xavier currently has 6 SSR memories in the global version: 3 limited banners and 3 standard pool. His SSR pool focuses heavily on Light-element damage output, with two of his limited cards — Stellar Contract and Radiant Dawn — consistently ranking among the top DPS memories in the entire game. Understanding his SSR lineup is critical for any Xavier main, as each card fills a distinct combat role.

Limited Banner SSRs

Stellar Contract (Light)
Xavier's signature limited SSR and the crown jewel of his kit. Active skill deals 450% Light damage with a 30% crit rate bonus. Passive: After using Resonance Skill, gain Stellar Mark — next basic attack deals 200% additional Light damage. At R1, Stellar Mark duration extends by 3 seconds. At R3, the additional damage increases to 300%. Widely considered the best Xavier SSR and a top-3 memory in the game.
Element: Light
Set: Solar
Base ATK: 885
Tier: S+
Radiant Dawn (Light)
Xavier's second limited SSR. A burst-oriented memory that rewards precise play. Active skill: Deploy a Light field for 8 seconds — Xavier gains 40% damage bonus while inside. Passive: Killing an enemy inside the field refunds 20% of Resonance Skill cooldown. R1 extends field duration to 10 seconds. R3 adds 15% party-wide crit rate while field is active. Excellent for wave-clearing and speed farming.
Element: Light
Set: Solar
Base ATK: 872
Tier: S
Twilight Verdict (Light/Flame)
Xavier's most recent limited SSR, introducing a dual-element hybrid playstyle. Active skill: Fire a Light-Flame projectile that leaves a burning field. Passive: Enemies hit take 15% increased Light and Flame damage from all sources for 10 seconds. R1 increases the debuff to 20%. R3 adds a 50% chance to apply burn on hit. This memory enables unique hybrid team compositions.
Element: Light + Flame
Set: Shadow
Base ATK: 890
Tier: A+

Standard Pool SSRs

Solar Flare (Light)
Standard pool SSR. Active skill: A wide Light-element wave that hits all enemies in front. Passive: Each enemy hit increases Xavier's ATK by 5% for 12 seconds, stacking up to 3 times. A solid general-purpose memory that provides consistent damage buffs. Available in the standard banner and as a 50/50 loss option. Not exceptional but definitely serviceable for F2P players.
Element: Light
Set: Solar
Base ATK: 845
Tier: B+
Light's Judgment (Light)
Standard pool SSR. Active skill: Single-target Light beam dealing 600% damage. Passive: If the target has less than 50% HP, damage increases by 35%. An execute-oriented memory that shines in boss fights. The execute condition can be tricky to optimize, but the payoff is substantial. Good for Abyss boss floors where you need single-target burst.
Element: Light
Set: Permafrost
Base ATK: 838
Tier: B
Guardian Protocol (Water)
Standard pool SSR and Xavier's only non-Light element memory. Active skill: A Water-element shield that absorbs damage equal to 20% of Xavier's max HP. Passive: While shielded, Xavier's damage reduction increases by 25%. A defensive option for survivability-heavy content. Not meta-relevant for DPS checks, but useful in Abyssal Chaos modes with attrition mechanics.
Element: Water
Set: Tidal
Base ATK: 810
Tier: C+

Team Synergies

Xavier's SSRs enable several high-performing team archetypes. The most effective compositions pair his Light-element burst with supports that amplify damage or provide elemental coverage:

Mono-Light Burst: Stellar Contract + Radiant Dawn + Light support buffer. This comp clears most PvE content in under 60 seconds by stacking Light damage bonuses and burst rotations. Ideal for Abyss speed clears and weekly boss farming.

Hybrid Light-Flame: Twilight Verdict + Flame SSR off-carrier. Uses Twilight Verdict's debuff to amplify both elements simultaneously. Requires careful rotation management but outputs the highest raw damage ceiling of any Xavier composition.

Sustain/Utility: Guardian Protocol + Stellar Contract. For content where survivability is paramount, Guardian Protocol's shield lets Xavier tank through mechanics while Stellar Contract provides the damage. Common in high-floor Abyssal Chaos and tower content.

Tip: Xavier's resonance chains significantly impact his SSR performance. R1 Stellar Contract alone is worth more than pulling a new SSR. If you have the resources, prioritize duplicating Stellar Contract to R1 before pulling for Radiant Dawn or Twilight Verdict.

Summary: Xavier's SSR pool offers exceptional Light-element DPS options. Stellar Contract and Radiant Dawn are top-tier investments, while standard pool SSRs provide solid filler. Focus on building a core of 2-3 strong limited SSRs, and let standard pool cards fill the remaining slots. With proper team building, Xavier can output the highest single-target burst damage in the game.
