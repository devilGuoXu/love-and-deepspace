Companion Photo Guide

Photo mode lets you capture special moments with your operatives in the Destiny Cafe. This guide covers poses, filters, backgrounds, and tips for the perfect shot.

ENTRY_#COM_052026.07.02PHOTO

Photo Mode Overview: Photo mode is accessed from the Destiny Cafe. It allows you to take selfies with the current operative, adjust camera angles, apply filters, and choose from various poses. Photos can be saved to your device or shared in-game.

Accessing Photo Mode

To enter photo mode: Enter the Destiny Cafe. Tap the camera icon in the bottom-right corner. The operative will stop their current activity and face the camera. You have 30 seconds to compose and capture your shot. The timer can be extended by tapping the clock icon (adds 30s, unlimited uses). Photo mode features: Camera Controls: Zoom in/out with two-finger pinch. Rotate the camera by dragging. Tilt by using the tilt slider. Switch between front-facing (selfie) and rear-facing camera. Pose Selection: Each operative has 6-8 poses unlocked by default. Additional poses unlock at Affinity 30, 60, and 100. Premium outfits sometimes include exclusive poses. Poses range from casual (waving, leaning) to intimate (hand-holding, close-up). Background: The Destiny Cafe background is the default. You can unlock additional backgrounds through events and the shop. Backgrounds include outdoor scenes, abstract patterns, and themed sets. Filters: 12 filters available by default. Additional filters unlock through photo mode achievements (take 50 photos, take 100 photos). Filters include: Warm, Cool, Vintage, Soft, Dramatic, Black & White, and seasonal filters from events.

Pose Guide by Operative

Xavier: Best poses: "Stargazer" (Affinity 30) — Xavier looks up dramatically. "Protector" (Affinity 60) — stands beside you with hand extended. "Tender" (Affinity 100) — leans close to your character. Xavier's poses tend to be gentle and romantic. His premium outfit "Celestial" adds a unique pose where he points at the sky. Zayne: Best poses: "Doctor's Visit" (default) — professional stance. "Warmth" (Affinity 30) — offers a scarf. "Caring" (Affinity 100) — gentle shoulder touch. Zayne's poses are restrained and elegant. His premium outfit "Frostborne" adds an ice crystal formation pose. Rafayel: Best poses: "Artist" (default) — holding a paintbrush. "Passion" (Affinity 60) — dynamic pose with flame effects. "Heartfelt" (Affinity 100) — hand over heart. Rafayel's poses are the most dramatic and expressive. Sylus: Best poses: "Noble" (default) — regal stance. "Shadow" (Affinity 60) — half-hidden in darkness effect. "Trust" (Affinity 100) — offers his hand. Sylus's poses are commanding and powerful.

Background & Filter Combinations

Certain filter-background-operator combinations create stunning photos: Xavier + Stargazer pose + Night Sky background + Cool filter: Creates a serene astronomical theme. Zayne + Warmth pose + Snow background + Soft filter: Winter romance aesthetic. Rafayel + Passion pose + Sunset background + Warm filter: Dramatic fiery composition. Sylus + Shadow pose + Dark background + Dramatic filter: Gothic elegance. Experimentation is encouraged. Photo mode has no limits — take as many photos as you want. The game does not charge resources for photo mode usage.

Photo Sharing & Achievements: Photos can be shared to your in-game profile, social media, or saved to your device. Taking photos contributes to achievements: 10 photos = 50 diamonds. 50 photos = 100 diamonds. 100 photos = exclusive photo frame. 500 photos = exclusive filter. Photo achievements are one of the easiest diamond sources in the game. Take a photo every day after your daily cafe visit. After 100 days, you will have the 100-photo achievement and 150 diamonds. After 500 days, you get the exclusive filter. Photo achievements are cumulative across all operatives — photos of any operative count toward the total.

Group Photo Mode

Group photos allow multiple operatives in a single shot. Unlocked at Account Level 30. Group photo mode supports up to 3 operatives plus your avatar. Each operative's pose can be individually adjusted. Group photos are more complex to compose but create more dynamic images. Tips: Position your avatar in the center for balanced composition. Use poses that complement each other (e.g., Xavier's romantic pose + Rafayel's dramatic pose = interesting contrast). Avoid cluttering the frame with too many elements. Group photos are the best way to show off multiple outfits and premium cosmetics.

Summary: Photo mode in the Destiny Cafe offers selfie and rear-facing cameras, 6-8 poses per operative (more unlock at Affinity 30/60/100), 12+ filters, and multiple backgrounds. Unlock exclusive poses through affinity and premium outfits. Experiment with filter-background-operator combinations for best results. Photo achievements give diamonds and exclusive cosmetics. Group photo mode unlocks at Account Level 30. Take at least one photo daily for achievement progression.
