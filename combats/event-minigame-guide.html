Mini-Game Events

Deepspace regularly features mini-game events — rhythm games, tower defense, puzzles, and racing. These events reward diamonds, materials, and exclusive titles without any gear check.

ENTRY_#EVT_042026.07.02MINIGAME

What They Are: Mini-game events replace combat with alternative gameplay. They are the only content in Deepspace where account power does not matter — a day-1 player can earn the same rewards as a veteran. Every mini-game event has 5-10 stages, each with 3 star conditions.

Rhythm Game Events

Format: Tap notes to the beat of Deepspace OST tracks. Star Conditions: (1) Clear the stage. (2) Achieve 80%+ accuracy. (3) Full combo (no misses). Strategy: Play on the lowest speed setting first to learn the pattern. Use the practice mode (unlimited retries) before attempting star conditions. The hardest condition is always full combo — if you miss one note, restart immediately. Rewards: 50 diamonds per stage × 7 stages = 350 diamonds. Exclusive title "Rhythm Master" for full combo on all stages. Difficulty: The last stage is significantly harder. Watch a video guide for the note pattern.

Tower Defense Events

Format: Place operative tokens on a grid to defend a point from wanderer waves. Star Conditions: (1) Clear all waves. (2) Lose fewer than 3 lives. (3) Clear within 5 minutes. Strategy: Place Xavier tokens at chokepoints for damage. Place Zayne tokens behind for support. Upgrade Xavier first (max level). Sylus tokens are good for cleanup but expensive. The "Lose fewer than 3 lives" condition is the hardest — build a wall of Zayne tokens at the farthest spawn point to slow enemies. Rewards: 40 diamonds per stage × 5 stages = 200 diamonds. Protocore enhancement materials.

Puzzle Events

Format: Match-3 / tile-matching / sliding puzzles with a Deepspace theme. Star Conditions: (1) Clear the puzzle. (2) Clear within X moves. (3) Collect X special items during the puzzle. Strategy: Focus on the "moves" condition first (least moves possible). Once you know the optimal solution, replay for the "special items" condition. Puzzle events have fixed solutions — you can look up the optimal path online. Rewards: 30 diamonds per stage × 8 stages = 240 diamonds. Exclusive name card background.

Racing / Endless Runner Events

Format: Auto-scrolling runner where you dodge obstacles and collect coins. Star Conditions: (1) Reach the finish line. (2) Collect X coins. (3) Clear without hitting any obstacles. Strategy: The "no obstacles" condition requires memorizing the course. Replay the same stage multiple times to learn obstacle placement. Coins are always on the same path — memorize the coin route and the dodge route separately. Rewards: 50 diamonds per stage × 5 stages = 250 diamonds. Limited home decoration item.

Pro Tip: Mini-game events are the most time-efficient source of diamonds in the game. A full mini-game event clear takes ~45 minutes and gives 800-1200 diamonds total. Compare this to Abyss which takes hours for the same diamond income. Always clear mini-game events before they expire — they are the F2P player's best friend.

Universal Mini-Game Tips

General rules that apply to every mini-game event: (1) Do the tutorial stage first — it gives free rewards and explains mechanics. (2) 3-star each stage in order of difficulty (star 1 → star 3). (3) If a star condition seems impossible, check if the stage has a "practice mode" or "easy mode" toggle. (4) All mini-game events have a total reward cap — once you earn all stars, there is no reason to replay. (5) Mini-game events are F2P-friendly — no stamina cost, no gear requirement. They are the only content where a whale and a F2P player are equal.

Summary: Rhythm (memorize patterns) → Tower Defense (Xavier chokepoints) → Puzzle (optimal solution first) → Racing (memorize course). 45 minutes = 800-1200 free diamonds. Zero stamina cost. Zero gear check. Do them every time they appear.
