Mini-Game Events

Deepspace regularly features mini-game events — rhythm games, tower defense, puzzles, and racing. These events reward diamonds, materials, and exclusive titles without any gear check.

Rhythm Game Events

Tap notes to the beat of Deepspace OST tracks. The most popular mini-game type.

Format & Star Conditions
(1) Clear the stage. (2) Achieve 80%+ accuracy. (3) Full combo (no misses). 7 stages total with increasing difficulty.
Stages: 7
Diamonds: 50 per stage = 350 total
Exclusive Title: "Rhythm Master" (full combo all stages)
Hardest Stage: Stage 7 — watch a video guide

Strategy: Play on the lowest speed setting first to learn the pattern. Use the practice mode (unlimited retries) before attempting star conditions. The hardest condition is always full combo — if you miss one note, restart immediately. The last stage is significantly harder — watch a video guide for the note pattern.

Tower Defense Events

Place operative tokens on a grid to defend a point from wanderer waves.

Format & Star Conditions
(1) Clear all waves. (2) Lose fewer than 3 lives. (3) Clear within 5 minutes. 5 stages total.
Stages: 5
Diamonds: 40 per stage = 200 total
Rewards: Protocore enhancement materials
Hardest Condition: Lose fewer than 3 lives

Strategy: Place Xavier tokens at chokepoints for damage. Place Zayne tokens behind for support. Upgrade Xavier first (max level). Sylus tokens are good for cleanup but expensive. The "Lose fewer than 3 lives" condition is the hardest — build a wall of Zayne tokens at the farthest spawn point to slow enemies.

Puzzle Events

Match-3 / tile-matching / sliding puzzles with a Deepspace theme.

Format & Star Conditions
(1) Clear the puzzle. (2) Clear within X moves. (3) Collect X special items during the puzzle. 8 stages total.
Stages: 8
Diamonds: 30 per stage = 240 total
Exclusive Reward: Name card background
Difficulty: Fixed solutions — can be looked up

Strategy: Focus on the "moves" condition first (least moves possible). Once you know the optimal solution, replay for the "special items" condition. Puzzle events have fixed solutions — you can look up the optimal path online. This is the only mini-game type where external guides can save significant time.

Racing / Endless Runner Events

Auto-scrolling runner where you dodge obstacles and collect coins.

Format & Star Conditions
(1) Reach the finish line. (2) Collect X coins. (3) Clear without hitting any obstacles. 5 stages total.
Stages: 5
Diamonds: 50 per stage = 250 total
Exclusive Reward: Limited home decoration item
Hardest Condition: No obstacles hit

Strategy: The "no obstacles" condition requires memorizing the course. Replay the same stage multiple times to learn obstacle placement. Coins are always on the same path — memorize the coin route and the dodge route separately. Treat each run as a memorization session, not a one-shot attempt.

Pro Tip: Mini-game events are the most time-efficient source of diamonds in the game. A full mini-game event clear takes ~45 minutes and gives 800-1200 diamonds total. Compare this to Abyss which takes hours for the same diamond income. Always clear mini-game events before they expire — they are the F2P player's best friend.

Universal Mini-Game Tips

General rules that apply to every mini-game event, regardless of type.

1. Do the Tutorial First
The tutorial stage gives free rewards and explains mechanics. Never skip it — even if you think you understand the game type.
2. 3-Star in Order of Difficulty
Clear star 1 → star 3 in sequence. Each star condition builds on the previous one. Going out of order wastes time.
3. Check for Practice Mode
If a star condition seems impossible, check if the stage has a "practice mode" or "easy mode" toggle. Many stages do.
4. Don't Over-Farm
All mini-game events have a total reward cap. Once you earn all stars, there is no reason to replay. Move on.
5. F2P Friendly
No stamina cost, no gear requirement. Mini-game events are the only content where a whale and a F2P player are equal. Take full advantage.

DO & DON'T Summary

DO
  • Clear all mini-game events before they expire
  • Use practice mode to learn patterns first
  • Watch video guides for difficult stages
  • Treat 3-star conditions as a checklist — do them in order
  • Enjoy the free 800-1200 diamonds with zero stamina cost
DON'T
  • Don't skip the tutorial stage
  • Don't attempt full combo on rhythm games without practice
  • Don't replay stages after earning all stars
  • Don't forget about mini-games during major events
  • Don't assume you can't do them — day 1 players clear them too

Summary: Rhythm (memorize patterns) → Tower Defense (Xavier chokepoints) → Puzzle (optimal solution first) → Racing (memorize course). 45 minutes = 800-1200 free diamonds. Zero stamina cost. Zero gear check. Do them every time they appear.
