01. SYSTEM CORE MECHANICS

SHIELD SYSTEM [PROTOCORE]

Most Elite Wanderers utilize a Protocore Shield. Direct HP damage is reduced by 60% while the shield is active.

PROTOCOL: Use Resonance Skills matching the enemy's elemental weakness to deplete shields 50% faster.
EVASION [CHRONO-STASIS]

Executing a Perfect Dodge (0.2s window) triggers a localized time-dilation field.

  • Enemy movement speed -50%
  • Resonance Energy +15%
  • Grants brief invulnerability frames (i-frames)
