BASIC PROTOCOLS — FARMING

Protocore Farming Routes

Maximize stamina efficiency when farming protocores — domain selection, synthesis strategy, and targeting specific slots.

ENTRY_#0542026.07.01FARMING

Farming Mindset: Protocore farming is a marathon, not a sprint. You will run thousands of domains. The difference between an efficient farmer and an inefficient one is not luck — it's knowing which domains to run and when to synthesize versus farm.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Domain Rotation

Domains cycle daily. Monday: ATK set (Slot 1-2 focus). Tuesday: DEF set (Slot 3 focus). Wednesday: HP set (Slot 4 focus). Thursday: CRIT set (Slot 5-6 focus). Friday: Mixed set (all slots). Saturday: All sets (double drops). Sunday: All sets (double drops).

Stamina allocation: Saturday-Sunday (double drop) = 60% of weekly farming. Monday-Thursday (targeted slots) = 30%. Friday (mixed) = 10%. Never farm protocores outside of these recommendations unless you need a specific set immediately.

Slot Targeting

Not all protocore slots are equally important to farm. Slot 1-2 have fixed main stats (ATK flat, HP flat) — they only differ in substats. These are least important to farm specifically. Slot 3 (DEF% or DEF flat) — moderately important. Slot 4 (ATK% or Element%) — very important. Slot 5 (CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG) — most important. Slot 6 (Energy Recharge or DMG Bonus) — very important.

Priority order: Slot 5 (CRIT main stat is the hardest to replace) > Slot 4 (ATK% is essential) > Slot 6 (ER breakpoints) > Slot 3 > Slot 1-2. When choosing between two domains, pick the one that drops Slot 5 or Slot 4 pieces.

Synthesis Strategy

Synthesis (crafting protocores from materials) should only be used for Slot 5 and Slot 4 pieces. Never synthesize Slot 1-2 — they drop frequently and have fixed main stats. Synthesis gives you a random main stat in the slot — use it to target CRIT Rate Slot 5 specifically, since this is the rarest combination.

Rule: Farm for substats on Slot 1-3. Farm for main stats on Slot 4-6. Synthesize only Slot 5 CRIT pieces. Salvage all protocores with wrong slot 4-6 main stats unconditionally — they cannot be salvaged (literally can't be useful) regardless of substats.

Stamina Budget

Weekly boss (3 runs) = 180 stamina. EXP books/leveling = 0 stamina (passive regen prioritization). Protocore farming = remaining stamina. A typical week with 1,200 total stamina: 180 on bosses, ~300 on exp/gold, ~720 on protocores. On double-drop weekends, shift budget to 90% protocore farming.

Protocore farming efficiency is measured in useful drops per stamina, not total drops per stamina. A useful protocore has correct main stat and at least one useful substat. At domain level 5, approximately 8% of protocore drops are useful by this definition. This means you need approximately 12 domain runs (480 stamina) per useful protocore drop. Plan your stamina budget around this efficiency rate to set realistic expectations for build completion timelines.

Protocore domains have hidden pity: an SSR guaranteed after 10 consecutive runs without one. Effective SSR rate is 26% over 10 runs. Track your dry streak — if at 8+ runs since last SSR, keep farming.

Key Insight: The biggest efficiency gain is knowing when to stop. If your main DPS already has correct main stats on all 6 slots with passable substats, STOP FARMING that set. Start farming for your second team. Perfect substats are a luxury, not a requirement. A "good enough" set on 2 teams > a perfect set on 1 team.

Protocore Domain Farming Route

Protocore domains are the most stamina-intensive farming activity in endgame. The most efficient route is to farm the highest-level domain that you can clear in under 90 seconds. Domain levels 5 and above have the same SSR protocore drop rate of approximately 20%. The difference between domain levels is the secondary material quality, not the protocore drop rate. If you can clear level 6 in 90 seconds but level 7 takes 150 seconds, level 6 is more efficient because you get more total runs per stamina refresh.

Summary: Farm double-drop weekends (60% of budget). Target Slot 5 (CRIT) and Slot 4 (ATK%). Synthesize only for CRIT slot 5. Salvage wrong main stats on 4-6. Stop when main stats are correct — build second team.
