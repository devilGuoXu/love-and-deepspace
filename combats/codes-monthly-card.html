Aurum Pass Basics

The Aurum Pass (also called Monthly Card or "the monthly") costs $4.99 per 30-day cycle. Upon purchase, you immediately receive 300 premium currency (gems). For each of the next 30 days, you receive 100 gems via the in-game mail (claimed daily). Total: 3,000 gems over 30 days = 20 Limited Pulls worth of gem income. The pass automatically renews each month unless you cancel through your platform's subscription settings (App Store subscriptions for iOS, Google Play subscriptions for Android). You can also purchase multiple passes in advance — the duration stacks up to 180 days (6 months maximum).

The Aurum Pass is available to all players regardless of account level. It is a pure source of premium currency — no resources, no materials, just gems. This makes it the most flexible purchase in the game because gems can be used for anything: limited banners, standard banners, stamina refills, shop items, or BP level skips. The pass's simplicity is its strength — it provides a steady, predictable income of the most valuable currency in the game. For $4.99/month, you effectively double or triple your monthly gem income compared to F2P.

Value Analysis

The 15x value multiplier of the Aurum Pass is calculated by comparing the cost per gem versus direct gem pack pricing. The standard gem pack rate is approximately $0.01 per gem (100 gems = $1). The Aurum Pass delivers 3,000 gems for $4.99, or approximately $0.0017 per gem — approximately 6x cheaper per gem than direct purchase. Additionally, the daily delivery structure means you cannot impulse-spend the gems all at once — they arrive 100 at a time, encouraging thoughtful spending.

To put the value in perspective: a $4.99 Aurum Pass gives 20 limited pulls per month. To get 20 pulls from direct gem purchase would cost approximately $15 (1,600-gem pack at $4.99 + 980-gem pack at $2.99 = 2,580 gems for $7.98, short of 3,000). The Aurum Pass provides more gems at 60% of the cost. Over a year, the Aurum Pass costs $59.88 and provides 36,000 gems (240 limited pulls). Direct gem purchases for the same 240 pulls would cost approximately $360. The Aurum Pass saves $300/year for the same pulling power.

Compared to smaller packs ($4.99 for 600 gems = $0.0083/gem), the Aurum Pass is 5x cheaper. There is no purchase in Love and Deepspace that approaches the gem-per-dollar efficiency of the Aurum Pass. The Aurum Pass gives you pure, unconditional gems — no gameplay required beyond logging in to claim the daily reward.

Subscription Management

Managing the Aurum Pass subscription requires attention to three details: renewal date, stacking limit, and cancellation process. The pass renews every 30 days from purchase date. You can stack up to 180 days (6 months) by purchasing multiple passes. Stacking is the most efficient strategy because it locks in the price — if the Aurum Pass price increases in the future (common in live service games), your stacked passes are unaffected. Stack also reduces the risk of forgetting to renew and losing a day of gem income.

To stack passes: purchase the Aurum Pass from the Shop as many times as you want (up to 6). Each purchase adds 30 days to your remaining duration. The cost is $4.99 per stack, charged immediately. For a full 180-day stack, the one-time cost is $29.94 — which provides 18,000 gems over 6 months at the best possible rate. This is the recommended approach for any player who is certain they will play LADS for the next 6 months. The stack provides 120 limited pulls (18,000 gems / 150 gems per pull) guaranteed over 6 months, enabling consistent pulling on every other limited banner.

Cancellation warning: The Aurum Pass auto-renews by default. If you only want 1 month, manually cancel the subscription immediately after purchase (before the renewal date). On iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Love and Deepspace > Cancel. On Android: Google Play > Subscriptions > Love and Deepspace > Cancel. Canceling does not affect your current pass duration — you keep all remaining daily gem deliveries. It only prevents the next 30-day cycle from auto-charging. You can always re-subscribe later if you change your mind. Always cancel if you are unsure about next month's playtime.

Renewal Strategy

The optimal renewal strategy depends on your play pattern. For daily players: stack 180 days upfront for convenience and price lock. For weekly players (play most days but not every day): buy month-to-month and only renew when you are actively playing. Do not renew during months you know you will be busy (vacation, work crunch, other game releases). The gem income from the Aurum Pass is only valuable if you log in to claim it — unclaimed daily gems are lost forever (mail expires after 30 days).

For F2P-to-dolphin converters: start with 1 month to test if the pass fits your playstyle. If you consistently claim daily gems for 2-3 weeks, stack to 90 days (3 months). If you miss days, evaluate whether the pass is worth it for you. The break-even point is approximately 15 claimed days out of 30 — if you claim fewer than 15 days, you would be better off buying the battle pass instead, which gives more flexible rewards. For committed players, the 180-day stack is optimal — set a calendar reminder 2 weeks before the stack expires to renew.

Gem Income & Budgeting

Understanding your total monthly gem income helps you budget pulls effectively. F2P monthly income: approximately 4,500-5,500 gems (from dailies, weeklies, Abyss, events). With Aurum Pass (+3,000): 7,500-8,500 gems/month. With Aurum Pass + Battle Pass (+7,500 gem equivalent over 6 weeks, or +5,000/month averaged): 12,500-13,500 gem equivalent/month. This translates to: F2P = 30-36 pulls/month. Monthly Card only = 50-56 pulls/month. Monthly + BP = 83-90 pulls/month. With 90 pulls to guarantee a limited SSR, a Monthly Card player can guarantee approximately 1 limited SSR every 2 months. A Monthly + BP player can guarantee approximately 1 limited SSR every 5-6 weeks.

Other Subscriptions

Beyond the Aurum Pass, Love and Deepspace offers two other recurring purchase options. The "Stamina Pass" ($2.99/month) provides 60 bonus stamina per day (delivered via mail). At 240 natural stamina per day, this increases your daily budget to 300 — a 25% increase. The stamina pass is valuable for players who farm heavily (domain grinding, event currency) but the value is lower than the Aurum Pass. The "Outfit Subscription" ($7.99/month) provides a monthly outfit crate (random from a limited pool) plus 200 gems upfront. This is purely cosmetic — only buy if you care about outfit collection.

Comparing subscriptions: Aurum Pass ($4.99, 3,000 gems) = best value by far. Stamina Pass ($2.99, 1,800 bonus stamina) = good for heavy farmers, equivalent to approximately 10 extra domain runs per month. Outfit Subscription ($7.99, 1 outfit + 200 gems) = worst value — the 200 gems are only worth approximately $0.80, meaning you are paying $7.19 for a random outfit. Only buy the Outfit Subscription if you are a dedicated cosmetic collector and understand you are paying for outfits, not currency efficiency. Never prioritize the Outfit or Stamina Passes over the Aurum Pass — buy Aurum first, then consider the others only if your budget allows.

For this price, you get: 3,200 gems, 1 random outfit, 1,800 bonus stamina. However, the Outfit Subscription's value depends entirely on how much you value random cosmetics. If you do not care about outfits, skip it and save $8/month.
