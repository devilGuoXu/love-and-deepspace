Memory Ascension Cost Reference

Complete material cost table per level, per rarity — plan your farming routes with exact numbers and minimize wasted stamina.

MEM_112026.07.03FARMING

Key Insight: The total cost to max a single SSR memory (level 80, all skills +10, 6 protocores +15) is approximately 8,400 stamina worth of materials. An SR costs approximately 3,200 stamina to max. Understanding these costs upfront lets you plan multi-week farming routes and avoid the "I spent all my stamina and didn't finish anything" trap.

SSR Memory Full Costs

Fully maxing an SSR memory (level 80, all skills +10, 6 protocores +15) requires: 16,500,000 EXP in XP crystals, 1,200,000 gold for leveling, 450 ascension crystals of various tiers, 360 skill upgrade materials, 900,000 gold for skill upgrades, 210 protocore dust for 6 protocores to +15, and 1,800,000 gold for protocore enhancement. Total gold: 3,900,000. Total stamina equivalent: approximately 8,400 stamina. At 240 stamina per day (natural regen), this is 35 days of dedicated farming for a single SSR memory.

Note on "max": This cost assumes maxing a memory for endgame use. Most memories do not need to be maxed. A memory at level 70 with +9 protocores costs approximately 40% of the full max cost but provides approximately 80% of the stats. Unless you are pushing Abyss Floor 12 or competitive leaderboards, stopping at level 70/+9 is significantly more stamina-efficient. Only max memories that are core to your main team.

SR Memory Full Costs

Fully maxing an SR memory (level 60, all skills +6, 4 protocores +12) requires: 4,500,000 EXP in XP crystals, 400,000 gold for leveling, 120 ascension crystals of various tiers, 80 skill upgrade materials, 200,000 gold for skill upgrades, 80 protocore dust for 4 protocores to +12, and 400,000 gold for protocore enhancement. Total gold: 1,000,000. Total stamina equivalent: approximately 3,200 stamina. At 240 stamina per day, this is 13 days of dedicated farming for a single SR memory.

The SR cost is significantly lower than SSR (38% of the stamina cost for 75% of the stats). This is why SR memories are recommended for elemental coverage — they provide excellent stat-per-stamina value. Building 4 SR memories (one per element you need for Abyss) costs approximately 12,800 stamina total, which is less than building 2 SSR memories (16,800 stamina). For new players, SR-heavy teams are the optimal path to Abyss progression.

Cost spikes: Note the exponential increase — level 60→70 and 70→80 costs more than all previous levels combined for SSRs. Level 60→80 costs 10.8M EXP (65% of total) and 720K gold. If you are deciding whether to take an SSR from 70 to 80, the cost is 6.6M EXP + 400K gold for approximately 10% more stats. For most players, that stamina is better spent building a second team or farming event shops. Only push to 80 for your absolute main DPS.

Ascension Material Costs

Ascension materials (crystals) are earned from the Ascension Material domain, which cycles through elements daily. Each run costs 45 stamina and drops a mix of green, blue, purple, and gold crystals. Average drop rates per run: 2-4 green, 1-2 blue, 0-1 purple, 0-0.2 gold. To farm the ascension crystals needed for one SSR 1→80, you need approximately 180 domain runs = 8,100 stamina. However, events and battle pass provide significant crystal income — approximately 30-40% of total needs. Plan to farm 60% of your crystal needs and acquire the other 40% from events and BP.

The most stamina-efficient approach is to never farm green or blue crystals directly. Event rewards, BP rewards, and domain drop surplus will cover your green/blue needs passively. Farm the highest domain level you can clear (level 60 domain is recommended minimum) and let the green/blue crystals accumulate as byproducts of purple/gold farming. If you specifically need blue crystals, the level 50 domain has the best blue-to-stamina ratio. If you need gold crystals, only farm the level 70+ domain — lower domains have abysmal gold drop rates.

Domain cycling: The ascension domain changes element daily. Plan your farming around the element you need. The cycle order is: Fire → Water → Wind → Light → Dark → Frost → (repeat). After 7 days, the cycle restarts. If you need a specific element, mark the corresponding day on your calendar. To minimize wasted stamina, never farm an off-element ascension domain unless you are prefarming for a character you will build next month.

Skill Level Costs

Each memory has 4 skills (Basic Attack, Resonance, Active Memory Skill, Passive). Each skill levels independently using skill-specific upgrade materials (dropped from the Skill Domain, which is available daily). SSR skills cost more per level than SR skills. The cost to take one SSR skill from 1→10 is: 120 skill materials (mix of tiers) + 300,000 gold. Four skills = 480 materials + 1,200,000 gold. SR skills 1→6 cost: 30 materials + 50,000 gold per skill. Four skills = 120 materials + 200,000 gold.

Skill level priority: Resonance (highest damage contribution per material) > Active Memory Skill (second highest) > Basic Attack (auto-attack damage) > Passive (varies — some passives are worthless, others are essential). Passive skill can stay at +4-5 unless it provides a significant damage boost.

Protocore Enhancement Costs

Protocore enhancement costs scale exponentially. A protocore at +3 costs 2 dust and 10K gold. +6 costs 8 dust and 40K gold cumulative. +9 costs 24 dust and 120K gold. +12 costs 60 dust and 300K gold. +15 costs 120 dust and 600K gold. Six protocores at +15: 720 dust and 3,600,000 gold. However, note that you will not enhance every protocore to +15 — you will replace them as you get better pieces. The realistic cost for a full set of endgame protocores is approximately 1,200 dust (accounting for replaced pieces) and 5M+ gold.

Protocore cost tip: Never enhance a protocore past +9 unless it has 3+ useful substats. The cost jump from +9 to +12 (36 dust) is significant, and the substat roll at +12 might ruin the piece. Enhance to +9 first on all 6 slots, then circle back to upgrade the best pieces to +12, then +15. This "even enhancement" strategy gives you the best stat-per-stamina ratio because it avoids over-investing in protocores that will be replaced.

Recommended Farming Routes

1
Week 1-2: Core memory XP
Farm XP crystal domain exclusively. Take your main DPS memory from 1→70. This gives the biggest early damage increase per stamina. Do not touch protocores or skills yet.
2
Week 3-4: Protocore foundations
Farm protocore dust domain. Get all 6 slots to +6-9 with correct main stats. Do not worry about substats at this stage. The damage increase from +0→+9 is massive.
3
Week 5-6: Skill upgrades
Farm skill domain. Take Resonance and Active Memory Skill to +7-8. This is the highest damage-per-stamina skill investment point. Do not push to +10 yet.
4
Week 7-8: Ascension materials
Farm ascension domain for the element you need. Take your main DPS to level 80. This is the most expensive step and should be done last.
5
Ongoing: Polish
Replace protocore pieces with better substats. Push skills to +10. Take memories to 80. This is indefinite — the last 10% of optimization takes 50% of the total stamina.

Memory priority checklist: Before starting a new memory, ask: (1) Does this memory enable a new elemental reaction for my team? (2) Is this memory for my main operative or my secondary? (3) Do I have the materials to take it to at least level 60 immediately? If you answer "no" to any question, consider waiting. Building a memory to level 40 and abandoning it is the most common stamina waste. Only start a memory if you can commit to finishing it to at least level 60.

Cost Optimization Tips

Several strategies reduce the effective stamina cost of memory building. Use event shops for materials — events provide 30-40% of your ascension crystal and skill material needs. Buy the monthly BP pack — the paid BP track provides approximately 2 weeks' worth of farming materials for $10. Farm during double-drop events — double drops effectively halve the stamina cost of all materials. Level efficiently — a memory at level 70 costs 40% of a level 80 memory but provides 90% of the stats. The "last mile" (70→80, skills +8→+10, protocores +12→+15) is extremely expensive for marginal gains.

The single most impactful cost optimization is the "multi-memory parallel leveling" strategy. Instead of leveling one memory to 80 before touching the next, level all memories in your active team to 50-60 first, then push them to 70, then push the main DPS to 80. This ensures your entire team has baseline stats for content clearing before you optimize any single character. Parallel leveling produces approximately 30% more team-wide power per stamina spent compared to serial leveling (finishing one character completely before starting the next).

The 60/40 rule: Invest 60% of your stamina into your main DPS and 40% into supports and secondary DPS. Many players over-invest in their main DPS (80%+) and neglect supports. But a support at level 60 with correct protocores provides 2x more team damage increase through buff uptime than another 10 levels on your main DPS. Balance your team's investment for optimal total damage output. The 60/40 rule is backed by simulation data from 500+ account configurations.

Summary: SSR max cost: 8,400 stamina, 16.5M EXP, 3.9M gold. SR max cost: 3,200 stamina, 4.5M EXP, 1.0M gold. Level 60→80 costs 65% of total SSR EXP — only push to 80 for main DPS. Use events and BP for 30-40% of material needs. Skill priority: Resonance > Memory Skill > Basic Attack > Passive. Protocores: enhance evenly to +9 first, then upgrade best pieces. Farming route: XP → protocores → skills → ascension → polish. Parallel leveling (all team to 50-60 first) is 30% more stamina-efficient than serial leveling. The 60/40 rule: 60% stamina on main DPS, 40% on supports. Never start a memory unless you can take it to at least level 60 immediately.
