EVENT ARCHIVE — SEASONAL

Seasonal & Holiday Events

Anniversary, Valentine's, Christmas, summer, and other seasonal events — these are the only source of limited cosmetics, outfits, and exclusive memories. They do not rerun frequently.

ENTRY_#EVT_032026.07.02SEASONAL

What They Are: Seasonal events are shorter than story events (7-14 days) but offer unique rewards: limited outfits (skin), portrait frames, name card backgrounds, and exclusive SR memories that are never added to the standard pool. These are FOMO events — if you miss them, the items may not return for 6-12 months.

Anniversary Event

The biggest event of the year. Expected timing: Game launch anniversary (usually January). This is the one event you should never miss.

Strategy: Save your diamond reserves for the anniversary. Stop pulling 1 month before the expected anniversary date. The free SSR selector alone is worth ~14,000 diamonds. The top-up reset means you should buy the monthly pass before the reset, then buy it again after.

Additional rewards: Double diamond top-up reset, login calendar with 7 days of premium rewards. The anniversary login calendar alone gives more value than most paid packs.

Valentine's / White Day

Romance-themed event with niche appeal — aimed primarily at collectors and character fans.

DURATION
7-10 Days
Shorter than most events. Plan your login schedule carefully to not miss the window.
REWARDS
Exclusive SR Memory + Photo Props
Usually a dating-themed SR memory, limited chat bubble, and portrait frame. Unique photo mode props and filters are also included.
STRATEGY
Pull Only for Favorites
The SR memory is decent but not meta-defining. Pull only if you are a collector or the character is your favorite. The photo mode props are permanent once obtained — worth grabbing if you enjoy the photo feature.

Strategy: Outfits in this event are usually the most popular in the community. If you care about cosmetics, prioritize the outfit over the memory. The mini-game usually gives 300-500 free diamonds over the event duration — do not skip it. Limited swimsuit outfits, exclusive mini-game mode, and event shop with summer-themed materials are all part of this event.

Christmas / New Year

Winter holiday event with a limited SSR and generous free pull calendar.

Limited SSR Memory (Holiday Themed)
A "cosmetic memory" — not top-tier meta but very good for collection. If you are F2P, this is the one seasonal banner worth pulling on because the holiday login calendar supplements your pull income.
Duration: 14 days
Login Calendar: 10 free pulls
Home Decor: Exclusive holiday items
Value: High for collectors, medium for meta

Pro Tip: Seasonal events are the only source of limited outfits. Outfits do not affect combat stats, but they are the primary endgame for collectors. If you plan to spend money on Deepspace, seasonal outfits are the best value purchase — they cost less than pulling for a limited SSR and last forever.

Seasonal Priority Ranking

Not all seasonal events are created equal. Here is how they rank by overall value.

1
Anniversary
Free SSR selector, top-up reset, 20 free pulls — unmatched value. This is the single best event in the game. Save everything for it.
2
Christmas / New Year
Limited SSR + free pulls. The holiday login calendar supplements your pull income, making this the best seasonal for F2P players.
3
Summer / Beach
Popular outfits + free diamonds from mini-games. Great for collectors who care about cosmetics.
4
Valentine's / White Day
Niche appeal — only for collectors or character fans. The SR memory is decent but not essential.

Rule: Always save 1 month of pull income before any major seasonal event. Do not spend everything on regular banners right before a seasonal. The regret of missing anniversary rewards is the #1 cause of player burnout.

DO & DON'T Summary

✅ DO
  • Save 1 month of diamond income before major seasonals
  • Prioritize Anniversary above all other events
  • Buy the monthly pass before and after top-up reset
  • Complete mini-games for free diamonds during summer events
  • Consider outfits if you are a collector
❌ DON'T
  • Don't spend everything before a seasonal event
  • Don't skip the free SSR selector at anniversary
  • Don't pull on Valentine's banner unless you love the character
  • Don't ignore login calendars during seasonal events
  • Don't expect seasonal items to rerun soon (6-12 month wait)

Summary: Anniversary > Christmas > Summer > Valentine's. Seasonal events are the only source of limited cosmetics. Save 1 month of income before each major seasonal. Anniversary free SSR selector is the single best reward in the game. Collectors should prioritize outfits over memories in seasonal shops.
