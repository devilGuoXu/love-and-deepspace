Seasonal & Holiday Events

Anniversary, Valentine's, Christmas, summer, and other seasonal events — these are the only source of limited cosmetics, outfits, and exclusive memories. They do not rerun frequently.

ENTRY_#EVT_032026.07.02SEASONAL

What They Are: Seasonal events are shorter than story events (7-14 days) but offer unique rewards: limited outfits (skin), portrait frames, name card backgrounds, and exclusive SR memories that are never added to the standard pool. These are FOMO events — if you miss them, the items may not return for 6-12 months.

Anniversary Event

The biggest event of the year. Duration: 21-30 days. Rewards: 10-20 free limited pulls, 1 free SSR selector (choose from a pool of standard SSRs), exclusive anniversary outfit, double diamond top-up reset, login calendar with 7 days of premium rewards. Strategy: Save your diamond reserves for the anniversary. Stop pulling 1 month before the expected anniversary date. The free SSR selector alone is worth ~14,000 diamonds. The top-up reset means you should buy the monthly pass before the reset, then buy it again after. Expected timing: Game launch anniversary (check the official release date). Usually January.

Valentine's / White Day

Romance-themed event. Duration: 7-10 days. Rewards: Exclusive SR memory (usually a dating-themed memory), limited chat bubble, portrait frame. Unique photo mode props and filters. Strategy: The SR memory is usually decent but not meta-defining. Pull only if you are a collector or the character is your favorite. The photo mode props are permanent once obtained — worth grabbing if you enjoy the photo feature.

Summer / Beach Event

Beach-themed seasonal. Duration: 14 days. Rewards: Limited swimsuit outfits (skin), exclusive mini-game mode, event shop with summer-themed materials, free SR memory. Strategy: Outfits in this event are usually the most popular in the community. If you care about cosmetics, prioritize the outfit over the memory. The mini-game usually gives 300-500 free diamonds over the event duration — do not skip it.

Christmas / New Year

Winter holiday event. Duration: 14 days. Rewards: Limited SSR memory (holiday themed), login calendar with 10 free pulls, exclusive home decoration items. Strategy: The limited SSR is usually a "cosmetic memory" — not top-tier meta but very good for collection. If you are F2P, this is the one seasonal banner worth pulling on because the holiday login calendar supplements your pull income.

Pro Tip: Seasonal events are the only source of limited outfits. Outfits do not affect combat stats, but they are the primary endgame for collectors. If you plan to spend money on Deepspace, seasonal outfits are the best value purchase — they cost less than pulling for a limited SSR and last forever.

Seasonal Priority Ranking

1. Anniversary: Free SSR selector, top-up reset, 20 free pulls — unmatched value. 2. Christmas/New Year: Limited SSR + free pulls. 3. Summer: Popular outfits + free diamonds from mini-games. 4. Valentine's: Niche appeal — only for collectors or character fans. Rule: Always save 1 month of pull income before any major seasonal event. Do not spend everything on regular banners right before a seasonal.

Summary: Anniversary > Christmas > Summer > Valentine's. Seasonal events are the only source of limited cosmetics. Save 1 month of income before each major seasonal. Anniversary free SSR selector is the single best reward in the game. Collectors should prioritize outfits over memories in seasonal shops.
