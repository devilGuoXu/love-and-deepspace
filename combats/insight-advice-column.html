Dear Hunter

Volume I — An Advice Column for the Lost

Readers write in with their struggles. I write back with honesty, empathy, and the occasional bad joke. The names have been changed. The problems are real.

Dear Hunter,

I have been playing for about two months and I already feel burned out. I am only level 38 but I dread opening the game. There is so much to do every day that it feels like a chore list. I have tried skipping some dailies but then I feel guilty about missing resources. How do you manage the daily grind without hating the game?

— Burnt Out at Level 38
Dear Burnt Out,

You are experiencing the early-game information overload, and it is completely normal. The game throws every system at you in the first month and expects you to manage all of them simultaneously. Here is the secret the game does not tell you: you do not have to do everything every day.

Dailies that matter: spend your stamina, do your three bounty missions, claim your daily login reward. Everything else is optional. The base gift? Optional. The photo mode daily? Optional. The social stamina? Optional. If you only do the three essential dailies, you miss maybe 10% of your daily resource income. That is a small price to pay for not burning out.

Also consider: take a day off. Seriously. One day of missed dailies costs you about 60 gems and some materials. That is nothing. The game wants you to feel like you cannot afford to miss a day, but you can. The fear of missing out is manufactured. Do not let it control your schedule.

— Hunter

Dear Hunter,

I lost the 50-50 on the latest banner and I am so angry I want to delete the game. I saved for three weeks. Three. I had 80 pulls saved. I lost the 50-50 to a standard SSR I already have. I am writing this instead of uninstalling. Talk me down.

— Tilting in Tennesee
Dear Tilting,

I have been where you are. The 50-50 loss is the single most frustrating moment in this game. The game designers know this. They designed the 50-50 specifically to create this feeling, because the frustration makes you more likely to spend to resolve it. Recognizing that your anger is a designed response, not a personal failing, is the first step.

The banner will rerun. Every limited banner in this game has rerun within 6-8 months. Your saved pity carries over. You are guaranteed the next limited character on their banner. The resources are not wasted. The time is not wasted. You are just delayed, and delay is temporary.

Close the game for 24 hours. Do not open it. Do not check the subreddit. Let the chemicals settle. When you come back, log in, do your dailies, and move on. The sting fades. It always fades. And when the rerun comes, you will get the character with guaranteed pity, and it will feel like vindication.

— Hunter

Dear Hunter,

I am a new player and everyone says I should only pull on limited banners, but I have been stuck using SR memories for weeks and I am falling behind my friends. Should I pull on the standard banner just to get some SSRs?

— Standard or Suffer
Dear Standard or Suffer,

This is one of those situations where the community advice is technically correct but practically unhelpful. Yes, limited banners are better value. Yes, you will get standard SSRs eventually from free pulls. But "eventually" does not help you when your friends are clearing floor 6 and you are stuck on floor 4 because your main DPS is an SR memory.

Here is my compromise: spend your gems on limited banners, but do not hoard your standard banner tickets. Use every standard ticket you get immediately. Also, check the shard shop. You can buy selectable SSR memories with shards earned from pulling duplicates. One focused SSR from the shard shop can carry your early game.

And honestly? If you are really struggling and it is affecting your enjoyment, throw 20-30 pulls at the standard banner. One early SSR can change your entire early-game experience. The 20-30 pulls you lose by not saving them for a limited banner is a small price to pay for not quitting the game out of frustration.

— Hunter

Dear Hunter,

I accidentally enhanced a protocore with flat DEF substat to +12 before I realized it was bad. I want to cry. Is there any way to recover the resources?

— Waste Not Want Not
Dear Waste Not,

You can salvage it for 60% of the enhancement dust you put into it. It is not a full refund, but it is something. Go to your inventory, select the protocore, and choose salvage. You will get back a portion of your investment.

I have done this more times than I care to admit. Everyone has. The flat DEF trap is a rite of passage in this game. Consider it a learning experience. Next time, check the substats before enhancing past +6. If the first two rolls go into bad stats, salvage early and save the remaining investment.

One bad enhancement does not ruin your account. I lost an entire +15 piece to bad rolls and my account survived. Yours will too.

— Hunter

Dear Hunter,

I think I might be spending too much on this game. I set a budget of $30 per month but I have gone over it two months in a row. I am not missing any bills or anything, but it bothers me that I cannot stick to my own limit. Do you have advice?

— Budget Breaker
Dear Budget Breaker,

First, give yourself some credit for recognizing the pattern. Awareness is the first step. Many people never get that far.

Try this: switch to prepaid cards. Buy a $30 gift card at the beginning of the month and use that for all your in-game purchases. When the card runs out, you physically cannot spend more. The extra step of buying the card gives you a cooling-off period. The hard cap prevents overspending.

Also, identify your triggers. Are you spending more when you are tired? After losing a 50-50? Late at night? If you can identify the pattern, you can interrupt it. My trigger was late-night pulling. I stopped pulling after 10 PM and my spending dropped by 60%.

If the spending is causing you genuine distress, consider taking a break from the game entirely. A month away will reset the spending habits. The game will still be here when you come back.

— Hunter
