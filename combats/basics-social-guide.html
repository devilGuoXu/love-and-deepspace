Social & Crew Guide

Crews, friends, and co-op — the social systems that give free resources and accelerate your account.

ENTRY_#019 2026.06.29 SOCIAL

Why Social Matters: Most players treat Love and Deepspace as a single-player game. But the social systems — Crew, friends, co-op — provide 15-20% of your total weekly resource income. Ignoring them is leaving free resources on the table.

Crew (Guild) System

Crews unlock at Account Level 10. Joining an active crew is the single most important social decision you'll make.

Daily Donation: Donate gold or gems to earn crew coins. Donation limit: 1 per day. Gold donation = 10,000 gold → 100 crew coins. Gem donation = 50 gems → 300 crew coins (not recommended for F2P).
Crew Shop: Spend crew coins on SSR memory shards (1,200 coins for 2 shards, buy daily), SR memories, skill materials, and XP scrolls. Always buy SSR shards first.
Crew Buffs: Active crews unlock passive buffs for all members — ATK+, DEF+, gold drop rate. Higher crew level = better buffs. Being in a top-tier crew is equivalent to having an extra memory equipped.
Crew Boss (Guild Raid): Weekly boss fight. 3 attempts per week. Rewards scale with damage dealt. Top contributors get bonus crew coins. Coordinate with crewmates for elemental coverage.

Crew Selection Criteria

Not all crews are equal. Here's what to look for:

☑ Level 5+ crew (unlocks best passive buffs)
☑ Active daily donations (check if 20+ members donated in last 24h)
☑ Crew Boss being cleared weekly (indicates active membership)
☑ Communication platform (Discord/LINE for coordination)

Friend System

Adding friends unlocks mutual benefits:

Support Unit Borrowing: Borrow 1 memory from a friend per day for solo content. A friend with a maxed Stellar Contract Xavier gives you access to a memory you might not own. Build reciprocal relationships with high-level players.
Friend Gift Exchange: Send and receive daily friend gifts (small stamina items). Max 30 friends. Each gift = 10 stamina. 30 gifts = 300 stamina per day if all friends exchange.
Co-op Mode: Some events and content allow co-op play. Coordinating with friends is significantly more efficient than matchmaking with strangers. Build a co-op team with complementary elements.

Friend Network Optimization

1 Add 30 active friends immediately. Friend capacity is 30. Fill it on day 1. Remove inactive friends (offline 7+ days) and replace them.
2 Curate for support units. Keep friends whose support memories complement your account. If you lack Rafayel, find a friend with a maxed Inferno Waltz Rafayel.
3 Exchange gifts daily. 10 stamina per gift × 30 friends = 300 stamina/day. This is equivalent to 5 hours of natural stamina regen. Missing this = significant lost progress.

Co-op Strategy

When playing co-op content, follow these principles:

Role Assignment: Decide who brings DPS and who brings support before entering. Two DPS with no support will struggle. One DPS + one support is the standard co-op formula.

Element Coverage: Each player should cover different elements. If the boss is weak to Frost, one player brings Zayne while the other brings Xavier for Light damage.

Communication: Coordinate ultimate timing. Stacking ults deals more damage than staggered use. Use voice chat or quick chat for burst synchronization.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Not joining a crew. Solo play misses out on crew shop SSR shards (30 shards/month = 1 SSR every 2 months from crew alone), crew buffs, and crew boss rewards. Join a crew the moment it unlocks.

Mistake #2: Staying in a dead crew. A crew where 50% of members are inactive gives half the crew coins, half the buffs, and may not clear crew boss. Don't be loyal to a dead crew — jump to an active one.

Mistake #3: Not curating friends. Having 30 friends who haven't logged in for 30 days = 0 benefit. Remove and replace friends weekly. Active friend networks provide 300 free stamina per day.

The Bottom Line: Love and Deepspace's social systems are not optional — they're a significant resource multiplier. Join an active crew. Donate daily. Buy SSR shards. Fill your friend list with active players. Exchange gifts daily. Borrow support units. And when co-op content is available, coordinate instead of queuing blindly. A well-connected account progresses 20-30% faster than an isolated one.
