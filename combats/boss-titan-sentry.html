THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Titan Sentry

A colossal guardian that crushes anything in its path. Slow but devastating — one mistake ends the run.

Recommended Power: 75k+ | Element: Physical/Light | Weakness: Light — Xavier excels here. Stay behind the Sentry at all times.

Phase 1: Steady Onslaught (100%-60%)

Stomp: The Sentry raises one foot and stomps. AoE shockwave in front. Dodge sideways — never backwards. Shockwave: Both fists slam the ground. AoE ring expands outward. Jump over or dodge through the ring. Laser Sweep: Beam attack from chest. Slow sweep across the arena. Run parallel to the beam, not away.

Phase 2: Warden's Fury (60%-30%)

Shield Wall: Sentry deploys a frontal energy shield. Attack from behind or wait it out. Orbital Strike: Marks the ground with targeting circles. Three waves. Keep moving. Rush: Charges across the arena. Dodge sideways at the last moment. Punish window is short — use quick attacks.

Phase 3: Final Stand (30%-0%)

Cascade: Rapid sequence of Stomp + Shockwave + Laser. Perfect dodge chain required. Core Overload: Sentry becomes immobilized but pulses massive AoE damage. Burst phase. Use all ults. Annihilation Beam: Arena-wide laser sweep. Only safe zone is directly under the Sentry.

Recommended Team: Xavier (DPS) — exploit Light weakness. Sylus (Sub-DPS/Break) — break the shield wall. Zayne (Support/Heal) — sustain through the AoE damage.

Positional Discipline and Threat Management

The Titan Sentry's frontal attacks cover a 180-degree arc in front of its body. Staying behind the Sentry at all times is not optional — it is the core positioning requirement for this fight. The Sentry rotates slowly, giving you approximately 2 seconds to reposition when it begins turning. Use this window to dash through the Sentry's legs to the opposite side. Never dash sideways; the Sentry's leg sweep hitbox extends further to the sides than its height suggests.

Threat management determines who the Sentry faces. The Sentry always faces the operative who dealt the most damage in the last 5 seconds. This means your main DPS controls the Sentry's facing direction. If you are the main DPS, alternate between attacking from the left side and the right side to keep the Sentry rotating in place rather than fixing on one direction. If you are the support, minimize your damage output during the main DPS's burst window to avoid pulling threat. A threat pull from the support is dangerous because the support often lacks the defensive stats to survive the Sentry's frontal combo.

Shield Break Optimization

The Sentry's Shield Wall in Phase 2 has 20 hits of health regardless of damage per hit. Fast-hitting attacks break the shield faster than slow, heavy attacks. Xavier's light attack chain at point-blank range breaks the shield in approximately 4 seconds. Sylus's execute breaks 8 hits instantly. Rafayel's charged fire attack breaks 6 hits over its duration. The optimal shield-breaking rotation is: Sylus execute (8 hits) + Xavier point-blank chain (10 hits) + any remaining hits from the third operative.

Breaking the shield stuns the Sentry for 4 seconds. During this stun window, all damage is doubled and the Sentry cannot attack. This is the best burst window in the entire fight. Save all resonance skills and Ardent Oaths specifically for the shield break stun. A coordinated team can deal approximately 30% of the Sentry's Phase 2 HP during a single stun window. If you miss the stun window because the shield was not broken in time, retreat and reset — attempting Phase 2 without the stun advantage extends the fight significantly.

The Titan Sentry's Laser Sweep cannot hit the ground directly beneath its body. When you see the chest panel glow, dash toward the Sentry and stand under its torso. Attack its legs while the laser passes harmlessly overhead.

Advanced Tip: The Sentry's shield wall counts as a weakness break opportunity. Use Sylus's execute to deal massive shield damage. Breaking the shield stuns the Sentry for 4 seconds.

Summary: Stay behind, dodge sideways, and break the shield. The Titan Sentry is a gear check — if your DPS is high enough, the fight is straightforward. If not, the Cascade phase will overwhelm you.
