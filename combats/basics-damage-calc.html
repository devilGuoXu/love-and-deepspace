Damage Calculation Guide

Understand the numbers behind your damage — formulas, breakpoints, and optimization math.

ENTRY_#0502026.07.01MATH

Why Math Matters: Most players build by feel — "this set feels strong" or "I need more CRIT." Math removes guesswork. A 10% damage optimization from understanding formulas is equivalent to two breakthrough tiers or one full memory set. Knowledge is the most efficient resource.

Core Damage Formula

Final Damage = ATK × Skill Multiplier × (1 + DMG Bonus) × (1 + CRIT DMG if crit) × (1 + Element Bonus) × Enemy DEF Multiplier × (1 + Stagger Bonus). Each multiplier is multiplicative with the others. This means buffs of different types are more valuable than stacking the same type.

Example: +20% ATK and +20% DMG Bonus = 1.2 × 1.2 = 1.44x (44% gain). +40% ATK (no DMG Bonus) = 1.4x (40% gain). The different-type buffs are 4% stronger. Small differences, but they compound across your entire build.

Stat Breakpoints

ATK%: Diminishing returns after +100% ATK (from base ATK). Optimal range: 80-120% bonus ATK. CRIT Rate: Optimal range 65-80%. Below 65%, your damage is inconsistent. Above 80%, you're over-investing — switch to CRIT DMG. CRIT DMG: Optimal range 140-180%. At 65% CRIT Rate and 160% CRIT DMG, your average damage multiplier from crits is 1.65 × 0.65 + 1 × 0.35 = 1.4225 (42% average gain). DMG Bonus: No diminishing returns in practice — stack as high as possible, but don't sacrifice ATK% or CRIT for it.

Substat Value

Each substat roll has a range. One roll: ATK% 4-6%, CRIT Rate 2.5-4%, CRIT DMG 5-8%, Flat ATK 25-40. Convert everything to "effective substat value" (ESV). 1 ESV = 1% ATK = 0.5% CRIT Rate = 1% CRIT DMG = 10 Flat ATK. A memory with 8 ESV across 4 substats is good. 12+ ESV is exceptional. 16+ ESV is god roll.

Enhancement strategy: Enhance to +3. If you get a bad substat roll, salvage. If good, enhance to +6. If second roll is bad, salvage. If both good, enhance to +9 and lock. Never take a memory to +12 without locking at +9 first — the +9 to +12 cost is very high for one more roll.

Team Damage Math

Team DPS = Main DPS DPS + Sub-DPS DPS × (field time ratio) + Support DPS × (field time ratio). In a hypercarry setup: Main DPS has 80% field time, Sub-DPS 15%, Support 5%. Optimizing the main DPS is 80% of team damage. A 10% improvement on main DPS = 8% team DPS gain. A 10% improvement on support = 0.5% team DPS gain.

Key Insight: Use the "1% rule" — if changing one substat or piece improves your calculated DPS by 1% or more, it's worth the resource investment. Below 1%, the improvement is negligible and resources are better spent elsewhere. This prevents perfectionism waste.

Summary: Damage = ATK × DMG Bonus × CRIT × Element × DEF × Stagger. ATK% 80-120%, CRIT 65-80%, CRIT DMG 140-180%. ESV system to evaluate substats. Enhance to +3/+6, salvage bad rolls. Main DPS optimization is 80% of team DPS.
