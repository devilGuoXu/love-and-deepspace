Resource Efficiency Guide

Farm smarter, not harder — stamina allocation and material optimization.

ENTRY_#0382026.06.30GAMEPLAY

Core Philosophy: This game has soft daily caps on every resource. You cannot out-grind inefficiency. The best players aren't the ones who farm the most — they're the ones who waste the least.

Stamina Allocation Priority

Stamina is the most scarce resource in the game. Your daily income is fixed (natural regen + daily missions). Every stamina point should have a clear purpose. Priority tier list: Tier 1 — Weekly Boss Rewards (limited per week, use all 3 claims). Tier 2 — EXP Books (if any operative is underleveled). Tier 3 — Memory Enhancement Materials (cores, shards). Tier 4 — Gold. Tier 5 — Protocore Farm. Tier 6 — Character Upgrade Materials.

Never farm Tier 5 or Tier 6 when Tier 2-4 are incomplete. Protocores are infinite RNG — you can always farm them later. Level caps are time-gated.

Event Efficiency

Events offer the best stamina-to-reward ratios in the game. Always clear event shops before spending stamina on permanent content. The event shop exchange rate is roughly 2x-3x better than permanent farming nodes. Prioritize event-limited items (event currency, exclusive memories) first, then standard materials. If you complete the event shop, stop spending stamina on the event — farm permanent content instead.

Bounty events (double-drop weeks): Check the event details. Some double-drop events apply to specific farming nodes. Use these exclusively for the featured material during the event period. Stockpile. Even if you don't need the material now, you will later. A week's worth of double-drop farming can cover a month of material needs.

Material Conversion

Know your conversion rates. 60 stamina = 1 boss material = 5 EXP books = 25K gold = 2 protocore runs. When choosing between farming nodes, convert to a common unit (stamina) and compare. Example: If you need both gold and EXP books, EXP books are more stamina-efficient because the EXP book node gives a fixed value while the gold node has variance.

Low-tier to high-tier conversion: 3 low-tier materials combine into 1 mid-tier. 3 mid-tier into 1 high-tier. This 3:1 ratio means farming high-tier directly is 2x more stamina-efficient than farming low-tier and converting up. Always farm the highest tier node you can clear.

Long-Term Planning

Plan one patch ahead. If the next banner features Rafayel, pre-farm his materials now. Pre-farming 2 weeks of materials saves you 2 weeks of waiting once the banner drops. Prediction tips: New event banners typically align with the story update. Elemental trends follow the Abyss rotation. If the current Abyss favors Light, the next might favor Dark or Frost. Pre-farm the counter-element.

Don't hyper-invest in a single operative. Spreading resources across 4-6 operatives (your top two per element) is more valuable than maxing one. Abyss requires 8 operatives. If you have only 2 built operatives, you're bottlenecked.

Key Insight: The "stamina bank" concept: stockpile 200-300 reserve stamina (from mail, battle pass, events). Never claim them until you need them. Emergency stamina for unexpected double-drop events or new content drops. This reserve can be the difference between clearing a new Abyss floor on day 1 vs week 3.

Summary: Priority order: Weekly boss > Level cap > Event shop > Books > Gold > Protocores. Pre-farm one patch ahead. Stockpile stamina reserve. Farm highest tier available. Efficiency compounds over months.
