EXTREME LAB — DIMINISHING RETURNS

Stat Diminishing Returns

Every stat in Deepspace follows the law of diminishing returns. The first 30% of CRIT Rate is worth more than the second 30%. Know where the breakpoints are and stop investing past them.

ENTRY_#LAB_022026.07.02MATH

Core Concept: Diminishing returns in Deepspace is not a mechanic — it is a mathematical inevitability. Every additive stat bracket has a saturation point where adding more of the same stat yields less percentage increase than adding a different stat. The optimal build is the one that balances all brackets, not one that maxes a single stat.

ATK% Diminishing Returns

Formula: 1 + (total ATK% from all sources). Base ATK is the operative's ATK at level 60 with no protocores. Each 10% ATK bonus adds 10% of base ATK as flat ATK. Breakpoints: 0-50% ATK: Each 10% ATK = +7-8% total damage increase (high value). 50-100% ATK: Each 10% ATK = +5-6% total damage (medium value). 100-150% ATK: Each 10% ATK = +3-4% total damage (low value). 150%+ ATK: Each 10% ATK = +2% or less (very low value — stop here). Rule: Once you have +100% ATK from all sources (memories, protocores, set bonuses), switch to investing in other brackets. The 100% ATK breakpoint is the single most important threshold in the game.

CRIT Rate Diminishing Returns

Formula: CRIT Multiplier = 1 + (CRIT Rate × CRIT DMG). This is a product, not a sum — the relationship between Rate and DMG is multiplicative, which creates a different kind of diminishing return. Breakpoints: 0-50% CRIT Rate: Each 10% Rate gives +5-8% total damage (assuming 100%+ CRIT DMG). Very high value. 50-70% CRIT Rate: Each 10% Rate gives +3-5% total damage. Medium value. 70-85% CRIT Rate: Each 10% Rate gives +2-3% total damage. Low value. 85%+ CRIT Rate: Each 10% Rate gives +1-2% total damage. Very low value. Rule: Stop investing in CRIT Rate at 70% for most operatives. Beyond 70%, the rolls are better spent on CRIT DMG or ATK%. The optimal CRIT Rate:CRIT DMG ratio is approximately 1:2 (e.g., 70% Rate : 140% DMG).

Energy Recharge Diminishing Returns

Formula: Energy Recharge affects how quickly you charge your Ardent Oath. The relationship is not linear — ER has a hard cap and a soft cap. Soft cap (120% ER): Enough for one Ardent Oath per rotation cycle. Most DPS operatives need 120% ER minimum. Medium zone (120-150% ER): Provides a comfortable energy buffer if you miss a few perfect dodges. Worth investing in for supports. Hard cap (150-180% ER): Beyond 150% ER, the energy gain per point drops by 50%. Only Zayne (support build) needs to go to 160%+ ER. Hard cap limit (200% ER): Absolute maximum. Any ER beyond 200% gives zero benefit. Never build ER on a DPS past 130% — every ER roll past 130% is a wasted damage roll.

The Golden Rule of Build Optimization: Your damage is the product of (ATK bracket) × (CRIT bracket) × (DMG Bonus bracket) × (Element bracket). To maximize the product, you need all brackets to be roughly equal. A build with +80% ATK / 60% CRIT Rate / 120% CRIT DMG / 30% DMG Bonus has a multiplier product of 1.8 × 1.72 × 1.3 = 4.02. A build with +120% ATK / 40% CRIT Rate / 100% CRIT DMG / 10% DMG Bonus has a product of 2.2 × 1.4 × 1.1 = 3.39. The first build is 18% stronger despite having lower ATK. Balance > peak stats.

Defense & HP Diminishing Returns

For support builds (Zayne): HP% has a similar curve to ATK% for DPS. 0-50% HP: Each 10% HP = +7% shield value. 50-80% HP: Each 10% HP = +5% shield value. 80%+ HP: Each 10% HP = +3% shield value. Zayne breakpoint: 35k HP (approximately +70% HP from base). Past 35k, invest in Energy Recharge instead. DEF% has even steeper diminishing returns — past 30% DEF from gear, each point gives less than 2% effective HP increase. Do not build DEF on anyone.

Summary: ATK% soft cap at +100%. CRIT Rate soft cap at 70% (1:2 ratio with CRIT DMG). ER soft cap at 120% for DPS, 160% for supports. Hard cap 200% ER. Build balance: ATK bracket × CRIT bracket × DMG Bonus bracket = damage. Balance beats peak stat stacking every time.
