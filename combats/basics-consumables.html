Food Buffs

Food buffs give percentage-based stat increases that last for the duration of a combat instance. They are crafted in the Home system using ingredients from the shop and event rewards. ATK Food (+15%): The most generally useful food buff. Use before any difficult combat encounter. HP Food (+15%): Useful for survivability in boss fights where you are getting one-shot. DEF Food (+15%): Less useful — DEF matters less than HP for survivability. CRIT Food (+10% rate): Excellent for CRIT-dependent operatives. Specialty Foods: Some event-only foods give unique buffs like energy regen or resonance cooldown reduction. These are rare — save them for the hardest content.

Potions & Elixirs

Potions are in-combat healing and utility items. They have a carry limit of 5 per combat instance, so choose wisely. Health Potion: Restores 30% HP. The most important potion for survivability. Energy Potion: Restores 20% energy. Use for longer fights where you need an extra Ardent Oath. Resonance Potion: Reduces skill cooldown by 15% for 10 seconds. Excellent burst damage tool. Stamina Potion: Restores 30 stamina (not combat — this is for the map). Use when farming materials. Potions are crafted or obtained from events. Do not hoard them — they are meant to be used.

Special Items

Special consumables come from events, battle pass tiers, and achievement rewards. Resin: Restores 60 stamina. Use during double-drop events for maximum value. Wishing Well Tokens: Used in the wishing well shop. Save these for SSR crates. Protocol Data Packs: Instantly level up memories. Save for level 70+ memories (higher level = more EXP per pack value). Energy Crystals: Open-world exploration items that give small stat boosts. Collect all of them for permanent account bonuses. Event-Exclusive Buffs: Some events give temporary buffs (e.g., "+20% ATK in Abyss"). Use these during the event period for maximum farming efficiency.

Farming Consumables

Consumables can be obtained from multiple sources. Home Cooking: The primary source of food buffs. Level up your kitchen facilities to unlock better recipes. Event Shops: Events always stock potions, food ingredients, and special items. Buy them — they are stamina-efficient. Battle Pass: The free track gives a steady trickle of potions and food ingredients. Weekly Bounties: Sometimes give consumables as rewards. Co-op Bosses: Small chance to drop potion ingredients. General advice: Never buy consumables with diamonds. They are not worth it. Get them from events and natural gameplay instead.

Crafting Guide

Crafting in the Home system requires ingredients and energy. Ingredients: Meat, vegetables, and spices from the shop (gold cost) or event rewards. Crafting Energy: Your Home has a daily energy pool for cooking. Use it every day. Priority recipes: +15% ATK food (best general buff), CRIT rate food (for DPS operatives), Health Potions (survivability). Batch crafting: Once you unlock a recipe, you can batch craft up to 5 at a time for a small energy discount. Crafting rotation: Focus on ATK food on weekdays (for weekly bosses and Abyss), save CRIT food for SHC attempts on weekends.

Tip: Always use a food buff before attempting a new Abyss floor for the first time. If you clear it with the buff, you know your stats are sufficient. If you fail, the food buff means one less variable in your diagnosis.

When to Use What

Weekly Bosses: ATK food + Health Potion. You know the fight mechanics, so maximize damage with a safety net. Abyss Floors (first clear): CRIT food + Resonance Potion. The burst damage helps with time pressure. SHC attempts: Specialty food (if available) + Energy Potion. Longer fights benefit from extra energy. Co-op: DEF food + Health Potion. Co-op boss damage is unpredictable, prioritize survival. Event farming: No consumables needed. Save them for difficult content. Only use consumables on content that actually challenges you. Using them on trivial fights is wasteful.

Summary: Food buffs give +15% stats — use ATK food for general combat, CRIT food for DPS operatives. Potions have a 5-carry limit, prioritize Health and Energy potions. Use special items (Resin, Data Packs) during double-drop events. Craft food daily in the Home system. Never buy consumables with diamonds. Consumables are free 10-15% power — do not ignore them.
