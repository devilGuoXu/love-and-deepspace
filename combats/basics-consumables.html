Combat Consumables Guide

Food, potions, and special items can give you a 10-20% combat advantage. Most players never use them. They are leaving free power on the table.

ENTRY_#0672026.07.02CONSUMABLES

Why Consumables Matter: A fully buffed character can deal 15-20% more damage than an unbuffed one. In Abyss floors 9-12, this is often the difference between a clear and a wipe. Consumables are cheap to make and last for the entire fight. There is no reason not to use them in difficult content.

Food Buffs (Pre-Fight)

ATK-Boosting Food: +10-15% ATK for 30 minutes. The most universally useful buff. Use before any difficult boss fight or Abyss floor. CRIT Rate Food: +8-12% CRIT Rate for 30 minutes. Excellent for burst teams that need to guarantee crits on their Ardent Oath window. DEF-Boosting Food: +15-20% DEF for 30 minutes. Niche — only useful on survival floors (Abyss floor 8). Otherwise, ATK food is better. Element-Specific Food: +15-20% elemental damage for 30 minutes. Best in slot if you know the boss's weakness. Stacking rule: You can have 1 food buff active at a time. Using a second food replaces the first. Choose the best food for the specific fight — do not stack randomly.

Potions (In-Combat)

HP Potion (Small): Heals 20% HP. Cooldown: 60s. Carry 3 max. Use when your support's shield is on cooldown and you are below 40% HP. HP Potion (Large): Heals 40% HP. Cooldown: 90s. Carry 1 max. Save for emergencies only (below 20% HP and no shield available). Energy Potion: Restores 30% Ardent Oath energy. Cooldown: 120s. Carry 1 max. Use to accelerate your Oath rotation in DPS races. Stamina Potion (Combat): Restores 1 dodge charge. Cooldown: 45s. Carry 2 max. Extremely valuable for boss fights with tight dodge windows — use it when you have used all 3 dodge charges and need one more. Potion limit: You can carry a maximum of 5 potions total into combat (any combination). Choose your loadout based on the fight. For DPS checks: bring 1 Energy + 4 Small HP. For survival checks: bring 1 Large HP + 3 Small HP + 1 Stamina.

Special Items

Revive Token (Limited): Revives a downed operative once per fight. Event-exclusive — do not waste on easy content. Save for Abyss floor 12 attempts. Damage Amplifier (Limited): +30% damage for 15 seconds. Extremely rare — save for your most serious clear attempts. Shield Booster (Limited): +50% shield value for next shield cast. Combine with Zayne for a massive 10k+ shield. Rule: Limited items are called "limited" for a reason. Do not use them on farming runs. Use them only when you are attempting a personal record or account-first clear.

Pro Tip: Food and potions can be crafted in the home base kitchen (if this system exists in Deepspace) or purchased from the shop. Always keep a stock of ATK food + Small HP potions — these are the most commonly needed. Craft in batches of 10 to save time. Set a weekly reminder to restock your consumable inventory. A player with consumables is 15% stronger than a player without — and consumables are free or cheap.

Consumable Priority List

Always carry: 2 Small HP Potions. 1 ATK Food. For boss fights: Replace one HP potion with Energy Potion. Use element-specific food if applicable. For Abyss floors 11-12: Bring max potions (5). Use ATK or element food. Bring 1 Stamina Potion. Consider using limited items if this is your first clear attempt. For co-op: Bring 3 Small HP + 1 Large HP + 1 Stamina Potion. ATK food (you cannot rely on co-op healers). For farming: No consumables needed. Save them for difficult content.

Summary: Food: ATK or Element-specific (30min). Potions: HP Small (60s cd), Energy (120s cd), Stamina (45s cd). Carry limit: 5 total. Always bring 2 HP + 1 ATK food to difficult fights. Limited items only for first-clear attempts. Craft in batches. Restock weekly. Consumables = 15% free power.
