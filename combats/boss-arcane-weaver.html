THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Arcane Weaver

Homing orbs. Dimension tears. A boss that punishes hesitation harder than any other.

ENTRY_#110 2026.06.29 ELITE_BOSS

The Core Insight: The Arcane Weaver is not a DPS check — it's a patience check. Most players fail because they try to burst through its mechanics instead of waiting for the openings. This boss has the tightest dodge windows in the game, but also the longest punish windows if you learn them. Treat it like a dance, not a race.

Boss Overview

The Arcane Weaver is a mage-type elite boss found in mid-to-late Abyss floors and high-level Open Orbit stages. It specializes in ranged magic attacks, dimensional manipulation, and punishing aggressive play.

Element Neutral (no inherent weakness)
Recommended Elements Light (Xavier) — interrupts channeling
Status Resistance High (difficult to freeze/stun)
Dodge Difficulty Very High (0.2-0.3s windows)
Punish Window Long (3-4s after each attack)

Attack Patterns — Complete Breakdown

The Arcane Weaver has seven distinct attacks. Each requires a different response.

Homing Orb Primary — Spammed Often

Tell: The Weaver raises its staff. A blue orb forms at the tip over 0.8 seconds.

Behavior: The orb tracks your position and fires after a 0.5-second lock-on. It follows you for up to 3 seconds before expiring.

Dodge Timing: Wait until the orb flashes white — that's 0.2 seconds before impact. Dodge sideways (not backward). Backward dodges don't clear the tracking radius.

Punish: After the orb fires (hit or miss), the Weaver is stationary for 2 seconds. Land 3-4 normal attacks or one RS.

Dimension Tear Phase 1 Special

Tell: The Weaver slams its staff into the ground. A purple rift opens on the floor.

Behavior: After 1.5 seconds, the rift explodes in a large AoE circle. Standing in the rift deals ticking damage (15% HP per tick) until it detonates.

Dodge Timing: You have 1.5 seconds. Do NOT panic dodge. Walk out of the rift circle — it's slow enough. Save your dodge for the follow-up attack.

Punish: After the rift detonates, the Weaver has a 1-second recovery. Use a charged attack or quick RS.

Arcane Barrage Phase 1 — Rage Mode

Tell: The Weaver glows purple and raises both arms. Three magic circles appear around it.

Behavior: Three rapid-fire projectiles in a spread pattern. Each projectile deals 25% HP. The third is aimed slightly behind your current position (catches panic back-dodgers).

Dodge Timing: Triple dodge — left, right, left. The third dodge is the most important: delay it by 0.1 seconds because the third shot is delayed. Most players get hit here.

Punish: After the barrage, the Weaver exhausts for 3 seconds. Full N5 string + RS.

Phase Shift HP Threshold — 60%

Trigger: When the Weaver reaches 60% HP, it teleports to the center of the arena and becomes invulnerable for 2 seconds.

Behavior: During invulnerability, it summons 4 homing orbs that circle the arena. After 2 seconds, the orbs fire simultaneously toward your position.

Strategy: Stop attacking at 62% HP. Wait for the teleport. Position yourself at the edge of the arena. When the orbs appear, count 1.5 seconds, then perfect dodge through the orb volley toward the Weaver.

Reward: If you dodge all 4 orbs, the Weaver is stunned for 4 seconds — your biggest damage window in the entire fight.

Void Salvo Phase 2 — New Attack

Tell: Below 60% HP, the Weaver gains a new attack. It creates a void sphere above its head that fires a continuous beam that sweeps the arena.

Behavior: The beam tracks your position slowly. It deals 10% HP per 0.5 seconds of contact. The beam lasts 4 seconds and covers 180 degrees.

Dodge Timing: Run perpendicular to the beam direction. It turns slowly — about 30 degrees per second. You can outrun it without dodging. Only dodge if you're cornered.

Punish: The Weaver is vulnerable during the entire beam channel. If you position behind it (which is safe), you get 4 seconds of free damage.

Arcane Nova Phase 2 — Enrage

Tell: Below 30% HP, the Weaver gains a nova attack. It charges for 2 seconds, then releases a full-arena explosion.

Behavior: The nova covers 100% of the arena. There is no safe zone. The only way to survive is a perfectly timed dodge at the moment of detonation.

Dodge Timing: Watch the Weaver's glow. When the light reaches maximum brightness, wait 0.3 seconds, then dodge. The window is 0.2 seconds — the tightest in the fight.

Punish: The nova consumes the Weaver's remaining energy. It enters a 5-second exhaustion state afterward. This is your kill window. Use everything.

Phase Summary

Phase 1 (100%-60%) Homing Orb, Dimension Tear, Arcane Barrage
Phase Transition (60%) Phase Shift — invulnerable + 4 orbs. Stun if dodged.
Phase 2 (60%-30%) Adds Void Salvo beam. Same other attacks.
Enrage (30%-0%) Arcane Nova added. Attack speed +20%.

Recommended Team Compositions

XAVIER (Main DPS)

Why: Light element interrupts the Weaver's channeling attacks. Xavier's RS can cancel the Dimension Tear charge, giving you an extra punish window. His N5 string fits perfectly into the 3-second punish windows.

Build: 6-piece Solar Resonance. Prioritize Crit Rate (70%+) for consistent burst.

ZAYNE (Support/DPS)

Why: While the Weaver has high freeze resistance, Zayne's Frost damage still applies the 25% water amplification for Rafayel. If running dual DPS, Zayne sets up Rafayel's detonation window during the exhaustion phases.

Build: 4-piece Permafrost + 2 Frostbloom. HP% focus for survivability.

RAFAYEL (Sustained DPS)

Why: Rafayel's DoT sustained damage is effective against the Weaver's long punish windows. Apply DoTs during the 3-4 second openings, then detonate during the Phase Shift stun. His AoE also helps if adds spawn.

Build: 6-piece Tidal Wrath. Stack ATK% and Crit Rate.

SYLUS (High Risk/Reward)

Why: Sylus's perfect dodge synergy shines against the Weaver's tight dodge windows. Each successfully dodged orb or barrage feeds his Dark Energy loop. However, one missed dodge breaks his rotation and costs significant DPS.

Build: 6-piece Shadow Bind. Energy Regen substats for dodge recovery.

Optimal Team

Xavier (Main) + Rafayel (Sub) + Zayne (Flex) — Xavier handles the interrupt game, Rafayel provides sustained damage during punish windows, and Zayne offers survivability and Frost amplification. This team has no significant weakness against the Weaver's moveset.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Dodging Homing Orbs too early. The orb tracks you for 3 seconds. If you dodge when it fires, you'll i-frame through it. If you dodge when it forms, you'll be in recovery when it actually hits. Wait for the white flash.

Mistake #2: Over-committing during Phase 1. The Weaver's punish windows are generous but not infinite. If you try to squeeze an extra N2, you'll get hit by the next Homing Orb. Learn the rhythm: attack → wait for counter → dodge → punish.

Mistake #3: Panicking during Phase Shift. Four orbs at once is intimidating. Don't spam dodge. Count to 1.5 after they appear, then one clean dodge through them. The timing is consistent every time.

Mistake #4: Ignoring the enrage timer. Below 30%, the Weaver attacks 20% faster. If you're used to the Phase 1 timing, you'll dodge early and get hit. Mentally adjust: if you think it's time to dodge, wait 0.1 more seconds.

The Bottom Line: The Arcane Weaver is a skill gate boss. If you can beat it consistently, you're ready for Floor 12. If you're struggling, the problem is almost always dodge timing — specifically dodging too early. Slow down. Watch the tells. Trust the flash. The Weaver punishes panic, but rewards patience more than any other boss in the game.
