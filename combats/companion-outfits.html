COMPANION GUIDE — OUTFITS

Companion Outfits Guide

Each operative has multiple outfits that change their appearance in the Destiny Cafe, dates, and photo mode. This guide covers every outfit and how to get it.

ENTRY_#COM_042026.07.02OUTFITS

Why Outfits Matter: Outfits are purely cosmetic but they change the operatives' appearance in all non-combat content — Destiny Cafe, date scenes, photo mode, and Tender Moments. Some outfits also unlock unique dialogue and interactions.

Outfit Categories

Outfits fall into four categories: Base Outfit: The default outfit every operative starts with. Xavier wears his hunter uniform. Zayne wears his doctor coat. Rafayel wears his casual artist clothes. Sylus wears his signature dark attire. These cannot be removed. Free Unlockable Outfits: Obtained through affinity milestones and event completion. No real money required. Examples: Xavier's casual wear at Affinity 50, Zayne's winter coat at Affinity 80. Paid Outfits (Battle Pass): The paid battle pass ($5) includes one outfit per season for a specific operative. These rotate every patch. Current battle pass outfits cannot be obtained after the pass ends, but they may return in the shop later. Premium Outfits (Shop): Direct purchase outfits costing 1,000-2,000 diamonds or real money. These are the highest quality outfits with unique animations, special idle poses, and exclusive voice lines. Premium outfits are permanent additions to the shop.

Celestial (Shop, 1,500 diamonds): Star-patterned coat with glow effects. Summer (Event, free): Light summer clothes. Zayne: Base: Doctor coat. Relaxed (Affinity 80, free): Cardigan and scarf. Bohemian (Affinity 50, free): Flowing shirt and accessories. Evening (Battle Pass S3, paid): Elegant dinner attire. Inferno (Shop, 1,800 diamonds): Flame-themed outfit with particle effects. Beach (Event, free): Summer beach look. Sylus: Base: Dark noble attire. Relaxed (Affinity 80, free): Open-collar shirt. Aristocrat (Battle Pass S4, paid): Formal Onychinus regalia. Shadowlord (Shop, 2,000 diamonds): Full dark armor with cape. Casual (Event, free): Simple dark clothes without armor elements.

Outfit Acquisition Strategy

Free outfits: Always unlock these first. They are tied to affinity milestones and limited events. Prioritize your main operative's affinity to reach their free outfit breakpoints. Battle Pass outfits: The $5 battle pass is the best value in the game for outfits. You get an exclusive outfit plus pulls and materials. If you are considering spending any money, start with the battle pass. Shop outfits: Premium shop outfits cost 1,200-2,000 diamonds. At the standard diamond-to-dollar rate, this is $10-20 per outfit. Only buy shop outfits for your favorite operative. They are expensive and purely cosmetic. Do not sacrifice pulls for outfits unless you are a collector. Event outfits: Limited event outfits are often free but require completing all event stages. Always finish event stories to claim the free outfit. Missed event outfits may never return.

Outfit Functionality: Outfits are not just skins. Each outfit changes: (1) The operative's appearance in the Destiny Cafe. (2) Their outfit in photo mode and Tender Moments. (3) Some outfits add unique idle animations (e.g., Xavier's Celestial outfit has a stargazing idle animation). (4) Premium outfits unlock exclusive voice lines when equipped. Outfits do NOT affect combat, stats, affinity gain, or any gameplay system. They are 100% cosmetic. However, the cosmetic quality varies significantly — premium shop outfits are noticeably more detailed than free or battle pass outfits. If you spend most of your game time in the Destiny Cafe, investing in a premium outfit for your main operative enhances the experience considerably.

Outfit Equipping

To equip an outfit: Enter the Destiny Cafe. Tap the wardrobe icon in the top-right corner. Select the operative. Choose the outfit slot. Outfits can be changed at any time with no cost. Each operative can save up to 3 outfit presets for quick switching. Outfit presets are useful if you want different looks for different moods or date scenes. Note that date scenes often use a specific outfit regardless of your preset — some Tender Moments have canon outfits.

Summary: Each operative has 5-6 outfits: 1 base, 2-3 free (affinity/events), 1 battle pass (paid), 1 premium shop (paid). Free outfits are tied to Affinity 50/80 and limited events. Battle Pass outfits ($5) are the best paid value. Premium outfits (1,200-2,000 diamonds) offer the highest quality. Outfits are purely cosmetic but enhance the Destiny Cafe and date experience. Prioritize free outfits first, then battle pass if spending, shop outfits only for favorites.
