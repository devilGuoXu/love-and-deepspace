Outfit Types

Companion outfits fall into several categories: Free Base Outfits (unlocked by default, through Affinity milestones, or through main story completion), Premium Shop Outfits (purchased with paid gems, usually $5-15 each), Event Reward Outfits (earned through event participation — often the most coveted), and Limited Collaboration Outfits (tied to real-world brand or IP collaborations, extremely rare and time-limited).

Each outfit includes a full character model retexture with unique details. Premium outfits often include accessory variants (glasses, hats, jewelry) that can be mixed and matched. Event outfits may have special effects like particle trails or unique idle animations. No outfit provides gameplay advantages, but they do unlock exclusive photo mode poses and dialogue variants when worn in the base.

Xavier's Wardrobe

Xavier's default outfit is his standard Hunter Corps uniform. His free Affinity milestone outfit (Level 30) is "Stargazer's Coat" — a elegant navy overcoat with subtle star patterns. His premium shop outfits include "Midnight Formal" (black tuxedo, $9.99) and "Celestial Traveler" (casual space-inspired jacket, $14.99). His event-exclusive "Fallen Star" outfit features a damaged celestial motif with animated particle effects.

Zayne's Wardrobe

Zayne's default is his medical lab coat attire. His free outfit (Affinity Level 30) is "Winter Physician" — a longer coat with fur-lined collar. Premium options include "Crystal Protocol" (formal suit with ice-blue accents, $9.99) and "Frost Walker" (cold-weather expedition gear, $14.99). His event-exclusive "Permafrost Lord" outfit features animated frost crystals around the shoulders.

Rafayel's Wardrobe

Rafayel starts in his artist's casual wear. Free outfit at Affinity 30 is "Coral Artist" — a flowing shirt with ocean-print patterns. Premium shop has "Masquerade Waltz" (formal ball attire, $9.99) and "Ocean Reverie" (beach-casual summer outfit, $14.99). Event-exclusive "Tidal Sorcerer" features flowing water animations on the fabric.

Sylus's Wardrobe

Sylus's default is his Onychinus commander outfit. Free outfit (Affinity 30) is "Shadow Commander" — a sleeker version of his default with gold trim. Premium options include "Unyielding Lord" (formal black suit with red accents, $9.99) and "Dark Protocol" (tactical combat gear, $14.99). Event-exclusive "Onychnus King" features animated shadow effects and a unique idle stance.

Tip: Before buying a premium outfit, check the photo mode poses it unlocks. Some outfits have unique poses that dramatically improve your photo composition options. The "Masquerade Waltz" Rafayel outfit, for example, unlocks a dance pose that's the most requested outfit feature in the community.

Summary: Companion outfits are cosmetic items that unlock unique dialogue and photo mode poses. Prioritize free Affinity milestone outfits (level 30 for each operative), then consider event-exclusive outfits for your favorite operative before they expire. Premium shop outfits are permanent additions, so there's no rush. Outfits have zero gameplay impact — spend only if you value the cosmetic experience.
