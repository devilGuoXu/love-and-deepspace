Event Currency Optimization

Every event introduces a temporary currency. The difference between efficient and inefficient spending can be 30%+ more rewards. Here is how to squeeze every drop of value from your event stamina.

ENTRY_#EVT_152026.07.03SHOP

Key Insight: Event currency exchange rates are not created equal. The first-tier rewards (limited materials, SSR shards) are worth 5-10x more per currency than the bottom-tier rewards (gold, XP materials). Many players run out of stamina because they clear the entire shop instead of prioritizing the top 3-5 items. A focused approach clears 90% of the value with 60% of the stamina.

Event Currency Types

Most events use one of two currency systems: a single universal currency used for all shop exchanges, or a dual-currency system where one currency is earned from combat and another from minigames/story. Understanding the system determines your farming strategy.

Event Tokens
Earned from stamina stages — main currency
⭐
Challenge Stars
Earned from boss challenges — one-time rewards
Story Tickets
Earned from event story — fixed amounts
Minigame Points
Earned from event minigames — capped daily

Exchange Value Rankings

Not all shop items are equal. Here is how to rank them by value per currency spent. Items are ranked from highest priority (buy first) to lowest priority (skip unless overflowing).

TIER 1 — MUST BUY
Limited SSR Shards, Limited Memory CRates, Namecards, Avatar Frames
These are exclusive items that will never return (or return only after 6+ months). SSR shards are the highest value item in any event shop — they directly progress your limited memory collection. CRates for limited memories are second. Namecards and frames are purely cosmetic but exclusive. Buy all of these before anything else.
TIER 2 — HIGH VALUE
Standard Banner Tickets, Limited Banner Tickets, Protocore Dust, Enhancement Crystals
Pull tickets are the best non-exclusive value in any event shop. Protocore dust is the most common bottleneck for endgame players. Enhancement crystals are valuable but slightly less efficient than dust. Buy these after securing all Tier 1 items.
TIER 3 — SITUATIONAL
Memory XP Materials, Weapon XP Materials, Elemental Cores, Skill Books
These items have stable exchange rates across events. Buy them if you need them immediately and have spare currency after Tiers 1-2. Do not farm extra stamina for these — they are not efficient compared to regular farming stages.
TIER 4 — SKIP
Gold, Basic Materials (Iron, Wood, etc.), EXP Potions
Gold and basic materials are available from regular farming stages at better exchange rates. Event shop gold is particularly overpriced — you are paying a premium for the convenience of not farming. Skip these entirely unless you have excess currency after clearing everything else.

Priority Order: Exclusive items (SSR shards, CRates, cosmetics) → Pull tickets → Protocore dust → Enhancement materials → XP materials → Gold. Following this order ensures you spend your event currency on the items with the highest long-term value. Gold is almost never worth buying from event shops — you can farm 50% more gold per stamina from regular gold stages.

Optimal Farming Routes

The optimal farming stage depends on the event structure. Most events have a "best stage" that offers the highest currency-per-stamina ratio. Here is how to find and farm it.

Standard Event Structure: Events typically offer 3-5 stages with increasing stamina costs (10, 15, 20, 25, 30). The highest stamina stage almost always has the best currency-per-stamina ratio. In Year 1 events, the ratio was: Stage 1 (10 stamina) = 1.0x, Stage 2 (15) = 1.1x, Stage 3 (20) = 1.2x, Stage 4 (25) = 1.35x, Stage 5 (30) = 1.5x. Always farm the highest stage available unless you cannot clear it efficiently.

Boss Challenge Events: Some events have a boss stage that costs 40 stamina but offers bonus currency for completing specific objectives. These stages are worth farming if you can clear all objectives. The bonus currency effectively increases the ratio by 20-30%, making the boss stage the best place to spend stamina even at the higher cost.

Minigame Events: Minigame currency is usually capped per day (e.g., 500 points/day from minigames). Always hit the daily cap before spending stamina on event stages. Minigame currency is "free" stamina that does not consume your normal stamina pool. Missing the daily cap is like deleting free event currency.

Tip: Before farming any event stage, check community data (Discord, Reddit) for the confirmed best stage ratio. The optimal stage is sometimes not the highest stam stage — a few events in Year 1 had their second-highest stage as the most efficient due to bonus drops. Never assume; always verify with community testing before committing stamina.

Daily Optimization Steps

Follow this daily routine to maximize event currency without burning out or wasting stamina.

1
Collect Passive Currency
Log in and collect any passive currency generation (auto-collect, dispatch, or hourly generation mechanics). Some events generate currency while you are offline. Claim this first before planning your stamina spend.
2
Minigame Daily Cap
Complete any event minigames to hit the daily currency cap. This is "free" currency that does not consume stamina. Minigames usually take 3-5 minutes. Always do this before spending stamina — the currency from minigames reduces the total stamina you need to spend.
3
Complete Daily Missions
Event daily missions reward both event currency and regular daily activity points. Complete these before farming — the mission rewards may change how much stamina you need to spend on farming stages.
4
Farm Optimized Stage
Spend remaining stamina on the optimized event stage. Use stamina refills only if you calculated that you need the extra currency to clear Tier 1-2 shop items. Do not refill just to farm Tier 3-4 items — that is inefficient.
5
Check Shop Progress
Review which Tier 1-2 items remain in the shop. Adjust your farming plan for the next day based on remaining items. Do not leave exclusive items unclaimed because you miscalculated your currency budget.

Should You Refill Stamina?

Stamina refills cost diamonds. The decision to refill depends on your account progression stage and how much you value the event exchange items.

REFILL — YES
You need the exclusive items (SSR shards, CRates)
Exclusive items have no alternative source. If you want the limited memory or cosmetic, refilling stamina to ensure you get it is justified. Budget 3-5 refills per day during the event period. This is the only scenario where stamina refills for events are recommended.
REFILL — MAYBE
You are an endgame player farming protocore dust
If the event shop offers protocore dust at a favorable exchange rate compared to regular protocore farming, refilling stamina for dust can be efficient. Calculate the dust-per-stamina ratio of the event stage vs regular dust farming before committing.
REFILL — NO
You only need Tier 3-4 items (XP, gold, basic mats)
Never spend diamonds to refill stamina for XP materials or gold. Regular farming stages offer the same or better rates. Diamonds are better spent on limited banner pulls. A single refill (50 diamonds) is worth 1/3 of a single pull — is a few XP potions worth that?

The 80/20 Rule: 80% of the event shop's value is in 20% of the items. Clear the top 20% (exclusives, tickets, dust) and ignore the rest. This saves you 40-50% of the stamina you would otherwise spend mindlessly farming Tier 3-4 items. The event shop is designed to make you feel like you need everything — you do not.

Tip: Never let event currency expire. At the end of an event, leftover currency is converted to gold at a terrible rate (usually 1:10). Plan your farming so that you exhaust your currency on Tier 1-2 items with 0-2 days remaining. If you have excess currency in the last 48 hours, spend it on Tier 3 items rather than letting it convert. Even Tier 3 items are better than the gold conversion rate.

Summary: Prioritize exclusive items (SSR shards, CRates, cosmetics) first, then pull tickets, then protocore dust. Skip gold and basic materials from event shops — they are overpriced. Farm the highest stamina stage for best currency-per-stamina ratio, unless community testing confirms a different optimal stage. Complete minigame daily caps before spending stamina. Refill stamina only for exclusive items or favorable dust exchange rates. Never let event currency expire — spend it on Tier 3 items rather than accepting the terrible gold conversion.
