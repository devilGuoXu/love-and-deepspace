Standard Banner SSRs (Permanent)

These Rafayel SSRs are available on the standard banner and may spook you on limited banners. Inferno Waltz (Pyro Set): ATK% main. Resonance: AoE fire damage + ATK buff for party (10%, 8s). Tier: A — solid all-rounder, fits any fire team. Crimson Tide (Pyro Set): CRIT% main. Resonance: single-target fire burst. Tier: B — outclassed by limiteds but functional. Flame Dancer (Scorching Set): HP% main. Resonance: DoT + self-heal. Tier: C — survival build. Standard banner tip: Do not pull specifically for standard SSRs. They will come naturally through lost 50/50s and standard tickets. Inferno Waltz is Rafayel's best standard — if you get it, build it.

Limited SSRs (Event Banners)

These Rafayel SSRs were available on limited banners. Most will rerun eventually. Blazing Sacrifice (Pyro 4-pc): Rafayel's BiS. ATK% main. Unique: resonance applies stacking fire vulnerability (+6% damage taken per stack, max 3). Tier: S — enables the highest fire team DPS. Must-pull. Ember Storm (Pyro Set): ATK% main. Resonance: massive AoE fire burst + CRIT rate self-buff. Tier: A — excellent for AoE content. Phoenix Ascent (Scorching Set): CRIT DMG% main. Resonance: multi-hit fire with burn extension. Tier: A — strong for sustained damage. Volcanic Heart (Pyro Set): ATK% main. Resonance: teleport + invincibility frames. Tier: B — utility focus. Limited pulls strategy: Blazing Sacrifice is Rafayel's BiS. Save 140 pulls for its rerun. Ember Storm and Phoenix Ascent are interchangeable second-best options.

Event & Free SSRs

Free Rafayel SSRs from events and login campaigns: First Anniversary SSR (Pyro Set): ATK% main. Free from anniversary login. Tier: B — solid stat stick, no unique effects. Summer Event SSR (Scorching Set): HP% main. Tier: C — cosmetic value primarily. Battle Pass SSR (Pyro Set): ATK% main. Tier: B — identical kit to Inferno Waltz. Good for building a second Pyro set for abyss teams.

Rafayel Memory Investment Priority: Blazing Sacrifice (S) > Ember Storm / Phoenix Ascent (A) > Inferno Waltz (A) > Anniversary SSR (B) > everything else. If you have Blazing Sacrifice, invest all resources into it. If not, Inferno Waltz will carry you through most content. Do not invest in HP% main stat SSRs for Rafayel — they waste his ATK-scaling kit. The difference between an S-tier and B-tier Rafayel SSR is approximately 28% damage output.

Memory Set Activation Notes

Rafayel's best sets: Pyro 4-pc: +30% fire damage for 12s after resonance. This is Rafayel's default best set. Scorching 2-pc + Pyro 2-pc: +15% ATK + +15% fire damage. Use this if you cannot maintain Pyro 4-pc uptime. Lunar 2-pc + ATK% 2-pc: F2P budget option. 10-15% worse than Pyro 4-pc. Set recommendation: Farm Pyro 4-pc as your primary set. Use the 2+2 hybrid as a temporary bridge.

Summary: 3 standard SSRs (Inferno Waltz A, Crimson Tide B, Flame Dancer C). 4 limited SSRs (Blazing Sacrifice S, Ember Storm A, Phoenix Ascent A, Volcanic Heart B). 3 free SSRs (Anniversary B, Summer C, Battle Pass B). Invest in Blazing Sacrifice first, Pyro 4-pc set. The S-to-B tier gap is ~28% damage.
