Abyssal Floor 7

The first true challenge. Floor 7 introduces the multi-wave format and tests your team's baseline readiness.

ENTRY_#1252026.06.30FLOOR_7
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Required Power: 45k+ | Clear Time: 5:00 | Stellactrum: Match at least 2 colors for significant damage boost.

Wave 1: Trash Mobs

Enemies: 6 standard Wanderers. Low threat individually but they surround you quickly. Strategy: Group them with a circular kite, then AoE them down. Rafayel excels here. Do not let them surround you — the stagger-lock can be fatal.

Wave 2: Elite Sentinel

Sentinel: A mid-tier elite with a shield that reduces damage from the front. Flank it. Add Spawn: At 75% and 50% HP, two additional mobs spawn. Kill them quickly before focusing the Sentinel. Pattern: Shield Bash (frontal cone) → Spin Attack (AoE) → Shield Restore (takes 3s, interrupt with stun).

Wave 3: Commander + Mobs

Commander: A heavier elite with two phases. Phase 1: sword and shield (defensive). Phase 2: greatsword (offensive). Mob Backup: 3 standard Wanderers spawn with the Commander. Kill the Commander first — the mobs despawn when it dies. Dodge Focus: The Commander's greatsword overhead slam has a 1-second windup. Perfect dodge for a 3-second slow-motion punish window.

Recommended Team: Rafayel (AoE clear — burns through mob waves). Xavier (Single target — handles Sentinel and Commander). Any third flex operative.

Stellactrum Matching Guide

Floor 7 is the first Abyss floor where Stellactrum matching provides a significant damage multiplier. Each operative has two Stellactrum colors assigned to their memory slots. Matching the floor's recommended colors to your operative's natural affinities doubles your effective damage output against shielded enemies. Check the floor's color requirement before building your team and assign operatives whose Stellactrum colors match naturally — forcing a mismatch reduces your damage by approximately 30%.

If your roster lacks the right Stellactrum colors, use memories from off-element operatives as filler. A memory's Stellactrum color is fixed, but the memory can be equipped on any operative. The stat penalty for off-element memories is minimal at this power level — approximately 5% stat reduction — which is far less punishing than the 30% penalty for failing Stellactrum matching. This tradeoff is especially useful for clearing Floor 7 with a limited roster.

Resource Management Between Waves

Between waves, you have approximately 10 seconds to prepare. Use this window to swap to your optimal starting operative for the next wave. If your resonance skill is on cooldown, spend these seconds positioning rather than attempting to build energy — energy carries over between waves but positioning does not. During Wave 1, reserve at least one resonance charge for the Sentinel in Wave 2. Using both charges on trash mobs is the most common mistake on this floor.

HP carries over between waves without regeneration. If you finish Wave 1 below 60% HP, consider restarting rather than pushing forward. The Commander in Wave 3 deals enough damage to kill a wounded operative within three hits, and the mob adds will finish the job. A fresh run costs 20 stamina. A failed run at Wave 3 wastes 10 minutes and still costs the stamina. Restart early and restart often if your HP pool is not comfortable for the next wave.

Floor 7's Commander boss uses a predictable attack rotation that repeats every 20 seconds. The rotation is: Shield Bash (2s windup), Spin Attack (1s windup, 360 degree AoE), Shield Restore (3s cast, interruptable), then repeat. Memorizing this rotation allows you to pre-position for each attack. The Shield Bash always targets the operative with highest aggro. The Spin Attack hits all operatives within 5 meters. The Shield Restore always occurs at 75% and 50% HP thresholds.

The Commander's Command Aura buffs all enemies within 10 meters with 20% increased damage and 15% increased attack speed. Kill the Commander first to remove this buff from remaining enemies.

Key Mechanic: Floor 7 is designed as a gear check. If you struggle here, your memories need leveling. Focus on reaching level 60 on your main DPS memories. Stellactrum matching Floor 7 gives roughly +30% damage — don't ignore colors.

Summary: Floor 7 is the Abyss tutorial. Group mobs, AoE them, flank shielded enemies, interrupt shield restores. If you can clear Floor 7 with 3 minutes to spare, you're ready for Floor 8. If you scrape by, farm more memory XP.
