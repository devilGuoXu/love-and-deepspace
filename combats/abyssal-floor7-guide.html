THREAT LEVEL: HIGH

Abyssal Floor 7

The first true challenge. Floor 7 introduces the multi-wave format and tests your team's baseline readiness.

ENTRY_#1252026.06.30FLOOR_7

Required Power: 45k+ | Clear Time: 5:00 | Stellactrum: Match at least 2 colors for significant damage boost.

Wave 1: Trash Mobs

Enemies: 6 standard Wanderers. Low threat individually but they surround you quickly. Strategy: Group them with a circular kite, then AoE them down. Rafayel excels here. Do not let them surround you — the stagger-lock can be fatal.

Wave 2: Elite Sentinel

Sentinel: A mid-tier elite with a shield that reduces damage from the front. Flank it. Add Spawn: At 75% and 50% HP, two additional mobs spawn. Kill them quickly before focusing the Sentinel. Pattern: Shield Bash (frontal cone) → Spin Attack (AoE) → Shield Restore (takes 3s, interrupt with stun).

Wave 3: Commander + Mobs

Commander: A heavier elite with two phases. Phase 1: sword and shield (defensive). Phase 2: greatsword (offensive). Mob Backup: 3 standard Wanderers spawn with the Commander. Kill the Commander first — the mobs despawn when it dies. Dodge Focus: The Commander's greatsword overhead slam has a 1-second windup. Perfect dodge for a 3-second slow-motion punish window.

Recommended Team: Rafayel (AoE clear — burns through mob waves). Xavier (Single target — handles Sentinel and Commander). Any third flex operative.

Key Mechanic: Floor 7 is designed as a gear check. If you struggle here, your memories need leveling. Focus on reaching level 60 on your main DPS memories. Stellactrum matching Floor 7 gives roughly +30% damage — don't ignore colors.

Summary: Floor 7 is the Abyss tutorial. Group mobs, AoE them, flank shielded enemies, interrupt shield restores. If you can clear Floor 7 with 3 minutes to spare, you're ready for Floor 8. If you scrape by, farm more memory XP.
