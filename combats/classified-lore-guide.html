🌑 The Core Insight: The N109 Zone isn't just a location — it's a character. It shaped Sylus. It broke everyone else. To understand the Lord of Onychinus, you must first understand the lawless land that created him.

What Is the N109 Zone?

The N109 Zone is a restricted area in the world of Love and Deepspace — a lawless wasteland where the Hunter's jurisdiction ends and chaos begins. Unlike Linkon City with its order and protection, N109 operates on one simple rule: power is the only law.

Located on the outskirts of civilized territory, the N109 Zone was originally a research sector that collapsed during the Protocore Crisis. What remains is a patchwork of abandoned facilities, criminal strongholds, and the desperate souls who have nowhere else to go.

"In the N109 Zone, there are no rules. Only survivors."
— Unknown resident

Geography — The Wasteland

The N109 Zone is divided into several distinct areas, each with its own character and dangers.

🏭
The Ruins
Abandoned research facilities. Filled with corrupted Protocores and rogue Wanderers. High risk, high reward for scavengers.
💀
The Pit
Underground fighting arena. Where criminals settle disputes and new talents are discovered. Sylus is rumored to have fought here.
🏛️
Onychinus HQ
The seat of Sylus's power. Heavily fortified. Few outsiders have seen the inside and lived to tell the tale.
🌆
The Market
Black market hub. Anything can be bought — information, weapons, even Evol-users. Neutral ground, but barely.

History — How N109 Became Lawless

The N109 Zone wasn't always a wasteland. Here's how it fell.

Year 0
Research sector established. Top scientists study Protocore phenomena.
Year 7
Protocore Crisis. The sector is overrun by Wanderers. Communications lost.
Year 9
Official abandonment. Linkon City declares N109 a "no-go zone." Survivors left behind.
Year 12
Criminal factions emerge. Territory wars begin. The Pit opens.
Year 15
Sylus arrives. Begins consolidating power.
Year 18
Onychinus established. Sylus becomes uncontested ruler.

Sylus — The King of N109

Sylus didn't inherit the N109 Zone. He conquered it. Piece by piece, faction by faction, he dismantled the old powers and built Onychinus in their ashes.

The Arrival
Little is known about Sylus before N109. What is known: he appeared from nowhere, with power that terrified even the most hardened criminals. His Evol — Dark Energy manipulation — is unlike anything seen in the Hunter ranks.
The Rise
Within three years, Sylus had eliminated or absorbed every major faction in the N109 Zone. His methods were brutal but efficient. Those who opposed him disappeared. Those who joined him prospered.
The Rule
Today, Sylus rules N109 with absolute authority. He doesn't need to enforce his power — his reputation does it for him. Few are foolish enough to challenge the Lord of Onychinus.

Life in the N109 Zone

What's it like to live in the lawless land? The answer depends on who you ask — and how much power you have.

For the Powerful
Members of Onychinus live well. Access to resources, protection, and a share of the profits. Loyalty is rewarded. Betrayal is fatal.
For the Desperate
Scavengers, mercenaries, and refugees scrape by on the edges. Danger is constant. Trust is rare. Survival is a daily struggle.
For the Hunters
Official Hunters are forbidden from entering N109. Those who do are rogue — and treated as enemies by Onychinus. Few return.

The Hunter's Perspective — Why N109 Is Off-Limits

From the official stance of the Hunter's Association, the N109 Zone is strictly off-limits. But the reasons are more complicated than simple danger.

⚠️ Official Hunter Policy: Any Hunter found within N109 Zone limits faces immediate suspension and loss of credentials. The Association claims this is for safety. Many believe it's because they can't control what happens there.

💡 Lore Note: The protagonist's journey into the N109 Zone in the main story is a significant turning point — not just for the plot, but for her relationship with Sylus. It's the first time she truly sees the world outside the Association's carefully constructed narrative.

Notable Locations — Deep Dive

📍 The Pit Arena
A brutal fighting pit where criminals, desperate souls, and even Wanderers are forced to battle. Sylus is rumored to have fought his way out of here — and then bought the place. Today, it's one of Onychinus's primary recruitment grounds.
📍 The Silent Market
The largest black market in the N109 Zone. Information, weapons, Protocores, and even people are traded here. Neutral ground — but "neutral" means everyone is armed.
📍 The Observation Deck
A ruined tower overlooking the entire zone. Sylus is said to visit here alone, watching the wasteland he rules. No one knows why.
