Event Types in Love and Deepspace

Love and Deepspace runs several distinct event types, each with different reward structures and efficiency profiles. Understanding the type of event you are facing is the first step to optimizing your participation.

Limited Banner Events. Events centered around a new SSR memory card release. These include event shops, event story stages, and mission reward tracks. Duration: 14-21 days. Value: Very High — these are the primary source of limited cards and concentrated resources.

Double Drop Events. Periods where specific farming stages provide 2x rewards. Duration: 3-7 days. Value: High — efficient stamina conversion, especially for protocore and ascension material farming.

Minigame Events. Events featuring side activities (photo challenges, exploration mini-games, rhythm games). Duration: 7-14 days. Value: Medium — rewards are moderate but require minimal stamina investment.

Login Events. Passive events where rewards are earned simply by logging in daily. Duration: 7-14 days. Value: Free — no effort required beyond daily login. Always claim fully.

Story Events. Events expanding the game's narrative without new banners. Duration: 7-14 days. Value: Medium — provide diamonds and materials without requiring pulls.

Collaboration Events. Cross-over events with other franchises or brands. Duration: 14-21 days. Value: Variable — depends on collaboration rewards but often includes exclusive limited items.

Event Shop Priority Framework

The event shop is where most event resources are concentrated. Proper prioritization can double the effective value you extract from an event. The following framework applies to all event shops with adjustments for specific events.

Tier 1 — Limited Items (Always buy first). Memory cards (if available), exclusive materials (only obtainable during this event), namecards and titles (limited-time cosmetics), and event-exclusive furniture or customization items. These items will not return or will take months to rerun. Prioritize them above all else.

Tier 2 — High-Value Consumables (Buy second). Limited wishes (standard and event), SSR memory card shards (for specific cards), enhancement crystals, and protocore packs that contain SSR-grade materials. These items provide immediate and significant account value.

Tier 3 — Standard Materials (Buy third). Gold (in-game currency), EXP materials (memory and operative), ascension materials, and medium-grade enhancement materials. These are valuable but available from regular farming.

Tier 4 — Low-Value Items (Buy last or skip). Low-grade enhancement materials, cosmetic consumables, and items available in unlimited quantities from standard farming. Buy these only if you have surplus event currency after clearing Tiers 1-3.

The 80/20 Rule of Event Shops. In most event shops, 20% of the items represent 80% of the total value. Identify the Tier 1 and Tier 2 items first, calculate how much currency you need for them, and work backward. Do not feel obligated to clear the entire shop — leaving low-value items unclaimed is efficient, not wasteful.

Stamina Budgeting During Events

Events create competing demands on your stamina. Normal farming (protocores, ascension materials) competes with event stage farming. Proper budgeting ensures you do not sacrifice long-term growth for short-term event gains.

Daily Stamina During Events. Your baseline daily stamina (300-320 including login bonus) should be divided: 30% to event farming (to earn shop currency), 40% to protocore farming (never stop this), 20% to ascension materials (as needed), and 10% reserve for unexpected needs or event bonus stages.

Adjustment for High-Value Events. During events with Tier 1 limited items (especially memory cards), increase event farming allocation to 50-60%. Reduce protocore farming temporarily — the limited item value exceeds the value of 1-2 weeks of protocore farming. After securing the limited items, return to normal allocation.

Stamina Pack Usage. Most events include free stamina packs in the event shop or reward track. Claim these first. Use stamina packs strategically: save them for double-drop events if possible, otherwise use them during high-value events to accelerate shop currency accumulation. Do not let stamina packs expire — use them before the event ends.

Event Stamina Refresh Decision Tree. The decision to refresh stamina with diamonds during events depends on: does the event have limited items you cannot otherwise obtain? If yes, consider stamina refreshes up to the amount needed to secure those items. Is the event shop value higher than your normal farming value? If the event shop provides better exchange rates than standard farming, refresh stamina up to the point where you have claimed all high-value items. Are you close to a milestone reward? A small number of refreshes to reach a milestone may be justified. Otherwise, do not refresh — save diamonds for pulling.

Reward Track Analysis

Most events have a multi-tier reward track (sometimes called a "pass") with rewards earned through event activity points. Understanding the reward track structure helps you set efficient participation targets.

Free Track. Always available to all players. Typically provides 60-70% of the total track value. Key rewards include diamonds, event currency, standard wishes, and materials. Target: complete the free track fully. The free track rewards are designed to be achievable through normal event participation without stamina refreshes.

Premium Track. Available for purchase (typically $9.99-14.99). Adds a parallel reward track with higher-value items: additional diamonds, exclusive namecards, premium enhancement materials, and sometimes memory card shards. The premium track approximately doubles the total reward value from the event. Value assessment: the premium track is generally good value if you plan to participate in the event actively. It is more value-efficient than direct top-up but less efficient than Monthly Card or Battle Pass.

Milestone Rewards. One-time rewards at specific activity thresholds. Often include the best value items in the event — diamonds, wishes, and exclusive materials. Prioritize reaching key milestones even if you do not plan to complete the entire track. Milestone rewards are typically front-loaded: the first 25% of progress delivers 40-50% of the reward value.

Banner Events: Pull Value Assessment

Banner events combine a limited memory card release with an event shop and reward track. They represent the most significant recurring event type and deserve the most careful analysis.

Free Pulls Assessment. Banner events typically provide 5-10 free event wishes through the event shop and reward track. These are the most efficient pulls you will ever do — they cost no diamonds. Always complete the content needed to claim all free event wishes before the event ends.

Banner Memory Card Value. Before pulling on a banner, evaluate: does the memory card enable a new team or significantly upgrade an existing one? Is the operative one you actively use? Does the card's kit synergize with your existing account? Banner memory cards are generally more powerful than standard pool equivalents, but "more powerful" does not always mean "worth pulling for your specific account."

Event Currency Efficiency. The event stages that grant banner currency typically provide additional rewards beyond the shop currency. Compare the per-stamina value of event stages versus standard farming. If event stages provide better material drop rates in addition to currency, they are worth farming even beyond the shop clear point.

Farming Events: Double Drop Analysis

Double drop events are deceptively simple — they double the rewards from specific stages for a limited time. However, optimal participation requires planning.

Pre-Farming Strategy. Before a double drop event, save stamina packs and consider reducing farming in the targeted category for 3-5 days before the event starts. This allows you to maximize stamina allocation during the double drop window. If you know a protocore double drop event is coming, reduce protocore farming pre-event and allocate saved stamina during the event.

Double Drop Priority. Highest priority: protocore double drops (protocore value is more stamina-dependent than any other material category). Second priority: memory card ascension materials (needed in large quantities for building multiple cards). Third priority: operative level-up materials (abundant from other sources). Lowest priority: gold double drops (gold is obtainable from many sources and is rarely the primary bottleneck).

Stamina Allocation During Double Drops. On double drop days, allocate 70-80% of your daily stamina to the double drop stages. Keep 20-30% for daily missions and event farming. If you have saved stamina packs, use them during double drop events — the value premium makes this the most efficient stamina pack usage in the game.

Minigame Events: Time vs Reward

Minigame events are the most time-efficient events in Love and Deepspace, typically requiring 5-15 minutes per day for full reward completion. However, they also present the highest time-to-reward ratio concerns.

Daily Minigame Completion. Most minigame events have daily attempts that reset. Complete these daily — they take minimal time and provide event currency that accumulates significantly over the event duration. Skipping even one day reduces your total event currency by 5-10%.

Score Targets. Minigame events typically have score thresholds for bonus rewards. Check the threshold requirements early and note the score needed for full rewards. If a threshold requires perfect play, decide whether the additional reward (usually a small amount of currency or materials) is worth the time investment. Often it is not — settling for 90% of the possible score provides 95% of the rewards.

Minigame Efficiency. The event currency per minute spent is typically higher in minigame events than in story events. If you are time-constrained, prioritize minigame events over story events. If you have ample time, minigame events are not the best stamina-to-value conversion — they are best treated as quick daily activities.

Login Events: Free Value

Login events are the simplest event type but also the most commonly missed by inconsistent players. They require zero time investment beyond opening the game.

Never Miss a Login Event. Login events typically provide 300-500 diamonds over 7-10 days, plus materials and sometimes a 4-star memory card selection. Missing a single login day during these events costs you not just that day's reward but potentially the final milestone reward. Set a daily reminder if needed — login events are free resources that require only presence.

Timing Strategy. Login events usually overlap with major content updates. Plan your real-life schedule around these periods — if you know a content update is coming, ensure you will be able to log in daily for the following week. If you will be unavailable, consider logging in briefly just to claim login rewards even if you do not have time to play otherwise.

Rerun Events: Should You Farm Again?

Rerun events bring back previous event content, often with the same shop and reward structure. These present a specific optimization question: how much effort should you invest in content you have already completed?

New Players. For players who missed the original event, reruns are essentially new content. Apply the standard event participation strategy. The event shop will be fresh with all items available.

Returning Players. If you partially completed the original event, check what you already claimed. Rerun event shops often reset partially — limited items and high-value consumables typically refresh, while standard materials may carry over. Prioritize the refreshed limited items and consumables.

Veteran Players. If you fully completed the original event, check what rewards the rerun offers. Some reruns provide duplicate-limited items that can be used for upgrades. Other reruns only offer standard materials. For veteran players, rerun participation can be minimal — claim the login rewards and free event wishes, then return to normal farming.

Event Calendar Strategy

Events in Love and Deepspace do not happen in isolation — they overlap and sequence in patterns that reward advance planning.

Monthly Event Pattern. A typical month includes: one major banner event (14-21 days), one smaller event (minigame or story, 7-14 days), one double drop event (3-7 days), and one login event (7-10 days). These overlap to create continuous event coverage with 2-3 active events at any time.

Advance Planning. Follow event prediction sources to know what is coming 2-4 weeks ahead. This allows you to: save stamina packs for upcoming double drops, pre-farm materials for upcoming memory card ascension, plan diamond expenditure around upcoming banner quality, and schedule real-life time for high-investment events.

Event Fatigue Management. With continuous events, burnout is a real risk. Conserve your energy by differentiating between "must-do" event activities (login rewards, free event wishes, limited shop items) and "optional" activities (score chasing, full shop clearance, premium track completion). Do the must-do activities for every event, and only do optional activities for events that genuinely interest you or provide exceptional value.

Personal Event Tracker Template

Keeping a personal event tracker helps ensure you do not miss important deadlines and can track your resource accumulation over time. Here is a recommended template structure:

Event Name and Duration. Record the event name, start date, and end date. Set a calendar reminder 2 days before the end date to ensure you complete all pending activities.

Key Rewards Checklist. List the limited items, high-value consumables, and milestone rewards you want from this event. Check them off as you claim them. This prevents the common problem of realizing you forgot to claim something after the event ends.

Stamina Budget. Record your planned stamina allocation for the event duration: how much per day to event stages, how much to normal farming. Adjust as needed based on event requirements.

Net Resource Gain. After the event ends, calculate your net resource gain: diamonds earned, wishes earned, materials accumulated, and any limited items obtained. Over time, this tracking builds a personal database of event value that informs future participation decisions.

The Event Efficiency Motto: "Claim first, optimize later." When an event goes live, immediately claim all one-time rewards (login bonuses, mail attachments, first-clear diamonds) before doing anything else. These are time-sensitive and require zero effort. You can optimize your farming strategy over the event duration, but those initial claims are the easiest resources you will ever earn in Love and Deepspace.

Summary: Event optimization in Love and Deepspace follows clear and repeatable principles. Identify the event type and adjust your participation accordingly. Prioritize event shop purchases using the four-tier framework (limited items first, consumables second, standard materials third, low-value items last). Budget stamina strategically between event farming and normal farming. Complete free reward tracks fully before considering premium tracks. Never miss login events. For double drop events, pre-save stamina packs and allocate 70-80% of daily stamina to the boosted stages. Track your event participation to build a personal efficiency database. The gap between optimized and unoptimized event play is 40-60% more monthly resources — equivalent to significant spending without the cost.
