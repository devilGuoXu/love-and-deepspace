THREAT LEVEL: EXTREME

Abyssal Siren

The ocean remembers. The Siren sings. And if you're not careful, you drown.

ENTRY_#112 2026.06.29 ELITE_BOSS

The Core Insight: The Abyssal Siren is a battlefield control boss. It doesn't just attack you — it reshapes the arena. Waves push you. Minions swarm you. The Siren sings from a distance while its minions do the dirty work. The key is not brute force — it's positioning. Stay mobile, clear minions fast, and never let the Siren dictate the floor layout.

Boss Overview

The Abyssal Siren is a water-element elite boss found in late Abyss floors and high-level Orbit stages. It specializes in AoE denial, minion summoning, and ranged pressure. Unlike other bosses that chase you, the Siren makes you come to it — through waves of obstacles and adds.

Element Water
Weakness Light (Xavier) — disrupts song channel
Resistance High Frost resistance (Zayne less effective)
Dodge Difficulty Moderate (telegraphed AoEs)
Special Mechanic Summon minions at 70% and 40% HP

Attack Patterns

Tidal Pulse Primary — Constant Pressure

Tell: The Siren raises its arms. Water ripples outward from its position across the arena floor.

Behavior: A ground-level wave that pushes you backward. Deals 15% HP on contact. The wave travels at moderate speed — you can see it coming.

Dodge: Jump over the wave (if your operative has a jump) or dodge through it. The wave has a 1-second active hitbox at any point. Time your dodge for the moment the wave reaches you.

Punish: After the wave passes, the Siren is stationary for 1.5 seconds. Close the gap and land 2-3 hits.

Siren's Song Phase 1 — Channel

Tell: The Siren glows blue and a musical note visual appears above its head. It begins to channel for 3 seconds.

Behavior: While channeling, the Siren regenerates 2% HP per second and applies a slow debuff to you (movement speed -30%). If the channel completes, it releases a full-arena shockwave that deals 40% HP.

Counter: Light-element attacks (Xavier RS) interrupt the channel. If you don't have Light, you need to deal 10% of the Siren's max HP during the channel to stagger it out of the song.

Punish: If interrupted, the Siren is stunned for 3 seconds. Maximum damage window.

Aqua Barrage Phase 1 — Ranged Pressure

Tell: The Siren spreads its arms wide. Five water orbs form in a circle around it.

Behavior: The orbs fire sequentially toward your position. Each orb deals 12% HP. The orbs fire in a spread pattern — first orb at your current position, second slightly to the left, third slightly to the right.

Dodge: Sprint sideways. The orbs have travel time. You can outrun all 5 without dodging if you start moving the moment you see the orbs form. Only dodge if you're cornered.

Punish: After all 5 orbs fire, the Siren rests for 2 seconds. Use this to close distance or land ranged attacks.

Minion Wave HP Threshold — 70% and 40%

Trigger: At 70% HP, the Siren teleports to the center and becomes invulnerable. It summons 4 Abyssal Spawn — small water-element minions.

Behavior: The minions chase you and explode on contact, dealing 20% HP each. They have low HP (can be killed in 2-3 hits). If not killed within 10 seconds, they merge into a large minion with 5x HP.

Strategy: Stop attacking the Siren at 72% HP. Wait for the teleport. Immediately AoE the minions as they spawn. Kill all 4 before they merge. Xavier's RS or Rafayel's AoE are excellent for this.

Reward: If all minions are killed before merging, the Siren exits invulnerability and is staggered for 2 seconds.

Tidal Tomb Phase 2 — Below 40% HP

Tell: The Siren raises both arms and the arena floor turns blue. Water starts rising from the edges.

Behavior: Over 4 seconds, water floods the arena from the edges inward. Standing in the water deals 10% HP per second. The safe zone shrinks to a 3-meter circle in the center by the end.

Strategy: Move to the center immediately. Do not fight the water — you cannot outrun it. Position yourself in the center and wait for the flood to complete. The Siren will be vulnerable in the center with you.

Punish: When the water reaches max height, the Siren emerges in the center and is exhausted for 3 seconds. All-out attack.

Ocean's Wrath Enrage — Below 20% HP

Tell: The Siren screams. The arena shakes. Water spouts erupt randomly across the floor.

Behavior: Water spouts deal 25% HP each and knock you airborne. 3 spouts are active at any time. They erupt every 2 seconds at random positions. The Siren continues all other attacks during this phase.

Strategy: Stay in constant motion. Do not stop moving. The spouts target your position at the moment they erupt — if you're moving, they'll hit where you were, not where you are.

Punish: The Siren has no new recovery windows in this phase. Punish during its existing attack recoveries, but never commit to more than 2 hits at a time.

Phase Summary

Phase 1 (100%-70%) Tidal Pulse, Siren's Song, Aqua Barrage
Summon Wave (70%) 4 minions spawn. Kill before merge. 10s timer.
Phase 2 (70%-40%) Same attacks. Second summon at 40%.
Tidal Tomb (40%) Arena floods. Move to center. Kill window.
Enrage (20%-0%) Ocean's Wrath — constant moving AoEs.

Recommended Team Composition

XAVIER (Main DPS)

Why: Light element interrupts the Siren's Song channel, which is the most dangerous mechanic in Phase 1. Xavier's AoE on RS also clears minion waves efficiently. He's the best all-rounder for this fight.

Build: 6-piece Solar Resonance. Prioritize ATK% and Crit Rate for wave clear.

RAFAYEL (Sub-DPS)

Why: Rafayel's water damage is not resisted by the Siren (it's neutral). His DoT sustained damage works well during the Siren's long channel phases. His AoE DoTs also damage minions while you focus the boss.

Build: 6-piece Tidal Wrath. ATK% focus for sustained pressure.

SYLUS (Flex)

Why: Sylus's mobility helps him navigate the flooded arena and water spouts. His Dark Energy state ignores the slow debuff from Siren's Song. However, his short range makes him vulnerable to Tidal Pulse knockbacks.

Build: 6-piece Shadow Bind. Energy Regen for constant Dark Energy uptime.

Optimal Team: Xavier (Main) + Rafayel (Sub). Xavier handles interrupts and wave clear. Rafayel provides sustained DPS during the Siren's stationary phases. Together they cover all phases effectively.

Positioning Strategy

Positioning is more important in this fight than any other boss. Here are the rules:

Rule 1: Stay at mid-range (not close, not far). Close range gets you hit by Tidal Pulse knockback. Far range leaves you unable to interrupt Siren's Song. Mid-range gives you time to react to both.

Rule 2: During minion waves, position yourself between the Siren and the minions. If you stand on one side, the minions approach from the front and you can AoE them without turning around.

Rule 3: During Tidal Tomb, move to center 1 second before the flood completes. If you move too early, you waste DPS time. Too late, and you take flood damage.

Rule 4: During enrage, move in a clockwise circle around the arena. This pattern avoids water spouts naturally because they track your position with a 0.5s delay — by the time they erupt, you've moved.

Common Mistakes

Mistake #1: Ignoring the Siren's Song. A full-channeled Song deals 40% AoE damage AND heals the boss. This is a run-ender. Prioritize interrupting it over everything else — even your own damage rotation.

Mistake #2: Over-committing before minion waves. If you push the Siren to 71% while in the middle of your Oath animation, you'll waste your ultimate on an invulnerable target. Track the HP threshold and stop attacking at 72%.

Mistake #3: Fighting the flood during Tidal Tomb. You cannot prevent the flood. You cannot outrun it. Move to center and wait. Players who panic-dodge into the edges take unnecessary damage.

Mistake #4: Standing still during enrage. Ocean's Wrath punishes immobility harder than any mechanic. If you stop to land a full N5 string, a water spout will erupt under you. Never commit to more than 2-3 hits at a time in the enrage phase.

The Bottom Line: The Abyssal Siren is not a DPS race — it's a positioning puzzle. Manage the minions. Interrupt the song. Respect the flood. Keep moving during enrage. This boss rewards spatial awareness more than mechanical skill. If you're stuck, the answer is almost always: you were standing in the wrong place. Fix your positioning, and the fight becomes straightforward.
