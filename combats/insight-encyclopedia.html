The Unofficial Encyclopedia of Bad Decisions

An A-Z of mistakes I made so you do not have to

Every entry in this encyclopedia is a mistake I personally made, observed in a friend, or saw repeated so often in the community that it deserves a formal entry. Read. Learn. Do not repeat.

A Auto-Equip
System: Memory Management • Severity: Medium

The auto-equip button is a trap. It equips memories based on raw power level, ignoring set bonuses, Stellactrum matching, and synergy. I used auto-equip for my first three weeks and wondered why my team felt weak. Manually equip your memories. It takes five minutes and makes a 20% difference.

Personal Note: I remember the exact moment I realized auto-equip had given my Zayne a full ATK set. He is a support. He needs HP. I felt so stupid.
B Building Four Operatives at Once
System: Resource Management • Severity: High

I built all four operatives to level 60 simultaneously. I had four mediocre operatives instead of one strong one. Resources in this game are scarce. Focus everything on one operative until they are level 80 with +15 protocores before you even look at a second one. The game rewards depth over breadth.

C Chasing Set Bonuses Too Early
System: Protocore • Severity: Medium

I spent two weeks farming a specific domain for a 4-piece set bonus. I ended up with garbage substats on all four pieces. A 2-piece set bonus with good substats outperforms a 4-piece with bad substats. Get your 2-piece, settle for good enough, and only chase the 4-piece when you have surplus stamina.

D Daily Login Anxiety
System: Psycho • Severity: Low

I once set an alarm for 3 AM to spend my stamina before it capped. I woke up, spent 140 stamina on a domain run, got nothing, and could not fall back asleep for an hour. The 140 stamina was worth about 15 cents in real money. I lost an hour of sleep over 15 cents. Never again.

E Enhancing Flat Stats Past +6
System: Protocore • Severity: Medium

Flat ATK, flat DEF, and flat HP are the worst substats in the game. They do not scale with percentage bonuses. I once took a protocore with flat ATK to +12 hoping it would get better. It did not. The flat stats got higher but the percentage of my total ATK they represented stayed tiny. If your first two rolls hit flat stats, salvage the piece.

F Farming the Wrong Domain Level
System: Stamina Efficiency • Severity: Medium

I farmed domain level 3 for two weeks because I could clear it 30 seconds faster than level 5. The drop rate difference between level 3 and level 5 is approximately 40% more SSR protocores per run. I was saving 30 seconds per run and losing 40% of the value. The slower clear at the higher level was more efficient.

G Gem Spending on Standard Banner
System: Gacha • Severity: High

I spent approximately 8,000 gems on the standard banner before I learned about limited banners. Those 8,000 gems would have been 57 limited banner pulls, which at soft pity rates would have given me approximately 0.9 limited SSRs. Instead, I got one standard SSR from the standard banner. The regret is real and enduring.

H Hoarding Protocores
System: Inventory Management • Severity: High

I once had 347 protocores in my inventory. I had not enhanced a single piece in six weeks. I was collecting rather than using. The hoarding was a coping mechanism for bad RNG. I staged an intervention with myself and trashed 296 pieces. My account got stronger in one evening than it had in the previous six weeks.

I Ignoring Stellactrum
System: Combat • Severity: Critical

I ignored Stellactrum entirely for my first month. I thought it was a minor bonus. It is a 30% damage multiplier at full matching. I was doing 30% less damage for a month because I could not be bothered to read the tutorial. This is the single biggest rookie mistake in the game.

J Judging Characters by First Impressions
System: Social • Severity: Low

I dismissed Zayne as boring in the first week. Three months later, he was my most-used support. The characters have more depth than their initial archetypes suggest. Give them time. Read their stories. The ones you least expect often surprise you.

K Knowing When to Stop Pulling
System: Gacha • Severity: Critical

The hardest skill in this game is knowing when to stop. I have pulled past my budget more times than I can count. The banner timer creates artificial urgency. The 50-50 loss creates emotional vulnerability. Set a hard pull limit before the banner starts and stick to it. No exceptions. The banner will rerun.

L Leveling Every Memory to 20
System: Memory • Severity: Medium

I leveled every memory I owned to 20 because I thought I would use them all. I have used approximately six memories total. The rest sit at level 20, mocking me with their wasted XP bottles. Only level memories you are actively using on your main teams.

M Main Character Syndrome
System: Psycho • Severity: Low

I spent three months forcing myself to main Xavier because I decided he was my favorite on day one. I ignored that his combat style did not click with me. I eventually switched to Sylus and the game got more fun immediately. You are allowed to change your main. Your day-one decision is not binding.

Encyclopedia entries based on personal experience. New entries added as new mistakes are made. This is an ongoing document.
