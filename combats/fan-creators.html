Content Creator Directory

A curated directory of Love and Deepspace content creators. Find guides, lore analysis, gameplay, and entertainment content across all platforms.

ENTRY_#FAN_032026.07.02CREATORS

About This Directory: This directory highlights active creators who consistently produce high-quality Deepspace content. Creators are listed by their content focus. All are English-language creators unless noted. This is not an exhaustive list — new creators emerge regularly.

YouTube Creators

YouTube has the largest concentration of Deepspace guide and analysis content. Deepspace Guides (Guide/Mechanics): The most comprehensive guide channel. Covers memory tier lists, abyss strategies, protocore builds, and pull planning. Uploads weekly. Hunter Station (Lore/Story Analysis): Deep dives into the game's story, character backstories, and lore theories. Essential for players who care about narrative. Stellactrum Studios (Gameplay/Entertainment): Challenge runs, F2P account showcases, and live pulls. Good for entertainment and seeing how different team compositions perform. Celestial Vault (News/Rumors): Leak coverage, datamine analysis, and future content speculation. Best for staying ahead of upcoming banners and events. Radiant Wings (Co-op/Boss Guides): Co-op strategy guides and boss fight tutorials. Excellent for players struggling with specific encounters. Memory Lane (Memory Reviews): Detailed reviews of each new SSR memory — art, story, gameplay performance. Useful before pulling on banners.

Twitch Streamers

Twitch has a smaller but dedicated Deepspace streaming community. Stream times are typically in the evening (EST/PST). LunarDreams: Daily streamer. Focus: abyss runs, pull sessions, Destiny Cafe interactions. Community votes on gacha pulls. AstralVoyager: Weekend streams. Focus: co-op boss runs with viewers, F2P account progression, Q&A sessions. FlameKeeper_Raf: Rafayel main. Content: Rafayel showcases, memory reviews, fan art reactions. FrostKnight_Zayne: Zayne main. Content: abyss clears, protocore optimizing, chill vibes. Twitch streams are great for real-time interaction and community building. Most streamers have Discord servers where the community discusses strategy and shares tips.

Written Content Creators

For players who prefer reading over watching: Deepspace Hub (This Site): Comprehensive written guides for all systems — combat, protocores, events, memories, and lore. Our guides are updated every patch. Deepspace Wiki (Fandom): Community-maintained wiki covering every game system, memory, and character. Good for quick reference. Reddit Guides (r/LoveAndDeepspace): Detailed text guides posted by community members. Sort by "Guide" flair. Medium/Tumblr Blogs: Several community members maintain personal blogs with in-depth analysis. Topics include character studies, narrative analysis, and poetry inspired by the game. Google Docs Community: Several theorycrafters maintain shared Google Docs with protocore math, damage calculations, and stamina efficiency spreadsheets. These are often linked in Reddit posts and Discord servers.

How to Support Creators: Content creation takes significant time and effort. Support your favorite creators by: (1) Subscribing, following, or bookmarking their channels. (2) Liking and commenting on their content — engagement helps algorithms surface their work. (3) Sharing their content with friends who play Deepspace. (4) Joining their membership/patreon if they offer one and you can afford it. (5) Sending Super Chat on Twitch streams during Q&A sessions. (6) Providing constructive feedback — creators appreciate knowing what content their audience wants. (7) Being respectful in comments and chat. Toxic comments discourage creators from making content. (8) Crediting them when referencing their work in discussions. A healthy creator ecosystem means more and better content for everyone. Support the creators you enjoy.

Finding New Creators

The Deepspace content creator community grows monthly. To find new creators: (1) Check the "Recommended" section on YouTube after watching Deepspace content. (2) Browse the Love and Deepspace category on Twitch. (3) Search for "Love and Deepspace guide" and filter by "This month" on YouTube. (4) Follow community hashtags on Twitter: #DeepspaceContentCreator, #DeepspaceGuide. (5) Join the official Discord and check the #content-creators channel. (6) Check Reddit for creator self-promotion posts (allowed on designated days). (7) Look for creator collaborations — creators often shout each other out. This directory will be updated periodically as new creators emerge and existing creators evolve their content focus.
