❄️ The Core Insight: Floor 9 isn't about damage — it's about crowd control. Two elites attacking simultaneously will overwhelm you fast. Freeze, stun, or stagger one while you kill the other. Without CC, this floor is a nightmare. With CC, it's straightforward.

Fight Overview — The Double Elite Problem

Floor 9 is the first "CC check" in Chaos Abyss. Two elite enemies spawn at the same time. Each has different attack patterns, but together they create deadly combinations. Here's what you're facing:

⚔️
Blade Master (Melee)
High damage, aggressive, chases you
Slash combo · Dash attack · Spin AoE
🏹
Arcane Archer (Ranged)
Stays at range, spams projectiles
Triple shot · Homing arrow · Rain of arrows

⚠️ Critical Mechanic: If both elites are alive for more than 15 seconds, they enter "synergy mode" — attack speed +50%, damage +50%. You must CC or kill one quickly.

Elite Attack Patterns — Learn Your Enemy

Blade Master (Melee)

⚔️ Slash Combo
Three quick slashes in front. High damage if all hit.
Tell: Sword glows blue
Counter: Dodge sideways through the first slash
💨 Dash Attack
Charges across the arena, hitting everything in path.
Tell: Leans forward, dust appears
Counter: Dodge perpendicular to charge line
🌀 Spin AoE
Spins 360 degrees, damaging all nearby enemies.
Tell: Spins sword above head
Counter: Back up or dodge through

Arcane Archer (Ranged)

🏹 Triple Shot
Fires three arrows in quick succession.
Tell: Bow glows green
Counter: Dodge sideways repeatedly
🎯 Homing Arrow
Fires an arrow that tracks your position.
Tell: Arrow glows purple
Counter: Dodge at last moment (0.2s before impact)
🌧️ Rain of Arrows
Creates a large AoE circle that rains arrows.
Tell: Circle appears on ground
Counter: Move out of circle immediately

Kill Priority — Which Elite First?

Two strategies. Both work. Choose based on your team.

Strategy A — Kill Ranged First

When to use: You have good CC (Zayne freeze)

  • Freeze the Blade Master
  • Kill the Arcane Archer (she's squishier)
  • Then kill the frozen Blade Master
✅ Recommended for most players
Strategy B — Kill Melee First

When to use: Your DPS is very high (burst comp)

  • Stun/CC the Arcane Archer
  • Kill the Blade Master (he's more dangerous up close)
  • Then kill the Archer
⚠️ Only if you can kill melee in 5 seconds

💡 My Recommendation: Strategy A (kill ranged first). The Arcane Archer's projectiles are harder to dodge while fighting the Blade Master. Take her out first, then deal with the melee elite.

Common Mistakes (I Made All of These)

❌ Fighting both elites at once
You will die. CC one, kill the other. Never fight them together.
❌ Saving freeze for "emergency"
Use freeze immediately at spawn. Don't wait. Prevention > cure.
❌ Ignoring ranged attacks
The Arcane Archer's arrows hurt. Kill her first or stay mobile.
❌ Standing in rain of arrows
The circle looks small but the damage is big. Move immediately.
❌ Not saving Ardent Oath
Use your ultimate during the freeze window for maximum damage.
❌ Panicking when CC misses
If freeze misses, dodge and wait for cooldown. Don't commit to a losing fight.

Summary — The Golden Rules

✅ DO
  • Bring Zayne for freeze
  • CC one elite immediately
  • Kill ranged elite first
  • Use Ardent Oath during freeze
  • Stay mobile to avoid arrows
❌ DON'T
  • Fight both elites together
  • Save freeze for later
  • Stand still vs Archer
  • Forget to save ultimate
  • Panic when CC is on cooldown

□ Ardent Oath charged before starting floor
□ Know which elite to freeze first (melee)
□ Know which elite to kill first (ranged)
