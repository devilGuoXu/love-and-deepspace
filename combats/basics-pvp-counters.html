PvP Team Counter Guide

Hunter Contest is Deepspace's PvP mode. Unlike PvE, you face another player's defense team controlled by AI. Counter-picking is the difference between top 10% and top 1%.

ENTRY_#0692026.07.02PVP

Core Difference from PvE: PvP defense teams are controlled by AI, which makes predictable decisions. AI always uses skills on cooldown. AI always targets the lowest-HP operative. AI never cancels skills or holds cooldowns for optimal timing. You can exploit all of these behaviors.

Reading the Opponent Team

Before the match starts, analyze the opponent's team: (1) Identify the main DPS: The highest-level operative with a DPS memory set. This is who you must counter. (2) Identify the support: Usually Zayne (shield/heal). Kill the support first — without Zayne, the enemy team has no sustain. (3) Identify the threat level: Xavier = consistent pressure. Sylus = execute burst. Rafayel = AoE burn. Zayne = sustain war. Counter based on the highest threat. (4) Check memory sets: Opponent portraits show their memory set icon. If Xavier has Light Seeker (4pc), expect burst damage. If Sylus has Dark Champion (4pc), play around execute threshold.

Counter Composition Strategies

Vs Xavier Hypercarry: Xavier relies on Light element damage. Bring Zayne (Frostguard) for shields. Sylus (Dark Champion) to pressure Xavier — Sylus's Dark element resists Light. Priority: Kill Xavier's support first (usually Zayne). Xavier alone cannot sustain through shields. Vs Sylus Execute Comp: Sylus is dangerous below 60% HP (his execute triggers at 50-30%). Keep all operatives above 60% HP. Bring a second healer or shield if possible. Zayne is essential. Priority: Never let any operative drop below 60%. Use HP potions proactively, not reactively. Vs Rafayel Burn Comp: Rafayel's burn DoT is his main damage. Bring Zayne (shield cleanses burn if Frostguard 4pc). Xavier can burst Rafayel before burn stacks. Priority: Kill Rafayel first. His burn damage ramps up over time — the longer the fight, the more damage he deals. Vs Zayne Stall Comp: Zayne sustain teams try to outlast you. Bring Xavier + Sylus for maximum burst. Kill Zayne first — without Zayne, the team crumbles. Priority: Burst Zayne with all cooldowns in the first 10 seconds. If you fail to kill him, the fight becomes an attrition war you will lose.

AI Exploitation Tactics

Tactic 1 — Bait the AI skill: The AI uses skills as soon as they are off cooldown. Stand at max range from the AI's main DPS — they will waste their skill on an empty area trying to reach you. Then close distance while their skill is on cooldown. Tactic 2 — Focus fire the AI: The AI does not switch targets. If you have a low-HP operative, the AI will tunnel them. Use this to control the AI's positioning — keep your low-HP operative behind cover while your other two operatives attack from the side. Tactic 3 — Energy advantage: The AI does not save Ardent Oath for optimal timing. AI uses Oath the moment it is available, often on a shielded or dodging target. You, as a human, can save your Oath for the right moment. This gives you a significant burst advantage.

Pro Tip: The most important PvP skill is manual targeting. Do not let auto-target decide who you attack. In close-range fights, auto-target often picks the tankiest enemy instead of the squishiest support. Manually tap the operative you want to focus. Kill order: Enemy support (Zayne) → Enemy main DPS → Enemy flex. If you kill the support first, the enemy team loses all sustain and crumbles within 30 seconds.

Team-Building for PvP

PvP team-building is different from PvE. Defense team: Zayne (mandatory for sustain) + Xavier or Sylus (DPS) + flex. AI plays defense, so survivability is more important than damage. Attack team: Xavier + Sylus + Zayne (balanced) or Xavier + Sylus + Rafayel (all-damage, high risk). Attack teams can afford to be aggressive because you control the fight. Stat priority for PvP: HP% and DEF% are more valuable in PvP than PvE (enemies are player-controlled and deal player-level damage). A PvP Xavier should have 10-15% more HP than a PvE Xavier. Memory sets for PvP: Same as PvE, but consider 2+2 over 4pc for more consistent damage (4pc burst windows are harder to execute in PvP because enemies dodge).

Summary: PvP AI is predictable — use cooldowns and focus fire to exploit it. Counter: Xavier (burst) → bring Zayne/Sylus. Sylus (execute) → keep HP above 60%. Rafayel (burn) → kill first. Zayne (stall) → burst Zayne first. Manual targeting is essential. Kill order: support → DPS → flex. PvP builds need more HP than PvE. Attack team can be aggressive; defense needs sustain.
