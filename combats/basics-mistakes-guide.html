Top 10 Common Mistakes

What NOT to do — the resource traps, bad habits, and misconceptions that hold players back.

ENTRY_#0522026.07.01MISTAKES

Warning: This guide is written from experience watching hundreds of accounts make the same mistakes. Some of these may contradict popular advice you've seen elsewhere. Test everything yourself.

1. Raising All Memories to +6

The most common trap. Players level every SSR they get to +6 "just in case." This wastes enhancement materials that should be concentrated on your main team. Only level memories you are actively using in your Abyss teams. Everything else stays at +0 until it has a purpose.

2. Farming Protocores Before Level Caps

Protocore farming is infinite RNG. Level cap increases are fixed progression. Players who spend 1,000 stamina on protocore farming while their DPS is level 50 waste weeks of progress. The stamina efficiency order is: Weekly boss > EXP books (if under cap) > Gold (if broke) > Protocore farming. Never skip the first three.

3. Building One Element Only

Players fall in love with one operative and build three memories of the same element. Abyss requires 8 operatives across 4 elements. A single-element account hard-stops at Floor 7. You need at least 2 elements by Floor 4, 3 by Floor 7, and all 4 by Floor 10. Build breadth before depth.

4. Wasting Diamonds on Standard Banner

Standard banner does not have a hard pity carry-over like limited banners. The standard pool is also diluted — you have a low chance of getting what you want. Diamonds should only be spent on limited banners where you want the rate-up memory. Use free standard tickets from events and battle pass — never diamonds.

5. Ignoring SRs Entirely

The opposite of mistake #1. Some players refuse to level any SR, waiting for SSRs to drop. An R3 SR at level 50 with +9 memories is stronger than an R1 SSR at level 1 with +0 memories. You need 6-8 built memories for Abyss. You will use SRs as filler for months. Level the good ones (#094).

6. Salvaging Wrong Main Stat Protocores

A protocore with a "wrong" main stat but perfect substats is still valuable. ATK% slot 4 isn't ideal for CRIT-scaling DPS — but if the substats are 4 rolls of CRIT Rate + CRIT DMG, it beats a correct main stat with flat DEF substats. Check substats before salvaging.

7-10. Quick Hits

7. Not doing weekly bosses (you lose limited crystal income). 8. Hoarding favorite items instead of gifting (affinity rewards are worth more). 9. Skipping daily missions (15 min/day = 450 gems/month). 10. Believing "this unit needs max dupes to be good" — R1 SSRs are 90% as strong as R6 in most content.

Key Insight: Most mistakes come from impatience or FOMO. This game rewards consistency over intensity. 15 minutes daily + stamina never capped + smart resource allocation beats 4-hour grinding sessions with scattered focus. Slow is smooth, smooth is fast.

Summary: Don't level unused memories. Farm exp before protocores. Build element breadth. Diamonds on limited banners only. Level good SRs. Check substats before salvaging. Do weeklies. Be consistent.
