Protocore Stat Weights

Not all stats are equal. Each operative has different scaling. CRIT Rate is not universally the best substat — it depends on the operative's base kit and team composition.

ENTRY_#0642026.07.02STAT_WEIGHTS

Core Concept: A stat's value depends on how much of it the operative already has. CRIT Rate has diminishing returns above 70% (the effective damage increase per point drops). ATK% has diminishing returns above +100% base ATK. Energy Recharge has a hard cap at 200%. Knowing where the breakpoints are lets you optimize your protocore substats rather than chasing infinite CRIT.

Xavier — Light Seeker

Main stat priority: CRIT Rate (Slot 4) > ATK% (Slot 1/3) > CRIT DMG (Slot 5). Substat weight: CRIT Rate (1.0) = CRIT DMG (0.9) > ATK% (0.7) > Energy Recharge (0.3) > Flat ATK (0.2) > HP%/DEF% (0). Breakpoints: CRIT Rate 65% minimum. CRIT DMG 140% minimum. Energy Recharge 120% minimum (to maintain burst rotation). Diminishing returns: CRIT Rate beyond 80% has sharply decreased value — Xavier's kit already provides 12% CRIT Rate from his passive. A protocore with 10% CRIT Rate is actually worth less than advertised because of this. Target set: 70-75% CRIT Rate, 150-160% CRIT DMG, 120%+ ER.

Sylus — Dark Champion

Main stat priority: ATK% (Slot 1/3) > CRIT DMG (Slot 5) > Energy Recharge (Slot 2). Substat weight: ATK% (1.0) > CRIT DMG (0.85) > CRIT Rate (0.7) > Energy Recharge (0.5) > Flat ATK (0.3). Breakpoints: ATK 2,500+ base before bonuses. CRIT DMG 160%+. CRIT Rate 50%+ (his execute below 30% does not require CRIT). Diminishing returns: Sylus's execute ability bypasses CRIT checks entirely on the killing blow. CRIT Rate is less valuable on Sylus than on other operatives. Prioritize ATK% and CRIT DMG over CRIT Rate. Target set: 2,500+ ATK, 50%+ CRIT Rate, 160%+ CRIT DMG, 140%+ ER.

Zayne — Frostguard

Main stat priority: HP% (Slot 1/3) > Healing Bonus (Slot 5) > Energy Recharge (Slot 2). Substat weight: HP% (1.0) > Energy Recharge (0.9) > Healing Bonus (0.8) > DEF% (0.4) > CRIT Rate/DMG (0). Breakpoints: HP 35k+ for maximum shield value. Energy Recharge 160%+ for permanent shield uptime. Diminishing returns: Zayne does not scale with ATK or CRIT at all. His shield value scales with max HP. His healing scales with HP + Healing Bonus. Building CRIT on Zayne is a complete waste of substat rolls. Target set: 35k+ HP, 160%+ ER, 20%+ Healing Bonus.

Rafayel — Inferno Blaze

Main stat priority: ATK% (Slot 1/3) > CRIT Rate (Slot 4) > Burn DMG (Slot 5, if available). Substat weight: ATK% (1.0) > CRIT Rate (0.85) > CRIT DMG (0.7) > Energy Recharge (0.6) > Flat ATK (0.2). Breakpoints: ATK 2,200+ base. CRIT Rate 60%+. Energy Recharge 130%+. Burn DMG is a unique stat that only appears on certain event protocores — it increases burn DoT by 30-50% and is Rafayel's strongest stat by far. Diminishing returns: Rafayel's burn damage does not crit. CRIT stats only affect his direct hits, which are ~40% of his total DPS. ATK% and Burn DMG are more valuable than CRIT stats because they affect all damage sources. Target set: 2,200+ ATK, 60%+ CRIT Rate, 130%+ CRIT DMG, 130%+ ER.

Pro Tip: The "one good roll" rule: A protocore with 1 great substat roll (CRIT Rate 10%+) is better than a protocore with 4 mediocre substat rolls (CRIT Rate 3% + ATK% 4% + HP% 5% + DEF% 4%). Do not keep protocores with 3+ bad substats just because they have one good one. The roll value cap is 15% per substat — chase high rolls on the right stats, not many stats.

Universal Stat Priority Table

For DPS (Xavier/Sylus): Main stat (correct) > CRIT Rate > CRIT DMG > ATK% > ER > Flat ATK. For Burn DPS (Rafayel): ATK% > Burn DMG > CRIT Rate > CRIT DMG > ER. For Support (Zayne): HP% > ER > Healing Bonus > DEF% > everything else. Rule of thumb: If a protocore substat roll goes into a stat the operative does not use (e.g., CRIT Rate on Zayne), the protocore is significantly devalued. 3 good rolls on 3 protocores > 2 good rolls on 5 protocores. Quality over quantity.

Summary: Xavier: CRIT > DMG > ATK (70/150/120 target). Sylus: ATK > CRIT DMG > CRIT Rate (2,500 ATK, 50/160). Zayne: HP > ER > Heal (35k HP, 160% ER). Rafayel: ATK > Burn DMG > CRIT (2,200 ATK, 60/130). Diminishing returns hit hard after breakpoints. High single rolls > many low rolls.
