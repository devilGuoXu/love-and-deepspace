Stealth & Ambush Wanderers

Three wanderer types that rely on invisibility, teleportation, and decoy mechanics. These enemies punish tunnel vision and reward predictive play. Learn to read the tells before they strike.

ENTRY_#WDR_132026.07.11STEALTH

Core Identity: Stealth wanderers use invisibility, decoys, and ambush attacks that require sound cues and predictive positioning to counter. Unlike brute-force wanderers, they force you to react to things you cannot always see. Master the tells and you control the engagement.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Variant A — Shadow Stalker

Appearance: Vaguely humanoid, constantly flickering between visible and semi-transparent. HP: Low. Ability: Cloak + Backstab — enters stealth for 5s (becomes fully invisible), then teleports behind the nearest operative and executes a backstab dealing 300% ATK damage. Detection: While stealthed, the Shadow Stalker leaves faint purple footprints on the ground and emits a low hum that grows louder as it approaches. Strategy: When you hear the hum or spot footprints, start dodging sideways rather than backward. After a backstab attempt, the Stalker is revealed for 3s — this is your burst window. A perfect dodge on the backstab stuns it for 4s, giving you ample time to eliminate it. Weakness: Light (reveals it instantly), AoE (damages it even in stealth).

Variant B — Void Assassin

Appearance: Dimensional distortion shimmer — a wavering heat-haze effect in deep violet. HP: Medium. Ability: Teleport Strike — warps to a random location, then 1s later dashes to the operative with a piercing attack. Attack pattern: Warps to a point → pauses for 1s → dashes in a straight line through the target. Strategy: The pause between teleport and dash is your counter window. Watch for the distortion indicator — a purple rift opens where the Void Assassin will warp. Pre-position yourself away from the rift so the dash misses. After the dash completes, the Void Assassin is vulnerable for 2s. Weakness: Stun during the pause interrupts the dash entirely, canceling the attack.

!
Teleport Warning: Purple rift indicators appear 0.5s before the Void Assassin warps. If you see a rift forming near your position, dodge immediately — the dash follows the warp, not your current location.

Variant C — Mirage Weaver

Appearance: Ethereal, surrounded by 2 shimmering afterimages that mirror its movements. HP: Low. Ability: Decoy — creates 2 identical copies of itself. All 3 copies take 1 second to fade, but only the real Mirage Weaver deals damage. Real tell: The real Mirage Weaver's eyes glow slightly brighter than the decoys. The decoys flicker at the edges while the real one maintains a solid outline. Strategy: Ignore the decoys — they have 1 HP and disappear after one hit, but killing them wastes time and leaves you open. Identify the real one by the eye glow, then focus your damage. The real Mirage Weaver's attacks have a 0.5s longer telegraph than its decoys — use timing to tell them apart. Weakness: Any auto-target ability (locks onto the real one only), AoE (hits both real and decoys equally).

Stealth wanderers as a class share one critical weakness: they cannot attack while setting up their ambush. The Shadow Stalker hums. The Void Assassin pauses. The Mirage Weaver reveals itself through glow. Every stealth wanderer gives you a tell before it strikes — the skill is in recognizing and punishing that tell consistently.

In mixed-wave compositions, stealth wanderers should be eliminated second (after Bursters but before standard infantry). A stealthed Shadow Stalker left alive will backstab your squishiest operative for 300% ATK. Always keep moving in waves with known stealth spawns — standing still makes you a predictable target for Void Assassin teleports.

Pro Tip: In Abyss floors with the "Ambush" modifier, stealth wanderers spawn pre-stealthed. For Shadow Stalker, use Xavier's Resonance to blanket the arena in light and reveal all hidden enemies instantly. For Void Assassin, stay near walls — it cannot teleport behind you if your back is to a solid surface.

Ambush Denial Tactics

Audio cues are your primary tool. The Shadow Stalker's hum and the Void Assassin's warp sound (a distinct dimensional tear) are audible through other combat noise. Turn your game volume up if you struggle with these encounters — audio tells are more reliable than visual tells in chaotic fights. Positioning: Never stand still. Strafe in unpredictable patterns. Void Assassin predicts your location based on your movement direction at the moment it warps — changing direction mid-warp causes it to dash to where you were, not where you are. Auto-target abilities: If you struggle with Mirage Weaver's decoy game, bring an operative with lock-on skills (Xavier's Ardent Oath, Sylus's execute). Auto-targeting systems ignore decoys entirely, forcing the real Mirage Weaver to take the hit.

Summary: Stealth wanderers counter tunnel vision. Shadow Stalker — listen for the hum, dodge sideways, punish the reveal. Void Assassin — watch for the rift, move away from the warp point, interrupt the pause. Mirage Weaver — look at the eyes, ignore the copies, trust auto-target. Audio beats invisibility. Movement beats prediction. Light element beats all three.
