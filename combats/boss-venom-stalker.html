THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Venom Stalker

A poison-spewing predator that punishes slow fights. The longer the fight goes, the deadlier it becomes.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Recommended Power: 80k+ | Element: Poison | Weakness: Light — Xavier cleanses poison stacks with his Resonance.

Phase 1: Hunter's Stance (100%-65%)

Poison Spit: Ranged projectile that leaves a poison puddle. Dodge sideways, not backward. Toxic Trail: Stalker leaves a venom trail while moving. Don't chase through it. Venom Burst: AoE poison explosion. Getting hit applies 3 poison stacks. Stack max: 10.

Phase 2: Toxin Overload (65%-30%)

Puddle Spread: Old poison puddles pulse and expand. Arena control becomes critical. Stalk: Stalker disappears and targets the furthest operative. Invisible charge. Watch for dust particles. Poison Rain: Arena-wide poison drizzle. Tick damage. Xavier's cleanse becomes essential.

Phase 3: Venom Frenzy (30%-0%)

Berserk: Stalker enrages. Attack speed +100%. Dodge everything or die. Toxic Explosion: All poison puddles detonate simultaneously. Move to the center — the edges are death. Final Sting: Single target nuke. Must be perfect-dodged. One-shots without i-frames.

Recommended Team: Xavier (Main DPS/Cleanse) — Light element cleanses poison. Zayne (Support) — shield and sustain through DoTs. Sylus (Sub-DPS) — execute pressure for fast kill.

Arena Control and Puddle Management

The Venom Stalker's poison puddles persist for the entire fight and expand during Phase 2. Managing the puddle placement is essential for maintaining a workable arena. During Phase 1, kite the Stalker in a circular pattern around the outer edge of the arena. This ensures the puddles form a ring along the edge, keeping the center of the arena clear for Phase 2 and Phase 3. If you kite randomly, the puddles will scatter across the arena and leave no safe zones for the poison rain and toxic explosion mechanics.

Puddles expand to approximately 150% of their original size during Phase 2's Puddle Spread mechanic. Plan for this expansion when placing puddles in Phase 1. Leave at least one operative-width gap between puddles to ensure you have movement corridors later in the fight. The center of the arena should remain completely puddle-free throughout Phase 1 and Phase 2. If a puddle spawns in the center due to an errant poison spit, consider restarting the fight — a center-puddled arena makes Phase 3 nearly impossible.

Xavier Cleanse Rotation

Xavier's Resonance skill removes 5 poison stacks on a 15-second cooldown. The optimal cleanse rotation is: use Resonance at 5 stacks, wait for the cooldown, use it again at 5 stacks, repeat. Do not wait for 10 stacks before cleansing — the damage at 8+ stacks is severe enough to force healing downtime that reduces your damage output. The rhythm of this rotation means you have approximately 15 seconds of aggressive play followed by a cleanse window. Time your burst damage to coincide with the cleanse window when your stacks are low.

If Xavier is not available, alternative cleanse sources exist but are less effective. Zayne's shield reduces poison damage taken by 60% while active but does not remove existing stacks. Sylus has no cleanse capability and must rely on positioning to avoid poison entirely. If you are running a team without Xavier, the fight demands near-perfect dodge uptime and extremely disciplined puddle management. A no-Xavier clear is possible but requires approximately 95k power to compensate for the lack of cleanse.

The Venom Stalker's poison puddles last 45 seconds before dissipating. Kite the Stalker around the arena perimeter clockwise to ensure puddles dissipate before you return to that area. The arena circumference is large enough that a clockwise kite pattern gives each puddle time to expire before your next pass.

Advanced Tip: Poison stacks determine berserk speed. At 0-3 stacks, the fight is manageable. At 7+ stacks, the Stalker enrages early. Xavier's Resonance skill removes 5 stacks — use it on cooldown. Stack management is the real mechanic of this fight.

Summary: Stack management wins this fight. Xavier keeps poison under control. Don't chase through toxic trails. Phase 3 is a dodge gauntlet — stay calm and focus on i-frame timing. Fast kills are rewarded; slow kills are punished.
