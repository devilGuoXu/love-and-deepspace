THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Venom Stalker

A poison-spewing predator that punishes slow fights. The longer the fight goes, the deadlier it becomes.

ENTRY_#1062026.06.29ELITE_BOSS

Recommended Power: 80k+ | Element: Poison | Weakness: Light — Xavier cleanses poison stacks with his Resonance.

Phase 1: Hunter's Stance (100%-65%)

Poison Spit: Ranged projectile that leaves a poison puddle. Dodge sideways, not backward. Toxic Trail: Stalker leaves a venom trail while moving. Don't chase through it. Venom Burst: AoE poison explosion. Getting hit applies 3 poison stacks. Stack max: 10.

Phase 2: Toxin Overload (65%-30%)

Puddle Spread: Old poison puddles pulse and expand. Arena control becomes critical. Stalk: Stalker disappears and targets the furthest operative. Invisible charge. Watch for dust particles. Poison Rain: Arena-wide poison drizzle. Tick damage. Xavier's cleanse becomes essential.

Phase 3: Venom Frenzy (30%-0%)

Berserk: Stalker enrages. Attack speed +100%. Dodge everything or die. Toxic Explosion: All poison puddles detonate simultaneously. Move to the center — the edges are death. Final Sting: Single target nuke. Must be perfect-dodged. One-shots without i-frames.

Recommended Team: Xavier (Main DPS/Cleanse) — Light element cleanses poison. Zayne (Support) — shield and sustain through DoTs. Sylus (Sub-DPS) — execute pressure for fast kill.

Advanced Tip: Poison stacks determine berserk speed. At 0-3 stacks, the fight is manageable. At 7+ stacks, the Stalker enrages early. Xavier's Resonance skill removes 5 stacks — use it on cooldown. Stack management is the real mechanic of this fight.

Summary: Stack management wins this fight. Xavier keeps poison under control. Don't chase through toxic trails. Phase 3 is a dodge gauntlet — stay calm and focus on i-frame timing. Fast kills are rewarded; slow kills are punished.
