Stamina Efficiency

Stamina is the most scarce resource in Love and Deepspace. You get 480 free stamina per day from natural regeneration (1 point per 3 minutes, 24h = 480). Additional sources: 60 from daily missions, 60-120 from event login, optional 60/120/180 from diamond refreshes. The value of each stamina point changes dramatically depending on what content you run.

Best stamina values (highest to lowest): Weekly bosses (guaranteed upgrade materials + chance at SSR protocores) > Protocore farming at level 70+ domains (EXP + protocores) > Ascension material stages (needed for level cap breaks) > Memory EXP stages (red books) > Gold stages. Never run gold stages if you can avoid it — events and dailies provide enough gold. The exception is the first week of a new account where you need gold for basic upgrades.

Weekly priority order: Clear all 9 weekly boss entries first (they are time-gated). Then run Abyss for diamond rewards. Then SHC to whatever rank you can reach. Finally, use remaining stamina on protocore domains or ascension materials. Do not let weekly boss entries cap — at 3/3 full, you lose the run for that week permanently.

Ascension Breakpoints

Character power is not linear — there are clear breakpoints where investing resources gives dramatically more returns. The most important milestones are the ascension thresholds that unlock new talent nodes and stat multipliers.

Efficient breakpoints to stop at: Stop at Level 70 for all operatives you use actively. The jump from 70 to 80 is expensive (requires top-tier ascension gems) and gives only ~5-8% more stats. Invest those materials into a second team instead. Only push to 80 for your absolute main DPS after you have two full teams at 70. This is the most common resource trap — players push one character to 80 and starve the rest of their roster.

Long-Term Planning

Love and Deepspace is a marathon, not a sprint. A well-planned account over 6 months will surpass a rushed account that spent money. Here is what to plan for each phase.

Month 4-6
Optimization Phase
Substat min-max, push Abyss floors, SHC rank advancement
Month 6+
Endgame Phase
All teams at 70+, targeting top 1% SHC, collection completion

Diamond hoarding strategy: Save diamonds for limited banners that match your main operatives. A rule of thumb is 14,400 diamonds per guaranteed limited SSR (hard pity). Never pull on standard banner with diamonds — use free wishes only. Plan banners 2-3 months in advance if possible (leak watchers can predict the next 1-2 banners).

The 60/60/60 Rule

A simple heuristic for resource priority: when deciding what to spend stamina on, ask three questions. 60 minutes of gameplay benefit: Will this help you clear content faster right now? 60 days of progress benefit: Does this contribute to your power level 2 months from now? 60 stamina efficiency: Is this the best use of this stamina point? If something passes all three checks, do it immediately. If it fails the 60-day check but passes the other two, it is a short-term fix — okay occasionally but not a habit.

✅ DO Prioritize
  • Weekly bosses before Monday reset
  • Limited banner planning 2-3 months ahead
  • Level 70 for all active operatives before 80 for one
  • Protocore farming at level 70+ domains
  • Event stamina consumption during event periods
❌ DON'T Waste On
  • Standard banner pulls with diamonds
  • Gold stage farming (events are better)
  • Pushing one character to 80 while others are 50
  • Resin refreshes past the 60-diamond tier
  • Farming protocores below level 60 domains

Common Pitfalls

Pulling on every banner: The most common new player mistake. You need ~14,400 diamonds minimum to guarantee a limited character. Pulling on a banner without enough savings is gambling. Ignoring events: Event shops are the most efficient use of stamina during event periods. A single event can give you 2-3 weeks worth of normal farming materials. Neglecting support operatives: Your main DPS matters, but support operatives (healers, buffers) at level 60-70 with correct gear will dramatically improve your clear times. Resin hoarding: Resin refreshes daily. Use them or lose them. Not planning weekly boss materials: Each operative needs specific boss materials for ascension. Pre-farm for your next planned operative.

Summary: Spend stamina on weekly bosses first, then protocores at 70+ domains, then ascension materials. Follow the 60/60/60 rule. Do not push past level 70 until all active operatives are at 70. Save 14,400 diamonds before pulling on limited banners. Plan months ahead, not days.
