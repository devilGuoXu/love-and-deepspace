Resource Planning Guide

Stamina, gold, XP, protocores, ascension materials — every resource has an optimal path. Here's how to never waste a single point of stamina.

ENTRY_#017 2026.06.29 RESOURCE_GUIDE

The Core Principle: Every resource in Love and Deepspace is a conversion of stamina. Gold? Stamina. XP scrolls? Stamina. Protocore energy? Stamina. The player who understands stamina efficiency will always progress faster than the player who doesn't. This guide teaches you to think in stamina — not in materials.

Resource Types & Stamina Conversion

Here's every major resource and how stamina-efficient it is to farm:

Resource Stamina/Unit Priority
Memory XP Scrolls (Purple) 0.2 stamina per 1 XP Farm only when needed
Gold 0.3 stamina per 1,000 gold Never farm directly
Skill Materials (Green) ~5 stamina per piece Weekly cap — farm every week
Skill Materials (Blue) ~10 stamina per piece Weekly cap — farm every week
Ascension Materials (T1) ~8 stamina per piece Farm as needed for breakpoints
Ascension Materials (T2) ~15 stamina per piece Farm as needed for breakpoints

Stamina Priority by Account Phase

Your farming priority changes as your account progresses. Here's the optimal stamina allocation for each phase:

EARLY GAME (Level 1-35)

Primary Goal: Level memories. All stamina goes to Bounty missions (memory XP).

Allocation: 80% Bounty (XP), 20% Main Story (first clear rewards).

Do NOT farm: Protocores (too early, low drop rate). Ascension materials (wait until you need breakpoints).

Smart Shortcut: Join a Crew day 1. Crew coins → SSR memory shards. Start this passive income stream immediately — it takes weeks to accumulate.

MID GAME (Level 35-50)

Primary Goal: Skill levels + second team assembly.

Allocation: 40% Skill Materials (Simulation Training), 30% Bounty (XP), 20% Ascension Materials, 10% Story/Events.

Key Insight: Skill materials have a weekly cap. If you skip a week, you can never recover that progress. Always cap your weekly skill material runs before farming anything else.

Warning: This is the phase where resource scarcity hits hardest. You need XP for 4 operatives, skill mats for 4 operatives, and ascension mats to unlock level caps. Prioritize your main DPS first, then spread to supports.

END GAME (Level 50+)

Primary Goal: Protocore optimization + Abyss depth.

Allocation: 40% Protocore Domain, 25% Skill Materials (weekly cap), 20% Bounty (XP), 15% Ascension Materials (as needed).

Key Insight: Protocores are the only RNG-based farm in the game. You will spend thousands of stamina chasing perfect substats. This is normal — accept that gold protocores with wrong stats are part of the process.

Tip: At this phase, gold becomes a bottleneck. Memory leveling costs escalate dramatically (1-60 costs ~600k gold per memory). Convert any extra stamina to gold if you're running low.

Weekly Boss Priority

Weekly bosses (World Boss / Domain Boss) are capped at 3 attempts per week. Here's the optimal order:

1
Memory Crystal Shards — Always pick this. Shards are the rarest material in the game. 3/week × 4 weeks = 1 SSR memory fragment.
2
Skill Material Boxes — Second pick. Skill materials are weekly-capped and boss boxes bypass the cap.
3
Gold / XP (if available) — Lowest priority. Farm these from normal stages if needed.

Week 1 Boss Strategy: Always use all 3 attempts before Sunday reset. If you can't clear the highest difficulty, clear the second-highest — the loot difference is smaller than missing a week entirely.

Ascension Breakpoint Guide

Not all ascension levels are equal. Here are the efficient breakpoints:

Level 40 → 50 High value — unlocks skill level 4
Level 50 → 60 High value — unlocks skill level 5, new passive
Level 60 → 70 Medium value — stat increase only
Level 70 → 80 Low value — massive cost for marginal gain. Last priority.

The 60/60/60 Rule

Before pushing any operative past level 60, get all 6 Abyss operatives to 60 first. The resource cost of level 60→70 on one operative equals the cost of level 1→60 on two operatives. Spread first, max later.

Daily Farming Route (Optimized)

Here's the exact daily loop to maximize resource efficiency:

Morning (5 min): Login → Collect passive stamina → Dispatch Crew missions → Do 1 Simulation Training run for daily mission.

Midday (5 min): Spend stamina on Bounty missions. If skill material weekly cap isn't reached yet, do Simulation Training instead.

Evening (10 min): Spend remaining stamina on highest priority (Protocore if 45+, Skill Materials if not capped, Bounty otherwise). Check events for limited stamina items. Claim accumulated stamina at lunch/supper timers.

Stamina Cap Rule: Never let stamina sit at max. Natural regen stops when stamina is full. If you sleep 8 hours, you lose 80 stamina (8h × 10 regen/hour). Log in at least once every 12 hours to keep regen working.

Long-Term Resource Projections

Knowing your long-term resource needs helps you plan ahead:

From 1 SSR → 70 (max) ~2.5 million XP, ~1.2 million gold, 4 weeks of skill mats
Full Abyss Team (6× level 60) ~6 million XP, ~3 million gold, 12+ weeks of skill mats
Monthly F2P Stamina ~7,200 stamina (240/day × 30)
Months to Full Abyss Team (F2P) 3-4 months of optimized farming

Reality Check: Full Abyss completion (Floors 1-12) in 3-4 months is the realistic F2P timeline. Anyone promising "Abyss ready in 1 month" is either a whale or exaggerating. The game is designed around steady progression — accept the timeline and optimize within it.

F2P vs Spender Resource Planning

F2P

Monthly stamina: ~7,200. Enough for 1 main operator + 1 secondary.

Strategy: Focus on 2 operatives max. Build to level 60, push main DPS to 70. Do NOT spread to 6 operatives until month 3. Use 4-star memories for gap filling. Never gem-refill stamina outside events.

Monthly Pass

Monthly stamina: ~9,600 (+2,400 from pass).

Strategy: Build 4 operatives comfortably. Main DPS to 70, two supports to 60, fourth filler to 50. You can afford 1-2 gem refills per week during events. Focus on skill materials — your extra stamina should go to Simulation Training.

Light Spender / Dolphin

Monthly stamina: ~12,000+ with regular refills.

Strategy: Build all 4 operatives to 70 comfortably. Premium farming allows chasing protocore substats. Your advantage is speed, not ceiling — you'll hit end game 1-2 months faster than F2P but the same content awaits.

Common Resource Mistakes

Mistake #1: Farming protocores before level 45. The drop rate for gold protocores (5-star) before level 45 is abysmally low. You'll get 90% blue/purple drops that become obsolete. Wait until the highest difficulty unlocks — the stamina efficiency difference is 3:1.

Mistake #2: Over-leveling memories past 60. Memory level 60→70 costs ~1 million XP and ~500k gold for approximately 8% stat increase. Level 1→60 on a new memory costs the same for 100% of that memory's value. Level 60 is the efficiency cap — push past it only for your absolute main DPS.

Mistake #3: Not capping skill materials weekly. Skill materials have a hard weekly cap. If you don't hit the cap this week, those materials are gone forever. There is no catch-up mechanic. Always prioritize weekly caps over unlimited farms.

Mistake #4: Forgetting to claim passive stamina. The lunch (12:00-14:00) and supper (18:00-20:00) stamina giveaways are easy to miss. 60 stamina each = 120 free stamina per day. Missing a week = 840 stamina lost. Set phone alarms.

Mistake #5: Leveling every SSR you pull. Every new SSR is tempting. But leveling a memory to 60 costs resources that could go to an operative you actually use. If you're not putting the SSR on one of your 6 Abyss operatives, leave it at level 1 until it's needed.

The Bottom Line: Resources in Love and Deepspace are a zero-sum game. Every point of stamina spent on one thing is a point not spent on something else. Prioritize weekly caps first. Know your efficient breakpoints (60/60/60 rule). Never farm protocores early. Claim stamina giveaways. And most importantly — accept the timeline. A 4-month Abyss clear is not slow — it's normal. The game is designed for steady daily play, not sprinting. Play consistently, optimize your daily route, and the resources will accumulate faster than you expect.
