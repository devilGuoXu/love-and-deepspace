Common Wanderers

Common Wanderers: Standard enemies that spawn in groups of 2-3. They are the primary source of enhancement materials and gold. Each common wanderer has a specific element weakness and attack pattern. Memorize these to clear domains faster.

FOREST SPRITE
Common — Forest — HP: 2,000-3,000 — Weak: Fire (Rafayel)
Low HP basic melee enemy. Attacks with a simple swing (telegraphed, easy dodge). Spawns in groups of 2-3. Uses a charge attack when below 25% HP that deals 2x damage but can be interrupted. Best counter: Rafayel's burn DoT kills them quickly. Drop: Sprite Dust (basic enhancement material, used for +1 to +6 enhancement). Respawn: 3 minutes.
THORN WALKER
Common — Forest — HP: 3,000-4,500 — Weak: Fire (Rafayel)
Ranged enemy that attacks with thorn projectiles. Each projectile applies a 3s poison DoT (stacks 3x). The thorns are dodgeable — strafe instead of dodging to conserve stamina. The poison tick damage is significant (100-150 damage per tick at 3 stacks). Use Zayne's Frostguard shield or cleanse to negate the poison. Drop: Thorn Vine (medium enhancement material).
SHADOW STALKER
Common — Urban — HP: 5,000-7,000 — Weak: Light (Xavier)
Fast melee enemy that dodges frequently. Has a 30% innate dodge chance against all attacks. Uses a three-hit combo that ends with a knockup. The knockup can be dodged (the third hit has a 0.5s windup). Xavier's Light element attacks reduce the dodge chance to 10% — making him the ideal counter. Drop: Shadow Essence (rare enhancement material, used for +9 to +12 enhancement).

Common enemy priority: In mixed groups, kill Thorn Walkers first (ranged + poison is dangerous), then Shadow Stalkers (dodge mechanic prolongs fights), then Forest Sprites (lowest threat). This priority prevents the most dangerous enemies from attacking while you are busy with weaker ones. Ranged enemies with debuffs are always the highest priority.

Elite Wanderers

Elite wanderers are stronger variants that appear solo or in pairs. They have unique mechanics and better drop rates. Elites are marked on the map with a red icon.

ANCIENT TREANT (FOREST ELITE)
HP: 25,000 — Weak: Fire — Resists: Ice, Physical. The Ancient Treant is the forest region's elite. It has three attacks: Root Grab (roots trap you in place — dodge the green circle on the ground), Acorn Barrage (AoE around the boss — run out of range), and Heal (regenerates 10% HP over 5s — interrupt with a stun). The Treant heals twice during the fight (at 75% and 50% HP). Save your interrupt skill for these moments. Burn (Rafayel) prevents the heal from being effective. Drops: Ancient Bark (rare material for +12 to +15 enhancement), Treant Memory (set piece, 5% drop).
SHADOW LORD (URBAN ELITE — NIGHT ONLY)
HP: 50,000 — Weak: Light — Resists: Dark. The Shadow Lord is the urban elite that only spawns at night (8PM-6AM in-game). It has: Shadow Clones (creates 2 decoys — hit the real one to cancel the mechanic), Darkness Bolts (homing projectiles — dodge at the last moment for perfect dodge counter), Execute (instant kill below 30% HP — the boss glows red, dodge the grab). The Shadow Lord is the hardest elite in the game. Bring Xavier (Light element) for maximum damage. Co-op recommended for first kills. Drops: Shadow Core (boss summoning item), exclusive weapon skin materials.
ABYSS HUNTER (COAST ELITE)
HP: 35,000 — Weak: Ice — Resists: Fire. The Abyss Hunter is a water-element elite that attacks with water jets and a lunging bite. It applies a stacking slow debuff (20% per stack, max 3 stacks). Uses a water shield that blocks all damage for 5s — break the shield by dealing 5,000 damage in 2 seconds or wait it out. Zayne's Ice element deals 50% bonus damage. The Abyss Hunter drops Leviathan materials and has a 8% chance to drop an SSR memory piece. Located in the underwater cave — bring oxygen management.

World Bosses

World bosses require summoning items and have weekly lockouts. Here is the quick reference for all four:

ANCIENT DRAGON
Mountain — Fire — Dragon's Scale — 4-6 min — Weak: Ice
Phase 1: Fire breath (frontal cone), tail sweep (360°). Phase 2 (50% HP): Flight mode — rains fire from above. Hide behind pillars. Phase 3 (25% HP): Enrage — faster attacks, reduced tell times. Bring Zayne for Ice damage and shields.
ABYSSAL LEVIATHAN
Coast — Water — Leviathan's Tear — 5-7 min — Weak: Ice
Phase 1: Water jets, dive attack. Phase 2 (60% HP): Tidal wave — dodge through the wave. Phase 3 (30% HP): Whirlpool — pull effect, run away. Floating platform — falling = death.
SHADOW LORD
Urban — Dark — Shadow Core — 6-8 min — Weak: Light
Phase 1: Shadow clones, darkness bolts. Phase 2 (50% HP): Execute mechanic — dodge the red grab. Phase 3 (25% HP): Permanent darkness — use Light element to reveal boss.

Boss rule of thumb: If you cannot kill the boss within 2 minutes of the enrage timer, you are not ready for that boss. The enrage timer is the game telling you your DPS is too low. Upgrade your protocores (especially CRIT Rate/DMG) before attempting again. Each boss has a recommended DPS threshold: Ancient Dragon (6K DPS), Abyssal Leviathan (7K), Shadow Lord (8K), Void Lord (10K+).

Element strategy: The element advantage system gives 50% bonus damage (attacks deal 1.5x to weak enemies) and 50% reduced damage (attacks deal 0.5x to resistant enemies). Always bring at least one character whose element is strong against the enemy. If you do not know the enemy's weakness, bring Xavier (Light) — Light element is neutral against almost everything, so you never deal reduced damage. Xavier is the safest choice for blind content.

Element team building: For general content (unknown enemy lineup), run Xavier (Light) + Zayne (Ice) + Sylus (Dark). This covers 3 elements and guarantees that at most one character deals reduced damage against any enemy. For specific content (known enemy lineup), bring the strong element character + two neutral or flex characters. Element advantage is a 50% DPS increase — it is worth building around.

Drop Table Reference

Quick drop table for farming decisions:

BASIC ENHANCEMENT (Lv 1-6)
Forest region — Sprite Dust, Thorn Vine — Common wanderers
Forest Sprites: Sprite Dust (90%), Gold (10%). Thorn Walkers: Thorn Vine (80%), Sprite Dust (15%), Gold (5%). Clear speed: fast. Efficiency: good for early game only. Most efficient route: forest full clear (12 spawns, 4 minutes).
MEDIUM ENHANCEMENT (Lv 6-9)
Urban region — Shadow Essence — Shadow Stalkers + Void Creepers
Shadow Stalkers: Shadow Essence (60%), Gold (30%), Shadow Core piece (10%). Void Creepers: Void Fragment (50%), Shadow Essence (30%), Gold (20%). Best farming in the game for mid-game materials. Urban region full clear (15 spawns, 8-10 minutes) yields 15-25 materials.
RARE ENHANCEMENT (Lv 9-12)
Mountain + Coast — Ancient Bark, Leviathan Scale — Elite enemies
Ancient Treant: Ancient Bark (70%), Treant Memory (5%), Gold (25%). Abyss Hunter: Leviathan Scale (60%), SSR Memory Piece (8%), Gold (32%). These materials are needed for +9 to +12 enhancement. Elite-only drops make this the bottleneck for character progression. Farm these daily.
ENDGAME MATERIALS (Lv 12-15)
Void region — Void Core Fragment — Void enemies
Void Spawns: Void Core Fragment (30%), Void Dust (60%), Gold (10%). Void Weavers: Void Core Fragment (50%), Void Memory Set piece (15%), Gold (35%). These are the hardest materials to farm. Required for max enhancement (+15) and character rank-up (R2+). Farm only at endgame (level 55+).

Farming priority by account stage: Early (1-30): Forest → basic materials. Mid (30-45): Urban + Mountain → medium + rare materials. Late (45-55): Coast + Mountain → rare materials + character rank-up. Endgame (55-60): Void → endgame materials + exclusive memory set. Do not farm materials from a tier above your current enhancement levels — the efficiency is lower because you need more basic materials for the next tier anyway.

Quick Battle Tactics

General tactics that work against all wanderer types:

1
Group enemies with positioning
Run to one side of the spawn group. Enemies converge on your position. Use AoE attacks to hit all grouped enemies simultaneously. This reduces clear time by 30-50% compared to fighting enemies individually. Xavier's Light Seeker 4pc AoE is excellent for grouped enemies.
2
Interrupt dangerous attacks
Elite enemies have telegraphed attacks (red indicator). Save your resonance skill or swap to a character with a stun to interrupt these. The Ancient Treant's heal (green glow) is the most important interrupt in early game. Missing the interrupt extends the fight by 30-60 seconds.
3
Use the environment
Pillars block line of sight for ranged enemies. Walls can be used to prevent knockback. Water surfaces cause lightning attacks (if available) to deal bonus damage. Terrain advantages are small but accumulate over long farming sessions.
4
Swap characters to share cooldowns
When one character's skills are on cooldown, swap to another character. The off-field character regenerates energy faster. This rotation technique maximizes uptime on your damaging skills. The optimal swap frequency is every 4-6 seconds for most compositions.
5
Perfect dodge for energy
Perfect dodging (dodging at the last moment) grants bonus energy. Learning perfect dodge timing against each enemy type significantly improves your energy economy. This enables more ultimate uses per fight, which translates to higher DPS.
DO
  • Check element weakness before engaging
  • Group enemies with positioning + AoE
  • Interrupt elite enemy special attacks
  • Use environment for tactical advantage
  • Swap characters to cycle cooldowns
  • Perfect dodge for bonus energy
DON'T
  • Don't fight with the wrong element (50% penalty)
  • Don't ignore enemy attack tells
  • Don't stand still — always strafe
  • Don't waste dodges (use strafe for basic attacks)
  • Don't hoard ultimates — use them on cooldown
  • Don't fight elites without knowing their mechanics
