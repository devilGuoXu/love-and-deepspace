Quick Operative Reference

Xavier (Light) — Best generalist. Works against 80% of standard wanderers. Hard counters Void Spawn, Phantom Sylus, and Void Corruptor. Use him when you don't know what you're facing.

Sylus (Dark) — Execute specialist. Best against Phantom Xavier. His execute passive below 30% HP is invaluable against high-HP targets. Use him in boss fights and against Phantoms.

Rafayel (Fire) — Shield breaker + DoT. Hard counters Frost Weaver, Phantom Zayne, and Crystal Monarch. Burn DoT is the only reliable damage against Freeze Armor. Use him in ice-heavy content.

Zayne (Ice) — Sustain + control. Hard counters Ember Weaver, Phantom Rafayel, and Titan Sentry. His passive slow and shields make him the safest pick for survival checks. Use him when you need to outlast.

Memory Rule: If you can only level one operative for wanderer clearing, pick Xavier. His Light element has the widest coverage. Level Sylus second for single-target dps. Level Rafayel and Zayne third based on the content you face.

Summary: Void Spawn → Light (Xavier). Elementals → opposite element. Phantoms → mirror element. Ancients → Ice/Fire/Light per boss. Xavier is the safest general pick. Swap operatives based on the wanderer type — never bring the wrong element.
