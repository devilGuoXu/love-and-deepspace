Team Builder Tool Overview

Team Builder Tools: The team builder is a web-based tool that lets you select operatives, assign memories and protocores, and see the combined team stats. It calculates team DPS, energy synergy, element coverage, and highlights gaps in your composition. The best team builder tools are maintained by the community and updated every patch.

A good team builder tool shows you more than just which characters you have selected. It analyzes: total team DPS (sum of all three operatives), DPS distribution (is one character carrying?), energy cycle (can your supports feed your DPS?), element coverage (how many elements does your team hit?), and survivability metrics (total HP, healing output, shield strength). These metrics help you identify weak points before you enter combat.

Understanding Team Roles

Every team needs three roles: Main DPS, Sub DPS/Support, and Flex. The team builder helps you assign these roles correctly.

MAIN DPS
Xavier (Light Seeker) or Rafayel (Inferno Blaze)
The Main DPS deals 50-60% of the team's total damage. They stay on the field most of the time. Build them with maximum damage stats: high ATK, high CRIT Rate/DMG, appropriate DMG Bonus. The Main DPS should have the best protocores in your roster. In the team builder, the Main DPS character is marked with a star icon — the tool calculates their field time as 60-70% of total combat time.
SUB DPS / SUPPORT
Zayne (Frostguard) or Sylus (Dark Champion)
The Sub DPS deals 25-35% of team damage while providing utility. Sylus offers execute burst. Zayne offers shields and debuff cleansing. Build Sub DPS with balanced stats — enough damage to contribute meaningfully, enough survivability to stay alive. In the team builder, the Sub DPS character has 30-40% field time. Their energy generation is critical — they feed energy to the Main DPS through specific memory set bonuses.
FLEX
Any character that covers team gaps
The Flex slot covers whatever the team is missing: element coverage, survivability, or extra damage. If the Main DPS is Xavier (Light) and the Sub DPS is Sylus (Dark), the Flex might be Zayne (Ice) for shield support. If the team needs more damage, Flex could be a second DPS with different element coverage. The team builder highlights what your Flex slot should provide based on the first two characters.

Role Assignment Rule: Your best-built character goes in Main DPS. Your second-best goes in Sub DPS/Support. Flex fills the gap. Do NOT put your best character in Flex because "they can do everything." Flex characters have less field time — you are wasting their potential. Maximize field time for your strongest character by putting them in Main DPS.

Synergy Analysis

The team builder calculates synergy scores between characters. Synergy measures how well two characters work together based on: energy generation matching, skill cooldown alignment, memory set interactions, and element reactions.

X-Z
XAVIER + ZAYNE
Synergy: 92% — Best combo. Zayne shields keep Xavier alive, Xavier's fast energy generation feeds Zayne's ultimate.
X-S
XAVIER + SYLUS
Synergy: 85% — Strong. Sylus execute finishes what Xavier starts. Cooldowns align well.
Z-R
ZAYNE + RAFAYEL
Synergy: 78% — Good. Zayne's shields let Rafayel stack burn safely.
R-S
RAFAYEL + SYLUS
Synergy: 55% — Poor. Both need field time. Cooldowns clash.

Xavier + Zayne is the highest-synergy pair in the game. Xavier generates energy quickly through his fast basic attacks. Zayne's passive energy generation (from Frostguard 4pc) feeds Xavier's ultimate. Their cooldowns align perfectly — Zayne's shield (12s cooldown) covers Xavier's burst window (10-12s rotation). This pair forms the core of most top-tier Abyss teams. The team builder shows a green synergy indicator for this pair.

Rafayel + Sylus is the lowest-synergy pair. Both characters want extended field time to maximize their damage — Rafayel needs to stack burn, Sylus needs to maintain pressure for the execute window. When paired together, neither character gets enough field time to perform optimally. The team builder shows a red synergy warning for this pair and suggests replacing one of them with Zayne for better team balance.

SYNERGY BREAKDOWN IN THE TOOL
The team builder calculates synergy using five factors: (1) Energy alignment — do their energy curves match? Xavier peaks at 8s, Zayne at 10s — close enough. (2) Cooldown alignment — do their skill windows overlap? (3) Memory set interaction — does one character's set buff another? (4) Element reaction — do their elements create reactions? (5) Field time competition — do they fight for field time? Each factor is scored 0-20, total 0-100.

Element Coverage

The team builder maps element coverage against enemy weaknesses. Each enemy type has a weakness (takes 50% more damage from that element) and a resistance (takes 50% less damage). A team that hits multiple elements adapts to more content.

Optimal element coverage: A team of Xavier (Light) + Zayne (Ice) + Sylus (Dark) covers 3 of 4 elements, meaning at most one enemy type in any content will have a resistance against your team. This is the "broad coverage" team composition recommended for general content where you do not know enemy lineups in advance.

Specialized element coverage: For Abyss floors where enemy types are known (check the Abyss floor guide), you can double up on elements. For example, Abyss floor 8 has Fire enemies (weak to Ice). Bring Zayne (Ice) + Xavier (Light — neutral) + Sylus (Dark — neutral). Zayne deals 50% bonus damage against the Fire enemies, effectively acting as a second Main DPS for that floor.

"I used to run Xavier + Rafayel + Sylus for every floor. After checking the team builder, I realized my element coverage was trash — three elements weak to one type. I swapped to Xavier + Zayne + Sylus and jumped from floor 8 to floor 10 in one cycle."

— Community Member, Team Builder Feedback Thread

Element Strategy Tip: The team builder shows a radar chart of your element coverage. When the chart is unbalanced (one element dominates), the tool highlights which content types will be difficult. Use this information to plan which character to build next. If your radar shows low Ice coverage, build Zayne next. The element coverage chart is the most underutilized feature of the team builder — most players ignore it and wonder why they struggle on specific floors.

Budget Team Building

Not everyone has all four characters built to level 60 with perfect protocores. The team builder has a "budget mode" that lets you set investment levels for each character and find the optimal team within your resource constraints.

1
Set your budget
Enter your available resources: gold, dust, stamina items, protocore exp. The tool calculates how much you can invest.
2
Choose investment priority
Decide: balanced team (all three at 70% power) or carry team (Main DPS at 100%, supports at 50%). The carry team strategy is more effective for early-game players.
3
Use the optimizer
The team builder's optimizer tests thousands of combinations and finds the best team for your specific resource level. It runs in your browser and takes 10-30 seconds.
4
Review the recommendation
The optimizer shows the top 5 teams for your budget. Each team is ranked by predicted performance. The differences between rank 1 and rank 5 are usually small (2-5%).
5
Adjust based on preference
If the optimizer recommends a team you dislike (wrong characters, boring playstyle), pick rank 2 or 3 instead. A team you enjoy playing will perform better in practice than an optimized team you resent.
BUDGET TEAM EXAMPLES
Early game (Hunter Level 20-30): Xavier (Lv40, 4pc Light Seeker, 4* protocores) + Zayne (Lv30, 2pc Frostguard, 3* protocores) + Flex (any Lv20). Estimated budget: 200K gold, 500 dust.
Mid game (Hunter Level 30-45): Xavier (Lv50, 4pc Light Seeker, 5* protocores) + Zayne (Lv45, 4pc Frostguard, 5* protocores) + Sylus (Lv40, 2+2 sets, 4* protocores). Estimated budget: 1.5M gold, 3K dust.
End game (Hunter Level 45-60): All three at Lv60 with full 5* protocore sets. Estimated budget: 5M+ gold, 20K+ dust.

Tool Features & Tips

The team builder has several advanced features that most users do not know about. These features save time and provide deeper insights.

Feature: Shareable Build Links
Click "Export Link" to generate a URL containing your entire team build. Share this link on Discord or Reddit when asking for feedback. Responders can open the link and see your exact setup — no screenshots needed. The link is ~200 characters and contains all build data. URLs are stateless — no server stores your build.
Feature: Enemy Database Integration
The team builder can import enemy data from the Abyss floor guide. Select the floor you are attempting, and the tool shows each enemy's element weakness and resist. It then highlights which of your team members will deal bonus damage and which will deal reduced damage. This integration is updated every patch cycle.
Feature: Upgrade Simulator
The upgrade simulator answers the question: "Should I level Xavier's memories or farm new protocores?" Input your planned upgrade path, and the tool simulates the expected DPS increase. It also estimates the resource cost (gold, dust, stamina). This helps you prioritize upgrades by ROI.
Feature: Team vs Team Comparison
Save two team compositions and compare them side-by-side. The comparison shows: DPS difference, survivability difference, element coverage difference, and resource cost difference. Use this when deciding between two team configurations.
Feature: Export to Calculator
The team builder can export your team's stats directly to the DPS calculator. This saves you the time of re-entering all stats manually. The export button is located in the top-right corner of the team summary panel.
