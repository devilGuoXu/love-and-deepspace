Team Builder Walkthrough

Plan your team compositions with confidence — use community team builder tools to optimize synergies before spending resources.

ENTRY_#WRK_042026.07.02TOOLS

How to Read This: Use this guide alongside the team builder tool. The theory section helps you evaluate the tool's suggestions. The practical section helps you input your roster correctly.

Why Use a Team Builder?

Team composition in Deepspace is deceptively complex. You need to consider: elemental coverage, stellactrum color matching, resonance skill synergy, energy regen distribution, and set bonus compatibility. Doing this manually for 3-4 teams across multiple content types is error-prone. A team builder tool automates the optimization. Input your available operatives and their gear, and the tool suggests the best 3-person teams for each content type. What the tool tells you: Which 3 operatives to use, which memories to equip on each, which protocore sets to prioritize, and how much DPS/survivability the team has. Most team builders rank teams by a composite score that balances damage, survivability, and elemental coverage.

How to Input Your Roster

Step 1: List all operatives you have built (level 40+ with at least +6 protocores). Do not include level-1 operatives — they cannot contribute meaningfully. Step 2: For each operative, note their best memory set and approximate stats (ATK, CRIT rate, elemental damage). Step 3: Note which stellactrum colors each operative covers. Step 4: Check the "team synergy" tags — does this operative provide team buffs, debuffs, or battery? Input tips: Be honest about gear quality. A "built" operative with +6 protocores is not equal to a "built" operative with +12 protocores. Most tools let you rate gear quality as Low / Medium / High. Use these ratings correctly to get accurate suggestions. Overrating your gear leads to team suggestions that underperform in practice.

Interpreting Team Builder Output

The team builder will suggest several team compositions ranked by score. Do not blindly pick #1. Consider: Score gap: If #1 is 10%+ better than #2, use #1. If the gap is under 5%, any team in the top 3 will work — pick based on personal preference. Content match: Some teams excel at single-target (abyss bosses) while others excel at multi-wave (trial tower). Match the team to the content type. Investment cost: A team that requires building a new operative from scratch costs weeks of stamina. A team that uses already-built operatives with a gear swap costs 2-3 days. The best team on paper is not the best team for your current account state. Future-proofing: Teams that share memory sets across operatives are easier to maintain than teams that require 3 completely different sets. Look for overlapping gear requirements.

Common Team Builder Mistakes: (1) Inputting too many operatives — you should only input 4-6 operatives that you actually use. The tool cannot know which operatives you enjoy playing. (2) Ignoring the support role — the tool may suggest a triple-DPS team with no healer. This works for speed clears but fails in sustained content. Manually flag one slot as "support" if the tool does not support role assignment. (3) Forgetting to update after gear changes — every time you upgrade a protocore from +9 to +12, the team rankings can shift. Re-run the tool after major gear upgrades. (4) Treating the tool's score as gospel — the score is an approximation. The actual performance in-game depends on your skill. A 10% score difference is usually negligible in practice.

Manual Team Building Checklist

If you prefer to build teams manually, use this checklist: (1) Pick 2-3 operatives you enjoy playing. (2) Check elemental coverage — at least 2 different elements. (3) Check stellactrum — all 6 colors should be covered across the team. (4) Assign roles — one main DPS, one sub-DPS or battery, one support. (5) Check energy — at least one team member should have 140%+ ER. (6) Check set bonuses — no overlapping unique set bonuses. (7) Verify against content — does this team have the right elements for this week's abyss/SHC? Following this checklist manually gives results that are usually within 5% of the tool's best suggestion.

Summary: Team builders save time and catch mistakes. Input only your real operatives (level 40+, geared). Check the score gap before picking #1. Match team archetype to content type. Update the tool after major gear changes. Use the manual checklist as a fallback. The tool is a guide, not a guarantee — your skill matters more than a 5% optimization.
