Stamina Basics: Income & Capacity

Stamina (also called Energy or Resin depending on your server) is the primary gated resource for account progression in Love and Deepspace. Almost every account-building activity consumes stamina, and stamina regeneration is capped by time rather than spending.

Regeneration Rate. Stamina regenerates at 1 unit per 6 minutes, or 10 units per hour. Daily passive regeneration: 240 stamina. Maximum storage capacity: 300 stamina (unlocked through account progression). Any stamina earned beyond the cap is lost, which makes regular spending essential.

Daily Free Stamina. Login bonus: 60 stamina. Friend gifts: 20 stamina (from active friends sending/receiving). Total daily free stamina (passive + login + friends): 320 stamina. This is the baseline all players should expect.

Bonus Stamina Sources. Level-up refills: full stamina refill on each Hunter level (one-time). Event rewards: variable — typically 100-300 stamina per event. Battle Pass: 200-400 stamina per pass cycle (paid track). Stamina packs: from events, mail, and achievements. These are supplemental sources that provide flexibility but should not be relied upon for baseline budgeting.

Stamina Packs. Event stamina packs (often found in event shops) are the most flexible stamina resource because they can be stored and used when most beneficial. Always claim free stamina packs from events. Save them for double-drop events when each stamina point is worth double its normal value.

Farming Efficiency Rankings

Not all stamina expenditures are equal. Understanding the efficiency ranking of different farming activities allows you to allocate stamina where it provides the most account value.

Tier S — Protocore Farming. Protocore stages provide the highest long-term value per stamina because protocores are the primary differentiator between accounts of similar memory card investment. A well-farmed protocore set for your main team provides 30-50% more effective damage than a poorly-farmed set. Protocore farming should never fall below 30% of your daily stamina budget.

Tier A — Memory Card Ascension Materials. These materials bottleneck your ability to level memory cards past 40, 50, and 60. Farming ascension materials proactively (before you need them) prevents situations where you have the memory card but cannot level it. Ascension material farming should be 20-30% of daily stamina, adjusted based on current needs.

Tier B — Character Level-Up Materials. Operative level-up materials are needed in moderate quantities. They are less gating than ascension materials because operative leveling is a slower process overall. Character material farming: 10-20% of daily stamina.

Tier C — Gold. Gold is needed for almost everything but is obtainable from many sources (events, missions, achievements). Dedicated gold farming is rarely efficient unless you have an urgent immediate need. Gold farming: 0-10% of daily stamina, only when needed.

Tier D — Memory Card Standard Farming. Farming memory cards from standard stages has poor drop rates relative to stamina cost. Only farm memory cards if you specifically need a card for set completion or breakthrough. Standard memory card farming: 0-5% of stamina, only for specific targets.

Daily Stamina Budget

A standardized daily stamina budget helps ensure consistent allocation without requiring daily decision-making.

Standard Day Budget (320 stamina). Protocore farming: 120 stamina (4 runs at 30 stamina each). Memory ascension materials: 80 stamina (2-3 runs). Character level-up materials: 60 stamina (2 runs). Reserve: 60 stamina (for daily missions, event stages, or urgent needs). This allocation assumes no active event and no immediate bottleneck.

Bottleneck Day Budget. When you have an immediate need (memory card ready to ascend, new character to level), shift allocation: bottleneck target: 180 stamina (60%). Protocore farming: 80 stamina (25%). Reserve: 60 stamina (15%). Address the bottleneck for 2-4 days, then return to standard allocation.

Event Day Budget. During active events: event stages: 120-200 stamina (40-60%). Protocore farming: 80 stamina (25%). Reserve: 40-60 stamina (15-20%). The exact event allocation depends on event value — high-value events with limited items justify higher event stamina allocation.

Protocore Farming Strategy

Protocore farming is the most important and most nuanced stamina expenditure. A strategic approach dramatically improves results.

Target Selection. Focus on one protocore set for one operative at a time. Do not spread farming across multiple sets until your primary set is functional (correct main stats on all 4 slots, acceptable substats). Expected timeline for a functional set: 2-4 weeks of dedicated farming.

Main Stat Priority. Slot Alpha: ATK% or Elemental DMG% (operative-dependent). Slot Beta: Crit Rate (if below 50% total) or Crit DMG (if Crit Rate is sufficient). Slot Gamma: ATK% (majority of operatives) or Energy Regen (specific burst-dependent operatives). Slot Delta: ATK% (DPS) or Healing Bonus (healers) or HP% (HP-scaling operatives). Correct main stat is non-negotiable — do not settle for wrong main stats even with good substats.

Substat Evaluation. For F2P and light spenders: 2+ relevant substats is keepable, 3+ is excellent, 4 is perfect but not expected. For whales: 3+ relevant substats is minimum. Substat priority varies by operative but generally: Crit Rate = Crit DMG > ATK% > ATK flat > Elemental DMG% > Energy Regen > DEF/HP.

Knowing When to Stop. The trap of protocore farming is perfectionism. A set with correct main stats and 2 acceptable substats in each slot will perform at 85-90% of perfect. The remaining 10-15% requires exponentially more farming time. Know when a slot is "good enough" and move on to the next bottleneck.

Ascension Material Farming

Memory card ascension materials become a bottleneck predictably — at levels 40, 50, and 60. Proactive farming prevents delays.

Material Tier System. Green (Tier 1) — needed for levels 1-30. Blue (Tier 2) — needed for levels 30-50. Purple (Tier 3) — needed for levels 50-60+. Gold (Tier 4) — needed for levels 60+. Higher tiers require more stamina per unit but provide proportionally more value.

Pre-Farming Strategy. When you know you will be ascending a card within the next week (because you have the card and are close to the next ascension threshold), start farming the required materials 3-5 days in advance. This spreads the stamina cost over multiple days rather than forcing a single high-stamina day.

Conversion Efficiency. Lower-tier materials can be converted to higher tiers (typically 3:1 ratio). This is less efficient than farming the correct tier directly. Only convert if you have a surplus of lower-tier materials and an immediate need for higher tiers. Never farm lower tiers with the intention of converting — always farm the tier you need.

Character Level-Up Farming

Leveling operatives requires specific materials that should be farmed efficiently to avoid wasting stamina.

Level-Up Material Types. Each operative requires their specific faction materials plus generic EXP items. Faction materials change by region and story progression. Once you unlock higher-level faction stages, never farm lower-level stages — the stamina-to-material ratio improves at higher stages.

Target Levels. For main team: level 60 is the efficient stopping point. For secondary team: level 50. For flex characters: level 40. The cost per level increases significantly above 60, and the stat returns diminish. Only exceed 60 if you have specific content requirements that demand it.

Raising Multiple Characters. Focus on one team at a time. Raising 3 characters to 60 is more valuable than raising 6 characters to 50. Team synergy matters more than individual character levels. A coordinated level-60 team will outperform a random collection of level-50 characters.

Gold & General Material Farming

Gold is the universal secondary resource, needed for everything from leveling to enhancement. Its ubiquity makes efficient gold management essential.

Gold Sources. Events (primary source — event shops provide excellent gold value). Missions (daily and weekly provide moderate gold). Gold farming stages (available but inefficient — only farm when desperate). Selling unwanted protocores and materials (supplemental income).

Gold Efficiency Tip. Never farm gold from dedicated stages unless you have an immediate need and have exhausted all other gold sources. The stamina-to-gold ratio on farming stages is approximately 50% worse than the ratio you get from event shops. Prioritize event shops for gold acquisition.

EXP Material Farming. Memory card EXP materials (enhancement crystals) are best obtained from events and protocore farming byproducts. Dedicated EXP farming is rarely efficient. If you need EXP materials, check event shops first, then protocore stage drops (which include EXP materials as secondary rewards), and only farm dedicated EXP stages as a last resort.

Event Stamina Allocation

Events create competing demands on stamina. The framework below helps you make allocation decisions based on event type and value.

High-Value Events (Major Banner Events). When the event shop contains limited items (memory cards, exclusive materials), allocate 50-60% of daily stamina to event stages. Reduce protocore farming to 20% temporarily. The limited items are time-sensitive and worth the temporary shift in allocation.

Medium-Value Events (Minigame, Story Events). When the event shop has good consumables but no limited items, allocate 30-40% of stamina to event stages. Maintain protocore farming at 30%. These events provide value but do not justify a major allocation shift.

Low-Value Events (Login Events, Small Celebrations). When the event provides mostly passive rewards (login bonuses, mail rewards), maintain standard stamina allocation. Use event stamina packs at your convenience rather than prioritizing event farming.

Event End Rush. In the final 2-3 days of an event, assess your remaining event currency needs. If you are short of a high-value item, temporarily increase event stamina allocation to secure it. If you have already claimed all high-value items, return to standard allocation and use remaining event currency on whatever materials you need most.

Stamina Refresh Analysis

Spending diamonds on stamina refreshes is a controversial topic. This section provides a framework for when it is worth it.

Refresh Cost. First daily refresh: 50 diamonds for 60 stamina. Second refresh: 100 diamonds for 60 stamina. Third and subsequent: 200 diamonds for 60 stamina. The cost escalates, making multiple daily refreshes increasingly inefficient.

When to Refresh (F2P). Almost never. The F2P diamond budget is too tight to spare for stamina. The only exception is during a high-value event where you are a few hundred event currency short of a limited item and have no other way to obtain it. In this case, one refresh (50 diamonds) may be justified.

When to Refresh (Spenders). Monthly Card holders can consider 1 refresh per day during events (50 diamonds, ~$0.08 value). This adds 60 stamina per day, 1,800 per month — approximately 6 extra days of stamina. The value is marginal but positive for players who prioritize account growth. Multiple refreshes per day are not recommended unless you are a whale.

Refresh Alternative. Before refreshing, check: have you claimed all free stamina packs? Have you collected friend stamina? Is your stamina capped (over 300)? Have you completed all stamina-giving achievements? These sources are free and should be exhausted before considering diamond refreshes.

Strategy by Account Stage

Stamina priorities change across account development stages. Tailoring your strategy to your current stage maximizes efficiency.

Early Game (Weeks 1-4). Priority: story completion (stamina-efficient first-clear rewards), character leveling to 40, memory card leveling to 30. Do not focus on protocores yet — you will replace early-game protocores quickly. Focus on building account baseline: one team at level 40 with level 30 memory cards.

Mid Game (Weeks 5-12). Priority: transition to protocore farming (30-40% of stamina), ascension materials for level 50 breakpoints, expand to second team. Your first team should reach level 50-60 in this phase. Begin Senior Hunter Contest and Abyssal Trial participation for diamond income.

Late Game (Months 3-6). Priority: protocore optimization (40-50% of stamina), two teams at level 60, targeted memory card ascension for specific content needs. Abyssal floor 9-10 clear becomes expected. Senior Hunter Contest at 12+ stars.

End Game (Months 6+). Priority: protocore perfection (farming for specific substats), three teams operational, memory card rank investment. Abyssal floor 11-12 attempts become realistic with focused account building. This stage is about incremental improvements across all dimensions.

Long-Term Resource Planning

Account development in Love and Deepspace is measured in months. Long-term planning prevents resource crises and ensures steady progress.

Monthly Resource Budget. Create a monthly budget for each major resource: stamina (9,600 baseline per month), diamonds (track actual income), gold (variable), enhancement crystals (variable). Knowing your monthly income for each resource helps you plan monthly expenditures without overspending.

Anticipating Bottlenecks. As you approach major account milestones (level 50 breakpoint, level 60 breakpoint, new memory card acquisition), pre-farm the required materials. A week of pre-farming costs 2,240 stamina (320/day) but saves you from a 2-week delay when the bottleneck hits.

Resource Conversion Awareness. Be aware of conversion rates between resources. Understanding that 50 diamonds = 60 stamina = approximately 2 protocore runs = approximately 10,000 gold event value helps you evaluate whether a specific purchase or refresh is worthwhile.

Tracking Tools & Templates

Consistent tracking is the foundation of good resource management. Here are practical tools and templates.

Daily Stamina Log. A simple notepad or spreadsheet: date, total stamina earned, total stamina spent, activity breakdown (protocore runs, ascension runs, event runs, etc.), and any stamina packs claimed or used. Review weekly to identify allocation patterns.

Weekly Farming Plan. At the start of each week: set stamina allocation percentages for the week (e.g., protocores 35%, ascension 25%, characters 15%, event 15%, reserve 10%). List specific goals (e.g., "complete protocore set for Xavier"). Review at week's end.

Bottleneck Forecast. Look ahead 2-4 weeks. What memory cards are you planning to ascend? What characters are you planning to level? What events are coming? Identify upcoming bottlenecks and plan pre-farming accordingly. A 10-minute planning session at the start of each month prevents resource crises.

The 80/20 Rule of Stamina: 80% of your account's power comes from 20% of your stamina expenditure — specifically, the protocore farming you do consistently every day. Everything else (ascension materials, character levels, gold) provides diminishing returns. If you only have time for one activity each day, make it protocore farming. Everything else can be caught up, but lost protocore farming days are permanently gone.

Summary: Stamina management is the most impactful skill for account growth in Love and Deepspace. The daily baseline is 300-320 stamina from passive regeneration, login, and friends. Protocore farming provides the highest long-term value and should never fall below 30% of daily stamina. Ascension materials should be farmed proactively to prevent bottlenecks. Event allocation depends on event value — high-value events with limited items justify 50-60% stamina allocation. Diamond refreshes are rarely worth it for F2P players; Monthly Card holders can consider one daily refresh during events. Strategy shifts across account stages: early game focuses on baseline building, mid game on protocore transition, late game on optimization, and end game on perfection. Consistent tracking and advance planning prevent resource crises and ensure steady account progression regardless of spending level.
