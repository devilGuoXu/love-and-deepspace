STYLE GUIDE — SHOPPING

Outfit Investment Priority

Not all outfits are created equal. Here is exactly which ones to buy, which to skip, and how to plan your outfit collection.

ENTRY_#STY_062026.07.02OUTFITS

How to Read This: Light spenders should focus on the value ranking. F2P players should skip to the free outfit section. Completionists need the rotation schedule.

Outfit Value Tiers

S-Tier (Battle Pass): The $9.99 battle pass outfit is the best value in the game. You get a full outfit + accessories + diamonds + materials. One battle pass outfit every 6 weeks = 8-9 outfits per year. Always buy the battle pass outfit before considering limited shop outfits. A-Tier (Limited Shop — SSR Outfits): Premium outfits at $29.99-$49.99. These have unique animations, special effects, and exclusive photo mode interactions. Buy only for your main operative. An SSR outfit for an operative you do not use is wasted money. B-Tier (Recolor / Variant): $14.99-$19.99 for a color swap of an existing outfit. Only buy if you main that operative and use the base outfit frequently. Recolors do not have new animations. C-Tier (Free Event Outfits): These are excellent — always claim them. Zero cost, unique designs. The only downside is that they are one-per-operative and may not return. F-Tier (Starter / Default): Already owned. Do not spend on upgrading default outfits unless you have specific photo mode needs.

Outfit Value Calculation

When comparing outfits, consider: Unique animations: An outfit with a new idle animation, walk cycle, or combat stance is worth 2x a recolor. Photo mode versatility: Outfits that photograph well from multiple angles and in multiple backgrounds are more valuable than outfits that only look good in specific poses. Operative coverage: An outfit for your least-used operative has zero value unless you plan to use them more. Reuse rate: Will you put this outfit on the operative for more than 10 photo sessions? If not, skip it. Time-limited vs permanent: Limited outfits that never return are inherently more valuable than permanent shop items. Buy limited first, permanent later. Simple formula: Outfit value = (hours of use) / (dollars spent) × 10. If the result is below 1, skip the outfit. A battle pass outfit scores ~5. A limited SSR outfit for your main operative scores ~3. A recolor for a side operative scores ~0.5.

Free Outfits You Might Miss: (1) New player 7-day login — gives a free outfit for your chosen starter operative. Pick carefully. (2) Companion affinity milestones — each operative gives a free outfit at affinity 50. Leveling affinity is free (gifts from gameplay). (3) Anniversary login — always gives a free outfit. Log in every day during anniversary to claim it. (4) Limited event shops — some events offer a free outfit as the top reward. Prioritize this over protocores if you care about cosmetics. (5) Pre-registration / code rewards — check the active codes section. Outfits are occasionally given through special codes. Total free outfits per year: approximately 6-8. Build your collection around these before spending money.

Rotation Schedule & Planning

Outfit banners follow a predictable schedule: New character banners: Always include a new outfit for the rate-up operative. This is the best time to buy for your main. Holiday rotations: Valentine's (February), Summer (July-August), Anniversary (varies), Christmas (December). Holiday outfits are the most elaborate. Battle pass season: Resets every 6 weeks. Check the upcoming outfit preview before buying the pass. Some seasons have weaker outfits — skip and save for the next season. Rerun banners: Older outfits return during dead periods between new events. If you missed an outfit, this is your second chance. Reruns are usually 6-12 months after the original release. Planning tip: Maintain a "outfit fund" of ~$30 per month if you are a light spender. This covers one battle pass ($10) plus one limited item ($20) per month. Skip months with weak offerings — the fund rolls over.

Collection Tracker Tips

Keep a record of which outfits you own per operative. The game does not have a good collection tracker. Use a simple spreadsheet: columns = operative, outfit name, date acquired, cost, rating (1-5). This helps you avoid buying duplicates and makes it easy to see which operative has the fewest outfits. Most players accidentally buy 2-3 outfits for one operative while neglecting others. A tracker prevents this imbalance.

Summary: Battle pass outfits are best value (S-Tier). Limited SSR outfits are A-Tier for your main operative. Recolors are B-Tier. Claim all free outfits. Track your collection in a spreadsheet. Budget $30/month as a light spender. Skip outfits you will use less than 10 times. Never buy an outfit for an operative you do not use.
