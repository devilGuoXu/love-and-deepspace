Outfit Purchase Priority

Not all outfits are equal. Some offer better value for photography, others are must-haves for character affinity. This guide ranks every outfit category so you spend gems and real money wisely.

Battle Pass vs Shop Pricing

Value Comparison: Battle Pass outfits cost approximately 1,200–1,500 paid currency and include additional resources (pulls, dust, stamina). Shop outfits cost 1,800–2,400 paid currency with no additional resources. The Battle Pass is always better value per dollar — but you must complete the pass to unlock the outfit. Shop outfits are instant unlocks but cost more.

BATTLE PASS VALUE BREAKDOWN
A typical Battle Pass (1,200 paid currency): outfit (value 2,100), 10 pull tickets (value 1,800), 200 stamina items (value 400), 50K dust (value 500), and various upgrade materials. Total value: ~4,800 paid currency equivalent. The outfit accounts for ~44% of the pass value. If you want the outfit AND the resources, the BP is a no-brainer. If you only want the outfit, you are still saving 900 currency compared to the shop.
SHOP OUTFIT COST BREAKDOWN
Shop outfits are 1,800–2,400 paid currency with no bundled resources. The higher cost is justified by: (1) instant unlock (no BP grind), (2) no time limit (available indefinitely), (3) often includes exclusive accessories or recolors that the BP version does not have. Shop outfits are better for players who do not have time to complete the BP grind but still want premium outfits.

Spending Rule: If you can complete the Battle Pass (reach level 60 in 30 days), always buy the BP over shop outfits. The BP gives you the outfit + resources that would cost 2x separately. If you cannot complete the BP (busy schedule, casual player), shop outfits are acceptable — you pay premium for convenience. Never buy both the BP outfit and the shop version of the same outfit skin — they are the same outfit with different colors.

S-Tier — Must Buy Outfits

These outfits are universally useful, look great on all characters, and perform well in all photography contexts. If you buy only one outfit per character, buy from this tier.

S-TIER — CLASSIC SUIT
Every character — 2,400 shop — Formal, elegant, timeless
The classic suit (black or dark navy) is the most versatile outfit in the game. It works for formal events, romantic dates, and elegant portraits. The clean lines create a strong silhouette. The dark color creates a natural contrast against light backgrounds. The suit has minimal patterns, so it does not distract from the character's face. This is the #1 recommended first purchase for any character.
S-TIER — CASUAL KNITWEAR
Every character — BP tier 30 — Cozy, approachable, warm
The casual knitwear (crewneck sweater or cardigan) is the best casual outfit. It makes characters look approachable and warm. The knit texture adds visual interest without being distracting. Works well in home base photography (cozy indoor aesthetic). The neutral colors (cream, grey, navy) match any background. This outfit is typically available through Battle Pass, making it excellent value.
S-TIER — SIGNATURE COLOR
Per character — 1,800 shop — Represents character identity
Each character has a signature color outfit: Xavier (light blue/white), Zayne (ice blue/silver), Rafayel (crimson/gold), Sylus (dark red/black). These outfits feature character-specific design elements that reference their lore. The signature outfit is the best choice for character identity shots — photos that say "this is Xavier" not "this is a character wearing a suit." These outfits often have unique lighting interactions (reflective elements, glowing accents).

S-Tier Purchase Order: (1) Classic Suit (versatility), (2) Signature Color (character identity), (3) Casual Knitwear (BP value). If you only buy 3 outfits total, this is the order. The Classic Suit covers formal needs, Signature covers character representation, Knitwear covers casual photography.

A-Tier — Great Value

These outfits are excellent for specific photography contexts or character concepts. They are not as versatile as S-Tier but outperform S-Tier in their specific niche.

LEATHER JACKET
Edgy, cool — Best for Sylus — BP tier 40 — Urban aesthetic
The leather jacket outfit is perfect for edgy, urban photography. The leather texture reflects light differently than fabric — create dramatic highlight/shadow contrasts. Best for nighttime city shots. Sylus's leather jacket has the most detailed texture (stitching, zippers, patches).
TRADITIONAL GARB
Cultural, elegant — Event limited — 2,400 shop — Ceremonial
Traditional garb outfits (usually tied to seasonal events) are stunning for ceremonial and cultural portraits. The intricate patterns and layered fabric create visual complexity. These outfits photograph beautifully in golden hour light. Limited availability — buy if you attend the event and have spare currency.
ATHLETIC WEAR
Active, dynamic — BP tier 50 — Action photography
Athletic wear is specialized for action photography. The fitted silhouette shows the character's physique. Best for battle pose and action shots. The moisture-wicking fabric texture (visible in close-ups) adds realism. Not suitable for formal or romantic photography contexts.

A-Tier Value Assessment: These outfits are worth buying if you specialize in a specific photography style. A player who only does formal portraits can skip the Leather Jacket. A player who loves urban night photography will use the Leather Jacket more than the Classic Suit. Consider your photography style before purchasing A-Tier outfits. These are not "must buy" but are "must buy if you shoot this style."

"My leather jacket Xavier shots got 3x more likes than my suit Xavier shots. The community craves variety — every character in a suit is boring. Give them something unexpected."

— Community Photographer, Style Showcase Winner

B-Tier — Situational

B-Tier outfits are either too niche, too similar to existing outfits, or too expensive for what they offer. Skip these unless you have excess currency or are a completionist.

Recolor variantsSimilar to existing outfit. Only buy if you love the specific color. Skip if you have the original.
Holiday specialsUsed once per year. Cute but limited use. Only buy if you celebrate that holiday.
Collaboration outfitsNever return after collaboration ends. Buy only if you love the collab IP.
Basic free outfitsDo not spend currency on these. Unlocked through gameplay or starter packs.

Danger: Do NOT buy outfit recolor variants unless you have already purchased all S-Tier and A-Tier outfits for that character. A recolor of an outfit you already own adds minimal photography value. The 1,800–2,400 currency is better spent on a different outfit category or saved for future releases. Recolors are collector bait — resist the FOMO.

Completionist strategy: If your goal is to collect every outfit for your main character, buy in this order: S-Tier → A-Tier → B-Tier recolor variants → B-Tier holiday specials → B-Tier collab. This order maximizes photography versatility per currency spent. A full collection of 20+ outfits for one character costs approximately 40,000–50,000 paid currency — plan your budget accordingly.

F2P & Low Spender Strategy

F2P players can earn approximately 2–3 premium outfits per year through accumulated free currency (daily logins, events, achievements). Low spenders (monthly card only) can earn 5–7 per year. Spend these limited resources wisely.

1
Save for Signature Color outfit (1,800)
This is your first purchase. The Signature Color outfit represents your main character's identity and is the most recognizable. It costs 1,800 shop currency — achievable with 2–3 months of free currency saving.
2
Save for Classic Suit (2,400)
Your second purchase. The Classic Suit covers all formal photography needs. Takes 3–4 months to save. This outfit will be in your rotation for 80% of your shoots.
3
Consider Battle Pass (1,200)
If you can save 1,200 paid currency (or spend $10), the BP gives you an outfit + tons of resources. Three BPs per year = 3 outfits + massive resources. The BP is the best value for low spenders.
4
Skip event limited outfits
Event limited outfits are tempting but expensive for F2P. The FOMO is designed to make you spend. Remind yourself: the outfit will return on a rerun. Only buy event outfits if you have spare currency after your priority purchases.
5
Focus on one character
A F2P player cannot outfit all four characters. Choose one main character and outfit them well. Three outfits for one character is better than one outfit each for three characters. Three outfits give you variety; one outfit per character limits every shoot.

F2P Monthly Currency Income: Daily login: 180. Weekly missions: 300. Event rewards: 400–800. Abyss cycle: 200–400. Total: approximately 1,200–1,700 free currency per month. A 1,800 shop outfit requires 1–2 months of saving. A 2,400 shop outfit requires 1.5–3 months. Do not spend currency impulsively — set a target outfit and save until you reach it.

Rerun & Limited Priority

Outfits that you missed on their initial release will return on reruns approximately 6–12 months later. Rerun pricing is typically the same as the original. Limited collaboration outfits are the exception — they may never return.

RERUN PRIORITY
  • Classic Suit recolor — if you missed it, buy on rerun
  • Signature Color — always worth waiting for rerun
  • Battle Pass outfits — may return as shop items later
  • Event outfits from last year — prioritize over current event outfits
SKIP ON RERUN
  • Recolor variants — still not worth it on rerun
  • Holiday specials — outdated design, save for new outfits
  • Collaboration outfits — buy original run or accept the loss
  • Old BP outfits — check if a better version released since

Limited collaboration outfits deserve special mention. Collab outfits are the rarest in the game — they have never rerun in any server. If a collab outfit features an IP you love, buy it during the event window. The cost is typically higher (3,000–4,000 currency) but the exclusivity is unmatched. The community treats collab outfits as prestige items — owning one signals that you were playing during that era.

Rerun prediction: Based on the game's update cycle, outfits typically rerun during the same season the following year. Summer outfits rerun in summer. Christmas outfits rerun in December. Anniversary outfits rerun during anniversary. If you missed an outfit, mark your calendar for its expected rerun window and start saving currency 2 months in advance. Outfits that have not rerun after 18 months are likely "vaulted" — accept that you may never get them and move on.

Summary: Battle Pass outfits are better value than shop (1,200 BP vs 1,800-2,400 shop, plus resources). S-Tier must-buys: Classic Suit (versatile formal), Signature Color (character identity), Casual Knitwear (BP value). A-Tier niche picks: Leather Jacket (urban), Traditional Garb (cultural), Athletic Wear (action). Skip recolor variants and holiday specials. F2P: 2-3 outfits per year — focus on one character. Reruns happen 6-12 months later except collab outfits. Save currency 2 months before expected rerun. Three well-chosen outfits per character enable more photography variety than one each for all four.
