Senior Hunter Contest Scoring

How scoring works, how to assign teams, and how to exploit the weekly buff rotation for maximum ranks.

ENTRY_#0752026.07.02SHC

How to Read This: Start with the scoring mechanics section to understand how points work. Then use the team assignment section to plan your weekly runs. The advanced section is for players already scoring in the top 10%.

Scoring Mechanics

Senior Hunter Contest (SHC) scores you on three criteria per stage: Clear Time (Primary): Faster clears = higher scores. Each second under the time limit adds to your score. This is the most important factor — a 30-second clear beats a 60-second clear even if you took more damage. Damage Taken (Secondary): Less damage = bonus points. Perfect dodges matter here. Getting hit costs you score but not nearly as much as slow clears. Ardent Oath Usage (Tertiary): Using Oath gives a small bonus. Missing Oath usage does not significantly penalize you. Total score: Sum of all three stages. Rankings are percentile-based: top 1% = Star Hunter, top 5% = Elite, top 15% = Veteran, top 30% = Hunter, below that = Recruit.

Team Assignment Strategy

SHC has 3 stages. You must assign 3 operatives per stage (9 total, no reuse). The optimal assignment: Stage 1 (Easiest): Your third-best team. Clear within 60-90 seconds. Do not waste your best operatives here. Stage 2 (Medium): Your second-best team. Clear within 45-60 seconds. This stage often has specific elemental requirements. Stage 3 (Hardest): Your best team. Clear within 30-45 seconds. This is where the score difference happens. Rule of thumb: If Stage 1 takes more than 90 seconds, your team quality is too low for this week's rotation. Farm more gear and try again next week. Do not waste 30 attempts brute-forcing a stage you are not geared for.

Weekly Buff Analysis

Each week SHC has a rotating buff that changes the meta. Elemental Boost Weeks: One element gets +50% damage. Build all three teams around that element if possible. Oath Boost Weeks: Ardent Oath builds faster or does more damage. Prioritize Oath frequency over sustained DPS. Resonance Boost Weeks: Resonance skills have reduced cooldown or increased damage. Bring your best resonance-focused memories. Defense/Utility Weeks: Shields/healing are enhanced. These weeks favor survivability — but do not sacrifice DPS entirely. Shield builds that also deal damage (like Zayne's Engraved set) shine here. Check the weekly buff before assigning teams. A good weekly buff can make up for a 20% gear gap.

Score Thresholds: The exact score needed for Star Hunter (top 1%) varies by server and week, but here are general targets: Stage 1: 35,000+ points (90 sec clear, minimal damage). Stage 2: 40,000+ points (60 sec clear). Stage 3: 45,000+ points (30 sec clear, Oath used). Total: 120,000+. If you are below 100,000 total, focus on farming gear rather than retrying. Gear is the bottleneck 90% of the time. The remaining 10% is team assignment optimization — moving operatives around can gain 5,000-10,000 points without any gear change.

Advanced Rotation Exploitation

Top players track the rotation schedule and prepare teams in advance. The rotation cycles through 4 buff types over 4 weeks. Week 1: Elemental boost. Build mono-element teams. Week 2: Oath boost. Equip Oath-generating protocores. Week 3: Resonance boost. Stack resonance damage. Week 4: Utility boost. Bring hybrid shield/DPS builds. By knowing next week's rotation, you can pre-farm protocores and pre-level memories. This preparation alone is worth 5-8% more score. Also: save your highest-scoring weekly attempt as a screenshot for reference — comparing how different team assignments scored helps optimize future weeks.

Summary: Clear time is king — faster > cleaner. Assign Stage 3 first with your best team, then Stage 2, then Stage 1. Check the weekly buff and build around it. Target 120,000+ total for Star Hunter. Gear is the bottleneck 90% of the time. Track rotations for pre-farming advantage.
