New Player Guide

Welcome to the Deepspace. Here's how to go from zero to Abyss-ready without wasting a single resource.

ENTRY_#012 2026.06.29 NEW_PLAYER

First Thing You Need to Know: Love and Deepspace is a marathon, not a sprint. The game deliberately gates your progression through stamina and daily limits. The players who succeed are the ones who optimize their daily routine and never waste stamina. This guide covers everything a new player needs — from your first login to your first Abyss clear.

Phase 1: The First Week (Level 1-30)

Your first week is about unlocking systems and building your foundation. Don't worry about team optimization yet — just follow this checklist:

Day 1-2 — Main Story Rush

Goal: Clear Main Story Chapter 1-4 to unlock all core systems (Stellactrum, Memories, Protocores).

Stamina: Spend all stamina on Main Story stages. Do not farm materials yet — story stages give better first-time rewards.

Gacha: Do the Beginner Banner (20-pull guaranteed SSR). Do NOT spend gems on standard banner yet.

Team: Use whoever the story gives you. Your first SSR determines your early direction.

Day 3-5 — Unlock Abyss Prep

Goal: Reach Account Level 15. Unlock the Simulation Training and Bounty missions.

Stamina: Switch to Bounty missions for memory leveling materials. You'll need thousands of XP scrolls.

Memories: Level your SSR to 40 and your SRs to 30. Don't go higher yet — resources are scarce.

Tip: Join a Crew (guild) as soon as it unlocks. Crew coins are a steady source of limited resources.

Day 6-7 — First Team Assembly

Goal: Build your first functional team. You need 2 operatives with matching Stellactrum.

Team: Your SSR + whatever support fills the Stellactrum slots. 4/6 match is acceptable at this stage.

Stamina: Split between Bounty missions (60%) and Story (40%).

Checkpoint: By day 7, you should have Account Level 20+, one SSR at level 50, and the first 3 Abyss floors cleared.

Phase 2: Mid Game (Level 30-50)

This is where most new players get stuck. The game's difficulty spikes sharply around level 35. Here's how to push through:

Target Account Level 40-50
Memory Levels SSR 60 / SR 40-50
Stellactrum Match 5/6 minimum on main team
Abyss Floor Target Clear Floor 6
1
Focus One Team Only

Do NOT spread resources to a second team yet. Your first team needs to clear Floor 6 before you start building a second. One strong team is better than two weak teams.

2
Farm Protocores After Level 40

Protocore farming is inefficient before level 40 because the drop rates for gold protocores are low. Wait until you can farm the highest difficulty stage. In the meantime, use the 3-star and 4-star protocores the game gives you.

3
Do Your Dailies Every Day

Daily missions are the single most important source of account XP and gems. Missing a day sets you back significantly. The game is designed around steady daily progress — don't skip.

4
Save Gems for Limited Banners

Standard banner SSR pool is permanent. Limited banners have exclusive memories that may not return for months. As a new player, pull on a limited banner only if you have enough for the 140-pit (140 pulls = 22,400 gems). Partial pity is wasted pity.

Phase 3: Abyss Preparation (Level 50+)

Once you hit level 50, it's time to start preparing for the Abyss (Floors 7-12). This requires a mindset shift from single-team to multi-team thinking.

The 6-Operative Wall: Abyss Floors 7+ require 2 teams (4 operatives), and Floors 9+ require 3 teams (6 operatives). Start building your second operative at level 50. Don't wait until you "feel ready" — by then it's too late.

At level 50, your priority order should be:

Priority 1: Get your main DPS to level 70 with 6/6 Stellactrum.

Priority 2: Get a second DPS to level 60 with 5/6 Stellactrum.

Priority 3: Level two supports to 50 with 4/6 Stellactrum each.

Priority 4: Fill remaining two slots with any operatives at level 40+.

Rule of Thumb: Your account is Abyss-ready when you have 6 operatives at level 50+, with at least 4/6 Stellactrum on each team. You don't need perfect gear — you need adequate gear on 6 characters instead of perfect gear on 2.

Resource Management Guide

Resources are tight in the early game. Here's exactly what to spend and what to save:

Resource Spend On Save For
Stamina Bounty missions (XP) Protocore domain (until 40)
Gems Limited banner (140 pulls) Stamina refills (low value)
Crew Coins SSR memory shards Cosmetics (late game)
Wish Tickets (Blue) Standard banner Do not convert to limited
Wish Tickets (Gold) Limited banner only Standard banner (waste)

Which Operative to Choose First?

Your first SSR (from the Beginner Banner) shapes your early game. Here's how to evaluate your options:

XAVIER
Great for new players. His Light element is never resisted by early bosses. Simple burst rotation, forgiving mechanics. Scales well into end game.
SYLUS
High skill ceiling. Excellent in end-game Abyss. Weak early game because his execute mechanic requires enemy HP management. Not recommended as first pick unless you're experienced.
ZAYNE
Best support for new players. Freeze mechanics make early content much easier. Can serve as main DPS early and transition to support later. Safe pick.
RAFAYEL
Strong AoE, weak single target. Excellent for story stages and Bounty missions (clear speed). Struggles against single-target Abyss bosses. Requires a strong single-target partner.

Daily Routine (Level 30+)

Here's the optimized daily loop that takes about 20-30 minutes:

① Daily Missions (5 min): Claim all daily missions. This gives ~400 account XP and 50 gems per day.
② Stamina Dump (10 min): Spend stamina on Bounty missions (memory XP) or Protocore domain (if level 40+). Never let stamina cap.
③ Crew Donation (2 min): Donate to crew for coins. Buy SSR shards daily.
④ Simulation Training (5 min): Do your simulation runs for skill level materials. This is a permanent bottleneck.
⑤ Bounty/Event Check (3 min): Check if any limited events are active. Events are the best source of rare resources.

Common New Player Mistakes

Mistake #1: Spending gems on the standard banner. Standard banner SSRs are permanent. You will get them eventually through free tickets and off-rate pity. Gems are for limited banners only. Every gem spent on standard is a gem that could have been a limited SSR.

Mistake #2: Leveling every memory equally. You need 6 leveled memories per operative across 6 operatives = 36 memories total. That's 36 sets of XP scrolls. If you level 40 memories to 30 each, you've wasted thousands of XP. Only level memories you're actively using.

Mistake #3: Ignoring 4-star memories. "It's not SSR so it's trash" is the most common new player trap. 4-star memories with the right element and set bonus are often better than 5-star memories with mismatched elements. A 4-star at level 60 outperforms a 5-star at level 30 in every scenario.

Mistake #4: Neglecting supports. New players focus entirely on their main DPS and leave supports at level 1. A level 1 support dies in one hit and contributes zero resonance or utility. Get all operatives to at least level 40 before pushing your main DPS beyond 60.

Mistake #5: Pulling on every banner. New banners are tempting, but spreading pulls across 3 banners leaves you with 0 pity on each. Pick one banner, commit to the 140-pull pity, and skip everything else until you have enough saved for the next guarantee.

The Bottom Line: Your first month in Love and Deepspace determines your entire account trajectory. Follow the phases: first week = story unlock, weeks 2-3 = first team assembly, week 4+ = Abyss prep. Never let stamina cap. Never spend gems on standard. Keep every operative above level 40. And when in doubt, remember: depth beats peak. A balanced roster of 6 operatives will carry you further than a single maxed-out one.
