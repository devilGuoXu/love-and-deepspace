Burn Mechanic Deep Dive

Rafayel's burn mechanic is the most complex damage-over-time system in the game and understanding it is essential for optimizing his build. Each burn tick rolls CRIT independently — this means CRIT Rate affects the probability that any given tick deals double damage, while CRIT DMG determines how much extra damage a CRIT tick deals. The math favors CRIT Rate heavily: at 75% CRIT Rate, each burn tick has a 75% chance to CRIT. Since the Inferno Blaze 4-piece gives burn ticks +35% CRIT chance, your effective CRIT Rate for burn ticks is your displayed rate plus 35%.

The burn spread mechanic triggers when a burn tick CRITs. The CRITting tick has a 50% chance to spread burn to one nearby enemy within 5 meters. This means Rafayel's AoE damage scales quadratically with enemy density — each additional enemy within spread range increases the chance of further spread. In practical terms, Rafayel against a single target deals approximately 60% of his potential DPS. Against three or more targets, he deals 100%+ of his potential DPS because the burn spread chain reaction supplements his direct damage. This makes Rafayel the strongest operative for add-heavy floors by a significant margin.

Farming Priority for Rafayel Build

Building Rafayel requires prioritizing CRIT Rate protocores above all other pieces. The Slot 2 protocore should have CRIT Rate as the main stat — do not accept any alternative for this slot. Slot 4 should be Fire DMG if available, with ATK% as a backup. Slot 5 should be ATK%. The substat priority across all slots is CRIT Rate > ATK% > CRIT DMG > Energy Recharge. Any protocore that rolls CRIT Rate as a substat is worth keeping for Rafayel even if the main stat is suboptimal — CRIT Rate substats are rare and valuable enough to justify using a non-ideal main stat temporarily.

The Inferno Blaze set requires four pieces to activate the burn CRIT passive. Prioritize completing this set over improving individual piece quality. A full Inferno Blaze 4-piece with average substats outperforms a 2-piece Inferno Blaze plus 2-piece ATK set with excellent substats. The 4-piece bonus is transformative for Rafayel's playstyle, changing him from a single-target burn applicator to an AoE burn spread machine. Do not settle for a partial set — farm Inferno Blaze pieces until the set is complete even if it takes multiple weeks of dedicated domain farming.

Rafayel's burn spread range is 5 meters. Group enemies with positioning or crowd control before applying burn. A well-grouped group of 4 takes 4 times the burn damage of a single target.

Key Insight: Rafayel's burn ticks benefit from CRIT differently than normal damage. Each tick has its own CRIT roll. At 75% CRIT rate, you have a 94% chance of at least one CRIT burn tick per skill. This means CRIT Rate is worth more for Rafayel than CRIT DMG — prioritize rate until 75%.

Summary: CRIT Rate is Rafayel's most important stat. 75% breakpoint enables consistent burn CRITs. Inferno Blaze 4-pc is mandatory for AoE fights. Prioritize CRIT Rate > ATK% > CRIT DMG. Rafayel burns brightest with high crit rate.
