Settings Optimization Guide

Finding the right settings balance can transform your Deepspace experience. This guide covers optimal settings for performance, visuals, controls, and gameplay.

ENTRY_#0832026.07.02SETTINGS

Why Settings Matter: Deepspace is a visually demanding game. Poor settings can cause lag, overheating, or missed dodge timings. Optimized settings ensure smooth gameplay and the best visual experience your device can handle.

Graphics Settings

Overall Quality Presets: Low: Best for budget phones (2019-2020 models). Medium: Good balance for mid-range devices. High: For flagship phones (2021+). Ultra/Extreme: For high-end phones and PC emulators. Recommended settings by device tier: Budget (Snapdragon 6xx/7xx, MediaTek G-series): Low preset, disable shadows, 30 FPS target. Mid-Range (Snapdragon 8xx, Dimensity 8xxx): Medium preset, enable shadows at medium, 45-60 FPS target. Flagship (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2+, A16+): High or Ultra preset, enable all effects, 60 FPS target. PC Emulator: Extreme preset, max resolution, 60+ FPS. Individual Settings: Resolution: Native resolution is ideal but 0.8x render scale saves significant battery with minimal visual loss. Frame Rate: 60 FPS is critical for combat timing. 30 FPS makes perfect dodging significantly harder. Shadows: Medium is the sweet spot. High shadows drain battery for minimal visual improvement. Anti-Aliasing: TAA is best. FXAA is lighter but blurrier. Post-Processing: Bloom and Depth of Field are cosmetic. Disable them for better combat clarity. Particle Effects: Keep at medium or higher during events — you want to see boss attack telegraphs.

Control Settings

Camera Sensitivity: Default 100% is good for most players. Increase to 120-150% if you play on a tablet (larger screen needs faster movement). Decrease to 70-80% if you use a small phone. Camera Distance: Set to maximum. A farther camera gives better battlefield awareness. The downside is smaller character models — worth the trade-off. Auto-Aim: Enable for general content. Disable for boss fights where you need to target specific body parts. Skill Button Layout: Default layout works for most. Consider enlarging the dodge button (accessibility settings) if you find yourself missing dodge timings. Tap vs Joystick: Tapping skills is more precise for targeting. Joystick mode is better for movement-heavy operatives (Rafayel). Experiment in the training room. Controller Support: If playing on PC emulator, use a controller. The game supports Bluetooth controllers on mobile. Recommended: Xbox or PlayStation controllers for best compatibility. Controller layout: Left stick movement, right stick camera, triggers for skills, face buttons for dodge and resonance.

Audio Settings

Voice Language: Chinese voice acting is the original and highest quality. Japanese voice acting has strong performances. Korean voice acting is excellent for certain characters. English voice acting is good but less emotive overall. Personal preference is the deciding factor. Voice Volume: 100% for story content. The voice acting adds significant emotional weight. Music Volume: 70-100%. Deepspace's soundtrack is exceptional. Do not mute it. SFX Volume: 80-100%. Combat sound effects provide important audio cues for enemy attacks. ASMR Mode (Secret Times): Enable "Headphone Optimization" for Secret Times tracks. This enables binaural audio effects that significantly enhance immersion. Audio Balance for Combat: If you struggle with boss timings, try: SFX 100%, Music 50%, Voice 70%. This emphasizes attack audio cues (roars, wind-ups) while keeping the atmosphere.

Battery Optimization: Deepspace is a battery-intensive game. To extend play sessions: (1) Enable Battery Saver mode in the game settings (reduces frame rate to 30 during non-combat). (2) Lower render scale to 0.8x. (3) Disable Ambient Occlusion and Bloom. (4) Set shadow quality to low. (5) Reduce in-game brightness (separate from device brightness). (6) Close background apps. (7) Play while charging if possible (but watch for overheating). (8) On iPhone: enable Low Power Mode before launching the game. (9) On Android: enable Game Booster or equivalent. These settings can extend battery life by 40-60% during gameplay. For long sessions (1+ hours), use these battery settings. For short sessions or photography, switch to high-quality settings.

Accessibility Settings

Deepspace includes several accessibility features: Button Size: Increase skill button and dodge button sizes by 20-50%. Color Blind Mode: Adjusts UI colors for protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia. Subtitle Size: Three sizes available. Large subtitles are recommended for mobile play. Screen Shake: Reduce or disable if you experience motion sickness during combat. Auto-Play in Story: Enables automatic dialogue advancement. Useful if you have difficulty tapping. High Contrast Mode: Makes enemy attack indicators more visible. Notification Settings: Customize which notifications appear (banners, events, character messages). Recommended: Enable all character message notifications. Disable promotional notification spam. Privacy Settings: Review data sharing preferences. Disable analytics sharing if concerned about privacy. Enable cloud save to protect your account.

Network Settings

Download Resources: Deepspace allows downloading all resources in advance for smoother gameplay. Recommended initial download: All character voices (2-3 GB). All memory art assets (1-2 GB). All event resources (500 MB-1 GB per event). Download over Wi-Fi Only: Enable to avoid mobile data charges. Pre-download Patches: Enable automatic pre-download in settings. This downloads patch data before maintenance ends, so you can play immediately after maintenance. Repair Client: If experiencing crashes or graphical glitches, use the Repair Client option in settings. This verifies all game files and redownloads corrupted ones. Does not affect account data.

Summary: Graphics: Match quality to your device tier. 60 FPS is essential for combat. Medium shadows are the sweet spot. Controls: Max camera distance, enable auto-aim, consider controller on PC. Audio: Chinese voices for authenticity, boost SFX for combat cues. Battery: Enable battery saver, reduce render scale during long sessions. Accessibility: Adjust button size, enable color blind mode if needed, use large subtitles. Network: Pre-download resources on Wi-Fi, enable pre-download for patches. The right settings can significantly improve your gameplay experience and device longevity.
