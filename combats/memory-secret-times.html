MEMORY ARCHIVE — SECRET TIMES

Secret Times Guide

Secret Times is a sleep-aid audio feature with original stories and ASMR-style narration from each operative. This guide covers how to access and unlock all tracks.

ENTRY_#MEM_082026.07.02SECRET TIMES

What Is Secret Times: Secret Times is a collection of audio-only stories designed to help you relax and fall asleep. Each track features one of the four operatives speaking directly to you in a calm, intimate setting. The tracks are fully voiced and range from 15 to 45 minutes in length. Think of it as an audio drama + ASMR hybrid.

How to Access Secret Times

Secret Times is accessed from the Destiny Cafe main menu. Look for the moon icon in the top-right corner. The menu shows available tracks organized by operative. Each operative has multiple tracks that unlock progressively. Requirements to unlock each track: Track 1 — Affinity 15, any SSR memory equipped. Track 2 — Affinity 35, Bond Memory 1 completed. Track 3 — Affinity 60, Bond Memory 2 completed. Track 4 — Affinity 90, any limited SSR memory. Track 5 — Affinity 130, all bond memories completed. Secret Times tracks can be played in the background (audio only, screen off) or with a looping animation of the operative in a sleep setting. The background playback feature is excellent for actual sleep use — you can start a track, turn off your screen, and let the audio play through.

Track Themes & Highlights

Xavier Track 1 — "Stargazing": Xavier takes you to a quiet observatory and describes constellations. Soft ambient music, slow delivery. 25 minutes. Perfect for unwinding after a long day. Xavier Track 3 — "Rainy Night": The sound of rain, a warm room, and Xavier reading poetry. Many players consider this the most relaxing track in the game. Zayne Track 2 — "Winter Cabin": Zayne describes a snowed-in cabin with a fireplace. ASMR elements: crackling fire, soft snowfall, tea being poured. 30 minutes. Zayne Track 4 — "Frozen Lake": Walking on a frozen lake under starlight. Minimal music, mostly ambient sounds and Zayne's calm voice. Rafayel Track 1 — "Bonfire": Rafayel by a campfire, telling a folktale. Includes fire crackling ASMR. 20 minutes. Rafayel Track 5 — "Ocean Waves": Rafayel on a beach at night. Waves, gentle breeze, and a personal story. The longest track at 45 minutes. Sylus Track 2 — "Library": Sylus reading from an old book in a quiet library. Page-turning ASMR, soft lamp hum. 22 minutes. Sylus Track 4 — "Rooftop at Dusk": City sounds from a high rooftop, Sylus reflecting on his past. The most melancholic track.

Practical Tips: Secret Times tracks respect your device's sleep timer. Use your phone's built-in sleep timer to auto-stop playback. For the best experience: use headphones, set your device volume to 40-50%, and lie down in a dark room. The tracks are designed for this exact scenario. Some players unlock all tracks just for collection — but the real value is using them as intended. The Secret Times feature is one of the most unique aspects of Love and Deepspace, and no other gacha game offers anything comparable in quality and length.

Secret Times Track List by Affinity

Tier 1 (Affinity 15): Track 1 per operative. Average length: 22 minutes. Theme: Introduction — daily life scenarios. Tier 2 (Affinity 35): Track 2. Length: 25 minutes. Theme: Nature — outdoor settings. Tier 3 (Affinity 60): Track 3. Length: 28 minutes. Theme: Intimate — personal stories. Tier 4 (Affinity 90): Track 4. Length: 32 minutes. Theme: Emotional — deeper connection. Tier 5 (Affinity 130): Track 5. Length: 35-45 minutes. Theme: Culmination — the most personal story. Each track builds on the previous one, creating a coherent narrative arc across all five tracks for each operative. Reading bond memories in between unlocks enhances the experience — the tracks reference events from the bonds.

Hidden Details

Secret Times tracks have subtle details that reward attentive listeners. Background ambience changes based on the story progression. Character voices shift slightly depending on the emotional tone — the voice actors deliver noticeably different performances compared to the main story. Some tracks contain soft background music that is exclusive to Secret Times and not available anywhere else in the game. Easter egg: In Xavier's Rainy Night track, if you listen carefully during the middle section, you can hear a faint melody from the main menu theme reworked as a lullaby. Similar musical callbacks exist in other tracks. Finding them is part of the charm.

Summary: Secret Times is a sleep-aid audio feature with 5 tracks per operative (20 total). Tracks unlock at Affinity 15, 35, 60, 90, and 130. Use with headphones and screen off for best experience. Tracks are 20-45 minutes long with ASMR elements and original stories. Track quality increases with tier — Tier 4 and 5 are the best. The feature is unique to Deepspace and well worth the affinity investment. Listen in order and read bond memories between tracks for maximum narrative impact.
