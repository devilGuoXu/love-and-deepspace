What Are Secret Times

Secret Times is a content category in Love and Deepspace that offers premium audio dramas featuring each operative. These are not simple voice lines — they are full-length audio productions with professional voice acting, ambient soundscapes, original background music, and narrative arcs. Each Secret Times episode runs 15-30 minutes and explores intimate moments between the Hunter and the operative that wouldn't fit into the main story's pacing.

The audio format allows for more personal, introspective storytelling. Without the need for character models or environments, the writers can explore quieter moments — late-night conversations, confessions, memories of the past. The voice actors deliver career-best performances in these episodes, with the intimacy of the audio-only format lending itself to more nuanced emotional delivery.

Unlock Requirements

Secret Times episodes are unlocked through a combination of bond level and memory collection milestones. Typically, each episode requires: bond level 6+ with the relevant operative, collection of at least 1 specific SSR or 3 specific SR memories for that operative, and completion of the associated main story chapter. The exact requirements vary per episode but follow this general pattern.

New Secret Times episodes are added alongside major story updates and limited banner events. When a new limited SSR is released for an operative, there is often a corresponding Secret Times episode that explores the story behind that memory card. These limited-tied episodes may require the specific SSR memory to be collected, making them aspirational content for dedicated operative fans.

Secret Times Catalog

Xavier — "Starlight Confession"
A quiet night on the observation deck. Xavier reflects on his journey through the stars and what brought him to this moment. The audio features subtle cosmic ambient sounds — distant stars, the hum of the station — as Xavier shares his fears and hopes. Required: Bond 6 with Xavier, Stellar Contract SSR. Runtime: 22 minutes.
Zayne — "Frost and Flame"
Set in Zayne's medical office after hours. The sound of a crackling fireplace contrasts with Zayne's cool demeanor as he discusses his research into Frost energy and its connection to his past. Required: Bond 6 with Zayne, Permafrost Covenant SSR. Runtime: 25 minutes.
Rafayel — "Tidal Lullaby"
On a beach at sunset, Rafayel hums an old melody while waves lap at the shore. He tells the Hunter about his childhood by the sea, his artistic awakening, and the song his mother used to sing. The production includes a original piano piece performed for this episode. Required: Bond 6 with Rafayel, Inferno Waltz SSR. Runtime: 18 minutes.
Sylus — "Shadow Oath"
In the Onychinus underground, Sylus prepares for a mission while sharing the story of how he built his organization from nothing. The episode features dramatic orchestral scoring and layered sound effects of the bustling underworld. Required: Bond 6 with Sylus, Twilight Oath SSR. Runtime: 27 minutes.

Production Quality

The production value of Secret Times episodes is genuinely impressive for a mobile game. Each episode is recorded with professional studio equipment, the voice actors deliver performances that rival anime dubbing quality, and the sound design team creates immersive ambient environments that transport the listener. The background music is original composition, with each operative's episodes featuring variations on their character themes.

The English voice cast deserves special mention — the localization team has done exceptional work matching the emotional tone of the original Chinese performances while ensuring natural English delivery. For players who appreciate voice acting as an art form, experiencing each operative's episodes in different languages offers different emotional interpretations: the Chinese tracks tend toward gentler, more poetic delivery, while the English tracks emphasize conversational intimacy.

Completion Rewards

Watching (or rather, listening to) Secret Times episodes rewards gems, affinity EXP, and exclusive base items. Each episode grants 50 gems on first completion plus 100 affinity EXP for the featured operative. Additionally, completing all available Secret Times episodes for an operative unlocks a unique base decoration representing a key moment from that operative's episodes.

Tip: Secret Times episodes can be downloaded for offline listening within the game. This is perfect for commutes or times when you don't have stable internet. Each episode is about 30-50 MB in audio-only format. The episodes play in the background even if you lock your phone or switch apps — treat them like podcasts.

Summary: Secret Times audio dramas are premium narrative content that expands character relationships through intimate, voice-acted stories. Unlocked through bond progression and memory collection, these 15-30 minute episodes offer some of the best writing and voice acting in Love and Deepspace. The audio-only format makes them perfect for passive enjoyment. Prioritize unlocking Secret Times for your favorite operative as a bond progression goal.
