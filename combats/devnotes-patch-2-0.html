2.0 Overview & Highlights

2.0 at a Glance: Version 2.0 is the most significant update since launch. The headline features: a new 5th operative (element unannounced at time of writing), Abyss expansion to floor 15 with new rewards, protocore system overhaul with substat locking, and over 50 quality-of-life improvements. The patch represents a shift in the game's design philosophy — from "gacha with combat" to "combat game with gacha."

Version 2.0 was announced during the 1-year anniversary livestream. The developers described it as "the version where Deepspace becomes the game we always wanted it to be." The update addresses the three biggest community complaints: protocore RNG (too punishing), endgame content (not enough), and operative variety (too few). Each of these is addressed in 2.0, but the execution details matter — not all changes are as generous as they first appear.

Pre-2.0 checklist: Before the update drops: (1) Save 140+ pulls for the new operative (guaranteed pity). (2) Save protocore dust — the substat lock system will require dust to use. (3) Do not spend Abyss cycle rewards until after the update — new floors may offer better rewards. (4) Level a diverse roster — floor 13-15 will require specific element coverage. (5) Read the full patch notes — 2.0 has over 50 changes and some may affect your farming priorities.

Abyss Expansion (Floors 13-15)

Abyss floors 13-15 represent a significant difficulty increase over floor 12. The developers stated that floor 15 is designed to be "the hardest content in the game" — requiring optimal builds, perfect rotations, and specific element coverage.

FLOOR 13 — ELEMENT CHECK
Requires 4-element coverage — resist mechanic introduced
Floor 13 introduces a new mechanic: "Elemental Lockout." Each wave has an element that deals 0% damage (not reduced — zero). If your team cannot hit the required element, you cannot damage that wave. This forces players to build all four elements. A team of Xavier (Light) + Zayne (Ice) + Sylus (Dark) covers 3 elements — you need a 4th to handle the remaining element. This is a deliberate design choice to encourage roster diversity.
FLOOR 14 — DPS CHECK
Enrage timer — 3 minutes — estimated 10K+ DPS required
Floor 14 is a pure DPS check with a tight enrage timer. Estimated DPS requirement: 10,000+ per team member. This is a significant jump from floor 12's ~8,000 DPS requirement. Characters must be level 60 with fully enhanced protocores (at least +15 on main stats). The enrage mechanic: boss gains 100% damage reduction and oneshots your team after 3 minutes.
FLOOR 15 — SKILL CHECK
Modified boss mechanics — perfect dodge required — 5 phases
Floor 15 is a skill check with a multi-phase boss that requires perfect execution. The boss has 5 phases with changing attack patterns. Each phase introduces a new mechanic that must be learned through practice. The fight takes 8-12 minutes for a well-built team. This floor is designed to be beaten by skilled players with good builds, not just whale builds with mediocre play.

New Abyss rewards: Floor 13-15 cycles offer premium currency (estimated 600-900 per cycle), new protocore enhancement materials, exclusive nameplate, and floor 15 clear title. The rewards are significant enough that clearing floors 13-15 doubles your monthly Abyss income. Invest in roster depth to access these rewards.

Protocore System Overhaul

The protocore system overhaul is the most impactful change for veteran players. The headline feature is substat locking — the ability to lock a substat so it does not change when enhancing. This dramatically reduces RNG in protocore enhancement.

Substat locking: When enhancing a protocore, you can now lock one substat. The locked substat will not be replaced when a new substat is added at enhancement milestones (+3, +6, +9, +12, +15). This means you can guarantee that your CRIT Rate substat stays while the other slots roll. The cost is 2,000 protocore dust per lock (estimated — subject to change). This makes protocore enhancement significantly more predictable but dust-hungry.

Main stat reroll: A new item (Protocore Reroll Token) allows you to reroll a protocore's main stat. This is a limited resource — obtainable from events and Abyss cycle rewards. Do not use reroll tokens on low-tier protocores — save them for 5-star protocores with good substats but wrong main stat. A 5-star Slot 4 protocore with great substats but HP% main stat is an excellent reroll candidate.

PROTOCORE STRATEGY FOR 2.0
Before 2.0: Save all protocore dust. Do not enhance protocores unless absolutely necessary (new character needing basic builds). The substat lock system will make your dust go much further.
After 2.0: Identify your best protocores with 3/4 good substats. Use substat lock to secure the good ones and reroll the bad ones. Priority: DPS protocores first (CRIT Rate/DMG), then support protocores (ER/HP).
Long-term: The new 6-star protocore tier (if implemented) will likely be more powerful but harder to obtain. Do not invest heavily in 5-star enhancement immediately — wait to see how 6-star availability shakes out.

Quality of Life Changes

Version 2.0 includes over 50 QoL improvements. Here are the most impactful ones:

1
Auto-battle for domains
Finally — auto-clear for domains you have already cleared with 3 stars. You can now sweep domains the same way you sweep resource stages. This saves approximately 30 minutes per day of domain farming time. Only available for domains cleared with all objectives completed.
2
Build loadout system
Save up to 5 build loadouts per character. Each loadout saves memory setup, protocore configuration, and skill preset. Swap between builds with one click. This is a massive quality-of-life improvement for players who switch between Abyss, co-op, and domain builds.
3
Protocore locking & filtering
Enhanced inventory management: mark protocores as "lock" (cannot be salvaged), "favorite" (priority for enhancement), or "junk" (auto-salvage candidate). New filter options: sort by number of useful substats, sort by roll quality, filter by specific main stat + substat combinations.
4
Team presets
Save complete team presets including all three characters' builds. Previously you had to manually assemble your team before each Abyss run. Now you can save "Abyss Team A," "Abyss Team B," and "Co-op Team" and switch between them instantly.
5
Material conversion
Convert excess materials at a 2:1 or 3:1 ratio. Have 5,000 blue exp crystals but need gold? Convert them. This reduces the pain of having unbalanced resources. Conversion rates are not 1:1 (the game takes a small efficiency loss to prevent abuse) but the convenience is worth the tax.

QoL priority: Set up your build loadouts and team presets on day 1 of the patch. The 30 minutes you spend organizing presets will save you hours over the next month. Organize by content: "Abyss Floor 13" loadout, "Domain Farm" loadout, "Co-op" loadout. Name them clearly so you can switch quickly between runs.

Meta Impact & Predictions

Version 2.0 will significantly shift the meta. Based on the announced changes, here are predictions for how the meta will evolve:

ROSTER DIVERSITY BECOMES MANDATORY
Floor 13's Elemental Lockout means you need all 4 elements
Gone are the days of one-tricking Xavier through the entire Abyss. Floor 13 forces you to build at least 3-4 characters across different elements. The new operative expands the roster to 5 — now you have choices. Players who invested in only one element will be locked out of floor 13 rewards. The meta shifts from "vertical investment" (one maxed character) to "horizontal investment" (four decent characters).
PROTOCORE RNG REDUCTION
Substat locking makes good builds more achievable
The gap between average players and min-maxers narrows. Substat locking means everyone can eventually get decent rolls on their key protocores. The dust cost creates a new bottleneck — players with large dust reserves (savers) will have an advantage over players who spent dust impulsively. This benefits F2P players who saved dust over whales who spent freely.
NEW OPERATIVE POWER LEVEL
Likely undertuned at release, buffed 4-6 weeks later
Following the established pattern, the new operative will release at moderate power. Do not expect an immediate meta revolution. The buff will come 4-6 weeks later, adjusting the character to fit the 2.0 meta. Early adopters who learn the character's playstyle before the buff will have an advantage when the buff drops.
