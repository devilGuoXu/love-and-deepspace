Home Decoration Guide

Your base is the one place in the game you can truly call your own. Decorate it well and it becomes a showpiece. Decorate it poorly and it is a warehouse with a bed.

Core System

Core System: The home/base system allows you to place furniture, decorations, and interactive objects in a 3D space. Furniture can be earned through gameplay, purchased from the shop, or obtained from limited events. The home is purely cosmetic — no gameplay benefits — but it is the most personal space in the game.

Despite having no gameplay effect, the home system is one of the most popular features in Deepspace. It is where you spend time between battles, interact with your operatives, and express your personal style. A well-decorated home is a conversation starter on friend lists and social media. Even if you do not care about decoration, placing the basic furniture set takes 5 minutes and unlocks the "home" features in the main menu.

Permanent Sources: Story progression (basic furniture set), achievement rewards (decorative items), shop (gold-purchased furniture, rotates weekly), Battle Pass (1 premium furniture piece per season).

Limited Sources: Event shop (seasonal decorations — summer, Christmas, anniversary), login events (free decorative items), limited-time bundles ($5–10 for themed sets).

Priority: Buy gold furniture from the weekly rotation first — these are cheap (5k–20k gold) and permanent. Event decorations are exclusive but cost event currency that could buy SSR shards. Only buy event decorations after you have secured all SSR shards and guaranteed protocores from the event shop. A decorative item is never worth missing an SSR pull.

Room Layout Principles

Great home design follows four principles. Apply them to every room for a professional look.

PRINCIPLE 1 — ZONING
Divide into functional zones with clear themes
Living area, bedroom, study, display hallway — each zone should have a clear visual theme. Do not mix themes in the same zone (beach items + gothic furniture = visual chaos). If you want multiple themes, use different rooms for different themes.
PRINCIPLE 2 — FOCAL POINT
One large piece per room
Each room needs one focal point — a large furniture piece or wall decoration that draws the eye. Place other furniture around the focal point, not competing with it. A room with two large focal points looks cluttered. A room with none looks empty.
PRINCIPLE 3 — NEGATIVE SPACE
Leave 30–40% of floor space empty
Do not fill every square meter with furniture. Empty space makes a room look larger and more intentional. Cluttered rooms feel smaller and less premium. If you have too much furniture, store half of it and rotate seasonally.
PRINCIPLE 4 — WALL DECOR
Walls are the most underutilized space
Hang paintings, shelves, and lighting fixtures on walls. Empty walls make a room look unfinished. A well-decorated wall can serve as the focal point if you do not have a large furniture piece. Wall lighting is especially effective for creating atmosphere.
Example layout: A living room zone with a large sofa (focal point), rug (zone definition), wall painting (visual interest), and 30% empty floor space for a spacious feel. Plants in corners add life without cluttering the center.

Event-Limited Decorations Worth Getting

Not all event decorations are worth your currency. Here is the tier list based on visual impact, exclusivity, and versatility.

S-TIER (MUST GET)
Anniversary wall tapestry, Christmas tree
Anniversary tapestry is a unique visual that has never returned. Christmas tree is the centerpiece for any holiday zone. These are the most impressive items in the game and will be the centerpiece of your home for years.
A-TIER (GET IF POSSIBLE)
Summer beach set, Valentine's rose display
Beach set creates a distinct tropical zone. Rose display is a romantic focal point. Both are versatile enough to use year-round, not just during their event season.
B-TIER (SKIP)
Single-purpose event items, recolored furniture
A single Mephisto statue only fits one theme. Recolored versions of existing furniture add no variety. Skip these unless you are a completionist with spare currency.

Rule: Event decorations are only worth the currency if you plan to use them long-term. If you are not actively designing a themed zone, skip the event decoration and prioritize SSR shards. A decoration that sits in your inventory unused is wasted currency. Only buy decorations that fit a theme you are currently building.

Flex value: Event-limited decorations from major events (anniversary, Christmas) are worth more "status" than expensive but permanent shop items. A Christmas tree from 2025 is worth more flex than a $20 premium bed that anyone can buy anytime. Display your rarest items prominently near the entrance — this is the first thing visitors (friend list) see when they visit your base.

Pro Tip: Use the "preview all furniture" option in the base editor to see every item you own before placing it. Plan your layout on paper or in a screenshot editor before spending decoration tokens. Moving furniture after placement costs no resources, but having a plan saves hours of trial-and-error. The best bases are planned, not improvised. Check social media for inspiration — the Deepspace community shares incredible base designs weekly.

Theming Guide

Here are three popular theme templates to get you started:

MODERN MINIMALIST
Neutral colors, clean lines, sparse furniture
Uses the base furniture set + a few premium pieces. Focus on negative space (50% empty), one focal point (a large painting or screen), and monochrome colors. Best for players who want a clean, professional look without spending real money.
TROPICAL PARADISE
Summer beach set + plants + warm lighting
Uses the summer event decorations as the foundation. Add indoor plants (purchasable with gold), warm-colored lighting (event or shop), and beach accessories. Best for players who have the summer set and want a year-round vacation vibe.
HOLIDAY WONDERLAND
Christmas tree + winter decorations + warm lights
Center around the Christmas tree (focal point). Add winter-themed wall decorations, warm lighting, and holiday-specific items. Best for the December season. Can be converted to a general "cozy" theme after the holidays by removing Christmas-specific items.

Budget & Free Options

You do not need to spend money for a great-looking base. Here is a complete free-to-play plan:

(1) Complete story progression for the basic furniture set. (2) Check the gold shop weekly — buy any new furniture pieces that match your planned theme. (3) Claim all login event decorations — even if you do not use them immediately, free items are free. (4) Save event currency for SSR shards first, but if you have leftover currency after buying all high-value items, spend it on event decorations. (5) Use the "rotate furniture" feature to change your base's look without spending anything — moving existing furniture is free and lets you refresh your space whenever you want.

Summary: Zone your rooms with clear themes. One focal point per room. Leave 30–40% empty space. Event decorations after SSR shards and protocores. Battle Pass furniture is best value. Plan before placing — use preview mode. Flex rare event items near the entrance. Home has no gameplay effect — decorate for yourself, not for score. F2P players can still create beautiful bases with gold shop items and login rewards.
