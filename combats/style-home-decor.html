STYLE GUIDE — HOME

Home Decoration Guide

Your base is the one place in the game you can truly call your own. Decorate it well and it becomes a showpiece. Decorate it poorly and it is a warehouse with a bed.

ENTRY_#STY_032026.07.02HOME_DECOR

Core System: The home/base system allows you to place furniture, decorations, and interactive objects in a 3D space. Furniture can be earned through gameplay, purchased from the shop, or obtained from limited events. The home is purely cosmetic — no gameplay benefits — but it is the most personal space in the game.

Furniture Acquisition

Permanent Sources: Story progression (basic furniture set). Achievement rewards (decorative items). Shop (gold-purchased furniture, rotates weekly). Battle Pass (1 premium furniture piece per season). Limited Sources: Event shop (seasonal decorations — summer, Christmas, anniversary). Login events (free decorative items). Limited-time bundles ($5-10 for themed sets). Priority: Buy gold furniture from the weekly rotation first — these are cheap and permanent. Event decorations are exclusive but cost event currency that could buy SSR shards. Only buy event decorations after you have secured all SSR shards and guaranteed protocores from the event shop.

Room Layout Principles

Zoning: Divide your base into functional zones — living area, bedroom, study, and display hallway. Each zone should have a clear visual theme. Do not mix themes in the same zone (beach items + gothic furniture = visual chaos). Focal Point: Each room needs one focal point — a large furniture piece or wall decoration that draws the eye. Place other furniture around the focal point, not competing with it. Negative Space: Do not fill every square meter with furniture. Empty space makes a room look larger and more intentional. Leave 30-40% of floor space empty. Wall Decor: Walls are the most underutilized space in most bases. Hang paintings, shelves, and lighting fixtures. Empty walls make a room look unfinished.

Event-Limited Decorations Worth Getting

S-Tier (Must Get): Anniversary wall tapestry (unique visual, never returns). Christmas tree (centerpiece for holiday zone). A-Tier (Get If Possible): Summer beach set (creates a distinct tropical zone). Valentine's rose display (romantic focal point). B-Tier (Skip): Single-purpose event items (e.g., a single Mephisto statue that only fits one theme). Recolored versions of existing furniture. Rule: Event decorations are only worth the currency if you plan to use them long-term. If you are not actively designing a themed zone, skip the event decoration and prioritize SSR shards.

Pro Tip: Use the "preview all furniture" option in the base editor to see every item you own before placing it. Plan your layout on paper or in a screenshot editor before spending decoration tokens. Moving furniture after placement costs no resources, but having a plan saves hours of trial-and-error. The best bases are planned, not improvised.

Home Score & Flex Value

The home has a "score" based on the number and rarity of furniture items. This score serves no gameplay purpose — it is purely for bragging rights. Score thresholds: 10k = Decent (basic furniture + some event items). 20k = Good (premium furniture + event items). 35k+ = Excellent (multiple limited sets + high-end items). Flex value: Event-limited decorations from major events (anniversary, Christmas) are worth more "status" than expensive but permanent shop items. A Christmas tree from 2025 is worth more flex than a $20 premium bed that anyone can buy anytime. Tip: Display your rarest items prominently near the entrance — this is the first thing visitors (friend list) see when they visit your base.

Summary: Zone your rooms with clear themes. One focal point per room. Leave 30-40% empty space. Event decorations after SSR shards and protocores. Battle Pass furniture is best value. Plan before placing. Flex rare event items near the entrance. Home has no gameplay effect — decorate for yourself, not for score.
