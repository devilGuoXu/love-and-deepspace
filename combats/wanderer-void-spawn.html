WANDERER ENCYCLOPEDIA — VOID SPAWN

Void Spawn: The Basic Strain

The most common wanderer type — weak individually but deadly in swarms. Master their patterns to clear trash waves effortlessly.

ENTRY_#WDR_012026.07.02VOID_SPAWN

Core Identity: Void Spawn are the standard infantry of the wanderer army. They have low HP, predictable attack patterns, and a universal Light weakness. No elite mechanics — pure execution checks. If you struggle against Void Spawn, you need to practice your dodge timing, not your gear.

Variant A — Slasher

Appearance: Humanoid, two arms ending in blade-like claws. Glowing purple eyes. HP: Low. Attack Pattern: Lunging slash — telegraphs by pulling its right arm back for 0.8s, then lunges forward 2 units. Parry Window: 0.3s after the lunge starts. Weakness: Light (interrupts its combo). Strategy: Bait the slash, perfect dodge, then counter. Two combos kill it. Never let a Slasher start its 3-hit combo — interrupt with resonance.

Variant B — Spitter

Appearance: Hunched, elongated arms, bulbous head. HP: Very Low. Attack Pattern: Ranged spit — fires a purple projectile in a slow arc. Telegraph: head tilts back for 0.5s. Parry Window: Not recommended (projectile is small). Dodge sideways instead. Weakness: Any knockback (they stagger for 3s). Strategy: Prioritize Spitters in mixed groups — their ranged attack interrupts your combos. One light attack kills them. Use Xavier's dash slash to close distance instantly.

Variant C — Charger

Appearance: Quadrupedal, thick armor plates on its back. HP: Medium. Attack Pattern: Charges in a straight line, stops at walls. Telegraph: lowers head, paws ground for 1s. Parry Window: 0.4s after it starts moving (tight). Weakness: Light + any stun. Stagger lasts 4s. Strategy: Don't stand in its charge line. Dodge perpendicular to its path. After it hits a wall, it's stunned for 2s — free damage window. Charger is the only variant that requires two full combos to kill.

Variant D — Burster

Appearance: Bloated, pulsating sac on its back. HP: Low. Attack Pattern: Self-destruct — glows red for 2s, then explodes in a 3-unit radius. Kills itself. Parry Window: None (can't parry explosion). Weakness: Kill it before it explodes. Strategy: Highest priority target in any group. If you see the red glow, either kill it immediately (one skill) or dodge away. The explosion also damages other wanderers — you can bait a Burster into a group and dodge to let it clear the pack for you.

Pro Tip: In Abyss floors 1-3, Void Spawn waves follow a fixed spawn order: 2 Slashers → 1 Spitter + 1 Slasher → 1 Charger + 2 Slashers → 1 Burster + 3 Spitters. Memorize the order to pre-position and AoE the spawn points.

Element Weakness Table

Light (Xavier): 1.5x damage, interrupts all variants. Fire (Rafayel): 1.0x, DoT helps against Charger. Ice (Zayne): 1.0x, slows Charger's charge speed. Dark (Sylus): 1.2x, execute works on all variants below 30%. Recommended element for pure Void Spawn floors: Light. Bring Xavier for maximum efficiency.

Group Combat Tactics

Small group (2-3): Focus Spitter first → Burster if present → Slashers → Charger. Use AoE skills to hit multiple at once. Large group (4+): Group them using positioning (run to one corner, all variants follow). Use your Ardent Oath when 4+ are clustered. One fully charged Oath kills all non-Charger variants. Mixed with elites: Clear the Void Spawn adds first before engaging the elite. Never fight an elite with Void Spawn behind you — they will interrupt your dodge timing.

Summary: Void Spawn are trash mobs — kill them quickly, don't let them accumulate, and they will never threaten you. Priority: Burster > Spitter > Slasher > Charger. Light element is their hard counter. Perfect dodge the Slasher, sidestep the Spitter, perpendicular-dodge the Charger, kill the Burster on sight.
