BASIC PROTOCOLS — SHATTERED SETS

Shattered & Engraved Memory Sets

The two "side-grade" memory sets that most players ignore. They are not optimal for general content but can be situationally stronger than the standard sets.

ENTRY_#0702026.07.02SETS

What Are These Sets: Shattered and Engraved are "advanced" memory sets obtained from specific domains or events. They offer unique bonuses that do not fit the standard ATK/CRIT/DMG paradigm. Most players never use them because the standard sets are more straightforward. But in specific content, these sets outperform everything else.

Shattered Set Analysis

2pc Bonus: +15% Energy Recharge. 4pc Bonus: When you perfect dodge, gain +30% ATK for 6 seconds. Cooldown: 8 seconds. Who benefits: Players with excellent dodge timing. If you can consistently perfect dodge every 8 seconds, this set gives you 75% uptime on +30% ATK — stronger than Light Seeker 4pc in the right hands. Best operative: Xavier (his dash makes positioning for perfect dodges easier). Sylus (his execute window pairs well with the ATK burst). When to use: Boss fights with predictable attack patterns (Titan Sentry, Crystal Monarch). Not recommended for: AoE clears, fights with long downtime between attacks, or players who cannot consistently perfect dodge. Verdict: S-tier in skilled hands. F-tier if you are not confident in your dodging. Practice on Titan Sentry (easiest boss to perfect dodge) before using this set in serious content.

Engraved Set Analysis

2pc Bonus: +15% DEF. 4pc Bonus: When your shield breaks, deal 200% of your DEF as damage to nearby enemies. Cooldown: 5 seconds. Who benefits: Zayne players who build DEF (unusual). This set turns Zayne into a hybrid tank-damage dealer. Best operative: Zayne (Frostguard) — his shields trigger the 4pc effect frequently. When to use: Co-op content where you need both survivability and damage. Abyss floors with multiple melee enemies that break your shield quickly. When NOT to use: Single-target boss fights (the 200% DEF explosion is AoE). Any content where shields do not break frequently. Building for Engraved: You need 1,500+ DEF for the 4pc effect to be meaningful (3,000+ damage per explosion). This requires sacrificing HP% rolls for DEF% rolls — only do this if you have Zayne's healing covered by other sources. Verdict: Niche but viable in very specific content (co-op with multiple aggro enemies, Abyss floors with add spawns). Generally worse than standard Frostguard 4pc for most content.

Comparison with Standard Sets

Shattered 4pc vs Light Seeker 4pc (Xavier): Shattered gives 75% uptime on +30% ATK = average +22.5% ATK. Light Seeker gives 66% uptime on +30% Light DMG = average +20% Light DMG. If Xavier already has high Light DMG bonus from other sources, Shattered's +ATK% may be more valuable due to diminishing returns. Engraved 4pc vs Frostguard 4pc (Zayne): Engraved gives DEF-scaling damage (useful in niche content). Frostguard gives healing (useful in all content). Engraved is never better than Frostguard in content where survivability matters. Only use Engraved if you are building a "meme" damage Zayne for fun. Rule: Standard sets are better 95% of the time. Shattered and Engraved are for experienced players who understand the stat system and want to optimize specific content niches.

Pro Tip: Do not farm Shattered or Engraved sets intentionally. They drop as byproducts of farming other domains. Keep high-roll pieces if you find them (CRIT Rate + ATK% on Shattered, DEF% + HP% on Engraved). You may need them later for a specific fight. But never use your limited domain stamina to chase these sets specifically — the opportunity cost is too high.

When to Use Non-Standard Sets

Shattered 4pc: Boss fights with predictable attack patterns. You can dodge consistently. You need more burst damage. Engraved 4pc: Co-op with multiple aggro enemies. You want to play damage Zayne (meme build). You have spare DEF protocores with good substats. Never use: Shattered on Zayne (ER is wasted, dodge windows do not align with his shield rotation). Engraved on Xavier or Sylus (DEF is completely wasted). Rafayel — neither set helps his burn DoT. Stick to Inferno Blaze 4pc or 2+2 ATK sets.

Summary: Shattered 4pc = perfect dodge → +30% ATK (75% uptime). Best on Xavier/Sylus. Engraved 4pc = shield break → 200% DEF AoE damage. Best on Zayne (niche). Standard sets are better 95% of the time. Do not farm these intentionally — keep high-roll drops. Only use in specific niche content where the conditional bonuses can be maintained.
