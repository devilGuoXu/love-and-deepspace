Combat Basics: Resonance & Ardent Oath

Understanding the core combat loop — and why mashing buttons is killing your damage.

ENTRY_#001 2026.05.16 BASIC_PROTOCOL

Let's be honest. When you first started playing, you probably just pressed buttons as they lit up. Resonance skill ready? Press it. Ardent Oath charged? Fire it. Basic attack until something else comes off cooldown.

I did the same thing. For weeks. And it worked — for early content. But somewhere around Floor 6 of Chaos Abyss, I hit a wall. My damage wasn't cutting it. My clears were slow. I was getting frustrated.

Then I learned the combat loop. And everything changed.

🔑 The Core Insight: Love and Deepspace's combat isn't about pressing buttons as fast as possible. It's about a specific rhythm: Basic Attack → Resonance → Ardent Oath → Repeat. Each step feeds into the next. Break the chain, and you lose damage.

The Energy Economy

Everything in combat revolves around one resource: Resonance Energy. Not stamina. Not your character's HP. Resonance Energy.

Here's how it works:

⚡ Basic Attack Generates 8-12 Resonance Energy per hit
✨ Perfect Dodge Generates 15 Resonance Energy
🎯 Resonance Skill Consumes 100 Resonance Energy
💥 Ardent Oath Charged by using Resonance Skills (20% per use)

Notice the loop? Basic attacks generate energy. You spend that energy on Resonance Skills. Each Resonance Skill charges your Ardent Oath. Ardent Oath is your biggest damage spike.

If you're wasting Resonance Energy by letting it sit at full, or using Resonance Skills without enough basic attack setup, you're breaking the loop.

🗡️ Basic Attack → ⚡ Resonance Energy → ✨ Resonance Skill → 💥 Ardent Oath Charge → 💀 Ardent Oath

Resonance Skills — Your Main Damage Button

Resonance Skills are your bread and butter. They do big damage. They apply debuffs. They charge your ultimate. But they're expensive — 100 energy per use.

Most players make two mistakes with Resonance Skills:

❌ Mistake #1: Spamming on cooldown. Using Resonance Skill the moment it's available — even when you're not in a damage window — wastes its potential.

❌ Mistake #2: Letting energy cap. If your Resonance Energy hits 100 and you don't use it, every extra basic attack is wasted. You're throwing away damage.

✅ The Fix: Stack energy until you're about to cap, then use Resonance Skill during an enemy's vulnerability window. This takes practice, but it's worth it.

Ardent Oath — Your Ultimate

Ardent Oath is the biggest damage button in your kit. But here's what most guides don't tell you: it gains bonus damage based on how you use it.

Using Ardent Oath immediately after a Resonance Skill gives a 25% damage bonus. Using it during an enemy's broken state (staggered or frozen) gives an additional 30-50% damage bonus. Stack them together, and you're looking at nearly double damage.

Base Ardent Oath Damage 100%
+ After Resonance Skill 125%
+ During Enemy Stagger 162%
+ Both (Optimal) 187%

That's an 87% damage increase just from timing. No gear upgrades. No protocore farming. Just pressing the button at the right moment.

The Perfect Rotation

After hundreds of hours of testing, here's the rotation that maximizes damage for every character:

Step 1 Basic Attack x4-5 (build Resonance Energy to 80-90)
Step 2 Perfect Dodge (if enemy is attacking) → generates 15 energy
Step 3 Resonance Skill (when energy is 100 or close to it)
Step 4 Basic Attack x2 (during the resonance animation cancel window)
Step 5 Ardent Oath (immediately after Resonance Skill, during enemy stagger)
Step 6 Repeat from Step 1

💡 Pro Tip: You can cancel the recovery animation of most Resonance Skills by dodging immediately after the damage registers. This saves about 0.5 seconds — enough to fit in an extra basic attack before your next rotation.

Character Differences

The core loop is the same for everyone, but each character has unique quirks:

CharacterSpecial Mechanic
Sylus (Dark)Perfect dodge refunds 15 Resonance Energy. Loop is even tighter.
Xavier (Light)Resonance Skill has a 4-frame damage window. Timing is everything.
Zayne (Ice)Resonance Skill freezes. Use Ardent Oath during freeze for bonus damage.
Rafayel (Fire)Resonance applies burn stacks. Ardent Oath detonates them for extra damage.

Common Rotation Mistakes (I Made All of These)

❌ Mistake: Using Ardent Oath at the start of a fight. You're not getting the Resonance Skill bonus. Wait for step 5.

❌ Mistake: Saving Ardent Oath for "the perfect moment." Don't hold it for a full minute. You'll lose 2-3 extra ultimates over the course of a long fight.

❌ Mistake: Ignoring basic attack chains. The 4th and 5th hits of most basic attack chains do significantly more damage and generate more energy. Complete the full chain.

❌ Mistake: Dodging unnecessarily. Perfect dodges are great. Normal dodges waste time. Only dodge when you need to avoid damage.

Summary — The Golden Rules

Rule 1 Complete your basic attack chains — don't cancel early
Rule 2 Don't let Resonance Energy cap — use it before 100
Rule 3 Resonance → Ardent Oath in sequence for bonus damage
Rule 4 Use Ardent Oath during enemy stagger or freeze for even more damage
Rule 5 Practice the rotation on easy floors until it becomes muscle memory

🎯 The Bottom Line: You don't need perfect protocores to clear content. You need a solid rotation. Master this loop, and you'll see your damage increase by 30-50% without changing a single piece of gear.

Go to the training room. Spend 20 minutes just practicing the rhythm. Basic attack → Resonance → Basic attack → Ardent Oath. Over and over until it feels natural.

Then take it into Abyss. You'll feel the difference immediately.
