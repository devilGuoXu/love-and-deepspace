Popular Genres

Love and Deepspace fan fiction covers a wide range of genres, but several are particularly popular in the community. Canon-divergent stories (changing a key moment in the main story and exploring the consequences) are the most common and consistently well-received. Missing-scene fics (filling in moments that happen between canon scenes) are the second most popular genre, with writers exploring what operatives do when the Hunter isn't watching. Alternate Universe (AU) fics — reimagining characters in modern, historical, fantasy, or sci-fi settings unrelated to the game — showcase the most creative worldbuilding.

Other popular genres include: Character studies (deep dives into a single operative's psychology), Fluff (light-hearted, romantic, no-conflict stories), Angst (emotional hurt/comfort narratives), and Crossover (Love and Deepspace characters interacting with characters from other games or media). Each operative has a different genre tendency — Xavier stories lean toward angsty celestial metaphors, Zayne stories favor slow-burn professional-to-personal arcs, Rafayel stories are often humorous or artistically themed, and Sylus stories tend toward dark romance with power dynamics.

Recommended Reading

"Starlight Letters" — Xavier/Hunter (AO3)
A series of letters Xavier writes but never sends, spanning months of the main story. Each letter reveals a different facet of his personality — his loneliness, his hope, his fear of attachment. Beautifully written with canon-compliant character voice. 12 chapters, complete. Rated T for thematic elements.
"Frost Pulse" — Zayne/Hunter (Wattpad)
A medical drama AU where Zayne is a cardiologist and the Hunter is a new patient. The story explores their connection through hospital visits, carefully maintaining Zayne's professional demeanor while showing cracks in his emotional armor. 30 chapters, ongoing. Rated T for medical content.
"Tidal Rhythm" — Rafayel/Hunter (AO3)
Post-canon story following Rafayel and the Hunter as they travel to a coastal town for an art exhibition. Lighthearted and romantic with beautiful descriptions of seaside settings and Rafayel's artistic process. 8 chapters, complete. Rated G.
"Shadow Protocol" — Sylus/Hunter (FanFiction.net)
Canon-divergent story exploring what would happen if the Hunter had to go undercover in the Onychinus organization. Sylus knows her identity from the start but plays along, leading to a tension-filled cat-and-mouse dynamic. 45 chapters, complete. Rated M for violence and mature themes.

Writing Tips

1
Know the CharactersReplay Tender Moments and Secret Times to internalize each operative's voice and mannerisms. Good fan fiction starts with accurate character portrayal.
2
Start SmallWrite a one-shot (single chapter story) before attempting a multi-chapter epic. A complete 2,000-word story teaches more than an abandoned 50,000-word WIP.
3
Tag AppropriatelyUse proper tags for content warnings, relationships, and genres. Proper tagging is a community expectation that helps readers find what they're looking for.
4
Accept FeedbackComments are the currency of fan fiction communities. Engage with readers, accept constructive criticism, and keep writing.

Where to Publish

The three primary platforms for Love and Deepspace fan fiction: Archive of Our Own (AO3) — best tagging system, largest active reader base, supports all content types; Wattpad — largest overall user base, mobile-friendly reading experience, good for serialized stories; and FanFiction.net — older platform, still active in anime/game fandoms, simpler interface. For stories focused on romantic relationships, AO3 has the most active Love and Deepspace reader community. For broader audiences and discoverability, Wattpad's algorithm helps new writers get noticed.

Community Etiquette

Do's
  • Tag major content warnings
  • Credit inspired works
  • Respond to comments
  • Respect ship preferences
  • Update regularly or mark hiatus
Don'ts
  • Don't plagiarize or repost
  • Don't bash ships you don't like
  • Don't pressure authors for updates
  • Don't leave unsolicited negative reviews
  • Don't post AI-generated content as original

Getting Started

Ready to write your first Love and Deepspace fan fiction? Start with a concept that excites you — a "what if" moment from the story, a missing scene you've always wanted to see, or a simple domestic moment between the Hunter and your favorite operative. Write 500-1,000 words, proofread once, and publish. The community is supportive of new writers. Use our recommended platforms above and tag your work with the relevant operative and genre tags. Your first story doesn't have to be perfect — it just has to be written.

Tip: The most engaging Love and Deepspace fan fiction focuses on character voice over plot complexity. Readers are here because they love the characters, not because they need an intricate plot. A simple story where Xavier makes coffee and has a quiet conversation with the Hunter will get more engagement than an elaborate action plot where the characters don't sound like themselves. Voice is everything in fan fiction.
