Stack vs Spread: Protocore Stat Strategy

The biggest protocore debate: should you stack one stat or spread across multiple? The answer depends on your operative, content, and current gear level.

ENTRY_#0962026.07.02CORE_SYSTEM
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

How to Read This: If you are new to protocores, skip to the Early Game section first. If you are optimizing an endgame build, read the Diminishing Returns section. The general rule: stack until diminishing returns, then diversify.

The Stacking Case: Why Focus Works

Stacking a single stat (e.g., all ATK% or all CRIT damage) gives the highest peak performance for specific scenarios. The math: protocore main stats and substats use additive multipliers within each stat type. If you stack 200% ATK, each additional ATK% is worth exactly the same flat ATK. However, damage formulas are multiplicative across stat types. A build with 200% ATK and 50% CRIT rate deals less damage than a build with 150% ATK and 100% CRIT rate, because the second build multiplies two stats together. When stacking is correct: (1) When you are early game and need raw power fast — stacking ATK% is the quickest path to clear content. (2) When a memory set gives multiplicative bonuses for a single stat (e.g., HP-scaling sets for Sylus). (3) When the content has a hard stat check (e.g., a DPS check floor where missing the threshold means wiping). Stacking gets you over the threshold fastest.

The Spreading Case: Why Balance Wins

Spreading stats across ATK, CRIT rate, CRIT damage, and elemental damage gives the highest total damage in most endgame scenarios. The reason: the damage formula multiplies these stats together. If you are missing one multiplier, the other multipliers have less impact. Example: Build A: 300% ATK, 20% CRIT rate, 50% CRIT damage. Build B: 150% ATK, 60% CRIT rate, 120% CRIT damage. Build B deals approximately 40% more average damage despite having "lower" individual numbers. When spreading is correct: (1) When you are endgame and have good gear across all slots. (2) When your operative's kit benefits from multiple stats (e.g., Zayne benefits from ATK, ice DMG, and CRIT). (3) When you are facing content with no specific hard stat threshold. For general use, a balanced spread always outperforms a single-stat stack.

The Diminishing Returns Trap

Every stat in Deepspace has diminishing returns, but not all at the same rate. ATK%: Linear — no inherent diminishing returns. Each point of ATK% gives the same flat ATK regardless of how much you have. However, ATK% loses relative value as you get more sources of base ATK (memories, bonds, resonance). CRIT Rate: Hard cap at 100%. Past 70%, the value of each additional point drops because you overcap on normal attacks and only benefit on resonance. CRIT Damage: No hard cap, but requires CRIT rate to realize its value. Without 50%+ CRIT rate, CDMG is a wasted stat. Elemental DMG%: High value initially (30-50%), then moderate value (50-80%), then low value past 80% due to diminishing returns in the multiplicative formula. Energy Regen: Only useful up to the breakpoint where you can Oath every rotation. Past ~140%, excess ER has zero value. The key insight: Stack ATK% until you have 3,000-4,000 base ATK. Then stack CRIT rate to 50-60%. Then stack CDMG to 120%+. Then return to ATK%. This progression gives the highest damage per stamina invested.

Operative-Specific Breakdown: Xavier: Light DMG% and CRIT damage synergize with his teleport-enhanced attacks. Stack CDMG slightly higher than usual. Sylus: HP% and ATK% mixed — his kit scales off both. Do not ignore HP% even in a "damage" build. Zayne: Ice DMG% has the highest value because his resonance applies freeze. Prioritize Ice DMG% over ATK% after 4,000 ATK. Rafayel: CRIT rate is slightly more valuable than CDMG because his burn ticks can crit. Balance them 1:1 if possible. These are nuances — the general ATK > CRIT rate > CDMG > ATK progression still applies to all four.

How to Evaluate Your Current Build

Use this quick checklist to determine if you should stack or spread: (1) What is your ATK? Below 4,000 = stack ATK% until you reach it. (2) What is your CRIT rate? Below 50% = do not invest in CDMG until you fix this. (3) What is your CDMG? Below 100% and CRIT rate above 50% = switch to CDMG focus. (4) What is your elemental damage? If below 40%, prioritize it over ATK%. (5) Are you meeting content stat thresholds? If yes, spread for general use. If no, stack to hit the threshold, then spread. This checklist applies to 95% of build decisions. The remaining 5% are operative-specific quirks (like Sylus HP-scaling) that override the general rules.

Summary: Stack ATK% until 4,000 ATK. Then build CRIT rate to 50-60%. Then add CDMG to 120%+. Finally, return to ATK% and elemental damage. Balance across stat types yields 40% more damage than pure stacking. The exception: early game where stacking ATK% clears content fastest. Use the 5-question checklist to evaluate your build. When in doubt, choose CRIT rate over any other stat below 50%. — CRIT rate is the most undervalued stat by new players and the most impactful.
