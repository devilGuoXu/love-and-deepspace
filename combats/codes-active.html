How to Redeem

To redeem codes in Love and Deepspace: open the game menu (top-right corner), select "Settings," go to the "Account" tab, find the "Redeem Code" field, enter the code exactly as shown (codes are case-sensitive), and tap confirm. Rewards are delivered through the in-game mail system. If a code doesn't work, double-check for typos — common mistakes include confusing 0 with O, 1 with l, and missing uppercase letters.

Code Types

Codes fall into several categories: Livestream codes (released during developer streams, valid for 24-48 hours — these are the most urgent to redeem), Update codes (released alongside major version updates, valid for 1-2 weeks), Social media milestone codes (celebrating follower counts, valid for 1-2 months), and Holiday/Event codes (seasonal codes with longer validity windows). Livestream codes are the easiest to miss because of their short windows — follow the official social channels to catch them.

Tracking Expiry

Code expiry follows a general pattern: livestream codes expire within 24-48 hours, update patch codes expire in 7-14 days, social media codes expire in 30-60 days, and holiday codes expire at the end of the event period (usually 2-4 weeks). Permanent codes (like HUNTERGUIDE) have no expiry. Always prioritize redeeming codes in order of expiration date — livestream codes first, then patch codes, then social codes, then event codes.

Where Codes Come From

The primary sources for redemption codes: Official Love and Deepspace Twitter/X account, Official Discord server (announcements channel), Developer livestreams on YouTube/Twitch, Community manager posts on Reddit and HoYoverse forums, and Content creator partnerships (some codes are distributed through streamers and YouTubers). Following these sources and enabling notifications is the best way to never miss a code.

Tip: Create a dedicated "Gacha Codes" folder in your email and subscribe to the official Love and Deepspace newsletter. Codes are often included in email blasts and you'll get them delivered directly to your inbox. This catches codes even if you miss the social media posts.
