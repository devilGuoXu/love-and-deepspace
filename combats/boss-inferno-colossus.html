THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Inferno Colossus

A volcanic giant that turns the arena into a furnace. Fire resistance and ice damage are non-negotiable.

ENTRY_#1052026.06.29ELITE_BOSS

Recommended Power: 78k+ | Element: Fire | Weakness: Ice — Zayne deals +40% damage. Fire resistance food is strongly recommended.

Phase 1: Volcanic Awakening (100%-65%)

Lava Pool: Colossus slams the ground, creating lava zones. They persist for the entire fight. Meteor Shower: Calls meteors that land in random locations. Watch the ground markers. Heat Wave: AoE burn pulse. Dodge timing: as soon as the ground glows.

Phase 2: Eruption (65%-30%)

Wildfire: Arena edges catch fire. The safe zone shrinks. Stay centered. Magma Fist: Colossus punches the ground, creating a lava wave. Jump over it. Inferno Breath: Cone of fire. Dodge sideways — never backward. The cone tracks slightly.

Phase 3: Supernova (30%-0%)

Meltdown: Colossus becomes molten. Contact damage. Ranged attacks only. Eruption Chain: Rapid succession of lava pools. Pre-position in safe spots. Final Fusion: Colossus explodes. Use Ardent Oath i-frames to survive.

Recommended Team: Zayne (Main DPS) — Ice element +40% damage. Xavier (Sub-DPS) — consistent ranged damage during Meltdown. Rafayel (Flex) — Fire resistance helps, but his damage is reduced.

Advanced Tip: Lava pools persist but don't overlap on the same spot. Kite the Colossus around the arena to spread pools evenly. Running out of safe space is the most common cause of death — plan your movement 30 seconds ahead.

Summary: Zayne is mandatory for this fight. Manage your arena space — never let the lava pools corner you. Phase 3 is ranged-only. Save i-frames for Final Fusion. Fire resistance food makes a significant difference.
