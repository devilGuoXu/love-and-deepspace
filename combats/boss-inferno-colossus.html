Inferno Colossus

A volcanic giant that turns the arena into a furnace. Fire resistance and ice damage are non-negotiable.

Recommended Power: 78k+ | Element: Fire | Weakness: Ice 閳?Zayne deals +40% damage. Fire resistance food is strongly recommended.

Phase 1: Volcanic Awakening (100%-65%)

Lava Pool: Colossus slams the ground, creating lava zones. They persist for the entire fight. Meteor Shower: Calls meteors that land in random locations. Watch the ground markers. Heat Wave: AoE burn pulse. Dodge timing: as soon as the ground glows.

Phase 2: Eruption (65%-30%)

Wildfire: Arena edges catch fire. The safe zone shrinks. Stay centered. Magma Fist: Colossus punches the ground, creating a lava wave. Jump over it. Inferno Breath: Cone of fire. Dodge sideways 閳?never backward. The cone tracks slightly.

Phase 3: Supernova (30%-0%)

Meltdown: Colossus becomes molten. Contact damage. Ranged attacks only. Eruption Chain: Rapid succession of lava pools. Pre-position in safe spots. Final Fusion: Colossus explodes. Use Ardent Oath i-frames to survive.

Recommended Team: Zayne (Main DPS) 閳?Ice element +40% damage. Xavier (Sub-DPS) 閳?consistent ranged damage during Meltdown. Rafayel (Flex) 閳?Fire resistance helps, but his damage is reduced.

Preparation and Consumables

Fire resistance food is not optional for this encounter 閳?it reduces the damage over time from lava pools by approximately 35%, which translates to significantly more uptime before you need to retreat and heal. Craft or purchase the highest tier Fire Resistance dish available before attempting this fight. Bring HP recovery items for the support slot as well, since passive regeneration cannot keep up with the ambient fire damage in Phase 2 and Phase 3.

Memory enhancement priorities differ from typical boss fights. Defense substats provide meaningful value against the Inferno Colossus because a significant portion of incoming damage is the ambient lava pool burn rather than direct boss attacks. HP percentage on your Solar pair main stats is recommended over ATK percentage for this specific encounter. Zayne's Ice memories should prioritize HP over damage for Phase 3 survivability 閳?his damage bonus from the elemental advantage is sufficient even with defensive main stats.

Alternative Team Compositions

If you lack a built Zayne, alternative compositions can still clear this floor at a higher power threshold. A double-support strategy using two healers with one DPS can outlast the Colossus through attrition, though this requires approximately 95k power for a comfortable clear. Xavier as primary DPS with Rafayel as fire-resistant support is the most accessible alternative. Xavier's ranged attacks are invaluable during Phase 3 when close combat becomes dangerous.

A mono-Ice team using two Ice operatives is the strongest composition for this fight if you have the roster depth. The double Ice damage bonus stacks additively, giving both operatives the +40% advantage. This configuration clears Phase 1 and Phase 2 approximately 25% faster than a mixed-element team, reducing the number of lava pools spawned and preserving arena space for the later phases.

The Inferno Colossus has a hidden enrage timer of 3 minutes. After 3 minutes, the Colossus enters a permanent enrage state with doubled attack speed and 50% increased damage. If your clear time consistently exceeds 2:30, you need more burst damage rather than sustain. Save all resonance skills for the 30% HP threshold where the Colossus becomes vulnerable. Bursting through the 30-0% phase quickly is more important than managing lava pools in earlier phases.

The Inferno Colossus's lava pools deal percentage-based max HP damage. Higher HP does not help survive them. Fire resistance reduces pool damage by 8% per 10 points. Stacking 30 fire resistance makes lava pools survivable for up to 5 seconds.

Advanced Tip: Lava pools persist but don't overlap on the same spot. Kite the Colossus around the arena to spread pools evenly. Running out of safe space is the most common cause of death 閳?plan your movement 30 seconds ahead.

Summary: Zayne is mandatory for this fight. Manage your arena space 閳?never let the lava pools corner you. Phase 3 is ranged-only. Save i-frames for Final Fusion. Fire resistance food makes a significant difference.
