Complete World Atlas

World Overview

The known world of Love and Deepspace consists of a single large continent and several island chains, though the full extent of the planet's landmass has not been explored. The Deepspace rift has made long-distance exploration dangerous, as dimensional instability increases with distance from the Hunter Corps' sphere of influence.

The Continent. The main continent stretches approximately 3,000 kilometers north to south and 2,000 kilometers east to west, hosting a diverse range of climates and terrains. The northern regions are temperate with distinct seasons, transitioning to subtropical in the central zones and tropical in the southern reaches. Mountain ranges run along the eastern coast, creating a rain shadow that produces arid conditions in the interior.

Island Territories. Several significant islands exist off the main continent's coast, including resort islands (developed for tourism and civilian habitation), research outposts (dedicated to Protocore and dimensional study), and unclaimed territories (where Wanderer activity is too high for permanent settlement). The most significant island is the one hosting the Deepspace rift — an uninhabited landmass that has been transformed by dimensional energy.

Political Divisions. The continent is divided into multiple sovereign states, but the Hunter Corps operates as a supranational organization with jurisdiction that supersedes national boundaries. This unique authority structure was established after the Deepspace rift appeared, as nations recognized that the dimensional threat required a unified response. The Hunter Corps Charter grants it authority to operate anywhere in the world for the purpose of Wanderer suppression and dimensional research.

Hunter Corps Headquarters

The Hunter Corps Headquarters is the operational center of global anti-Wanderer efforts. Located in a purpose-built facility that serves as both administrative center and combat command post, the HQ is the most heavily fortified non-military installation in the world.

Location. The HQ is situated on the eastern coast of the continent, approximately 200 kilometers from the N109 Zone border. Its position was chosen for strategic access to the Deepspace rift (which lies to the east), the N109 Zone (to the west), and the major population centers (to the north and south). The facility is built into coastal cliffs, making it defensible from land and sea approaches.

Facility Layout. The HQ complex includes the main administrative tower (housing command staff, intelligence analysis, and mission planning), operative quarters (residential facilities for active operatives), training facilities (combat simulation chambers, Evol training ranges, and dimensional research labs), Protocore research wing (dedicated to Protocore classification, storage, and enhancement technology), and medical bay (equipped to treat combat injuries and dimensional exposure).

Command Structure. The HQ operates under a dedicated Commander who reports to an international oversight council. The Commander has authority over all operative deployments, research priorities, and resource allocation. The current Commander has held the position since the Corps' founding and is widely respected for maintaining operational effectiveness despite political pressures from member states.

Major Cities & Settlements

Beyond the Hunter Corps HQ, several major cities form the backbone of civilized territory. Each has its own character, strategic importance, and connection to the operatives' stories.

Lumina City. The largest city on the continent, located in the northern temperate zone. Lumina City is the cultural and economic capital, home to the finest universities, hospitals, and cultural institutions. It is also where Zayne practices medicine at the city's premier hospital. The city's architecture blends classical and contemporary styles, with broad boulevards, extensive parks, and a waterfront district that is popular with tourists and residents alike. Its distance from the N109 Zone makes it the safest major city.

Azureport. A major port city on the eastern coast, south of the Hunter Corps HQ. Azureport is a center of trade, fishing, and naval operations. Its economy is built around the sea, and its culture reflects the maritime heritage of the region. Rafayel maintains a studio in the city's arts district, drawing inspiration from the ocean views and the vibrant creative community. The city has a moderate Wanderer threat level due to its coastal proximity to the Deepspace rift.

Havenbrook. A mid-sized city located near the border of the N109 Zone, Havenbrook serves as the primary staging point for Hunter Corps operations into restricted territory. The city has a frontier character — practical architecture, a high concentration of combat personnel, and an economy built around Corps support services. Xavier has been known to spend time in Havenbrook, drawn by its proximity to both the N109 Zone and the wilderness areas where he can practice his abilities without observation.

Veridian. An inland agricultural center that supplies food to multiple cities. Veridian is notable for being the hometown of the Hunter in certain story branches. It is a quiet, unremarkable city by most standards, but its significance in the Hunter's personal story makes it a location of interest. The city has minimal Wanderer activity due to its distance from dimensional instability zones.

N109 Zone: Complete Geography

The N109 Zone is the most significant restricted territory in the known world. Its geography is as hostile as its ruler, shaped by dimensional energy and decades of conflict.

Physical Territory. The N109 Zone covers approximately 500 square kilometers of territory on the eastern coast, directly west of the Deepspace rift island. It is bordered by the ocean to the east, mountain ranges to the west and south, and a heavily fortified checkpoint to the north (the only official entry point from Hunter Corps territory). The terrain within the zone is varied — urban ruins from before the zone's establishment, industrial complexes built by Onychinus, and wilderness areas that have been reclaimed by dimensional vegetation.

District Breakdown. The zone is divided into several districts, each with a distinct character. The Core District houses the Onychinus command center and Sylus's personal quarters — it is the most heavily fortified area of the zone. The Market District is the economic heart, where Protocores, weapons, and information are traded. The Industrial District contains Onychinus's research and manufacturing facilities. The Ruins District consists of pre-zone buildings that have been abandoned to dimensional overgrowth. The Docks provide the zone's maritime access and are the primary point for goods entering and leaving the territory.

Dimensional Geography. The N109 Zone's location — between the Hunter Corps mainland and the Deepspace rift island — gives it unique dimensional properties. The boundary layers within the zone are thinner than in any other inhabited territory, creating an environment where Protocores form more readily, Evol abilities are stronger, and Wanderers manifest at higher rates. This dimensional geography is both the zone's greatest resource and its greatest danger.

Access Points. Official access to the N109 Zone is restricted to a single checkpoint at the northern border, jointly monitored by Onychinus and (nominally) the Hunter Corps. Unofficial access points exist along the coastline and through the mountain passes, but these are dangerous — both because of Onychinus patrols and because they pass through areas of high dimensional instability.

Deepspace Rift Region

The Deepspace rift region — the area immediately surrounding the dimensional tear — is the most hazardous environment on the planet. It is also the most scientifically valuable.

The Rift Island. The Deepspace rift is located on an island approximately 100 kilometers off the eastern coast, beyond the N109 Zone. The island was once a standard landmass, but constant exposure to dimensional energy has transformed it. The native ecosystem has been replaced by dimensional-adapted flora and fauna that have no counterparts elsewhere. The island's geography changes regularly as dimensional energy reshapes the terrain.

Energy Zones. The rift region is divided into concentric zones based on dimensional energy concentration. The Proximity Zone (0–1 km from the rift) has energy levels that are lethal to humans without full protective equipment. The Research Zone (1–10 km) is accessible with protective gear and hosts the Hunter Corps' most advanced observation posts. The Transition Zone (10–50 km) has elevated energy levels that are uncomfortable but survivable, with higher-than-normal Protocore formation rates. The Peripheral Zone (50–100 km) has mildly elevated energy and is the safest area for study.

Research Outposts. The Hunter Corps maintains research outposts in the rift region, staffed by scientists who study dimensional physics, Protocore formation, and Wanderer manifestation. These outposts are self-sufficient and heavily fortified, as they are at constant risk of Wanderer attack. Communication with the mainland is maintained through specialized dimensional relay equipment that can penetrate the energy interference.

Frontier Territories

Beyond the established civilization zones, frontier territories exist where dimensional instability has made permanent settlement impossible but where resources — particularly Protocores — attract explorers and operatives.

The Ash Wastes. A large inland area where a dimensional instability event decades ago transformed fertile land into a barren, ash-covered plain. The Ash Wastes are rich in Protocore deposits — the event that created them scattered Protocores across the entire region. However, the dimensional instability that created the wastes persists, making long-term operations dangerous. Expeditions into the Ash Wastes are typically short-duration and heavily protected.

The Crystal Forest. A region where dimensional energy has caused crystalline growths to sprout from the ground, creating a forest of energy-conducting crystals. The Crystal Forest is beautiful but deadly — the crystals emit energy that can interfere with Evol abilities and cause disorientation in unprotected humans. The forest is a research site for studying how dimensional energy interacts with geological formations.

The Sunken Coast. A coastal region where rising dimensional energy has caused the coastline to become unstable, creating a labyrinth of tidal pools, submerged ruins, and energy-emitting marine life. The Sunken Coast is rich in aquatic Protocore variants that are highly valued for their unique properties. Access requires specialized marine equipment and training.

Lost Philos Geography

The geography of lost Philos exists primarily in theory. Its physical remnants are scarce, but analysis of available evidence allows for educated reconstruction.

Presumed Location. Philos was likely located on a landmass that no longer exists above water. Geological evidence suggests a large landmass subsided in the region now occupied by the Deepspace rift island approximately 5,000 years ago. The timing corresponds with the estimated date of Philos's fall. If Philos was located where the rift island now stands, the dimensional instability of the region may have been caused by Philos's fall rather than being a natural phenomenon.

Philos Urban Design. Based on recovered texts and architectural fragments, Philos cities were organized around a central Aether Core resonance point, with crystalline buildings radiating outward. The cities were designed as three-dimensional structures — not just spreading across the surface but extending into the air and underground. Philos architecture integrated living and working spaces, with most citizens living within walking distance of their workplaces. The cities were self-sufficient, with all resources provided by Core-powered systems.

The Central Spire. The most significant Philos structure was the Central Spire — the building that housed the Aether Core and served as the residence of the Resonance Elite. The Spire was the tallest structure in Philos, visible from anywhere in the civilization. It was said that the Spire's peak touched the dimensional boundary, allowing the Elite to observe other dimensions from its summit.

Strategic Importance Analysis

Understanding the geography of the Love and Deepspace world is essential for strategic planning. The following analysis ranks key locations by strategic importance.

Critical Strategic Locations. The Deepspace rift (source of all dimensional energy, Protocore formation, and Wanderer threat), the Aether Core's known location (control of the Core means control of dimensional energy), the N109 Zone (gateway to the rift, Protocore production hub, Onychinus stronghold), and the Hunter Corps HQ (command and control center for global anti-Wanderer operations). Loss of any of these locations would have catastrophic consequences for global stability.

High Strategic Importance. The research outposts in the rift region (irreplaceable scientific facilities), the major cities (population centers that would cause massive casualties if lost), Protocore-rich frontier territories (economic resources that fund operations), and the border checkpoint between Hunter Corps territory and N109 (main point of access control).

Medium Strategic Importance. Secondary cities and settlements, transportation infrastructure (roads, ports, airfields), agricultural regions (food supply), and standard Protocore mining sites.

Low Strategic Importance. Remote wilderness areas with minimal dimensional activity, small settlements with no strategic resources, and locations that have been rendered uninhabitable by dimensional instability.

Geographic Observation: The three most significant locations in the known world — the Deepspace rift, the N109 Zone, and the Hunter Corps HQ — form a rough triangle approximately 300 kilometers per side. This triangular region contains the highest concentration of dimensional activity, Evol users, and strategic importance on the planet. Understanding this triangle is the key to understanding the geopolitical dynamics of the world.

Summary: The Love and Deepspace world consists of a single large continent with diverse climates and terrains, divided into multiple sovereign states united under the supranational authority of the Hunter Corps. The Hunter Corps Headquarters is the operational center, located on the eastern coast near the N109 Zone and the Deepspace rift. Major cities include Lumina City (northern cultural capital), Azureport (eastern port city), Havenbrook (frontier staging point), and Veridian (Hunter's hometown). The N109 Zone is a 500-square-kilometer restricted territory divided into districts, with unique dimensional geography that makes it both valuable and dangerous. The Deepspace rift region consists of concentric energy zones around the dimensional tear, hosting critical research outposts. Frontier territories like the Ash Wastes, Crystal Forest, and Sunken Coast offer resources but require specialized operations. Lost Philos was likely located where the rift island currently stands, with its cities built around Core resonance points. Strategic importance analysis identifies the rift, Core, N109 Zone, and Hunter Corps HQ as critical locations forming a triangular power region.
