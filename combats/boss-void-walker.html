Portal Prediction Mastery

Mastering portal prediction is the single most impactful skill for this encounter. When the Void Walker spawns two portals, one portal emits a faint shimmering effect approximately 1 second before the Walker emerges from it. This shimmer is visible as a distortion in the air around the portal opening. Train your eyes to scan both portals immediately after they spawn rather than focusing on the boss's current position. The shimmer is easier to spot if you reduce your camera sensitivity slightly before the fight, allowing for smoother visual tracking.

After predicting the correct portal, position yourself between the portal and the arena center rather than directly in front of it. This gives you maximum reaction time for the Walker's emerging attack. The Walker always attacks within 0.5 seconds of emerging, and the attack type depends on which portal it used. Side portals produce a sweeping beam attack. Top portals produce a ground slam. Center portals produce a void mine spread. Adjust your dodge direction based on portal position: dodge right for side portals, dodge backward for top portals, dodge forward through the boss for center portals.

Mirrored Realm Coordination

The Mirrored Realm mechanic in Phase 2 splits the arena into two parallel dimensions. Operatives in different dimensions cannot see or interact with each other. The team must stay within the same dimension to maintain coordination. The dimension split is telegraphed by a 3-second visual ripple effect across the arena. When you see the ripple, move toward the nearest teammate and stay within 5 meters of them until the ripple resolves.

If you become separated into different dimensions, use audio cues to coordinate. The Walker's attacks produce different sound effects depending on which dimension it is attacking. A deep bass rumble means the Walker is attacking your teammate's dimension. A high-pitched screech means it is attacking yours. When you hear the bass rumble, your teammate is in danger and you should prepare to intercept the Walker when the dimensions merge again. The dimensions always merge after 20 seconds, signaled by a bright flash.

The Void Walker's portal flicker mechanic has a consistent tell: the correct portal emits a heat distortion particle effect 1 second before the Walker emerges. This tell is easier to spot at lower graphics settings. If struggling to identify the correct portal, temporarily reduce graphics quality for this boss fight.

Advanced Tip: The Void Walker always uses the portal that flickers 1 second before emerging. Watch for the visual tell. Sylus's execute has slight homing 閳?use it when the Walker enters a portal to deal damage across dimensions.

Summary: Portal prediction is everything. Watch for the flicker. Stay close during Mirrored Realm. Stop attacking during Temporal Loop. This fight rewards patience and pattern recognition over raw DPS.
