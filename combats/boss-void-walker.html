THREAT LEVEL: CRITICAL

Void Walker

A dimensional mage that hops between realities. Tracking it through teleports is the core challenge.

ENTRY_#1082026.06.29ELITE_BOSS

Recommended Power: 85k+ | Element: Void | Weakness: Dark — Sylus's tracking abilities negate teleport advantage.

Phase 1: Rift Walker (100%-65%)

Rift Walk: Walker opens a portal and emerges elsewhere. Two portals appear — it uses one. Watch which one flickers. Void Mines: Places dimensional mines that detonate on proximity. Can be destroyed with ranged attacks. Gravity Well: Pulls everyone to center then explodes. Run outward immediately.

Phase 2: Dimensional Shatter (65%-30%)

Mirrored Realm: Arena splits into two dimensions. Teammates in different dimensions can't see each other. Stay close. Temporal Loop: Walker rewinds 3 seconds. Damage dealt during that time is healed. Stop attacking when you see the visual cue. Void Lance: Piercing beam across the arena. Tracks slightly. Keep moving perpendicular.

Phase 3: Reality Collapse (30%-0%)

Rift Storm: Multiple portals open. Walker rapidly switches between them. Predict the pattern. Annihilation Wave: Arena-wide void pulse. Jump to avoid. Void Heart: Walker's core is exposed. All damage tripled. End it.

Recommended Team: Sylus (Main DPS/Tracker) — Dark element tracks through rifts. Xavier (Sub-DPS) — Light damage on exposed core. Rafayel (Flex) — AoE for mines.

Advanced Tip: The Void Walker always uses the portal that flickers 1 second before emerging. Watch for the visual tell. Sylus's execute has slight homing — use it when the Walker enters a portal to deal damage across dimensions.

Summary: Portal prediction is everything. Watch for the flicker. Stay close during Mirrored Realm. Stop attacking during Temporal Loop. This fight rewards patience and pattern recognition over raw DPS.
