Deepspace Trial Strategies

Orbital tower is the toughest solo content. Here is how to build teams for every element floor and push your highest clear.

ENTRY_#0742026.07.02TRIALS

How to Read This: The trial tower is divided into element-locked floors. This guide tells you which operatives and memory sets to use for each element. Use it as a reference when you hit a wall on a specific floor.

Element Floor Archetypes

Trial floors restrict you to operatives of a specific element (or shared elements like Solar/Lunar). The key to pushing high floors is understanding the archetype: Single-Element Floors: You can only use operatives of one element. These are the hardest because they limit your team composition. Solution: Build 2 DPS operatives of that element. Dual-Element Floors: Two elements are allowed. These are easier because you can bring a primary DPS + support from the second element. Free Floors: No element restriction. These are your "push as high as possible" floors — use your strongest team. Always max out free floors before attempting element-locked ones.

Fire Element Teams

Fire floors (Rafayel, fiery passive): Main DPS: Rafayel with Oceanic or Ember set. Prioritize CRIT rate and ATK%. Sub-DPS/Support: Any Lunar-aligned operative with healing or shield memories. Strategy: Fire floors favor sustained damage over burst. Use Rafayel's enhanced attacks to apply burn and stack damage over time. Do not rush — fire floors are endurance tests. Bring healing resonance if you have it.

Ice Element Teams

Ice floors (Zayne, frost passive): Main DPS: Zayne with Frostbite or Eternal set. Stack ATK% and resonance damage. Support: Healer or shielder — Zayne's self-sustain is good but not enough for high floors. Strategy: Ice floors favor hit-and-run tactics. Use Zayne's freeze CC to interrupt enemy attack patterns. Save Ardent Oath for when the freeze is on cooldown. Ice has the best crowd control in the game — use it.

Light Element Teams

Light floors (Xavier, solar passive): Main DPS: Xavier with Radiance or Lightforge set. CRIT damage is especially valuable here. Support: Speed buffers or energy batteries. Strategy: Light floors are all about speed. Kill enemies before they can attack. Xavier's teleport and enhanced combos let you delete priority targets in seconds. These are the easiest element floors if your Xavier is built — the hardest if he is not.

Dark Element Teams: Dark floors (Sylus, lunar passive): Main DPS: Sylus with Darkstar or Chaos set. HP% and resonance damage are core stats. Support: Sylus is self-sufficient — bring any support for set bonuses. Strategy: Sylus floors are brawls. He trades HP for damage, so managing your HP pool is critical. Use his resonance for massive AoE clear on multi-wave floors. Dark floors typically have the tightest DPS checks — make sure your Sylus is well-geared before attempting high floors.

Multi-Wave Floors (20+ enemies)

Some trial floors throw 20+ waves of enemies at you. These are resource management challenges: (1) Do not use Ardent Oath on the first few waves — save it for waves 15+ when enemy density peaks. (2) Use AoE resonance memories. Single-target damage is wasted on multi-wave floors. (3) Positioning matters more than dodging — group enemies with your movement before using AoE attacks. (4) Bring a healer if available. Chip damage adds up over 20 waves and will kill you on wave 19 if you are not careful. (5) Learn the enemy spawn order — some floors spawn ranged enemies first that you should prioritize.

Increasing Your Floor Count

Every floor above 100 is significantly harder. Here is how to push past plateaus: (1) Check your gear first — +12 protocores on all 6 slots are mandatory past floor 80. (2) Upgrade memories to level 60 minimum on your main DPS. (3) Level up resonance and bonds for your operative. (4) Watch a video clear of the specific floor you are stuck on — positioning tricks are often the missing piece. (5) Take breaks — tilted play leads to worse performance. The trial tower does not expire. You can take a week to farm better gear and try again.

Summary: Build 2 DPS per element for single-element floors. Fire = endurance, Ice = CC, Light = speed, Dark = brawling. Save Oath for late waves on multi-wave floors. GEAR is the most common barrier past floor 80. Do not tilt — tower is permanent progression.
