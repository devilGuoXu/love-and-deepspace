Fan Music Scene

The Love and Deepspace fan music community spans multiple platforms and languages, with creators producing content across every genre imaginable. Vocal covers of the game's instrumental OST tracks are among the most popular — fans write original lyrics to melodies that originally had none, interpreting character themes through their own vocal styling. Piano arrangements dominate the instrumental side, with solo pianists reimagining battle themes, tender moments, and character motifs as intimate solo pieces. Remixes push the soundtrack in new directions — electronic, orchestral, lo-fi, and even metal interpretations exist, each bringing a different emotional lens to familiar melodies.

Original songs inspired by the game represent the most ambitious category of fan music. These are entirely new compositions — lyrics, melody, arrangement — written in response to the game's narrative and characters. Some capture a specific operative's emotional arc; others tell stories from the Hunter's perspective. A handful have gained significant traction, amassing hundreds of thousands of views on YouTube and becoming unofficial anthems within their respective operative fandoms.

YouTube
Largest collection of fan covers and original songs. Search by operative name + "cover" for the widest selection. Many creators provide download links in descriptions.
Bilibili
Primary platform for Chinese-language fan music. High-quality vocal covers and piano arrangements. Use Chinese character names for best results.
SoundCloud
Audio-only hub for original compositions and remixes. Smaller community but dedicated following for electronic and experimental interpretations.
Spotify
Growing number of fan covers available on streaming platforms. Not all fan music is permitted on Spotify due to copyright policies, but original inspired works thrive here.

Most Covered Tracks

Certain OST tracks are covered far more frequently than others, usually because their melodies are memorable, emotionally resonant, or particularly well-suited to vocal or instrumental interpretation. Below are the most commonly covered tracks in the Love and Deepspace fan music community, along with estimated cover counts based on platform searches.

Main Title Theme — "Love and Deepspace"
The game's main theme is the most covered track by a wide margin. Its sweeping melody and emotional resonance make it a favorite for piano arrangements and orchestral covers. The melody's clear phrasing and dynamic range give performers room for personal interpretation. Estimated 80+ covers across all platforms.
Xavier's Theme — "Stellar Radiance"
Xavier's light, celestial melody translates beautifully to piano and strings. Cover artists often emphasize the ascending arpeggios that characterize his motif. Vocal covers tend to be wistful and airy, matching his dreamlike quality. Estimated 60+ covers.
Zayne's Theme — "Frost Waltz"
Zayne's piano-driven theme is naturally suited to solo piano covers. The wide intervals and unresolved chords create a sense of longing that performers lean into. Vocal covers of this theme are less common but particularly striking when done well. Estimated 50+ covers.
Rafayel's Theme — "Tidal Flame"
Rafayel's flamenco-infused theme with its rapid shifts between major and minor keys presents a technical challenge that attracts skilled musicians. Guitar covers and full band arrangements are most common. Estimated 45+ covers.
Sylus's Theme — "Shadow Sovereign"
The dark, brooding quality of Sylus's theme with its heavy brass and cello sections lends itself to dramatic covers. Bass-heavy remixes and orchestral arrangements are popular. The descending chromatic "Mephisto motif" is often isolated and expanded upon. Estimated 40+ covers.
Abyssal Chaos Battle Theme
The high-energy battle track with its dynamic layering system has inspired electronic remixes and metal covers. The track's clear sectional structure makes it ideal for arrangement experiments. Estimated 25+ covers.

Getting Started with Music Covers

Creating your own Love and Deepspace music cover is more accessible than you might think. Whether you are a vocalist, instrumentalist, or producer, the tools and resources available today make it possible to produce quality work with minimal investment.

Karaoke Tracks
For vocal covers, start with instrumental versions of the OST tracks. Search YouTube for "Love and Deepspace instrumental" or use AI vocal removal tools like VocalRemover.org to extract instrumentals from the original tracks. Some creators share karaoke versions specifically for cover use.
MIDI Files
For piano and arrangement covers, MIDI files are invaluable. Community-transcribed MIDIs of OST tracks are available on sites like MuseScore and BitMidi. These files give you the note structure to build your own arrangement around, saving hours of ear-training work.
DAW Software
Digital Audio Workstations are the central hub for recording and producing covers. Free options like LMMS (music production) and Audacity (audio recording/editing) are fully capable for cover production. Paid options like FL Studio ($199, one-time) and Logic Pro ($199, Mac only) offer more advanced features and a smoother workflow.
Recording Tips
For vocal covers, room treatment matters more than microphone quality. Record in a closet full of clothes (the clothes absorb reverb). Position the microphone 6-12 inches from your mouth. Use a pop filter to reduce plosives. Apply light compression and reverb in post-production. Record multiple takes and comp the best parts.

Tip: When posting your cover, mention in the description whether you used the original instrumental, a karaoke version, or transcribed it yourself. Include the key you recorded in — this helps other musicians who want to collaborate. Tagging #LoveAndDeepspace #FanCover #PianoCover (or your instrument) improves discoverability. Many fan musicians also include links to sheet music or MIDI files they created, contributing back to the community that supported them.

Notable Fan Musicians

The Love and Deepspace fan music scene features creators who have built dedicated followings through consistent, high-quality output. Here are several who have made significant contributions to the community.

PIANIST — XPianoCovers
Piano Arrangements Specialist
XPianoCovers has produced piano arrangements for every operative theme plus the main title, abyssal battle tracks, and several memory card background pieces. Their style is clean, expressive, and faithful to the original while adding personal ornamental touches. They provide free sheet music PDF downloads for every arrangement, making them a cornerstone of the fan music community. Find them on YouTube and MuseScore.
VOCALIST — LunaSings
Vocal Cover Artist
LunaSings is known for ethereal, layered vocal covers of character themes with self-written lyrics in both English and Japanese. Her cover of "Stellar Radiance" (Xavier's theme) has over 200,000 views on YouTube. She frequently collaborates with piano arrangers, combining her vocals with their instrumental tracks. Her vocal range and emotive delivery have made her one of the most recognized voices in the fandom.
PRODUCER — DeepspaceBeats
Electronic Remix Producer
DeepspaceBeats specializes in electronic and lo-fi remixes of OST tracks. Their "Lofi Deepspace" series — study and relaxation mixes featuring slowed-down, reverbed versions of character themes — has become a staple of the community. They also produce original electronic tracks inspired by the game's aesthetic, blending ambient pads with rhythmic elements that evoke the game's blend of romance and combat.
MULTI-INSTRUMENTALIST — MelodyOfTides
Full Arrangement Creator
MelodyOfTides creates full multi-track arrangements of OST pieces, layering piano, strings, flute, and light percussion. Their arrangements of "Tidal Flame" (Rafayel) and "Frost Waltz" (Zayne) demonstrate exceptional production quality and arrangement creativity. They also produce tutorial content explaining their arrangement process, helping aspiring fan musicians develop their skills.

Collaborative Music Projects

Some of the most impressive fan music works come from collaborative projects that bring together multiple musicians. These projects showcase the community's ability to organize, coordinate, and create something greater than any individual could produce alone.

Community Cover Projects — Multiple singers each record a section of a song, which is then mixed into a single track. These projects typically focus on a single character theme, with each vocalist bringing their own interpretation to their assigned section. The final mix creates a rich, varied sound that highlights the diversity of voices in the community. Organizers typically recruit through Discord and Twitter, set vocal ranges and section assignments, mix and master the final track, and release on YouTube with credits for all participants.

Remix Competitions — Periodic competitions challenge producers to create original remixes of a specific OST track. Judges rate entries on creativity, production quality, and emotional impact. Winners receive community recognition and sometimes prizes from community sponsors. These competitions have produced some of the most innovative interpretations of the game's music and serve as a launchpad for new producers.

Fan Album Projects — The most ambitious collaborative format is the fan album, where a group of musicians collectively produce a full album of covers and original songs inspired by the game. Past fan albums have included themes like "Four Seasons of Love" (one track per operative per season) and "Memories of Deepspace" (musical interpretations of iconic memory card moments). Fan albums require significant organization — project managers, track producers, vocalists, mixing engineers, and cover artists for album art — but the results are extraordinary.

To join or start a collaborative music project: join the Love and Deepspace fan music Discord servers (invites are shared in YouTube video descriptions and Twitter posts), introduce yourself with your vocal range or instrumental specialty, follow project organizers who post recruitment calls, and be reliable — collaborative projects depend on every participant delivering their part on time. If you want to start your own project, begin small: a duet cover with one other musician teaches you the coordination workflow before you scale up to a 10-person ensemble piece.

Summary: The Love and Deepspace fan music community is a vibrant, multi-platform ecosystem of vocalists, instrumentalists, and producers. The most covered tracks include the Main Title theme (80+ covers), Xavier's "Stellar Radiance" (60+), Zayne's "Frost Waltz" (50+), Rafayel's "Tidal Flame" (45+), Sylus's "Shadow Sovereign" (40+), and the Abyssal Chaos Battle Theme (25+). Getting started is accessible with free tools like LMMS and Audacity, community-transcribed MIDI files, and karaoke tracks. Notable fan musicians like XPianoCovers, LunaSings, DeepspaceBeats, and MelodyOfTides produce regular content and often collaborate. Collaborative projects — community cover tracks, remix competitions, and fan albums — represent the community at its best. Whether you are a seasoned musician or a complete beginner, the fan music community welcomes your contribution.
