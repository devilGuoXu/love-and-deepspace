Theorycrafters & Analysts

Theorycrafters: The backbone of the Deepspace community. They test skill multipliers, calculate optimal rotations, and discover mechanics that even the developers might not have fully documented. Without theorycrafters, the meta would take months longer to develop.

⭐ DEEPSPACE_MATH (Reddit)
Protocore optimization — CRIT breakpoints — DPS formula derivation
The most referenced theorycrafter in the community. Deepspace_Math derived the damage formula that all DPS calculators are based on. Their protocore optimization guide is the gold standard — they proved that CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG should be balanced at a 1:2.5 ratio for optimal damage. They post detailed breakdowns with raw data, not just conclusions. Recommended reading: "Protocore Economics" series on Reddit.
⭐ MATH_WIZ (Discord)
Rotation optimization — Skill frame data — Energy modeling
Math_Wiz focuses on combat mechanics rather than stats. They discovered that Xavier's Light Seeker 4pc has a hidden internal cooldown of 0.5s per target (not per hit) — a detail the game does not explain. Their rotation modeling showed that optimal Xavier DPS comes from alternating basic attack strings with resonance skills, not spamming the highest-damage skill. They maintain the rotation database used by the team builder tools.
⭐ PROTOCORE_PRO (YouTube/Twitter)
Substat value rankings — Farming efficiency — Investment ROI
Protocore_Pro created the substat value ranking system: CRIT Rate (2.0), CRIT DMG (1.8), ATK% (1.5), ER% (1.0), Flat ATK (0.5), DEF%/HP% (0.2). These weights help players evaluate protocores at a glance. A protocore with CRIT Rate + CRIT DMG + ATK% has a value score of 5.3 — excellent. A protocore with HP% + DEF% has 0.4 — trash. Their farming efficiency guide maps which domains give the best substat distribution per stamina.

How theorycrafters work: Theorycrafters test mechanics through controlled experiments. They record gameplay footage, count frames, measure damage numbers, and control variables. A typical test: record 100 basic attacks, note the minimum and maximum damage, calculate the variance, and determine if the variance is due to CRIT or hidden mechanics. Results are shared in Google Sheets with raw data — not just conclusions. Trust theorycrafters who show their work, not those who state opinions as facts.

FEATURED PHOTOGRAPHER — LUMINA (Twitter/IG)
Lumina is known for cinematic Xavier portraits using the 3-point lighting system with custom color grading. Their signature style: warm key light, cool ambient fill, and a rim light that creates a glowing edge effect. Lumina discovered that setting the rim light to 80% intensity and positioning it 15° above the character's head creates a "divine glow" effect that mimics sunlight breaking through clouds. They offer free editing presets for the community.
FEATURED PHOTOGRAPHER — SHADOW_FRAME (Reddit)
Shadow_Frame specializes in Sylus photography with dark, moody aesthetics. Their technique: use low key light (40%), high rim light (90%), and position the camera below eye level for a heroic angle. They discovered that Sylus's dark outfit absorbs 90% of ambient light, requiring significantly higher fill light than lighter-clad characters. Their tutorials on "editing dark mode portraits without losing detail" are considered essential reading for aspiring photographers.
FEATURED PHOTOGRAPHER — AURORA_BLUE (Discord)
Aurora_Blue is the go-to resource for location scouting. They maintain a detailed map of all photo spots with in-game coordinates, best time of day, and recommended weather. They personally discovered 12 hidden locations that are not marked on any map. Their "Golden Hour Route" — a path through 6 locations that hits golden hour in each one sequentially — is the most shared photography resource in the community.

Get featured: If you want to be featured in future Creator Spotlight editions, post your work to the community channels with the tag #CreatorSpotlight. The selection committee looks for: original techniques, educational value, consistent quality, and willingness to share knowledge. Photography contest winners are automatically considered for the next spotlight.

Guide Writers

Guide writers distill complex mechanics into readable, actionable advice. The best guide writers combine theorycrafter data with practical experience — they do not just report what works, they explain why it works and how to adapt it.

NEW PLAYER GUIDES
Focus: Onboarding, resource management, starter teams
The best new player guide writer is "Haven_Tutorial" on YouTube. Their guide "From Zero to Abyss Floor 8 in 30 Days" is the most-watched guide in the community. They focus on actionable daily routines, not overwhelming information dumps. Their resource management calculator (free Google Sheet) helps new players plan their spending for the first 60 days.
ADVANCED GUIDES
Focus: Endgame optimization, protocore min-maxing, speedrun strats
"Endgame_Echo" writes the most detailed advanced guides on the subreddit. Their protocore enhancement guide includes statistical analysis of roll probabilities — they calculated that the chance of getting 3+ useful substat rolls on a single protocore is 12.7%. Their guides include confidence intervals and sample sizes, making them more scientific than typical gaming guides.
CHARACTER GUIDES
Focus: Per-character deep dives, build options, team comps
"Main_Xavier" maintains the definitive Xavier guide, updated every patch. The guide covers: all viable memory sets (4pc Light Seeker vs 2+2 comparisons), optimal protocore main stats per content type, skill rotation with frame data, and team composition options for every Abyss floor. The guide is 15+ pages in Google Docs and is cited by other content creators worldwide.

"I wrote my first guide because I kept seeing the same questions on Reddit. 'Which build is good for Xavier?' over and over. I realized the community needed a single definitive source, not fragmented comments. One guide, updated every patch, linked in every discussion. That guide now has over 50,000 views."

— Main_Xavier, Character Guide Author

Tool & Resource Creators

Tool creators build the infrastructure that the community relies on. Their work is often behind the scenes but enables everything from build sharing to resource planning.

BUILDER_DEV (Web Developer)
Team Builder Website — DPS Calculator — Build Export Tool
Builder_Dev maintains the most popular team builder website. They built it from scratch using React, with a database of all memories, protocores, and character data. The tool gets 10,000+ monthly active users. Builder_Dev updates the tool within 48 hours of every patch — new memories, protocores, and characters are added before most players even finish reading the patch notes. They accept community contributions through GitHub.
DATA_HUNTER (Data Miner)
Game data extraction — Hidden mechanics discovery — Datamining reports
Data_Hunter extracts game files to discover hidden mechanics that the developers do not document. Their discoveries include: exact skill multipliers (the game shows rounded numbers, Data_Hunter extracts precise values), hidden cooldown timers, and unobtainable enemy data. Their datamining reports are published on the subreddit with warnings about potential account risks. Data_Hunter does not datamine unreleased content — only existing mechanics that are poorly documented.
SHEET_KEEPER (Spreadsheet Creator)
Resource calculators — Upgrade planners — Achievement trackers
Sheet_Keeper maintains the community resource calculator — a Google Sheet that tracks all farmable resources, daily income, and optimal spending routes. The sheet includes: daily stamina planner, event resource calculator, protocore dust tracker, and a "build planner" that estimates total resource cost for any upgrade path. The sheet is updated every patch and shared freely. Version 4.0 added a "what if" simulator that predicts how long it takes to reach specific stat targets.

How to support tool creators: Tool creators rarely monetize their work — most are students or hobbyists who build tools for the love of the game. Support them by: (1) Reporting bugs constructively — "the team builder shows wrong ER for Xavier" is helpful; "your tool sucks" is not. (2) Contributing to open-source repositories — many tools accept pull requests for new features. (3) Sharing the tools with other players — word of mouth is a tool creator's only reward. (4) Sending a thank-you message — creators report that a single kind message motivates them for weeks.

Streamers & Video Creators

Video creators bring Deepspace to life through gameplay demonstrations, live theorycrafting, and entertainment content. They are often the first point of contact for new players discovering the community.

HAVEN_GAMING (YouTube — 50K+ subs)
Haven_Gaming produces weekly Deepspace content: Abyss floor clears with commentary, build showcases, and money-saving tips (free-to-play optimization series). Their signature series "Budget Builds" tests how far you can push under-invested characters. They cleared Abyss floor 12 with Xavier at R0 (no rank-ups) and level-40 protocores — proving that player skill matters more than stats. Their clear video has 200K+ views and spawned a "skill over stats" movement in the community.
LUMI_LIVE (Twitch — 5K+ average viewers)
Lumi_Live streams Deepspace 4x per week with a focus on viewer interaction. Their "Build Review Friday" segment invites viewers to share their builds on stream for live analysis. Lumi uses the team builder and DPS calculator on stream to evaluate builds in real-time. They have reviewed 500+ community builds and helped thousands of players improve. Their stream is known for positive energy — no toxicity, no elitism, just helpful advice.
PHOTO_OP (YouTube/IG — 20K+ followers)
Photo_Op creates photography tutorials and showcases. Their videos demonstrate specific techniques: "How to 3-Point Light Your Xavier in 60 Seconds" and "Editing Dark Mode Portraits Like a Pro." They also host the quarterly community photography contest with prizes (in-game currency giveaways sponsored by community donors). Their photography showcase videos are the most relaxing Deepspace content — set to lo-fi music with slow transitions between stunning screenshots.

Creator caution: Not all creators are trustworthy. Some promote "secret builds" that are mathematically inferior or recommend spending on low-value items. Cross-reference any creator's advice with the team builder or DPS calculator before following it. Red flags: creators who do not show their own stats, creators who claim "hidden mechanics" that are not documented anywhere else, creators who aggressively push spending. The best creators cite their sources and encourage you to verify their claims.

How to Support Creators

The community thrives on volunteer contributions. Supporting creators does not always mean money — most creators value engagement and appreciation more than donations.

1
Engage with content
Like, comment, share, and subscribe. Engagement metrics determine which content gets recommended to new players. A single share on Reddit can bring 1,000+ new readers to a guide. Comments with specific questions help creators understand what the community needs.
2
Provide constructive feedback
Creators improve through feedback. If a guide missed a detail, comment politely. If a tool has a bug, report it with steps to reproduce. If a streamer made an error, message them privately rather than calling them out publicly.
3
Contribute if you can
Can you code? Contribute to open-source tools. Can you write? Help update guides. Can you design? Create infographics. Every skill is valuable. The community runs on volunteer contributions — the more people contribute, the better the resources become.
4
Financial support (optional)
Some creators accept donations through Ko-fi, Patreon, or direct support. Only donate if you have disposable income and genuinely benefit from the creator's work. A one-time donation of $5 is more meaningful than a subscription that strains your budget. Creators appreciate the gesture, not the amount.
5
Pay it forward
The best way to support creators is to become one. When you learn enough to help others, write a guide, make a tool, or answer questions in the community. Every creator started as a learner. The community grows when knowledge is shared freely.
DO
  • Share creator content with new players
  • Report bugs constructively
  • Cross-reference advice before following it
  • Contribute skills if you have them
  • Say thank you — it matters more than you think
DON'T
  • Don't demand updates or content on a schedule
  • Don't spread rumors about creators
  • Don't follow advice without verifying
  • Don't pressure creators to datamine or leak
  • Don't compare creators — appreciate each for their unique contribution

Nominate a creator: Want to see someone featured in the next Creator Spotlight? Send a nomination to the community moderation team. Include: creator name, platforms they post on, what they create, and why they deserve recognition. Nominations are reviewed every patch cycle. Self-nominations are accepted — if you create Deepspace content and want visibility, tell us about your work.
