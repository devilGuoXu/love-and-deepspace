COMPANION GUIDE — INTERACTIONS

Base Interaction Guide

The Destiny Cafe is your home away from missions. This guide covers all base interactions, daily routines, and how to unlock every possible interaction.

ENTRY_#COM_032026.07.02INTERACTIONS

What Are Base Interactions: Base interactions are the idle animations, tap responses, and contextual dialogues that occur when you visit the Destiny Cafe. Each operative has 20+ unique interactions that unlock progressively through affinity levels and bond completions.

Interaction Types

The cafe supports four interaction categories: Tap Interactions: Tapping an operative triggers a voice line and animation. Different body parts trigger different responses. Head taps are affectionate. Shoulder taps are casual. Hand taps sometimes trigger holding animations. Each operative has 8-12 tap responses that rotate randomly. Proximity Interactions: Walking your avatar near an operative causes them to react. Some react positively (waving, smiling), others negatively (stepping away). Sylus is the only operative who does not react negatively — he always acknowledges you. Contextual Interactions: Certain actions trigger specific dialogues. Reading a book triggers a book-related comment. Checking your phone triggers a curious question. Drinking coffee triggers a comment about your caffeine habits. These are affinity-gated — higher affinity unlocks more contextual responses. Random Events: Occasionally, an operative will initiate an interaction unprompted. They might call your name, ask a question, or comment on something in the room. These events are rare and feel organic. They are the most rewarding interactions emotionally.

Interaction Unlock Progression

Interactions unlock in tiers based on affinity: Affinity 1-10: Basic tap responses (3-4 per operative). Standard proximity reactions. No contextual interactions. Affinity 11-30: Additional tap responses (2-3 more). First contextual interactions unlock. Random events begin (rare). Affinity 31-60: All standard tap responses unlocked. Advanced contextual interactions. Random events become more common. Affinity 61-100: Special tap responses unlocked (unique animations). Full contextual interaction set. Random events include gift-giving moments. Affinity 101-150: Exclusive interactions including unique poses, special greetings, and bond-specific dialogues that reference completed bond memories. The interaction system is designed to reward long-term investment. Players who reach high affinity with an operative are rewarded with increasingly personal and unique interactions that make the Destiny Cafe feel alive.

Daily Interaction Routine

Optimize your daily cafe visit with this routine: Step 1: Enter the Destiny Cafe. Wait 3 seconds for the operative to acknowledge you. Step 2: Walk around the operative in a circle (triggers proximity interaction). Step 3: Tap the operative 3 times on different body parts (head, shoulder, hand). Step 4: Interact with a cafe object (book, phone, coffee) to trigger contextual dialogue. Step 5: Exit and re-enter to check for random events. Total time: 2-3 minutes. Total XP: 30 XP (3 interactions x 10 XP) + potential bonus from random events. This routine should be done for your primary affinity operative daily. For secondary operatives, just do the 3 taps.

Hidden Interactions

Some interactions require specific conditions: Late Night Visit: Enter the cafe between 11 PM and 4 AM in-game time. Operatives have special sleepy dialogues. Rainy Weather: When the cafe window shows rain, operatives comment on the weather. Post-Reading: Immediately after completing a bond memory, visit the cafe. The operative references the bond. Gift Aftermath: After giving a disliked gift, the operative's next interaction reflects their mood. Wait 2 hours before visiting again. Anniversary: On the anniversary of your account creation, all operatives have special dialogue. These hidden interactions add depth to the cafe experience. They are not required for affinity grinding but are worth experiencing for the narrative value.

Cafe Customization: The Destiny Cafe can be customized with furniture and decorations unlocked through events and the shop. Different furniture layouts can trigger unique operative interactions. For example, placing a bookshelf near Xavier increases the chance of book-related contextual dialogues. Placing a fireplace near Zayne triggers warm-related comments. Furniture is purely cosmetic for interaction variety — it does not affect affinity gain. However, the aesthetic value and interaction variety make it worth investing in if you spend significant time in the cafe.

Summary: Base interactions in the Destiny Cafe include taps, proximity, contextual, and random events. Interactions unlock progressively through affinity levels (up to 150). Daily routine: 3 taps + proximity + contextual action = 30 XP. Hidden interactions exist for late night, rainy weather, post-bond, and post-gift scenarios. Customize furniture for interaction variety. The cafe is a living space that grows more interactive as your bond with each operative deepens.
