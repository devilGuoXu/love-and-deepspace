Standard Banner SSRs (Permanent)

These Sylus SSRs are available on the standard banner. Shadow Stalker (Void Set): ATK% main. Resonance: AoE dark damage + DEF shred (20%, 8s). Tier: A — excellent debuffer, fits many team comps. Nightshade Edge (Void Set): CRIT DMG% main. Resonance: single-target dark burst. Tier: B — decent damage, outclassed by limiteds. Dark Covenant (Veil Set): DEF% main. Resonance: party shield + taunt. Tier: C — support niche only. Standard banner tip: Shadow Stalker is Sylus's best standard — build it if you get it. Do not waste resources on DEF% main stat SSRs for Sylus unless you specifically need a tank build.

Limited SSRs (Event Banners)

Midnight Sovereign (Void 4-pc): Sylus's BiS. ATK% main. Unique: resonance marks enemies, marked targets take 25% more damage from all sources for 10s. Tier: S — enables the highest team DPS in the game. Must-pull. Shadow Dominion (Void Set): ATK% main. Resonance: line AoE dark damage + 40% ATK self-buff 10s. Tier: A — excellent burst DPS. Veil of Night (Veil Set): HP% main. Resonance: DEF-scaling explosion + large taunt. Tier: B — niche Veil build. Limited strategy: Midnight Sovereign is Sylus's must-pull. Budget 140 pulls for its rerun. Shadow Dominion is strong but depends on your team needs.

Event & Free SSRs

First Anniversary SSR (Void Set): ATK% main. Tier: A — good free placeholder for Midnight Sovereign. Valentine's SSR (Veil Set): HP% main. Tier: C — cosmetic value only. Battle Pass SSR (Void Set): ATK% main. Tier: A — identical to Shadow Stalker kit. Useful for second Void set in abyss.

Sylus Memory Strategy: Midnight Sovereign (S) > Shadow Dominion / Anniversary SSR (A) > Shadow Stalker (A) > Nightshade Edge (B). Sylus is the strongest pure DPS operative when equipped with his BiS. F2P: Anniversary SSR is the best free placeholder. The S-to-B gap is approximately 30% damage, making Midnight Sovereign a top-priority rerun target.

Set Activation

Void 4-pc: +35% dark damage when enemy HP is above 50%. Easy to maintain with Sylus's front-loaded burst. Veil 2+2: niche DEF-scaling build. Lunar + ATK% 2+2: F2P fallback, 15-18% weaker than Void 4-pc. Farm Void 4-pc as primary.

Summary: 3 standard, 3 limited, 3 free SSRs. Midnight Sovereign (S) is the must-pull — it enables the highest team DPS in the game. Void 4-pc is default best set. Budget 140 pulls for BiS. F2P: Anniversary SSR is functional placeholder. Sylus rewards investment more than any other operative.
