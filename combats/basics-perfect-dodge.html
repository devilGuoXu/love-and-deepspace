Perfect Dodge: Invincibility Frames

0.8 seconds. That's all you get. Make them count.

⚡ The Core Insight: Perfect dodge isn't just about avoiding damage. It's about creating opportunities. 0.8 seconds of invincibility. 30% slow-motion on enemies. And for Sylus players — 15% Resonance Energy refund. Master this, and you control the fight.

What Is a Perfect Dodge?

A perfect dodge triggers when you dodge at the exact moment an enemy's attack would hit you. Not before. Not after. Right when the attack connects.

The game gives you a small window — about 0.2 to 0.3 seconds before impact — to input the dodge. Hit it, and you're rewarded with:

✨ Invincibility Frames 0.8 seconds
⏸️ Enemy Slow-Motion 30% for 2.5 seconds
⚡ Resonance Energy (Sylus) +15% refund
🎯 Damage Window Free hits during slow-mo

The Frame Data

On a 60fps display, 0.8 seconds equals 48 frames of invincibility. That's enough to dodge through multi-hit attacks, reposition, and even counter-attack before the enemy recovers.

Why Perfect Dodge Matters

Most players think dodge is just for survival. That's wrong. Perfect dodge is an offensive tool.

🎯 The Real Value: The 30% slow-motion effect gives you a 2.5-second damage window. During this time, enemy attacks are slower, giving you more time to react. But more importantly — you can land your full combo without interruption.

In high-level Abyss floors, the difference between a normal dodge and a perfect dodge is often the difference between a clear and a wipe. Normal dodge just moves you. Perfect dodge moves you, protects you, and sets up your next attack.

Normal Dodge Moves you. No i-frames. No bonus.
Perfect Dodge 0.8s invincibility + 30% slow + potential energy refund

How to Train Your Dodge Timing

Perfect dodge is a skill. You can't read about it — you have to practice it. Here's how.

STEP 1 — LEARN THE TELLS
Every enemy has a visual or audio cue before attacking. The flash. The roar. The wind-up. Memorize these. They're your trigger.
STEP 2 — DODGE INTO THE ATTACK
Counter-intuitive, but true. Dodging toward the attack often gives you better i-frame coverage than dodging away. The hitbox is smaller at the origin point.
STEP 3 — PRACTICE ON EASY ENEMIES
Don't learn on Floor 15 bosses. Go to early floors. Find an enemy with a slow, telegraphed attack. Practice until you can perfect dodge it 10 times in a row.
STEP 4 — LISTEN FOR THE SOUND
Perfect dodge has a distinct audio cue — a "whoosh" sound. Use it as feedback. If you don't hear it, you were early or late.
STEP 5 — RECORD AND REVIEW
Record your gameplay. Watch in slow motion. Count the frames between the enemy's tell and your dodge. Adjust accordingly.

Common Dodge Mistakes

❌ Mistake #1: Spamming dodge Multiple dodges don't give multiple i-frames. You get one window. Time it right, or you're vulnerable.

❌ Mistake #2: Dodging away from enemies Dodging away keeps you safe but puts you out of position to counter-attack. Dodge into or sideways to stay in range.

❌ Mistake #3: Ignoring audio cues The "whoosh" sound tells you if you succeeded. If you're not hearing it, you're not perfect dodging.

❌ Mistake #4: Panic dodging When you see a big attack coming, your instinct is to dodge immediately. Wait. Watch the tell. Dodge at the last possible moment.
