Abyssal Trials: Floor 6

The twins boss fight — two elites that share a health pool and attack in sync.

ENTRY_#1332026.06.30FLOOR_6
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Floor 6 Threshold: Recommended power level 30,000. Single team. Twins boss — two elites sharing a health bar. If one dies, the other enrages. You must kill them within 5 seconds of each other.

Boss — Twin Harbingers

The Twin Harbingers are two elite Wanderers that share a single health pool. Each twin has its own attack pattern and aggro table. One twin is melee-focused (close range slashes), the other is ranged (energy bolts and AoE placement). They coordinate attacks — when one uses a heavy attack, the other follows up with a cross-attack 1.5 seconds later.

Critical mechanic: The twins must die within 5 seconds of each other. If one dies first, the remaining twin enrages: +100% damage, +50% attack speed, and gains a new AoE attack. An enraged twin will wipe your team in 10-15 seconds. You must bring both HP bars to near-zero before finishing them.

Positioning Strategy

Stay between the twins — center of the arena. This positioning lets you see both attack telegraphs clearly. When the melee twin charges, dodge towards the ranged twin (the charge has a narrow hitbox). When the ranged twin marks an AoE circle, step out of it while staying in center position.

Do not chase one twin. The melee twin will come to you. Focus damage on the ranged twin when possible — she is squishier and her AoE attacks are more disruptive.

Kill Window Execution

Bring both twins to approximately 5% HP each. Use an AoE ultimate or resonance chain to kill both simultaneously. Xavier's Light Seeker resonance has the widest AoE coverage. If using single-target DPS, manually swap targets to even out HP. The HP bar is shared visually but each twin has an independent remaining HP pool.

Rewards

First clear: 150 diamonds, 5 Advanced Resonance Crystals, 10 Purple EXP Books. Weekly reset: 50 diamonds. 3-star clear bonus: 70 diamonds.

Team Composition for Twin Harbingers

The best team composition for Floor 6 includes one AoE DPS operative and one single-target DPS operative with a support filler. Xavier with Light Seeker set is the strongest choice for this floor because his Resonance skill has the widest AoE coverage of any operative, capable of hitting both twins simultaneously when they are within 8 meters of each other. Rafayel is a strong second choice — his burn DoT ticks on both twins independently, which naturally balances their HP bars because each twin takes burn damage based on proximity.

Avoid using Sylus as the primary DPS on this floor. Sylus's execute mechanic becomes a liability because executing one twin below 30% HP triggers the enrage mechanic. If Sylus's execute accidentally kills one twin at 30% HP instead of the intended 5%, you must restart the floor. If you must bring Sylus, manually control his Resonance usage and save Ardent Oath for the final kill window rather than using it on cooldown. Zayne as support is recommended for his freeze control, which can temporarily disable one twin while you adjust HP balance.

HP Balancing Techniques

Balancing the twins' HP bars requires active target management. The shared health bar display in the UI shows the combined HP, not individual HP. To track each twin's individual HP, watch the damage numbers — each twin displays its damage separately above its head. A practical technique is to focus the ranged twin with single-target attacks while letting AoE splash damage naturally reduce the melee twin's HP. If the melee twin's HP drops too low, swap your focus to the ranged twin exclusively until the bars even out.

For the final kill window, stop all damage when the first twin reaches approximately 8% HP. Switch to basic attacks only while monitoring the second twin's HP bar. When both twins are between 3-8% HP, use an AoE resonance or Ardent Oath to finish them simultaneously. Xavier's Resonance with Light Seeker set at five stacks deals enough burst damage to kill both twins from 8% HP in a single cast. If your first AoE attempt leaves one twin alive with a sliver of HP, use a quick basic attack to finish it — the 5-second window starts from the first twin's death, not from the kill attempt.

Key Insight: The 5-second kill window is the hardest part. If you kill one too early, restart. Use a save point macro or pause to check HP bars. A well-timed Xavier or Rafayel AoE ultimate is the easiest way to finish both simultaneously.

Summary: Twins share HP, must die within 5s. Stay center, focus ranged twin first. AoE ultimate for simultaneous kill. 30,000 power threshold.
