STYLE GUIDE — PROFILE

Avatar & Profile Customization Guide

Your profile is your identity in Love and Deepspace — the first thing other players see and the canvas on which you display your achievements, taste, and dedication. This guide covers every element of profile customization and how to build a profile that represents you.

The most impressive profiles are not those with the rarest individual items but those where every element — frame, badge, title, avatar — tells a coherent story about the player's journey, priorities, and taste.

Profile Customization System Overview

The profile customization system in Love and Deepspace consists of several interdependent elements that combine to create your visible player identity. Understanding how these elements work together is the first step to building an impressive profile.

The Profile Canvas. Your profile is displayed as a composite image: a central avatar (typically a cropped image of your favorite operative or Hunter memory card art), surrounded by an avatar frame that adds decorative borders and effects. Below or beside the avatar, badges display your achievements and participation in various game activities. Your title appears as text beneath your name, communicating your chosen identity. The background or namecard provides the foundation for the entire composition.

Layered Expression. Each element of the profile communicates different information. The avatar shows your favorite character or memory. The frame signals your dedication (event-limited frames are status symbols). Badges display your activities and achievements. The title communicates your current focus or identity. Together, these elements create a portrait of you as a player — what you value, how long you have played, and what you have accomplished.

Collection and Rotation. Profile elements are collected over time through various activities. Most players accumulate more elements than they can display at once, creating opportunities for curation and rotation. Changing your profile seasonally, per event, or based on your current focus keeps your presentation fresh and shows ongoing engagement.

Avatar Frames Complete Catalog

Avatar frames are the most visible and prestigious profile element. A rare frame signals veteran status, event participation, or significant achievement.

Standard Frames. Basic frames are unlocked through account progression — reaching certain Hunter levels, completing story chapters, or achieving basic milestones. These frames are accessible to all players and serve as the foundation of frame collection. They are simple designs that frame the avatar without competing for attention.

Event-Limited Frames. The most desirable frames are tied to specific events and are never obtainable again after the event ends. These frames feature unique designs — animated effects, character-specific motifs, event-themed decorations — that make them status symbols. A player wearing an event-limited frame from six months ago signals that they have been playing consistently and participated in that specific event.

Battle Pass Frames. Each battle pass season includes an exclusive frame available at the premium tier. These frames are less rare than event-limited frames (the battle pass returns regularly with new frames) but still signal that the player invests in the game. Battle pass frames often coordinate with the season's theme and outfit designs.

Ranked/Arena Frames. High-ranked PvP participation is rewarded with exclusive frames that signal competitive achievement. These frames are among the most difficult to obtain, requiring significant skill and investment in the combat system. A high-tier arena frame communicates both dedication and combat proficiency.

Animated Frames. The pinnacle of frame design, animated frames feature moving elements — flowing particles, shifting colors, or character cameos that cycle through animations. These frames are almost exclusively limited-edition and are the most sought-after profile elements in the game.

Profile Badges & Achievements

Badges are smaller decorative elements that accompany your avatar, displaying specific achievements or participation in game activities.

Event Participation Badges. Most major events include a participation badge awarded for completing specific event milestones. These badges commemorate the event and show that you were present. Collecting all event badges over time creates a visual timeline of your game journey.

Character Affinity Badges. Reaching certain affinity levels with each operative unlocks dedicated badges. These badges signal your dedication to specific characters and are often among the most personal profile choices — selecting a Rafayel badge communicates a different identity than selecting a Sylus badge.

Achievement Badges. Completion of difficult achievements — max-leveling certain equipment, completing challenge stages, reaching collection milestones — is rewarded with badges that signal player capability. These badges are respected because they represent genuine achievement rather than time-limited participation.

Seasonal Badges. Holiday events and seasonal celebrations typically include themed badges. These badges add variety to your profile rotation and allow for seasonal styling — using a winter-themed badge during December, a beach badge during summer events.

Titles & Nameplates

Titles are text-based identifiers that appear beneath your name on your profile and in certain game screens. They offer a different kind of expression: linguistic rather than visual.

Title Categories. Titles fall into several thematic categories: combat achievements (relating to battle performance or rankings), character relationship (tied to affinity with specific operatives), collection milestones (relating to memory or outfit collection), event participation (commemorating specific events), and humorous/easter egg titles (lighter options for personality expression).

Title Rarity. Like frames, titles vary in rarity. The rarest titles are those tied to limited-time events or high-tier competitive achievements. Some titles are so rare that seeing another player with the same title creates an immediate sense of shared experience or mutual respect.

Title Coordination. The best profiles coordinate title meaning with frame, badge, and avatar choices. A combat-focused title pairs with a ranked arena frame and a badge from a difficult achievement. A character-affinity title pairs with that character's event frame and affinity badge. This coordination creates a profile that tells a clear, intentional story.

Avatar Selection & Coordination

The avatar itself is the centerpiece of your profile. While frames, badges, and titles surround and support it, the avatar is what draws the eye first.

Memory Card Art Avatars. The most common avatar source is memory card art — cropped images from the game's memory illustrations. These avatars are inherently attractive because they use the game's best artwork. Choosing a specific memory card art avatar can signal your favorite memory, character, or story moment.

Photo Mode Avatars. Avatars created from your own photo mode captures offer the most personal expression. A well-composed photo mode portrait of your Hunter with a favorite operative creates a unique avatar that no other player can duplicate. This is the pinnacle of avatar personalization.

Avatar Cropping Considerations. Avatar frames crop images into specific shapes (typically circular or rounded square). When selecting or creating an avatar, consider how the framing will affect the composition. Faces should be centered and appropriately sized — too close and the frame clips the image unpleasantly; too far and the subject becomes lost in the frame.

Avatar and Frame Coordination. The avatar and frame should work together as a single visual unit. A dark-frame pairs best with a well-lit avatar that provides contrast. A busy, decorated frame calls for a simpler avatar composition. A minimalist frame allows more complex avatar imagery. Test combinations before finalizing your profile selection.

Profile Curation Strategy

Building and maintaining an impressive profile requires intentional curation — not just collecting elements but selecting which ones to display and when.

The Coherence Principle. The most important rule of profile curation is coherence. Every element should relate to a central theme or identity. A profile that displays a Rafayel affinity badge, a combat achievement title, an event participation frame, and a random memory card avatar tells no clear story. A profile with all Rafayel-themed elements tells a specific story about the player's dedication to that character.

Rotation Schedule. Consider changing your profile on a regular schedule: when new events begin (swap to event-themed elements), when you achieve something significant (highlight the achievement), when seasons change (seasonal themes), or when you reach new affinity milestones with a character. Regular rotation shows active engagement and gives you opportunities to show off new acquisitions.

Status Signaling. Be aware of what your profile communicates. A profile with all standard elements suggests a new or casual player. A profile with one rare element and otherwise standard elements suggests a player who values that specific achievement. A profile with all rare, coordinated elements signals a dedicated, high-investment player. Choose the signal that matches your identity.

Minimalist vs. Maximalist Approaches. Both approaches can be effective. A minimalist profile uses simple frames, a single meaningful badge, and understated titles — the focus is on the avatar and the overall clean presentation. A maximalist profile uses elaborate frames, multiple badges, and prominent titles — the focus is on demonstrating collection depth and achievement breadth. Neither is superior; choose the approach that reflects your personality.

Rarest Profile Elements

Certain profile elements are so rare that they function as the ultimate status symbols. Knowing which elements are rarest helps you prioritize acquisition when opportunities arise.

Day-1 Player Frames. Frames awarded only to players who created accounts during the launch window are among the rarest elements in the game. These frames cannot be obtained by any current means. A player wearing a launch frame signals that they have been with the game since the beginning.

Top-100 Competitive Frames. Frames awarded to the top 100 players in ranked competitive events are extraordinarily rare. Only 100 players in the entire server population possess each of these frames, making them the ultimate status symbol for competitive players.

Max-Affinity Badges. Badges awarded for reaching maximum affinity with an operative require immense investment over months of gameplay. These badges signal both dedication and resource commitment that few players achieve.

Limited Collaboration Elements. Elements tied to real-world collaborations or special promotions are rare because they require external participation beyond the game itself. These elements are unpredictable in their availability and often limited to specific regions.

Curation Exercise: Take a screenshot of your current profile. Write down three words that describe what it communicates. If those words do not match how you want to be seen by other players, it is time to curate your profile elements toward a more intentional presentation.

Summary: Profile customization in Love and Deepspace is a layered status system where every element — avatar, frame, badge, title — contributes to your visible identity. Avatar frames are the most prestigious element, with event-limited and animated frames being the most desirable. Badges display achievements and participation, while titles offer linguistic expression. The key to an impressive profile is coherence: all elements should tell the same story about who you are as a player. Regular rotation keeps profiles fresh and showcases new acquisitions. The rarest profile elements — launch frames, top-100 competitive frames, max-affinity badges — function as ultimate status symbols. Whether you choose minimalist or maximalist presentation, intentional curation creates profiles that stand out.
