Summoner & Swarm Wanderers

Summoners actively create new wanderers during combat, overwhelming players who lack AoE or fail to prioritize correctly. A single unchecked summoner can turn a manageable fight into an unwinnable swarm.

ENTRY_#WDR_142026.07.11SUMMONER

Core Identity: Summoners actively create new wanderers during combat, overwhelming players who lack AoE or fail to prioritize correctly. Every second a summoner lives, the fight gets harder. Rush them down or drown in adds.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Variant A — Rift Caller

Appearance: Floating orb surrounded by crackling dimensional energy. HP: Low. Ability: Dimensional Rift — opens a portal that spawns 1 Void Spawn every 4s. Max 6 active portals. If left unchecked, a Rift Caller can flood the arena with 15+ adds. Strategy: Kill Rift Caller FIRST every time. It has low HP — one combo kills it. If you cannot reach it (shielded or behind enemies), use AoE to clear the adds it already spawned. Xavier's teleport-strike is excellent for closing the gap. Weakness: Light, any interrupt during portal cast.

PORTAL Each active portal glows with a purple rift. Destroying the Rift Caller closes all portals instantly. Max portal count: 6.

Variant B — Brood Mother

Appearance: Large, bulbous wanderer with a pulsating abdomen. HP: High. Ability: Spawn Hatchlings — releases 4 hatchlings every 10s. Hatchlings are very low HP (one hit kills) but explode on death if not killed quickly enough (2s timer). Explosion = 200 AoE Dark damage. Strategy: Don't let hatchlings stack up. AoE clear them as soon as they spawn. Killing them before they explode is critical. Position yourself so hatchlings spawn between you and the Brood Mother — use their spawn as a shield against other enemies. Brood Mother has no other attacks — it is purely a spawner. Weakness: Fire (hatchlings are extra weak to fire), AoE damage.

TIMER Hatchling death explosion timer: 2s. Kill all hatchlings within 2s of spawn to avoid 200 AoE Dark damage each. Fire-element skills clear them fastest.

Variant C — Phantom Weaver

Appearance: Distorted clone of an operative (corrupted version). HP: Medium. Ability: Clone — creates a perfect copy of itself that has 50% of its stats. The clone mimics the original's attacks. Every 20s, if the clone is still alive, it creates another clone. This can cascade. Strategy: This is a DPS race. Burn the original Phantom Weaver as fast as possible. When clones exist, use AoE to damage both original and clone simultaneously. Do not let clones live long enough to create sub-clones. Rafayel's burn DoT is excellent — it damages both original and clone. Weakness: AoE, Light (clones take double Light damage).

CASCADE Each active clone spawns another every 20s. A single unchecked clone leads to exponential growth. Break the chain by focusing the original or clearing clones with wide AoE.

Summoner-type wanderers demand a fundamentally different approach than standard enemies. You cannot out-sustain a summoner — the maths simply does not work. Every 4s from a Rift Caller adds another body to the field. Every 10s from a Brood Mother adds 4 more. The exponential pressure means that defensive play is a losing strategy. The only winning move is aggressive, targeted elimination of the summoner itself.

All summoners share a critical weakness: their spawn abilities can be interrupted. A stagger, knockback, or stun during the cast animation cancels the spawn entirely and triggers a 3-5s cooldown before they can attempt again. This is especially valuable against the Brood Mother, whose hatchling spawn has a lengthy 1.5s cast animation with a visible abdomen glow telegraph.

Pro Tip: In Abyss floors with summoner enemies, always check the spawn order in the pause menu. If a summoner appears in wave 2, save your Ardent Oath for wave 3 when the adds have accumulated. One well-placed Oath can clear the entire board of spawned units and leave the summoner exposed for a kill.

Element Weakness Table

Light (Xavier): 1.5x damage, interrupts all summoner cast animations. Fire (Rafayel): 1.3x damage, burn DoT ticks on both summons and summoner simultaneously. Ice (Zayne): 1.0x, freeze stops spawns for duration. Dark (Sylus): 1.0x, execute works on all summoners below 30% HP. Recommended element for summoner floors: Light with a Fire sub-DPS for hatchling clearing.

Swarm Control Priority

Single Summoner: Ignore adds entirely. Rush the summoner. One stun rotation kills Rift Caller and Phantom Weaver. Brood Mother requires two rotations — clear hatchlings in the gap. Multiple Summoners: Kill Rift Callers first (fastest spawn rate, lowest HP), then Phantom Weavers (cascade risk), then Brood Mothers (slowest spawn rate). Summoner + Elite: Clear the elite first only if the elite has one-shot potential. Otherwise, the summoner must die first — the swarm pressure overtakes the elite's damage over time.

Summary: Summoners are force multipliers — kill them before their army materializes. Priority: Rift Caller > Phantom Weaver > Brood Mother. Interrupt spawn casts with Light damage or any stagger. AoE is your best friend for add management. A dead summoner is a safe arena.
