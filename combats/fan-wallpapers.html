Official Wallpaper Sources

Papergames releases official wallpapers through several channels: Game Client — Loading Screens: The game's loading screens feature high-resolution artwork of the operatives. These are often the highest quality official images available. You can find them extracted and shared on fan sites. Official Twitter/X: @LoveDeepspace_EN posts new artwork with every major update. These are typically in 1920x1080 resolution. Check the media tab for all posted images. Official Website: The Love and Deepspace official website has a media section with download links for key artwork. Resolution varies from 1920x1080 to 4K. Official YouTube: Video thumbnails and chapter markers often feature unique artwork not available elsewhere. Screenshot these for exclusive wallpapers. Promo Materials: Collaboration promos, convention banners, and merchandise previews often feature exclusive art. These are harder to find but worth seeking out for collectors. Estimated count: Approximately 50-60 unique official wallpapers have been released since launch. New ones are added with every patch.

Fan-Made Wallpaper Sources

The fan community creates thousands of wallpapers. The best sources: Pixiv: The primary platform for high-quality Deepspace fan art. Search tags: "ラブアンドディープスペース" (Japanese), "Love and Deepspace," and individual character names. Many artists allow wallpaper use. Check each artist's profile for usage terms. Pinterest: Curated boards with collections of Deepspace wallpapers. Search for "Love and Deepspace wallpaper" and "Deepspace mobile wallpaper." Quality varies but curation is good. Reddit (r/LoveAndDeepspace): Fans regularly post wallpaper edits, fan art, and extracted game assets. Sort by top of all time for the best collections. DeviantArt: Established artists post high-resolution Deepspace work. Good for desktop wallpapers. Twitter: Fan artists share work under hashtags like #LoveAndDeepspaceFanArt, #DeepspaceArt.

Device Resolution Guide

Mobile (Phone): Standard: 1080x1920 or 1170x2532 (iPhone). Most fan art is created at these resolutions. Look for portrait orientation images. Tablet: 2048x2732 (iPad Pro), 1600x2560 (Android tablets). Tablet wallpapers are rarer but exist. Desktop (16:9): 1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3840x2160 (4K). Official art is usually at 1920x1080. Fan artists often provide 4K versions. Desktop (Ultrawide 21:9): 3440x1440. Rare — may need to be cropped from 16:9 sources or use tiling. Dual Monitor: 3840x1080 or 5120x1440. Very rare. Consider using a single high-res image stretched across both monitors. Tip: When downloading wallpapers, prefer the highest available resolution. Downscaling looks better than upscaling. Use image upscaling tools (waifu2x, ESRGAN) for low-res images if needed.

Wallpaper Editing Tips: (1) Most official artwork has logos and text. Use image editing software (Photoshop, GIMP, free online tools) to remove text for a clean wallpaper. (2) Consider color balance. Xavier and Zayne have cool color palettes (blue, white, purple). Rafayel and Sylus have warm palettes (red, orange, gold). Match your wallpaper to your device's aesthetic. (3) For mobile: ensure the most important elements (faces, focal points) are in the top or center third of the image — not where your app icons will cover them. (4) For desktop: taskbar covers the bottom. Keep important elements above the bottom 10% of the image. (5) Dark mode users: look for wallpapers with dark backgrounds. Bright wallpapers can make text hard to read. (6) Animated wallpapers: use apps like Wallpaper Engine (Steam) for animated Deepspace wallpapers. Search the Workshop for community-created animated versions.
