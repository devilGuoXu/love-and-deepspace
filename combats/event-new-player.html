New Player Event Priority

As a new player, you face a unique problem: too many events, too little stamina. Every event tempts you. Most are traps for new accounts. Here is how to filter.

ENTRY_#EVT_092026.07.02NEW_PLAYER

Core Problem: A new player's stamina (240/day) must cover story progression, resource domains, AND event farming. You cannot do everything. The game's event system is designed to overwhelm new players into buying stamina pots. Resist the FOMO. Not every event is mandatory for a new account.

Event Types — Ranked for New Players

Every event type has a different value for new accounts. Here is how to prioritize them.

1
Story Events with SSR Shards
These are the only events that directly give you an SSR memory. SSR memories are the most important account progression metric. If a story event is running, you farm it. Period. Stamina split: 60% event, 40% story/main quests. You need the main story unlocked to access higher-tier event stages anyway.
2
Double Drop Events
Double drops are 2x efficiency. As a new player, your resource shortage is acute. Double drops are the only time you should spend ALL your stamina on a single domain. Even if you are in the middle of story progression, pause it for double drops.
3
Mini-Game Events
Zero stamina cost. Zero gear check. Free diamonds. Always do these, even on day 1. They are the only event type that does not compete with your main progression stamina. 45 minutes = 800-1200 free diamonds.
4
Boss Challenge Events
Skip if you are under 2 weeks old. The rewards are not worth the frustration. Your gear is not ready. Come back when you have level 50+ memories. The login rewards alone are worth claiming — just don't stress about score ranks.
5
Seasonal Events (outfit-focused)
Skip if you are F2P and under 1 month old. Outfits do not help progression. The free login rewards are enough — do not spend stamina on cosmetic events. Your priority is building a foundation, not looking good.

Stamina Allocation for First 30 Days

Your stamina allocation changes dramatically as your account matures. Follow this phased approach.

DAYS 1-7
80% Story Quests
You need to unlock domains and abyss. 20% resource domains (only if you hit a wall). Zero event farming unless SSR shards are at stake.
DAYS 8-14
50% Story / 30% Domains / 20% Events
Story progression continues. Resource domains (XP and dust alternating) to level your main team. You should be able to clear event story stages now (they are easy).
DAYS 15-30
30% Story / 40% Events / 30% Protocore
Story catch-up continues. The main event shop is now your priority. Protocore domains become important — your memories are leveled, now fix your gear.

Do NOT level more than 2 operatives in the first 30 days. Spreading resources thin is the #1 new player mistake. Pick 2 operatives, level them to 50, and ignore everyone else. A focused roster beats a wide one every time.

Banner Pulling for New Players

Pulling on banners is the most exciting — and most dangerous — activity for new accounts.

First 30 days pull rule: Pull on the beginner banner (20 pulls guaranteed SSR). Pull on the current limited banner if the rate-up character is one of your 2 chosen operatives. Otherwise, SKIP. Save your pulls for a banner that has a character you actually use.

Mistake to avoid: Pulling on every banner "because FOMO." You will end up with 5 different R0 characters and no strong team. 2 R1 characters at level 60 clear more content than 5 R0 characters at level 40. Focus is power.

Pro Tip: The most powerful thing a new player can do is read event descriptions in the official news. Most events have a "recommended power level" listed. If your account is below the recommended power, skip the combat stages and do only the story/login parts. You will survive without the challenge stage rewards. The login rewards alone are usually worth 200+ free diamonds per event — claim those and ignore the rest until you are ready.

The 30-Day Rule

Every event in Deepspace will return eventually. SSR memories enter the standard pool after ~6 months. Outfits may return for rerun events. If you are under 30 days old, your job is not to clear every event — your job is to build a foundation.

A strong foundation (2 operatives at 50+, correct protocores, 1 main team) lets you clear ALL future events efficiently. A weak foundation (5 operatives at 30, no protocores, no main team) means every event is a struggle. Invest in your foundation first. Events are temporary; your operative levels are permanent.

DO & DON'T Summary

DO
  • Prioritize story events with SSR shards above all else
  • Pause story progression for double drop events
  • Complete mini-games — they cost zero stamina
  • Pick 2 operatives and level only them for 30 days
  • Pull on beginner banner and only banners matching your operatives
  • Claim login rewards even if you skip the combat stages
DON'T
  • Don't farm boss challenges before 2 weeks
  • Don't spend stamina on cosmetic seasonal events
  • Don't level more than 2 operatives
  • Don't pull on every banner out of FOMO
  • Don't skip story quests for events (unlock domains first)
  • Don't ignore the recommended power level in event descriptions

Summary: Story events (SSR) > Double Drops > Mini-Games > Boss Challenges > Cosmetics. 60/40 stamina split in favor of events only if SSR shards are on the line. Pick 2 operatives and ignore everyone else. Do not pull on banners that don't match your chosen operatives. Your foundation beats event FOMO every time.
