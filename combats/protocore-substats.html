🔴 The Core Insight — I Learned This the Hard Way: For my first two months, I salvaged every protocore that didn't have the "perfect" main stat. I was throwing away god rolls. A +15 protocore with a "wrong" main stat but perfect substats is almost always better than a +0 protocore with the "right" main stat and garbage substats. Substats scale. Main stats don't.

The Mistake — What Everyone Does Wrong

Let me paint you a picture. You're farming protocores. You get a Triangle with HP% main stat. You sigh. You salvage it immediately.

But what if that same protocore had:

  • CRIT Rate +6%
  • CRIT DMG +12%
  • ATK% +8%
  • Energy Regen +10%

You just threw away a god roll because you were married to the "perfect" main stat.

⚠️ I've done this. Hundreds of times. Don't be me. The main stat is ONE number. Substats are FOUR numbers that roll up to FIVE times each. The math isn't close.

The Experiment — Same Protocore, Different Levels
"Perfect" Main Stat CRIT Rate
+0: 33% CRIT Rate | Substats: flat DEF, flat HP, HP%
"Wrong" Main Stat HP%
+15: 46% HP | Substats: CRIT Rate 9%, CRIT DMG 18%, ATK% 12%
Result: The "wrong" protocore gives +30% effective damage. The "perfect" one gives +0% damage. Substats won.

💡 The Math: A main stat at +15 is worth the same as at +0. A substat can increase by 40-60% over the same levels. At +15, your substats are worth more than your main stat on most protocores.

The Substats That Matter — Tier List

S Tier — Game Changers
CRIT Rate · CRIT DMG · Energy Regen (for Sylus/Zayne)
These determine your damage ceiling. Prioritize them above all else.
A Tier — Very Good
ATK% · Elemental DMG%
Solid damage increases. Take them if S tier isn't available.
B Tier — Fine
ATK (flat) · Energy Regen (for others)
Better than nothing. Usable while farming for better.
F Tier — Dead Stats
DEF · DEF% · HP · HP%
Zayne doesn't scale with these. Salvage immediately.

⚠️ Exception: If a character scales with HP or DEF (rare), those stats move up. But for 99% of builds, HP/DEF are dead substats.

The "Wrong" Main Stat That Can Be Good

Some main stats are considered "wrong" but are actually fine — sometimes even optimal.

💡 The Rule: Before salvaging any protocore, ask: "Are the substats good?" If yes, keep it. Level it. The main stat is secondary.

How to Evaluate a Protocore — Step by Step

1
Look at the substats first. Ignore the main stat initially.
2
Count how many of the substats are S/A tier. If you have 3+, keep it.
3
Level to +3. If the roll hits a good substat, continue. If it hits a dead stat, consider salvaging.
4
At +9, you should have at least 3 good substats. If not, cut your losses.
5
At +15, substats > main stat. Don't regret the investment.

Common Mistakes — The RNG Trap List

❌ Salvaging based on main stat only
I did this for months. So many god rolls lost.
❌ Not leveling protocores to check substats
You can't see all substats at +0. Level to +3 or +6 before deciding.
❌ Stopping at +0 because "main stat is wrong"
Wrong. Level it. See what substats reveal themselves.
❌ Using +0 protocores over +15 "bad" ones
A +15 protocore with bad stats is still better than a +0 with perfect main stat.
❌ Giving up too early
Farming protocores is a marathon. Keep going. The god roll will come.
