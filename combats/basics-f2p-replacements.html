Best SR replacements for missing SSR memories — power loss estimates and optimal F2P build targets.

Replacement Math

Understanding the difference between SR and SSR at various awakening levels is the key to building efficiently. R0 SSR baseline: 100% power. R3 SR: ~90-95% of R0 SSR power in the same role. R5 SR: ~100-110% of R0 SSR power (some SR memories outperform base SSR at max awakening). R3 SR vs R1 SSR: R1 SSR is about 15-20% stronger than R3 SR. The gap widens at higher awakening tiers. Key insight: An R3 SR on a correct stellactrum color is better than an R0 SSR off-color. Stellactrum penalties are severe enough that color matching matters more than rarity in most cases.

SR Tier List

Not all SR memories are created equal. Some have excellent passives that make them almost as good as SSR in their role. S-Tier SR: Memories with team-wide energy regen or ATK buffs. These are always worth leveling. A-Tier SR: Single-target damage SR with good multipliers. Serve as solid DPS replacements. B-Tier SR: Situational SR with niche uses (specific elements, specific team compositions). F-Tier SR: SR with poor stat scaling or useless passives. Only level if you literally have no other option. General advice: Check the memory's passive description before investing. A good passive can make an SR outperform a bad SSR.

Optimal Build Targets

Distribution priority: Your SSRs should go to the most impactful slots (main DPS memories, tank memories). Your SRs fill the remaining slots. Target split for F2P: 4 SSRs (main DPS + support + second DPS + flex) + 2 R3 SRs (stellactrum fillers). This composition can clear all content up to Abyss floor 80. SR awakening target: Aim for R3 on the SR you use most. R3 is the sweet spot for SR — gives good power without the steep material cost of R4-5. Do not use awakening gems on SR past R3 unless that SR is a permanent fixture in your endgame team.

Power Loss Estimates

Here is what using SR instead of SSR costs you per memory slot. One SR slot: 10-15% damage loss on that operative. Two SR slots (same operative): 20-25% damage loss. This is significant — try not to run two SR on the same character. Three SR slots: 30%+ loss. This is where content becomes noticeably harder. One SR on support: 5-10% loss. Support memories have less impact on overall team damage. Practical advice: Focus your limited SSR acquisition on covering your main DPS first. A main DPS with two SR memories is crippled. A support with SR memories is fine. Color matching matters more. A well-matched SR is better than an off-color SSR.

Budget Team Compositions

Hypercarry Composition (F2P): Main DPS with 2 SSRs + 1 R3 SR. Support with 1 SSR + 2 R3 SRs. Flex slot with 1 SSR. This is 4 SSR + 3 R3 SR total — the standard F2P endgame target. Dual DPS Composition (F2P): DPS A with 2 SSRs + 1 R3 SR. DPS B with 2 SSRs + 1 R3 SR. Support with R3 SRs. This is 4 SSR + 2 R3 SR — works well for content that needs two damage dealers. Budget Composition (month 1-3): 2 SSRs + 4 R3 SRs. This can clear most content up to Abyss 50-60. The key is getting those 2 SSRs on your main DPS.

Long-Term Planning

As a F2P player, you get roughly 10-12 SSRs in 6 months from free pulls and pity. Plan which SSRs you target carefully. Month 1-3: Focus on getting 2-4 SSRs for your main team. Use SR to fill gaps. Month 4-6: Expand to 6-8 SSRs, replacing SR in the most impactful slots first (main DPS > off-DPS > support). Month 6+: Start replacing the last SR slots, aiming for all-SSR teams for your top 2-3 operatives. Always save 14,400 diamonds before pulling on a limited banner. An incomplete pull that leaves you at 50 pity with no SSR is worse than saving for a guarantee.

Warning: Do not fall into the trap of "I will wait for the perfect SR." A good SR at R3 today is better than a perfect SR in a month. Level the SR you have and replace it later if needed. The resources you spend on SR are not wasted — they generate progress that helps you farm more resources faster.

Summary: 4 SSR + 2 R3 SR clears all content. SR at R3 is ~90-95% of R0 SSR. Color match matters more than rarity. Focus your SSR on main DPS first. Do not pull incomplete banners. A decent SR leveled today is better than a perfect SR saved for later.
