F2P Abyss Path

You do not need a wallet to clear Abyss. You need a plan. This is the exact path I used on my F2P alt account to reach Floor 10 within three months of starting.

ENTRY_#1352026.07.11F2P_STRATEGY
Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

F2P Target: Floors 7-10 cleared with 2-3 stars each. This yields approximately 600-700 gems per reset, or 2,400-2,800 per month. You do not need to clear Floor 11 or 12 as F2P. Those floors exist for spenders and launch players.

The F2P Mindset Shift

The biggest obstacle F2P players face in Abyss is not power. It is expectations. Every showcase video you see of a Floor 12 clear is either a spender or a launch-day player. Comparing your month-three F2P account to those clears is setting yourself up for frustration. The F2P Abyss path is not about clearing everything. It is about clearing efficiently and knowing where to stop.

Here is the truth that nobody tells F2P players: you should not expect to clear Floor 11 or 12 as a free player. The gear check on those floors requires a level of investment that takes 6+ months of focused farming. Instead, focus on clearing Floors 7 through 10 with as many stars as possible. Four fully-cleared floors with stars give more gems than two cleared floors with no stars. The F2P priority is breadth over depth.

The second mindset shift is accepting that you will use SR memories. Many F2P players hoard resources waiting for SSR memories that never come. Meanwhile, they are sitting on R5 SR memories that can clear content today. An R5 SR memory at level 70 with good protocores outperforms an R0 SSR memory at level 60. Stop waiting. Build what you have.

Month 1-2: Laying the Foundation

Goal: Clear Floors 1-6 for free gems. Do not attempt Floor 7 yet.

During your first two months, your only Abyss objective is Floors 1 through 6. These floors are tuned for new accounts and require minimal investment. Your first SSR, whichever one it is from the beginner banner, will carry you through this entire range. Do not worry about team composition at this stage. Use your strongest operative and fill the remaining slots with whoever is highest level.

Focus your resources on leveling exactly two memories: your main DPS's Solar pair (the two memories with matching set bonuses). Get them to level 60. Equip whatever protocores drop from your domain runs with ATK or CRIT main stats. Do not spend stamina optimizing protocores at this stage. The difference between good and perfect protocores matters at Floor 10, not Floor 4. Your stamina is better spent on ascension materials to level your memories higher.

The monthly reset gives you four attempts at each floor. Use them all. Even partial clears give partial rewards, and the practice matters more than the gems at this stage. Learn the boss patterns. Learn your rotation. By the end of month two, you should be able to auto-clear Floors 1-6 without thinking.

Month 3-4: Building the Second Team

Goal: Clear Floor 7 with 2 stars, unlock Floor 8 attempts.

Floor 7 is the first floor that requires two teams. This is the gate that stops most F2P accounts. You need four operatives, not two. At this point, you should have acquired your second and third SSRs from either limited banner pulls or lost 50/50s. If you only have one SSR, build an SR as your second DPS. Xavier's R1 SR memory at level 70 outperforms a level 50 SSR.

Build your second team around whatever element the floor needs. Check the Stellactrum requirements before building. Do not invest in a memory whose element does not match any upcoming floor requirement. This is the single most important resource management rule for F2P players. Every resource spent on a mismatched memory is a resource that could have gone toward a memory that enables more floor clears.

For Floor 7 specifically, you need one strong team (your main DPS team) and one functional team (your secondary DPS plus supports). The functional team does not need to clear fast. It needs to survive and contribute damage. Give your secondary DPS your second-best protocores and focus on survival substats for the support slot. If your secondary team can clear the first wave in under 2 minutes, your main team can carry the second wave within the time limit.

Month 5-6: Pushing to Floor 10

Goal: Clear Floors 8-10 with 2+ stars each.

By month five, you should have two teams that can handle Floor 7 comfortably. Now the real work begins. Floor 8 requires survival, which means you need a healer or shielder. Zayne is the best option for F2P players because his base kit includes shields. If you do not have Zayne, build Rafayel as a support — his burn damage provides off-field DPS while you focus on dodging.

Floor 9 requires two elites killed within 10 seconds of each other. This is the first floor where F2P players need to think about burst alignment. Save both teams' Resonance skills for the elite spawn. Use Ardent Oath immediately when both elites are on the field. The 10-second window is generous enough that even modest gear can meet it if you align your burst correctly.

Floor 10 is the current realistic F2P endgame. The dual-element shield mechanic requires either two elements on your team or multi-hit abilities. Xavier's light attack chain is the best F2P tool for this floor because it shreds shields regardless of element. If you built Xavier as your main DPS, you have a significant advantage on this floor. If you built Sylus, bring whatever off-element character has the most hits per second and use them exclusively for shield breaking.

F2P Memory Priority for Abyss

The most common question from F2P players is which memories to build for Abyss progress. Here is the priority list based on my F2P alt account experience. This list assumes you have memories at R0 unless otherwise specified.

Tier 1 — Build First: Any SSR Solar pair for your main DPS. The set bonus from a Solar pair is worth more than any other investment you can make. If you have an SSR Solar pair for Xavier or Sylus, build it immediately. If you only have SR Solar pairs, build the SR pair to level 60 and replace them when you get an SSR pair.

Tier 2 — Build Second: Lunar memories that match your main DPS's element. Having one strong team is better than two mediocre teams. Invest in your main DPS's second memory slot before spreading resources to a second team. A single level 70 DPS with +12 protocores can carry a level 50 support through Floor 7.

Tier 3 — Build Third: Your secondary DPS's Solar pair. This should be SR at minimum, SSR if you have one. The secondary DPS does not need perfect gear. They need functional gear that lets them clear their assigned wave without dying. Focus on survival protocores (HP, DEF) for the secondary DPS's supports and offensive protocores for the DPS itself.

Tier 4 — Fill As Needed: Off-element memories for Stellactrum matching. Only build these when you are stuck on a specific floor because of a color mismatch. Do not pre-build for colors you have not encountered. The resources are too scarce for speculative investment.

Sustainable Weekly Reset Routine

As an F2P player, your weekly Abyss routine should follow a consistent pattern that minimizes wasted attempts. Monday is reset day. Do not attempt floors on Monday. Check the rotation and plan your team assignments first. Tuesday through Thursday, clear all floors you have previously completed. Focus on consistency, not improvement. Friday and Saturday, attempt to push one floor further than your previous best. Dedicate up to 10 attempts to the push. If it does not happen this week, it will happen next week. Sunday is rest day. Spend your remaining stamina on domain farming, not Abyss attempts.

The most important discipline for F2P players is knowing when to stop. If you have spent 10 attempts on a floor and your best run still misses the clear by 15 seconds or more, the floor is not ready for your account. Come back next week. The extra week of farming will make more difference than another 10 attempts with the same gear. Progression in this game is measured in weeks and months, not hours and days. The F2P path requires patience above all else.

SR Memory Tip: Do not ignore your R5 SR memories. An R5 SR with correct main stats at level 70 is equivalent to an R0 SSR at level 65 in terms of stat output. If you are waiting for an SSR that has not dropped in three months, build the SR. You can always feed the SR into the SSR later.

Summary: F2P target is Floors 7-10, not 11-12. Build two functional teams before optimizing one perfect team. Use SR memories aggressively — they are not wasted investment. Focus on survival for the second team. Reset weekly, push monthly. The gems will come with patience.
