Developer Interviews Summary

Key takeaways from published developer interviews — what the dev team thinks about balance, story direction, and community feedback.

ENTRY_#DEV_052026.07.02VERSION

How to Read This: This is a compilation of public developer interview content. Use it to understand the team's design philosophy and predict future content direction. All information is from officially published interviews.

Design Philosophy

The development team has stated their core principles across multiple interviews: Character-first design: Every gameplay mechanic should reinforce character identity. Xavier is fast and precise because he is a light-element hunter. Zayne is methodical and defensive because he is a doctor. The kit comes from the character, not from a spreadsheet. No direct nerfs: The team has committed to never directly nerfing an operative. Instead, they buff underperformers and adjust encounter design. If an operative is too strong, future content is designed to challenge them rather than reducing their power. This philosophy means power creep is managed through content difficulty, not through taking things away from players. Accessibility over complexity: The team prioritizes making combat accessible over adding mechanical depth. This is why protocores are the main optimization layer — they are complex but optional. The baseline combat (dodge, attack, resonance) is designed to be simple while the optimization layer (protocore substats, set bonuses) rewards deep investment.

Story Direction Insights

Interviews have revealed several long-term story plans: 4 operative structure: The core four (Xavier, Zayne, Rafayel, Sylus) are the permanent main cast. New operatives will be added but will not replace any of the core four. The story will continue to rotate focus among all characters. Deepspace origin: The nature of the Deepspace will be fully revealed within the next major story arc. The "50-year warning" and the Protofield are connected — the reveal has been planned since launch. MC's evolution: The protagonist's evolving powers are central to the story. Each story arc will show MC gaining new abilities that reflect their emotional journey. The resonance with each operative is tied to MC's growth, not just the operative's power. N109 future: The N109 Zone story is not over. Sylus's role will expand significantly in future patches. The zone itself will be revisited with new areas and new lore.

Community Feedback Loop

The development team has a structured feedback process: Official surveys: Sent every 2-3 months. Topics include combat difficulty, event design, and character popularity. Response rates above 50,000 are considered significant. Social media monitoring: The team monitors Chinese and global social media. They prioritize feedback that appears consistently across both regions. Known community requests: The team has acknowledged: (1) Request for more male characters — under consideration but no timeline. (2) Request for outfit reruns — system being developed. (3) Request for bond story expansions — planned for future patches. (4) Request for protocore QoL improvements — loadouts and mass-lock are being prototyped. Response time: The team typically responds to major feedback within 2-3 patches. Minor QoL requests take 3-6 patches. Feature requests (new systems) take 6-12 months. If a feature has not been acknowledged after 3 surveys, it is unlikely to be implemented.

Interview Source Guide: Developer interviews are published through: (1) Official website dev blogs — monthly, most detailed. (2) Chinese gaming press (Gamersky, 17173) — quarterly, exclusive reveals. (3) Anniversary livestreams — annual, biggest reveals. (4) App store feature interviews — occasional, operational focus. Reliability rating: Dev blogs = 95% reliable (official, written, edited). Press interviews = 80% reliable (may be mistranslated or taken out of context). Livestreams = 70% reliable (off-the-cuff statements may change). Community-translated interviews = 50% reliable (translation errors common). Always verify information from translated sources against official announcements.

Key Predictions from Interview Analysis

Based on consistent developer statements: Short-term (next 3 patches): Protocore loadout system, one new limited SSR operative, bond story expansion for an existing operative. Medium-term (3-6 patches): New permanent game mode (co-op expansion hinted), outfit trading or gifting system, stellactrum QoL improvements. Long-term (6-12 patches): Second anniversary content (major), new region beyond N109, potential anime/collab announcements. These predictions are extrapolations, not confirmations. The team's plans can change based on development constraints and market conditions.

Feedback: surveys every 2-3 months, QoL takes 3-6 patches, feature requests take 6-12 months. Key predictions: protocore loadouts, new operative, bond expansions in short term.
