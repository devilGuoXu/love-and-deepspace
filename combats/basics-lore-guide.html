Lore & Worldbuilding

The story behind the combat — factions, characters, and the mysteries of the Deepspace.

ENTRY_#024 2026.06.29 LORE

The World: Love and Deepspace is set in a near-future Earth where a dimensional rift called the "Deepspace" has opened, unleashing corrupted creatures (Wanderers) and granting certain humans extraordinary abilities. The Hunter Corps was formed to combat this threat — and you are their newest recruit.

Major Factions

Hunter Corps
The main organization. Protects humanity from Wanderers. All four operatives are current or former members. The Corps has branches worldwide and operates under UN oversight.
Onychinus
A shadow organization with ties to Sylus's past. Operates outside the law. Their goals are unclear but they frequently clash with the Hunter Corps over Wanderer-related artifacts.
N109 Zone
A quarantined region where Deepspace corruption is most concentrated. The zone is home to the most powerful Wanderers and holds secrets about the Deepspace's origin. Only the strongest Hunters can operate here.

Character Backstories

Xavier: A veteran Hunter with a mysterious past. His Light element is rare and powerful. He joined the Corps after a personal incident involving a Wanderer attack. Known for his calm demeanor and tactical brilliance.

Sylus: Former Onychinus operative turned independent contractor. His Dark element and execute abilities make him feared by allies and enemies alike. Joined the Hunter Corps under special circumstances.

Zayne: A medical researcher who developed the Frost treatment protocol. His scientific approach to combat makes him unique among Hunters. His Frost element was originally a side effect of his experiments.

Rafayel: An artist and thrill-seeker whose Flame element awakened during a Deepspace exposure incident. He treats combat as an art form. His AoE burn style reflects his creative personality.

The Story Continues: Love and Deepspace's story is still being told. Each major update adds new chapters, new revelations about the Deepspace, and deeper character development. The Classified N109 archives contain additional lore for those who dig deeper.
