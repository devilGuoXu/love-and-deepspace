Lore & Worldbuilding

The story behind the combat — factions, characters, and the mysteries of the Deepspace.

ENTRY_#024 2026.06.29 LORE

The World: Love and Deepspace is set in a near-future Earth where a dimensional rift called the "Deepspace" has opened, unleashing corrupted creatures (Wanderers) and granting certain humans extraordinary abilities. The Hunter Corps was formed to combat this threat — and you are their newest recruit.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Major Factions

Hunter Corps
The main organization. Protects humanity from Wanderers. All four operatives are current or former members. The Corps has branches worldwide and operates under UN oversight.
Onychinus
A shadow organization with ties to Sylus's past. Operates outside the law. Their goals are unclear but they frequently clash with the Hunter Corps over Wanderer-related artifacts.
N109 Zone
A quarantined region where Deepspace corruption is most concentrated. The zone is home to the most powerful Wanderers and holds secrets about the Deepspace's origin. Only the strongest Hunters can operate here.

Character Backstories

Xavier: A veteran Hunter with a mysterious past. His Light element is rare and powerful. He joined the Corps after a personal incident involving a Wanderer attack. Known for his calm demeanor and tactical brilliance.

Sylus: Former Onychinus operative turned independent contractor. His Dark element and execute abilities make him feared by allies and enemies alike. Joined the Hunter Corps under special circumstances.

Zayne: A medical researcher who developed the Frost treatment protocol. His scientific approach to combat makes him unique among Hunters. His Frost element was originally a side effect of his experiments.

Rafayel: An artist and thrill-seeker whose Flame element awakened during a Deepspace exposure incident. He treats combat as an art form. His AoE burn style reflects his creative personality.

The Deepspace Rift and Wanderer Connection

The Deepspace rift is the central mystery of Love and Deepspace's world. It appeared 17 years before the main story begins, a tear in reality over the Pacific Ocean that has been expanding ever since. The rift emits a unique form of energy that permeates the entire world, causing three significant effects: it awakens latent Evol abilities in humans exposed to high concentrations, it corrupts ordinary matter into Wanderers, and it enables Protocore formation at specific distances from the rift. The N109 Zone represents the area where these effects are most concentrated, making it both the most dangerous and most valuable region on Earth.

Wanderers are not random monsters but direct consequences of the Deepspace energy interacting with the material world. Each Wanderer type corresponds to a specific energy frequency — Void Spawn form from unfocused energy, Elemental Wanderers crystallize around specific element types, and Corrupted Operatives are former humans who were overwhelmed by energy exposure. Understanding this connection helps players appreciate why certain enemies appear in specific locations and why the N109 Zone has the highest Wanderer density. The lore establishes that Wanderers are symptoms of a deeper dimensional problem rather than standalone threats.

Story Timeline and Reading Order

The game's main story is divided into chapters that unlock based on player level and affinity milestones. The recommended reading order is: Main Story Chapters 1-8 (introduces all four operatives and the Hunter Corps), then individual character storylines (Tender Moments and Secret Times), followed by the Classified N109 archives (deeper lore on the Deepspace and Philos civilization). The side stories unlock additional context about each operative's background and motivations that enrich the main story experience.

New story chapters release approximately every six to eight weeks alongside major updates. Each update advances the main plot by one to two chapters and adds new character-specific story content. Players who fall behind on the story can catch up through the "Story Archive" feature, which allows replaying previously completed chapters without progression requirements. Lore-focused players should prioritize the Classified N109 entries after completing the main story — these contain the most significant revelations about the Deepspace, the Aether Core, and the lost civilization of Philos that are not covered in the main story chapters.

Elements reflect operative personalities: Xavier's Light represents clarity and truth. Sylus's Dark represents shadow and secrets. Zayne's Frost represents preservation and control. Rafayel's Fire represents passion and transformation.

The Story Continues: Love and Deepspace's story is still being told. Each major update adds new chapters, new revelations about the Deepspace, and deeper character development. The Classified N109 archives contain additional lore for those who dig deeper.
