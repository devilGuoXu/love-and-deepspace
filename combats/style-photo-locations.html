Golden Hour Locations

Golden Hour: The 10-minute window around sunrise and sunset where the sun is low and creates warm, diffused light. In Deepspace, golden hour is tied to the in-game clock — wait at a location until the sun is 10–20° above the horizon. The game renders stunning volumetric lighting during this window, with long shadows and warm color temperatures that make characters glow.

The home base balcony facing east is the most accessible golden hour spot. Arrive at 5:50 AM in-game time and wait for the sunrise. The light rays stream through the railing, creating dramatic dappled light patterns on your character. The beach area on the west side of the map has the best sunset shots — the sun sets over the water, creating a golden reflection that silhouettes your character beautifully. Position your character with their back to the sun and use rim light to outline their profile.

City rooftop: Available after completing Chapter 5. The rooftop has a 270° view of the city skyline. At golden hour, the city buildings cast long shadows and the sky gradient transitions from orange to purple. This location works best for full-body shots — position the character on the edge of the roof with the cityscape behind them. Use FOV 35mm to capture both the character and the expansive background.

Garden gazebo: The gazebo in the central garden area has filtered light through the vines. This creates natural dappled lighting effects on the character's face. Best for intimate close-up portraits. The green foliage provides a natural color contrast against warm-toned outfits. Visit during the morning golden hour (6:00–6:15 AM) when the light angle is low enough to penetrate the gazebo's roof gaps.

Weather Effects & Combos

Weather dramatically changes the mood of a photo. Deepspace has dynamic weather that cycles every 2–3 in-game hours. You can wait for specific weather or use the photo mode's weather override (if available in the current version).

CLEAR
High contrast, sharp shadows, vibrant colors. Best for casual and happy portraits.
CLOUDY
Diffused light, soft shadows, muted colors. Best for moody and emotional portraits.
RAIN
Reflective surfaces, moody atmosphere, wet textures. Best for dramatic and cinematic shots.
FOG
Atmospheric depth, mysterious feel, reduced visibility. Best for ethereal and fantasy portraits.

Rain + Night combo: The most underrated weather combination. Wet surfaces reflect neon lights from the city. Position your character near puddles or wet pavement and crouch to capture the reflection. The neon sign near the central plaza creates pink and blue reflections in puddles during rain at night. This creates a cyberpunk aesthetic that contrasts beautifully with the game's fantasy elements.

Fog + Forest combo: The forest area east of the map becomes atmospheric with fog. The trees fade into the mist, creating natural depth of field without using the blur tool. The fog diffuses light, creating a soft glow around the character. This is the best combination for Xavier photos — the ethereal look matches his light-based aesthetic. Position the character with backlighting through the fog for a glowing effect.

Clear + Snow combo: In colder regions, clear weather creates harsh shadows that emphasize facial structure. The snow acts as a natural reflector, bouncing light onto the character's face from below. This reduces the need for a fill light — the snow provides it for you. Best for Zayne portraits that emphasize his ice element. The contrast between bright snow and dark clothing creates striking compositions.

"The weather system is my favorite photography tool. I once waited 45 real-time minutes for fog in the forest just to get one shot of Xavier with the morning light filtering through mist. It was worth every second."

Home Base Spots

Your home base is the most controllable photography environment. You can rearrange furniture, change wallpaper, and control lighting through decoration placement. Every home base has these prime photo spots:

LIVING ROOM WINDOW
Natural light portrait spot — morning and afternoon
Place a couch or chair facing the window. Position the character sitting with the window light illuminating their face from the side. The window provides a natural key light. Morning light is cooler (blue tint) — afternoon light is warmer (orange tint). Choose based on the mood you want. The window also provides a natural catchlight in the character's eyes (the white reflection that makes eyes look alive).
BEDROOM MIRROR
Reflection shots and self-portraits
The full-length mirror in the bedroom allows for reflection shots. Position the camera to capture both the character and their reflection. This creates depth and storytelling potential — the character looking at themselves, or looking away from the mirror. The bedroom lighting is warm and intimate, especially with the bedside lamp turned on (place a lamp prop near the mirror).
DINING TABLE
Candid and lifestyle shots
Place food props on the dining table (if available from events). Position the character sitting at the table. Candid shots of the character looking at the food or looking out the window next to the table. The overhead pendant light creates a pool of light on the table. This spot works best for relaxed, everyday aesthetic shots that show the character's domestic side.

Pro Tip: Place light-colored furniture near your photo spot. White or cream-colored objects bounce light back onto your character, acting as natural fill lights. A white bookshelf or light-colored wall opposite the window will dramatically improve your indoor portraits without needing the game's lighting tools. Light-colored surfaces reflect 70–80% of incoming light, while dark surfaces absorb 90% — this is why interior portraits look better in well-decorated rooms.

Limited Event Maps

Event maps are the most valuable photography locations because they are temporary. Once the event ends, those backdrops are gone forever — possibly never to return. The community archives event map screenshots in dedicated channels, but nothing beats capturing them yourself.

SUMMER EVENT BEACH
The summer event added a beach area with crystal-clear water, palm trees, and a wooden pier. The water reflects the sky, creating mirror-like surfaces. Best time: sunset (5:30 PM in-game) when the sky and water both turn golden. The pier provides leading lines that draw the eye to the character. Position the character at the end of the pier with the camera on the beach, looking toward them. The wooden planks create natural perspective lines.
WINTER FESTIVAL SQUARE
The winter event square had festive lighting, snow-covered rooftops, and holiday decorations. The string lights create beautiful bokeh when shot at f/1.4. The snow reflects light, reducing shadow harshness. Best for warm-toned outfits that contrast with the cold environment. The central fountain, when frozen, creates a unique ice-textured surface for reflection shots.
CHERRY BLOSSOM GARDEN
A spring event introduced a cherry blossom garden with pink petal particles floating in the air. The petals can be captured mid-fall for dynamic shots. The pink color palette is ideal for romantic portraits. The garden's winding path creates natural composition lines. The pavilion in the center has a roof that frames shots perfectly — use it as a natural vignette.

Event Photo Checklist: (1) Visit every event map on day 1 to scout photo spots before crowds share them. (2) Screenshot during different weather conditions — rain in event maps is rare and creates unique shots. (3) Screenshot with and without your character — clean background screenshots are valuable for editing. (4) Upload your best event map screenshots to community archives — once the event ends, your screenshots become historical records.

Abyss Arena Photography

Abyss arenas have some of the most dramatic lighting in the game. Each floor has a unique arena aesthetic with different color palettes and lighting conditions. The arenas are accessible during combat and in the "arena preview" mode (if available). Arena photography is challenging because of the combat timer — prepare your camera settings before entering.

Floor 1-4
Dark stone, blue ambient
Floor 5-8
Lava glow, orange/red
Floor 9-10
Ice crystals, cold blue
Floor 11-12
Void purple, dramatic

Floor 9-10 arenas are the most photogenic in the game. The ice crystal formations create natural frames for your character. The cold blue ambient light is perfect for Xavier and Zayne shots. The crystals reflect light from your character, creating a blue glow on their face. Position the character near a large crystal formation and use a warm key light (3000K) to create color contrast — warm character against cold background.

Floor 11-12 void arenas have purple ambient lightning with floating void particles. The particles catch light and create a starry effect. The ground reflects the void sky like a mirror. This is the best location for Sylus shots — the dark purple aesthetic matches his dark element theme. Use rim light at 100% to create a dramatic silhouette against the void background.

Combat photography technique: To capture action shots in arenas, use the "freeze frame" feature during skill animations. Xavier's ultimate (Light Slash) creates a bright light burst that illuminates the entire arena. Capture the frame just before the burst hits — the light illuminating the character's face from the skill creates a dramatic key light that cannot be replicated in photo mode.

Arena access tip: If you want to explore an arena without the combat pressure, enter the floor and immediately hide in a corner. Wanderers spawn at fixed positions and do not aggro immediately. You have approximately 10–15 seconds before first contact. Use this window to take clean arena background shots. For longer exploration, clear all enemies and use the post-combat grace period (30 seconds before auto-exit) to take character shots in the arena.

Hidden & Secret Spots

The game world has hidden locations that are not marked on any map. These are discovered by players who explore every corner of the environment. Some are accessible through glitches or unlisted paths. Here are the community's top secret photo spots:

1
The Abandoned Observatory
Located at the top of the mountain in the northeast region. Accessible by climbing the cliff face behind the waterfall in the forest area. The observatory has a broken dome that frames the sky. At night, the stars are visible through the dome opening. The telescope prop can be used as a foreground element. Best for Xavier — the astronomy theme fits his character.
2
The Underground Reservoir
Below the central plaza, there is a maintenance access point behind the flower shop. Follow the stairs down to an underground reservoir with still water that creates perfect mirror reflections. The reservoir has blue-tinted lighting that gives photos a moody, aquatic feel. Best for Rafayel — the water theme matches his ocean connection.
3
The Rooftop Garden
Not the main rooftop, but a hidden garden accessible by jumping from the hospital building's fire escape. This garden has overgrown vines, wildflowers, and a broken bench. The overgrown aesthetic creates a "ruins reclaimed by nature" look. Morning light through the vines creates stunning dappled light effects. Best for Zayne — the quiet, overgrown garden matches his reserved personality.
4
The Neon Alley
Behind the nightclub in the entertainment district. A narrow alley with neon signs on both sides. The colored light creates a cyberpunk atmosphere. Red and blue neon light mixing creates purple shadows. Best for Sylus — the dark, urban aesthetic matches his underworld connections. Shoot during rain for wet reflections on the pavement.
5
The Lighthouse at Dawn
Accessible after completing Chapter 8. The lighthouse is on a small island visible from the beach. A boat takes you there (interact with the dock). The lighthouse interior has spiral stairs and windows that create dramatic light shafts. The top platform has a 360° ocean view. Dawn at the lighthouse is the single best photo location in the entire game according to community polls.
DO
  • Scout locations during different weather conditions
  • Use natural elements (trees, windows, water) as frames
  • Visit event maps on day 1 for exclusive backdrops
  • Position character with back to the sun for rim lighting
  • Use the environment's natural light as your key light
DON'T
  • Don't rely only on the photo studio backgrounds
  • Don't ignore weather timing — wait for the right conditions
  • Don't neglect home base decoration for better indoor shots
  • Don't forget to screenshot clean backgrounds for editing
  • Don't skip Abyss arenas — they have unique lighting

Location Mastery Tip: Create a photo location journal. For each location, record: (1) how to access it, (2) best in-game time, (3) best weather condition, (4) recommended FOV range, (5) which character outfits work best. Share your journal with the community — location scouting is a collaborative effort. The best photo spots in the game were discovered by players who shared their findings.

Summary: Golden hour (sunrise 5:50 AM, sunset 5:30 PM) provides the best natural lighting. Each weather condition creates a different mood — rain for cyberpunk, fog for ethereal, clear for vibrant. Home base window and mirror are the most controllable indoor spots. Event maps are temporary — capture them before they expire. Abyss arenas (especially floors 9-12) have dramatic ambient lighting. Hidden spots like the observatory, reservoir, and lighthouse offer unique backdrops that most players never see. The lighthouse at dawn is the community's #1 rated photo location. Scout in different weather, shoot at golden hour, and share your discoveries.
