Free Resources Compendium

Every free resource in the game, organized by source and value. Zero spending required.

ENTRY_#021 2026.06.29 F2P

The F2P Reality: A free player earns approximately 6,200 gems per month, 40+ blue tickets, enough stamina for one maxed operative per 2 months, and consistent shard income. This guide maps every source so you never miss a single free resource.

Gems (Premium Currency)

Source Amount Type
Daily Missions 50/day = 1,500/month Daily
Login Rewards ~600/month Daily
Abyss Reset (biweekly) ~1,600/month Biweekly
Event Rewards ~2,000/month (estimated) Events
Achievements ~3,000 (one-time total) One-time
Main Story First Clear ~2,000 (one-time total) One-time
Crew Contributions ~200/month Misc

Monthly F2P Total: ~6,200 gems = 38 pulls (plus 10-15 blue tickets from events/login = 48-53 total pulls per month)

Wish Tickets

Blue Tickets (Standard)
Login events: 2-5 per event. Abyss shop: 3/month. Event shops: 5-10 per event. Crew shop: 1/week. Battle pass (free): 5 per patch. Total: ~15-20 per month.
Gold Tickets (Limited)
Purchased with gems (160 each). No free gold tickets from any source. All limited banner pulls come from gem conversion.

Stamina & XP

Natural Regen: 240 stamina/day (1 per 6 min). 7,200/month.
Meal Stamina: 60 stamina × 2 per day (lunch + supper). 120/day = 3,600/month. This is the most commonly missed resource.
Friend Gifts: 10 stamina × 30 friends = 300/day (if all actively exchange). 9,000/month.
Crew Stamina: Occasional crew rewards. ~200-300/month.
Event Stamina: Events often give 60-300 stamina total. Variable.

Maximum F2P Stamina: 7,200 (regen) + 3,600 (meals) + 9,000 (friends if optimized) = ~19,800 stamina/month. Most players get ~10,000-12,000 because friend gifts are underutilized. This is the single largest source of free resources that players leave on the table.

Memory Shards

Crew Shop: 2 SSR shards/day × 30 days = 60 shards/month = 1 full SSR every ~2 months.
Event Shop: 10 SSR shards per event × ~1.5 events/month = 15 shards/month.
Abyss Shop: 5 SSR shards/month.
Standard Banner Pity: 1 SSR per ~70 pulls. Off-rate SSRs from limited banner 50/50 losses also count.

One-Time Resources

Main Story: ~2,000 gems + 10+ blue tickets + SSR beginner guarantee.
Achievements: ~3,000 gems + 15+ blue tickets + titles.
Collection Milestones: ~1,000 gems + 5 blue tickets.
Level Rewards: Gems and tickets at account level milestones.

Daily Checklist

☐ Claim daily missions (50 gems, account XP)
☐ Collect meal stamina (lunch 12:00-14:00, supper 18:00-20:00)
☐ Send friend gifts (30 friends)
☐ Donate to crew (gold donation, buy SSR shards)
☐ Spend stamina (skill mats if weekly cap not reached, otherwise events/protocore/XP)
☐ Check events for daily tokens/attempts

The Bottom Line: F2P is not a handicap — it's a different pace. 6,200 gems/month, 40+ pulls, consistent SSR shards, and ~12,000 stamina. This is enough to build one main DPS to 70 and two supports to 60 within 3-4 months. The key is never missing the daily resources: meal stamina, friend gifts, crew donations, and event attempts. Missing one day of meal stamina = 120 stamina lost. Missing a week = 840 stamina. Over a year, the difference between a diligent player and a casual one is thousands of dollars worth of resources.
