Daily & Weekly Checklist

Optimized routine for maximum resource gain in minimum playtime.

ENTRY_#0282026.06.29ROUTINE

Total Daily Time: 15-20 minutes for dailies. 30-40 minutes with Abyss/events. This checklist prioritizes efficiency — do the high-value tasks first.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Daily (15-20 min)

1. Login Rewards (1 min): Collect daily login, check mail, claim any expired items.

2. Base Visit (2 min): Collect garden resources, check for visiting operative (affinity gain), assign training room.

3. Stamina Dump (5 min): Spend all stamina on highest-priority farm. Priority: Boss materials > Memory XP > Gold. Use skip tickets if available.

4. Daily Missions (5 min): Complete the 5 daily tasks. Most overlap with other activities. Do anything remaining.

5. Friend Gifts (1 min): Send and receive friend gifts. Quick affinity + gold.

6. Shop Check (1 min): Free daily items in the shop. Refresh if you have currency.

Weekly (30-40 min)

Abyss Reset: Push as far as you can. Even clearing 1 extra floor is worth the gems.

Weekly Bosses: 3 boss kills. Double rewards on first 3. Don't skip.

Faction Battles: Complete all tickets. Even if you lose, participation rewards are worth it.

Crew Donations: Donate for crew XP and personal rewards. Gold donation is best value.

Event Check: Check limited events. Prioritize event shop over standard farm.

The Bottom Line: Consistency beats optimization. A 15-minute daily routine completed every day yields more resources than a perfectly optimized 2-hour session once a week. The daily login reward streak alone is worth more than most gameplay rewards.

Daily Mission Priority

Completing your daily missions in Love and Deepspace should take approximately 10-15 minutes. The highest priority daily tasks are those that award limited currency: the daily login bonus, the bounty board missions, and the stamina spend missions. These three tasks alone provide the majority of your daily resource income. Secondary priority tasks include affection interactions with operatives, base visits, and photo mode activities, which award affinity and social resources. The daily missions system is designed to be completable in a single focused session, making it feasible for busy players to maintain their daily income.

Weekly Mission Strategy

Weekly missions reset on Monday and offer larger rewards than daily missions. The key weekly missions to prioritize are: Abyssal Trials (any floors you can clear), Simulation Chamber (all difficulty tiers you can complete), and Bounty missions (the highest tier you can handle). These three mission types provide the bulk of weekly diamond income. Plan your weekly stamina budget around these missions before spending stamina on farming activities. A well-organized weekly routine takes approximately 30-45 minutes total across the week for mission completion.

Weekly Reset Priority System

Weekly resets occur every Monday at server reset time. The priority order for weekly activities is: Abyss attempts (limited per week, highest gem value), weekly boss kills (3 attempts, double rewards on first clear), domain farming (unlimited but time-gated by stamina), and event content (time-limited rewards). Complete Abyss attempts before spending stamina on domains ? Abyss rewards are fixed value while domain rewards scale with your stamina efficiency. If you only have time for one activity after reset, do your three weekly boss kills for the double reward bonus.

Daily Login Efficiency Routine

An optimized daily login routine takes approximately 10 minutes and covers all essential activities. The priority order: collect base resources (30 seconds), send and receive friend stamina (30 seconds), complete daily missions (3 minutes), spend natural stamina on priority domains (3 minutes), complete one Abyss attempt (2 minutes), and check event shop for time-limited items (1 minute). Skipping any of these steps consistently over a month costs approximately 500 gems and 2,000 stamina worth of resources in cumulative value.

Daily mission completion rewards 60 gems and 200 activity points. Activity point thresholds at 100 and 200 give bonus rewards including gold and upgrade materials. Missing a single day costs 60 gems plus approximately 30,000 gold in missed activity rewards. Over a 30-day month, consistent daily login is worth 1,800 gems and 900,000 gold.
