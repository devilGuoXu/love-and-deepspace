Xavier: Combat Rotation Guide

How to play Xavier — optimal combos, burst windows, and position management.

ENTRY_#0392026.06.30OPERATIVE_GUIDE

Playstyle: Xavier is a burst-oriented DPS who excels at quick in-and-out engagements. His damage comes from precisely timed Resonance and Ardent Oath windows. Unlike Sylus (sustained pressure) or Rafayel (AoE control), Xavier wants to build energy, dump everything in a 4-second window, then reset.

Core Rotation

The standard Xavier rotation: Auto x3 → Hold Attack (Charged Strike) → Resonance → Auto x2 → Swap Cancel. The Charged Strike after 3 autos has the best damage-per-frame ratio. Resonance should always be used immediately when available — it resets your auto-attack chain. After Resonance, do 2 quick autos then swap to your second operative to cancel recovery frames.

Ardent Oath timing: Use Oath immediately after a Resonance crit. The Light Seeker 4-pc bonus gives +30% CRIT DMG for 4s after Resonance — your Oath should land in this window. If you don't have Light Seeker, use Oath whenever the boss is staggered for the +20% damage bonus.

Positioning

Xavier is most effective at close-mid range (2-5m). His Charged Strike has a narrow hitbox — you need to be facing the target directly. Stay close enough to land Charged Strikes consistently but far enough to see telegraphs. Against mobile bosses, Xavier struggles — use Sylus instead if the boss teleports frequently.

Advanced Tech

Light Dash: After Charged Strike, tap dodge forward. Xavier dashes through the target and lands behind them. Use this to reposition behind bosses during their attack animations. Resonance Cancel: You can swap during the last 0.3s of Xavier's resonance animation to cancel recovery. This tightens your rotation by ~0.5s. Energy Bank: If the boss is about to phase (untargetable), hold your Resonance. Use it immediately when the boss becomes targetable again — your full rotation is ready.

Practice Drill: Go to a training dummy. Do: Auto x3 → Charged Strike → Resonance → Auto x2 → Swap. Repeat for 2 minutes without pausing. Target: complete one rotation in under 4 seconds.

Summary: Burst-and-reset playstyle. Lead with Charged Strike. Resonance resets auto chain. Oath in CRIT DMG window. Swap cancel recovery. Maintain 2-5m range.
