Why SRs Matter

The common wisdom in gacha games is "SSR or bust," but Love and Deepspace's memory system makes SRs surprisingly relevant well into the endgame. An SR memory at level 60 with the correct element and set bonus provides roughly 75% of the stats of an SSR at the same level — but SRs cost a fraction of the resources to ascend and level. More importantly, SRs fill elemental coverage gaps that would otherwise leave your Abyss teams vulnerable to elemental disadvantage penalties.

For new accounts, building a core of 4-5 strong SR memories to level 60 enables clear up to Abyss Floor 60, which in turn generates the gems and resources needed to pull for SSRs. Trying to clear Abyss with only 2-3 SSRs and no SR support is an exercise in frustration. The math is clear: SR investment pays for itself within two weeks of Abyss progress.

SR Tier List

Twilight Veil (Xavier — Light) S
The best SR in the game. Active: 350% Light damage with 100% crit rate against enemies below 30% HP. An execute effect on an SR is unheard of. Every Xavier player should level this SR to 60 immediately. It remains relevant even with full SSR collections as a secondary execute option for multi-wave content.
Permafrost Shard (Zayne — Frost) S
Active: 300% Frost damage that freezes for 1.5 seconds. Passive: Frozen enemies take 20% increased Frost damage. Despite being an SR, this memory enables the entire freeze-detonate rotation without requiring Permafrost Covenant. Essential for Zayne players who don't have his limited SSR yet.
Coral Dance (Rafayel — Water) A
Active: 4-hit Water combo dealing 280% total. Passive: Each hit increases dodge speed by 10% for 3 seconds. A fantastic SR for learning Rafayel's high-mobility playstyle. The dodge speed buff helps new players develop muscle memory for his dodge-cancel combos.
Shadow Step (Sylus — Dark) A
Active: Teleport behind the target and strike for 350% Dark damage. Passive: Next attack after teleport deals 50% bonus damage. Excellent for positioning and burst. The teleport mechanic is unique among SRs and provides utility that some SSRs lack.
Standard Elemental SRs B
Every operative has 2-3 standard elemental SRs that provide straightforward damage skills. These are your elemental coverage fillers and account for the bulk of early-game SR investment. None are standout, but collectively they enable Abyss progression. Level them to 50-60 as needed for elemental coverage.
Off-Element/Utility SRs C
SRs with off-element typing or purely utility effects (healing, shielding without damage). These see niche use in specific game modes but are generally not worth investing past level 40. Level only if you need their utility for a specific Abyssal Chaos challenge.

By Operative

For Xavier, prioritize Twilight Veil and Solar Spark. For Zayne, Permafrost Shard and Ice Needle are your go-to SRs. Rafayel players should build Coral Dance and Water Whip first. Sylus mains get the most value from Shadow Step and Night Blade. Each operative has at least two A-tier SRs that are worth taking to level 60 — the rest of their SR pool can stay at 40 for set bonuses.

Acquisition Sources

SR memories are abundant in Love and Deepspace. The primary sources: gacha pulls (every 10-pull guarantees at least one SR or higher), event shops (each event offers 1-2 SR memories for currency), the Crew Shop (SR shards available daily), Achievement rewards (one-time SR memory boxes at collection milestones), and the Battle Pass (paid track offers SR selection boxes).

The most efficient SR acquisition strategy is to never actively chase them. SRs accumulate naturally through banner pulls and event shops. Focus your limited currency on SSR banners — SRs will fill in over time. The only exception is the monthly Crew Shop SR shards, which provide a targeted way to complete specific SR memories you need for elemental coverage.

Common Mistakes

DO Invest
  • Level S/A-tier SRs to 60
  • Use SRs for element coverage
  • Buy SR shards from crew shop
  • Keep SRs for set bonuses
  • Ascend SRs before unused SSRs
DON'T Waste
  • Don't level SRs past 60
  • Don't use universal shards on SRs
  • Don't pull banners for SRs
  • Don't ignore SRs for "SSR only"
  • Don't ascend more than 2 SRs per operative

Tip: The Crew Shop sells 2 SR shards per day for 150 crew coins each. At 30 shards per SR, that's 15 days to complete an SR from scratch. However, you're better off spending crew coins on SSR shards once your core SR collection is established. Switch to SSR shards after your first month of play.

Summary: SR memories are a cost-effective investment for elemental coverage and Abyss progression. Focus on S and A-tier SRs, level them to 60, and never invest further. Use the Crew Shop for targeted SR completion in early game, then pivot to SSR shards. With smart SR management, you can clear Abyss Floor 60+ while building your SSR collection organically.
