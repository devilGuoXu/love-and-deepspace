WANDERER ENCYCLOPEDIA — MECHANICS

Spawn Mechanics & Wave Patterns

Wanderer spawns are not random — every wave follows a fixed script. Learn the rules and you can pre-position, pre-charge, and wipe waves before they fully materialize.

ENTRY_#WDR_072026.07.02MECHANICS

Core Rule: Wanderer spawns are triggered by player position, not by a timer. Every arena has defined spawn points. Every wave has a defined composition. Once you know the trigger line, you control the fight. The game spawns wanderers in "squads" of 2-4 enemies. Killing all members of a squad triggers the next squad to spawn. Empty arenas do not spawn more enemies.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Abyss Mode Spawns

Floors 1-4: Spawns are room-based. Each room has 2 squads. Squad 1 is on the arena floor. Squad 2 spawns after Squad 1 is eliminated (50% HP remaining on the last enemy triggers the spawn — not full death). Spawn points: Always the corners of the arena (NE, NW, SE, SW). Pre-position in the center for a 2s reaction window. Wave tell: A purple shimmer appears at the spawn point 1.5s before enemies materialize. You can pre-cast skills at the shimmer to hit enemies the moment they appear.

Floors 5-8: Single arena with 3 waves. Wave 1 spawns immediately on entry. Wave 2 spawns at 60s or when Wave 1 is fully cleared. Wave 3 spawns at 120s or when Wave 2 is fully cleared. Key difference: Wave 2 and 3 have a 3s delay between the wave trigger and actual spawn. Use these 3 seconds to reposition and regenerate energy. Spawn points: Wave 1 — directly in front of you. Wave 2 — both sides of the arena. Wave 3 — center (surrounding you).

Floors 9-12: Boss arena with adds. Adds spawn at fixed HP thresholds of the boss: 75%, 50%, 25%. Each threshold spawns 2-4 adds. The adds always spawn at the edges of the arena. Critical rule: Do not damage the boss past the threshold until you have cleared the adds. The boss's damage reduction increases by 30% while adds are alive — hitting the boss during add phases is inefficient.

Domain Mode Spawns

Resource Domains: Continuous spawn until the timer expires. Enemies spawn in squads of 3 from 2 fixed points. Each squad's spawn is triggered 8s after the previous squad dies. This means faster clear = more spawns = more loot. Do not wait between squads — kill and move to the spawn point. Xenochroam Domain: Spawns are 2x faster than other domains — bring AoE operatives (Rafayel or Xavier) for maximum efficiency.

Protocore Domains: 3 waves, wave-triggered. Wave 1: 2 standard wanderers. Wave 2 (50s mark): 2 standard + 1 elite. Wave 3 (100s mark): 1 elite + 1 ancient (mini-boss). Spawn points are fixed at the 3 cardinal directions. Pre-position at the center and rotate to face each spawn point. Optimal clear: Ignore the elite in Wave 2 until you've pushed the boss in Wave 3 to 50% HP. This despawns Wave 2's elite and saves time.

Event Mode Spawns

Event spawns are almost always infinite waves with a set duration (typically 180s). Enemies spawn based on a hidden "threat meter" that fills over time. The higher the meter, the stronger the spawns. Meter mechanics: +2/s passive fill. +5 per enemy killed. +10 per elite killed. At 100 meter: boss spawns. Strategy: If you want more loot: kill fast and fill the meter to spawn more bosses. If you want survival: kill only what threatens you and let the timer run. Event spawns are the only mode where you can control difficulty by throttling your clear speed.

Voyage Mode Spawns

Voyage spawns are tied to hazard nodes. Each hazard node has 1-3 pre-determined squads. Unlike Abyss, Voyage squads do NOT trigger based on position — they trigger based on proximity to the node. Proximity range: 4 units from the node center. You can see the node's activation radius on the ground (faint blue circle). Strategy: Activate the node, kill the first squad, then step OUT of the blue circle. The second squad will not spawn until you re-enter the circle. This lets you heal and reposition between squads — abuse this in high-difficulty Voyage sectors.

Pro Tip: The most important spawn mechanic in the entire game: despawn range. If you move more than 12 units away from a wanderer's spawn point, it despawns and returns to full HP. This is useful for resetting bad elite spawns — if an elite spawns with an unfavorable aura, you can run away, let it despawn, and re-enter to get a different aura roll. Works in all modes except Abyss floors 9-12.

Spawn Priority Cheat Sheet

Abyss 1-4: 2 squads per room → pre-cast at corner shimmers. Abyss 5-8: 3 waves, 60s intervals → 3s spawn delay = free reposition. Abyss 9-12: Boss HP thresholds → clear adds before pushing boss. Domains: 8s respawn timer → kill speed = loot speed. Events: Threat meter fills → control difficulty by kill rate. Voyage: Hazard node proximity → step out of blue circle to pause spawns.

Summary: Spawns are scripted, not random. Know the trigger: Abyss = kill last enemy. Domains = timer. Events = threat meter. Voyage = proximity. Pre-position at spawn points. Pre-cast at shimmer indicators. Abuse despawn range to reset bad elites. Control spawns to control the fight.
