Why the Base Matters: Your base is more than decoration. It provides passive affinity gain, daily resource generation, and unlocks special events. A well-decorated base is a functional one.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Base Features

Living Room: Passive affinity gain when operatives visit. Higher decoration level = more frequent visits. Prioritize furniture upgrades here first.
Training Room: Passive combat XP for reserve operatives. Scale scales with equipment level. Second priority.
Garden: Daily resource node. Plant and harvest upgrade materials. Low priority 閳?nice to have but not critical.
Photo Studio: Unlocks additional photo mode backgrounds and props. Medium priority 閳?useful for events.

Furniture & Decoration Tips

Set Bonus: Furniture from the same set gives bonus stats. Mixing sets loses the bonus. Pick one theme and stick with it.
Operative Preferences: Each operative prefers certain furniture styles. Matching their taste gives bonus affinity per visit.
Upgrade Order: Living Room > Training Room > Photo Studio > Garden. Focus resources on the rooms that give the most value.
Event Furniture: Limited-time furniture from events often has unique bonuses. Always collect at least the key pieces.

Daily Base Routine

Log in: Collect garden resources (~30s).

Check visitors: Interact with any visiting operative for affinity (~1min).

Training Room: Assign reserve operatives to training (~30s).

Decoration: Rotate furniture or adjust placement weekly for variety.

Operative Visit Mechanics

Operatives visit your base randomly, with the visit frequency determined by your current affinity level with that operative and your base's decoration score. A higher decoration score in the Living Room significantly increases visit frequency 閳?each 100 points of decoration score adds approximately 5% to the base visit chance. The maximum visit rate is capped at one visit per operative per day. When an operative visits, interacting with them grants 30-60 affinity EXP depending on the interaction type chosen. Always prioritize the unique dialogue option over generic responses for maximum affinity gain.

Each operative has preferred furniture types that increase their visit satisfaction. Xavier prefers minimalist and modern furnishings. Zayne likes classical and nature-themed decorations. Rafayel favors artistic and colorful pieces. Sylus responds best to dark and luxurious styles. Matching the operative's preference doubles the affinity gained from that visit. If you are focusing on building affinity with a specific operative, temporarily rearrange your Living Room furniture to match their preference before their expected visit window. The visit window is predictable 閳?operatives tend to visit within 2 hours of their daily greeting time.

Resource Generation Optimization

Your base generates resources passively over time, with generation rates determined by room levels. The Training Room generates combat EXP for reserve operatives at a rate of 100 EXP per hour at level 1, scaling to 500 EXP per hour at level 5. The Garden produces upgrade materials every 8 hours, with a maximum storage capacity of 48 hours. You lose potential resources if you let the Garden sit at max capacity 閳?check and harvest at least twice daily. The Living Room generates a small amount of gold (500 per hour at level 1, 2,000 per hour at level 5).

Resource generation continues while offline for up to 12 hours. After 12 hours, generation pauses until you log in and collect. This means logging in at least once every 12 hours is optimal for resource collection. If you can only log in once per day, schedule your login to collect resources after exactly 12 hours rather than 24 閳?collecting at 24 hours means you lost 12 hours of potential generation. Setting a collection alarm is a small optimization that adds up to significant resource gains over weeks of play.

The photo studio unlocks 2 new poses per upgrade level, totaling 10 exclusive poses at max level. These include combat-ready stances and romantic close-ups unavailable elsewhere.

The Bottom Line: Your base is a passive income system. Invest in the Living Room first for affinity gains. Don't neglect event furniture 閳?some pieces never return. And remember: a cluttered base is harder to navigate than an empty one.
