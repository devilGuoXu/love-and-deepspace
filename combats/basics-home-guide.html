Home & Base Guide

Decorate, upgrade, and manage your Hunter base — your personal hub between missions.

ENTRY_#027 2026.06.29 HOME

Why the Base Matters: Your base is more than decoration. It provides passive affinity gain, daily resource generation, and unlocks special events. A well-decorated base is a functional one.

Base Features

Living Room: Passive affinity gain when operatives visit. Higher decoration level = more frequent visits. Prioritize furniture upgrades here first.
Training Room: Passive combat XP for reserve operatives. Scale scales with equipment level. Second priority.
Garden: Daily resource node. Plant and harvest upgrade materials. Low priority — nice to have but not critical.
Photo Studio: Unlocks additional photo mode backgrounds and props. Medium priority — useful for events.

Furniture & Decoration Tips

Set Bonus: Furniture from the same set gives bonus stats. Mixing sets loses the bonus. Pick one theme and stick with it.
Operative Preferences: Each operative prefers certain furniture styles. Matching their taste gives bonus affinity per visit.
Upgrade Order: Living Room > Training Room > Photo Studio > Garden. Focus resources on the rooms that give the most value.
Event Furniture: Limited-time furniture from events often has unique bonuses. Always collect at least the key pieces.

Daily Base Routine

Log in: Collect garden resources (~30s).

Check visitors: Interact with any visiting operative for affinity (~1min).

Training Room: Assign reserve operatives to training (~30s).

Decoration: Rotate furniture or adjust placement weekly for variety.

The Bottom Line: Your base is a passive income system. Invest in the Living Room first for affinity gains. Don't neglect event furniture — some pieces never return. And remember: a cluttered base is harder to navigate than an empty one.
