Event Resource Planning

How to budget your diamonds, tickets, and stamina for upcoming events — predictive planning for F2P and light spenders.

ENTRY_#0812026.07.02PLANNING

How to Read This: Start with the income section to understand your monthly resource budget. Then use the predictive planning section to decide what to pull on and what to skip. The emergency fund section is for when your favorite operative unexpectedly gets a banner.

Monthly Income Breakdown

Knowing your monthly income is essential for planning. F2P Monthly Income: Daily commissions (60 diamonds × 30 = 1,800). Abyssal Trial resets (600-1,000 depending on floor count). SHC resets (400-800 depending on rank). Event + maintenance compensation (1,000-2,000 average). Total: 4,000-5,000 diamonds per month = 25-31 pulls. Monthly Pass Income ($4.99): +90 diamonds per day = +2,700 per month. Total with pass: 6,700-7,700 diamonds = 42-48 pulls. Battle Pass Income ($9.99): +1,000 diamonds per pass cycle (every 6 weeks) + 5 limited tickets. Spread across months: +500 diamonds/month + ~3 pulls. Total Light Spender (monthly + pass): 7,200-8,200 diamonds = 45-51 pulls per month. Important: These numbers are averages. Good months with generous events can give +50% more. Bad months with no events give less. Always budget conservatively — assume the low end.

Predictive Banner Planning

Limited SSR banners cost approximately 140 pulls on average (hard pity = 140, soft pity around 70-100). At 45 pulls per month (F2P), you need 3+ months of saving per guaranteed limited SSR. Budgeting strategy: (1) Identify your "must-pull" operatives — the characters you enjoy most. (2) Save 140 pulls (22,400 diamonds) before their banner drops. (3) For "nice-to-have" banners, allow yourself 40-50 pulls (soft pity chance). If you do not get it, cut losses. (4) Skip banners for operatives you do not main. A limited memory for an operative you do not use is worth 0 diamonds. (5) Track the banner schedule through community sources — knowing when your target's banner will rerun helps you plan savings windows. F2P note: You can afford approximately 3-4 limited SSRs per year if you save everything. Choose wisely.

Event Stamina Budgeting

Events often have stamina-spending tasks that compete with your normal farming. Event shop priority: (1) Limited SSR memory / outfit (if available) — always get this first. (2) Limited protocores / memory sets (if available). (3) Diamond packs (best value per event currency). (4) Standard tickets (good value). (5) Level-up materials (decent value if you need specific types). (6) Gold (last resort). Stamina allocation: During events, spend 80% of your stamina on event tasks until you clear the shop of high-value items, then return to normal farming. Do not spend reserves (resin) on events unless the event has double drops. The event shop materials are generally equal to or slightly worse than normal domain farming — the value is in the exclusive items (SSR, outfit, protocores).

The Emergency Fund System: Unexpected limited banners happen (character birthdays, holiday specials, collabs). The emergency fund system protects you: (1) Maintain a reserve of 40 pulls (6,400 diamonds) at all times. This covers soft pity on an unexpected banner. (2) If you dip into the emergency fund, replenish it before spending on any other banner. (3) The emergency fund is for "I really want this" situations, not for every banner. If you use it more than once every 3 months, you are not saving enough. (4) Veterans maintain two emergency funds: 40 pulls for SSR guarantee, plus 20 pulls for outfit resonance (if you care about cosmetics). This is 9,600 diamonds total — a 2-month F2P savings target.

Long-Term Account Planning

Beyond individual banners, think about your account trajectory: Month 1-3 (New): Spend resources to build a baseline team. Do not save every diamond — you need a functional roster for content clears. Month 4-6 (Establishing): Start saving for specific targets. You should have 2 functional operatives by now. Month 6-12 (Optimizing): Pure saving mode for limited SSRs. Your core team is strong enough to clear most content. Year 2+ (Veteran): You should have 1-2 fully built operatives plus 1-2 secondary operatives. At this point, you can afford to be picky — only pull for operatives you genuinely want to main, not for meta reasons. Key insight: Most players quit because they spend resources inefficiently and feel stuck. Following a plan triples your account trajectory. Track your diamond income and spending in a spreadsheet — you will be surprised how much you save when you see the numbers.

Summary: F2P monthly income: 4,000-5,000 diamonds. Limited SSR costs ~140 pulls average. You can afford 3-4 limited SSRs per year as F2P. Maintain a 40-pull emergency fund. Budget stamina during events for exclusive items first. Long-term planning prevents impulse spending. Track your income in a spreadsheet. The best account is the one that has the operatives you love, not every meta memory.
