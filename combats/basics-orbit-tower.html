Orbit Tower Complete Guide

Endless tower mode — team rotation, elemental floors, and deep progression.

ENTRY_#0442026.06.30CHALLENGE

Orbit Tower Overview: The Orbit Tower is an endless climbing mode with 100+ floors. Each floor has specific elemental requirements and boss combinations. Unlike Abyss (reset every 2 weeks), the Tower is permanent content — once cleared, it stays cleared. This makes it the best one-time diamond source in the game.

Written by Kaelen — Deepspace Hunter Station Team

Floor Structure

Floors are divided into elemental cycles: Floors 1-20 mixed elements (easy), 21-40 single-element focus, 41-60 dual-element, 61-80 triple-element, 81-100 rotation cycles. After 100, floors repeat with stat inflation. Each floor has an elemental recommendation — matching the element gives +30% damage. Mismatching gives -20%. There is no penalty for using neutral element, but the recommendation is strong.

Team lock: Starting from Floor 21, operatives used on a floor are locked for that floor's set (10 floors). Plan your rosters carefully — you can't reuse Xavier on floors 21-30 if you already used him on 21-25.

Team Building Strategy

Build 3 teams of 2 operatives each (6 total operatives minimum). Distribute elements across teams. Team A: Xavier + Zayne (Light + Frost). Team B: Sylus + Rafayel (Dark + Fire). Team C: Flex slots (filler operatives). Each team should have at least one element matching the floor's recommendation.

For floors 61-80 (triple-element), you need one team to cover 3 elements. Use Stella matching for coverage — each operative has a primary element and can also match a secondary through their memory's Stellactrum color.

Reward Efficiency

One-time diamonds: Floors 1-20 = 50 each, 21-40 = 80 each, 41-60 = 100 each, 61-80 = 120 each, 81-100 = 150 each. Total one-time diamonds from Floors 1-100: approximately 8,000 diamonds. This is the largest one-time diamond bank in the game. Priority: Push Tower on days when you're stuck in Abyss — Tower progress is permanent and never resets.

Key Floors

Floor 30 (first wall): Dual Frost elites. Bring Rafayel or Xavier with Fire Stellactrum. Floor 50: Single DPS check boss with 2m enrage. Bring your best single-target team. Floor 70: Triple element gauntlet — need all 3 elements covered. Floor 100: Final boss gauntlet — recommended 100k+ power.

Orbit Tower Floor Strategy

Orbit Tower floors follow a predictable difficulty curve that rewards sustain over burst damage. Each floor has a recommended power level displayed at the start. Your actual effective power is calculated based on your team's total stats with a multiplier for Stellactrum matching. Matching at least three Stellactrum colors gives approximately a 25% effective power bonus. The tower also has a hidden fatigue mechanic ? using the same operative on consecutive floors reduces their stats by 5% per floor. Rotate your operatives between floors to maintain optimal performance.

Tower-Specific Memory Loadouts

Orbit Tower floors have recommended elemental affinities displayed before each attempt. Switching memories between operatives between floors is allowed and costs no resources. Keep a pool of 4-6 flex memories with neutral set bonuses that can be moved between operatives as needed. The flex pool should include one HP-set for support roles, one ATK-set for DPS roles, and one CRIT-set for burst roles. Swapping a flex memory between operatives takes approximately 10 seconds and allows your team to adapt to each floor's elemental requirements without maintaining multiple complete sets.

Tower floors above level 50 introduce elemental resistance mechanics. After using the same element three times in a row, subsequent attacks deal only 70% damage. Rotate between two elements every two attacks to avoid triggering this penalty. If your team has only one element, the resistance mechanic effectively reduces damage by 30%.

Pro Tip: Don't Rush. Tower floors don't expire. Climb when you're strong enough for the next 10-floor bracket. Stopping at floor 20, 40, 60, or 80 is fine — the rewards are backloaded at 10-floor intervals. Pushing one extra floor for 50 diamonds isn't worth if you're stuck.

Summary: 8,000 total one-time diamonds from Tower. Build 3 teams with 6 operatives. Match elemental recommendations. Team lock every 10 floors from 21+. Don't rush — Tower is permanent.
