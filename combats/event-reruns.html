Rerun Prediction Calendar

When will your favorite banner return? This guide analyzes rerun patterns and predicts future rerun schedules for events and limited banners.

ENTRY_#EVT_132026.07.02RERUNS

Rerun Philosophy: Love and Deepspace follows a predictable rerun cadence similar to other Papergames titles. Understanding the pattern helps you plan your savings and avoid FOMO-driven impulse pulls.

Rerun Patterns Observed

Analysis of reruns so far reveals these patterns: Limited Banners rerun after 6-8 months: The first limited banners (launch-window SSRs) began rerunning around month 7. Subsequent banners have followed a similar timeline. The exact interval depends on the operative's popularity and schedule alignment. Event Reruns are rarer: Only 2 event reruns have occurred in the game's history (Valentine's 2025 reran as a mini-event in 2026). Major seasonal events (Summer, Winter) have not rerun — their items were instead added to the premium shop. Double Reruns sometimes happen: During anniversary and major celebrations, two limited banners may run simultaneously — one new and one rerun. This pattern may become more common as the SSR roster grows. Birthday Banners are annual: Each operative has a confirmed annual birthday banner. These always feature the operative's first limited SSR. Xavier (Dec 25), Zayne (Feb 14), Rafayel (Oct 3), Sylus (Apr 17). Birthday banners are the most predictable rerun and the safest to plan around.

Predicted Rerun Schedule (July-December 2026)

Based on the 6-8 month rerun window and known event cadence: July 2026: Possible rerun: Valentine's 2026 SR (Sylus) in a mini-event. No limited SSR rerun predicted — summer event takes priority. August 2026 — 1.5-Year Anniversary: High probability of reruns. Predicted: Launch window limited SSRs rerun (Twilight Oath Xavier, Eternal Winter Zayne, Blazing Sacrifice Rafayel, Midnight Sovereign Sylus). These are the most demanded reruns. The 1.5-year anniversary is the most likely window for them. September 2026: Possible filler rerun of an earlier limited SSR (Celestial Blade Xavier or Frozen Edge Zayne). Lower probability. October 2026 — Rafayel Birthday: Guaranteed: Rafayel's first limited SSR (Blazing Sacrifice) reruns alongside his birthday event. If Blazing Sacrifice already reran in August, his second limited SSR (Ember Storm) may rerun instead. November 2026: No confirmed reruns. Possible filler. December 2026 — Winter Event + Xavier Birthday: Guaranteed: Xavier's birthday banner (Twilight Oath or Celestial Blade). Winter event maybe include a rerun of the 2025 Winter SSR. January 2027 — 2-Year Anniversary: Predicted: Multiple rerun banners. Possibly all four launch SSRs or a special "selector" banner where you can choose which limited SSR to pull for.

How to Prepare for Reruns

Savings Target: A guaranteed limited SSR costs 140 pulls (worst case: lose 50/50 and hit hard pity). At 150 diamonds per pull, that is 21,000 diamonds. For a specific rerun, start saving at least 14,000 diamonds (100 pulls) 2 months before the predicted rerun window. Resource Audit: Before a predicted rerun, audit your current resources: diamonds, deepspace wishes (limited pulls), stardust (for shop conversion), pity counter (if you are close to pity, the cost drops significantly). Pity Tracking: Your pity carries over between banners of the same type. If you are at 50 pity with a guaranteed rate-up, you only need 20 more pulls for the rerun — a huge discount. Do not waste this by pulling on banners you do not care about. Wish Crystal Management: Do not convert all your diamonds into wishes immediately. Convert only when the rerun banner is confirmed. Wishes are non-refundable. Diamonds are flexible. Priority Setting: Rank reruns by how much they improve your account. S-tier reruns (BiS memories) > A-tier > B-tier. Do not pull on a rerun just because it is available. Pull because it fills a roster gap or is your favorite operative.

F2P and Low-Spender Rerun Strategy: If you are F2P or a light spender, you cannot pull on every rerun. Be selective: (1) Prioritize your main operative's BiS SSR. If you main Xavier, save everything for Twilight Oath's rerun. (2) Skip reruns of memories you already have a substitute for. (3) Skip reruns of memories that are only sidegrades to your current roster. (4) Do not pull for "collection" — pull for power. (5) Use the 50/50 tracker: if you won the last 50/50, expect to lose the next one. Budget accordingly. (6) The paid battle pass ($5) gives ~10 pulls worth of diamonds over a month. If you can afford $5/month, it significantly accelerates your savings. (7) Skip weapon/Protocore banners unless you are a dolphin/whale. The value is lower than memory banners.

Rerun Value Assessment

How to evaluate whether a rerun banner is worth pulling on: Power Increase: How much will this memory improve your account? If it is your main operative's BiS and you do not have it, the value is very high. If it is a sidegrade, value is low. Roster Gap: Do you lack a strong memory of this element/role? A rerun that fills a gap is more valuable than one that replaces an existing memory. Future Proofing: Some memories have unique effects that remain relevant even as new memories release. "Eternal Winter" Zayne's unique debuff stacking is still BiS even after newer Zayne SSRs released. Cosmetic Value: The Tender Moments scenes are exclusive to limited SSRs. If you care about story content, reruns are your only chance to access stories you missed. Timing: If a rerun is 2 months before a major event (anniversary), consider whether the anniversary SSR might be stronger. If the anniversary SSR powercreeps your target, skip the rerun.

Summary: Limited banners rerun every 6-8 months. Birthday banners are annual and predictable. Events rarely rerun — their exclusive items may return in the premium shop. Predicted major rerun windows: August 2026 (1.5-year anniversary — launch SSRs), October 2026 (Rafayel birthday), December 2026 (Xavier birthday), January 2027 (2-year anniversary — possible multi-rerun). Preparation: save 14,000+ diamonds for a guaranteed pull, track your pity, know your 50/50 status. Be selective with reruns as F2P. Prioritize your main operative's BiS. Evaluate rerun value based on power increase, roster gaps, and timing relative to upcoming events.
