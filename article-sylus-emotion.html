💭 PLAYER CONFESSIONS · EMOTIONAL

I finished Sylus's route. I admit — I'm broken.

📅 May 18, 2026 ✍️ Just a regular player 💬 1.2k comments ❤️ 3.8k hearts

Let me start with a confession: I'm not a lore expert or a hardcore theory-crafter. I'm just someone who plays a couple of hours after work, chasing dailies and slowly chipping away at the story.

When Sylus first dropped, I was... unimpressed. Dark, brooding, "bad boy" archetype? Felt a bit cliché. Plus he literally kidnaps the protagonist? I rolled my eyes and thought: "Here we go again."

I was wrong. So wrong. 😭

A few days ago, I finally finished the entire N109 storyline. I sat in front of my screen for a solid ten minutes, just staring. Eyes stinging. Heart pounding. I know a lot of you felt the same, so I need to write this down.

“

I don't fear the darkness. I built it. I rule it. I am it.

—— Sylus

01 · First impression: This guy is annoying

Honestly? When I first entered the N109 Zone, Sylus just felt... fake. That smug "I know everything" tone. That condescending stare. And that annoying crow Mephisto constantly watching you. My inner monologue was just: "Can you please leave me alone?"

And he kept "testing" the protagonist. Forcing choices. Watching reactions. Like a predator studying its prey. I really thought this character was just unlikeable.

02 · The turning point: That one line

It was around Chapter 3. The protagonist gets hurt. Sylus walks over. I braced myself for another sarcastic remark.

🎭 SYLUS
"Your heartbeat... it's too fast. Are you scared? Or just excited to be with me?"
—— Chapter 3, N109 Main Story

That's when it hit me. He wasn't mocking her. He was checking. Checking if she was still alive. Still conscious. Still... there with him.

His carefree attitude isn't arrogance. It's armor. He plays the villain to protect himself. And to test if the protagonist is worth trusting.

03 · The breaking point: Mephisto's secret

This is the moment that completely destroyed me.

🎭 SYLUS
"Mephisto isn't just a bird. He's the part of me that died a long time ago. Still watching. Still waiting for a reason to come back."
—— Hidden dialogue, N109 Zone

My nose stung. Eyes watered.

That crow isn't a tool or a weapon. It's the part of himself he lost. The part that used to believe in things — before the world crushed him. He keeps "the old him" by his side, watching, waiting for a reason to live again.

And that reason... is the protagonist.

04 · Why Sylus hits different

I've been thinking about why so many players (including me) end up falling for him.

01
He doesn't treat you like a damsel
He never asks the protagonist to hide behind him. He wants her to fight, to prove herself. That dangerous "equal" dynamic? Very attractive.
02
His softness is buried under sharp edges
He won't say "I love you." He'll say "Don't die." He won't comfort you with sweet words — but he'll show up the moment you're hurt. That restrained affection hits harder than any confession.
03
His past breaks your heart
The darkness of N109 wasn't his choice. It was forced on him. He became the "villain" the world wanted him to be — but the boy who once believed in light is still in there, buried deep.

05 · To every player who's still unsure

If you haven't finished Sylus's route yet — or if you're early in and find him "annoying" — please. Stick with it.

His charm isn't instant. It's hidden in the throwaway lines. In the way he moves to protect you without hesitation. In the rare moments when his mask slips and you see the vulnerability underneath.

He's not the traditional "good guy." But he's real. He's layered. He's someone you want to understand.

❤️

"I don't force. I choose."
That choice... is yours.

—— And it's mine too.

So... thank you, Sylus. For making this game, on some random night, feel a little more real. A little more worth it.
