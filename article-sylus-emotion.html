I finished Sylus's route. I admit — I'm broken.

A player's honest confession: From hating Sylus to falling for him.

Let me start with a confession: I'm not a lore expert or a hardcore theory-crafter. I'm just someone who plays a couple of hours after work, chasing dailies and slowly chipping away at the story.

When Sylus first dropped, I was... unimpressed. Dark, brooding, "bad boy" archetype? Felt a bit cliché. Plus he literally kidnaps the protagonist? I rolled my eyes and thought: "Here we go again."

I was wrong. So wrong. 😭

A few days ago, I finally finished the entire N109 storyline. I sat in front of my screen for a solid ten minutes, just staring. Eyes stinging. Heart pounding.

“

I don't fear the darkness. I built it. I rule it. I am it.

—— Sylus

01 · First impression: This guy is annoying

Honestly? When I first entered the N109 Zone, Sylus just felt... fake. That smug "I know everything" tone. That condescending stare. And that annoying crow Mephisto constantly watching you.

02 · The turning point: That one line

It was around Chapter 3. The protagonist gets hurt. Sylus walks over. I braced myself for another sarcastic remark.

🎭 SYLUS
"Your heartbeat... it's too fast. Are you scared? Or just excited to be with me?"
—— Chapter 3, N109 Main Story

That's when it hit me. He wasn't mocking her. He was checking. Checking if she was still alive. Still conscious. Still... there with him.

03 · The breaking point: Mephisto's secret

🎭 SYLUS
"Mephisto isn't just a bird. He's the part of me that died a long time ago. Still watching. Still waiting for a reason to come back."
—— Hidden dialogue, N109 Zone

My nose stung. Eyes watered. That crow isn't a tool or a weapon. It's the part of himself he lost. The part that used to believe in things — before the world crushed him.

04 · Why Sylus hits different

01
He doesn't treat you like a damsel
He never asks the protagonist to hide behind him. He wants her to fight, to prove herself. That dangerous "equal" dynamic? Very attractive.
02
His softness is buried under sharp edges
He won't say "I love you." He'll say "Don't die." He won't comfort you with sweet words — but he'll show up the moment you're hurt.
03
His past breaks your heart
The darkness of N109 wasn't his choice. It was forced on him. He became the "villain" the world wanted him to be.
❤️

"I don't force. I choose."
That choice... is yours.

—— And it's mine too.

So... thank you, Sylus. For making this game, on some random night, feel a little more real. A little more worth it.
