Stamina Basics · How Much You Get

240 base stamina · +60 snack · +?? monthly card

Stamina is your most valuable resource. Every activity — bounty hunts, core hunts, story stages, flower breeding — consumes stamina. Learning to allocate it efficiently is the single most important skill for account progression.

⚡ Daily Stamina Income
  • Base regeneration: 240 stamina per day (1 per 6 minutes)
  • Daily snack (login reward): +60 stamina (claim from events tab)
  • Monthly card (low spender): +60 stamina per day (optional)
  • Total F2P: 300 stamina per day
  • Total with monthly card: 360 stamina per day

⚠️ Critical: Stamina caps at your maximum limit. Don't let it sit at cap — you lose regeneration time. Check in at least twice a day.

Priority System · What to Farm When

Not all days are the same. Here's how to prioritize based on your current needs:

🆕
New Player (First 2 weeks)
Focus on account level and story progression.
  • Clear main story stages (first-time rewards)
  • Bounty Hunt gold/EXP daily
  • Unlock all game modes
📈
Building a Character
You have a specific character you want to raise.
  • Farm their ascension materials first
  • Core Hunt for protocores
  • Bounty Hunt for gold/EXP as needed
⚔️
Endgame (Abyss Floors)
You need optimized protocores and stats.
  • Core Hunt primary (protocore dust)
  • Bounty Hunt gold (reforging costs)
  • Element-specific mat farming
🏠
Home System Focus
You want bond levels and furniture.
  • Garden performing (flower breeding)
  • Core Hunt dust for furniture (some require dust)
  • Bounty Hunt gold for furniture purchases

FAQ · Common Questions

Q: Should I refresh stamina with diamonds?
A: For F2P — no. Save diamonds for pulls. For monthly card players — maybe once daily if you're pushing Abyss. For whales — refresh as needed.
Q: Should I save stamina for events?
A: No. Events usually have separate stamina systems or boosted rates. Spend your stamina normally — don't let it cap.
Q: Is Garden worth spending stamina on?
A: Long-term, yes. Flowers give bond points and gifts. Short-term, focus on Bounty and Core first. Use leftover stamina for garden.
Q: What should I farm on Sunday?
A: Core Hunt. It's the only unlimited high-value farm. Use leftover stamina before weekly reset.
Q: I'm stuck on a boss. Where should I put stamina?
A: Farm ascension mats for your main DPS first. Then Core Hunt for better protocores. Bounty Hunt gold for upgrading.
Q: How much gold do I need?
A: Millions. Leveling characters, upgrading protocores, buying furniture — gold is always needed. Never skip Bounty Hunt gold runs.

Final Recommendations

⚡

Bottom line — the optimal daily stamina routine:

  1. Always do Bounty Hunt Gold ×3 and EXP ×3 first. Non-negotiable. This is your highest value.
  2. Spend remaining on Core Hunt. Protocore dust is the second most valuable resource.
  3. Farm ascension mats only on correct element days. Don't waste stamina on off-days.
  4. Use leftover stamina on Garden or extra Bounty Hunts. Any stamina spent is better than stamina capped.

Follow this routine every day, and your account will grow faster than 90% of players.
