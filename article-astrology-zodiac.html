I. Introduction: Why Astrology Matters in Character Analysis

Astrology is a language of archetypes — a symbolic system that maps human personality onto celestial patterns. Whether you believe in it as divination or appreciate it as literary analysis, the zodiac offers a powerful framework for understanding fictional characters. The writers of Love and Deepspace clearly drew from astrological archetypes when crafting their male leads. Each character embodies core traits of their sun sign, and deeper analysis of their potential moon and rising signs reveals hidden layers of their psychology.

How We Determined the Signs:
  • 📅 Sun signs — Based on official birthdays where available, or inferred from canonical personality
  • 🌙 Moon signs — Inferred from emotional patterns and private behaviors
  • ⬆️ Rising signs — Inferred from how they present themselves to the world
  • ⭐ Planetary placements — Based on key personality traits (Mercury for communication, Venus for love, Mars for drive)

II. Sylus — The Scorpio King

Sun Sign: Scorpio (October 23 - November 21)
  • Intensity, passion, and control
  • Fear of vulnerability masked by dominance
  • Transformation and rebirth as life themes
  • The "shadow" side: jealousy, possessiveness, secrecy
Rising Sign: Capricorn
  • Presents as authoritative and composed
  • Commanding presence, natural leadership
  • Emotional distance as armor
Planetary Placements:
  • Mercury in Scorpio: Speaks with intensity. Every word carries weight. Rarely speaks without purpose.
  • Venus in Scorpio: Loves obsessively. All-or-nothing devotion. Jealousy as proof of love.
  • Mars in Capricorn: Strategic in conflict. Never fights without a plan. Cold anger, not hot rage.
  • Pluto (Ruling Planet) in Scorpio: Deeply transformative. Has died and been reborn — metaphorically and perhaps literally.

Scorpio is the most misunderstood sign of the zodiac — and so is Sylus. Scorpios are not evil; they are intense. They feel everything deeply but show nothing. Their fear is not of pain but of vulnerability. Sylus's journey is quintessentially Scorpionic: he has already died (the explosion, the cybernetic transformation) and been reborn. Now he seeks someone who can see past his armor — not to save him, but to stand beside him in the dark.

III. Xavier — The Pisces Dreamer

Sun Sign: Pisces (February 19 - March 20)
  • Dreamy, intuitive, and deeply emotional
  • Escapism as coping mechanism
  • Sacrificial love — would wait forever
  • The "shadow" side: avoidance, martyr complex, emotional drowning
Rising Sign: Libra
  • Presents as calm, balanced, and aesthetically attuned
  • Diplomatic even when hurting
  • Seeks harmony and avoids conflict
Planetary Placements:
  • Mercury in Pisces: Communicates poetically. Says more with silence than words.
  • Venus in Pisces: Unconditional love. Sees the divine in the beloved. Will forgive anything.
  • Mars in Cancer: Fights protectively. Aggression only surfaces when loved ones are threatened.
  • Neptune (Ruling Planet) in Pisces: Lives between worlds. Reality is permeable. Dreams feel as real as waking life.

Pisces is the sign of the mystic, the artist, the one who dissolves boundaries between self and other. Xavier has waited two hundred years — not out of obligation, but because he cannot let go. Pisces love is oceanic: vast, deep, and capable of drowning. His tragedy is not that he loves too little, but that he loves too much, and has no idea how to love himself.

IV. Zayne — The Virgo Healer

Sun Sign: Virgo (August 23 - September 22)
  • Analytical, meticulous, service-oriented
  • Emotions processed through actions, not words
  • Perfectionism as both gift and curse
  • The "shadow" side: self-criticism, inability to relax, emotional repression
Rising Sign: Capricorn
  • Presents as professional, competent, slightly distant
  • Commands respect through expertise
  • Uses work as emotional shield
Planetary Placements:
  • Mercury in Virgo: Precise communicator. Chooses words carefully. Frustrated by ambiguity.
  • Venus in Virgo: Love expressed through acts of service. Cold medicine as poetry.
  • Mars in Virgo: Methodical in conflict. Never loses control. Anger channeled into productivity.
  • Mercury (Ruling Planet) in Virgo: The mind is both his greatest tool and his prison.

Virgo is the sign of the healer — and the critic. Zayne saves lives, but he cannot save himself from his own standards. He gives love through doing because words feel insufficient. His tragedy is believing that competence is the only thing he has to offer — not realizing that his quiet presence is the greatest gift of all.

V. Rafayel — The Pisces Artist (with Cancer Rising)

Sun Sign: Pisces (February 19 - March 20)
  • Shares sun sign with Xavier — different expression
  • Artistic, emotional, boundaryless
  • Fear of abandonment masked by drama
  • The "shadow" side: victim mentality, emotional manipulation,逃避
Rising Sign: Cancer
  • Presents as sensitive, nurturing, and emotionally open
  • Wears heart on sleeve — but not the whole heart
  • Protective of inner world while appearing vulnerable
Planetary Placements:
  • Mercury in Pisces: Communicates through art. What cannot be said is painted.
  • Venus in Aries: Falls fast and hard. Impulsive in love. Drama as proof of passion.
  • Mars in Cancer: Fights emotionally. Aggression is reactive, not proactive.
  • Neptune (Ruling Planet) in Pisces: Reality is a canvas to be reshaped. Truth is subjective.

Rafayel and Xavier share the same sun sign — but their expressions could not be more different. Where Xavier internalizes, Rafayel externalizes. Both are Pisces: both are artists, both are haunted, both have waited a thousand years. But Rafayel's Cancer rising makes him wear his vulnerability openly, while Xavier's Libra rising makes him hide it behind calm. Rafayel's tragedy is that he fears being forgotten — so he makes himself impossible to ignore.

VI. MC — The Sagittarius Seeker

Sun Sign: Sagittarius (November 22 - December 21)
  • Curious, adventurous, truth-seeking
  • Optimistic even in darkness
  • Freedom as core value
  • The "shadow" side: restlessness, commitment issues, blunt honesty
Rising Sign: Unknown / Player Choice
  • MC is designed as an avatar — her rising sign is whatever the player projects
  • This ambiguity allows every player to see themselves in her
Why Sagittarius Fits:
  • She asks questions constantly. Sagittarius is the philosopher of the zodiac.
  • She seeks the truth about her past. The quest is more important than the destination.
  • She refuses to be controlled. By EVER, by fate, by anyone.
  • She believes in possibility. Even after trauma, she hopes.

VII. Xia Yizhou — The Capricorn Survivor

Sun Sign: Capricorn (December 22 - January 19)
  • Ambitious, disciplined, burdened
  • Survival as primary drive
  • Emotional repression as armor
  • The "shadow" side: workaholism, isolation, inability to accept help
Rising Sign: Scorpio
  • Presents as intimidating, mysterious, untouchable
  • Rarely reveals true intentions
  • Others sense his power before he speaks
Planetary Placements:
  • Mercury in Capricorn: Speaks with authority. Every word is measured.
  • Venus in Capricorn: Love shown through provision and protection. Actions over words.
  • Mars in Scorpio: Fights ruthlessly when cornered. Never forgets an enemy.
  • Saturn (Ruling Planet) in Capricorn: Life is a series of tests. Rest is not allowed.

Capricorn is the sign of the mountain climber — always striving, never resting. Xia Yizhou's tragedy is that he has climbed so high that he can no longer see the ground. He protects his sister by becoming something unrecognizable. He survives by sacrificing his humanity. Saturn's lesson is harsh: you cannot save others by destroying yourself. But Capricorns learn that lesson last.

Written in the Stars

Astrology offers a language for patterns we already see: Sylus's intensity, Xavier's patience, Zayne's precision, Rafayel's emotion. Whether or not the writers consciously used astrological archetypes, the stars have aligned — each character embodies their sign so perfectly that the zodiac feels like destiny.

But the stars do not dictate fate. They describe tendencies, not outcomes. Sylus can learn vulnerability. Xavier can learn self-worth. Zayne can learn emotional expression. Rafayel can learn security. And MC, the Sagittarius archer, can choose any horizon she wishes.

The question is not who the stars say you should love. The question is: whose constellation calls to you?
