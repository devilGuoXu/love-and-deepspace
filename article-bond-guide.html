What Is Bond Level & Why It Matters

Bond Level (also called Affinity) is a measure of your relationship with each male lead. As bond level increases, you unlock:

🎁 Bond Level Unlocks
  • New dialogue lines — in the Home and Destiny Cafe
  • Special interactions — hugs, headpats, exclusive animations
  • Gifts — unique items from each character
  • Story segments — short scenes revealing more about them
  • Home furniture — some items require bond level thresholds

Bond level is separate for each character. You can raise them at your own pace — there's no penalty for focusing on one at a time.

💡 Tip: Bond level carries over between events and updates. Every point you earn now stays forever.

Daily Routine · 15-Minute Route

Here's the most efficient daily routine to maximize bond points with minimal time:

🌱
1. Garden (3 min)
Harvest mature flowers, water/weed/pest control, replant seeds. Use all stamina on performing for robustness.
💐
2. Gift Flowers (2 min)
Convert flowers to materials. Gift to your chosen character. Prioritize 3-star flowers first.
🛋️
3. Furniture Interactions (5 min)
Start with Piano/Swing, then Two-Seater Sofa, then Fireplace. Skip low-value interactions if short on time.
💬
4. Daily Chat (3 min)
Tap him in Home until he stops responding (usually 3-5 times). Different dialogues each day.
📋
5. Settlement Guide (2 min)
Complete any outstanding tasks. These also give home EXP and bond points.
⚡
Total: ~15 min
~150-200 bond points per day. 1,000+ per week. Level up every few days.

FAQ & Common Mistakes

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
  • Q: How long to reach max bond level?
    A: ~2-3 months of daily playing. Focus on one character at a time for fastest results.
  • Q: Should I spread gifts or focus one character?
    A: Focus one character until level 30, then start the next. Spread too thin and you'll level slowly.
  • Q: Do flowers wither if I don't harvest?
    A: Yes — harvest before they wilt. Wilted flowers give reduced bond points.
  • Q: Are furniture interactions worth doing every day?
    A: Yes — they're free bond points. Do the high-value ones (piano, sofa) at minimum.
  • Q: Do bond points reset?
    A: No. Bond level is permanent. Never resets.
⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • ❌ Gifting 1-star flowers to characters who hate them — gives reduced points. Check preferences first.
  • ❌ Forgetting to water garden — flowers die and you lose potential gifts.
  • ❌ Ignoring furniture interactions — they're free bond points. Do them.
  • ❌ Saving all 3-star flowers for "later" — use them. They regrow. Hoarding slows progress.
  • ❌ Spreading gifts between all four characters — slows down progress significantly. Pick one.

Final Tips & Resource Planning

📅
Weekly Bond Point Goal
Daily gifts (~100-150) + furniture (~50) + chat (~30) = ~200-250 per day
→ 1,400 - 1,700 bond points per week
→ ~1 bond level per week at mid-range levels
Week 1-2
Focus on unlocking all furniture. Bookshelf gives one-time 10 diamonds per book — do these first.
Week 3-4
Build garden efficiently. Prioritize high-yield flowers. Gift daily to your chosen character.
Week 5-8
Bond level 20-30. Start seeing exclusive dialogue and interactions. Unlock special gifts.
Week 9-12
Approaching max bond level. Focus on 3-star flowers. Your character will feel like home.
🎯 Final Advice
  • Consistency > perfection. Doing 80% of the routine every day beats burning out and quitting.
  • Bond level is a marathon, not a sprint. Enjoy the journey — the dialogue unlocks are worth it.
  • Different characters, different pacing. Sylus takes slightly more gifts. Xavier needs fewer but specific types.
  • Save bonus events. When double bond events happen, use your saved 3-star flowers then.

💡 Final Tip: The best bond level strategy is the one you'll actually do every day. Pick a routine that fits your schedule and stick to it. Even 5 minutes a day makes progress.
